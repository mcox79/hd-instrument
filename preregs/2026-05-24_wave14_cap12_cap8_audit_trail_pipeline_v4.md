## Purpose

Re-run the Composition A audit at the now-available v1c VAMP iterate traces
(30 valid trace files at `data/exp_wave14_cap8_vamp_iterates_srht_hadamard_v1c/iterates/`).
Compute Spearman rho(kappa_n divergence component, Schur-Weyl irrep (n)-mass
deviation) for ALL 4 hard families: Kerdock, SRHT, Hadamard, RM(1,m), at
N=4096 / 5 seeds. This is Composition A's first true 4-family quantitative
test.

## Pairing (unchanged from v3)

For each codebook family C and order n in {2..5}:
- `x_n(C) = | kappa_n_emp(C) - kappa_n_MP |` (kappa divergence)
- `y_n(C) = | schur_mass_(n)_emp(C) - schur_mass_(n)_MP |`

When iterate trace is available for (C, alpha=1.0, seed):
- append `I_n(C)` to x-vector and `log1p(|I_n(C)|)` to y-vector

Compute Spearman rho on the combined vectors per seed, then aggregate.

## HARD PASS (Composition A LICENSED, 12th-capability adjacent)

- `rho_aggregate >= 0.60` in >= 3 of 4 hard families
- AND no family with `rho < 0.30`
- AND no family is TIED (i.e., the iterate-derived component DID break ties)

## HARD FAIL (Composition A KILLED, prose-only)

- `rho < 0.30` in >= 2 of 4 hard families with finite rho.

## MIDDLE BAND (Composition A holds narrowly)

  per-family annotation tightening required.

## Open questions / risks

- If the v1c traces happen to be VAMP-converged within 5 iterations (early
  stop), the iterate-fingerprint component will be dominated by the first
  few iterations only -- still non-degenerate, but with a narrower dynamic
  range than full-length traces. The `iterate_fingerprint` formula
  `I_n = sum_i (mse[i] - se_pred)^2 * (i+1)^n` weights LATER iterations
  more strongly, so an early-stop trace gives a weaker signal at high n.
- RM(1,m) does NOT have iterate traces in v1c (only SRHT + Hadamard).
  RM(1,m) falls back to spectrum-only mode; this can re-introduce the
  Schur-Weyl tie problem and route RM(1,m) into a TIED state. If that
  happens, RM(1,m) is counted as middle-band evidence (not a hard fail).
