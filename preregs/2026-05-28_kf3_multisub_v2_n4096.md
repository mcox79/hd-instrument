## Hypothesis

Multi-substrate isolation (KF-3) holds at production N=4096 in the clean loading regime (M/N=4).
Cross-substrate info leakage and state contamination scale as ~1/sqrt(N), so at N=4096
the N=1024 MIDDLE_BAND result (leakage=0.014, contam=0.066) should fall below HP thresholds.

## Context

kf3_multisub_isolation_v1 ran only in smoke mode (N=1024); verdict KF3_MIDDLE_BAND.
v2 tests the same isolation hypothesis at FULL N=4096 with 5 seeds and M/N in {4,8,12}.
This is the first TRUE N=4096 isolation measurement for KF-3.

## Pre-registered Thresholds

- **HARD_PASS:** max_leakage < 0.01 AND max_contam < 0.05 at M/N=4 (clean regime).
  Prediction: at N=4096, expected_leakage ~ 0.007, expected_contam ~ 0.033 (below thresholds).
- **HARD_FAIL:** max_leakage > 0.10 OR max_contam > 0.30 at coupling=0, any M/N.
  Interpretation: structural isolation failure; product isolation claim would be false.
- **MIDDLE_BAND:** leakage or contam exceeds HP thresholds but below HF.
  Outcome plan: if M/N=4 is borderline, investigate M/N dependence.

## Calibration

Prior empirical anchor: kf3_multisub_isolation_v1 N=1024 smoke: leakage=0.014, contam=0.066.
Smoke v2 N=1024 confirms: leakage=0.027, contam=0.074 (consistent order of magnitude).
N-scaling prediction: leakage * sqrt(1024/4096) = 0.027 * 0.5 = 0.013 at N=4096.
HP leakage=0.01 is below the predicted 0.013. MIDDLE_BAND outcome is possible.
Bands widened to +-50% per calibration-probe policy (first N=4096 FULL measurement).

## Formula Self-Tests (from script docstring)

1. Random HD vectors cosine-sim ~ N(0, 1/sqrt(N)); max over n=200 probes at N=4096:
   ~ sqrt(2 * ln(200) / 4096) ~ sqrt(2 * 5.3 / 4096) ~ 0.051. HP_contam=0.05 near this bound.
2. Info leakage for orthogonal W_A, W_B: expected |mean_corr| ~ 1/sqrt(N) = 1/64 = 0.016.
   HP_leakage=0.01 is ~0.6x of theoretical floor. May not be achievable.

## Timeout Estimate

smoke_wall_s = 0.14s at N=1024, 1 seed, 1 M_frac, 2 coupling_counts.
Full: N=4096, 5 seeds, 3 M_fracs, 5 coupling_counts.
N-scale: (4096/1024)^2 = 16 (dominant cost is matrix W build, O(N^2)).
Seed/M_frac/coupling scale: 5 * 3 * (5/2) / 2 = ~19x cell count increase.
Formula: ceil(1.5 * 0.14 * 16 * 19) = ceil(63.8) = 64s.
Safety 10x: 640s. PROT-019 floor: 3600s.
**timeout_s = 3600** (PROT-019 floor; actual expected << 1h)

## OOM Check

W float32 at N=4096: 64MB x 2 substrates = 128MB.
Codebook: 16384 x 4096 x 4 = 256MB.
Peak: 2*W + codebook = ~384MB. Well under 6GB. PASS.
