## Hypothesis

With the CUDA generator bug fixed, the extended beta sweep {2,4,6,8,10,12,16} at N=4096 5-seed will show critical slowing down with ratio >= 1.5 and peak near training beta=8.

## Context

v2 crashed in 3.13s due to RuntimeError: Generator(device='cuda') passed to CPU-default torch.rand. Fix: use Generator(device='cpu') in run_one_seed. v1 baseline HARD_PASS (ratio=1.64) unchanged.

## Pre-registered Thresholds

- **HARD_PASS:** max(tau_by_beta) / min(tau_by_beta) >= 1.5 AND peak_beta in {6,8,10}
- **HARD_FAIL:** ratio < 1.0 (contradicts v1)
- **MIDDLE_BAND:** ratio in [1.0, 1.5)

## Calibration
