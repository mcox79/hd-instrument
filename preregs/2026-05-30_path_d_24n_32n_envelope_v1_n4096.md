## Scientific question
U1 found no ceiling at 16N depth=50. G7 extends to 24N and 32N to identify
whether Path D maintains accuracy past 16N.

## Pre-registered bands
- HARD_PASS: all 40 cell-seeds (2 M * 4 depth * 5 seeds) >= 0.85 (no ceiling
  within 32N depth=50 envelope).
- HARD_FAIL: >= 10 cell-seeds < 0.50 (ceiling identified within envelope).
- MIDDLE_BAND: otherwise.

## Config
- N = 4096 (PROT-018 _n4096)
- M sweep [24N, 32N] = [98304, 131072]
- depth sweep [10, 20, 30, 50]
- K_paths = 100
- Seeds: [7, 17, 23, 31, 41]
- N_STARTS = 16
- beta_D = 4.0

## OOM check
- W = 4096*4096 float32 = 64 MiB
- Codebook 32N = 131072 x 4096 float32 = 2 GiB
- K_paths=100 x depth=50 index buffers ~0.4 MiB
- Peak ~2.5 GiB, under 6 GiB headroom.

## Self-test
- N_FULL == 4096
- M_GRID_FULL == [98304, 131072]
- 40 expected cell-seeds
- Verdict gates HP/HF/MB exercised
- Live CPU smoke at N=1024 M=512 depth=3

## Timeout estimate
- smoke wall ~5s
- 40 cell-seeds; depth=50 dominates
- scaling_exp = 1.5; estimate = ceil(1.5 * 5 * 4^1.5 * 40 / 5) = 480s smoke->full
- Per-cell-seed ~50-90s at depth=50; total ~50 * 40 = 2000s + overhead
- timeout_s = 14400 (user spec).

## Importance
HIGH - first envelope extension past U1's 16N upper bound.
