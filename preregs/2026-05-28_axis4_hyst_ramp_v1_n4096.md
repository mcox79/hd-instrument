# Pre-registration: axis4_hyst_ramp_v1_n4096

**Date:** 2026-05-28

**Queue:** overnight_queue

## Hypothesis

The substrate exhibits a hysteresis loop in retention-vs-M space: loading (adding memories) and unloading (removing memories) produce different retention curves at the same M value, with normalized loop area >= 0.10.

## Context

Pred-4 v211 confirmed hysteresis in BPC vs beta (max_gap=1.84). This experiment tests the RETENTION vs M analog: does history of memory loading matter? First-order phase transition signature.

## Pre-registered Thresholds

- **HARD_PASS:** loop_area >= 0.10 * M_max at at least 1 rate AND at >= 2/3 seeds
- **HARD_FAIL:** loop_area < 0.01 * M_max at ALL rates and ALL seeds (path-independent)
- **MIDDLE_BAND:** loop_area in [0.01, 0.10) * M_max

## Calibration

Prior anchor: Pred-4 v211 max_gap=1.84 (BPC-based hysteresis). Different order parameter.
Bands widened to +-50% per calibration-probe policy (retention-M protocol is first measurement).
HP threshold 0.10 = 50% of expected 0.20 loop_area at a first-order transition.
HF threshold 0.01 = trivially small (noise level for path-independent process).

## Timeout Estimate

150 ramp steps total x 3 seeds. Per step: ~0.2s on GPU. Safety 10x: ~900s.
PROT-019 floor: 3600s. **timeout_s = 3600**

## N-suffix

_n4096 suffix; production N = 4096 (PROT-018 binding).

## OOM Check

W float32 at N=4096: 64MB. key_idx buffer: 32768 * 4 = 128KB.
Peak: W + codebook + index buffer = ~130MB. Under 6GB. PASS.
