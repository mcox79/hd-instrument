## Hypothesis

Path D maintains >= 0.95 accuracy at M in {48N, 64N} = {196608, 262144}
at N=4096, extending the confirmed G7_HARD_PASS (24N-32N) to a larger
envelope without finding a ceiling.

## Configuration

- N: 4096 (PROT-018 binding)
- M grid: {196608, 262144} (= 48N, 64N)
- Depth grid: {30, 50} (upper depths only; 10 and 20 confirmed clean in G7)
- K_paths: 100
- Seeds: [7, 17, 23] (3 seeds; G7 showed near-zero variance at 32N depth=50)
- N_starts: 16
- beta_D: 4.0

## Pre-registered bands

**HARD-PASS (HP)**:
  All 12 cells (2 M x 2 depth x 3 seeds) accuracy >= 0.95.
  Interpretation: Path D ceiling is above 64N at N=4096; no capacity cliff found.
  Cap-map impact: R-PATH-D-NO-CEILING lifts from 0.88-0.97 toward 0.92-0.98+.

**HARD-FAIL (HF)**:
  Any cell accuracy < 0.50 (sharp cliff found in 48N-64N range).
  Interpretation: Path D hits a capacity cliff between 32N and 64N.
  Next step: bisect between 32N and 48N to locate the cliff precisely.

**MIDDLE-BAND (MB)**:
  Some cells below HP (< 0.95) but none below HF (>= 0.50).
  Interpretation: gradual performance degradation in the 48N-64N range.
  Next step: distinguish depth-driven vs M-driven degradation from per-cell detail.

## Calibration note

G7_HARD_PASS showed all 40 cell-seeds >= 0.85 at 24N-32N. HP raised to 0.95
(from G7's 0.85) because: (a) we are extending a confirmed-clean envelope,
(b) near-zero variance observed at G7 means 0.85 is a weak lower bound,
(c) 3-seed sample is adequate for envelope characterization given G7 variance.
This is NOT a calibration-probe (prior empirical anchor at 32N exists).

## OOM pre-check

N=4096: W matrix = 4096x4096 x float32 = 64 MiB.
Codebook at max M=262144 x 4096 = 4.0 GiB.
K_paths=100 x depth=50 index arrays ~ negligible.
Peak memory estimate: ~4.5 GiB. Under 6 GiB headroom (A10 has 24 GiB).

## Timeout estimate

G7 at 40 cells (2 M x 4 depth x 5 seeds) ran ~8-12 min cloud GPU.
This anchor: 12 cells (2 M x 2 depth x 3 seeds) but M values are 1.5-2x larger.
Codebook construction scales ~linearly with M. Per-path ops scale with depth.
Estimate: ~12-18 min for 12 cells at larger M.
Formula: 1.5 * 600s * (12/40)^1.0 * (3/5)^1.0 * (M_ratio=1.75) = ~1260s
Timeout: 2700s (45 min, generous margin for 4 GiB codebook alloc).

## Strategic value

PASS: completes Path D ceiling characterization at N=4096 in the 48N-64N range;
lifts R-PATH-D-NO-CEILING confidence band.
FAIL: locates a capacity cliff; changes Path D product story from "no ceiling"
to "ceiling at ~32N-48N" which is still competitive and productizable.
