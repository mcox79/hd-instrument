The original Tier-1 spec (< 1e-6) is unreachable with softmax over C=16384 atoms (floor is 1/C=6.1e-5). The reformulated claim:
  (a) above_thresh_frac=0 in all seeds at M <= N (binary structural impossibility)
  (b) mean_oos_max_conf <= 10/C = 6.1e-4 (near-uniform OOS responses)
  (c) max_oos_max_conf < 50/C = 3.05e-3 (no individual probe near threshold)

## Pre-registered thresholds

**HARD_PASS:** (a) above_thresh_frac=0 in ALL 5 seeds at M <= N
  AND (b) mean_max_conf <= 10/C in >= 4/5 seeds
  AND max_max_conf < 50/C in all cells.
  Interpretation: substrate structurally cannot hallucinate + OOS responses near-uniform.

**HARD_FAIL:** any seed shows above_thresh_frac > 0 at M <= N.
  Interpretation: structural hallucination detected -- KF-1 claim fails.

**MIDDLE_BAND:** above_thresh_frac=0 (claim a OK) but near-uniform bound (b) fails in >1 seed.
  Middle-band plan: Investigate whether ratio_to_uniform = mean * C is N-dependent.
  If ratio falls at N=4096 vs N=1024 (as v2 data suggests: 2.8x at N=4096 vs 17x at N=1024):
