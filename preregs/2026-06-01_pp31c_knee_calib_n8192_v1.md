## Hypothesis

Precision-coverage knee is stable across seeds at N=8192. Prior PP31-2A MIDDLE_BAND
(avg_knee=0.740, 2/5 seeds at HP) -- knee detection exists but is seed-dependent.

## Pre-registered bands

- HARD-PASS: knee_std < 0.05 AND avg_knee in [0.65, 0.85] AND >= 4/5 seeds stable.
- MIDDLE: knee_std in [0.05, 0.15] OR avg_knee outside [0.65, 0.85] OR only 2-3/5 seeds stable.
- HARD-FAIL: knee_std > 0.15 OR avg_knee outside [0.50, 0.95] OR < 2/5 seeds stable.

## Design

N=8192. M in {50, 100, 200, 500}. tau_grid: 25 points in [0.20, 0.90].
200 noisy queries per (seed, M, tau). Overlap-based score. 5 seeds (smoke: 2).
