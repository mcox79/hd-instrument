# Pre-reg: Wave 14 Bet V meta-cognition at N=65536 v1

**Filed:** 2026-05-22
**Source:** `strategy_request_to_exp_dev_BetY_V2D_mechanism_revision_2026-05-22.md` (Strategy 13:15 EDT) — V2.D Phase 1 sub-test #5.

## Question

At N=65536, does the substrate's confidence margin gap (stored_conf − unstored_conf) exceed 0.424, extending cycle 103's N-scaling result (0.285 → 0.424)?

## Hypothesis

H_scale: gap > 0.424 — substrate's meta-cognition (self-knowledge) scales with N. Aligns with V2.D Phase 1 substrate-scale-up pivot.

H_null: gap stays ≤ 0.30 — V2.D N-scaling thesis fails for Bet V.

## Pre-declared verdicts

- `BET_V_N65K_PASS` — gap ≥ 0.424.
- `BET_V_N65K_PARTIAL` — 0.30 ≤ gap < 0.424.
- `BET_V_N65K_KILLED` — gap < 0.30.
- `BET_V_N65K_INCONCLUSIVE` — metric collection error.

## Method

Reuses `betv.run_one_seed` from validated `exp_wave14_betV_largeN.py`:
- Store 100 random (s, r, o) triples in M = sign(Σ entity[s]·relation[r]·entity[o]).
- Stored queries: probe = M · entity[s] · relation[r] for 50 stored facts; confidence = (top1_sim − top2_sim) / |top1|.
- Unstored queries: 50 random (s, r) NOT in stored facts; same confidence.
- gap = mean(stored_conf) − mean(unstored_conf).
- 3 seeds: [17, 23, 31].

## Acceptance thresholds

- 0.424 = cycle 103 largeN extrapolation target.
- 0.30 lower bound = "still substantial separation".

## Config

- N=8192 smoke, 65536 full.
- num_entities=200, num_relations=20.
- num_facts=100, n_probes=50.
- 3 seeds full.

- **KILLED**: N-scaling fails on Bet V. Either Bet V is N-saturated at N=4096 OR cycle 103 measurement was anomalous.
