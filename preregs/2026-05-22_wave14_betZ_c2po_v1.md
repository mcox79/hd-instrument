## Question

Does a Glauber-MC substrate, primed with cue1 = c·ξ_A then cue2 = c·ξ_B after a delay, reveal pattern-pair coupling in the differential overlap (echo) vs single-cue baseline?

Research's hypothesis (from Jonsson 2001 + Jalabert-Pastawski 2001 classical analogs):
- Diagonal A=B: positive echo (auto-recall).
- Off-diagonal orthogonal patterns: echo ≈ 0 (noise floor).
- Off-diagonal correlated patterns (planted overlap ~0.5): nonzero echo (cross-coupling).

## Hypothesis

H_pass: all 3 conditions satisfied (diagonal > 0.10, off-orthogonal mean |echo| ≤ 0.05, off-correlated mean |echo| ≥ 0.05). Substrate-novel diagnostic confirmed.

H_kill: diagonal echo < 0.05 — substrate doesn't even auto-recall under 2-cue protocol; cue mechanism broken.

## Method

1. Generate K random bipolar patterns. Inject n_corr_pairs pairs by setting pattern[K-i] = sign(0.5·pattern[i] + 0.5·noise) — corr_overlap ≈ 0.5.
2. Build Hopfield W = (1/N) Σ patterns_μ patterns_μ^T; zero diagonal.
3. For each pair (A, B), run **baseline** (cue1 only) and **probe** (cue1, delay, cue2); echo = probe_overlap_B − baseline_overlap_B.
4. Average echoes across n_reps random initial states.
5. Report: diagonal mean (A=B), off-orthogonal mean |echo|, off-correlated mean |echo|.

## Acceptance thresholds

- 0.10 diagonal — substrate auto-recall strong.
- 0.05 off-orthogonal upper bound — noise floor.
- 0.05 off-correlated lower bound — pair coupling > noise.
- 0.05 broken threshold — cue mechanism failed.
