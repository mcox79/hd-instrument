## Hypothesis

lambda_max of W obeys Marchenko-Pastur upper edge lambda_plus = (1+sqrt(alpha))^2
with relative error < 10% at N=32768 and alpha in {0.01, 0.02, 0.05}.

## Pre-registered bands

- HARD-PASS: |lambda_max - lambda_plus| / lambda_plus < 0.10 AND sigma_dev < 3
             in >= 4/5 seeds for all alpha.
- MIDDLE: 10-20% relative error OR 3-10 sigma deviation.
- HARD-FAIL: > 20% relative error OR > 10 sigma (MP law violated).

Calibration probe: no prior anchor at N=32768. Bands +-50% per policy.

Gram matrix approach: G = Pats @ Pats^T (M x M), lambda_max(W) = lambda_max(G/N).
