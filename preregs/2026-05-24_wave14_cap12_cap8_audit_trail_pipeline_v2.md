## Purpose

Re-run the Composition A audit-trail anchor with the now-available SRHT +
Hadamard iterate traces (generated by Anchor 1). Compute Spearman
rho(kappa_n divergence component, Schur-Weyl irrep (n)-mass deviation)
for ALL 4 families: Kerdock, SRHT, Hadamard, RM(1,m), at N=4096 / 5 seeds.

## What's new vs v1

- **Iterate-trajectory cross-check.** Loads Cap 8 VAMP iterate traces for
  SRHT + Hadamard (from Anchor 1). Adds an iterate-derived fingerprint
  vector I_n = sum_i (mse_iter[i] - mse_se_pred)^2 * (i+1)^n for n=2..5,
  to break the Schur-Weyl tie that produced NaN on these families in v1.
- **Robust Spearman.** TIED-family detection (stddev < 1e-9 on either
  fingerprint vector) reports `tied=True` and routes to middle band
  rather than NaN-killing the family.
- **File-exists + retry loop.** v2 waits up to 15 min for Anchor 1's
  iterate files; falls back to v1-style spectrum-only fingerprint if
  Anchor 1 fails / is slow.

## Pairing (unchanged from v1)

For each codebook family C and order n in {2..5}:
- `x_n(C) = | kappa_n_emp(C) - kappa_n_MP |` (kappa divergence)
- `y_n(C) = | schur_mass_(n)_emp(C) - schur_mass_(n)_MP |`

When iterate trace is available for (C, alpha=1.0, seed):
- append `I_n(C)` to x-vector and `log1p(|I_n(C)|)` to y-vector

Compute Spearman rho on the combined vectors per seed, then aggregate.

## HARD PASS (Composition A LICENSED)

- `rho_aggregate >= 0.60` in >= 3 of 4 hard families
- AND no family with `rho < 0.30`
- AND no family is TIED (i.e., the iterate-derived component DID break ties
  where needed)

## HARD FAIL (Composition A KILLED)

- `rho < 0.30` in >= 2 of 4 hard families with finite rho.

## MIDDLE BAND

- Anything else. Composition stays plausible per-family but does not
  elevate; per-family annotation tightening required.

## Dependency on Anchor 1

This experiment expects iterate trace files under
`data/exp_wave14_cap8_vamp_iterates_srht_hadamard_v1/iterates/`.
The runner runs serially per-queue, so Anchor 1 should complete first.
v2 includes a 15-min file-exists wait as defense-in-depth; if Anchor 1
fails entirely, v2 falls back to spectrum-only mode and reports that in
`summary.codebook_results[*].iterate_status_per_seed`.

## Open questions / risks

- Schur-Weyl masses for near-MP spectra can be near-tied across n; v1's
  NaN-on-SRHT problem was likely this tie. v2 adds the iterate-derived
  fingerprint to break this tie. If the iterate-derived component is
  ITSELF near-tied (all iterations converge identically near se_pred),
  the family is reported as TIED and the verdict drops to middle band.
- If RM(1,m) requires iterate traces too (because it's also affected by
  the Schur-Weyl tie), v2 will tie-flag it without breaking; this needs
  a v3 follow-up to extend Anchor 1 to RM(1,m).
