`actual` never cancels, so the metric MOVES with per-seed telemetry) scored **vs a RE-FIT QUADRATIC** (`a +
b*phi + c*phi^2` re-fit on the SAME low landed folds fill<=0.3516) at the **FAR/CRITICAL folds**. The VET

depths while the bounded a-priori mech law stays directionally correct. So the honest competitor is beaten
WHERE IT MATTERS (far out of range).

Forward-split is LEAKAGE-CLEAN: mech AND quad fit on the landed low folds (fill <= OOR_SPLIT=0.3516) ONLY;
`collT` is the closed-form a-priori birthday collision (seed-independent); only `actual` varies per seed. No

### Note on the "crossing"
Empirically the mech law is a PERSISTENT OVERSHOOTER (mech_pred > actual out to fill 1.05) -- `actual` does
NOT cross `mech_pred`. What crosses is the QUAD competitor: it plunges through the actuals and through zero.
The percolation-critical band is therefore where the honest competitor becomes physically absurd (quad_pred
<= 0), which is the "leaves the overshoot/lookup regime" event for the mech-vs-quad comparison. This is the
honest empirical finding, reported as such (not forced).
