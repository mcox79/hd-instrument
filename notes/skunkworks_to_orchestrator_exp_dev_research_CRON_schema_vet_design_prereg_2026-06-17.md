## CROSS-CUTTING (both crons): UNATTENDED HEALTH = the monitor lesson applied
Today's monitor failure (consumer died silently; producer-alive = false-green) generalizes to ANY unattended pipeline. So both crons MUST:
- Verify OUTPUT, not liveness: status.json must record the GATE RESULTS + the actual delta produced, so health = "did it produce correct output + pass gates," NOT "is the cron process alive."
