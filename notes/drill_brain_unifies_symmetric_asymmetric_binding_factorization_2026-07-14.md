## HEADLINE

The brain's answer is **not** "one population does both" and **not** "two subsystems that only meet at the very end" — it is a **two-stage hybrid**: (1) structurally SEPARATE construction of a content code and a role/structural code (TEM's `x` vs `g`), (2) BOUND into ONE combined vector via conjunctive coding (hippocampal `p ≈ g⊗x`, or the TPR sum-of-role-filler-binds `Σ rᵢ⊗fᵢ`), from which (3) **different LINEAR PROJECTIONS of the SAME combined population extract either the order-invariant/abstract signal or the nonlinear/conjunctive signal** — this is not conjecture, it is the direct, well-replicated finding of Bernardi, Benna, Rigotti, Munuera, Fusi & Salzman, *Cell* 2020, "The Geometry of Abstraction in the Hippocampus and Prefrontal Cortex": the SAME population in both HPC and PFC simultaneously carries a low-dimensional abstract/generalizable format and high-dimensional nonlinear mixed-selectivity terms, separable only by which linear readout you apply. **Verdict: JOINT CODE, SELECTIVE READOUT** — not physically separate subsystems composing downstream, and not a single fused representation with no internal structure either. Asymmetry itself lives predominantly in the STRUCTURAL CODE'S TRANSITION DYNAMICS (TEM's non-commutative per-relation transition matrices `Wa`) or, in a separate unreconciled account, in additive representational geometry read out linearly (Lippl et al. 2024 PNAS) — this specific sub-question is genuinely **contested/unresolved** in the literature, not settled.

## (a) How the brain holds BOTH channels — separate-subsystems-composed-at-readout vs one-conjunctive-code

**Evidence-backed answer: a hybrid, and the hybrid is the point.**

- **Separate construction, different regions.** Lateral entorhinal cortex builds environment-specific *content* (`x`); medial entorhinal cortex builds a reusable *structural/relational* code (`g`) via action/relation-specific transition matrices `g_{t+1} = f(Wa, g_t)` (Whittington et al. 2020, *Cell*, Tolman-Eichenbaum Machine). These are architecturally distinct pathways feeding the hippocampus.
- **Bound into one conjunctive code downstream.** Hippocampus forms `p ≈ g⊗x` (an outer-product/Hebbian conjunction) — this is a single bound representation, not two vectors kept apart. Hippocampal place/landmark cells are literally this bound conjunction and, notably, do NOT generalize across environments the way MEC's `g` does — the content-specificity is baked in at the bind step.
- **At the DG/CA3-vs-MEC anatomical level, the systems ARE genuinely separate** (not just a data-analysis lens): DG-specific lesions abolish fine-grained rate remapping/pattern separation in CA3 while sparing MEC-driven global remapping — a real, lesion-dissociable, hierarchical two-stage architecture (Leutgeb et al. 2007, *Science*; 2025 bioRxiv DG-CA3 remapping study, preprint, unreviewed).
- **Within MEC, "conjunctive grid × head-direction cells"** (Sargolini et al. 2006, *Science*; Gerlei et al. 2016, *PLoS ONE*, n=157 conjunctive cells, preferred directions periodically clustered at ~36° increments) are real, replicated, single-unit evidence of ONE cell type carrying two tuning dimensions at once — **but this is multiplicative GAIN MODULATION of a periodic spatial code by a scalar heading variable, not a fused symmetric-AND-plus-asymmetric-order algebraic code** in the sense our substrate needs. Treat this as a narrower, adjacent phenomenon, not direct proof of the general coexistence claim.
- **At the readout level, the strongest and most directly relevant finding of this whole drill**: Bernardi et al. 2020 show the SAME PFC and hippocampal populations carry both an abstract/order-invariant/generalizable geometric format AND nonlinear mixed-selectivity terms that support conjunction/XOR-type decoding, extractable from the identical population by choosing different linear projections. This directly answers "coexist in the same population" — yes, at the level of population geometry and multi-purpose linear readout, not at the level of "one neuron does both algebraically."

**Honest calibration:** no single paper directly tests "separate subsystems merge downstream" against "one joint code, selective readout" in a controlled head-to-head. The synthesis above is our own integration of separately-documented pieces (TEM's construction stage, DG/CA3-vs-MEC anatomical separation, Bernardi's joint-population/selective-readout finding) — each piece is independently well-evidenced, but their combination into one pipeline is a research synthesis, not a directly-cited unified account. Flag accordingly in the P_deflated below.

## (b) Where does the asymmetry physically live?

**Contested — three independent accounts, not reconciled in the literature:**

1. **In the structural code's transition dynamics** (TEM): `g`'s per-relation transition matrices `Wa` are explicitly non-commutative — the paper's own worked example: "uncle" must produce the same change in `g` as "father-then-brother," but a DIFFERENT change than "brother-then-father." Asymmetry lives in the ORDER OF MATRIX APPLICATION to `g`, not in the `g⊗x` bind itself (the bind just preserves whatever `g` already carries).
2. **In the plasticity/learning-rule symmetry, architecturally split by hippocampal subfield** (2024/2025 successor-representation line of work): a SYMMETRIC local learning rule produces a symmetrized-transition-matrix successor representation proposed as a model of **CA3** (order-invariant); an ASYMMETRIC (temporally-ordered, STDP-like) learning rule reproduces the classical forward-biased successor representation proposed as a model of **CA1** (order-sensitive). This would mean symmetric and asymmetric grid-like codes are built by architecturally SEPARATE subfields using different plasticity rules on the same underlying spatial substrate — a genuinely different locus (in the learning rule, not the transition matrix or the bind) than account (1).
3. **In additive representational geometry exploited by a linear readout, independent of grid/entorhinal machinery** (Lippl et al. 2024, *PNAS*, "A mathematical theory of relational generalization in transitive inference"): transitivity (A>B>C ⟹ A>C) emerges automatically from item-wise ADDITIVE representations `g(X,Y) = g₁(X) + g₂(Y)`, reducible to a scalar rank; a single "conjunctivity factor" governs how well a linear readout on a FIXED representation supports transitive inference, independent of any grid-cell mechanism at all.

**None of these three papers cites or reconciles the other two.** This is a genuinely open question — do not let the substrate design below overclaim a settled answer here. What IS consistent across all three: asymmetry is never claimed to live in a symmetric elementwise bind operator itself — it always requires either an order-sensitive transition/composition rule, an order-sensitive plasticity rule, or an order-sensitive readout/rank-extraction, i.e. asymmetry has to be injected somewhere upstream or downstream of a plain symmetric multiply, never emerging from the plain multiply.

## (c) How the two channels are combined/gated at readout

**Evidence for three mechanisms, each real but demonstrated in different task contexts — no paper runs the exact "AND-plus-order" combination task this drill asks about:**

- **Gating/selection is real** (Malik et al. 2022, *Cell*: direct monosynaptic PFC→hippocampal GABAergic projections onto VIP interneurons causally gate hippocampal signal-to-noise; Barbosa et al. 2023, *Nat. Commun.*: mPFC top-down input gates which stimulus dimension drives downstream cortical readout) — but these are demonstrated for SELECTING relevant-vs-irrelevant information under context, not for combining two SIMULTANEOUSLY-NEEDED signal types. Theta-phase PFC-hippocampal locking (Siapas et al. 2005; Jones & Wilson 2005) is a plausible routing substrate but the evidence is correlational (tracks task engagement), not a demonstrated hard switch.
- **Joint conjunctive coding upstream is the dominant THEORETICAL account** for exactly this "need both abstract-invariant and specific-conjunctive information" problem (Bernardi et al. 2020; Rigotti et al. 2013, *Nature*) — because it avoids needing an explicit combination step: both signals are already present in the same population, extractable by parallel linear readouts running concurrently, not sequentially gated.
- **Additive/weighted combination has some support** at the level of value integration (vmPFC mixture-of-experts weighting of multiple predictive streams, PubMed 33444700) but the strongest-claimed "additive compositional concept combination" claims (the familiar "king − man + woman" style vmPFC story) could NOT be pinned to a single verified primary citation this cycle — flag as weakly sourced, do not build on it.

**Honest synthesis:** the field's center of gravity favors joint-conjunctive-code-with-parallel-linear-readouts as the general-purpose account for "need both at once," with gating reserved for context-dependent stream SELECTION (different problem: choosing which channel matters right now, not combining two channels that both matter right now).

## (d) Ranked design recommendation — two-channel combined code for a shared-code+readout VSA substrate

**Core insight from this drill, directly buildable:** the coexistence trick used throughout the literature (TPR, TEM, Bernardi 2020) is always **"asymmetric bind first, symmetric aggregation/pooling second, then multiple readout lenses on the aggregate"** — never a single symmetric OR asymmetric primitive doing everything. Our substrate already has the two primitives this needs (bind = asymmetric if role-keyed; bundle/addition = symmetric aggregation) — the fix is a COMPOSITION RECIPE plus learned readout heads, not a new operator.

| Rank | Design | Mechanism | CONSTRUCT vs LEARNABLE | Brain analog |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Shared combined vector, dual linear-lens readout.** Build `z = (r1⊙a + r2⊙b) + λ·(a⊙b)` where `r1,r2` are fixed role vectors (asymmetric TPR-style term, order-sensitive) bundled additively with a role-free elementwise product term (symmetric/configural term). Read out order info via **unbind-by-specific-role** (project onto the `r1,r2` subspace); read out configural/AND-presence info via a **separate learned projection** tuned to the `a⊙b` subspace. | `r1⊙a+r2⊙b` = TPR sum-of-role-filler-binds (asymmetric); `a⊙b` = existing symmetric bind; `+` = existing bundle (already-native VSA primitive, no new operator) | Role vectors + bind + bundle: **CONSTRUCT** (near-zero cost, reuses existing primitives). Per-channel readout projections: **LEARNABLE** (two small trained linear heads on the same underlying `z`) | Directly mirrors Bernardi et al. 2020: same population (`z`), different linear projections extract abstract-invariant vs nonlinear-conjunctive signal. Also mirrors TEM's `p=g⊗x` bind-then-read pattern and TPR's sum-then-query-by-role pattern. |
| **2** | **Shared learned-bilinear head with tied-vs-untied projections.** Extend the prior drill's Rank-1 mechanism (`z=(Pa)⊙(Qb)`, learned low-rank bilinear bind) with TWO readout heads sharing the same `a,b`: a **tied head** `P=Q` (elementwise-commutative regardless of `a,b` order → this IS the symmetric/configural channel, order-invariant by construction) and an **untied head** `P≠Q` (order-sensitive → asymmetric channel). One learned mechanism, two heads, cheap incremental swap from the already-recommended Rank-1 fix. | Tied `(Pa)⊙(Pb)` vs untied `(Pa)⊙(Qb)` bilinear pooling (Kim et al. 2016) | Both heads: **LEARNABLE** (P, Q trained end-to-end; tying/untying is a hyperparameter choice = CONSTRUCT decision about which head gets which structure) | Same brain grounding as prior drill's Rank 1 (parietal gain fields, PFC rank×identity cells) plus Bernardi 2020's dual-lens-on-one-population logic |

| **4** | **Fully separate parallel vectors + downstream combiner** (fallback if 1-3 fail). Two independent vectors — one order-sensitive (TPR-style), one order-invariant (symmetric product) — combined only at a final concatenation+MLP stage. | Two parallel channels, no shared vector | Vectors: **CONSTRUCT**; combiner: **LEARNABLE** | DG/CA3-vs-MEC anatomical separation (genuinely separate subsystems, lesion-dissociable) | Most conservative, most brain-precedented AT THE ANATOMICAL-SEPARATION level, but loses the cheap shared-storage/shared-readout efficiency of ranks 1-2 and is the literature's model for when genuine architectural separation (not just readout selection) is required. |

**Why rank 1 over rank 4:** the DG/CA3-vs-MEC separation is real, but it separates CONTENT-construction from STRUCTURE-construction (upstream of binding) — not the two READOUT channels downstream of a bind. Once bound (`p=g⊗x`), the hippocampal representation is read by MULTIPLE downstream consumers via different projections (Bernardi 2020), not physically re-split into two vectors. Rank 1 reproduces this: separate CONSTRUCTION (role vectors vs content vectors, already how our substrate assigns role-keys) → single BOUND vector → multiple LEARNED READOUT LENSES. Rank 4 is the correct fallback only if empirical interference between the two lens-readouts is observed (see decisive test below), mirroring the brain's own fallback to genuine anatomical separation where a shared-population account would not suffice (Rudy & Sutherland's contested hippocampal-necessity debate, cited in the prior drill, is exactly this kind of "does one shared system suffice, or is dedicated separate machinery required" empirical question, unresolved even in biology).

## Cheap decisive test

Build `z = (r1⊙a + r2⊙b) + λ·(a⊙b)` (Rank 1) in the shared-code+readout harness already used for the prior drill's Rank-1/2/3 tests. From the SAME stored `z`, measure (i) order-discrimination accuracy via unbind-by-role query, and (ii) configural/AND-presence accuracy via the learned role-agnostic projection, on the SAME held-out relation set used previously. Compare each to its ISOLATED-channel baseline (order-only vector with no `λ·(a⊙b)` term added; configural-only vector with no role-keyed term added) to measure cross-channel interference.

**One line for the harness:** log both channel accuracies from a single `z` and diff each against its isolated-channel control; if degradation stays small, ship rank 1; if it's large, escalate straight to rank 4 (separate vectors) rather than iterating gate designs (rank 3), since the brain literature gives no strong reason to expect gating alone fixes genuine representational interference.

## Falsifiable predictions

- **HARD-PASS (Rank 1, joint shared-vector design):** order-discrimination accuracy >= 0.70 via unbind-by-role query **AND** configural/presence accuracy >= 0.70 via the role-agnostic projection, **both simultaneously from the SAME stored `z`**, with <= 10% relative degradation on EACH channel vs. its isolated-channel control (i.e., adding the second channel doesn't meaningfully hurt the first).
- **HARD-FAIL (Rank 1):** either channel <= 0.20 when read from the shared vector, OR > 25% relative degradation vs. isolated-channel baselines on either channel (genuine destructive interference between the two lenses) -> escalate to Rank 4 (physically separate vectors + downstream combiner), skipping Rank 3 (gating is not brain-evidenced as a fix for THIS specific failure mode).
- **HARD-PASS (Rank 2, tied/untied bilinear heads):** tied-head accuracy on configural/AND task >= 0.70 AND untied-head accuracy on order task >= 0.70, using the SAME underlying `a,b` representations (only the P/Q head differs).
- **HARD-FAIL (Rank 2):** either head <= 0.20, or tying `P=Q` fails to reach configural HARD-PASS even though the untied head reaches order HARD-PASS (would indicate symmetry-by-tying is not sufficient and a genuinely different symmetric mechanism, e.g. plain elementwise product per prior drill, is still needed for the configural channel).

## Substrate-product implications

If Rank 1 clears HARD-PASS, this closes the architectural wall named in the original trigger with close to zero new operators: reuse existing bind + bundle primitives in a specific composition order (role-keyed bind, then additive bundle with a symmetric-product term), plus two small trained readout heads. This is a genuine unification, not a compromise between the two prior separate fixes — both channels become simultaneously queryable from ONE stored representation, which is a real storage and readout-efficiency win over maintaining two fully separate parallel representations (Rank 4). If Rank 1 hard-fails via cross-channel interference, the brain's own DG/CA3-vs-MEC precedent says this is not surprising — genuine architectural separation between structure-construction and content-construction pathways, composed only via a late bind, may be required, and Rank 4 is a well-precedented (if less efficient) fallback, not a research dead-end. Either outcome is informative: a HARD-FAIL for Rank 1 quantifies exactly how much of the brain's DG/CA3-vs-MEC anatomical separation is load-bearing (interference-avoidance) versus how much of Bernardi's shared-population account was a favorable special case of the specific tasks that paper tested.
