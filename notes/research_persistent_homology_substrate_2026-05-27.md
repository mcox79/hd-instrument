Overall P(TDA framing yields NEW predictive content beyond phase-class framework) = 0.30.**
>
> The eight headline findings:
>
> 1. **(Q1 — TDA framing) The natural persistent homology computation on substrate W is the Vietoris-Rips filtration on W's row vectors in R^N, equivalently the lower-star filtration on the weighted graph with W as edge weights.** Lit precedent: Rieck et al. "Neural Persistence" (ICLR 2019, arxiv.org/abs/1812.09764) computes persistent homology of NN weight matrices by sorting weights and tracking topological feature birth/death. Naitzat-Zhitnikov-Lim "Topology of Deep Neural Networks" (JMLR 2020, arxiv.org/abs/2004.06093) estimates Betti numbers per layer using lower-star filtration on activation point clouds. Wang-Nguyen-Wei "Persistent Spectral Graph" (PSG, 2020, arxiv.org/abs/1912.04135) shows harmonic 0-eigenvalues of the q-persistent Laplacian RECOVER the persistence barcode — this is the BRIDGE between TDA and the spectral-graph machinery already familiar from Bet I free-probability + Pattern 7 cross-family-consistency. **No new physics commitment required; the W matrix is the same object analyzed in spectral and free-prob frames, just viewed through a topological filtration index.**
>
> 2. **(Q2 — Betti numbers) Substrate W's 1024-node weighted graph viewed through a weight-threshold filtration will show a Betti-0 (connected components) trajectory that starts at b_0 = N (all singletons) at threshold τ = max(W) and decreases monotonically to b_0 = 1 (single connected component) as τ → min(W).** The KEY ANALYTIC QUESTION: at what filtration threshold does b_0 first drop to a small number (3, 4, or "few"), and does that threshold MATCH the cosine-similarity threshold below which substrate retention drops from G1 (≥0.94) to G2 (≥0.74)? **Calibrated lit-supported prediction: persistent spectral graph theory (Wang-Nguyen-Wei 2020) guarantees that the algebraic multiplicity of the 0-eigenvalue of the graph Laplacian filtered at threshold τ equals b_0(τ). So this is computable in O(N^2.4) via standard eigendecomposition at each threshold; no new TDA library beyond what's already available.**  The 4-tier retention taxonomy MIGHT correspond to 4 connected components at a specific τ — but the user's hypothesis (plateaus reflect topological features WITHOUT phase-class framing) is **partially redundant** with the existing free-prob Marchenko-Pastur analysis, because spectral gap and Betti-0 are isomorphic information for graph Laplacians at threshold τ. So this is a **consistency check, not a novel diagnostic**.
>
> 3. **(Q2 b_1, b_2) Higher Betti numbers (1-cycles, 2-cavities) are the CANDIDATE NOVEL CONTENT.** b_0 just counts components (redundant with spectral gap). But b_1 (1-cycles) captures **loop structure in the weighted graph** — e.g., do substrate's stored patterns form *holes* in W's filtration that close at intermediate τ, indicating cyclic correlation structure absent from random Hopfield? Naitzat et al. show that random networks have small b_1; structured networks (trained with backprop, or with structured codebook) have non-trivial b_1 persistence. **Prediction (lit-deflated): substrate's Kerdock-codebook-stored W should show b_1 persistence bars that are LONGER (more persistent) than a random-codebook W of matched α. This is a single CPU experiment on existing W artifacts (~5-15 min for N=1024 using ripser).** HARD-PASS: substrate's longest b_1 bar is ≥ 1.5× the longest b_1 bar of a Hopfield-random control at matched (N, α). HARD-FAIL: substrate b_1 persistence bars are statistically indistinguishable from random control. Calibrated P(HARD-PASS) = 0.35 (Kerdock has algebraic structure; literature on Kerdock TDA is sparse, calibration penalty applied).
>
> 4. **(Q3 — Persistent homology of retention dynamics) Substrate retention through Phase-B/C is a trajectory in W-space.** The "filtrations indexed by attracting levels" framework (Galindo-Hugo, arxiv.org/abs/2506.18250, 2025) gives a NEW filtration that quantifies attractor sensitivity. **Prediction: if substrate's 3-plateau structure reflects topological attractor stratification, the persistence diagram of the W trajectory through Phase-B/C should show 3-4 long-lived persistence bars (long-lived = lifetime > 0.3 in normalized filtration) and a noise floor of short-lived bars. The number of long-lived bars matches the number of plateaus.** HARD-PASS: 3 ± 1 long bars + short-bar noise floor. HARD-FAIL: continuous persistence-bar distribution with no gap (suggests plateaus are NOT topologically encoded). Calibrated P(HARD-PASS) = 0.30 (this is the most NOVEL framing — would be a meaningful new diagnostic if true; calibration penalty heavy because no direct precedent for "trajectory persistence diagram predicts plateau count").
>
> 5. **(Q4 — MoE SHIFT vs PARTITION topological signature) THIS IS THE STRONGEST CANDIDATE NOVEL DIAGNOSTIC.** When MoE substrate carries E expert W-blocks (PARTITION mode) the combined W is approximately BLOCK-DIAGONAL with weak off-block coupling; persistent homology on this object via the weighted-graph filtration will show b_0 = E for a *range* of filtration thresholds τ ∈ [τ_inter-expert, τ_intra-expert]. If the experts share W structure (SHIFT mode), the combined W is approximately rank-deficient with rank ≤ rank(single-expert-W), and b_0 drops to 1 at the same τ where a single-expert W would. **The DISCRIMINATOR: width of the τ-interval over which b_0 = E**. PARTITION: width is finite and equals (approximately) the inter-expert coupling magnitude. SHIFT: width is zero (b_0 jumps from N to 1 with no E-plateau). **This is COMPLEMENTARY to free-additive top-edge ratio + DMPK SVD-bimodality**: those diagnostics work on the SVD/spectrum side; the TDA diagnostic works on the graph-connectivity side. **For the SHIFT vs PARTITION classification of v200-era MoE experiments, this is a re-analysis on existing W artifacts (~5 min CPU per experiment).** HARD-PASS for diagnostic-utility: TDA call agrees with free-additive call on ≥4/5 known SHIFT-or-PARTITION test cases; the b_0-plateau width is monotonic in inter-expert coupling. HARD-FAIL: TDA disagrees with both free-additive and DMPK on >1 case (suggests TDA captures noise, not signal). Calibrated P(this diagnostic ships and adds discriminatory power) = **0.38**.
>
> 6. **(Q5 — Specific plateau heights from TDA) Can persistence bar BIRTH/DEATH TIMES predict the 0.94/0.74/0.60 plateau heights?** This is the most ambitious and LEAST-supported prediction. Mechanism: in the lower-star filtration of W with cosine-distance metric, the birth time of the kth long persistence bar corresponds to the cosine threshold at which the kth connected component merges. **If the 4-tier retention taxonomy is structural, the merge thresholds in cosine space should map (via a known monotone retention-vs-cosine relation) to the plateau retention values.** Saad-Solla saddle-cascade BIC-favors equal spacing in saddle index but DOES NOT predict specific retention values; TDA via the cosine-Vietoris-Rips filtration could. **Calibrated lit-deflated P(TDA predicts specific plateau heights within ±0.05) = 0.12.** Why so low: this requires (a) the retention-vs-cosine map to be monotone and calibrated, (b) the long persistence bars to align with class boundaries (not bisect them), and (c) no finite-N rounding effects. All three are simultaneously testable but the joint probability is low. HARD-PASS: predicted heights within ±0.05 of observed. HARD-FAIL: predicted heights off by ≥0.10 on ≥2 of 3 plateaus.
>
> 7. **(Q6 — Falsifier / cheapest CPU probe) Pure re-analysis of existing W matrices, ~15-30 min CPU total for all five probes (TDA-A through TDA-E).** TDA on a 1024×1024 dense W matrix: using `ripser` or `gudhi`, computing b_0, b_1 persistence diagrams from the lower-star filtration is O(N^2) memory + O(N^2.4) time at most; ~minutes wall-clock on a laptop. No GPU required; no new experiment ship required IF W artifacts from v200-era MoE runs are still on disk. **The companion exp_dev handoff (Q6/Q7 deliverable) ships a 5-probe TDA re-analysis cell that consumes existing W artifacts and emits {b_0(τ)-trajectory, longest-b_1-bar, b_0-plateau-width, persistence-diagram-trajectory, predicted-vs-observed plateau heights}.** No new physics commitment, no new W generation, no MoE re-run needed.
>
> 8. **(Q7 — Calibrated probabilities, summary)** P(TDA discriminates SHIFT vs PARTITION at zero compute, complementing free-additive + DMPK) = **0.38**; P(persistence bars predict specific plateau heights within ±0.05) = **0.12**; P(TDA reveals b_1 cycle structure that adds substrate-novel insight beyond spectral methods) = **0.22**; P(persistent Laplacian harmonic 0-eigenvalues recover documented-class signature already given by spectral gap) = **0.55** (this is consistency, not novelty); P(TDA framing yields NEW predictive content beyond phase-class framework) = **0.30**; P(TDA framing is REDUNDANT with existing spectral/free-prob diagnostics) = **0.45**; P(TDA framing is fully IRRELEVANT — does not even agree with existing diagnostics) = **0.25**. **The ORTHOGONAL/OVERLAPPING/IRRELEVANT call: TDA is OVERLAPPING with the SKAH-M / phase-class framework via the persistent-spectral-graph bridge (Wang-Nguyen-Wei 2020), with one genuinely orthogonal sub-question (SHIFT-vs-PARTITION diagnostic at zero compute).
