## HEADLINE

Two independent VET'd experiments (29537 MM, af29a98ef HARD_FAIL_2) converge on: given GOLD facts, combination via bind+settle reaches Challenge 0.696 (way above chance); given REAL retrieved facts, Challenge stays at chance (+0.046) because ~50% of misses are "the right fact was never retrieved." Our retrieval is flat single-shot cosine NN (`retrieval_pool()` in `experiments/exp_arc_aggregation_retriever_bindsettle_v1.py`, top-K=10 by `QQ @ SV_store.T`) -- exactly the "match the cue's surface form" failure mode the brain does NOT use for associative recall. Three convergent literatures (hippocampal pattern completion / modern Hopfield attractors, Collins & Loftus spreading activation / ACT-R fan effect / HippoRAG, and Tulving encoding-specificity + PFC discriminative retrieval) all point at the SAME missing ingredient: **retrieval must be relational (cue -> activate via typed links, not raw cosine) AND discriminative (relevant to the decision among choices, not merely similar to the stem)**. The single most direct brain-faithful analog with a strong empirical precedent is **HippoRAG** (Gutierrez et al., NeurIPS 2024) -- explicitly modeled on hippocampal indexing theory, using Personalized PageRank (PPR) over a knowledge graph seeded from query-linked entities, achieving +20pts on multi-hop QA specifically because it reaches passages with NO lexical overlap to the query. We ALREADY attempted a PPR/spreading-activation smoke test (`experiments/exp_ppr_spreading_activation_cpu_v1.py`, HARD_FAIL, recall=0.217) -- but it ran on a small (120-node) SYNTHETIC random graph with every triple superposed into ONE bundled HD vector, which the literature's own failure-precondition list (small/sparse/no-community-structure graphs, superposition crosstalk) predicts will fail regardless of whether spreading activation is the right mechanism. That HARD_FAIL is CONFOUNDED, not a refutation -- the fair test is on the REAL WorldTree fact-graph (4365 questions, mean 5.69 facts/question, already ingested, already typed KINDOF/SYNONYMY/IFTHEN/CAUSE/COUPLEDRELATIONSHIP/REQUIRES/AFFECT/USEDFOR).

## Biology synthesis

### 1. Content-addressable / attractor completion (hippocampal CA3, modern Hopfield)
CA3's recurrent collaterals form an autoassociative attractor network (Marr 1971; McNaughton & Morris 1987; Rolls & Treves): a partial cue is pulled by recurrent dynamics into the nearest stored attractor basin, completing to the full pattern. Dentate gyrus does the complementary job -- pattern SEPARATION -- keeping distinct memories from overlapping so CA3 has clean, non-interfering basins to complete into (O'Reilly & McClelland 1994 complementary learning systems; Yassa & Stark 2011). Modern Hopfield / dense associative memory (Hopfield 1982; Krotov & Hopfield 2016; Ramsauer et al. 2020 "Hopfield Networks is All You Need") shows attractor capacity and basin sharpness scale favorably (exponential in dimension for dense variants) -- but robustness to a REPHRASED cue (same meaning, different surface form) is NOT automatic: it is a property of the ENCODING the attractor operates over. If the encoding is lexical/surface, a paraphrased cue lands in the wrong basin no matter how good the attractor dynamics are; if the encoding is semantic/relational, the same dynamics succeed. Known failure mode: cross-talk / spurious attractors when a cue sits closer (in the network's metric) to the wrong stored pattern -- exactly our surface-lure problem (a distractor answer shares more surface features with the stem than the correct answer does, ARC-Challenge naive-overlap accuracy is measured BELOW chance at 0.197, per the ARC-CHALLENGE=SURFACE-TRAP finding already banked in the ingestion note).
**Read for us:** we already HAVE a cleanup/attractor primitive (`hdlab/hd_fact_store.py` `_cleanup()`, exact `(subj,rel)`-key argmax cleanup, round-trip 1.0). But it is EXACT-KEY cleanup (attractor over an identity key), not a FUZZY semantic attractor over paraphrased natural-language cues -- per 29531's own VET sharpening, "no fuzzy/surface-variant tested (USA vs United States)". So attractor completion, as currently built, is not the lever for THIS problem; it is a plausible SECONDARY robustness layer once the semantic-relational retrieval front-end (below) is in place, not the primary fix.

### 2. Spreading activation (Collins & Loftus 1975; ACT-R fan effect; HippoRAG)
Collins & Loftus (1975): activation starts at a cue node and spreads outward along LABELED relational links, with decay per hop/distance -- a concept several hops away, sharing zero surface features with the cue, can still be reached and activated above threshold if the RELATIONAL PATH exists. ACT-R declarative memory (Anderson & Lebiere) formalizes this with an activation equation combining base-level strength + spreading activation from ALL currently-active goal/context elements simultaneously; critically, the FAN EFFECT (Anderson 1974) means when TWO cues are both active (e.g. a question mentions concept X and concept Y), a fact connected to BOTH gets activation contributions that SUM -- multi-cue convergent activation is how the brain finds "the fact about X and Y" without either cue alone dominating. High-fan (hub) nodes dilute activation per outgoing link -- a documented failure mode requiring node-specificity / degree down-weighting (IDF-like discounting).
HippoRAG (Gutierrez et al., NeurIPS 2024) is the direct modern computational realization: explicitly framed as implementing hippocampal indexing theory, it runs Personalized PageRank seeded from query-linked entities over an LLM-extracted knowledge graph, reaching passages with no lexical overlap to the query; +20pts over standard dense retrieval on multi-hop QA specifically because multi-hop answers live behind relational bridges, not surface similarity. Its own documented failure preconditions: (a) seed/entity-linking quality is make-or-break -- garbage seed entities produce garbage PPR regardless of graph quality; (b) hub-node mass absorption needs explicit down-weighting; (c) the walk needs a graph with REAL density/community structure -- small, sparse, or randomly-generated graphs starve the walk or converge to noise, independent of whether the underlying mechanism is sound.
**Read for us:** our own `exp_ppr_spreading_activation_cpu_v1` HARD_FAIL (recall 0.217, conv_k 5.3) matches failure precondition (c) almost exactly -- VE=120 random entities, degree~2, ALL 240 triples superposed into one HD bundle `M` (superposition crosstalk on top of graph sparsity). This is not evidence against spreading activation; it is evidence FOR needing the REAL WorldTree graph (thousands of typed triples, real taxonomic/causal community structure) and a SHARDED (not superposed) representation, which the substrate's already-VET'd sharded fact store (29532/29534, per-fact independent bundles, no crosstalk to 1M facts) already provides.

### 3. Relevance as discriminative, not similarity-based (Tulving; Badre & Wagner; retrieval-induced forgetting)
Tulving & Thomson's encoding specificity principle (1973): retrieval success depends on overlap between encoding context and retrieval cue -- not on cue-target similarity considered in isolation. Badre & Wagner's work on PFC and controlled/strategic retrieval (and Moscovitch's "working with memory" account) show the brain does not just activate everything similar and stop -- there is a top-down, goal-biased SELECTION step among activated competitors, and retrieval-induced forgetting (Anderson & Levy) shows the brain actively SUPPRESSES competing-but-irrelevant activated items, not merely fails to retrieve them. In IR/ML terms this is the difference between similarity-based retrieval (top-K by cosine to the query alone) and DISCRIMINATIVE relevance (which facts, given the full candidate answer SET, actually separate the correct choice from the distractors) -- the same distinction contrastive/DPR-style retrieval formalizes computationally.
**Read for us:** our aggregation pipeline already encodes question+ALL choices (`QQ`) but the retrieval STEP only uses cosine-to-stem-ish `QQ @ SV_store.T` per-question-row without exploiting the multi-choice discriminative structure at retrieval time (discrimination currently happens only downstream, in the settle/score step, over whatever got retrieved). This is exactly the gap: relevance-gating should happen AT retrieval (fetch facts that discriminate among choices), matching Tulving/Badre-Wagner, not only at combination.

### 4. Iterative / constructive retrieval (Bartlett; Kintsch CI; IRCoT; Thagard ECHO)
Retrieval-as-reconstruction (Bartlett 1932 schemas; Kintsch's construction-integration, which the substrate's existing CI settle step already implements) and modern IRCoT (Trivedi et al. 2023) both treat retrieval as a MULTI-STEP process where each step's output re-seeds the next retrieval -- empirically IRCoT gains +11-21 recall points over one-shot retrieval on multi-hop benchmarks (this citation was already in our KB per `research_drill_iterative_multihop_where_it_works_5x_2026-06-08.md`). The known failure mode is error compounding: a bad first hop misguides everything downstream, so iterative methods need a verify-before-use step. Thagard's ECHO (1989/1998) explanatory-coherence model is a non-VSA precedent for exactly our CI settle dynamic (facts as nodes, support/contradiction as edges, coherence maximized by settling) -- already credited in the prior contradiction-representation research note.
**Read for us:** per the WorldTree task-shape diagnostic, ARC's aggregate support-sets are SHORT (mean 2.47 central facts, p90=5) -- this argues for 1-2 hop iterative expansion at most, not deep multi-hop chaining. khop.py's `traverse()` is built for exactly this kind of relation-path following but REQUIRES an explicit `start_entity` + `relation_path` list as input -- it cannot spread open-endedly from a natural-language question without an upstream entity/relation extraction step, which is a known soft spot (prior notes flag NER as "wrong tool" for this kind of extraction). This favors PPR/spreading-activation (which only needs seed ENTITIES, not full relation paths) over khop's directed traversal as the primary mechanism, with khop's Merkle-audit machinery reusable for producing an inspectable multi-hop audit trail once seeds are found.

## Concrete retrieval-design recommendation

Build **multi-cue Personalized-PageRank / spreading activation over the REAL WorldTree fact-graph, seeded by semantic entity-linking from question+ALL choices, with node-specificity down-weighting, followed by discriminative re-ranking over the candidate pool** -- combining lit-scan findings (2), (3), and using (1)/(4) as secondary/audit layers. Concretely, this is options (a) + (c) from the task's menu, with (b) khop reused only as the audit/traversal primitive once seeds exist (not as the seed-finding mechanism), and (d) attractor cleanup kept as the existing exact-key layer (not extended yet -- flagged as a future fuzzy-conflict lever, not this cell's job).

Pipeline:
1. **Seed identification (semantic, not lexical):** encode each content word/phrase in the question stem AND in every answer choice with SemanticHDEncoder; match against WorldTree graph node terms by cosine (meaning-match, not string-match) to get a seed-entity SET per question, tagged by which surface element (stem vs choice-i) it came from. This is the ACT-R "multiple simultaneously active cues" setup.
2. **Multi-cue spreading activation / PPR:** run personalized PageRank (or the substrate's damped-activation FHRR update, corrected for the crosstalk bug -- shard per-fact per 29532/29534, do not superpose into one bundle) from ALL seeds jointly. Per Anderson's fan-effect equation, activation from stem-seeds and choice-seeds SUMS on any fact-node connected to both -- this is exactly the multi-hop "bridge fact" the surface-cosine retriever misses. Node-specificity down-weight (divide contribution by log(degree) or an IDF-style term) to stop KINDOF/hub nodes from swamping activation (predicted failure mode from the HippoRAG lit-scan).
3. **Depth cap at 1-2 hops** (not open-ended): per the WorldTree task-shape diagnostic, aggregate support-sets are short (median 2 central facts, p90=5); over-spreading dilutes activation and risks the same fan-effect dilution the literature warns about.
4. **Discriminative re-rank (Tulving/Badre-Wagner layer):** from the activated candidate pool, select the facts whose presence most changes the RELATIVE score gap between the answer choices (i.e., facts that discriminate, not merely facts closest to the stem) -- operationalized as: for each candidate fact f, compute `discriminative_score(f) = max_choice_score(f) - second_max_choice_score(f)` and take top-K by this score, replacing (or blending with) the current pure `QQ @ SV_store.T` stem-cosine ranking.
5. **Feed the SAME existing bind+settle combination step** (unchanged) -- isolates retrieval as the one variable per the design-gate discipline.

This is NOT a from-scratch build: steps 1 and 4 reuse the already-banked SemanticHDEncoder and the aggregation cell's existing `QQ` multi-choice encoding; step 2 reuses the sharded HD fact store's crosstalk-free storage plus a corrected (non-superposed) version of the existing PPR smoke-test code; step 3 is a parameter constrained by an already-measured empirical fact (WorldTree support-set size); khop.py is available for audit-trail production once seeds are found, not required for the core mechanism.

## Cheap decisive test (can-fail cell design -- NOT dispatched)

**Candidate anchor name:** `arc_retrieval_multicue_ppr_discriminative_v1`

**Arms (one variable: the retrieval mechanism; combination step held fixed):**
- A. `baseline_single_shot` -- current `retrieval_pool()` top-K=10 stem-cosine (existing code, unchanged).
- B. `ppr_spreading_only` -- multi-cue PPR/spreading activation over the real WorldTree graph (steps 1-3 above), ranked by final activation mass, K=10.
- C. `ppr_plus_discriminative` -- B's candidate pool, re-ranked by discriminative_score (step 4), K=10.
- D. `shuffled_graph_control` -- same as B/C but with WorldTree graph edges randomly permuted (destroys real relational structure while preserving degree distribution) -- MUST collapse toward baseline A; if it does not, the "lift" isn't coming from relational structure (leak/artifact check).
- E. `hub_dilution_ablation` -- same as C but with node-specificity down-weighting DISABLED -- should show measurably worse recall than C if the down-weighting is doing real work (ablation, not decorative).

**Primary metric (per arm, per question):** recall@K of GOLD CENTRAL support facts against the WorldTree gold explanation graph (already parsed, `scratchpad/worldtree_taskshape_diag.py` precedent) -- this is measured recall against a real external gold standard, independent of end-to-end accuracy.
**Secondary metric:** end-to-end ARC accuracy (Easy + Challenge separately, per-grade where available) plugging each arm's retrieved pool through the UNCHANGED existing bind+settle combiner.

## Falsifiable predictions (HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL, pre-registered)

**HARD-PASS (mechanism):** arm C (or B) recall@central-facts >= baseline A recall + 0.15 absolute, AND arm D (shuffled graph) recall falls back to within 0.03 of baseline A (proving the lift is graph-structure-driven, not an accounting artifact), AND arm E (no down-weighting) recall is measurably lower than arm C (hub-dilution fix is load-bearing not decorative).

**HARD-PASS (end-to-end):** with the above mechanism confirmed, ARC-Challenge accuracy under arm C rises to a level statistically distinguishable from chance (binomial 95% CI excludes 0.25, roughly +0.08 or more at n~1119) -- this is the bar the prior aggregation cell's "+0.046 ~chance" result did NOT clear.

**MIDDLE-BAND:** recall lift positive (0.05-0.15) but shuffled-graph control does not fully collapse (partial confound), OR recall rises clearly but end-to-end Challenge stays flat (would mean retrieval was necessary-but-not-sufficient -- redirect to a SECOND diagnostic: is the aggregator now getting more RIGHT facts into a pool still large enough to dilute the settle step, i.e. K too high?).

**HARD-FAIL (mechanism):** arm C/B recall@central-facts does not exceed baseline A beyond noise (<0.05 absolute, not clearing a paired significance test), OR shuffled-graph control D performs equivalently to real-graph C/B (meaning whatever lift exists isn't from relational structure at all -- would REFUTE the spreading-activation hypothesis for this task, redirect to a different mechanism, e.g. the discriminative re-rank ALONE without the graph spread, isolating which of steps 2 vs 4 is doing the work).

**HARD-FAIL (seed quality, diagnostic sub-check):** if entity-linking / seed identification (step 1) itself has low precision/recall against WorldTree gold entity mentions (e.g. <0.5), that is a SEPARATE, prior-stage failure that would explain a HARD-FAIL on B/C without indicting the spreading mechanism itself -- must be measured and reported independently (per the HippoRAG lit-scan's "seed quality is make-or-break" precondition), so a HARD-FAIL is correctly attributed to seeding vs spreading vs re-ranking.

## Cross-thread synthesis

- **vs 29537 / af29a98ef:** both established combination-given-gold already works (Challenge 0.696) and end-to-end fails because retrieval misses ~50% of central facts. This design targets exactly that gap, leaving the (already-proven) bind+settle combiner untouched -- a clean one-variable follow-on, not a redesign of the working part.
- **vs the ARC-Challenge surface-trap finding:** naive word-overlap scores BELOW chance on Challenge (0.197) because distractors are designed to out-overlap the correct answer. Multi-cue spreading activation is specifically the brain mechanism that does NOT rely on surface overlap (Collins & Loftus: cue and target need share zero surface features if a relational path connects them) -- directly targets the surface-lure failure mode, not just a generic recall bump.
- **vs the WorldTree task-shape diagnostic:** ~40% of gold support is KINDOF/SYNONYMY lexical-taxonomic bridging, and central-fact count is short (median 2, within Cowan-4 working memory) -- both facts directly justify the depth-cap (1-2 hops, not khop-style long chains) and explain why a SemanticHDEncoder-based (meaning, not string) entity-linker is the right seeding mechanism (most bridges ARE taxonomic/synonym links, exactly what that encoder was built and VET'd for).
- **vs the contradiction-representation research (2026-07-24):** that drill found combination cannot yet resist a lure via CONTRADICTION signals (crude WordNet-antonym polarity net-corrupted, HARD_FAIL_2). This design does not depend on that unresolved thread -- discriminative re-ranking at RETRIEVAL time (picking facts that separate choices) is a different, retrieval-stage lever from settle-time polarity, and is testable independently.
- **vs the prior PPR HARD_FAIL:** re-diagnosed above as confounded by a synthetic sparse graph + single-bundle superposition, not evidence against the mechanism; the real-graph + sharded-store re-test is the fair version of that same test.

## Substrate-product implications

- If HARD-PASS: gives the substrate a brain-faithful, glass-box, inspectable multi-hop retrieval mechanism (seed entities + activation trace + audit chain via khop) that is auditable in a way opaque dense-retrieval embeddings are not -- direct product differentiator for the auditable-memory thesis (every retrieved fact traces back through an explicit relation path, not a black-box embedding similarity score).
- If HARD-FAIL on mechanism but seed-quality is also poor: identifies entity-linking (not spreading activation itself) as the true next bottleneck -- redirects effort precisely rather than concluding "retrieval doesn't work."
- Either way, the shuffled-graph and hub-dilution ablations are themselves reusable DIAGNOSTIC tools for any future graph-based retrieval mechanism on this substrate, not single-use scaffolding.
