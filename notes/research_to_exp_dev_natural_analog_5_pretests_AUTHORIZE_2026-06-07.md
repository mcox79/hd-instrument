## Authorize 5 cheap empirical pre-tests (one per analog)

### Analog 1: HIPPOCAMPAL — TMR priority gating pre-test
~2 hr CPU. Customer-flagged "important" queries get priority in sleep defrag
accumulation (Targeted Memory Reactivation analog from McClelland CLS framework).

Method: 100 queries; 20 flagged as "high-priority"; substrate sleep defrag prioritizes
these in aggregation. Measure: priority-flagged binding accumulation quality vs
unflagged baseline.

HARD-PASS: priority-flagged bindings achieve >= 1.5x retrieval F1 vs unflagged at
held-out queries on same topic.

### Analog 2: ANT COLONY — Time-windowed Misra-Gries decay pre-test
~30 min CPU. Add explicit pheromone decay to Misra-Gries counters (decay rate α).

Method: synthetic 10000-query stream; topic-distribution shift at query 5000
(simulates customer KB drift). Compare current Misra-Gries vs time-windowed (with
configurable decay rate).

HARD-PASS: time-windowed Misra-Gries identifies the shift faster (within 100 queries)
than current Misra-Gries (which may take 1000+ queries).

### Analog 3: IMMUNE — Per-source trust scoring pre-test
~1-2 hr CPU. Tag bindings with source confidence; high-trust sources override low-trust
on conflict (V(D)J negative selection analog).

Method: synthetic KB with 3 sources (high-trust customer docs; medium-trust LLM-distilled;
low-trust public web); inject contradictions; substrate audit identifies authoritative
source.

HARD-PASS: substrate correctly prefers high-trust source >= 95% of cases; flags
medium-vs-low conflicts for review.

### Analog 4: MYCORRHIZAL — Hub-weighted initialization pre-test
~2 hr Pythia-160M, $0 cloud. Established customer's (high-coverage substrate) routing
patterns serve as warm-start for new customer (hub-mediated transfer per mycorrhizal
mother-tree analog).

Method: simulate 2 customers; customer A has 10K queries accumulated; customer B is new.
Compare B's bridge coverage growth with vs without A's hub initialization.

HARD-PASS: B with hub initialization achieves 70%+ bridge coverage at Q=100 vs 30% cold.

### Analog 5: QUORUM SENSING — Signal-level adversarial detector EMA pre-test
~5-10 min CPU laptop, $0. Detect injection attacks via high-frequency semantically-
legitimate query flooding (Quorum Quenching analog).

Method: simulate customer query stream; inject adversarial signals (high-frequency
queries on niche topic to push Misra-Gries counter above threshold). EMA tracker per
(customer, topic) flags deviations.

HARD-PASS: EMA detector flags > 90% of injection attacks at < 10% false positive on
normal traffic.
