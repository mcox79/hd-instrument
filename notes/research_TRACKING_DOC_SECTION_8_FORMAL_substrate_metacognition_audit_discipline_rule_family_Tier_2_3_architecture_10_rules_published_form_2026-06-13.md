1. **Appearance count ≥ 3**: candidate has been invoked in at least 3 distinct cells, artifacts, or drills
2. **Empirical witness**: at least 1 cell or test produced a verdict that the rule materially changed
3. **Cross-cell breadth**: rule applies to multiple substrate-architectural domains (not isolated to one cell type)

Calibration:
- Bayesian Bayes factor BF ≥ 10 (per drill 15 + standard scientific-method threshold)
- Lakatos progressive program criterion (rule expands what substrate can handle)

When a rule fails its criteria after temporary promotion (e.g. via adversarial audit), it is honestly DOWNGRADED back to candidate-with-qualifier status (per 7th USER-LOCKED rule always reconsider).
