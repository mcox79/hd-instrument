# Deep brain-foundation drill (angle 2/3): goal-owner attribution — flow + order

Date: 2026-08-04 (compute local; commit timestamp may show 08-05 due to session boundary)
Type: VERIFICATION drill (live web search this pass) + synthesis
Question: how does the brain attribute a GOAL to an AGENT and track WHO OWNS IT across a narrative, and is our pipeline's flow/order brain-faithful?

## HEADLINE

The brain's goal-owner pathway is **event-segmentation -> thematic-role/argument-structure parsing (fast, MTG/posterior-temporal) -> GENERATIVE goal INFERENCE via mentalizing (TPJ/dmPFC, embedded in DMN) -> goal bound to the protagonist's situation-model index (hippocampal/DMN relational binding) -> standing goal-state maintained until resolved -> outcome monitored (ACC/appraisal) and bound back to the same owner-index**. Our pipeline gets the ORDER right (role-labeling before goal-typing before outcome before owner-selection) but is **lexically gated where the brain is generatively inferential**: we only instantiate a GOAL when a psych/desiderative verb fires; the brain infers goals from action sequences even with **no goal-verb present at all** (Trabasso/Graesser goal-plan inference; mentalizing from intentional-action cues, Saxe desire>physical-causation contrast). That's the single most important divergence — not a wrong order, a missing generative-inference component sitting behind the frame-role gate.

## Cheap decisive test

Take N narrative snippets where a character's goal is conveyed **only via an action/outcome sequence, no explicit psych-verb** (e.g., "Maria walked to the shelter and filled out the adoption forms three times a week" — goal = adopt-a-dog, never stated lexically). HARD-PASS for "our pipeline is adequate as-is": frame-primary + psych-verb gate correctly abstains (returns no-goal / low-confidence) on these AND downstream Component-5 doesn't silently misattribute an outcome to a false owner. HARD-FAIL: pipeline either (a) hallucinates a goal-owner via positional/frequency proxy when no psych-verb exists (repeats the exact class of bug the frame-primary rebuild was supposed to fix), or (b) simply produces zero recall on the entire goal-implied-by-action class — in which case the missing capability is confirmed load-bearing, not a tail case.

## Falsifiable predictions

## Detailed findings by section

### 1. Intentionality/goal dimension of the situation model — event-indexing model

**VERIFIED (this pass, via mentalizing lit-scan sub-agent live search):** The mentalizing/theory-of-mind network (TPJ, dmPFC, precuneus/posterior cingulate) is functionally embedded within, and closely overlapping, the default-mode network (DMN) — Spreng & Grady-type DMN-mentalizing overlap findings, and Mar's (2011, "The Neuropsychology of Narrative," Neuropsychologia) meta-analysis linking narrative/situation-model comprehension to this same network, were returned as VERIFIED hits by the live lit-scan. Saxe-type contrasts (desire/intention conditions activating TPJ more than physical-causation control conditions) were also returned VERIFIED.

**VERIFIED (this pass, live lit-scan sub-agent, returned after initial synthesis and folded in):**
- Zwaan & Radvansky (1998), *Psychological Bulletin* 123(2):162-185 — confirmed via institutional repository + PDF host. Five tracked dimensions converge across independent sources as **time, space, entity/protagonist, causation, and motivation** (the paper itself favors "motivation" over "intentionality" as the label for the goal/plan dimension). Reading-time discontinuity studies confirm goal/motivational shifts produce processing cost like the other four dimensions — goals are a first-class tracked situation-model dimension, not a derivative of causation. Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH (abstract/secondary-source level; full primary-text quote not obtained, PDF fetch failed).
- The specific active-vs-resolved-goal accessibility decay claim is better sourced by **Radvansky & Curiel (1998), "Narrative comprehension and aging: The fate of completed goal information," *Psychology and Aging* 13:69-79** (confirmed via PubMed) than by the originally-guessed "Rinck & Bower (2000)" citation (likely a misremembered author/year on my part — flagging the correction). Finding: goal-probe accessibility is **highest for still-open/failed goals, lower once completed, lowest for neutral information** — i.e. an unresolved goal stays elevated in the situation model and drops off on resolution, exactly the standing-state-bound-to-entity behavior our pipeline needs Component-5 to reproduce. Confidence: HIGH for this paper; MEDIUM that my original citation guess was the right one (it wasn't, corrected here).
- Graesser, Singer & Trabasso (1994), *Psychological Review* 101(3):371-395 — confirmed via PubMed/ResearchGate. Constructionist theory: goal-directed explanatory inference ("why did X do Y") is a core, reliably-generated inference class during narrative reading — this is the same generative-inference evidence base underlying the headline divergence finding in section 2. Trabasso/van den Broek causal-network model explicitly codes clauses as Setting/Event/Internal-Response/**Goal**/Attempt/Outcome as linked causal-network nodes — i.e. the brain's own narrative representation already has a GOAL node type distinct from Event/Outcome, structurally validating that our pipeline's goal-typing-as-distinct-from-event/outcome-typing stage is the right node granularity, independent of the lexical-gating issue.
- Goal state bound persistently to a specific protagonist entity (not recomputed per sentence): not a single directly-quoted theoretical claim, but a reasonable synthesis of the above three converging findings (protagonist-specific goal accessibility that persists/decays as a state). Marked as VERIFIED-BY-SYNTHESIS rather than VERIFIED-BY-DIRECT-QUOTE.

### 2. Flow/architecture: text -> goal-owner

**VERIFIED:** mentalizing network (TPJ/dmPFC) is the region set implicated specifically in intentional/goal-directed content, distinguishable in fMRI contrasts from generic semantic or physical-causal content (Saxe-style desire>physical-causation), and this network operates within/alongside DMN during narrative comprehension (Mar 2011 meta-analysis, Spreng-type overlap). This supports treating goal-INFERENCE as a distinct computational step layered on top of (not identical to) general situation-model/DMN processing — consistent with the proposed order: event/role parsing first (faster, more posterior/lexical), goal inference via mentalizing next (slower, more anterior/inferential), then relational binding of the goal to the entity's standing situation-model index.

**VERIFIED (generative, not lexical, goal attribution):** Trabasso-line goal-plan generative inference — readers infer a character's goal from an observed action or action sequence, generating the goal as an inference even when it is never stated — was returned as a VERIFIED finding by the lit-scan. This is the load-bearing piece of evidence for the headline divergence: the brain's goal-attribution step is NOT gated on a goal-word appearing in the text; it is a generative inference process that can and does run off action sequences alone.

### 3. Who-owns vs who-is-affected — thematic roles

**TRAINED-ONLY this pass** (the dedicated thematic-role lit-scan sub-agent covering Dowty proto-roles / psych-verb experiencer-subject literature was still in flight when synthesis was ordered to proceed without further waiting): the EXPERIENCER-as-subject pattern for desiderative/psych predicates (want, desire, hope, fear, need) is standard in argument-structure linguistics (Dowty 1991 proto-agent/proto-patient properties; Belletti & Rizzi psych-verb classes) and is consistent with, and directly motivates, the substrate's frame-primary design (psych-verb subject = EXPERIENCER = goal-holder). The higher-confidence, actually-verified claim from this drill is the mentalizing evidence above establishing that role-assignment (fast, frame/lexical) and goal-INFERENCE (slower, generative, mentalizing-based) are two distinct processing stages — which is the structural point that matters for judging our pipeline's order, independent of whether every detail of the psych-verb argument-structure literature was re-confirmed live today.

## DIRECT VERDICT

**Our pipeline's flow/order is brain-faithful in SEQUENCE** — role-labeling before goal-typing before outcome-typing before owner-selection mirrors the brain's fast-role-parsing -> goal-attribution -> owner-binding -> outcome-evaluation cascade. The ORDER is not the problem.

**The single most important divergence:** the brain's goal-attribution step is a **generative mentalizing inference** (TPJ/dmPFC, VERIFIED this pass) that can construct a goal from an observed action sequence with **no goal-word present** (Trabasso/Graesser goal-plan inference, VERIFIED this pass) — whereas our pipeline's goal-typing step is **lexically gated**: a goal only exists if a psych/desiderative verb fires. This means our pipeline is not wrong in shape, it is **narrower in scope than the brain's mechanism** — it implements the fast frame-role-parsing stage faithfully but substitutes a lexical trigger for the brain's generative-inference stage rather than implementing that stage. This is a **missing-component gap** (per the standing "every negative -> check missing LEARNING/inference component" discipline), not an ordering bug, and it is structurally the same class of gap as the OOV frame-induction problem already identified in Component-3b — both need a generative/bootstrapped inference organ where the current design has a lookup/gate.

**Is this a reasonable approximation, or does it miss a load-bearing component?** Reasonable approximation WITHIN the explicit-psych-verb register (already measured adequate, ~0.83-0.86). Misses a load-bearing component for the GENERAL case, because action-implied goals (no psych-verb) are plausibly a large fraction of naturalistic goal-bearing narrative content (children's stories routinely convey goals through action, e.g. "she kept knocking on every door on the street" implying "find someone home" with no desire-verb) — this specific prevalence estimate is UNVERIFIED/TRAINED-ONLY this pass and is the recommended cheap decisive test / follow-up corpus count.

**Confidence (deflated per lit-scan calibration discipline):** P(this is the correct diagnosis of the main structural gap) = 0.58, deflated from an un-deflated internal estimate of ~0.78 (0.20 deflation applied). Raised slightly from the initial 0.55 pre-synthesis pass now that section 1's Zwaan & Radvansky / Trabasso node-typing / Radvansky & Curiel findings landed VERIFIED and directly corroborate the goal-as-distinct-tracked-state architecture — but still capped below 0.60 per the novel-synthesis cap discipline, since the specific claim "this is the SAME organ as OOV frame-induction" remains a novel synthesis, and the prevalence-of-implicit-goals estimate driving the "load-bearing not tail-case" severity claim is still unverified.
