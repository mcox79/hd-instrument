## 1. Framing: Lean = a SECOND path to T0 (formal-proof), not a replacement
The substrate has ONE T0 path today: cert-grade experiment PASS (full-mode, >=3 seeds, pre-reg bands). Lean adds a SECOND: a math claim FORMALLY PROVEN by Lean 4 / mathlib4 (deterministic, exact, no-LLM = exactly the substrate's strength applied to itself). Approach A (external oracle): substrate generates a proof obligation -> lake build verifies -> PASS/FAIL -> [my SCHEMA-VET] -> T0-PROVEN-FORMAL atom. Directly on the USER substrate-autonomy directive (eventually self-certify). The 6 shipped L6-PROOF chains (convolution / Bayes / CLT / Cauchy-Schwarz / Pythagoras-IP / spectral) are the READY consumers.

## 2. THE CRITICAL GUARD (the whole design hinges on this): Lean-PASS is NECESSARY-NOT-SUFFICIENT
A Lean PASS proves a STATED proposition P_lean. For the substrate to call the corresponding claim T0-PROVEN, TWO things must hold:
   (a) Lean verified P_lean (lake build PASS), AND
   (b) **SEMANTICS-MATCH**: P_lean is the SAME claim as the substrate's proposition P_substrate (not a different/weaker/vacuous statement).
(b) is the cert-owner's job and is the EXACT formal analog of the disciplines that fired all day:
   - anchor-mechanism-match (R4-18): the operator verified must be the operator claimed.
   - no-Goodhart: the metric/proof must measure/prove the CLAIMED thing, not a proxy.
   - 100th-rule (audit-tooling-self-verify): confirm the verifier verified what we think.
Lean-PASS + semantics-MISMATCH = a FALSE-T0 (the formal version of the R4-18 mismatch). This is the dominant failure mode and the reason Lean-PASS does NOT auto-promote.

## 3. Lean-verified atom schema (proposed)
- kind = PROOF_RECORD (or extend EXPERIMENT_RECORD's machinery; like research/experiment records).
- confidence_tier = T0_PROVEN_FORMAL (a T0 sub-kind, distinct from T0_PROVEN_EXPERIMENTAL; both load-bearing).
- proof_obligation metadata: {lean_source_hash, lean_toolchain_version (e.g. Lean4 v4.31.0), mathlib_version, proof_target (the proposition text), verification_evidence (lake-build PASS + UTC timestamp + exit-code), semantics_match_vet (cert-owner confirmation: who/when + the P_lean<->P_substrate mapping)}.
- **STRUCTURAL GUARD (conservative, mirrors RESEARCH_FINDING)**: a Lean-verified atom carries NO algebra field BY DEFAULT -> it is queryable PROVEN-KNOWLEDGE but does NOT enter axiom_term. Promotion of a verified IDENTITY into the algebraic core (axiom_term) is a SEPARATE, EXPLICIT cert-owner action gated on semantics-match -- never automatic. (Same discipline as research: structural exclusion by default; explicit gated promotion only. A Lean-PASS-but-semantics-mismatch can then never corrupt axiom_term, because entering axiom_term requires the explicit semantics-match promotion, not just the PASS.)

## 4. T0 promotion criteria
T3/T2 conjecture -> Lean proof obligation -> lake-build PASS -> [my SCHEMA-VET: semantics-match + tool-self-verify] -> T0_PROVEN_FORMAL (confirmed_by = lean-proof-record). lake-build FAIL -> REFUTED + KEEP as negative knowledge (a disproof is also valuable). Promotion into axiom_term (algebraic core) = a further explicit cert-owner step (semantics-match of an algebra-bearing identity), NOT implied by T0_PROVEN_FORMAL.

## 5. Pipeline + staleness guard
- FIRE at WRITE-TIME (cert-gate): a T0_PROVEN_FORMAL atom is created only after lake-PASS + my semantics-match VET. (Consistent with the existing cert-chain -- cert established at promotion, not re-checked per query.)
- STALENESS GUARD: if lean_toolchain_version or mathlib_version changes, RE-VERIFY (a proof valid under Lean v4.31 should re-run under the new toolchain). Analogous to the index-refresh + completeness-guard recurrence-detectors. A scheduled re-verify (or a version-delta alert) prevents a silently-stale formal claim.

## 6. Failure-mode coverage (100th-rule applied to Lean)
   (i) PRIMARY: Lean-PASS but P_lean != P_substrate (semantics-mismatch) -> the semantics-match VET (Sec 2b) is the guard.
   (ii) Vacuous/trivial PASS: Lean proves a true-but-irrelevant or vacuously-true statement -> semantics-match VET must confirm P_lean is the NON-trivial claim asserted.
   (iii) Build-error-as-false-green: a lake build that errored/aborted mis-read as PASS (the cert-via-system-python false-green pattern in my memory) -> tool-self-verify: confirm exit-code + that the target theorem (not a placeholder) was the thing built. READ the output, don't tail-pipe it.
