## HEADLINE

**The reframe is correct, and it is now the best-supported explanation on the table for why all four real-data conjunction tests read UNREADABLE.** Three independent literatures converge: (1) real pairwise-interaction magnitudes in genetics, chemistry, and drug synergy are NOT continuously distributed — they are zero-inflated/heavy-tailed, with hit rates clustering 3-15% (Dede 6%, Costanzo ~3-4%, Kramer MMP 5-9%, paralog-screen median ~11%) — so a continuous-MAE / variance-explained readout is measuring the wrong thing on the wrong-shaped target; (2) the correct, field-standard evaluation for this data shape is ranking/detection (AUPRC, enrichment factor, hurdle/two-stage models), not regression accuracy — this is not a novel proposal, it is what the genomics field itself already uses to score interaction-scoring methods; (3) the brain's own conjunction-reading systems most analogous to this problem (DG/CA3 pattern separation, cerebellar sparse expansion coding) are explicitly characterized in the primary literature as **discrimination/detection** mechanisms, not magnitude-estimation mechanisms — directly supporting the reframe as biologically correct, not just statistically convenient.

**However, two distinct claims must not be conflated, and only one of them is now well-evidenced.** The MEASUREMENT claim ("readable_rel≈0 under continuous-MAE evaluation is a metric artifact given a zero-inflated target, not proof of zero signal") is now strongly supported and should be treated as near-certain. The REPRESENTATION claim (the director's phase-diagram addendum: "the mechanism was run in the wrong SUBSTRATE PHASE — dense superposition smears the sparse minority into the bulk, and moving to a sparse-expansion operating point is required for the signal to even be extractable, independent of readout metric") is a plausible, brain-analogous, mechanistically-motivated HYPOTHESIS but is **untested on this program's own data** and should be evaluated as a separate, ablatable variable, not folded silently into the same fix. The honest recommendation is a 2x2 test (regime x readout), not a single combined intervention, so a positive result can be attributed correctly.

## (1) Is genuine real 2-way interaction better modeled as sparse detection than continuous regression?

**Yes, with strong convergent evidence across three independent domains, all now directly checked (not merely recalled from the prior 07-14/07-15 drills, which established WHERE interaction concentrates but not the SHAPE of its distribution):**

- **Genetic interaction / synthetic lethality.** Costanzo yeast SGA: of ~23M double mutants tested, only ~350K positive + ~550K negative interactions cross significance — roughly 3-4% of tested pairs. Dede et al. enCas12a paralog screen: 24/403 pairs called synthetic-lethal (~6%), and even within that hit tail, reproducibility itself is graded (79% replicate in >=2/3 lines, 58% in all 3 — the "positive" population is itself not homogeneous). Parrish/Big Papi pgPEN: 105/1,191 (~9%). Cross-study paralog-screen synthesis: hit rates cluster ~6-15% (median ~11%), with 68-86% of individual hits being single-cell-line-only — a sparse, context-dependent tail, not a broad continuous effect.
- **Chemistry MMP nonadditivity.** Kramer et al. 2021: only ~9% of in-house (AZ) and ~5% of public (ChEMBL) double-transformation cycles clear the noise-derived nonadditivity threshold — the strong majority sit at/near zero (indistinguishable from assay noise), with a sparse minority carrying real structurally-informative signal (Peltason & Bajorath 2015 frame "strong nonadditivity" explicitly as a rare outlier class, not a graded continuum).
- **Drug synergy.** NCI-ALMANAC/O'Neil ComboScore distributions sit majority near-zero/antagonistic-to-additive, with true strong synergy a minority tail (exact population percentages per Bliss/Loewe bin not independently re-verified this session — flagged below).
- **Why this defeats a continuous-MAE / variance-explained readout, generically:** pseudo-R²/variance-explained-style metrics for zero-inflated or rare-event targets are a well-documented statistical pitfall (Statistical Horizons, UCLA OARC, pseudo-R² review literature) — they read as spuriously uninformative (or spuriously high, depending on formulation) because the bulk near-null population dominates the metric's denominator regardless of whether the sparse tail is being read correctly. **Flag:** the lit-scan sub-agent could not pin this specific claim to one canonical single-source citation — it is a synthesized cross-literature consensus (hurdle-model / zero-inflated-model discussions), not a single verbatim quote. Treat as high-confidence-by-convergence, not single-source-verified.

**This directly and mechanistically explains the readable_rel~0.0042 Dede result named in the task**: even the correctly-curated near-zero-singles precondition population is still ~94% neutral pairs (Dede's own 24/403 rate), so a continuous-magnitude metric computed across that population is, by construction, measuring mostly noise around a near-zero majority — exactly the population regime where the pitfall above applies. The near-zero-singles curation captured the PRECONDITION correctly; it did not, and structurally cannot, make the OUTCOME distribution continuous.

## (2) Correct readout + evaluation for a sparse-interaction signal

**Ranking/detection is the field-standard fix, not a novel proposal — the genomics literature itself already scores interaction-prediction methods this way:**

- **AUPRC over ROC-AUC under class imbalance.** Davis & Goadrich (2006, ICML) proved PR-curve dominance implies ROC-curve dominance (not vice versa) and showed ROC curves can look deceptively good under heavy class imbalance because the false-positive-rate denominator (all negatives) is huge — large absolute false-positive counts barely move it. Saito & Rehmsmeier (2015, PLOS ONE) is the standard bioinformatics-imbalance citation making this operational. **Chance-level AUPRC approximates the base rate** (e.g., ~0.05-0.10 for a 5-10% positive rate) — the correct "beats chance" bar is materially above the base rate, NOT above 0.5. (Flag: the exact identity "chance AUPRC = base rate" is a widely-used approximation in the ML-imbalance literature, not verified as a formal theorem in the two cited papers themselves.)
- **Two-stage / hurdle models are the textbook detect-then-magnitude architecture.** Mullahy (1986) hurdle models: stage 1 = binary classifier (is this pair interacting, yes/no), stage 2 = magnitude regression CONDITIONAL on stage-1 positive only (zero-truncated). Distinguished from zero-inflated (Lambert 1992, ZIP/ZINB) models, which allow two zero-generating processes rather than assuming stage 1 explains all zeros — the distinction matters for model choice but not for the core architectural move (separate detection from magnitude).
- **This is already how the field evaluates interaction-scoring methods.** The Benchmarking-GI-Scores paper (bioRxiv 2025.03.31.645224 — the same harmonized paralog compendium identified as the ingest target in `notes/research_dataset_scout_high_snr_conjunction_module1_replacement_2026-07-15.md`) reports AUROC **and** AUPRC side by side against curated gold-standard hit lists (De Kegel Hits, Köferle Hits), plus enrichment factor (fold-enrichment of true hits in top-ranked predictions vs random — one pan-cancer perturbation-screen paper reports ~14-fold). This is directly transferable: evaluate the conjunction module the same way the field already evaluates competing GI-scoring methods, not with a bespoke continuous metric the field itself does not use for this data shape.

**Avoiding target leakage when curating on outcome — this is the one place where genuine methodological risk lives, and the literature has a specific, well-developed answer:**

- Conditioning training/evaluation-set MEMBERSHIP on the outcome itself (selecting pairs because they show large |interaction|) risks **collider/selection bias** (Cole et al. 2010, "Illustrating bias due to conditioning on a collider," PubMed 19926667) and, more sharply, **winner's curse** — effect-size estimates for associations selected because they crossed a significance/magnitude threshold are systematically inflated by regression-to-the-mean under thresholding (standard in GWAS; correction methods exist — BR-squared, ascertainment-corrected MLE — but the standard architectural fix is an untouched held-out set, not a statistical correction after the fact).
- **The correct design, directly transferable from the case-control/Heckman-selection literature:** (a) the near-zero-singles PRECONDITION filter (both SMF-A, SMF-B ~ WT) is safe to use for defining the eligible population — it conditions on pre-outcome covariates, not on the outcome; (b) the ground-truth POSITIVE/NEGATIVE label (large-|interaction| vs not) must come from a FIXED, externally-defined gold-standard call (the study's own published significance threshold, e.g. Dede's zdLFC<-3, or Costanzo's |epsilon|>0.08 p<0.05) applied once, not re-tuned by inspecting the held-out split; (c) the model must predict from CONSTITUENT features only — SMF-A, SMF-B, gene identity/embedding — never from any outcome-derived quantity (DMF, zdLFC, epsilon itself must not leak into the input features, only into the label); (d) held-out evaluation should ideally be CROSS-STUDY where possible (train on one screen's calls, evaluate on an independent replicate/screen — e.g. Dede vs Parrish/pgPEN, both folded into the harmonized compendium) rather than a within-study random split, which is the closest practical analog to Heckman's prospective/retrospective distinction and directly guards against winner's-curse-style optimism from evaluating on the same discovery population used to set thresholds.

## (3) Does the mechanism's proven dense-plant capability transfer to detection/ranking on sparse real data?

**Partially yes by construction, but two distinct risks must be separated — and this is where the director's phase-diagram addendum is a real, additional hypothesis, not a restatement of the readout fix.**

- **Ranking is a strictly weaker requirement than calibrated magnitude regression.** A mechanism proven to read planted continuous interaction magnitude with pos_ctrl 0.908 is, in principle, AT LEAST as capable of correctly rank-ordering pairs by interaction strength (monotonic ordering is implied by accurate magnitude reading; the reverse is not required). So the readout-metric fix alone (switch to AUPRC/ranking) should recover some signal even with NO architecture change, PROVIDED the underlying representation still carries the ordering information — this is the null hypothesis the reframe needs to test first, cheaply, before any regime change.
- **But there is a real, separate risk the readout fix alone does NOT address: training-signal starvation.** If the module's training objective is still a magnitude-MSE-style loss computed across a target that is ~90-95% near-zero, gradient signal is dominated by the null majority — a model trained this way may never specialize toward the rare tail even if its readout is later re-scored with AUPRC. This is a genuinely independent failure mode from the evaluation-metric problem in part (1)/(2), and argues for retraining with a hurdle-style or class-weighted objective (per Mullahy 1986), not merely re-scoring an unchanged model's existing outputs.
- **The director's phase-diagram framing (sparse-expansion operating point vs dense-superposition regime) is a third, independent hypothesis, layered on top of the two above.** The claim — that dense superposition binds cause the sparse interacting minority's signal to be smeared/attenuated in the representation itself (crosstalk-style interference from the ~90%+ null bulk), and that moving to a sparser, higher-active-fraction-reduced operating point is the native regime for detecting a rare minority against a bulk — is mechanistically plausible and has a real, generic precedent in classical associative-memory theory: sparse/orthogonalized codes are well established to have LOWER interference and HIGHER effective storage capacity per stored item than dense superposition codes (Willshaw, Buneman & Longuet-Higgins 1969 sparse associative-net capacity result; Marr's 1969 archicortex/dentate-expansion theory; Kanerva's Sparse Distributed Memory). **This citation set is recalled from training, not independently re-fetched by a lit-scan sub-agent this session** — flag accordingly, lower confidence than the three sub-agent-verified sections above. It is a real, textbook-standard argument, but it has NOT been checked against this program's own phase-diagram sweep data (the director states the substrate's own capacity/decode curves already exist across K/N/binding-type; this drill did not verify those curves, only the generic theory that predicts sparse-should-help-here).
- **Honest answer to "does it transfer or does sparsity defeat it":** we do not yet know, and should not claim to. The readout fix (AUPRC/hurdle) is well-evidenced and should be tried FIRST because it is cheap (no architecture change, re-score existing outputs) and diagnostic (if AUPRC recovers real signal on the EXISTING dense-regime run, that already falsifies "dense regime structurally destroys the signal" — the phase-move hypothesis would then be unnecessary). Only if the readout-only fix fails to clear a meaningful bar above chance should the phase-move (regime change + retrain) be treated as the next, separately-attributable intervention. Running both blindly bundled together would leave the program unable to tell which intervention did the work — this is the same "isolate cheap fixes before committing to the heavier change" discipline already used in the 2026-07-14 brain-grounding drill's rank-ordering of bind-mechanism fixes.

## (4) Brain-ground: does the brain detect sparse conjunctions rather than regress interaction magnitude?

**Yes for the systems most load-bearing and most analogous to this problem — with one important, honestly-reported complication.**

- **DG/CA3 pattern separation** is characterized in the primary literature as producing discrete/categorical orthogonalization, not graded interaction-magnitude coding. Leutgeb et al. (2007, *Science*) show sparse, near-orthogonal rate-remapping in DG; Yassa & Stark (2011, *Trends Neurosci*) frame the computational role explicitly as discrimination/decorrelation ("transforms similar inputs into orthogonal codes... reduces overlap among confusable items") and, clinically, as a binary-ish "exact match vs similar lure" judgment — directly analogous to "is this specific pair a true interacting hit, not a near-neighbor null pair," not to "how strong is the interaction."
- **Cerebellar/DG sparse EXPANSION coding** (Marr-Albus "codon" theory) is explicitly framed around discrimination/associative-learning capacity via extreme sparsity ("binary addressing," minimizing interference) — a detection-oriented framing structurally distinct from Olshausen & Field's sparse coding (which is a reconstruction/efficient-coding framework for continuous natural-image statistics, not a detection framework — flagging this domain-mismatch explicitly rather than papering over it, since it means "sparse coding" is not a single monolithic brain principle; the DETECTION-flavored sparse coding is the DG/cerebellar variant, not the V1/Olshausen-Field variant).
- **Nonlinear mixed selectivity** (the ~5-10% PFC/parietal minority from the 07-15 sibling drill) is framed by Rigotti et al. (2013, *Nature*) and follow-on work around dimensionality-expansion enabling flexible LINEAR CLASSIFICATION/readout, with task-error trials coinciding with a measurable "collapse" in that dimensionality — a discrimination/task-performance framing, not a continuous-interaction-strength framing. The lit-scan did not find direct literature statements characterizing NMS firing rate itself as a graded "interaction magnitude" variable (flagged as a real, if less exhaustively searched, gap — moderate rather than high confidence on this specific point).
- **Important honest complication:** novelty/salience circuits (locus coeruleus phasic responses, mismatch negativity) are NOT purely categorical — MMN amplitude is graded, proportional to the degree of statistical/physical deviance and to prediction-error magnitude, and LC phasic responses scale with aversive/behavioral significance rather than firing as a pure binary. So "sparse systems are always binary detectors" is NOT a universal brain law — it is specifically the pattern-separation/expansion-coding systems (DG/CA3, cerebellar granule layer) that are detection-flavored, while salience/novelty-signaling systems are graded. This matters because it means the brain-analogy support for the reframe is real but should be scoped precisely to "conjunctive/pattern-separation coding is detection-flavored," not overgeneralized to "sparsity always means binary" — a distinction worth preserving for honesty even though it does not change the bottom-line verdict (the systems that are the actual biological analog of a conjunction-reading module ARE the detection-flavored ones).

## VERDICT

**The reframe is correct as a diagnosis, and should be adopted for the evaluation methodology immediately and cheaply (re-score existing outputs with AUPRC/enrichment before any architecture change).** The phase-diagram regime-move is a real, brain-consistent, mechanistically-motivated hypothesis worth testing NEXT, as a separately-attributable second intervention, not bundled into the first. This is not the same claim as "the fix definitely recovers usable signal" — the underlying biological noise floor (Dede's own hits only replicate 79%/58% across cell lines/all lines) means even a perfectly-executed detection reframe may hit a real ceiling below what a clean synthetic AND-gate plant would suggest, and a frequency/degree-based baseline (hub-gene connectivity is a known confound in SL screens) may already claim a nontrivial share of the AUPRC lift, raising the bar for what counts as the module ADDING value beyond a naive baseline.

## Cheap decisive test (2-step, cheapest-first)

**Step 1 (readout-only, no retrain, ~1hr):** take the EXISTING Dede-run outputs (the ones that produced readable_rel=0.0042) and re-score them under AUPRC/precision@k against the fixed gold-standard zdLFC<-3 hit list, holding the model exactly as-is. This is a pure re-scoring exercise — zero retraining risk, immediately diagnostic. If AUPRC materially exceeds the ~6% base rate, that alone falsifies "dense regime structurally destroys the signal" and means the prior four nulls were a pure measurement artifact, not a representational failure — no regime move needed.

**Step 2 (regime-move ablation, only if Step 1 is weak/null):** run a 2x2 — {dense-superposition regime, sparse-expansion regime} x {continuous-MAE readout, AUPRC/hurdle readout} — on the same Dede/harmonized-compendium held-out split, holding everything else fixed (same train/eval split, same constituent features, same gold-standard label). This isolates which of the two interventions (metric fix vs regime move) is doing the work, rather than bundling them and losing attribution.

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS (Step 1, readout-only):** AUPRC on the existing dense-regime model's outputs, scored against the fixed zdLFC<-3 (or equivalent) gold-standard label, is >= 2x the base rate (e.g. >=0.12 against a ~6% base rate) AND the additive-baseline's own AUPRC on the identical task is closer to the base rate (i.e. the conjunction module is doing detection work a simple additive/linear detector cannot). This would mean the readable_rel~0 nulls were a metric artifact, full stop — no architecture or regime change needed, only the evaluation harness.

**HARD-FAIL (Step 1):** AUPRC on the existing model's outputs is statistically indistinguishable from the base rate (chance) even under the more forgiving ranking metric — proceed to Step 2's regime-move ablation, since this would indicate the dense-regime representation itself does not carry rank-order-recoverable interaction information, not merely that the wrong metric was applied.

**HARD-PASS (Step 2, if reached):** the sparse-expansion regime's AUPRC materially exceeds the dense regime's AUPRC (both scored with the same ranking metric, isolating the regime effect) AND clears >= 2x base rate AND beats the additive+frequency baselines' AUPRC on the same held-out cross-study split (train on one screen's calls e.g. Dede, evaluate on an independent screen e.g. Parrish/pgPEN within the harmonized compendium, per the leakage-avoidance design in part 2).

**HARD-FAIL (Step 2):** both regimes sit at or near chance-level AUPRC even with a hurdle-style detect-then-magnitude retrain — this would mean the paralog-buffering AND-gate signal, while mechanistically real per the 2026-07-15 negative-drill's variance-fraction evidence, is not recoverable from the available constituent features (SMF-A/SMF-B/gene-identity) at the base rate this program can currently access; the correct scoping conclusion at that point is "genuine non-additivity exists but requires richer input features (e.g. protein-complex membership, pathway annotation, structural/sequence features) than bare single-mutant-fitness constituents supply," a real and useful negative result distinct from "the reframe was wrong."
