## HEADLINE

**Costanzo per-pair epsilon (~1.8 cells/pair) is the wrong resolution for near-zero-singles AND-gate pairs; purpose-built combinatorial paralog-CRISPR screens are 1-2 orders of magnitude more replicated and are the correct replacement class.** The single-best verified target is the **Benchmarking-GI-Scores harmonized paralog-synthetic-lethality compendium** (github.com/cancergenetics/Benchmarking-GI-Scores; Figshare `figshare.com/s/59ee190b1879fe3eb191`), which pools 5 purpose-built combinatorial-CRISPR paralog screens — including the enCas12a/Dede screen (replicate Pearson r = 0.87-0.94) and the Parrish/"Big Papi" pgPEN screen (triplicate, mean r = 0.93) — into ~8,000+ harmonized human paralog pairs with multiple precomputed GI scores (GEMINI, Parrish z-score, Orthrus, zdLFC). This clears the N-adequacy problem that the standalone Dede screen (only 24 called synthetic-lethal pairs) would itself fail on the ~50-clean-pair HARD-FAIL threshold pre-registered in `notes/drill_negative_why_real_interactions_additive_capturable_where_genuine_2026-07-15.md`. **One verification gap remains before dispatch**: none of today's fetches opened the harmonized table's actual columns, so it is NOT yet confirmed whether raw per-study single-mutant-fitness (SMF-A/SMF-B) constituents are retained alongside the GI scores, or whether the harmonized table ships GI scores only (in which case the Dede-standalone raw file, independently verified down to exact schema and file size, is the zero-risk fallback that plugs directly into the pairwise+constituents+target requirement).
