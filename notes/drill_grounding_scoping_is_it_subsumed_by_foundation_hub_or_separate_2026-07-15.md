# Scoping drill: is grounding subsumed by the canonical-ID-hub + measured-attribute foundation, or a separate build?

Date 2026-07-15. Method: synthesis over dense existing on-topic research (7+ notes 2026-07-08 through 2026-07-15 already answer most of Q1/Q2 in depth) + 2 parallel Sonnet lit-scans dispatched this cycle to close the two narrowest gaps (modern LLM/KG grounding-debate status 2022-2026; how abstract *scientific* theoretical entities specifically get grounded). Generic academic terms only in external queries (Harnad, symbol grounding, causal theory of reference, entity realism, embodied cognition — standard philosophy/cog-sci vocabulary, no substrate specifics went off-platform).

## HEADLINE

**Grounding is CONDITIONALLY SUBSUMED by the architecture — but only if two things that are NOT automatic get engineered on top of it, and the architecture as described conflates two genuinely different jobs that must be kept separate.** The canonical-ID hub solves **identity** (is this the same gene/drug/chemical across modules) — that job is close to solved (per `notes/drill_cross_module_interface_entity_sharing_brain_and_bestinclass_2026-07-15.md`, Babel/NodeNorm pattern). The measured-attribute modules solve **grounding** (does the symbol refer to anything real) — and here the literature is unambiguous that this is the *correct* mechanism class for abstract scientific entities specifically, matching both the brain's own answer for non-perceptual concepts and philosophy-of-science's answer for theoretical terms. But an entity that has a canonical ID with **no reachable measured-module content** is a bare symbol with a well-maintained name — identity is not grounding, and conflating the two is the single failure mode this drill exists to prevent. The fix is not a new architectural layer; it's (1) a governance rule that only counts an entity as grounded when it is composition-reachable to a genuinely measured node, and (2) a reachability-audit tool (not yet built) that measures this per entity and predicts, in advance, where relational reasoning will fail.

## (a) Brain answer: relational vs sensorimotor grounding for abstract concepts

**Not a clean dichotomy — a dimensionality/regime split within the same machinery, and abstract concepts are grounded by RELAY, not by direct perception.** Two convergent findings, already established in `research_abstract_grounding_relational_role_vs_attribute_reframe_2026-07-11.md`:

- When continuous attribute dimensions exist (bird morphology, social competence/trust), the hippocampal-entorhinal system builds a joint-coordinate map over them — a multi-D **attribute** code (Constantinescu, O'Reilly & Behrens, *Science* 2016; Park, Constantinescu & Behrens, *Nat Neurosci* 2021/*eLife* 2024) — still attribute grounding, just geometrically organized.
- When stimuli are genuinely discrete/symbolic with zero attribute content, the same machinery falls back to **pure relational/graph-communicability coding** (Garvert, Dolan & Behrens, *eLife* 2017) — a real, distinct, neurally dissociable regime. This is the closer analog for abstract scientific relations (an "epistasis coefficient" doesn't have a natural 1-2D perceptual magnitude the way bird-neck-length does).

**For abstract concepts with no direct sensory referent, the field's answer is RELAY / secondhand grounding, not "something else entirely.**" Harnad's own 1990 resolution licenses exactly this: composite symbols (his example, "zebra" = "horse" + "stripes") inherit groundedness through composition from already-grounded primitives, with no direct zebra-experience required — the requirement is only that the chain bottoms out *somewhere* in non-symbolic causal contact, not that every symbol does. This session's fresh lit-scan corroborates and sharpens it directly for technical/scientific abstracta: **Reinboth & Farkaš, "Ultimate Grounding of Abstract Concepts: A Graded Account," *J. Cognition* 2022** (PMC9400652) propose direct (sensorimotor) vs **indirect, language-mediated grounding** as the two pathways, explicitly citing mathematics as an indirect-grounded case; the Frontiers 2022 "Indirect Verbal Grounding" (IVG) study frames verbal relay from an already-grounded speaker as the mechanism for concepts "not directly accessible for sensorimotor interaction" — precisely our case (genes, synergy coefficients, chemical properties). **Mason, Schumacher & Just, npj Science of Learning 2021** (fMRI of physics faculty reasoning about fermions/dark matter) found a dedicated neural dimension for "measurability vs. immeasurability" and machinery for reasoning about intangibles *separated from direct experience* — direct empirical evidence that experts represent unobservable theoretical entities via non-perceptual, inference-based machinery, not simulated sensory imagery. **Verdict for Q1: relational structure + relay/composition from already-grounded (measured) anchors is what grounds abstract scientific concepts in the brain — not raw sensorimotor perception, and not pure symbol-symbol structure with no anchor either.**

## (b) Symbol-grounding-problem honest status: is a measured-anchored relational KG grounded?

**No live consensus that text-only/symbol-only structure suffices; but the "must be sensorimotor" reading is also wrong, and the correct resolution is causal-instrument contact, which a measured-attribute KG genuinely provides.**

- **Bender & Koller 2020 "Climbing towards NLU"** (the octopus argument) remains the dominant skeptical position: text-only training gives no way to learn reference. **No settled consensus 2022-2026** — Millière 2024 (arXiv:2407.21070) argues the claimed *a priori* impossibility is empirically unproven (LLMs solve tasks predicted impossible); Bender herself has not walked it back (2025-2026 retrospectives reaffirm the core claim). This drill treats it as **live, unresolved, and not dispositive either way** — it is squarely about *text-only, no-causal-channel* systems, which is NOT our architecture (our modules ingest actually-measured lab data, a causal channel).
- **Mollo & Millière, "The Vector Grounding Problem"** (arXiv:2304.01481, *Philosophy and Mind Sciences*) distinguish five grounding types (sensorimotor, communicative, epistemic, relational, **referential**) and argue referential grounding (a historical-causal link to worldly referents) is achievable without perception, via training/deployment causal chains — directly supports that non-perceptual causal contact is a legitimate grounding path, not merely a fallback.
- **The decisive resolution for our specific case comes from philosophy of science, not from the LLM debate**: **Kripke/Putnam's causal theory of reference** for natural-kind and theoretical terms ("gold," "electron," "gene") holds that a term's reference is fixed by causal contact at an original "baptism" (pointing at the actual phenomenon), then refined by ongoing scientific investigation — never by anyone's perceptual description. **Ian Hacking's entity realism** (*Representing and Intervening*, 1983) sharpens this for exactly our case: the **measurement apparatus is the causal contact point itself** ("if you can spray them, they are real") — belief in an unobservable entity is licensed by our ability to causally manipulate/measure it via instruments, independent of full theoretical understanding. **This is the single most load-bearing citation in this drill**: it establishes that instrument-mediated measurement is philosophically equivalent to sensorimotor transduction as a grounding channel — a gene's canonical identity anchored to real sequencing/assay measurements is grounded in exactly the same sense an electron is grounded by cloud-chamber tracks, and neither requires nor lacks anything sensorimotor perception would add. (Caveat, honestly flagged by both lit-scans: no single source explicitly states "therefore a numeric-literal-enriched knowledge graph is grounded" — this is a synthesis across two adjacent literatures the field has not yet been asked to connect directly, not a directly-quoted verdict.)
- **Bridgman's operationalism** (a term's meaning *just is* its measurement operations) is the closest historical precedent to "measured data = the whole grounding," but declined within philosophy of science for being unable to handle theory change and multiple-operation convergence — the honest lesson is: measurement grounds reference, but does not exhaust *meaning* (relational/inferential structure still carries the rest, matching (a) above).

**Honest current-science answer: a pure symbol-symbol web with zero causal contact anywhere is NOT considered grounded by any camp in this debate (this part IS consensus). A knowledge graph whose symbols are anchored, via composition-reachable paths, to instrument-measured quantities IS grounded in the same sense theoretical scientific terms have always been grounded (causal-historical reference-fixing via measurement, Kripke/Putnam/Hacking) — this is a strong, convergent, if not explicitly-fused-in-one-paper, answer.**

## (c) STRAIGHT VERDICT for our substrate

**Largely SUBSUMED, conditional on two things that must be actively engineered, not assumed:**

1. **Identity ≠ grounding — keep them conceptually and mechanically separate even though the architecture bundles them as "hub-and-spoke."** The canonical-ID hub (Babel/NodeNorm-style crosswalk registry) solves cross-module entity identity — a real, mostly-solved, different problem (per the 2026-07-15 cross-module drill). It does **not**, by itself, ground anything: a bare canonical ID with zero reachable measured content is exactly Harnad's ungrounded symbol with a very good name tag. Grounding is contributed **only** by the measured-attribute/relation modules (epistasis ε-scores, synergy Bliss/Loewe scores, chemistry nonadditivity coefficients — all real instrument/assay-derived numbers, already sourced per `notes/drill_real_nonadditive_experimental_datasets_for_conjunction_modules_2026-07-15.md`), acting as Hacking-style causal-instrument contact points.
2. **A reachability audit is required and does not yet exist.** Per the 2026-07-11 note's own unimplemented recommendation: every entity/relation claimed grounded needs a checked, finite composition path to at least one measured-module node. Without this check, the architecture *could* silently be building Bender-Koller's octopus (a well-structured, richly-connected, but causally-disconnected symbol web) for any entity whose only module membership is the bare hub registry. This is cheap (a graph-reachability traversal, no new data) and is the single missing piece between "architecture that COULD ground" and "architecture that IS verified to ground."

**What is NOT missing, and does not need to be built:** a sensorimotor/embodied/perceptual layer. Every literature drilled — brain (relay/indirect grounding for abstracta), philosophy of science (causal-historical reference via instruments), and the modern LLM-grounding debate (referential grounding without perception, Mollo & Millière) — independently agrees that non-perceptual, instrument/measurement-mediated causal contact is a complete and legitimate grounding channel for genuinely abstract, non-perceptible scientific entities. Chasing embodied/sensorimotor grounding for genes and synergy coefficients would be solving a problem that does not apply to this domain (matches the earlier information-theoretic finding already banked in `research_grounding_topicB_synthesis_and_next_levers_2026-07-14.md`: Data Processing Inequality / Information Causality prove the ONLY grounding paths are bake-in of external measured data or active sampling — sensorimotor perception is one *instance* of "external channel," not a requirement; instrument measurement is an equally valid instance).

**Does it matter for glass-box reasoning?** Yes, directly and predictively. The standing track record (`project_relational_capability_is_the_core_requirement_make_it_real_USER_2026-07-10.md`, 6 on-substrate negatives of relational codes failing to beat frequency/popularity) is best explained by relational structure having been built over a **closed relational island** (a symbol graph with no reachable measured anchors) — exactly the regime philosophy of mind says cannot ground. The reachability audit is therefore not academic hygiene: it predicts, per-entity, where relational/geometric reasoning is expected to underperform (closed-island entities) versus where it has a real chance (measured-module-reachable entities), turning a philosophical distinction into an operational, falsifiable diagnostic.

## Cheap decisive test

Build and run the reachability-audit tool (not yet implemented; cheap — a k-hop graph traversal from every canonical-ID hub entity to the nearest node carrying genuine instrument-measured data, e.g. an epistasis ε-score, synergy score, or nonadditivity coefficient) over the current foundation graph (hub registry + whatever measured modules are ingested to date). For each entity, record: (i) reachable within k<=2 hops to a measured node (**grounded**) vs not (**closed relational island**); (ii) existing relational-inference held-out accuracy for both groups, using the same eval already used for the grounding-improves-relation-inference cell (`research_multi_attribute_grounding_fusion_ATL_hub_2026-07-10.md` lineage).

**HARD-PASS** (architecture is genuinely doing the grounding job, verdict confirmed):
- (i) a clear majority (>=60%) of canonical-ID hub entities that participate in at least one ingested module are reachable within k<=2 hops to a measured node;
- (ii) reachable ("grounded") entities show materially better held-out relational-inference accuracy than closed-island entities on the SAME eval (>=15% relative gap), replicating the existing "GROUNDED_ONLY 0.618 >> RELATIONAL 0.387" pattern already observed on a per-audited-group basis rather than in aggregate;
- (iii) a deliberately-injected bare-ID entity (canonical ID registered, zero module content) is correctly flagged as a closed relational island, not silently treated as grounded.

**HARD-FAIL** (any of):
- (i) a majority (>50%) of hub entities are closed relational islands — module coverage is far too sparse for "largely subsumed" to hold in practice; the priority becomes coverage-expansion (more measured modules), not new architecture, but the verdict downgrades from "largely subsumed" to "subsumed only for a thin, densely-measured core";
- (ii) closed-island and reachable entities perform statistically indistinguishably on relational-inference accuracy — meaning the reachability distinction, however philosophically correct, is NOT operationally load-bearing for reasoning quality on this substrate, undermining the "matters for reasoning" half of the verdict (would need re-drilling why, likely a different confound such as degree/popularity dominating both groups equally);
- (iii) the audit cannot distinguish bare-ID from measured-reachable entities cleanly (e.g. every entity is trivially "reachable" because the graph is densely enough connected regardless of measured content) — meaning k-hop reachability is too weak a discriminator and a stricter measure (e.g. requiring the *specific* relation being reasoned about, not any relation, to be measured-anchored) is needed instead.

## Substrate-product implications

1. Do not build a sensorimotor/embodied/perceptual grounding subsystem for this domain — three independent literatures agree it is not required and would not be the right kind of grounding for non-perceptible scientific entities regardless.
2. Do not credit canonical-ID hub membership alone as "grounded" in any product-facing claim, metric, or cap_map row — reserve that word for entities/relations with an audited, measured-module-reachable path. Update any existing cap_map language that currently conflates "has a canonical ID" with "is grounded."
3. Build the reachability-audit tool next (cheap, no new data, a graph traversal) — it is the single missing piece that converts "architecture that should ground" into "architecture verified to ground," and it doubles as a predictive diagnostic for where relational reasoning will and won't work, directly actionable against the standing 6-instance relational-code-failure track record.
4. When scoping new measured-interaction modules (chemistry, epistasis, synergy, and beyond), prioritize by how much they *increase reachability* (bring previously-bare hub entities within k-hop reach of a measured node) over raw row-count or novelty — reachability, not module count, is the actual grounding-coverage metric.
5. Frame this externally (if ever product-facing) as: "this system's facts are anchored the same way scientific theoretical terms have always been anchored — via measurement, not via having eyes" — an honest, defensible framing that does not overclaim perceptual/embodied understanding the substrate does not have and does not need.

## Falsifiable predictions summary (HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL)

See "Cheap decisive test" above for the full pre-registered thresholds. Compressed:
- P1 (identity-hub-alone is insufficient for grounding): HARD-PASS if reachable vs closed-island entities show a real accuracy gap on relational inference; HARD-FAIL if indistinguishable.
- P2 (current architecture's measured modules provide majority coverage): HARD-PASS if >=60% of hub entities are within k<=2 hops of measured data; HARD-FAIL if <50%.
- P3 (reachability, not raw connectivity, is the correct discriminator): HARD-FAIL if k-hop reachability is trivially satisfied by graph density regardless of measured content (needs a stricter, relation-specific version).

Per lit-scan calibration discipline: **P_deflated = 0.45** (near, not at, the 0.50 novel-synthesis cap). Raised toward the cap relative to typical novel-synthesis drills because this verdict is a convergent capstone over 2+ independent well-evidenced literatures (philosophy of science's causal-instrument-contact account, and the brain's relay/indirect-grounding account) plus prior on-substrate empirical results (grounded-alone 0.618 >> relational 0.387 from the 2026-07-14 grounding-reasoning cell) that already point the same direction — not a first-principles guess. Deflated below the cap because: (i) no single source explicitly fuses "instrument-measurement = grounding channel" with "therefore a numeric-literal KG is grounded" — this drill's fusion of those two literatures is itself the novel step; (ii) the reachability-audit tool that would make this verdict directly testable has not yet been built or run; (iii) the "identity ≠ grounding" framing, while a natural reading of the source material, is this drill's own sharpening, not a directly-quoted position from any cited paper.
