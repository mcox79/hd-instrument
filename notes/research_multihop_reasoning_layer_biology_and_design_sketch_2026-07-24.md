## (a) HEADLINE

**The substrate does not need a new multi-hop reasoning mechanism — it already has one (`substrate/khop.py`), already validated at production-relevant depth (2-hop FB15K-237 r@5=0.705, WebQSP 97.6%, CWQ 92.6%, Merkle-audited per-hop). The open question is NOT "can the substrate chain," it's "is ARC-Challenge's flatness actually a chaining problem at all."** Lit-scan evidence (WorldTree explanation-graph annotations + a 2025 methodology critique) says ARC's typical explanation is a ~6-fact **support SET** (parallel facts, not a strict subject→relation→object path) and that a meaningful chunk of the ARC-Challenge/Easy gap is an **evaluation-format artifact**, not depth. This reframes the whole question: build a diagnostic BEFORE building a chain-cell. Biology backs this reframe directly — the brain's transitive-inference literature shows small rehearsed relational sets are read out as a **single integrated comparison** (not hop-by-hop), while genuine multi-step path-planning (TEM/hippocampal-replay literature) IS modeled as iterative hop-by-hop retrieval-with-cleanup — these are two different cognitive regimes mapping to two different ARC failure hypotheses, and only one of them is what `khop.py` is built for.

## (b) Cheap decisive test

**Before authoring any new cell:** classify a stratified sample (n=40-60) of ARC-Challenge questions the current fact-retrieval substrate gets wrong, by REQUIRED TASK SHAPE, using the existing WorldTree/QASC explanation annotations (public, already-labeled — no new labeling infra needed):
1. Single-fact, distractor-resistant (retrieval-precision problem, not chaining)
2. Multi-fact **aggregate/parallel support set** (WorldTree-shape: ~6 co-required facts, no relational path between them)
3. Genuine 2-3-hop **relational path** (subject→rel1→obj1→rel2→obj2, WebQSP/CWQ-shape — the shape `khop.py` targets)
4. Implicit/unstated background knowledge not present as any KB triple (a generalization gap, not a retrieval gap)

This costs ~1-2 hours of annotation-reuse (WorldTree V2 already has structured explanation graphs per question — this is a lookup/classification pass, not new annotation) and answers, empirically, whether a chain-cell is even the right tool. **Do not skip this step** — it is the direct analog of the Stage-2 benchmark reframe's core lesson (`preregs/2026-07-03_stage2_benchmark_reframe...md`): every prior HARD_FAIL in this codebase's history came from testing a mechanism on a task-shape it wasn't designed for.

## (c) Falsifiable predictions

**Prediction 1 (task-shape distribution).** Category 2 (aggregate/parallel support set) will be the PLURALITY category, category 3 (genuine path-chain) a MINORITY (~15-30%).
- HARD-PASS: category 3 <= 30% of the sample.
- HARD-FAIL: category 3 >= 60% of the sample (would mean ARC really is path-chain-shaped and khop.py is directly the right lever — a good outcome, just a different prediction miss).

**Prediction 2 (if category-3 subset is built as a cell).** A 2-hop khop.py cell over the vetted `hd_fact_store.py` KB, restricted to the category-3 subset, will score well ABOVE a single-hop-only baseline (the fair can-fail discriminator) but will NOT reach the >=0.70 production numbers already seen on FB15K-237/WebQSP, because ARC's KB coverage (elementary-science facts) is far sparser than Freebase/Wikidata.
- HARD-PASS: chain-accuracy >= 0.70 on category-3 subset AND single-hop baseline <= 0.30 (proves the discriminator can fail) AND audit-chain Merkle-verifies 100%.
- HARD-FAIL: chain-accuracy statistically indistinguishable from single-hop baseline (within 1 seed's noise band) — means the questions aren't really chain-shaped even after classification, OR coverage is so sparse that cleanup never has a clean target to snap to.
- MIDDLE-BAND (most likely per calibration penalty): chain beats baseline but < 0.70 — diagnostic for **coverage-limited, not depth-limited** (see (d) below — this is the precedent pattern for this exact codebase).

**Prediction 3 (depth envelope is a non-issue for ARC).** Whatever ARC's real hop-need turns out to be, it will be <= 4 (per HotpotQA=2, CWQ<=4, MetaQA<=3, StrategyQA~2.93-step precedent), which sits nowhere near the substrate's own measured depth cliff (d=25 collapse, K_max~5-8 in a separate stress test). If category-3 accuracy is still poor at hop-depth<=4, depth-extension work (R8 rescues, FHRR/hybrid) is NOT the fix — coverage or per-fact contention is.
- HARD-FAIL trigger for depth-extension investment: if a diagnostic shows failures cluster at hop-depth <=3, do NOT authorize resonator/hybrid depth-extension work; it would be optimizing a non-bottleneck.

Deflated P estimates throughout (lit-scan calibration penalty, -0.15 to -0.25 applied): P(category-3 is dominant ARC shape) ~0.20-0.30 (deflated from a naive ~0.45 prior); P(2-hop cell reaches HARD-PASS if built) ~0.35-0.45; P(the whole reframe changes the ARC strategy, i.e., MEDIUM-or-higher-importance finding) ~0.55-0.65.

This refines wave14e's "F is the master variable" into "the WORST-CASE-contention facts in the chain are the master variable" — real KGs have skewed (hub/degree) structure, so average-F predictions can be optimistic if a chain passes through a high-degree node. This connects to this codebase's own prior percolation-theory annotation on `PP-11` (assortative-correlation degree-effects on giant-component coverage) — the two literatures (VSA-mechanistic and this codebase's own percolation annotation) are independently converging on the same "degree-heterogeneity is the real risk, not average density" story.

## Biology synthesis (2 lit-scan agents, cross-checked)

**Transitive inference (small rehearsed relational sets) is NOT hop-by-hop at retrieval.** Dusek & Eichenbaum 1997 (PNAS) and Bunsey & Eichenbaum 1996 (Nature): hippocampal lesions spare learning of individual premise pairs but abolish flexible recombination (the inferential B>D judgment) — hippocampus builds the capacity to recombine, but does so during TRAINING. At test, the symbolic-distance effect (larger rank-gaps = FASTER/more-accurate, not slower) is the opposite of what step-by-step chaining predicts, and is best explained by an integrated ordinal "mental line" representation read out in a single comparison. Rostrolateral PFC (Wendelken/Bunge) contributes the explicit manipulation/recombination on top of the hippocampal relational substrate.

**Genuine multi-step PATH reasoning (planning/graph-traversal over a large novel space) IS modeled as iterative hop-by-hop retrieval-with-cleanup.** TEM (Whittington et al. 2020, Cell) explicitly chains: grid-cell-like structural state binds with sensory content, stores associatively (Hopfield-style), entorhinal recurrence predicts the next state, hippocampal memory is re-queried — repeated pattern-completion cycles, one hop at a time. Jensen, Hennequin & Mattar 2024 (Nat. Neurosci.) make this literal with a meta-RL rollout model tied to hippocampal replay: one imagined action per step, explicit rollout depth cap (tested 4/8/12, plateau ~15) — a real behavioral depth limit in a computational model, interestingly close in magnitude to this codebase's own empirically observed K_max~5-8 envelope (a resonance worth noting, NOT a proof of correspondence — different systems, same order of magnitude, flagged appropriately per calibration discipline).

**These are two different cognitive regimes mapping to two different ARC hypotheses.** Small rehearsed relational sets (TI, ~5-8 items) → single-shot integrated comparison. Large/novel graph traversal (TEM/replay) → iterative hop-by-hop retrieval-with-cleanup — this is what `khop.py` already implements and what real KG benchmarks (WebQSP/CWQ) actually need. ARC-Challenge's WorldTree-annotated ~6-fact explanations look, per the lit-scan, more like **neither** cleanly — closer to an aggregate SUPPORT SET (several facts jointly required, not necessarily a directed relational path between them) than to either TI's tiny ordinal scale or TEM's directed graph traversal. This is the biological grounding for why the diagnostic in (b) matters: applying path-chain machinery to a support-SET task would be exactly the "mechanism analog is not task analog" mismatch this codebase has repeatedly hit and repeatedly had to correct for (5-witness pattern in the Stage-2 reframe prereg; the 3 early multi-hop-revival HARD_FAILs).

**Capacity limits, biological.** No paper found deriving a hop-count bound directly FROM the CA3 autoassociative-capacity formula (Treves & Rolls: p_max ≈ C_RC · a·ln(1/a) · k — a total-associations bound, not natively a depth bound). Halford's relational-complexity framework gives the closest quantitative "how much can be held/composed at once" number: humans handle a quaternary relation (~4 interacting variables) in parallel before requiring serial segmentation/chunking; quinary is rare even under optimal conditions. This is a soft biological analog for "why the substrate's own realistic operating depth (2-4 hops, matching real benchmark hop-needs) is the right target, not depth-25."

**Attractor reset (cleanup) between steps is the dominant, well-supported biological mechanism** — CA3 recurrent-collateral pattern completion re-stabilizes a degraded/partial cue to a discrete stored attractor at each step (Guzman et al. 2016; "signature of attractor dynamics in CA3," PMC4031055). No literature was found supporting "graceful noise accumulation without reset" as a biologically operative alternative. This directly validates `khop.py`'s cleanup-per-hop design as the biologically correct choice, not an engineering workaround — a genuine, cross-domain (biology + wave14e math + 2026 VSA lit) convergence.

## Envelope fit (question 3, direct answer)

- WorldTree/WorldTree V2 (Jansen 2018, Xie 2020 LREC): ARC/science-QA explanations average **6 facts, range 1-16**. Substantially higher than HotpotQA (2, fixed), CWQ (up to 4), MetaQA (1-3), QASC (fixed at 2), StrategyQA (~2.93 steps). But — critically — WorldTree's structure is an **explanation GRAPH**, and multiple facts are frequently co-required in parallel (e.g., "sound needs a vibration" + "vibration needs a medium") rather than chained subject→relation→object→relation→object. This is a genuinely different task shape from the path-chain khop.py targets.
- A 2025 methodology paper ("ARC 'Challenge' Is Not That Challenging," arXiv:2412.17758, ACL Findings 2025) found that a large share of the modern-LLM ARC-Challenge-vs-Easy accuracy gap is attributable to **evaluation-format artifact** (per-option scoring vs. all-options-shown), closing up to 6x of the gap for a 70B model by switching format alone. This is an important caveat on the premise "ARC-Challenge is flat because it needs multi-hop reasoning" — some of the flatness may not be a reasoning-depth problem at all.
- **Direct answer to the question as posed:** if ARC-Challenge questions genuinely need chained (not aggregate) multi-hop reasoning, the realistic hop-count (2-4, per comparable benchmarks) is well within the substrate's already-validated production envelope (2-hop FB15K r@5=0.705, WebQSP 97.6%, CWQ 92.6%) and nowhere near the d=25 cliff. So **if** the wall persists after building a category-3-only chain-cell, it is very unlikely to be a depth-mechanism problem — it is much more likely to be (a) KB coverage of elementary-science facts (matching the multihop_revival precedent exactly), (b) the aggregate-support-set task-shape mismatch, or (c) evaluation-format sensitivity unrelated to reasoning at all. This matches the task's own hypothesis in question 3, now with lit-backed and disk-verified support rather than just a hunch.

## (e) Substrate-product implications

- **Do not re-derive multi-hop chaining from scratch.** `substrate/khop.py` + `substrate/audit.py` are production-validated, Merkle-audited, and already reused across 3 real KG benchmarks. The reasoning-layer story for the human-scale climb is "reuse the existing glass-box K-hop chainer over the new vetted fact store," not "invent a new reasoning primitive."
- **The most valuable next investment is a coverage/task-shape DIAGNOSTIC, not a depth-extension cell.** This is a cheap (~1-2hr), high-information-value first move that could save weeks of misdirected engineering (R8 depth-extension work, resonator hybrids) if ARC's wall turns out to be aggregate-shape or coverage, matching the exact pattern this codebase already lived through once with the hypernym multihop revival.
- **The audit-chain / per-hop-provenance mechanism is a genuine differentiator to carry into the ARC-facing product story regardless of outcome** — "every reasoning hop is Merkle-verifiable" is already built and already self-tested; this is a real, inspectable capability no black-box LLM retrieval baseline offers, and it costs nothing extra to surface in the ARC design.
- **If the diagnostic shows genuine 2-3-hop path structure is a meaningful minority of ARC's wrong-answer set**, the concrete next cell (for exp_dev, when ARC fact-retrieval climb lands and this hands off) is: 2-hop khop.py cell over `hd_fact_store.py`, single-hop-only fair-fail baseline, HARD-PASS/HARD-FAIL/MIDDLE-BAND bands exactly as pre-registered in (c) above. Do not build this cell before the diagnostic — building it blind repeats the exact mistake the 3 early multihop-revival attempts made.
- **Degree-heterogeneity (hub facts) is a real, under-examined risk** for any real (non-synthetic, non-uniform) KG chain — both the 2026 VSA mechanistic paper and this codebase's own prior percolation annotation converge on this. Worth a percolation/network-science drill (see next-drill candidate below) before over-committing engineering time to a chain-cell whose failure mode might be concentrated in a small number of high-contention entities rather than distributed depth-decay.
