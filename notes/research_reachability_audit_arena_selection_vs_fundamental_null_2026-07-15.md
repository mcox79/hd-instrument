## HEADLINE

1. **The v2 gate did its job correctly — it detected a true property of the chosen arena (80.5% homophily-dominated), not a broken control.** MAIN=0.0242, RANDOM=0.0011 (22.3x arena margin — the KGE fit is doing real work), but SCRAMBLE_REFIT=0.0197 sits close to MAIN: only 0.0045 of the 0.0231 beyond-random margin (19.5%) survives a train-time relation-label-shuffle-and-refit. That is a correct, standard "relation ablation" reading (RotatE-style relation-pattern analysis), and it says: on *this* induced CSKG subgraph, ~81% of what looks like "relational" competence is actually homophily/degree-carried structure the model recovers even with relation identity destroyed.
2. **The SAME code, on a different arena, already shows the opposite regime — this is the strongest single piece of evidence for arena-selection over fundamental null.** The v2 self-test runs on `build_planted_bucket_arena` (n=150, deliberately relation-diverse by construction) and gets `scramble_fires=True`, `rel_specific_frac=0.75` — i.e. the identical tool, same gate math, same fit recipe, flips from homophily-dominated to relation-dominated purely as a function of which graph it's run on. This is an existence proof internal to the project, not an analogy borrowed from outside literature.
3. **External KGE/network-science literature independently and heavily confirms that benchmark/arena composition is a known, quantified, large-effect driver of homophily-vs-relation-specific fraction.** Dettmers et al. 2018 (ConvE, arXiv:1707.01476) quantify a trivial inverse-relation rule scoring MRR .963/.660 on WN18/FB15k (leakage-dominated) collapsing to .35/.010 once the leakage is engineered out (WN18RR/FB15k-237) — a >10x-90x swing in "how much of the score is structural vs relational" driven purely by which subgraph/benchmark variant is used. Safavi & Koutra 2020 (CoDEx, arXiv:2009.07810) show a pure relation-frequency baseline matches embeddings on ~40% of FB15k-237 test triples even in the "cleaned" benchmark — genuine relation-specific signal is realistically a MINORITY-concentrated phenomenon even in good arenas, which calibrates how much lift to expect.
4. **A direct, specific, verifiable mechanism for WHY the current CSKG-induced subgraph lands homophily-heavy: ConceptNet-family graphs are documented to be dominated by one generic, near-symmetric relation.** Li et al. 2019 (arXiv:1905.05538) note ConceptNet's structure is dominated by the generic `RelatedTo` relation. CSKG is a ConceptNet-inclusive merge; the induced subgraph used in v1/v2 was selected for graph-theoretic density (dense k-core), not relation-type diversity — exactly the selection criterion the RotatE/CoDEx/ConvE literature identifies as the one that produces homophily-dominated arenas. This is not a generic "maybe" — it names the specific relation family to filter.
5. **Brain-grounding gives a real but tempering verdict: path-length/graph-distance is a genuine, independently-documented predictor of relational-inference/retrieval difficulty in SOME paradigms, but it is consistently secondary to, or structurally entangled with, degree/hub-ness — never cleanly dissociated from it in the literature.** This means: don't expect a relation-diverse re-slice to produce a *dominant* reachability signal; expect a real, modest, still-degree-adjacent one, consistent with what v2 already found (partial_rho 0.14-0.16 — real, p=0.002, but not large).

## Brain-grounding detail (full sub-agent report, medium confidence)

- **Symbolic distance effect (transitive inference):** Moyer & Landauer 1967 (*Nature*), Potts 1972 (*JVLVB*/*JML* 11(6):727-740), Vasconcelos 2008 (*Behavioural Processes* 78(3):313-334) — RT is a monotonic DECREASING function of ordinal distance: **more remote pairs are answered FASTER, not slower.** This is the opposite direction from "more hops = harder" — a load-bearing caution against over-reading reachability-as-difficulty by direct analogy to this specific paradigm.
- **Hippocampal cognitive-map graph distance:** Behrens et al. 2018 (*Neuron* 100(2):490, "What Is a Cognitive Map?") frames hippocampal-entorhinal coding as generically relational/graph-structured. A 2024 eLife paper (eLife 93357 / PMC11136493, "Reactivation strength during cued recall is modulated by graph distance within cognitive maps") found reactivation strength DECREASES as graph distance increases, and only on successful-retrieval trials — the cleanest case in the literature of graph-distance predicting difficulty in the expected direction, but for retrieval-within-a-learned-map, not inference-accuracy in a link-prediction-style task.
- **PFC relational-integration load:** Halford (relational complexity theory, BBS), Waltz et al. 1999 (*Psychological Science*, PFC-lesion patients show catastrophic multi-relation-integration deficits) — difficulty scales with NUMBER OF RELATIONS SIMULTANEOUSLY INTEGRATED (working-memory load), a different construct from sequential path-length, though thematically adjacent (this echoes the project's own already-locked "resolution scales with # constraints" reasoning-theory finding — an independent cross-confirmation worth noting for cross-thread synthesis, not a new claim).
- **Degree/hub-ness vs distance — the load-bearing entanglement finding:** Steyvers & Tenenbaum 2005 (*Cognitive Science* 29(1), arXiv:cond-mat/0110012) show semantic networks are small-world/scale-free — **short path length is a byproduct of hub topology**, meaning degree and path-length are structurally confounded in real semantic graphs, not independently manipulable constructs. No source found in this scan cleanly holds degree/fan constant while varying path-length. This directly explains, biologically, WHY v2's own raw_spearman(reachability, RR)~0.22 is so close to spearman(degree,RR)~0.16 — the entanglement is not a substrate artifact, it is the generic structure of real relational graphs, brain-associative or KG alike. It also somewhat RAISES the value of v2's partial-rho result (0.14-0.16, p=0.002 both seeds): succeeding in showing a degree-independent residual at all is a harder-won result than the literature usually manages to isolate cleanly.
- **Competing predictors (interference/fan, schema-consistency):** Anderson's fan effect and van Kesteren et al. 2010 (*J. Neurosci.* 30(47):15888) schema-congruency effects are real, well-replicated, and largely ORTHOGONAL to distance — meaning if the audit tool wants a stronger biologically-motivated relational-failure predictor down the line, degree/fan and schema-consistency are legitimate alternative or additive channels, not competitors that refute reachability.

## Substrate-product implications

1. **Do not drop the reachability-audit tool.** The tool/methodology is validated as working correctly (self-test firing on a relation-diverse arena, correct fail-closed behavior on a homophily-dominated one); the negative result localizes to ARENA CHOICE, a fixable input, not a flaw in the audit's logic.
2. **Any future map-builder or reasoning-map construction that selects an induced subgraph "by density/k-core" alone (as v1/v2's arena was selected) should be flagged as likely to land homophily-heavy** — this is a second, independent confirmation of the standing discipline already established in the percolation/reachability note (`Prediction 3`: graph centrality and exogenous groundedness/relation-richness are not interchangeable selection criteria). Extend that discipline: density-based subgraph selection also does not guarantee relation-type diversity, and CSKG specifically is known (Li et al. 2019) to concentrate mass in one generic relation.
3. **Calibrate expectations for the audit's ultimate predictive value downward from "strong standalone predictor" to "real, modest, degree-adjacent signal" even in the best case**, per the brain literature's consistent finding that path-length/graph-distance is never cleanly dissociated from degree/hub-ness in any paradigm examined. If the tool ships as a relational-failure predictor, it should be reported and used as ONE modest signal among several (alongside degree, fan/interference-style local density, and schema/prior-consistency-style measures) — not oversold as a dominant one.
4. **The zero-compute stratify-by-relation-type re-analysis of the already-collected v2 data should be tried before spending a new remote CPU run** — if the existing artifacts retain per-triple relation labels, this is a free discriminating look.
