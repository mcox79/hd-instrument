# BRAIN-DRILL: mental simulation / model-based reasoning / grounding -- does fact-retrieval+combine miss the mechanism the brain uses for causal science questions?

**Date:** 2026-07-24. **Filed by:** research (3 parallel Sonnet lit-scans + director synthesis).
**Trigger:** the ARC-answering pipeline currently in flight (`the_climb_wire_meaning_ingest_vetted_triples_arc_retrieval_2026-07-24.md`) answers science MC questions by SemanticHDEncoder fact-retrieval + argmax over a trust-vetted triple store. A person answering "a planet rotates faster after an impact -> most likely effect? (shorter days)" may not be retrieving a stored proposition at all -- they may be SIMULATING the rotating planet, or running a qualitative causal model. This drill asks: is retrieve-facts+combine missing that mechanism, and if so is it THE gap or a secondary one.

## HEADLINE

**The literature converges cleanly on a THREE-WAY split, not a binary "simulate vs. retrieve" -- and the substrate's actual gap is narrower and more mechanical than "we lack simulation."**

1. For genuinely dynamic/perceptual physical prediction (will the tower fall, which way does the gear turn), the established mechanism is RUNNING A MODEL FORWARD -- either a noisy analog physics simulator (Battaglia, Hamrick & Tenenbaum 2013, *PNAS*, "intuitive physics engine") or a step-by-step mental animation of one mechanical component at a time (Hegarty 1992/2004, evidenced by dual-task interference specifically with VISUOSPATIAL not verbal working memory -- a strong, direct signature that this is NOT propositional retrieval). Deflated P=0.45 that this class is real and distinct from fact-lookup (well-established in primary lit; deflation is for the generalization, not the core finding).

2. For causal reasoning over an ALREADY-IDENTIFIED structured relationship (a known graph of variables and causal links), the established mechanism is Pearl-style INTERVENTIONIST COMPUTATION -- people derive correct answers for held-out, never-observed hypothetical interventions by propagating do(X) through the graph, not by recalling a similar observed instance (Gopnik, Glymour, Sobel, Schulz, Kushnir & Danks 2004, *Psych Review*; Waldmann & Hagmayer's "seeing vs. doing" asymmetry; Sloman 2005 book; Ahn/Rehder causal-model categorization). This is the single most relevant literature for the "planet spins faster -> day length" item class: it is a monotonic, two-variable COUPLEDRELATIONSHIP (rotation rate up -> day length down) sitting inside a taught causal structure, not a novel physical scene requiring perceptual simulation. Deflated P=0.45 (well-established that adults compute over structure rather than retrieve instances; deflation for applying it cleanly to this item class specifically).

3. For OVER-LEARNED, curriculum-taught covariation facts specifically -- once a relationship has been explicitly taught and is being tested verbatim or near-verbatim -- the honest literature finding is that this frequently reduces to CACHED, FRAGMENTARY, CUE-TRIGGERED KNOWLEDGE (diSessa's p-prims, 1993) or verbally-stored rules that can be recited WITHOUT a correct underlying runnable model (Chi/Slotta/Vosniadou's conceptual-change literature: students routinely state the correct textbook relationship while their functioning mental model still predicts the wrong direction on a transfer item). This is the crucial nuance: for CANONICAL, IN-DISTRIBUTION science-exam relationships, fact-retrieval of the correct covariation direction is not obviously wrong or even obviously suboptimal relative to what a human does -- humans also frequently just recall "I was taught X causes Y" for these. Deflated P=0.35 (this specific claim -- that canonical exam items route through cached covariation rather than simulation -- has NO direct study confirming it; it is an extrapolation from adjacent conceptual-change literature, honestly flagged as such by the lit-scan itself).

**Item 4 in the original ask (how much of science-exam performance specifically is simulation vs. retrieval) has THIN direct evidence.** All three lit-scans independently and honestly flagged that no study directly measures, on ARC-style rotation/day-length or pressure/volume MCQ items, whether correct answers correlate with verbatim recall vs. demonstrated transfer to unstated relationships. This is a real, searched-for, and confirmed literature gap -- not a search failure. The adjacent evidence (Force Concept Inventory work, conceptual-change literature) supports the general pattern (memorized formulas transfer poorly; working mental models transfer well) but was never run on this exact item class.

**BLUNT ASSESSMENT: this is a SECONDARY gap for the substrate, not a primary one -- but a real, cheap, well-targeted one.** The substrate is NOT missing the causal-model mechanism at the framework level: cap_map row PP-307 (`comp22_causal_at_l3_cpu_v1`, HARD_PASS, recall_l3=1.000, recall_l1=1.000, gap=0.000) already demonstrates Pearl do-calculus intervention propagating correctly through a composite L3 causal chain, extending the atomic founding result PP-270. The gap is that **the currently-staged ARC-answering pipeline (`the_climb`) does not route through that primitive at all** -- it does flat semantic fact-retrieval (encode Q+choice, cosine-match against stored triples, argmax), which is architecturally the WRONG mechanism specifically for item 2's class (coupled monotonic relationships requiring do()-propagation across >=2 variables) and only accidentally adequate for item 3's class (canonical single-hop covariation facts, where retrieval and "cached fact recall" coincide with what a human plausibly also does). This is a wiring/routing gap, not a missing-capability gap -- the fix is cheaper and more concrete than "add simulation," and is falsifiable before committing to a heavier build.

## Cheap decisive test

**Setup.** From the ARC-Easy/Challenge corpora already staged (`data/corpora/arc/ARC-V1-Feb2018-2/`), triage a subset of items whose gold answer requires a monotonic COUPLEDRELATIONSHIP ("X increases -> Y increases/decreases") spanning >=2 named variables not co-stated verbatim in a single retrievable sentence (e.g. rotation-rate/day-length, pressure/volume, distance/gravitational-force, insulation/heat-loss). This is exactly the item class the user's own example names ("COUPLEDRELATIONSHIP as X up Y down / IFTHEN which dominate the HARD ARC questions").

**Two arms, real baseline required (per design-gate discipline):**
- **Arm A (current):** the_climb's semantic fact-retrieval (encode Q+choice with SemanticHDEncoder, cosine-match stored triples, argmax) -- this is the REAL baseline already staged, not a strawman.
- **Arm B (do-calculus routed):** extract/encode the qualitative monotonic direction as a first-class COUPLEDRELATIONSHIP triple (variable_A, direction, variable_B) rather than a flat subject-predicate-object fact, and invoke the EXISTING do-calculus primitive (PP-270/307 code path) to propagate an intervention (do(rotation_rate = increased)) through the chain to read off the predicted direction of the target variable, then match against MC choices by direction-sign rather than lexical/semantic surface similarity.

One variable changes (retrieval-and-match vs. do-calculus-propagate-and-match); both arms share the same encoder, same store, same MC harness, same held-out ARC slice.

## Falsifiable predictions

**Prediction 1 (does the COUPLEDRELATIONSHIP subclass exist in non-trivial size).** P=0.40 (deflated).
**HARD-PASS:** the triage above finds a non-trivial (not near-empty, say >=5% of ARC-Easy or >=8% of ARC-Challenge) subset of items that are genuinely two-or-more-variable monotonic covariation questions not answerable from a single co-stated sentence.
**HARD-FAIL:** the subset is near-empty -- meaning most ARC "hard" items are actually single-hop covariation facts already coincident with fact-retrieval (item 3's class), and the do-calculus-routing rescue has no material item population to help. This would NOT be a wasted test: it would sharpen the diagnosis that the current fact-retrieval ceiling on Challenge is dominated by something else (coverage, or genuine multi-hop composition of DIFFERENT facts, not direction-propagation).

**Prediction 2 (does do-calculus routing beat fact-retrieval on that subclass specifically -- THE CENTRAL CLAIM).** P=0.40 (deflated, capped below the general 0.50 novel-synthesis ceiling because the exact combination -- ARC MC-QA answered via do()-propagation over extracted qualitative-covariation triples -- has never been tested, only the two halves separately: do-calculus composition works in isolation (PP-307), and fact-retrieval works in isolation (the_climb's own upcoming numbers)).
**HARD-PASS:** on the Prediction-1 subset, Arm B's accuracy exceeds Arm A's by a margin that clears noise (e.g. >=10pp absolute on this subset, n sufficient for the gap to be non-trivial relative to seed variance), with the same no-answer-key-leak and broad-ingest controls the_climb already mandates.
**HARD-FAIL:** Arm B does not beat Arm A on this subset, or underperforms it -- meaning either (a) extraction of clean (variable, direction, variable) triples from ARC_Corpus/ConceptNet text is too noisy/low-coverage to matter (a coverage failure, distinguish from mechanism failure by checking whether Arm B's do-calculus step ever actually fires vs. sits idle for lack of extracted structure), or (b) the covariation-direction-match scoring is itself no more accurate than semantic retrieval was already doing implicitly. Either sub-cause should be reported, not blended.

**Prediction 3 (canonical single-hop covariation facts do NOT need do-calculus routing -- the honest complementary control).** P=0.35 (deflated).
**HARD-PASS:** on ARC items OUTSIDE the Prediction-1 subset (single-hop, co-stated covariation facts), Arm A (plain fact-retrieval) performs at least as well as Arm B -- confirming the literature's item-3 finding (cached covariation recall is adequate/human-plausible for canonical in-distribution relationships) and confirming the do-calculus rescue should be SCOPED to the multi-hop subclass, not applied universally.
**HARD-FAIL:** Arm B beats Arm A even on single-hop items too -- which would be a genuinely surprising, informative result suggesting the do-calculus/COUPLEDRELATIONSHIP encoding is simply a better-structured representation across the board, not narrowly useful for multi-hop chains; would justify replacing the flat-triple store's representation more broadly rather than adding a routing branch.

**Cross-check.** If 1 passes and 2 passes: ship the do-calculus routing branch as a scoped addition to the_climb, gated on the Prediction-1 triage classifier (route COUPLEDRELATIONSHIP-class items to Arm B, everything else stays Arm A) -- this is the single cheapest, most concretely actionable outcome of this drill. If 1 passes but 2 fails: the mechanism gap is confirmed real (humans plausibly DO use do-calculus-style propagation here per the Gopnik/Sloman literature) but the substrate's EXTRACTION of clean covariation triples from noisy corpus text is the bottleneck, not the do-calculus primitive itself (which already passed PP-307) -- redirect to triple-extraction quality, not to abandoning the routing idea. If 1 fails: the ARC "hard" residual is not dominated by this item class at all; do not invest further in do-calculus routing for ARC specifically (though PP-270/307 remain valid, useful primitives for other tasks). If 3 fails (Arm B beats Arm A universally): treat as a representation-upgrade signal, escalate scope.

## Cross-thread synthesis

**Composes with `research_mental_simulation_scene_verifier_error_signal_2026-07-19.md`:** that drill asked the same simulate-vs-retrieve question at the sentence-parse grain (does a scene-coherence check help pick the right syntactic reading) and landed on a discrete WordNet/VerbNet attribute-lookup as the buildable, brain-consistent answer -- NOT full perceptual simulation, because (per that drill's Angle 3 findings, Louwerse's "linguistic processes precede perceptual simulation," and dual-coding theory) categorical/relational facts of that kind are handled by fast symbolic/verbal processing, with perceptual simulation reserved for slower/deeper tasks. This drill's finding is the SAME shape one level up: canonical, taught, categorical covariation facts (item 3's class) are plausibly handled by cached/verbal recall too, while GENUINELY novel or multi-variable causal composition (item 2's class) needs structure-propagation. The throughline across both drills: **the mechanism scales with novelty/depth of the question, not with "is it a causal/scientific topic."** Shallow, taught, single-hop facts -> retrieval. Deep, compositional, or genuinely novel structure -> propagate/simulate. Neither drill finds evidence that PIXEL-level perceptual simulation is required for either grain -- Johnson-Laird-style structured (non-perceptual) models and Pearl-style causal graphs are the operative "simulation," and both are symbolic/glass-box-compatible, not requiring an embodied/perceptual substrate. This directly counters a naive reading of "we lack simulation/grounding" as requiring pixels or embodiment -- the actual missing piece, per this literature, is graph-structured do()-propagation, which the substrate has already built and banked (PP-270/307).

## Substrate-product implications

If Predictions 1 and 2 both pass: this is a cheap, scoped, high-leverage addition -- reusing an ALREADY-BANKED primitive (do-calculus, no new mechanism to build/validate from scratch) to close a specific, well-diagnosed piece of the ARC ceiling, with a natural product story (glass-box causal-graph reasoning over vetted facts, auditable at every do()-step, contrasted with an LLM's opaque pattern-completion on the same item class -- this composes cleanly with the existing FDA/regulatory-audit-traceability product angle, PP-215, since do-calculus propagation is inherently step-traceable). If 1 passes/2 fails: redirect investment to triple-extraction quality (a data/engineering problem) rather than mechanism-design, and keep the honest floor language ("ARC-Easy broad-ingest still near-chance") until extraction improves. If 1 fails: this specific rescue does not apply to ARC's residual and effort should stay on curriculum-breadth/coverage per the ingestion pivot's existing NEXT-sequence, not on causal-mechanism-building. Either way, the useful, durable product insight independent of the test outcome: for a knowledge-answering substrate, "does it need simulation" is not a single yes/no -- it decomposes into (a) shallow taught-fact recall [cheap, already have], (b) structured multi-variable causal composition [have the primitive, need routing -- this drill's target], (c) genuinely dynamic/perceptual physical prediction [NOT evidenced as needed for ARC-style science MCQ, no product case made here to build a pixel-level physics simulator].
