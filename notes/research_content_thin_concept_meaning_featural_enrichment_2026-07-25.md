## HEADLINE

**The literature gives an unusually clean, convergent answer, and it directly explains our own architecture's failure mode: the brain's fine-grained concept differentiation (hydroelectric-vs-nuclear-vs-coal) is NOT an emergent side-effect of more co-occurrence exposure — every computational/connectionist model that reproduces it (Rogers & McClelland's PDP semantic-cognition network) is trained on EXPLICIT item-relation-attribute property triples, not on passive text statistics, and every empirical comparison of distributional-vs-property representations (Lucy & Gauthier 2017; Grand/Blank/Pereira/Fedorenko 2022) finds distributional vectors are comparatively GOOD at coarse/taxonomic "same-category" structure and comparatively WEAK, entangled, and axis-dependent at exactly the perceptual/causal/mechanistic content that separates near-neighbors. Our own substrate is currently running the co-occurrence-only regime the literature predicts fails at fine differentiation — and our own diagnosed failure mode (retrieve nuclear/coal "power plant converts energy" facts for a hydroelectric question) is architecturally the same signature as semantic-dementia patients whose ATL hub is damaged: superordinate category knowledge (it's a power plant, it makes electricity) survives, subordinate/specific content (which mechanism) is lost first (Warrington 1975; Hodges, Graham & Patterson 1995; Bozeat et al. 2000).**

The cheapest, most brain-faithful fix is a DIFFERENT lever than the prior drill's typed relation-structure fix (which addresses argument-order/relation-type, not filler content): **bind each concept's own DISTINGUISHING PROPERTY RELATIONS — already present, unused, in the WorldTree tablestore (SOURCEOF, REQUIRES, CAUSE, KINDOF, MADEOF, PARTOF, PROP-*-RENEWABLE, etc.) — into the concept's OWN representation**, the same way Lambon Ralph's ATL hub integrates multiple modality/feature-specific spokes into one bound concept (Hoffman, Evans & Lambon Ralph 2014's direct finding: hub damage specifically breaks trials requiring COMBINING multiple feature dimensions, not single-feature recall) and the same way Rogers & McClelland's network is trained on multiple property triples per item, not one. This is feasible with zero new infrastructure: the role-bind primitive (`bind(REL,rel)+bind(ARG0,subj)+bind(ARG1,obj)`) is already built and proven at 1M-fact scale (`hdlab/hd_fact_store.py`); WorldTree's own tables already contain, in machine-readable form, exactly the rows that distinguish hydroelectric from nuclear from coal (verified on disk, see below) — the gap is that the concept encoder (`SemanticHDEncoder`) never reads them.

**Honest calibration caveat, confirmed independently by 2 of 3 lit-scans:** no paper anywhere in the literature directly tests whether property/feature-augmented representations improve SELECTION of the correct instance from a set of same-category lures (as opposed to general similarity/categorization/word-association tasks, or feature-verification response times). This is a genuine, confirmed gap — we would be the first to test this exact task shape. P estimates below are deflated accordingly.

## Biology + math synthesis

### 1. What the brain's concept representation actually contains, and its context-gated retrieval (Barsalou)

Barsalou's Perceptual Symbol Systems (1999, *BBS*) treats a concept not as a position in a similarity space but as a re-enactable simulation of the actual sensorimotor/introspective states experienced with the referent — a difference in KIND, not degree, from a co-occurrence statistic: co-occurrence gives relational position among words, situated simulation gives content ABOUT the referent (what fission is, what falling water does to a turbine). Barsalou's later situated-conceptualization work (2003 *Language & Cognitive Processes*; 2009 *Phil Trans R Soc B*; 2016) makes a second point directly relevant to our design: a concept is not one static bundle but "the productive ability to generate many different situated conceptualizations," each activating only the SITUATION-RELEVANT subset of stored content — i.e. the full distinguishing content (mechanism, fuel type) is stored, but only surfaces when the question calls for that axis. This maps cleanly onto product design: we do not need every concept to carry every property always-active; we need the DISTINGUISHING property bundle stored and available to be read when a question's frame calls for it (e.g. "how is X's electricity generated" triggers the mechanism axis; a generic "what is a power plant" question does not need it).

### 2. Why distributional co-occurrence is structurally the wrong tool (not just a coarser one)

The distributional hypothesis (Harris 1954; Firth 1957) formalizes SUBSTITUTABILITY in a paradigmatic slot — "___ power plant produces electricity" — which is exactly why hydroelectric/nuclear/coal cluster together: they are mutually substitutable in that slot, which is precisely the WRONG axis for us; we need them pulled apart on the very dimension co-occurrence collapses. This is not merely an intuition; it has direct empirical documentation. Distributional vectors are well-documented to conflate synonym/antonym/co-hyponym pairs because near-identical local context ("water is hot"/"water is cold") produces near-identical vectors (Nguyen, Schulte im Walde & Vu 2016, ACL); an entire sub-literature (Weeds, Clarke, Reffin, Weir & Keller 2014, COLING; the ROOT9/ROOT13 line, Santus et al.) exists purely BECAUSE plain distributional similarity cannot natively separate hypernym-pairs from co-hyponym-pairs from random pairs, requiring external supervised correction. **The single closest direct empirical match found in this scan**: Lucy & Gauthier (2017, ACL Language-Grounding-for-Robotics workshop) test static word embeddings against two independent human property-norm datasets and find embeddings encode TAXONOMIC properties (category membership — "these are all power plants") significantly more accurately than PERCEPTUAL/PHYSICAL properties (what it's made of, how it actually works) — exactly the axis separating hydro/nuclear/coal.

Grand, Blank, Pereira & Fedorenko (2022, *Nat Hum Behav*, "semantic projection") sharpen this further and answer the "is it hidden-but-recoverable" question directly: projecting a word vector onto a feature axis DEFINED BY AN ANTONYM PAIR recovers a graded human-like property judgment (median r~0.47 across 52 category-feature pairs), but projecting onto a single raw word vector alone collapses to r~0.06 — the feature content is present but ENTANGLED, decodable only if the diagnostic axis is supplied from outside the embedding, and the paper explicitly reports the method "fares relatively poorly" on perceptual/mechanistic properties specifically, the weakest case being exactly our use case. **This rules out "just add a linear readout to the existing GloVe vectors" as a viable content-enrichment path** — the content is weakest, entangled, and axis-dependent precisely where we need it; the axis itself (fuel-type/mechanism) has to come from an EXTERNAL structured source, not be discovered inside the co-occurrence geometry.

### 3. The strongest single piece of evidence: how the brain-model literature's OWN successful models actually get fine differentiation (Rogers & McClelland PDP)

Rogers & McClelland's semantic-cognition PDP framework (2004 MIT Press monograph; McClelland & Rogers 2003 *Nat Rev Neurosci*; Rogers & McClelland 2008 *BBS* précis) is the standard connectionist account of PROGRESSIVE DIFFERENTIATION: broad category distinctions (animal vs plant) emerge in development before fine within-category distinctions (robin vs canary), because broad distinctions are supported by many high-frequency, strongly-covarying input features while fine distinctions rest on rare, weakly-covarying ones ("coherent covariation"). **The load-bearing detail for our design, confirmed from the training-corpus structure**: the network that reproduces this differentiation is trained on explicit item-relation-attribute PROPOSITIONS — "canary CAN [fly]," "canary ISA [bird]," "robin IS [red-breasted]" — not on raw co-occurrence text. The properties that separate robin from canary (song, color) are supplied AS labeled training input; the model explains the LEARNING DYNAMICS/TIMING of differentiation given that distinguishing content is present in the training signal, not that co-occurrence exposure alone suffices to manufacture that content. This is the single strongest piece of literature-based justification for the specific fix this drill proposes: bind explicit property triples into the concept representation, rather than hoping richer training or a better readout over co-occurrence data will manufacture the same content.

### 4. Featural/encyclopedic content is TYPED, sparse, and diagnostically weighted by distinctiveness (McRae/Cree feature-norm tradition)

The human feature-norm literature (McRae, Cree, Seidenberg & McNorgan 2005, *Behavior Research Methods*; Cree & McRae 2003, *JEP:General*; Devereux et al. 2014 CSLB norms; Buchanan et al. 2019 extended norms) establishes concept knowledge decomposes into TYPED features — perceptual, functional, encyclopedic, taxonomic — and that most of the content that distinguishes a specific concept from its near-neighbors comes from a SMALL NUMBER of low-frequency, idiosyncratic (not broadly-shared) features: 67% of McRae features and 37% of Buchanan features occur in only a single concept. Cree, McNorgan & McRae (2006, *JEP:LMC*) directly show DISTINCTIVE features (occurring in few concepts) are verified FASTER than shared features in speeded categorization — the literature's clearest statement that distinctiveness itself carries privileged diagnostic weight for discrimination, though Randall et al. (2004) show the opposite pattern for living-thing categories specifically (a genuine boundary condition, not just noise). **Critically for our domain**: Ahn (1998, *Cognition*) shows CAUSAL/functional features (not perceptual ones) are the centrally-weighted feature type specifically for ARTIFACT/technical-kind categories — power plants, being artifact/process categories differentiated almost entirely by internal mechanism rather than appearance, are exactly the category type where mechanism/functional-relation content (SOURCEOF/REQUIRES/CAUSE-type facts), not perceptual content, should carry the diagnostic weight. This is a specific, literature-grounded reason to prioritize WorldTree's mechanism/property tables over any future perceptual-grounding investment for THIS domain.

### 5. The hub-and-spoke integration mechanism: fine distinctions require BINDING multiple typed features, not adding one

Lambon Ralph and colleagues' ATL hub-and-spoke model (Patterson, Nestor & Rogers 2007, *Nat Rev Neurosci*; Lambon Ralph, Jefferies, Patterson & Rogers 2017, *Nat Rev Neurosci*; Rogers et al. 2004, *Psych Review*) holds that the transmodal ATL hub's job is to INTEGRATE across modality/feature-specific spokes into one coherent, context-sensitive concept; the model's own architecture is trained jointly on perceptual AND verbal/relational inputs mapped through a shared hub layer specifically because either channel alone under-differentiates. The single sharpest direct mechanistic finding here is **Hoffman, Evans & Lambon Ralph (2014, *Cortex*)**: ATL-damaged (semantic dementia) patients can still learn INDIVIDUAL feature dimensions in isolation, but fail specifically on trials requiring COMBINING/weighing MULTIPLE feature dimensions to reach the correct categorization — i.e. the hub's function is combinatorial BINDING of several typed features into one judgment, not passive storage of any single feature. This directly informs the cell design below: the enrichment should BUNDLE multiple WorldTree property-relation types per concept (SOURCEOF + REQUIRES + KINDOF + PROP-RESOURCES-RENEWABLE, not just one), matching the hub's multi-feature integration, not a single added dimension.

The coarse-to-fine SD degradation gradient (Warrington 1975, *QJEP* — subordinate knowledge more impaired than superordinate, the founding observation; Hodges, Graham & Patterson 1995, *Memory* — patient stays in-range on broad category fluency, "animals," "household items," but is near-unable to generate exemplars of narrower categories, "breeds of dog," "musical instruments"; Bozeat, Lambon Ralph, Patterson, Garrard & Hodges 2000, *Neuropsychologia* — the degradation is amodal, not verbal-specific) is, mechanism-for-mechanism, an almost exact description of our own diagnosed failure: superordinate "power plant / converts energy" survives (the substrate correctly retrieves SOME power-plant fact), the subordinate "which mechanism" content is lost first. Our architecture is currently operating in the regime of a damaged, not an intact, semantic hub for this class of question — not because the substrate is incapable in principle, but because the concept encoder was never given the property content an intact hub integrates.

### 6. Computational precedent: external property/relation data measurably sharpens distributional vectors, and is NOT redundant with co-occurrence on fine distinctions

Faruqui, Dodge, Jauhar, Dyer, Hovy & Smith (2015, NAACL, retrofitting) and Speer, Chin & Havasi (2017, AAAI, ConceptNet Numberbatch — relation-graph-augmented embeddings, SOTA on rare-word similarity, first place across SemEval-2017 multilingual word-similarity subtasks) are direct, mature engineering precedent that binding external relational/property-graph content into distributional vectors measurably improves representation quality, and that the correction must come from an external structured resource (the raw co-occurrence space does not natively separate these distinctions — retrofitting/correction methods are themselves evidence of that). **Derby, Miller et al.'s Feature2Vec (2019, EMNLP)** is the closest direct engineering analog to what this drill proposes: it learns an embedding for each PROPERTY LABEL (not just each word) in the same space as pretrained word vectors, enabling composition of a concept vector from property labels — structurally identical in spirit to binding WorldTree property-relation fillers into a concept's HD vector. Andrews, Vigliocco & Vinson (2009, *Psych Review*) and the Riordan & Jones (2011, *Topics in Cognitive Science*) counterpoint together give the correctly-calibrated expectation: feature/property content and distributional content are substantially REDUNDANT on coarse category structure (so don't expect enrichment to help generic similarity judgments) but genuinely DIVERGENT on fine/idiosyncratic distinctions (so DO expect it to help exactly the hydro/nuclear/coal-type discrimination) — this is the precise, literature-calibrated expectation for our discriminator.

## Direct WorldTree tablestore verification (disk-checked, not assumed)

Confirmed on disk at `data/corpora/worldtree/WorldtreeExplanationCorpusV2.1_Feb2020/tablestore/v2.0/tables/` — the property-relation tables the encoder currently ignores already contain rows that DIRECTLY distinguish hydro/nuclear/coal by mechanism, not just category:

- `SOURCEOF.tsv`: **"a hydroelectric plant ... produces ... hydropower"**; **"... nuclear reactor ... generates ... electrical power"**; **"nuclear fusion ... is a source of ... energy"**; **"burning fossil fuels ... is a source of ... air pollution"**.
- `PROP-RESOURCES-RENEWABLE.tsv`: **"hydroelectric power is a renewable resource"**; **"fossil fuels are a nonrenewable resource"** — a direct, single-fact renewable/nonrenewable discriminator between hydro and coal.
- `CAUSE.tsv`: **"nuclear reactor ... can cause ... long-term waste disposal problems"**; **"fossil fuels burning ... causes ... pollution"**; **"gases released during the use of fossil fuels causes global warming"** — mechanism-specific downstream-effect facts unique to nuclear vs coal respectively.
- `KINDOF.tsv`: **"coal is a kind of fossil fuel"**; **"natural gas is a kind of fossil fuel"**; **"nuclear fusion is a kind of nuclear reaction"** — taxonomic scaffolding the encoder could bind alongside the property facts.
- `REQUIRES.tsv`: **"forming coal ... requires deposition and burial of decaying vegetation"** — a mechanism-specific formation fact for coal with no analog for hydro/nuclear (a genuinely distinguishing absence, not just a differing value).

This is exactly the typed, multi-relation, DISTINGUISHING property content the biology literature (sections 3-5 above) says an intact hub integrates and a PDP-style differentiation-producing model is trained on. It already exists, in machine-readable form, unused by `SemanticHDEncoder`.

## Cheap decisive test (can-fail cell design — NOT dispatched)

**Candidate anchor name:** `exp_concept_featural_enrichment_v1` (composes 29544/29545's harness: same wide contains-gold RR pool, same `sel_gold_precision` / end-to-end Challenge-accuracy metrics, oracle ceiling ~0.97; ONE variable = whether the CONCEPT representation itself carries bound property content, independent of any per-fact relation-structure feature).

**Arms:**
- **A. `baseline_thin_concept` (regression check)** — the existing flat `SemanticHDEncoder` concept vector (GloVe fusion + WordNet synonym/hypernym pull), unchanged. Must reproduce the established `sel_gold_precision` floor from 29544/29545 before anything else is trusted.
- **B. `featural_enriched_concept`** — for every concept/entity mentioned in a candidate fact or question stem, look it up (as subject OR object) across WorldTree's property-relation tables (`SOURCEOF`, `REQUIRES`, `CAUSE`, `KINDOF`, `MADEOF`, `PARTOF`, `PROP-RESOURCES-RENEWABLE`, and siblings), and BUNDLE `bind(PROP_REL_i, filler_i)` for each retrieved property triple into (added to, not replacing) the concept's existing flat vector — reusing the exact `bind`/`bundle`/`quantize` primitives from `hdlab/hd_fact_store.py` at the CONCEPT level instead of the fact-instance level. Cap at K properties per concept (K=5-8, matching Cowan-4-adjacent bounded integration) to avoid dilution; if a concept has >K available properties, prefer the ones flagged DISTINCTIVE (occur for few sibling concepts under the same KINDOF parent) over shared ones, per the Cree/McNorgan/McRae distinctiveness-weighting finding.
- **C. `scrambled_property_control`** — same features/dimensionality as B, but property-relation type tags and filler assignments randomly permuted across concepts (destroys genuine property-content binding, preserves added dimensionality/noise level). MUST collapse toward Arm A — this isolates genuine content from mere added-dimension noise.
- **D. `single_property_ablation`** — same as B but bundling only ONE property relation per concept (the single highest-coverage table, likely `KINDOF` or `SOURCEOF`) rather than the multi-relation bundle. Tests the Hoffman/Lambon-Ralph BINDING claim directly: if fine discrimination requires INTEGRATING multiple feature dimensions (not just adding one), D should underperform B materially while still beating A.
- **E. `hydro_nuclear_coal_planted_subset` (mechanism-clean sub-test)** — construct a small, hand-verified subset of question/fact pairs specifically built from the disk-verified WorldTree rows above (hydroelectric/nuclear/coal/natural-gas energy-source questions where the gold fact and the lure facts share IDENTICAL relation-type and argument structure, e.g. all "X produces electricity," differing only in X's identity/mechanism). On this subset: Arm A should perform AT OR NEAR CHANCE (by the same near-tautological argument as the prior drill's swapped-argument subset — a flat sum over shared surface words cannot separate same-slot substitutable fillers); Arm B should show the largest, cleanest gain if property-content binding is doing genuine work, since this subset is specifically the case where relation-structure (already tried, 29546) provably cannot help and only concept-content can.

**Primary metric:** in-sample TRAIN precision on the coverage-filtered subset (concepts/questions where WorldTree property tables have >=2 distinguishing rows) — the sharpest, cheapest measurement, directly comparable to 29545's established 0.189 ceiling.
**Secondary metrics:** TEST `sel_gold_precision` and end-to-end Challenge accuracy on the FULL pool (honest, uncherry-picked), vs the A_gate/LEARNED baselines already established.
**Tertiary/mechanism-clean metric:** Arm E (planted hydro/nuclear/coal subset) precision specifically, and the B-vs-D gap (tests whether multi-feature BINDING, not just added content, is doing the work).
**Coverage diagnostic (mandatory, run before trusting any positive result):** what fraction of concepts in the retrieval pool have >=1 usable WorldTree property-relation row at all. If coverage is low (e.g. <30% of candidate concepts), a positive result on the covered subset is real but narrow — report coverage explicitly, do not blend covered/uncovered performance into one number.

## Cross-thread synthesis

- **vs `research_relational_grounded_meaning_relevance_wall_2026-07-24.md` (29544/29545, and the user-cited 29546 fix attempt):** that drill diagnosed and (per the trigger) partially closed a STRUCTURE problem — symmetric cosine similarity cannot encode relation TYPE or argument ORDER (X-causes-Y vs Y-causes-X).
