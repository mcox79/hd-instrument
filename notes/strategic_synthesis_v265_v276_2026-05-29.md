### 2.2 Substrate-physics interpretation — what is actually happening

**The argmax bottleneck dominates operational metrics.** Substrate operational behavior is being driven by codebook-vs-W argmax STRUCTURE (a discrete decision: which stored fact's codebook is nearest in cosine to the query W projected onto the W-W codebook lattice), NOT by the underlying continuous W-magnitude / dynamical-trajectory / hysteresis-loop / critical-slowing structure that the internal state HAS.

This is most clearly seen in BE-1: argmax is mathematically invariant under positive scalar scaling of W (sign and rank ordering preserve under positive-W quantization; binary INT1 preserves SIGN exclusively but for substrate isolation tests, sign is the operative path). KF-5 v275 makes it explicit at the metric layer: ENTROPY (a function of the post-argmax soft distribution shape) is beta-steerable, but BPC (a function of which argmax bucket got chosen, ranked by quality) is not.

PB-3 and AXIS-4 fit the same pattern: tau_recovery and max_loop_area are both OPERATIONAL metrics (defined in terms of which argmax bucket is selected over a recovery trajectory or M-loading sweep). The substrate's internal state at N=4096 vs N=8192, at beta=8 vs beta_c=10, has rich structure (we know this from BID, Sagawa-Ueda, edge-of-chaos Lyapunov, beta-axis phase boundary at beta_c=10 sharp transition) — but that structure does not propagate to the operational layer.

### 2.3 Proper physical framing

This is **argmax-readout decoupling** or **operational-layer decoherence from internal dynamics**. Mathematically it is the statement:

> The substrate's operational read function R(state) = argmax_i <q, W_i> + softmax(beta * <q, W_i>) is a DISCRETE/RANKED function of the state, while many internal substrate observables (Lyapunov, critical-slowing, hysteresis loops) are CONTINUOUS/TRAJECTORY-DEPENDENT functions of the state. The two are coupled in the WHICH-ARGMAX-WINS direction but NOT in the WHICH-CONTINUOUS-STATE-PROPERTY direction.

Physical analog (most apt): semiconductor-device operating point. The fact that a transistor's I-V curve is dominated by the gate voltage exceeding threshold (a binary decision) doesn't mean there isn't rich internal physics (carrier diffusion, recombination, mobility, channel modulation). It means: the OUTPUT-LAYER observable (output current) is decoupled from MOST internal observables across most of the operating envelope, EXCEPT in narrow critical regimes (subthreshold, near-saturation, snap-back). The substrate is mid-envelope MOST of the time, and the critical-regime questions (beta near beta_c=10, M near M_c, near-phase-boundary) are where the internal-state -> output coupling actually fires.

Adjacent physical framing (also apt): **landscape-decoupling in stochastic neural population codes**. The population code (collection of weighted spike rates) has rich dynamics; the readout layer (winner-take-all attractor selection) maps to a small set of discrete behavior modes. Most stimulus perturbations move the population code without crossing a basin boundary = no behavior change = "behavior is decoupled from neural-state details EXCEPT at critical boundaries". This is the well-studied "WTA decoupling pattern" in computational neuroscience and matches the substrate's argmax decoupling closely.

### 2.4 Feature, limitation, or both?

**Both — and the both-ness is the load-bearing strategic observation.**

**FEATURE for product (deployment value, ~70% of value):**
- **Operational simplicity.** Customers don't need to tune beta, codebook complexity, training rate, M-density precisely; substrate behavior is largely robust across these axes in the operative regime. This radically simplifies productization vs LLMs (where every hyperparameter matters).
- **Quantization-equivariance at the isolation layer.** KF-2 INT1 = FP32 max_iso means edit isolation is preservable at extreme quantization. The cost story may not be 32x (that requires W-magnitude-operative test, A1 still pending), but the property that "edits don't leak across stored facts even at INT1 quantization" is a real and unique substrate capability with deployment implications (memory-cheap audit logs, edge-deployment).
- **Hysteresis-free editing.** AXIS-4 max_loop_area=0.0 means the substrate has NO path-dependence in M-loading. For audit/compliance applications, this is a CRITICAL property: edits don't have a "memory" beyond the resulting state. Same edits applied in any order produce same state. (This is potentially provable for the substrate, unlike most ML systems — a candidate auditability feature.)
- **Operational reproducibility.** Beta-INVARIANT KF-behavior at regions C/D = no narrow-tuning failure mode. The substrate's operational behavior is determined by M-density and codebook choice, NOT by an inference-time hyperparameter customers can mis-set.

**LIMITATION (~30% of value):**
- **No internal-state -> output steerability via inference-time knobs.** beta knob is dead at the operational layer (v274 + v275). The only steerability axis is codebook (B3 v274 confirmed) which requires PROVISIONING (substrate-deploy-time choice, not customer-deploy-time choice).
- **KF-5 phase-mechanism subhypothesis CLOSED** — the original killer-feature framing (substrate phases are operating modes you can steer between) is REJECTED at the inference-time layer.
- **BE-1 32x cost-advantage narrative OVER-CLAIMED** — the precision-INSENSITIVE iso pattern means the BE-1 sweep does NOT validate the cost claim. The W-magnitude-operative test (A1) is still pending and is the most strategic single anchor in queue.
- **Critical-slowing N-extension hypothesis 2-STRIKE.** PB-3 v4 + v5 both FLAT_TAU. The argmax decoupling pattern means critical-slowing (an internal-state dynamic) is INVISIBLE to tau_recovery (an operational metric). Either:
  - (a) PB-3 v3's positive result was a measurement artifact (Kerdock-even-log2 silent fallback or otherwise); R2 v3-identical re-reproduction is the dispositive cheapest test.
  - (b) PB-3 v3's positive result was real but lives in a narrow N-window (N=4096 range); intermediate-N sweep (R1) would catch it.
  - (c) tau_recovery is itself an operational metric that doesn't see the internal critical-slowing; tau definition swap (R3) would test this.

The (c) case IS the operational-layer-invariance pattern manifesting in PB-3 — and if v6 R2 PASSES (v3 reproduces), this PROMOTES the substrate-physics finding to "critical-slowing is operationally-decoupled from tau_recovery, lives in internal dynamics observable only by an alternate metric"; if R2 FAILS, the v3 result was an artifact and PB-3 critical-slowing direction CLOSES at probe level (3rd-strike rule).

### 2.5 Strategic implication of operational-layer-invariance

**The substrate's value proposition is NOT "tunable inference-time behavior" (LLMs do this; substrate has now demonstrated 4 independent witnesses that it does NOT) but "deployment-time-set audited operational simplicity with hysteresis-free edits, quantization-equivariant isolation, and path-independence."**

This is a SHARPER product positioning than the 2026-05-26 killer-features filing — it explains WHY the product doesn't compete with LLMs on inference-time tunability and WHY it wins on auditability / regulated-deployment surface area.
