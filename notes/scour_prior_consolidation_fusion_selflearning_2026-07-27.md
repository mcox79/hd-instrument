# Scour: prior consolidation + fusion mechanisms for the self-learning loop

Date: 2026-07-27
Scope: mine experiments/ (~5000 cells) + data/*/metrics.json + cert_ledger for REUSABLE consolidation/fusion mechanisms to feed the current frontier (read prose -> extract -> sleep-consolidate -> improve). Suspected weak points per task brief: (1) consolidation regresses under plain averaging, (2) text+grounding fusion dilutes at naive 50/50. This note verifies which prior cells already solved either problem, and what's certified known-broken.

All verdicts below are pulled from `data/<cell>/metrics.json` (or `_smoke/metrics.json`) directly, or cross-checked in `data/substrate_index/meta/cert_ledger.jsonl` / `tools/director_kb_query.py`, not from docstring claims alone. Per lit-scan calibration discipline, treat any "reusable" framing below as HYPOTHESIS-pending-reapplication, not a guaranteed transfer — these are same-substrate results, not external lit, but the transfer to the NEW loop (real prose, new corpus) is unproven until re-tested there.

## HEADLINE

The substrate has already independently solved both suspected weak points at small scale: **CA3-style pattern-completion-before-write beats plain averaging by a huge, significant margin** (exp_cls_ca3complete_consolidation_v1, HARD_PASS, retention 0.933 vs 0.020 naive, cleanup-specific lift +0.055 p=0.0003 over n=8 seeds), and **a learned convex gate recovers a diluted grounding channel from naive equal-weight fusion** (exp_grounding_gated_fusion_relation_inference_mammal_v1, HARD_PASS, gate beats naive FUSED_EQ by +0.2332 MRR). Both mechanisms are cheap, glass-box, and directly portable as the consolidation-step and fusion-step primitives for the new loop. The single biggest known-broken thing to avoid repeating is NOT the consolidation math — it's the **knowledge-feedback-into-reading gate**: every attempt to let condensed knowledge override a strong structural/positional baseline reader has net-hurt or only marginally helped, and the wins that did land were capped by thin coverage (cycle3: only fires on 31.6% of test items) or failed to survive cold-transfer (chaingrade_FULL: naive baseline beat the trained composed reader 11/13 vs 10/13 on held-out LitBank).

## 3. Reader / extraction — prior self-learning-loop lineage (cycle1 -> cycle2 -> cycle3)

This is the DIRECT precursor of the loop currently being iterated. All three cycles live at `experiments/exp_ingest_learn_sleep_loop_cycle{1,2,3}*.py`, wired to `hdlab/situation_reader.py` (READ), `hdlab/clarify_gate.py` (FLAG/abstain), `experiments/exp_online_knowledge_condenser_selectional_v1.py` (CONDENSE), and `hdlab/learner/core.py` (SLEEP/rule-induction — same MDL gate as section 1).

- **Cycle1** (`data/exp_ingest_learn_sleep_loop_cycle1_v1/metrics.json`): first end-to-end wiring on McGuffey grade-1. Reader extractor only fired on VBD/VBN, missing present/possessive/copular verbs -> predicate recall 0.222, triple F1=0.364. Sleep step correctly distinguished class-level rule-induction (compression_ratio=1.174, real compression) from per-item episodic collapse (ratio=0.964, the averaging-collapse control worked as designed). No reader<-knowledge feedback path existed yet, so reading-curve delta was 0 by construction.
- **Cycle2**: FIX1 (spaCy clause-head predicate detector) took predicate recall 0.222 -> **1.000**, a real and reusable fix — carry this extractor forward. FIX2 (wire condensed knowledge back into the reader's patient-choice decision) is the **HURT_REGRESSION**: on 1778 real ambiguous who-did-what items, accuracy went from 0.9826 (no knowledge) to 0.8369 (conservative gate, delta -0.1457) or 0.6445 (aggressive always-override, delta -0.3381). Root cause per the cell's own gate-design note: the structural nearest-noun heuristic already resolves ~98% of cases, so there's no headroom — any override policy is net-negative on the overall metric even though it helps on the genuinely-hard subset (hard_acc 0.0 -> 0.2581 on the 31 items where nearest!=gold). A cleanly separate, non-circular unseen-noun 2AFC (Part D) passed (0.75 vs 0.50, delta +0.25) — i.e. the condensed knowledge IS real, it's just being applied where it isn't needed.
- **Cycle3** (PP-attachment, scored against independent human UD-EWT gold to remove cycle2's circularity): ingesting all 189 OneStop units gave rise=+0.0157 (below the +0.03 HARD_PASS bar) -> **MIDDLE_BAND**, diagnosed `COVERAGE_GAP`: coverage_frac=0.316 (knowledge only had an opinion on 141/446 items); on that active subset it did beat structure (0.7163 vs 0.6667, active_lift=+0.0496) — the mechanism works where it fires, it just doesn't fire often enough.

Other reusable reader-side components verified:
- `hdlab/situation_reader.py`: DEMONSTRATED on 25 real LitBank books (coref_acc=0.40, xsent_acc=0.45, timeline margin +0.47, causation margin +0.64 — but causation margin is fully reducible to connective+recency, not real plausibility reasoning). Reusable as the base READER, but carry cycle2's spaCy predicate-detector fix, not the base VBD/VBN extractor.
- `hdlab/clarify_gate.py`: fires correctly (flag_recall=1.0, FP=0.0) in both cycle1/cycle2, but only tested against a synthetic 0.9/0.2 confidence proxy — the new loop needs a genuine calibrated confidence signal from the reader, not a stand-in.
- `experiments/exp_reader_selfimprove_case_sleep_udewt_v1.py`: the cleanest positive "case+sleep corrects a frozen component" precedent — coherent fix_rate=0.5632 vs scramble fix_rate=0.1817 (collapse 0.3815, well over 0.15 bar), net_gain=+0.0471, leak-clean. Reuse this case+sleep-correction pattern for grammar/structural correction specifically.
- `experiments/exp_reader_meaning_correction_case_sleep_affectedness_v1.py`: same mechanism applied to meaning/verb-affectedness — **negative** (net_gain=-0.0071, scramble did not collapse), attributed to oracle scarcity (only 22 base errors, VerbNet-oracle agreement ~0.46). Lesson: case+sleep correction needs a reliable oracle signal; it degrades gracefully to noise when the oracle itself is weak, it doesn't fail silently-positive.
- `exp_consolidated_reader_chaingrade_FULL_v1`: **CHAIN_GRADE_HELDOUT_PARTIAL** — on cold transfer to LitBank, the naive positional baseline beat the trained composed reader outright (11/13 vs 10/13 who-did-what items). This is the single clearest "doesn't generalize cold" data point in the whole family and the strongest argument for testing any new loop's reader on a genuinely different corpus before trusting same-corpus deltas.
- No `online-knowledge-condenser` by a different name was found beyond `exp_online_knowledge_condenser_selectional_v1.py` itself — that IS the condenser; no separate/newer implementation exists on disk.

## 4. Cross-thread synthesis — what to actually wire into the new loop

Composing the verified pieces into a candidate pipeline for "read prose -> extract -> sleep-consolidate":

1. **READ**: `situation_reader.py` base + cycle2's spaCy clause-head predicate-detector fix (predicate recall 0.22 -> 1.00, verified).
2. **FLAG/ABSTAIN**: `clarify_gate.py`, but replace its synthetic confidence proxy with a real calibrated score before trusting its abstain decisions.
3. **FUSE** (text extraction score x grounding/relational score): the gated-fusion mechanism from `exp_grounding_gated_fusion_relation_inference_mammal_v1` (learned convex gate, scramble-controlled) — this is the verified fix for "naive 50/50 dilutes." RRF is a reusable fallback if the two channels aren't on comparable scales.
4. **CONSOLIDATE** (sleep step): cluster-gate first (p4_replay_consolidation: only merge atoms above a calibrated similarity threshold), then CA3-style pattern-completion/cleanup on the replayed cue BEFORE writing the average (exp_cls_ca3complete_consolidation_v1 — the single strongest verified beat-plain-averaging result, +0.055 lift p=0.0003). Gate the whole commit behind the MDL accept/reject (`hdlab/learner/core.py`) so a bad consolidation is refused rather than silently averaged in.
5. **FEEDBACK GUARDRAIL**: before wiring condensed knowledge back into the reader's decisions (cycle2/cycle3's failure point), measure `coverage_frac` on the target test distribution FIRST. If the structural/positional baseline already resolves >90% of cases (as it did in cycle2), an override gate has no headroom and will net-hurt regardless of how good the knowledge is. Only apply overrides where structure has admitted uncertainty, and validate on a genuinely cold-transfer corpus (not same-corpus ablation) before trusting any accuracy delta — chaingrade_FULL's naive-baseline-wins-on-transfer result is the standing counter-example.

## 5. Substrate-product implications

- The consolidation weak point named in the task brief (plain averaging regresses) is NOT a fundamentally open problem for this substrate — a verified, cheap, glass-box fix (cluster-gate + CA3-completion) already exists at small scale and should be ported directly rather than re-derived. Estimated engineering cost to wire in: low (both mechanisms are already implemented cells, not new research).
- The fusion weak point (naive 50/50 dilutes) likewise has a verified fix (learned convex gate) sitting unused outside its original mammal-KG test. Porting it to text+grounding fusion is a wiring task, not a new invention — but re-verify the scramble-control margin holds on the new corpus before trusting it (per [[feedback-synthetic-toy-corpus-outcomes-can-be-construction-determined]], the mammal-KG result could be construction-favorable in ways real prose is not).
- The actual open risk is NOT consolidation math or fusion math — it's the **reader-feedback loop's coverage/headroom problem**, which has failed the same way three times (cycle2, cycle3, chaingrade_FULL) across three different corpora and two different tasks. This is a structural pattern, not a one-off bug: any new loop must pre-measure coverage_frac and structural-baseline headroom on its target distribution before wiring knowledge-override behavior, and must validate on a cold-transfer corpus, not a same-corpus split.

## Cheap decisive test (for the next iteration)

Before wiring any of the above into the live self-learning loop: run the composed pipeline (READ+spaCy-fix -> FUSE-gated -> CONSOLIDATE cluster+CA3 -> MDL-gate commit) on a held-out, genuinely cold corpus slice (not the corpus the fusion/consolidation gates were calibrated on) and check three numbers before trusting any headline delta: (a) `coverage_frac` of the knowledge channel on the test set, (b) the structural/no-knowledge baseline accuracy (if >90%, expect no headroom regardless of gate quality), (c) scramble-control margin for both the fusion gate and the consolidation cluster-gate on THIS corpus (not the original mammal-KG / synthetic corpus they were verified on).

## Falsifiable predictions

HARD-PASS: on a genuinely cold-transfer corpus, composed pipeline (gated-fusion + cluster+CA3-consolidation) beats a no-knowledge structural baseline by >=0.03 accuracy/F1 AND the fusion-gate scramble-control margin on the new corpus is within 0.5x of the mammal-KG margin (+0.14) AND coverage_frac >= 0.4.

HARD-FAIL / respec trigger: if coverage_frac < 0.32 (matches cycle3's ceiling) OR the structural baseline already exceeds 0.95 accuracy on the target distribution (matches cycle2's no-headroom regime) OR the fusion-gate's scramble margin collapses to <0.05 on the new corpus (construction-artifact signal) — any of these should trigger a respec of the target distribution/task choice before blaming the consolidation or fusion math.
