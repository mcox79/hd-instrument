## (a) HEADLINE

**Meaning grows from a grounded seed to abstract, unboundedly compositional language via three structurally distinct mechanisms stacked on top of each other — not one mechanism scaled up — and two of the three independently converge on the SAME missing primitive already flagged by the cascade-depth drill: an explicit, invertible bind/unbind (role-filler binding) operator.**

1. **Grounded-to-abstract (the "abstraction" rung).** Abstract concepts (justice, because, understand, freedom) do not get meaning by having more relational data added to them — they get it by **metaphorical/associative inheritance from concrete, embodied source domains**, via a specific developmental mechanism (Grady's *primary metaphor / conflation hypothesis*: early, repeated simultaneous co-activation of a subjective/abstract experience and a concrete sensorimotor one — e.g. being held (warmth) while feeling cared-for (affection) — leaves a durable associative link that survives differentiation into the adult metaphor UNDERSTANDING IS SEEING, AFFECTION IS WARMTH, ARGUMENT IS WAR). This is corroborated independently by historical semantics (Sweetser 1990: perception verbs systematically grammaticalize into cognition verbs cross-linguistically — "see" -> "understand" is not accidental, it recurs in unrelated language families' etymological histories) and by the honest caveat that this mechanism is **heterogeneous, not universal**: concepts like "justice" or "number" resist a single clean sensorimotor source domain and instead recruit **social/linguistic/interoceptive grounding routes** (Borghi et al. 2017's "words as social tools" account) rather than pure image-schema metaphor.
2. **Abstract/concrete-to-compositional (the "productivity" rung).** However meaning is grounded (directly or metaphorically), getting from a finite set of grounded atoms to **unbounded, novel, systematic** meaning requires a structure-preserving map from syntax to semantics (Frege/Montague/Partee's compositionality principle: meaning of the whole = function of the parts + how they're combined). Fodor & Pylyshyn's 1988 systematicity argument specifies WHAT this structurally requires beyond mere productivity: not just that novel expressions are producible, but that specific recombination abilities come paired (if you can represent "X relates-to Y" you can represent "Y relates-to X") — and their claim, still actively contested in 2025-26 preprints, is that this REQUIRES structure-sensitive, constituent-preserving representations, not flat similarity-based ones. The **only mechanism with a settled, constructive answer for how to get this out of distributed/vector representations without abandoning them** is Smolensky/Plate/Gayler's binding operator (tensor-product / circular-convolution / elementwise-multiply role-filler binding, with an approximate inverse for unbinding) — **the identical mechanism the cascade-depth drill (filed hours earlier the same day) independently identified as the fix for the 1-hop propagation cap.** Two entirely different literatures (developmental/graph-diffusion cascade depth vs. philosophy-of-language/formal-semantics compositionality), dispatched as separate drills on separate topics, converged on the same missing primitive. That is a strong, non-redundant cross-validation signal, not a coincidence of search terms.
3. **Compositional-to-referential (the "does it really mean anything" rung).** Even a system with grounding AND compositional binding is not automatically guaranteed to REFER to anything, per four classical philosophy-of-language positions (Frege's sense/reference distinction, Wittgenstein's meaning-as-use, Kripke's causal-historical reference, Quine's indeterminacy of translation) plus Searle's Chinese Room / Harnad's symbol grounding problem as a fifth reference point. The live-verified philosophy lit-scan's synthesis: **every position converges on requiring some connection beyond the symbols themselves** — Frege (on the standard, non-inferentialist reading) needs sense to bottom out in reference; Wittgenstein needs a *public/social* practice that checks correctness ("seeming right" vs. "being right," PI §258); Kripke needs an actual *causal-historical* chain to a real baptism event; Quine's negative result shows behavioral/observational data ALONE cannot uniquely fix reference, even in the limit; Harnad needs non-symbolic sensorimotor grounding. **Important, honest caveat the philosophy lit-scan surfaced that must not be smoothed over: there is a genuine, non-fringe DISSENTING minority position** — inferentialism (Dummett's assertibility-conditions semantics 1973; Brandom's *Making It Explicit* 1994; Peacocke's Fregean-informativeness criterion 1992) — which holds that content/meaning can be constituted PURELY by inferential role within a system of expressions and a rich enough social/inferential PRACTICE, with no further causal-worldly grounding required at all. This is not resolved by scholarly consensus. The honest reading: near-consensus that "internal coherence/token-manipulation alone, absent ANY further practice or causal connection, is insufficient" — but real, live disagreement on whether the further ingredient must be causal-worldly (Kripke/Harnad) or can be purely social-inferential-practical (Wittgenstein/Brandom) with no causal chain required. This is the philosophical version of the SAME cross-domain meta-pattern already flagged twice this week in the sibling notes ("a closed system cannot manufacture the property it lacks from more of the same internal material; it needs an exogenous or structurally-distinct channel") — here applied to REFERENCE itself, with the caveat that "exogenous" may, on the inferentialist reading, mean "a rich shared practice" rather than strictly "a causal link to the physical world."

**Net diagnosis**: the ladder is real, has three distinct rungs with three distinct required mechanisms (metaphorical/associative extension; compositional binding; exogenous/causal reference-fixing), and the middle rung's fix (binding operator) is now doubly-converged evidence — the single highest-confidence buildable target from this entire two-day drill arc.

## (b) Cheap decisive test

**Test 1 — metaphor-style co-occurrence seeding as a THIRD grounding channel (isolates the abstraction rung, extends Prediction B from the grounding note).**
Per Grady's conflation hypothesis, attach an existing directly-grounded seed atom (e.g. a concrete attribute-bearing atom from the earlier grounding-note seed set) to a currently-ungrounded abstract-relation atom via a **simultaneous co-occurrence exposure** during training (both present in the same training instances, mirroring the developmental "conflation" window), THEN test whether the abstract atom's downstream grounded-attribute-probe accuracy improves relative to an abstract atom with no co-occurrence exposure to any grounded seed.
- **HARD-PASS**: co-occurrence-seeded abstract atoms show grounded-attribute-probe improvement over non-co-occurrence-seeded abstract atoms by a pre-registered margin (e.g. >=15 points), replicating the "conflation creates a durable link" mechanism in a training-data intervention rather than a readout-time propagation change.
- **HARD-FAIL**: no improvement, or improvement indistinguishable from simply adding one more relational edge (i.e., the "co-occurrence" framing isn't doing anything beyond ordinary relational propagation, meaning the metaphor-specific mechanism doesn't transfer to this substrate's training regime).
- P estimate: **P=0.30** (deflated; capped at 0.50) — plausible mechanism, cheap to test (reuses existing seed data + training pipeline), but genuinely untested in any HD/relational-graph system and the human literature itself is honest that this mechanism is heterogeneous/concept-dependent, not universal.

**Test 2 — bind/unbind operator retrofit, systematicity-specific probe (extends Test 2 from the cascade-depth note with a DIFFERENT, sharper falsification criterion: paired recombination, not just hop-depth).**
Using whatever bind/unbind primitive the readout is retrofitted with (per the cascade note's Test 2), construct a **systematicity-specific** probe pair: if the encoder/propagation correctly represents "atom A relates-to atom B" (via a specific relation type), test whether it ALSO correctly represents the swapped/recombined "atom B relates-to atom A" for a NOVEL pair never seen in that exact configuration during training (only the reverse or a structurally analogous pair was seen).
- **HARD-PASS**: swapped-pair accuracy is statistically indistinguishable from same-direction accuracy (the paired-recombination signature Fodor & Pylyshyn identify as the hallmark of genuine systematicity, not just productivity).
- **HARD-FAIL**: swapped-pair accuracy is substantially lower than same-direction accuracy (the system can represent SEEN combinations productively but does not have the paired systematicity property — informative: it would mean the substrate has productivity without systematicity, a documented dissociation in the NN-systematicity-debate literature, e.g. SCAN-style benchmarks, and would mean binding alone, absent an explicit systematicity-promoting training signal, doesn't automatically deliver the strongest form of compositional generalization).
- P estimate: **P=0.25** (deflated; capped at 0.50) — this is the SHARPEST and most diagnostic test in this note but also the hardest to pass cleanly; the 2025-26 arXiv literature (contested, unreplicated) suggests even binding-equipped/attention-based systems often fail this exact test in NNs, so a HARD-FAIL here would not be surprising and should not be treated as a design failure, only as evidence that binding is necessary-but-not-sufficient and an explicit compositional-generalization training curriculum (per Lake & Baroni 2023 MLC, see (c) Prediction 3 below) may also be required.

## (c) Falsifiable predictions (HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL)

**Prediction 1 — metaphor/conflation co-occurrence seeding grounds abstract atoms (addressed by Test 1).** Thresholds as stated. P=0.30 (deflated, capped 0.50).

**Prediction 2 — binding operator delivers paired systematicity, not just productivity (addressed by Test 2).** Thresholds as stated. P=0.25 (deflated, capped 0.50) — the lowest P in this note by design, reflecting genuine contested state of the underlying NN literature.

**Prediction 3 — syntactic-bootstrapping analog: structural/relational POSITION (not direct grounding) can partially fix meaning for concepts with no perceptual referent.** Per Gleitman's syntactic bootstrapping (children infer verb meaning, including non-perceptual mental-state verbs like "think"/"know"/"want", from the sentence frames the verb appears in — number of arguments, presence of a clausal complement, etc. — even without observing the referent event), the substrate analog is: an atom's typed-relational-edge SLOT PATTERN (which relation types it participates in, and in which argument position) should carry partial information about its grounded-attribute class even for atoms that can never be directly perceptually grounded (abstract relations, connectives, mental-state-like predicates in the graph).
  - Buildable cell: for atoms with NO path to any grounded seed (ungroundable-by-construction, e.g. pure relation-type atoms or highly abstract hub nodes), test whether a classifier trained only on the atom's typed-edge slot-pattern signature (which relations it takes, in which argument role) predicts a coarse semantic-class label (e.g. "physical entity" vs. "abstract relation" vs. "mental-state-like predicate") above chance, using ONLY structural/positional features, no grounded-attribute features at all.
  - **HARD-PASS**: slot-pattern-only classification of coarse semantic class exceeds chance by a wide margin (e.g. >=2x chance baseline), mirroring the finding that syntactic frame alone carries real information about verb-meaning class even without perceptual access to the event.
  - **HARD-FAIL**: slot-pattern signature carries no more information than random relational degree/frequency (would mean the "syntactic bootstrapping" analogy doesn't transfer — the substrate's relational typing scheme may not be rich enough to carry the frame-like distinctions that make syntactic bootstrapping work in natural language, e.g. argument-count and complement-type distinctions).
  - P estimate: **P=0.40** (deflated, capped at 0.50; raised from an initial 0.35 given the direct Papafragou/Cassidy/Gleitman 2007 empirical precedent confirmed below) — the best-supported of the three new predictions in this note, because it requires NO new architecture (pure post-hoc classifier over existing relational-edge-type metadata) and the underlying developmental mechanism (Landau & Gleitman 1985's blind-children verb-learning result — blind children successfully learn "look"/"see" via syntactic frame despite zero visual grounding, corroborated by Naigles 1990's transitive/intransitive construal experiment and Fisher et al. 2020's finding that one-to-one NP-to-participant-role mapping is used as early as 15 months) is one of the most robust, oft-replicated findings in developmental psycholinguistics. **The abstract/non-perceptual extension of this mechanism is now directly, empirically confirmed** by Papafragou, Cassidy & Gleitman (2007, *Cognition*, "When we think about thinking: the acquisition of belief verbs") — children/adults infer that a novel verb ("gorp") denotes a MENTAL STATE purely from its clausal-complement syntactic frame ("Matt gorps that…"), with false-belief scenes increasing use of *think* — this is the single most directly on-point precedent in the whole drill arc for "structural/syntactic position alone can ground a concept that has no direct perceptual referent," and substantially raises confidence in this prediction specifically (though the same literature, per White 2018 and Hacquard & Lidz, notes the mechanism only resolves the COARSE verb class — representational/tensed "think/know/say" vs. non-representational/infinitival "want/need" — not fine-grained sense distinctions within a class, e.g. "think" vs. "believe" vs. "suppose"; the substrate analog should expect the same coarse-not-fine resolution).
  - **Honest calibration downgrade on the sibling semantic-bootstrapping mechanism**: Landau & Villion (PMC5516671, "When Meaning Is Not Enough") give a direct quantitative test in child-directed speech showing semantic cues ALONE correctly classify only 67.9% of noun TYPES and 39.9% of noun TOKENS (roughly half of nouns encountered are not concrete perceivable objects), with combined semantic+distributional/syntactic cues needed to reach 90.1%/70.1% — a load-bearing empirical falsification of "grounded meaning alone bootstraps grammar," reinforcing that the substrate needs BOTH channels (grounded-attribute AND relational/structural-position) working together, not either in isolation.

**Prediction 4 — an explicit compositional-generalization training objective (meta-learning-style curriculum), not binding architecture alone, may be needed for full systematicity.** Per Lake & Baroni's 2023 *Nature* result (meta seq-to-seq learning, MLC, reaches human-level systematic generalization scores WITHOUT hand-built symbolic binding, by training explicitly on a curriculum that rewards compositional reuse), it is possible that binding-operator architecture (Prediction 2) is necessary but not sufficient, and that an explicit curriculum-level training signal is the complementary, possibly even alternative, route to systematicity.
  - Buildable cell (longer-horizon, flagged as a bet not a near-term commitment): construct a small meta-learning-style curriculum over the existing relational graph that explicitly rewards correct novel recombination (held-out swapped-pair generalization, per Test 2's probe), and compare systematicity-probe scores against the same architecture trained WITHOUT that curriculum (ordinary training only).
  - **HARD-PASS**: curriculum-trained version shows substantially higher paired-recombination (systematicity) accuracy than the architecture-only (binding-but-no-curriculum) version, at matched binding-operator configuration.
  - **HARD-FAIL**: no difference — systematicity is fully explained by the binding operator's presence/absence (Prediction 2's result) and the curriculum adds nothing, meaning architecture alone is doing all the work (a cleaner, simpler result if true).
  - P estimate: **P=0.20** (deflated, capped at 0.50; the most novel and speculative prediction in this note — genuinely unclear whether MLC's result transfers outside its narrow seq2seq/SCAN-style domain into a relational-graph HD substrate).

**Honest structural caveat (calibration discipline — do not oversell):** the compositionality lit-scan is explicit that the "does binding suffice for systematicity" question is **actively, currently contested in the literature as of 2025-2026 preprints** (arXiv:2506.01820 and arXiv:2606.14512 both argue systematicity failures persist in binding-adjacent/attention-based neural systems). This note's P estimates for Predictions 2 and 4 are deliberately the lowest in the entire drill arc for this reason — this is the sharpest open scientific question in the whole ladder, not a settled implementation detail.

## (e) Substrate-product implications

1. **The binding operator is now the single most cross-validated buildable target in the entire two-day drill arc** — three independent literatures (graph-diffusion/cascade depth; VSA/tensor-product/systematicity; formal semantics/compositionality-as-homomorphism) converge on it from different starting questions. Prioritize the cascade note's Test 2 (bind-chain propagation) AND this note's Test 2 (paired-systematicity probe) as a matched pair in the same build cycle — they use the same retrofit and give complementary evidence (multi-hop reach vs. paired-recombination correctness).
2. **Bake-in vs. learn — the literature gives a real, non-obvious answer, and it is split by mechanism, not a uniform choice:**
   - *Binding/systematicity*: the TPR/VSA tradition (Smolensky/Plate/Gayler) treats the bind/unbind operator as an architectural primitive that should be **baked in** — this is the lower-risk, better-precedented path and aligns with the substrate's existing near-random-code design (which already anticipates binding-compatible, non-correlated codes). The competing MLC/Lake-Baroni-2023 result shows systematicity CAN instead be **learned** emergently via an explicit compositional-generalization training curriculum, without a hand-built bind operator — a higher-risk, higher-novelty alternative (Prediction 4). Recommend baking in the operator as the primary near-term path (matches existing design, cheaper, better precedent), and treating curriculum-based emergent systematicity as a longer-horizon research bet worth one buildable cell, not a replacement plan.
   - *Metaphor/abstraction extension*: this mechanism is fundamentally **learned/data-driven**, not architectural — it requires no new operator, only a specific CO-OCCURRENCE DATA STRUCTURE (simultaneous exposure of a concrete grounded atom and an abstract target atom, mirroring developmental conflation). This is cheap and reuses the existing training pipeline; recommend as a near-zero-cost addition to any future grounding-seed data collection cell.
   - *Syntactic-bootstrapping analog (Prediction 3)*: also **learned/inferable from existing metadata**, not architectural — a pure post-hoc classifier over already-logged relational-edge-type data, zero new data collection required. This is the cheapest of all five predictions in this note and should be run first.
3. **Replicability verdict — calibrate which parts of this ladder to lean on structurally vs. treat as fragile:**
   - **ROBUST** (build on these): (i) the cross-linguistic/etymological regularity of perception-to-cognition metaphor shift (Sweetser 1990 — historical-corpus evidence, not priming-dependent); (ii) the heterogeneity finding that abstract concepts need multiple grounding routes, not one (Borghi et al. 2017 — current consensus review); (iii) Fodor & Pylyshyn's classical systematicity argument and the formal compositionality-as-homomorphism result (Frege/Montague/Partee — settled, decades-old formal semantics, not an empirical-replication-fragile claim); (iv) Landau & Gleitman's blind-children syntactic-bootstrapping result (one of the most robust findings in developmental psycholinguistics).
   - **FRAGILE / DO NOT LEAN ON AS A MECHANISM** (own-knowledge caveat, reinforced by the metaphor lit-scan's honest reporting): the specific claim that ONLINE co-activation/priming causally and reliably shifts judgment in the moment (Williams & Bargh 2008 physical-warmth-primes-social-warmth; Boroditsky's original space-time priming) has **failed multiple independent replications** (Lynott et al. 2014; large-N Bayesian field replications; six failed replications of the original time-space priming result). The STRUCTURAL/developmental version of the mechanism (Grady's conflation hypothesis as a slow, early-childhood, one-time associative-formation account) is NOT the same claim as "priming works reliably on demand," and this note's Test 1 design deliberately targets the structural/training-time version (co-occurrence during a training/formation phase), not an online-priming analog, specifically to avoid inheriting the fragile part of this literature.
   - **ACTIVELY CONTESTED, LOW CONFIDENCE** (treat any positive result here as provisional): whether binding-operator/attention-based systems achieve genuine paired systematicity (not just productivity) is unsettled in the most current (2025-26, largely unreplicated preprint) literature — this is exactly why Predictions 2 and 4 carry this note's lowest P estimates.
4. **Frame the referential-adequacy question honestly, per house discipline**: even a fully-grounded, fully-compositional substrate is not automatically guaranteed "genuine reference" in the strongest philosophical sense (Kripke's causal-chain requirement, Wittgenstein's shared-practice requirement) — this is not a defect to engineer away but an honest, acknowledged open philosophical question that should inform how strongly any future capability claim is worded (avoid claiming the substrate "means" something in the full philosophical sense; claim instead that it has grounded, compositionally-structured, causally-anchored representations sufficient for the product's functional purposes — a weaker, defensible, and still genuinely valuable claim).
