## The six-part inventory (biology-first, mechanism + role)

### 1. Reward-driven learning (dopamine RPE, basal-ganglia actor-critic)

**Is task performance TRAINED by reward in the brain? Yes, unambiguously.** Schultz, Dayan & Montague (1997, *Science* 275:1593-1599) established phasic dopamine as a scalar signed reward-prediction-error; Montague, Dayan & Sejnowski (1996, *J Neurosci* 16:1936-1947) formalized the actor-critic split (ventral striatum = critic/state-value via a successor representation, Dayan 1993; dorsal striatum = actor via Frank/O'Reilly's PBWM Go/NoGo D1/D2 pathway model, 2001-2006). **On this substrate:** this exact primitive is not hypothetical -- `exp_pfc_gate_cfrpe_trained_v2.py` is a working, HARD_PASS-certified (closure=0.661, V1200/depth4, cv=0.037, sign_p<0.0001) implementation: an SR-value critic trained by a TD(0)/cf-RPE delta rule feeding a Go/NoGo winner-take-all actor. **The gap is not existence, it's application**: this mechanism was built and validated on a synthetic navigation/goal task, and has never been wired to "was this science-QA answer correct" as its reward channel. Per `research_dopamine_modulated_LR_alternatives_2x_drill_2026-06-23.md`, the brain-canonical dopamine effect on learning RATE is duration-extension of the eligibility window (Gong/Coddington 2026, *Science*), not magnitude-amplification -- a substrate meta-LR cell that tested magnitude HARD_FAILed; duration-extension remains the untested, higher-P (0.50 capped) rescue.

### 2. Predictive processing / predictive coding

**Established, high-confidence mechanism** (Rao & Ballard 1999; Friston's free-energy framing; Whittington & Bogacz 2017 proving PC with local Hebbian updates provably approximates backprop under settling conditions). Its single most load-bearing structural property: ONE error signal drives BOTH online inference (fast timescale) AND weight learning (slow timescale) -- removing this collapses PC into something else entirely. **On this substrate:** `hdlab/predictive_coding.py` already exists (Rao-Ballard predict + residual-magnitude + precision-weighted write-gate), and a cell family built on it (`exp_substrate_concept_encoder_spoke1_predictive_coding_competitive_allocation_v1/v2/v3`) already ran, with the toughest apples-to-apples check landing MIDDLE_BAND (margin over a strong softmax baseline was +0.031, short of the +0.05 bar) -- **genuinely tested, not proven, not refuted.** Per `research_prediction_error_native_learning_signal_grounding_link_2026-07-09.md`: the two truly load-bearing PC requirements (shared processing/learning signal; precision-weighting) are already FREE given the existing primitive; the missing piece is a hierarchical/multi-level generative pathway (deferred, moderate-to-high cost) and, separately, applying the residual-magnitude signal to the substrate's real ingest/reasoning stream rather than only a synthetic concept-formation task.

### 3. Neuromodulation as precision/gain control

**Established across (at least) six separable channels**, per `research_neuromodulatory_self_manager_controller_2026-07-08.md`'s 4-lit-scan synthesis: NE/locus-coeruleus = gain + discrete interrupt (Aston-Jones & Cohen 2005); ACh/basal forebrain = encode-vs-retrieve mode switch (Hasselmo 1999; McClelland-McNaughton-O'Reilly 1995 CLS); 5HT/dorsal raphe = adaptive time-horizon/patience (Doya 2002; Miyazaki et al. 2014-2020); tonic dopamine = vigor/opportunity-cost (Niv, Daw, Joel & Dayan 2007); ACC = expected-value-of-control halting (Shenhav, Botvinick & Cohen 2013); homeostasis+sleep = offline renormalization (Tononi & Cirelli SHY) and replay-based consolidation (Hinton et al. 1995 wake-sleep -- the single strongest DIRECT algorithmic, not just biological, precedent in that whole drill). Every channel is a cheap SCALAR-in/scalar-out feedback loop, not a new learned network -- and critically, all six answer the SAME underlying design problem: **a single fixed global parameter cannot simultaneously satisfy two regimes that place opposite demands on it** (explore vs exploit, encode vs retrieve, patient vs impulsive). **On this substrate:** a full 6-channel design exists (P_deflated=0.45, general thesis) but is UNBUILT; the certified gates already on disk (`exp_substrate_gen_lm_combinedgate_recency_content_v8`, `exp_pfc_gate_cfrpe_trained_v2`) have hand-set, FROZEN scalar hyperparameters (GATE_TAU, RECENCY_GAP_TARGET, decision-depth) that are structurally the exact knobs the six channels would modulate live -- the substrate independently re-derived the right PARAMETER SHAPE for neuromodulation before this drill ever looked for it; it just never made those parameters dynamic.

### 4. Error-driven consolidation / complementary learning systems (CLS)

**Established, canonical** (McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly 1995): fast hippocampal one-shot encode -> slow interleaved cortical generalization via offline replay (sharp-wave-ripple reactivation, ~20x compressed). This is the single MOST heavily drilled topic in this Store's history (60+ notes/cells across CLS/consolidation/replay/Hebbian-escape threads, dated 2026-05-21 through 2026-07-22) -- and the 07-20 SYNTHESIS note's headline finding is that **this whole family converges on ONE missing base element**: not capacity, not the working-memory buffer, not the reasoning algebra (all independently confirmed mature) -- an **error-driven/predictive-coding CORRECTION LOOP** that lets the substrate notice its own errors and repair its compositional model from experience, rather than every model being either fully hand-derived (construction-determined) or hand-ruled (bounded, ceiling ~0.557). `research_brain_confidence_weighted_learning_consolidation_2026-07-20.md` adds a load-bearing refinement: the brain gates consolidation strength by a SELF-GENERATED (no-ground-truth) confidence signal (Kepecs/Lak double-dissociation, OFC), and the substrate ALREADY has a validated analog (atom 29367's metacognitive S1 score, spearman=0.444 vs correctness) sitting unused as a consolidation gate.

### 5. Credit assignment (fresh drill today -- previously undrilled as its own topic)

**A single global scalar signal is well-established in the literature as necessary but NOT sufficient** for credit assignment -- it says WHEN something mattered, never WHICH synapse/unit/component caused it (Minsky 1961's original "credit assignment problem" framing; Izhikevich 2007's "distal reward problem"). The brain's answer is always a **companion local/structural mechanism paired with the global signal**, never the global signal alone:
- **Three-factor rules** (eligibility trace x neuromodulator x pre/post activity; Frémaux & Gerstner 2016, Gerstner et al. 2018): STDP tags exactly which synapses were causally active in the relevant window; a later diffuse dopamine pulse gates whether TAGGED synapses consolidate. Well-established for temporal linkage; generalization to arbitrary deep hierarchies is theoretical/contested.
- **Spatial/structural credit-assignment analogs to backprop**: feedback alignment (Lillicrap et al. 2016, random fixed feedback weights still carry directional error info, but degrades at depth -- contested at scale); target propagation (Bengio/Lee et al. 2015, local per-layer targets instead of a global gradient -- speculative, no direct neural evidence); predictive coding as approximate backprop (Whittington & Bogacz 2017, theoretically strong); dendritic/segregated-compartment models (Sacramento, Costa, Bengio & Senn 2018; Guerguiev, Lillicrap & Richards 2017 -- apical/basal dendrites separately carry feedforward drive and top-down teaching signal within ONE cell, matching real anatomy, still trailing backprop's task performance at scale); node/weight perturbation (Fiete & Seung; real in zebra-finch birdsong learning, but high-variance/poorly-scaling -- the weakest mechanism, evidence that a bare global signal alone is an impoverished solution).
- **Granularity contrast, direct biological evidence that one global signal is not enough**: basal-ganglia dopamine is diffuse/coarse (broadcast across large striatal territory); cerebellar climbing fibers are spatially exact (one fiber per Purkinje cell / narrow microzone, Herzfeld, Kawato & Shadmehr 2018). The brain runs BOTH, for different control problems, in the same animal -- direct evidence nature itself does not rely on the coarse signal alone.
- **Current state of the art (2023-2026):** no biologically-plausible local rule has closed the gap to exact backprop at scale (Lillicrap et al. 2020 "Backpropagation and the Brain" remains the reference synthesis; 2024-2025 e-prop/feedback-control-for-RNN-credit-assignment work is still actively trying to close, not having closed, this gap).

**On this substrate:** there is no analog of a three-factor local tag or a dendritic/cerebellar spatially-exact error channel at all. The credit-assignment function is currently performed entirely by the human Director's research-drill diagnosis loop (this session's own five parallel notes today -- multihop reasoning, bindsettle CI, ARC fact-combination, contradiction representation, ARC retrieval -- are each, functionally, a manual "which component is to blame" diagnosis, exactly the job three-factor tags/climbing fibers do automatically and locally in the brain, but at a timescale of hours instead of milliseconds and requiring a full literature review each time rather than a synapse-local computation).

### 6. Is there a unifying learning principle? (fresh drill today)

**No -- this is a contested minority position, not settled consensus, and the honest calibration matters for what to build.** The free-energy principle (Friston 2010) is the most-cited candidate: perception, action, and learning as ONE imperative (minimize variational free energy), with dopamine reframed as PRECISION on prediction error rather than raw reward-value (Friston, Shiner, FitzGerald et al.). But serious philosophy-of-science critiques argue it is not a validated, falsifiable mechanistic theory: Colombo & Wright (2018, *Synthese*) call it an organicist heuristic/formalism, not a mechanism; Andrews (2021) argues the bare math is not itself a scientific theory; Klein (2018) argues the core claim reduces to a near-tautology; a 2025 critique explicitly charges it with unfalsifiability ("adapts to every contradiction"); a technical critique (PMC7997279) shows a key derivational step fails without unstated extra assumptions. Silver/Singh/Precup/Sutton's competing "Reward is Enough" (2021) hypothesis has its own rebuttal (Vamplew et al. 2022, "Scalar reward is not enough"). And recent dopamine physiology itself is ambiguous: some transients look like genuinely non-model-free, inference-informed signals (which FEP proponents read as support for one unified prediction-error currency), while critics read the SAME data as evidence of real anatomical/computational multiplicity (striatal model-free vs. cortical model-based systems) that has not been shown to reduce to one variational quantity, only to loosely resemble it. The stronger-evidenced picture: the brain is a **stack of multiple, partially-interacting, evolutionarily distinct plasticity/control mechanisms** (Hebbian, STDP, three-factor, homeostatic scaling, RPE/TD, precision-weighted PC) that were not derived from, and have not been shown reducible to, one common law.

**Why this matters for what to build:** it reframes the target. The task is NOT "find/implement the One True Learning Law the substrate is missing" (that thing may not exist even in the brain). The task is "wire the SPECIFIC, already-catalogued, cheap local mechanisms (items 1-5 above) to the substrate's real task-correctness signal, one at a time, the way the brain layers them" -- exactly the additive, modulating-layer-over-an-existing-core design the self-manager note (item 3) already recommended on independent grounds.

## Falsifiable predictions (HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL / MIDDLE-BAND, calibration-deflated)

- **HARD-PASS:** `ARM_RPE_DURATION_ADAPTIVE` beats `ARM_FIXED` on held-out accuracy by >= +0.03 absolute AND beats `ARM_RANDOM_WALK_WEIGHT` by >= +0.02 absolute (proves the REWARD SIGNAL's content is doing the work, not generic weight variance) AND `ARM_SHUFFLED_RPE` collapses to within noise of `ARM_FIXED` (anti-tautology guard) AND cv across seeds < 0.05.
- **MIDDLE-BAND:** beats `ARM_FIXED` by +0.01 to +0.03, or beats it clearly but fails the `ARM_RANDOM_WALK_WEIGHT` margin (adaptivity helps, but not demonstrably BECAUSE of the correctness signal specifically).
- **HARD-FAIL:** `ARM_RPE_DURATION_ADAPTIVE` within +/-0.01 of `ARM_FIXED`, OR `ARM_SHUFFLED_RPE` performs comparably to the real-signal arm (discriminator not telemetry-sensitive -- report as INCONCLUSIVE_TAUTOLOGICAL, not as a negative mechanism result, per house discipline), OR the adaptive arm UNDERPERFORMS fixed (real evidence that hand-tuning already found a near-optimal point on this particular weight, and a native loop is not currently load-bearing -- a genuinely informative negative, not a wash).
- **P_deflated (HARD-PASS): 0.30.** Two already-independently-designed pieces (self-manager ACC/EVC-style adaptive knob, P=0.40-0.45; duration-extension dopamine-LR formula, P=0.50 capped) composed onto a THIRD, brand-new target (the ARC pipeline's real hand-set weights) that neither prior design was built or tested against -- deflated below either individual estimate per the compounding-uncertainty convention used elsewhere in this Store (e.g. the composed attention-value-gate cell, P=0.38, for the same reason: composing two independently-good pieces onto a new joint task is never assumed to inherit the higher of the two priors).

## Substrate-product implications

- **Blunt bottom line on the user's exact question**: the hand-designed substrate is missing (a) a wired reward-loop from real task correctness to any of its existing hand-set weights, (b) any local/structural credit-assignment mechanism (three-factor tag, dendritic split, or spatially-exact error channel) to replace the Director's manual diagnosis cycle, and (c) live/dynamic neuromodulatory gain on parameters that are currently frozen constants. **All three gaps are real and structurally analogous to documented brain necessities** (a global signal alone is proven insufficient in the brain; fixed knobs are proven suboptimal across at least 6 independent brain channels) -- this is not a hand-wave, it is convergent with a decade of computational-neuroscience literature.
- **Is the gap load-bearing for IMPROVING at science-QA specifically, right now?** Calibrated answer: **not yet a blocker, but it is the compounding-return bottleneck.** Every one of today's 5 parallel ARC threads produced a concrete, buildable, hand-designed fix WITHOUT a native learning loop -- so the absence is not currently stopping any single fix from landing. But every one of those fixes required an hours-long, literature-grounded, human-run diagnosis cycle (functionally: manual credit assignment) to identify WHICH component was wrong. A substrate that could apply even ONE of the already-designed pieces above (e.g., the cheap decisive test's adaptive-weight arm) to its OWN correctness signal would start closing that gap between hand-tuned-once and continuously-improving -- the difference between a system that gets better only when a human runs a research cycle, and one that gets better from its own graded experience, which is the textbook definition of what items 1-5 buy the brain over a fixed circuit.
- **Recommended sequencing, consistent with existing platform-maturity finding**: do NOT attempt to build "the" unifying learning principle (item 6's finding says that target may not even be coherent in the brain itself). Build the cheap decisive test above on ONE already-identified hand-set weight from today's parallel threads, once that thread's own hand-tuned version lands and has a stable held-out metric to beat. This is the concrete, sequenced, brain-faithful next step -- not a new architecture, a wiring job on pieces that already exist.
