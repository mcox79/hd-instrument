# Research: functional-vs-topical encoding -- is location/artifact conflation a fixable dependency-context limit? (2026-07-20)

Director synthesis of a 3-way parallel lit-scan (brain functional/topical organization; dependency-vs-window distributional semantics; affectedness/patient-selection instance-level difficulty). Trigger: atom 29391 (VET'd) found the window-PPMI-SVD codebook (atom 29368, held-out word-similarity AUC 0.927, topical/thematic content) CONFLATES location with artifact -- location-side typing loses to a naive baseline, carrying an artifact prior. Central question: is this an ENCODER limitation (fixable with a different context type) or a genuine data/grounding limit, and how does it relate to the harder, already-VET'd-null affectedness wall (29375)? All prior work credited (learn-from/build-on, never "steal").

## HEADLINE

**Location-vs-artifact conflation is very likely a WINDOW-CONTEXT artifact, not a data limit -- and there is a strong, decades-old, direct mechanistic precedent (Levy & Goldberg 2014) for the fix: re-key the SAME PPMI-SVD pipeline on DEPENDENCY-TYPED context instead of linear-window context.** This is a different difficulty class from the affectedness/patient wall (29375), which the same-shape literature (generalized-event-knowledge vs. situational specificity, reporting-bias) says is NOT fixed by dependency-typing alone. The brain side independently supports this split: taxonomic/thematic dissociation (ATL vs. temporo-parietal) and a place-network/tool-network dissociation (PPA/RSC vs. tool-use network) both point to functional-category membership (what type of thing is X) being a STABLE, separately-computed, frequency-recoverable property -- structurally the same kind of fact a dependency-typed slot-filler classifier recovers -- whereas per-instance affectedness is event-combinatorial and requires integrating agent+verb+context (Bicknell et al. 2010), a fundamentally richer and less textually-available signal (PIQA reporting-bias). Recommend building and can-fail-testing a dependency-PPMI codebook variant against the 29391 location/artifact typing task BEFORE touching the affectedness problem again.

## (1) Brain: functional vs. topical organization is a real, if graded, dissociation

- **Taxonomic vs. thematic semantic relations are neuroanatomically separable.** Schwartz, Kimberg, Walker, Faseyitan, Brecher, Dell & Coslett (2011, *PNAS* 108:8520) -- lesion-symptom mapping in 86 aphasic patients: anterior temporal lobe (ATL) lesions -> more taxonomic naming errors (pear-for-apple); temporo-parietal cortex (TPC) lesions -> more thematic errors (worm-for-apple). A genuine double dissociation, later synthesized across ~12 studies (Mirman, Landrigan & Britt 2017, *Psychological Bulletin*, PMC5393928). Kalénine, Mirman, Middleton & Buxbaum (2012, 2014) tie the thematic/TPC side specifically to action/sensorimotor knowledge, not generic associative co-occurrence.
- **Place vs. artifact is a parallel, well-established category-level dissociation at the perceptual/scene level.** Parahippocampal place area / retrosplenial complex / occipital place area (Epstein & Baker 2019, *Ann. Rev. Vision Sci.* 5:373; Persichetti & Dilks 2020, *PNAS*) are anatomically and functionally distinct from the tool/manipulable-artifact network (medial fusiform, inferior parietal lobule, premotor; Lewis 2006; Mahon & Caramazza 2011, *Trends Cogn. Sci.*). Martin 2007 (*Ann. Rev. Psych.* 58:25) reviews this as grounded, modality/action-tied category organization.
- **No paper directly asserts** "location-vs-artifact is a primitive relational distinction computed separately from topical/associative co-occurrence" as a single claim -- this is a plausible synthesis of the two dissociations above, not a directly cited finding. Flagged honestly as inferential, not established.
- **Computational framing:** Lambon Ralph et al.'s hub-and-spoke model (2017, *Nat. Rev. Neurosci.* 18:42) gives a mechanism -- taxonomic similarity = feature-overlap convergence at a transmodal ATL hub; thematic/functional relations = binding across DIFFERENT concepts' modality-specific spokes (action, location) via posterior temporo-parietal circuitry. This maps cleanly onto "co-occurrence in an undifferentiated window" (topical, hub-like blending) vs. "co-occurrence typed by grammatical/event role" (functional, spoke-like binding) -- which is exactly the window-vs-dependency contrast below.

## (2) Computational: dependency-typed context is a KNOWN, MECHANISTIC fix for topical-vs-functional conflation

**Direct, load-bearing precedent: Levy & Goldberg 2014, "Dependency-Based Word Embeddings" (ACL, P14-2050).** Their qualitative table is close to a direct analog of our problem:
- BoW/window context for "Florida" -> Gainesville, Jacksonville, Tampa, Lauderdale (places WITHIN Florida -- topical/meronymic, i.e. exactly our codebook's failure mode: pulling in things textually near the target regardless of TYPE).
- Dependency context for "Florida" -> Texas, Louisiana, Georgia, California, Carolina (other US STATES -- functionally co-typed / substitutable-in-the-same-slot).
- Mechanism: a window pulls in anything textually proximate; a dependency-typed relation (subj-of, dobj-of, amod, conj) conditions on grammatical role, and typed relations like conjunction structurally enforce same-type conjuncts -- so the induced similarity shifts from **relatedness** (BoW) to **similarity/co-type** (deps). The paper's own framing (relatedness vs. similarity) is essentially our topical-vs-functional distinction, independently named.

**Direct extension to slot-typing:** Padó & Lapata 2007 (*Comp. Ling.* 33(2):161) built dependency-filtered semantic spaces and beat window-based spaces on synonymy/WSD tasks. Selectional-preference induction from TYPED argument slots -- Erk 2007 (ACL P07-1028), Ó Séaghdha 2010 (ACL P10-1045), and the earlier Rooth, Riezler, Prescher, Carroll & Beermann 1999 (EM-clustering of subject/object co-occurrence, arXiv cs/9905008) -- is mechanistically the same move: condition co-occurrence on a TYPED slot (locative-PP-object vs. create/use-verb-object) rather than linear proximity, and the induced clusters become "things that fill this role" rather than "things textually near this mention." This is exactly the operation that would separate LOCATION nouns (fillers of in/at/near-type PP slots) from ARTIFACT nouns (fillers of make/build/use-type direct-object slots).

**Calibration nuance (both scans flagged this):** the location-vs-artifact instantiation specifically has NOT been directly tested in the literature -- no paper does this exact contrast. And dependency contexts are known to be sparser/noisier than window contexts on smaller corpora (relation x word blow-up); several papers (Komninos & Manandhar 2016 NAACL; arXiv:1809.04283) find COMBINING window + dependency context beats either alone, because window contributes topical/relatedness signal useful for some tasks while dependency contributes functional signal useful for others. So the fix is not "throw away window," it is "add a dependency-typed channel," and there is a real risk that pure-dependency-only underperforms on a small corpus purely from sparsity, which would be a confound, not a refutation of the mechanism.

## (3) The affectedness/patient case is a DIFFERENT, harder difficulty class -- consistent with the 29375 wall

- Ferretti, McRae & Hatherell 2001 (*JML* 44:516): verbs prime typical agents/patients/instruments strongly, but priming for LOCATION is weak/absent -- an early, direct dissociation suggesting location is a more stable, categorical, script-level fact while patient/affectedness is richer and more combinatorial.
- Bicknell, Elman, Hare, McRae & Kutas 2010 (*JML* 63:489, "checked the spelling/battery"): the congruent-patient advantage depends on agent x verb x context TOGETHER (generalized event knowledge, GEK), not verb-alone typicality -- i.e. even the best distributional/role-typed models here are computing an AGGREGATE corpus expectation, not resolving a specific novel instance.
- Pedinotti, Rambelli, Chersoni, Santus, Lenci & Blache 2021 (arXiv:2107.10922, "Did the Cat Drink the Coffee?"): explicitly tests whether transformers reproduce human dynamic thematic-fit updating on NOVEL scenarios -- finds partial, brittle correspondence. GEK/typicality generalizes; genuine situational specificity does not.
- Bisk et al. 2020 (PIQA, arXiv:1911.11641): reporting bias -- physical outcome/state-change facts are systematically underreported in text (people don't narrate banal physical facts), starving exactly the signal instance-level affectedness needs. Independent, orthogonal barrier (data coverage, not context-type/architecture).
- Erk, Padó & Padó 2010 (*Comp. Ling.* 36(4):723) is the most directly comparable dependency-typed model to what we'd build for location/artifact -- and it is explicitly a role-slot TYPICALITY scorer, not an instance disambiguator. This is the same mechanism proposed for location/artifact, but the lit is clear it stops at typicality, which is sufficient for a stable category split (location vs artifact) but NOT sufficient for "which of these two entities was affected here."

**Calibrated read (lit-scan 3's own explicit number, consistent with our house discipline of treating scan outputs as inputs not verdicts): ~75% confidence that dependency/role-aware modeling helps the CATEGORY-level distinction (location/artifact) much more than the INSTANCE-level distinction (affectedness).** This is corroborative, not new evidence, for keeping 29375 closed as a genuine (likely grounding/perception-adjacent, or at minimum outcome-annotation-adjacent) wall while treating location/artifact as a live, cheap, mechanistically-motivated fix.

## Cheap decisive test

Build a **dependency-context PPMI-SVD codebook variant**, same downstream pipeline as atom 29368 (PPMI + SVD -> codebook), differing ONLY in the co-occurrence feature: instead of (word, word-within-window), key on (word, typed-dependency-relation + direction), e.g. `(word, dobj_of:V)`, `(word, nsubj_of:V)`, `(word, pobj_of:P)` where P in {in, at, near, on} vs {make, build, use, create}. Requires an offline, static, glass-box-legal dependency parse pass over the training corpus (spaCy or similar -- a build-time tool, not a runtime LLM dependency, same legality argument as the COMET-offline-dump precedent in the companion engineering-resources note).

**Design gate (per [[feedback-experiment-design-gate]], must verify at smoke before full run):**
1. Real baseline = the EXISTING window-PPMI codebook (29368) run through the EXACT SAME 29391 location/artifact typing eval -- not a strawman.
2. Discriminator can-fail: dependency-PPMI must be able to score WORSE than window-PPMI (no thumb on the scale; sparsity risk is real and could show up as worse, not just "different").
3. Difficulty actually on: use the SAME items/split that produced the 29391 conflation (location-side losing to naive baseline) -- do not cherry-pick an easier split.
4. One variable differs: context type (window vs. dependency-typed) only. Same SVD rank, same PPMI smoothing, same corpus, same codebook downstream consumer.

**Arms:** (a) window-PPMI [existing baseline, =29368]; (b) pure dependency-PPMI [the genuine test article]; (c) combined window+dependency-PPMI [per Komninos & Manandhar precedent -- the likely real-world winner, included so a pure-dependency loss due to sparsity doesn't get mistaken for mechanism failure]; (d) random-context control [sanity check that the codebook pipeline itself isn't doing the work].

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS:** dependency-PPMI (b) OR combined (c) beats window-PPMI (a) on location-vs-artifact typing (29391 task) by a PRE-REGISTERED margin (>= 0.05 AUC, or closes at least half the gap to the naive baseline it was previously losing to) WITHOUT a corresponding drop in artifact-side typing or overall held-out word-similarity AUC (must not regress below ~0.85, i.e. the fix can't come at the cost of wrecking the topical content that DOES work). If only (c) passes and (b) alone underperforms (a), diagnose as sparsity-limited, not mechanism-refuted -- re-test (b) on a larger corpus before concluding the mechanism fails.

**HARD-FAIL:** neither (b) nor (c) beats (a) by a meaningful margin (< 0.02 AUC difference, within noise) on location-vs-artifact typing specifically. This would mean the conflation is NOT a window-vs-dependency context-type effect, and the likely next hypothesis shifts to: insufficient dependency-relation coverage in the parse (locative-PP relations may be rarer/more variably-attached than direct-object relations), corpus size/genre mismatch, or a genuine deeper limit in what the PPMI-SVD architecture (any context type) can recover for location-typing specifically -- in which case escalate to the harder grounding-adjacent hypothesis, not another context-type sweep.

**Must-fail control:** the random-context arm (d) must NOT beat window-PPMI (a) -- if it does, something is wrong with the eval harness itself (label leakage, degenerate task), independent of any encoder hypothesis.

## Cross-thread synthesis

- Builds on **29368** (window-PPMI-SVD codebook, topical content, AUC 0.927) as the control arm and pipeline template.
- Directly explains the mechanism behind **29391**'s location-vs-artifact conflation as a context-type property, not a capacity or corpus-size property -- this reframes it from "content-derivation is topical-not-functional, full stop" to "content-derivation is topical-not-functional UNDER A WINDOW CONTEXT SPECIFICALLY, and there is a known lever."
- Sharply distinguishes this from **29375** (affectedness/patient signal-strength bound, VET'd real-null) -- the lit independently supports treating these as different difficulty classes (stable category-membership fact vs. event-combinatorial instance fact), so this drill does NOT reopen 29375; it narrows what "topical-not-functional" means to specifically the category-typing layer.
- Connects to **notes/research_brain_novel_atom_generalization_fewshot_composition_2026-07-20.md**'s discriminator (similarity-based downstream needs CONTENT-correct codes; format alone insufficient) -- a dependency-typed codebook is exactly a richer CONTENT-encoding lever for the same "encode into a structured space" step identified there as the genuine bottleneck.
- Connects to **notes/research_plausibility_web_engineering_resources_adoptable_foundation_2026-07-20.md**'s adoptable resources: VerbNet's explicit selectional restrictions (`+location`, `+concrete`, etc., already confirmed present in our own KB per the substrate_query hit on `Location` / `RESTRICTS_TO->+location`) is a STATIC, glass-box-legal cross-check / possible seed vocabulary for which prepositions and verbs to treat as locative vs. artifact-creating slots when building the dependency-relation vocabulary -- adopt, don't hand-build from scratch.
- The brain side (taxonomic/thematic ATL-vs-TPC dissociation; place-network-vs-tool-network dissociation) gives an independent line of support that the substrate's target distinction (functional/category-type organization) is a real, biologically-separate computation from topical/associative organization -- reinforcing that this is worth building rather than an artifact of our own task design.

## Substrate-product implications

- If the cheap decisive test HARD-PASSes: the fix is a genuinely CHEAP, glass-box, build-time-only change (one offline dependency parse pass + a re-keyed PPMI matrix, same SVD/codebook consumer) -- no architecture rebuild, no runtime LLM, no grounding/perception escalation needed for this specific layer. This directly strengthens the product's core differentiator (transparent, inspectable content-derivation) rather than trading it away for capability.
- If it HARD-FAILs: it cleanly localizes the true bottleneck away from "which context window" and toward either data/coverage (dependency-relation sparsity) or a structural PPMI/SVD ceiling -- either way it is diagnostic progress, not wasted effort, and correctly gates against prematurely reaching for the (expensive, glass-box-threatening) grounding/perception route for a problem that turns out to be encoder-fixable.
- Either outcome keeps the affectedness/patient wall (29375) closed and correctly separated -- this drill does not risk re-litigating an already-VET'd real-null; it is scoped strictly to the category-typing layer.
- The VerbNet selectional-restriction data already indexed in-house (confirmed live in the KB) means the dependency-relation vocabulary for the locative-vs-artifact slots can be SEEDED from an existing, licensed, static resource rather than hand-designed from zero -- consistent with "adopt/build-on" engineering recombination, not novel research, for the scaffolding part of this cell.
