## HEADLINE

**Both hypotheses are real, but they resolve at DIFFERENT loci for different negatives, and the honest split is now falsifiable.** Negative #4 (capacity-growth null) is a near-exact replication of an established 26-year-old ML/cognitive-science result (Rohde & Plaut 1999 directly refuted Elman's 1993 "starting small" hypothesis at scale) -- this is NOT evidence of missing structure, it is the textbook signature that **the bottleneck is the credit/objective path, not raw capacity**, and it correctly redirects effort away from "add more capacity." Negatives #1 and #2 (eligibility trace and credit-assignment ties with a correlation counter) are **most parsimoniously explained by task-triviality**: the biological literature shows a *local* eligibility trace is *itself* mathematically a decaying correlation record with an acknowledged, unsolved recency bias -- the brain does NOT fix this locally, it adds a **non-local, cross-synapse/cross-time structure** (synaptic tagging-and-capture, structured neuromodulator release, replay/compression) that our substrate attempt never implemented, and which only pays off on tasks that require distal/delayed credit -- which our test tasks likely did not require. Negative #3 (predictive reader UNDERPERFORMING bigram, not merely tying it) is the one genuinely diagnostic of **missing structure**: task-triviality predicts a *tie*, not *active harm* from having more context; underperformance implies the wider context is being integrated with **no relevance-weighting**, diluting a sharp local signal with unweighted noise -- exactly the failure mode that a predictive-coding-style top-down error/relevance pathway is built to prevent, and exactly the ingredient every "credit assignment without backprop" mechanism in the neuro-AI literature (NGRAD family) requires and a flat correlation counter structurally cannot have.

**Ranked verdict:** (1) Negative #4 = expected null, not a gap -- close it, don't re-attempt capacity-growth. (2) Negative #3 = the load-bearing missing-structure finding -- build a relevance-gated (predictive-coding-style) context integrator. (3) Negatives #1-#2 = probably task-triviality as currently tested, but the eligibility-trace fix (decouple capture from recency via a global salience-gated sweep, biology's actual answer) should be built anyway because the *fair test* requires a task with genuinely delayed/non-adjacent credit to even distinguish triviality from poverty -- and that fair test does not yet exist in our test suite.

**P_deflated (at least one of the two proposed structural fixes clears its HARD-PASS bar on a task certified to defeat correlation) = 0.45**, under novel-synthesis cap 0.50, deflated 0.20 from a naive lit-transfer prior of ~0.65 (strong mechanism precedent -- Guerguiev/Sacramento dendritic-error nets hit MLP-class accuracy on structured tasks -- but uncharted for our specific VSA/HD implementation and construction).

## Cheap decisive test

**Task: delayed k-parity-in-context, embedded in a token stream (kills BOTH the recency shortcut and the correlation-solvability of the current tests simultaneously).**

Construction: a stream of symbols from vocabulary V. At scattered query positions q, the correct label is the **parity (XOR) of two marker tokens at fixed offsets t-k1 and t-k2 relative to q**, with k1 != k2, and (critically) **neither offset is the most-recent position** -- i.e. the two informative tokens are deliberately NOT the tokens closest to the query. Optionally: the reward/label signal for a query is only delivered several steps AFTER the query (a delayed-credit variant), decoupling the informative markers from both recency AND from temporal adjacency to the reinforcement signal.

Why this is a fair, can-fail, one-variable test:
- **Real baseline**: the SAME oracle-windowed correlation counter that currently ties/beats our mechanisms, applied unmodified to this task.
- **Can-fail discriminator, mathematically guaranteed**: a parity target has exactly zero pairwise/marginal correlation with either marker alone (classical fact -- this is literally the textbook XOR-non-separability result). The correlation counter is PROVABLY bounded at chance (~0.50) on true k-parity; if it ever exceeds ~0.55 the task construction is leaking and must be fixed before drawing conclusions.
- **Difficulty-on**: markers are non-adjacent and NOT the most recent tokens -- this simultaneously stresses the eligibility-trace recency bias (negative #1) and the correlation-tie (negative #2) under a task where correlation is formally insufficient.
- **One variable**: only the learning/credit mechanism differs across arms; same encoder, same window size, same vocabulary exposed to every arm.

**Arms (3-5 seeds, CPU-cheap):**
| Arm | Mechanism | Targets which negative |
|---|---|---|
| ARM_CORR | oracle-windowed correlation counter (current baseline) | control -- must stay at chance |
| ARM_ELIG_LOCAL | current as-built three-factor/eligibility trace, unmodified | replicate negative #1 on a task built to need non-local capture |
| ARM_TAG_CAPTURE | eligibility trace + a **global threshold-gated capture sweep**: a salience/surprise event (already in substrate) triggers a sweep across ALL currently-live traces above a magnitude threshold and consolidates them, regardless of recency -- decouples "who gets consolidated" from "who fired most recently" by construction | fixes #1 (biology's actual answer: synaptic tagging-and-capture) |
| ARM_PREDCODE | a top-down prediction generated from the compressed context/gist; compute a per-position prediction ERROR; weight each context element's contribution to the readout by that error magnitude (relevance gate) instead of uniform/unweighted window integration | fixes #3 (predictive-coding-style relevance gating) |

**Decision rule:**
- HARD-PASS: ARM_TAG_CAPTURE and/or ARM_PREDCODE reach accuracy >= 0.65 on held-out parity queries (binomial-significant above chance p<0.01, 3+ seeds) WHILE ARM_CORR and ARM_ELIG_LOCAL remain within noise of chance (<=0.55). This is unambiguous because correlation is mathematically barred from parity -- any real win here cannot be a construction artifact, unlike the prior (natural-sequence) negatives.
- HARD-FAIL: ALL FOUR arms stay <=0.55 (chance). This is decisive in the OTHER direction: even with the two literature-motivated structural fixes, the substrate cannot yet do multi-step nonlinear credit assignment at this construction/scale. Per the brain-check discipline (see below), this would NOT be read as "the brain also can't do this" (delayed-XOR-class tasks are routinely solved by recurrent/hierarchical biological and artificial systems -- e.g. the classical Elman/Williams-Zipser delayed-XOR RNN demonstrations) -- it would mean the specific glass-box construction is still missing an ingredient (try a second hierarchy level, or a stronger gate) rather than that a structural wall has been found.

## Per-angle findings (from the 4 parallel lit-scans)

### Angle 1 -- synaptic plasticity / biology (is biological learning "just correlation," gated?)
Three-factor rules (Fremaux & Gerstner 2016; Gerstner, Lehmann, Liakoni & Brea 2018) formalize plasticity as coincidence (Hebbian/STDP) x eligibility trace x neuromodulator gate. Izhikevich (2007) shows this solves the *distal reward problem* via trace-dopamine coincidence; Yagishita et al (2014) gives direct cellular evidence of a narrow (0.3-2s) dopamine-action window. **Critically, the literature itself flags that a single local eligibility trace is a leaky integrator -- structurally identical to RL eligibility traces -- and is INTRINSICALLY recency-biased** ("trace-mediated peak/recency bias" is an acknowledged, still-open problem, not something biology has cleanly solved at the single-synapse level). STDP + homeostasis (BCM sliding threshold, Turrigiano synaptic scaling, metaplasticity) add genuine structure -- competition and self-normalization -- but remain local. The genuinely NON-local structure is **synaptic tagging-and-capture** (Frey & Morris 1997; Redondo & Morris 2011) and its behavioral analog (Moncada & Viola 2007/2011): a weak, tagged synapse can be rescued into long-term potentiation by an unrelated salient/novel event occurring elsewhere in time or space, via shared plasticity-related-protein synthesis. This is credit-sharing that is explicitly cross-synapse and cross-event, not recency-ordered.
**Verdict: local synapse rule = correlation, gated (recency-biased, same as ours); the brain's escape from pure correlation is a NON-LOCAL system-level structure (tagging-and-capture + structured neuromodulator release + replay) our substrate attempt has not built.**

### Angle 2 -- the credit-assignment problem (neuro-AI, deep credit without backprop)
NGRAD (Lillicrap, Santoro, Marris, Akerman & Hinton 2020, *Nat Rev Neurosci*) is the umbrella term for the family: feedback alignment (Lillicrap et al 2016 -- fixed random feedback weights still transmit a usable direction because forward weights rotate to align with them over training), predictive coding (Rao & Ballard 1999; Whittington & Bogacz 2017 formally prove equilibrium-settling local-error rules approximate backprop), dendritic/compartmental error computation (Guerguiev, Lillicrap & Richards 2017; Sacramento et al 2018 -- apical/basal segregation multiplexes feedforward and feedback signals in one neuron, hits 1.96% MNIST error), target propagation (Bengio 2014; Lee et al 2015), equilibrium propagation (Scellier & Bengio 2017).
**The single structural ingredient common to all of them and absent from pure correlation: a top-down/feedback pathway carrying an error or target signal back across MULTIPLE processing stages, coupled to a generative, inverse, or energy-based model that shapes it.** A single-layer online co-occurrence tally has no channel and no computation for "was this helpful for a distal outcome" to reach back through representational stages it never directly touched -- this is not a matter of scale or data, it is a structural absence.

### Angle 3 -- why correlation is not enough (theory + the triviality-vs-poverty diagnostic)
XOR/linear-separability (Minsky & Papert 1969): a correlation/linear-threshold reader can only respond to a weighted sum of raw inputs -- it cannot construct a nonlinear combination feature ("exactly one is active") regardless of data volume; the fix is a hidden layer that re-represents the input before the linear read. Confounding (Pearl's causal hierarchy, Simpson's paradox): correlation can be *directionally wrong* for intervention questions under an unobserved confounder; the fix is a causal/generative model + intervention, not more co-occurrence data. Depth-separation theorems (Telgarsky 2016; Hastad 1986 on parity/AC0 circuits) formalize that shallow/one-step rules require exponentially more resources (or fail outright) on compositional/nonlinear targets that a small amount of extra depth solves trivially.
**The established diagnostic for telling mechanism-poverty from task-triviality: construct or select a task with CERTIFIED hidden/latent/nonlinear structure where the correlation-only ceiling is mathematically computable (parity/k-XOR, confounded synthetic generators, needle-in-haystack decoupled from surface co-occurrence) and check whether the richer mechanism closes the gap. A tie on a task without that certification is uninformative** -- exactly the gap in our own negatives #1-#2, which is why the cheap decisive test above is designed around parity-in-context rather than a natural-language re-run.

### Angle 4 -- ML optimization (why capacity-growth can fail even when it "should" help)
Universal approximation (Cybenko 1989; Hornik 1991) is an existence result, not a training theorem. Vanishing/exploding gradients (Bengio, Simard & Frasconi 1994; Pascanu, Mikolov & Bengio 2013) show that when the credit path degrades, added capacity sits unused. Double descent / overparameterization (Belkin et al 2019; Nakkiran et al 2019) shows capacity increases are normally neutral-to-helpful in the modern regime -- making a strict null (zero benefit from growth) diagnostically unusual and therefore informative. **Directly on point: Elman's (1993) "starting small" / incremental-capacity hypothesis for sequence learning was empirically REFUTED by Rohde & Plaut (1999) at larger scale** -- growing capacity was neither necessary nor sufficient; outcomes tracked the informativeness of the *training signal*, not the capacity *schedule*, and staged/incremental capacity sometimes actively hurt as task complexity increased. This is close to an exact historical repetition of our negative #4.
**Verdict: a clean capacity-growth null result, with the credit path/optimizer held fixed, is the textbook diagnostic that the bottleneck lives in the credit-assignment/objective path, not in parameter count** -- redirect effort there, do not re-attempt capacity growth.

### Angle 5 -- the structural verdict (synthesis)
See HEADLINE. The four angles converge cleanly: biology's local plasticity rule is genuinely correlation-based and recency-biased (matching our eligibility-trace negative almost exactly) -- but biology's NON-local escape hatch (tagging-and-capture) is a nameable, buildable structure we have not attempted. The neuro-AI credit-assignment literature independently confirms the missing ingredient is a feedback/error pathway across processing stages, which explains why our predictive reader UNDERPERFORMING bigram is the most diagnostic negative (dilution by unweighted context is exactly what a relevance-gated error pathway prevents and a flat correlation-style integrator cannot). The correlation-limits theory literature supplies the missing methodological piece our prior tests lacked: a certified-hidden-structure task, without which "ties a correlation counter" is not evidence of anything. The ML-optimization literature independently confirms our capacity-growth null is expected and should not be re-attempted.

## Brain-check (explicit, not pre-assumed per [[feedback_dont_assume_brain_check_outcome_brain_may_fail_same_way_then_fix_is_native_2026-07-17]])

**At the single-synapse level: brain-SAME.** Biological plasticity is fundamentally Hebbian/STDP-correlational, and the local eligibility trace has the identical recency-bias property our substrate attempt showed. Taken alone, this would be an "accept the bound" outcome.

**At the systems/circuit level: brain-DIFFERENT.** The brain does not fix recency-bias by inventing a better local trace formula -- it adds non-local structure on top: synaptic tagging-and-capture (cross-synapse, cross-time, salience-gated rescue of weak traces), structured (not uniform) neuromodulator release, hippocampal replay/compression, and cortical predictive-coding/dendritic-segregated error pathways for deep credit assignment. **None of these system-level additions are present in our substrate's current three-factor or predictive-reader attempts.** Per the discipline, brain-different means the fix is the brain's mechanism, not acceptance of a bound -- this resolves the tension: the single-synapse comparison alone would mislead toward "accept it," but the correct unit of comparison is the *system*, where the brain demonstrably does more than pure correlation via nameable, buildable structures.
