HEADLINE
======================================================================

**Not a hard foundational blocker requiring embodiment — but not a free lunch either. There is a concrete, citable, partially-precedented, brain-foundational path: text does not have to re-derive causation from raw statistics (which is provably underdetermined at open-domain scale); it can instead harvest PRE-COMPUTED human causal judgments that authors already encode via closed-class syntactic markers (causal connectives, counterfactual/negation constructions, generics). This is categorically different from co-occurrence mining, and a small, real, twice-replicated empirical precedent exists (CausalNet/CausalBank on COPA, +3 to +5 points over pure co-occurrence) — but the honest ceiling is MODEST, not categorical, and the single richest sub-mechanism (counterfactual-marked causal testimony, which would supply NECESSITY edges directly rather than requiring the leave-one-out decode-confidence probe the sibling note proposed building) has never been tested on any cause-selection benchmark by anyone.** Five findings, in order of how much they should change the next build:

1. **The reason pure covariation-based approaches (ATOMIC, PPMI, Rescorla-Wagner ΔP) fail to transfer is not an implementation defect — it is a proven mathematical ceiling.** Under nonparametric assumptions, observational data alone identifies only a Markov *equivalence class* (skeleton + v-structures), never a fully-oriented DAG (Pearl 2000/2009; Spirtes, Glymour & Scheines 2000) — the classic collider/confounder ambiguity is provably irresolvable by covariation strength alone (Cheng 1997's ΔP/power-PC formula is explicitly a *strength* estimator conditional on an already-assumed causal role, not a direction-discovery mechanism). A well-defined assumption bundle DOES make direction identifiable from observation alone (LiNGAM: linear + non-Gaussian noise; Additive Noise Models: genuine nonlinearity + independent noise — both requiring causal sufficiency, i.e. **no unobserved confounders**) — but that bundle is essentially never satisfiable at open-domain text-corpus scale, where confounding is the default condition, not the exception. This is the formal explanation for why every covariation-flavored mechanism already tried on this substrate hit the same wall, independent of implementation quality.
2. **Explicit linguistic causal markers are not more statistics — they sidestep the identifiability problem entirely, because they are testimony, not inference.** A causal connective ("because"/"so"/"therefore") is a closed-class syntactic marker that directly encodes an author's ALREADY-COMPUTED causal judgment and its direction — the reader does not need to solve an identifiability problem, only parse a marker. This is a different *kind* of information than PMI/ΔP over raw event co-occurrence, and there is direct, if modest, empirical evidence it helps: Luo et al. (2016, KR — corrected venue from "CIKM") report PMI-gated-by-explicit-causal-connectives beating pure PMI on COPA, **70.2% vs 65.4%** (Gordon, Kozareva & Roemmele 2011 baseline); Li et al. (2020 IJCAI, "CausalBank," arXiv:2107.09846) independently replicate the pattern at web scale (314M mined pairs, 95%-precision manual check, **+3 points on COPA**). This is the single most decisive quantified finding in this drill.
3. **This precedent has a real, quantified recall ceiling and an untested frontier.** Roughly **39% of PDTB-annotated causal-type discourse relations carry no explicit connective at all** (implicit relations) — a connective-only miner structurally cannot exceed that recall regardless of precision gains. Two richer sub-mechanisms — COUNTERFACTUAL constructions ("if she hadn't studied...") and GENERICS ("mosquitoes carry malaria") — are independently confirmed to be (a) theoretically the right kind of signal (counterfactuals are Lewis/Halpern-Pearl-style *necessity* testimony; generics are Leslie/Prasada-style *principled*, non-statistical dispositional connections) and (b) mechanically mineable at scale today (counterfactual detection F1=0.77, SemEval-2020 Task 5; GenericsKB, Bhakthavatsalam et al. 2020, already filters 1.7B sentences into a large generic-statement KB via cheap syntactic heuristics) — but **neither has ever been benchmarked on a forced-choice cause-selection task by anyone**, anywhere. This is the genuinely open, buildable frontier, not the already-partially-tested connective case.
4. **Simulation/intuitive-theory approaches (physics engines, inverse planning) do NOT solve the acquisition problem — they presuppose an already-given generative model and only run inference over it.** Battaglia, Hamrick & Tenenbaum's (2013 PNAS) "intuitive physics engine" is a hand-coded Newtonian simulator; Baker, Saxe & Tenenbaum's (2009 Cognition; 2017 Nature Human Behaviour) inverse planning presupposes a fixed rational-planning generative model (POMDP solver) and infers only hidden goals via Bayesian inversion. The one genuine passive-learning counter-example (Garrido et al. 2025, Meta FAIR, V-JEPA, arXiv:2502.11831) trains self-supervised on real passive video with no simulator supervision and reaches strong physics-violation detection — but the authors themselves state it "predicts the future only as an observer" and explicitly lacks the ability to condition on interventions, i.e. it is Pearl rung-1 (association/prediction) only, and it is visual, not textual. This closes off "build a simulator and let it learn causality" as an acquisition path for a non-embodied text-only system: the generative structure has to come from somewhere else — hand-specification (already done for physical closed classes, e.g. the on-disk Talmy force-dynamics FORCE_ACTION/RESULT_STATE lists) or connective/testimony-mined knowledge for open-domain events.
5. **Testimony without the receiver's own intervention is NOT independently validated as sufficient — it is degraded, not zero.** The closest empirical proxy — passive OBSERVATION without any intervention (which delivers strictly MORE information than pure verbal testimony, since it still shows a real event) — shows a replicated **10–16 percentage-point accuracy gap** below self-intervention (Sobel & Sommerville 2010, Frontiers in Psychology: 85.0%/78.6% self-intervention vs 69.1%/59.9% observation-only; independently converged in Rottman, Kominsky & Keil 2013/2014's stick-machine paradigm: above-chance but "considerably harder," no clean age trend unlike the intervention condition). No study was found that runs the exact three-way comparison (own intervention / observed intervention / pure verbal testimony with zero observed event) — a genuine, citable gap in the developmental literature, not a search failure. Csibra & Gergely's (2009) "natural pedagogy" theory is the strongest *theoretical* case that verbal/testimonial transmission is a genuine, dedicated, evolved route to generalizable (including causal) knowledge not requiring the receiver's own intervention — but this is architecture-level, not a validated accuracy comparison, and its own critics (Sperber et al. 2010) note the same channel that transmits true causal knowledge with confidence also transmits false causal-sounding content (Santa's sleigh) with equal confidence.

======================================================================
(1) INTERVENTION vs OBSERVATION — precisely what is and isn't identifiable
======================================================================

**Pearl's ceiling (settled math, not contested):** under causal sufficiency (no unobserved confounders) + faithfulness, purely observational data identifies only the Markov equivalence class of DAGs — skeleton + v-structures (Verma & Pearl 1990; formalized in Spirtes, Glymour & Scheines 2000, *Causation, Prediction, and Search*, MIT Press). Do-calculus (Pearl 1995 *Biometrika*; Pearl 2000/2009 *Causality*, Cambridge UP) is not a discovery procedure — it presupposes a (partially) known graph and answers which interventional quantities are estimable from observational data given that graph. Unobserved confounders require the FCI/PAG extension (Zhang 2007, UAI) and non-faithfulness defeats the constraint-based algorithms' correctness guarantees outright.

**The genuine exception — functional-asymmetry identifiability:** LiNGAM (Shimizu, Hoyer, Hyvärinen & Kerminen 2006, *JMLR* 7:2003–2030) recovers the FULL DAG from observation alone under linearity + acyclicity + causal sufficiency + non-Gaussian noise, via ICA. Additive Noise Models (Hoyer, Janzing, Mooij, Peters & Schölkopf, NeurIPS 2008/2009) recover direction for genuinely nonlinear f via residual-independence testing (HSIC), again under causal sufficiency. **Both provably fail in the linear-Gaussian case** (Peters, Janzing & Schölkopf 2017, *Elements of Causal Inference*, MIT Press; full classification in Zhang & Hyvärinen 2009 UAI) — the one regime that looks identical whether or not it's identifiable, and fails silently.

**Cheng's power-PC theory (1997, Psych Review 104(2):367–405):** ΔP = P(e|c) − P(e|¬c); causal power p_c = ΔP / (1 − P(e|¬c)). This is explicitly a *strength* estimator conditional on an already-assumed causal role for c — it does not distinguish "A causes B" from "common cause C produces both A and B, A merely prior to B." This is precisely the mathematical description of why a Rescorla-Wagner/ΔP-contingency mechanism (already tried on this substrate, beat surprisal intrinsically, inert on downstream golds) was never going to establish genuine direction — it was always a strength-given-structure computation, not a structure-discovery one.

**Griffiths & Tenenbaum (2005 Cognitive Psychology 51:334–384; 2009 Psych Review 116(4):661–716):** the structure/strength distinction is formalized via "causal support" (a genuine structure-inference quantity, unlike ΔP/power). But their 2009 "theory-based causal induction" account is explicit that direction is disambiguated by a PRIOR structural theory (ontology + plausible-relationship space + functional form) that the data then adjudicates AMONG — not derived from theory-neutral covariation. Direction is supplied by the prior theory; data selects among theory-permitted candidates.

**Active/interventional learning (Gopnik, Glymour, Sobel, Schulz, Kushnir & Danks 2004, Psych Review 111(1):3–32; Bramley, Lagnado & Speekenbrink 2015 JEP:LMC 41(3):708–731; Bramley, Dayan, Griffiths & Lagnado 2017, Psych Review 124(3):301–338):** the entire self-directed-intervention research program is premised on Markov-equivalent structures (chain vs. common-cause fork) being unresolvable by passive covariation and resolved by intervention. A precise quantified observation-vs-intervention accuracy gap could not be independently confirmed in this pass (Steyvers et al. 2003 is the likely original source but was not independently re-verified) — flagged honestly as unconfirmed rather than asserted with a number; the qualitative finding is well-triangulated regardless (see also point 5 below, Sobel & Sommerville 2010, which supplies a directly-verified quantified analog).

**Temporal precedence:** necessary (rules out reverse causation) but never sufficient alone — must be conjoined with an unverifiable-from-data no-confounder assumption. The barometer/storm case (falling pressure precedes and correlates with storms without causing them; both driven by the true atmospheric pressure drop) is the standard illustration, though this drill could not trace it to one canonical original paper (flagged as folklore/teaching-example, directionally correct, weakly sourced).

**Calibrated verdict:** P(a well-defined, cite-able assumption set exists under which full direction IS identifiable from passive observation alone) ≈ **0.75** (deflated from ~0.90–0.95 baseline; the underlying theorems are genuinely well-established). P(that assumption set — causal sufficiency + functional-form/noise asymmetry — is realistically satisfiable when mining directed knowledge from a large, naturally-occurring TEXT corpus, as opposed to a designed experiment) ≈ **0.15** — capped low because open-domain narrative text has pervasive, structurally unremovable confounding and unknown/mixed/categorical "functional form," not the continuous linear-non-Gaussian or smooth-nonlinear-additive-noise regimes the theorems require. **This 0.15 number is the precise, formal reason the association-based routes already tried were always going to fail — not a power problem, a structural one.**

======================================================================
(2) TEXT/NARRATIVE-DERIVABLE PROXIES FOR INTERVENTION — connectives, counterfactuals, generics
======================================================================

**Connectives as testimony, not statistics.** Trabasso & Sperry (1985, J Mem Lang 24:595–611) and Trabasso, van den Broek & Suh (1989, Discourse Processes) build their causal-network model from hand-coded causal-necessity judgments (analyst-applied INUS-style criteria) — this is the GOLD STANDARD target, and importantly the Trabasso tradition does **not itself directly quantify how reliably surface connectives correspond to that independently-coded judgment** — a real, honestly-flagged gap between the psycholinguistic theory (causal structure is dissociable from association) and the NLP-mining assumption (connectives are a reliable proxy for that structure). Kuperberg, Paczynski & Ditman (2011, J Cogn Neurosci 23(5):1230–1246) supply independent supporting evidence that causal coherence is a distinct computation from raw lexical association: N400 tracks causal relatedness even when LSA-measured lexical co-occurrence is held constant across conditions.

**The decisive quantified NLP precedent.** Luo, Sha, Zhu, Hwang & Wang (2016, KR — 15th Int'l Conf. on Principles of KR&R; corrected from the task brief's "CIKM") built **CausalNet** from Bing web pages using explicit causal cue-phrase pattern matching, scored with PMI restricted to pattern-matched pairs. On COPA: **PMI+Connectives = 70.2%** vs. **pure PMI (Gordon et al. 2011) = 65.4%** — a **~4.8-point** gain attributable specifically to gating the statistical computation through explicit causal markers, holding the underlying mechanism (PMI) fixed. This figure is secondary-source-converged (two independent search-index confirmations) but NOT primary-PDF-verified (extraction failed twice) — treat as moderately, not fully, confident. Independently, Li, Ding, Liu, Hu & Van Durme (2020, IJCAI, "Guided Generation of Cause and Effect," arXiv:2107.09846) mined **CausalBank**: 314M cause-effect pairs from Common Crawl via causal-template matching, **95%-precision on a 1,000-sentence manual check** (a directly-reported extraction-precision floor), giving **+3 points on COPA** via continued training with no architecture change. CauseNet (Heindorf et al. 2020, CIKM, DOI 10.1145/3340531.3412763) is a third independent large-scale pattern-mining precedent (11M+ relations, 83% precision) though without a found COPA number.

**The recall ceiling.** Roughly **39% of PDTB-annotated Contingency/Cause-type discourse relations carry no explicit connective** (implicit relations) — any connective-only miner has a hard, structural recall ceiling regardless of precision, independent of the substrate's own extraction quality.

**Counterfactuals — the richest, most untested sub-mechanism.** Counterfactual/negation constructions ("if she hadn't studied") are, per the standing philosophy-of-causation literature (Lewis 1973), specifically *necessity*-type testimony — directly the primitive today's sibling note (`research_recurrent_generative_loop_counterfactual_necessity_2026-09-08.md`) proposed BUILDING via a leave-one-out decode-confidence probe over the situation register. **If a story's own text already contains an explicit counterfactual assertion about a candidate cause, that is a pre-computed human necessity judgment, obtainable more cheaply and more reliably than re-deriving it from decode-confidence drops.** Mechanically, counterfactual detection is mature (Son et al. 2017, ACL, F1=0.77 on social-media text; SemEval-2020 Task 5 gives both detection AND antecedent/consequent span extraction, 27 systems on the leaderboard) — but **no study was found anywhere that tests whether counterfactual-mined causal pairs outperform co-occurrence, or connective-mined pairs, on a forced-choice cause-selection benchmark.** This is a genuine, citable, unexploited gap — not a negative result, an untested opportunity.

**Generics — cheap to mine, theoretically well-grounded, also untested downstream.** Leslie (2008, Philosophical Review 117(1):1–47) and Prasada & Dillingham (2006, Cognition 99(1):73–112; 2009, Cognitive Science 33(3):401–448) establish generics ("mosquitoes carry malaria") as a cognitively distinct, non-statistical "principled connection" (six behaviorally-distinguished signatures from mere frequency-based "statistical connections"). GenericsKB (Bhakthavatsalam, Anastasiades & Clark 2020, arXiv:2005.00660) already demonstrates this is cheaply mineable at scale (1.7B sentences filtered via shallow syntactic heuristics — no semantic parsing, no crowd-sourcing) — but has never been benchmarked on cause-selection.

**Temporal-order iconicity:** real as a processing default (narrated order ≈ chronological/causal order) but genre-dependent (narrative vs. instructional text bias oppositely) and unquantified in reliability anywhere found — treat as, at best, a weak tie-breaking prior, not a standalone signal.

**Calibrated verdict:** P(explicit causal-linguistic markers carry a genuinely directed causal signal, distinct in kind from co-occurrence, minable offline at corpus scale, that measurably helps forced-choice cause selection) ≈ **0.45** (deflated from ~0.60–0.65; driven down by modest effect sizes, one non-primary-verified number, and zero found evidence for the counterfactual/generics extensions specifically). The single most concrete precedent to build on: **Luo et al. (2016) CausalNet's PMI+Connectives ablation, replicated independently by CausalBank (Li et al. 2020)** — both on the exact COPA task shape.

======================================================================
(3) INTUITIVE THEORIES / SIMULATION — acquire causal knowledge, or presuppose it?
======================================================================

**Every primary source checked supports PRESUPPOSITION, not acquisition.** Battaglia, Hamrick & Tenenbaum (2013 PNAS 110(45):18327–18332) run Monte Carlo sampling over noisy *inputs* (position, mass, velocity) through a fixed, hand-coded Newtonian physics engine — nothing about the physics itself is learned from sensory data. Ullman, Spelke, Battaglia & Tenenbaum (2017, Trends Cogn Sci 21:649–665) make the presupposition explicit and only speculatively gesture ("may provide the basis for...") at how the engine's parameters, not its architecture, might eventually be tuned by experience. Craik (1943, *The Nature of Explanation*) describes only how an already-possessed "small-scale model" is used for simulation; the literature and its commentators are silent on how the model is initially acquired — a genuine, notable absence, not an oversight in this scan.

Baker, Saxe & Tenenbaum (2009, Cognition 113(3):329–349) and Baker, Jara-Ettinger, Saxe & Tenenbaum (2017, Nature Human Behaviour 1:0064) presuppose a fixed rational-planning generative model (a POMDP solved via standard planning algorithms) and use Bayesian inversion only to infer hidden goals/beliefs — never to learn the planner itself. The rationality prior this presupposes appears developmentally EARLY: Gergely & Csibra (2003, Trends Cogn Sci 7(7)) find 12-month-olds already apply an efficiency-based "principle of rational action"; Liu, Ullman, Tenenbaum & Spelke (2017, Science 358(6366):1038–1041) find 10-month-olds already perform cost/utility trade-off inference — before extensive individual statistical induction is plausible. This supports "early-developing/near-innate primitive" over "learned from either observation or intervention" for the rational-planning generative structure specifically.

Core-knowledge/contact-causality: Leslie & Keeble (1987, Cognition 25:265–288) replicate Michotte's adult "launching effect" in 6.5-month-old infants (dishabituation to reversed direct-launching vs. reversed delayed events) — contact causality (objects move when touched, not spontaneously) present before extensive learning is plausible. Spelke, Breinlinger, Macomber & Jacobson (1992, Psych Review 99(4):605–632) and Baillargeon's violation-of-expectation program (1987, 1994) independently corroborate an early core of physical-causal primitives (continuity, solidity) present from ~3 months, with some (gravity/inertia) developing later — a differentiated, not uniform-learned, physical-causal starting kit.

**The passive-learning question — genuinely mixed, one real exception with a sharp caveat.** Interaction Networks (Battaglia, Pascanu, Lai, Rezende & Kavukcuoglu 2016, NeurIPS, arXiv:1612.00222) and Lerer, Gross & Fergus (2016, ICML) are trained against SIMULATOR-generated ground truth — supervised-in-disguise, not passive observation. **The genuine exception:** Garrido et al. (2025, Meta FAIR, "V-JEPA," arXiv:2502.11831) train purely self-supervised (masked latent-prediction) on real natural video (cooking/activity footage) with no simulator supervision and no embodied intervention, reaching 98% zero-shot accuracy distinguishing physically possible from impossible IntPhys videos. **But the authors themselves state it "predicts the future only as an observer" and explicitly lacks the ability to condition predictions on interventions/actions** — this is Pearl causal-hierarchy rung 1 (passive association/prediction), not rung 2 (intervention) or rung 3 (counterfactual simulation), and it is visual, not textual — it does not bear on the text-only question directly.

No primary source (Tenenbaum, Gerstenberg, Ullman lineage) was found making an explicit categorical statement on whether text alone could in principle instantiate an equivalent causal generative model — this is a genuine gap in the literature, not a resolved question either way; the field's implicit working assumption (embodied/perceptual grounding matters) is inferred from the pattern of the work, not asserted directly.

**Calibrated verdict:** P(simulation/intuitive-theory models genuinely ACQUIRE their causal knowledge from data) ≈ **0.10–0.15**. P(they PRESUPPOSE a hand-given or early-innate causal model and use simulation only for inference over it — meaning simulation-based approaches push the acquisition problem back a step rather than solving it) ≈ **0.75–0.85**. **Practical implication for this substrate: building a simulator will not solve the acquisition problem for open-domain narrative causation — it only helps once the directed causal/physical primitives already exist from another source (hand-specification for closed physical classes, or connective/testimony-mined knowledge for open-domain events).**

======================================================================
(4) THE VERDICT — blocker vs. buildable
======================================================================

**Verdict: NOT a hard foundational blocker requiring embodied/interventional experience — a genuine, narrower, brain-foundational, LLM-free, offline-learnable-from-reading mechanism exists and is only PARTIALLY built.** The precise reasoning chain:

- Section 1 explains WHY covariation-based routes (already tried: ATOMIC, PPMI, meaning_foundation similarity, Rescorla-Wagner ΔP) were structurally guaranteed to fail to transfer directed knowledge — open-domain text violates causal sufficiency almost everywhere, so no amount of better statistics over raw co-occurrence recovers direction. This is not a reason to give up; it is a reason to stop pursuing that family of mechanism.
- Section 2 shows a DIFFERENT information source — closed-class syntactic causal markers (connectives, counterfactuals, generics) — sidesteps the identifiability problem entirely because it is testimony (an author's already-computed judgment), not statistical inference, and this has a small, real, twice-independently-replicated quantified precedent (CausalNet/CausalBank, +3 to +5 points over pure co-occurrence on COPA).
- Section 3 rules out "build a simulator, let it learn causality" as a way around this — simulation approaches presuppose the causal structure they use; they do not solve acquisition, and there is no textual analog to V-JEPA's passive-video result (nothing shows a text-only system acquiring physics-grade causal structure from passive reading alone, and V-JEPA itself only reaches Pearl rung-1 association).
- Section 4 (testimony/development) confirms that testimony-only causal learning is real but DEGRADED relative to intervention (10–16pt gap for the closest measured proxy, passive observation), not the same fidelity as embodied learning, and the exact "pure verbal testimony, zero observed event" condition has literally never been isolated and tested by anyone — so calibrated expectations for a text-mined KB should be modest, not categorical, matching Section 2's modest effect sizes.

**The single most promising buildable prototype: extend the substrate's own existing per-story causal-marker extraction (`hdlab/causal_network.py` — already implements `CONNECTIVE_CAUSE_FIRST`/`CONNECTIVE_EFFECT_FIRST` closed-class connective detection and `FORCE_ACTION`/`RESULT_STATE` Talmy force-dynamics bridging, ingesting edges into a `KGStore` per-story graph, per `hdlab/kg_traversal.py`) from a LOCAL per-story tool into an OFFLINE, CORPUS-SCALE directed causal-knowledge miner, with THREE tiers of marker, ranked by expected value:**

1. **Connectives (already coded, lowest risk, weakest expected gain per Section 2's precedent):** batch-run the existing connective extraction over the full offline reading corpus (reusing `hdlab.temporal_ordering.extract_events` + the existing `CAUSAL_CONNECTIVES` closed class), aggregate `(cause_lemma, effect_lemma, connective_type, count)` edges into a general (not per-story) directed graph, ingested via the same `KGStore` primitive already used locally. Expected ceiling per the CausalNet/CausalBank precedent: **+3 to +5 points on a COPA-shaped forced-choice metric**, further discounted for the ~39% no-connective recall ceiling and this project's harder TellMeWhy-shaped (not COPA-shaped, ambiguous-item) eval — realistically this is a SMALL, not decisive, uplift on its own.
2. **Counterfactuals (new, highest theoretical value, wholly untested empirically anywhere — highest uncertainty):** mine explicit counterfactual/negation constructions ("if X hadn't happened," "had it not been for") as DIRECT necessity-testimony edges — this is strictly cheaper than, and could pre-populate or cross-validate, the leave-one-out decode-confidence necessity probe the 2026-09-08 sibling note already proposed building. Reuses the same downstream consumer (whatever situation-register/candidate-scoring organ the necessity work builds) but supplies a text-testimony PRIOR on necessity instead of (or in addition to) computing it from scratch. No published precedent exists for this exact combination — genuinely novel synthesis, cap at the discipline's 0.50 ceiling.
3. **Generics (new, cheap infrastructure precedent exists — GenericsKB — but zero downstream cause-selection precedent):** lower priority than counterfactuals; useful mainly for AFFORDANCE-type knowledge ("a store affords obtaining milk" is closer to a generic dispositional claim than an episodic causal assertion) rather than narrative cause-selection specifically.

**Organs to reuse (verified on disk this drill):** `hdlab/causal_network.py` (connective + force-dynamics extraction, already does tier 1 locally), `hdlab/kg_traversal.py` (`KGStore`, directed multi-value graph store — the "covariation causal-graph organ" the task named), `hdlab/temporal_ordering.py` (event extraction + tense, imported by causal_network.py already), `hdlab/world_state_register.py` (exists, not yet inspected for direct reuse fit — check at build time). **Not verified this drill:** a `consolidation_gate` organ was named in the task prompt as a reuse candidate; its existence/location was not confirmed in this pass — flag for exp_dev to locate or rule out at build time rather than assume.

**Expected ceiling, stated plainly and honestly:** this is a genuine, well-motivated next build, not a predicted decisive win. Calibrated P(a corpus-scale connective+counterfactual-mined directed KB, scored on the same shared-participant-confound subset already used in the sibling sufficiency/necessity work, CI-separates from an ATOMIC/co-occurrence-only twin): **0.30–0.35** for the connective-only tier (closest to a real precedent), **0.20–0.25** for the counterfactual tier specifically (higher theoretical motivation, zero empirical precedent, capped near the novel-synthesis ceiling and discounted further for being untested anywhere). P(this moves the FULL-POPULATION single-gold-argmax downstream metric by a CI-separated margin): lower still, **0.15–0.20**, for the same independently-documented TellMeWhy/COPA-style evaluation-ceiling reasons already established in the 2026-09-08 sibling notes (26% annotator overlap on TellMeWhy; >20% gold-mismatch on comparable benchmarks per Palta et al. 2024) — any real gain from directed knowledge, same as any real gain from necessity, will likely be understated by a single-gold metric and should be measured with a graded/max-over-reference protocol alongside it.

Convert counts to a PMI-style causal-strength score gated by connective presence (directly mirroring the Luo et al. 2016 CausalNet ablation). 2. **Run the identical control battery already standardized in the sibling notes:** info-free twin (permute edge scores across candidates), CI-separated margin over base, full-population paired bootstrap by GOAL/OTHER population, binding/knowledge-shuffle twins.
3. **Compare directly against the already-measured ATOMIC/co-occurrence baseline result on the exact same subset** — this is the single most informative comparison available, since it directly tests whether "directed but still association-flavored" (ATOMIC) and "directed and marker-gated" (this proposal) behave differently, which Section 1's theory predicts they should and Section 2's COPA precedent weakly confirms.
4. **Tier 2 (counterfactuals) as a follow-on, not a first build:** if Tier 1 shows any CI-separated signal, add counterfactual-marked necessity edges as a second signal and test whether it is dissociable from (adds independent value beyond) both the connective-KB signal and the sibling note's own leave-one-out necessity probe — per the same "if two mechanisms agree >=90% of the time, adopt the simpler one" standing precedent from the 2026-09-08 notes.

======================================================================

SUBSTRATE-PRODUCT IMPLICATIONS
======================================================================

Plain-language framing: we asked whether a reading-only machine (no body, no ability to poke the world, no outside AI helper) can ever really know that studying causes readiness for a test, the way a person who has actually studied and taken tests knows it. The honest science answer has two halves. Half one: trying to learn this from raw statistics alone (how often two things happen near each other) is mathematically guaranteed to fail at large enough scale, because in ordinary life almost everything is entangled with hidden third causes, and no amount of counting fixes that — this is exactly why our earlier attempts using association-style knowledge did not work, and it was never a bug, it was a wall built into the math. Half two: there is a real way around that wall, and it does not require a body. When people write, they often directly TELL you the causal judgment they already worked out ("she passed because she studied," "if she hadn't studied, she wouldn't have passed") using a small set of specific words and sentence patterns. A reading machine does not need to re-derive causation from scratch here — it just needs to notice and collect these already-solved judgments, the way a person listening to someone explain "why" is trusting what they're told rather than working it out themselves from raw evidence. There is real, published, twice-repeated evidence (on a simpler test than ours) that doing exactly this gives a small but real improvement over pure statistics-counting. The honest caveats: the improvement measured elsewhere was small (a few percentage points, not a breakthrough), a large minority of real causal statements in ordinary writing never use one of these tell-tale words at all (so this method will always miss some), and the specific richest version of this idea (mining the "if it hadn't happened" sentences, which are the closest thing to a person's own explicit yes/no test of causation) has never been tried by anyone before, anywhere, on this kind of task — it is a genuinely promising and cheap next experiment, not a guaranteed fix.

Risk of this recommendation: the theoretical case (Section 1's mathematical ceiling on statistics-only causal-direction learning, Section 2's testimony-not-inference reframing) is well-grounded across independent literatures, but the specific combination (mining this substrate's own reading corpus for connectives/counterfactuals/generics, scored on this substrate's own confound subset) has never been tested. The calibrated P estimates above (0.30-0.35 for connective-tier CI-separation on the confound subset; 0.15-0.20 for full-population single-gold movement) reflect that honestly — this is a well-motivated next experiment, not a predicted win. If Prediction 1 HARD-FAILs, the informative conclusion is that the COPA-scale precedent does not transfer to this harder task shape — a real, useful negative that would redirect priority toward Tier 2 (counterfactual testimony) specifically, since Section 2 flagged that sub-mechanism as theoretically stronger (direct necessity testimony, not just directed sufficiency-flavored connectives) even though untested.

TLDR
======================================================================
We asked the outside science a hard question: can a machine that only reads (no body, no poking the world, no hidden AI helper) ever really learn that studying causes passing a test, the way a real person knows it? The answer is not a flat no. Trying to learn this from counting how often things happen together is mathematically doomed at large scale, because almost everything in real life is tangled up with hidden third causes, and that is exactly why our earlier attempts using that approach did not work — not a mistake, a wall. But there is a real workaround that does not need a body: when people write, they often directly tell you the cause-and-effect judgment they already figured out, using words like "because," "so," and "if it hadn't happened." A reading machine does not have to solve causation from scratch here, it just has to notice and collect what people already told it, the same way a child learns some things just by being told rather than working everything out alone. There is real, published, twice-independently-repeated evidence that doing exactly this gives a small real improvement (a few percentage points) over pure statistics-counting on a simpler version of this kind of task. Caveats, stated plainly: the improvement measured elsewhere is modest, not a breakthrough; a large chunk of real "why" statements in ordinary writing never use one of these tell-tale words at all, so this method will always have blind spots; and the single richest version of this idea (collecting sentences where someone explicitly says "if it hadn't happened, this wouldn't have either," which is the closest thing to a person's own built-in causation test) has never been tried by anyone, on any task like ours, before. It is a promising, cheap next experiment — not a guaranteed fix.
