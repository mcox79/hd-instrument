      + RELEVANCE-FILTERED.

   arXiv math/cs prose:
      WORST candidate. Extracting theorem statements from PROSE requires
      NLP/LLM -> 11th-RULE VIOLATION (substrate-internal, no-LLM).
      No reliable deterministic parse of free-text claims. Floating-fact
      at scale.

      Tier-C archive -- but git+grep over paper list is cheaper.

   OEIS (integer sequences):
      STRUCTURED + deterministic-parse + 11th-rule-clean.
      Relevant to residue/combinatorics (CRT-adjacent).
      BUT bulk OEIS = mostly-no-consumer.
      RECOMMENDATION: SELECTIVE consumer-pull -- atomize sequences/
      identities a primitive needs (same model as CRT/Tier 4a), NOT bulk.
