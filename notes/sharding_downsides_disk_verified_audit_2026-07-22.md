## Per-downside verdicts (disk-verified)
1. **Label-free routing (self-routing vs oracle): PARTIAL, pitch-inflated.** PP-128 routing=1.000=oracle BUT the routing feature is constructed as `center[true_shard] + 0.5*noise` on 16 well-separated synthetic Gaussians -- favorable-by-construction, NOT real entity embeddings (no literal label leak, but not a real-data demonstration). b2_self_improving_routing (cold 0.944->warm 0.998) = same synthetic-cluster story, real online-centroid improvement but toy data. **"self-routing = oracle" is UNVERIFIED for real/production embeddings.**
2. **Cross-shard retrieval (scatter-gather / sequential 2-hop): RESOLVED at small single-seed scale** -- cross_shard_query recall=1.000, kg_crossshard_2hop=1.000. BUT it is SEQUENTIAL (lookup shard A -> route -> lookup shard B), not joint multi-shard bind; and PP-313 "40 shards x 1000" was MISCATEGORIZED (it is single-shard selection, belongs under routing).
3. **Cross-shard RELATIONAL / multi-hop reasoning: PARTIAL -- the deepest + most-open, disk-confirmed a REAL cost.** Sequential composition (per-hop cleanup OR known map) works near-ceiling; raw superposition chaining COLLAPSES by hop-2 in both domains (grounding NO_CLEANUP reach@2=0.011-0.025); JOINT/parallel binding across shards (tensor-product two-shard, operator-trace correlation) HARD_FAILs on EVERY operationalization (tensor_acc 0.018 vs seq 1.000; xshard-corr AUC 0.459 = below random); autonomous (no oracle, no known map) multi-hop is REAL but WEAK = ~36% of oracle-waypoint ceiling. **The `grounding_multihop_sr_reachability_routing_v1` "HARD_PASS_CG_SR_REACHABILITY" is NOT a genuine chain-grade -- disk+prose confirmed MM:** the SR resolvent is closed-form from the FULLY-KNOWN transition matrix; its own reciprocal-necessity self-test showed aliasing the learned-code term to useless leaves routing UNCHANGED (1.0) = zero substrate-learned representation enters; its follow-up note reclassifies it MM and pre-registers the real held-out-subgraph CG test = NEVER BUILT. **"ZERO false chain-grade" HOLDS.** Transfers to grounding: YES (matched real-data test in grounding domain, not extrapolation).
4. **Elastic overflow/capacity: RESOLVED** (pre 0.160 -> post 1.000) but ~restates the N/(2 ln N) capacity floor (split until per-bundle load < floor). Live-query-correctness-during-split-race = UNTESTED. Domain-general.
5. **Granularity: RESOLVED for "which axis wins"** -- subject-entity sharding beats relation-sharding 2.3x (1.000 vs 0.4325); hierarchical (relation-then-subject) rescues per-relation MIDDLE to 1.000. BUT the "10-100x capacity / per-concept rho=0.5" material is THEORY (the drill's own header: "NO empirical verification"); its cheap decisive test = never run.
6. **SOFT SHARDING via sparse/DG codes (the brain-faithful alternative that would sidestep routing/partition cost): UNRESOLVED / mostly NO-DATA.** The one real test `grounding_encoder_sparse_block_binding_v1` = HARD_FAIL_BLOCK_BINDING_INSUFFICIENT: sparse block arm recall 0.528 vs dense 0.423 (+0.105, a small REAL positive signal, dg_expansion_net_positive=True) but NO arm clears HP, and DG-then-resonator-cleanup BACKFIRES to 0.052. The two other candidates (`_substrate_swr_v3_2_sparse_dg_burst`, `_probe_flagship_decrowd_survives_sparse`) = NEVER DISPATCHED (no data/ dir).

## BOTTOM LINE
- SOLID + safe to build on (closed-set imagery ingest): overflow/capacity + granularity + sequential cross-shard retrieval. All near-ceiling, disk-verified, but closer to restating capacity theory than novel infra.
- **NOT resolved for the REASONING store:** (a) routing-beats-oracle is synthetic-only; (b) cross-shard RELATIONAL composition is a real, open, disk-confirmed cost (joint binding HARD_FAILs; autonomous multi-hop ~36% of ceiling; the one "CG" is MM known-map search); (c) SOFT SHARDING -- the thing that would dissolve the downsides -- has essentially NO supporting data (one HARD_FAIL small-positive, two never-run).
- **UNIFYING DIAGNOSIS (store thread <-> learner thread, same shape):** the pieces that WORK are the FIXED / known-map / sequential / closed-form ones; the pieces that are OPEN are the LEARNED / autonomous / relational ones.
