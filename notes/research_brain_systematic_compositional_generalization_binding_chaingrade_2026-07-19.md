## SCAN 2 (developmental learning curve) -- the design-critical result
- **Compositional generalization has a REAL, MEASURABLE LEARNING CURVE, NOT zero-shot from structure alone** (high confidence, converging: wug-test age-graded [Berko]; overregularization U-curve [Marcus]; Tomasello item-based -> gradual abstraction ~age 3).
- **Highest-confidence quantitative finding: the curve is a THRESHOLD PHASE-CHANGE, not a smooth ramp.** Yang Tolerance Principle: a rule becomes productive once exceptions < theta_N = N/ln(N). Schuler-Yang 2023 infants: generalized at 11/5 (68.75%, above threshold) but FAILED at 10/6 (62.5%, below), NO gradual scaling above threshold = quantal. => gradual EXEMPLAR ACCUMULATION until threshold, then abrupt switch to productivity.
- **TYPE frequency (# distinct exemplars instantiating a pattern), NOT token frequency, drives productivity** (Bybee); exemplar DIVERSITY/variability benefits generalization (Wonnacott). Curriculum "starting small" (Elman) helps.
- **IMPLICATION FOR OUR RESULTS: the factorization's zero-shot-perfect held-out generalization (1.000 vs flat 0.003) is the CONSTRUCTION-DETERMINED RED FLAG** -- a factored VSA with oracle role-keys recovers held-out combos trivially by unbinding, which is NOT what the brain does (brain LEARNS over a curve to a threshold). The USER-directed flexible/IMPROVING property = the LEARNING CURVE. So the chain-grade must FORCE learning + MEASURE the curve, not report zero-shot accuracy.

## SCAN 3 (agent/patient asymmetry + test design)
- **Asymmetry: carried by DISTINCT ROLE representations + a COMMUTATIVE/symmetric composition, NOT an asymmetric operator.** Frankland & Greene 2015 (PNAS): distinct cortical subregions encode agent vs patient identity, ROLE-GENERAL (abstracted from filler); distinguishes "truck hit ball" vs "ball hit truck" at 57% (tracks THEMATIC role not surface position). Lalisse & Smolensky 2021 reanalysis: bindings non-orthogonal, spatially overlapping, recovered by ADDITIVE superposition (TPR-like). Plus a downstream agent-first HEURISTIC + P600 reanalysis layered on top.
- **CONFIRMED (high, textbook): TPR/HRR/VSA all use COMMUTATIVE bind (outer product / circular convolution / Hadamard) + DISTINCT ROLE VECTORS; ALL asymmetry lives in the distinct role vectors, NOT the operator.** => VALIDATES the Director self-correction: our commutative bind handles agent!=patient fine (distinct role keys); "break commutativity" was WRONG. The BRAIN also composes commutatively with distinct roles = SAME as our FHRR. NO asymmetry wall.
- **CROSS-DOMAIN CONVERGENCE (load-bearing): the ML fix for compositional generalization = the neuroscience binding solution = SEPARATE ROLE FROM FILLER (TPR-style factorization).** Every successful ML approach (meta-seq2seq, permutation-equivariant, syntax-semantics-separation, compositional-parsers) injects explicit role/filler separation -- which IS our substrate's native inductive bias. Vanilla seq2seq fails (SCAN jump split ~0; COGS structural ~0).
- **TEST DESIGN (CFQ/DBCA/MCD, Keysers 2020 + COGS Kim-Linzen 2020):** decompose into ATOMS (primitive roles/fillers) + COMPOUNDS (combinations); hold ATOM divergence LOW (every atom seen in training at similar freq -> rules out "never saw the word") while MAXIMIZING COMPOUND divergence (novel combinations). COGS analog: a noun seen ONLY as subject in training, tested as OBJECT. Dose-response: accuracy degrades monotonically with compound divergence.
- **"MUST-FAIL"/anti-leakage controls (this literature's standard):** (1) atom-divergence control = ONE variable (only combinations differ); (2) in-distribution NEAR-CEILING gap = the must-fail control (proves both arms CAN fit, aren't undertrained); (3) param-matched flat baseline WITHOUT the compositional bias must still fail (isolates inductive bias not scale/capacity); (4) compound-divergence dose-response (rules out a single-threshold artifact).

## SCAN 4 (TEM structure-content transfer -- the direct biological analog; refines the design + reveals our gap)
- TEM (Whittington/Behrens 2020, Cell) = the biological twin of our factorization: structural code g (grid/transition) bound with sensory content x into a hippocampal conjunctive code p -- via a HEBBIAN associative memory (a learned/gated bind, NOT pure elementwise g(x)x; a nuance vs our fixed FHRR bind).
- **What TEM holds out = a NEW ENVIRONMENT (new node<->stimulus bindings, SAME relation/topology class), NOT new relation-types.** So TEM generalizes "same structure, new content," not "new structure."
- **TWO-STAGE learning shape (load-bearing, matches Scan-2's threshold): (a) SLOW structure-formation, then (b) FAST ~ONE-SHOT content-binding into the learned structure, then genuinely ZERO-SHOT USE (inference on untraversed edges).** Sharpest empirical = Tse 2007: schema took ~15 trials/~1 month to form; ONCE established, a NEW flavor-place association learned in ONE trial. So the curve is: slow to build the role-scaffold, then one-shot to slot new fillers -> generalize.
- **FAILURE BOUNDARY (difficulty-on / can-fail): novel CONTENT in known structure generalizes fast (one-shot); novel STRUCTURE / relation-TYPE (not just a new arrangement of known primitives) BREAKS zero-shot transfer** -- g(x)x factorization only pays off when structure REPEATS (Bakermans 2025 prediction; Zhang 2026 preprint, low-confidence). Also one-shot re-use is fast-but-DISTORTED (grid cells, Nature 2024), needing a ~10-trial downstream correction (BTSP) -- fast-but-imperfect with a slower corrective layer.
- **>>> THE GENUINE GAP WE FILL (creditable contribution): TEM + successors report NO param-matched factorized-vs-entangled (LSTM/MLP) sample-efficiency / learning-curve comparison -- they demonstrate factored generalization but never quantified its sample-efficiency EDGE vs a non-factored baseline. Our chain-grade cell (native-binding vs param-matched flat, learning-curve + threshold) fills exactly this open gap. <<<**
- **DESIGN REFINEMENT:** separate (i) STRUCTURE-learning (the slow role-scaffold curve) from (ii) CONTENT-binding (fast/one-shot slotting of new fillers into known roles). The construction-determined guard sharpens: "zero-shot per held-out role-filler combo" is NOT automatically trivial -- it is BRAIN-FAITHFUL *if the STRUCTURE had to be learned first* (the slow part). Trivial/construction-determined = structure handed as oracle role-keys. Brain-faithful = structure LEARNED over a curve, THEN new content one-shot-generalizes. Flat baseline must relearn per-combination (no structure to reuse) -> factored's advantage GROWS with structure-reuse. HONEST BOUNDING: include a novel-relation-TYPE condition where BOTH factored and flat should FAIL (g(x)x can't reuse absent structure) -- prevents over-claiming the edge.
