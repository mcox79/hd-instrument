# Research drill — graded necessity + cross-sentence CAUSE-dominance
Problem: `causation_is_typed_per_clause_not_across_the_causal_network`
Drill date: 2026-08-30 · Director/hdi_research · ONLINE LITERATURE, leads-with-biology

Scope: two measured walls on the discourse causal-network EDGE typer (Wolff force
dynamics composed over the Trabasso network). WALL 1 = cross-sentence links are
overwhelmingly plain CAUSE; ENABLE rare, PREVENT ~absent, both packed WITHIN a
clause. WALL 2 = discrete CAUSE/ENABLE/PREVENT label vs graded necessity, plus the
"held his tongue because he promised" = CAUSE-or-PREVENT framing ambiguity.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## VERDICT 1 — Cross-sentence CAUSE-dominance IS brain-foundational (foreground/background). The negative is PRINCIPLED, not a corpus artifact.

The corpus pattern falls straight out of three independently-established mechanisms;
this is over-determined, not a small-sample fluke.

**(a) The narrative causal CHAIN is the foregrounded object, and the chain is read
as CAUSE links.** Trabasso & van den Broek (1985) and Trabasso, van den Broek & Suh
(1989): events with more causal connections to other events are recalled more often
and recalled first; the "causal chain" of occurring events is the spine of the
situation model. Zwaan & Radvansky (1998) / Zwaan, Langston & Graesser (1995)
event-indexing: causality is one of five monitored dimensions and readers foreground
goal-/chain-relevant content across sentences. The cross-SENTENCE relation the reader
maintains is the forward event chain — a chain of occurrences — which is exactly the
CAUSE force-dynamic pattern (affector drives a not-yet-tending patient to a reached
endstate).

**(b) ENABLE is definitionally a figure-against-GROUND relation, so its "ground" is
a backgrounded STATE, not a cross-event node.** In Wolff's (2007) force theory ENABLE
(=HELP) requires the patient to ALREADY tend toward the endstate (concordant forces,
endstate reached). That prior tendency is a pre-existing condition — Mackie's causal
FIELD, the presupposed background against which the abnormal/covarying factor is
selected as THE cause (causal selection: Cheng & Novick 1991; Hilton). Trabasso, van
den Broek & Suh (1989) confirm the split empirically: a pure causal criterion
SEPARATES enabling relations from the three genuine causes (physical, motivational,
psychological), and enabling scores LOWEST on necessity-in-the-circumstances —
enabling is "necessary but not sufficient," i.e. background. Grammatically the whole
Agonist-tendency + Antagonist interaction is co-present at ONE point, so it
lexicalizes into a single predicate taking a complement ("let Ann get IN", "prevented
him FROM going") — Talmy's (1988) force dynamics is intrinsically a clause-level
lexical-semantic phenomenon, whereas CAUSE chains span temporally separated events
across sentences. Hence ENABLE packs within a clause and is rare as a cross-sentence
edge.

**(c) PREVENT is a NON-occurrence, and event chains encode occurrences.** In the force
theory PREVENT = endstate NOT reached; Wolff & Barbey (2010, "For want of a nail: how
absences cause events", Cognition/Cog Sci) and Gerstenberg et al.'s counterfactual
simulation model represent prevention/omission via counterfactual force-REMOVAL, not
as a positive event token. A prevented event did not happen, so it never becomes a
node in the forward chain of occurring events — it can only be expressed lexically,
within a clause, as a counterfactual/state ("the wall kept the flood out"). This
predicts cross-sentence PREVENT ≈ 0, exactly as measured.

**Take:** the two-layer structure is the brain's structure — a foregrounded
cross-event CAUSE chain (occurrences) + a backgrounded STATE layer holding the
enabling/preventing conditions (lexicalized within clauses). Trying to mine
cross-sentence ENABLE/PREVENT edges is fighting the architecture. BUILD the two
layers; do not treat the absence as a detector failure.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## VERDICT 2 — The brain's per-edge causal representation is GRADED (a necessity/sufficiency strength); CAUSE/ENABLE/PREVENT is a DERIVED discretization. Graded is the higher-fidelity organ.

**Evidence the underlying quantity is continuous:**
- Trabasso, van den Broek & Suh (1989): edges are weighted by degree of
  necessity-in-the-circumstances (a graded counterfactual strength), and naive
  causal judgment uses BOTH necessity AND sufficiency — i.e. a 2-D graded
  (necessity, sufficiency) quantity, not a 3-way label. The type ordering (physical >
  motivational > psychological > enabling) is itself a graded necessity axis.
- Kuperberg, Paczynski & Ditman (2011, J. Cog. Neurosci. 23(5):1230–1246,
  "Establishing causal coherence across sentences"): the N400 to a critical word
  varied GRADEDLY with causal relatedness (unrelated > intermediate > highly related),
  co-occurrence matched by LSA. The brain computes a graded causal-coherence signal
  ONLINE — direct neural evidence against a discrete online type.
- Cheng (1997) Power PC: generative power and preventive power are SEPARATE continuous
  [0,1] quantities → a 2-D graded (generative, preventive) space, from which a discrete
  sign/type is a read-out.
- Wolff (2007) force theory: CAUSE/ENABLE/PREVENT are DISCRETIZED from continuous
  force-vector configurations (patient tendency, affector–patient concordance,
  endstate progress). Sloman, Barbey & Hotaling (2009) likewise derive the three
  verbs from underlying causal-model structure. The label is the projection, not the
  representation.

**The framing ambiguity is resolved by going graded.** "He held his tongue because he
promised" has ONE invariant force configuration: a promise-force opposing a
speaking-tendency, resolving to restraint (= non-speaking). Labeling it CAUSE (of the
restraint) or PREVENT (of the speaking) is a choice of which endstate you take as the
patient's reference — a perspective on the READ-OUT, not two different edges. A discrete
typer cannot hold both because it discards the reference frame; a graded force/necessity
edge stores the configuration once and lets the label be computed per-clause against an
EXPLICIT reference endstate. The ambiguity lives in the discretization, not in the brain's
representation.

**Mechanism — what the brain computes on each edge, and how force dynamics feeds a
graded necessity read-out:**
1. Per edge, represent Wolff continuous force vectors: affector force **A** (magnitude +
   direction toward/away from endstate E) and the patient's own tendency **P** (magnitude
   + direction), plus the E-threshold.
2. **Necessity of A** = counterfactual: is E reached without A? = f(sign & magnitude of P
   vs E-threshold). Patient NOT tending to E (P away/weak) → A highly necessary → CAUSE
   region. Patient already tending to E (P toward, strong) → A only necessary against
   opposition → LOW necessity → ENABLE region. This reproduces Trabasso's finding that
   enabling scores lowest in necessity — because the patient reaches E largely on its own.
3. **Sufficiency of A** = does A alone drive resultant past the E-threshold (magnitude /
   concordance).
4. **Preventive strength** = the negative-direction component: A opposes P and holds the
   resultant below the E-threshold → PREVENT region; endstate-reached flag separates
   PREVENT (not reached) from CAUSE/ENABLE (reached).
So the per-edge read-out is a graded **(necessity, sufficiency)** pair — equivalently
(generative_strength, preventive_strength) — obtained by counterfactual simulation over
the force vectors. This is the exact bridge: Wolff force dynamics (REPRESENTATION) →
Trabasso necessity-in-circumstances / Kuperberg online graded signal (READ-OUT). The
discrete type is a thresholded projection of the same pair.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## CONCRETE NEXT-BUILD RECOMMENDATION
1. Replace the discrete 3-way classifier with a GRADED per-edge estimator that outputs a
   continuous **(necessity, sufficiency)** pair (≈ generative_strength, preventive_strength
   ∈ [0,1]), derived from the Wolff force vectors via a counterfactual read-out
   (necessity = counterfactual necessity of A given patient tendency P; sufficiency = A
   magnitude vs endstate threshold; preventive = opposing-component holding E below threshold).
2. Make CAUSE/ENABLE/PREVENT a THRESHOLDED PROJECTION of that pair, read out per-clause
   against an EXPLICIT reference endstate (perspective). Same edge, two labels under two
   reference endstates → this is what dissolves the "because he promised" ambiguity.
3. Model the DISCOURSE two-layer structure: keep the cross-EVENT network as the
   foregrounded CAUSE chain (occurring events); encode ENABLE/PREVENT as WITHIN-CLAUSE /
   background-STATE annotations on the situation-model state layer, NOT as cross-sentence
   edges. Stop treating the cross-sentence ENABLE/PREVENT absence as a miss.
4. BRAIN-FIDELITY VALIDATION (can-fail): the graded output must reproduce (a) Trabasso's
   human necessity-strength ordering physical > motivational > psychological > enabling,
   and (b) Kuperberg's graded-N400-by-relatedness ordering. If the force→necessity
   read-out does NOT recover these gradings, the read-out is wrong — that is the fair,
   one-variable test of the higher-fidelity organ against the brain's own metric.

## Calibration note
Both verdicts are convergent across ≥3 independent literatures (discourse psychology,
force-dynamics linguistics, causal-induction cognition) plus a direct online neural
signature (Kuperberg graded N400). Confidence HIGH for the DIRECTION (graded > discrete;
