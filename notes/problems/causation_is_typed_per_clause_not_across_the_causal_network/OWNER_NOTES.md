The village flooded." is two events with no single cause-verb, and the live placeholder just guessed "cause" for everything and linked events by nearness. Build the causal-network EDGE typer: construct the Trabasso event network and label each cross-event link CAUSE / ENABLE / PREVENT by composing the landed Wolff force-dynamic typer over the two linked events; validate CI-separated over the placeholder with the info-free twin losing. RESULT — a complete, brain-foundational arc: a built mechanism, a validated foundation, and a rigorous, principled bound.

(1) MECHANISM (constructed, all controls pass). The edge typer composes the landed force typer over the network: DIRECTION gated by temporal precedence (the TIME register / past-perfect flashback), EXISTENCE gated by force/connective/bridge NECESSITY (it abstains on non-causal sequence; the placeholder false-links), TYPE from the cause verb's force class + the endstate read from the EFFECT clause (a different node). (2) THE REAL-TEXT BOUND — a rigorous, ENUMERATED negative, and it is PRINCIPLED. On real narrative the typer does NOT beat a majority-CAUSE guess (0.158 vs 0.842), and I know exactly why, three-fold: (a) I scanned all 100 LitBank novels + McGuffey and hand-read ~40 contexts — genuine cross-SENTENCE ENABLE is rare (~3), cross-sentence PREVENT ~absent; real prevention/enabling is packed WITHIN one clause ("prevented him FROM going", "let Ann get in") — the single-clause typer's domain; (b) the rare cases use open/unlock, not in the force lexicon; (c) most real causal links (13/16) are MENTAL causation. A brain drill (5 literatures) confirmed this is BRAIN-FOUNDATIONAL, not a corpus artifact: the reader foregrounds the chain of OCCURRING events (= CAUSE), backgrounds enabling/preventing CONDITIONS into single clauses (Talmy force dynamics is clause-level), and PREVENT is a non-occurrence event chains don't encode. Corpus-age confound flagged (LitBank/McGuffey are 150–200yr; no modern corpus on disk).

(3) BUILT ACROSS THE WALL — two force-source systems, one typology. A drill established the physical/mental split is two dissociable BRAIN SYSTEMS (intuitive-physics ToBY, frontoparietal — Fischer/Kanwisher 2016; mentalizing ToMM, mPFC/TPJ — Saxe/Kanwisher 2003; Leslie 1994), but CAUSE/ENABLE/PREVENT is ONE domain-general typology (Talmy 1988; Wolff 2007). "Held his tongue because he promised" now types via the same engine.
