## The rule that keeps the expansion honest (non-negotiable)

The instrument must be **frozen before the mechanism is built or its per-item output is ever read.**
Grading a test with the student taking it is contamination — the brief bars it and it disqualifies
the author of the gold. So the ORDER is load-bearing: **bank first, locked, never edited to fit the
mechanism; mechanism second, graded against the frozen bank.**
