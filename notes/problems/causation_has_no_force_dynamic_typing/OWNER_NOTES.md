THE PROBLEM. The reader's CAUSATION dimension was a placeholder: it linked events by cue words (because/so) and by "what came just before," and its own docstring admitted it "fails where cause != most-recent." It could not TYPE a causal relation — CAUSE vs ENABLE vs PREVENT — and could not represent a PREVENTED outcome that never happened. The brain types causation by FORCE DYNAMICS (Talmy 1988; Wolff 2007): CAUSE/ENABLE/PREVENT fall out of a tiny discrete truth-table over three questions — does the patient tend toward the outcome on its own, do the forces push together or against, did the outcome actually happen? The brief: build that force-dynamic typer over the existing (agent, patient, predicate) extraction + a substrate-native force-dynamic verb lexicon, and show it types the three CI-separated over BOTH the placeholder AND precedence-only, with the info-free (force-class-shuffled) twin losing. No do-calculus (already HARD_FAILED), no external LLM.

RESULT — SOLVED (the bar is met, and I pushed past it three ways). Copied Wolff's COMPUTATION exactly; swept the PARAMETERS (lexicon, frames, polarity).

CORE (exp_causal_force_dynamic_typer_v1): on connective-neutral minimal pairs (n=42), force-dynamic typing 0.929 [0.833,1.000] vs the connective/adjacency PLACEHOLDER 0.190 and PRECEDENCE-ONLY 0.190 — both beaten CI-separated. Info-free force-class-shuffle twin 0.383 (p95 0.500) LOSES. PREVENT killer (outcome never happens): 0.900 vs placeholder 0.000 — the placeholder has no node to link; only force dynamics represents a prevented endstate (Wolff/Barbey/Hausknecht 2010; Kaup negation-as-simulation). CAUSE-vs-ENABLE verb isolation (outcome held constant): 1.000 vs twin 0.441 — the lift is genuinely the verb force-semantics, not the endstate bit. The lexicon is EXTERNAL (FrameNet Causation family, 422 verbs, built before the test gold — escapes the probe's construction-proof), and the win SURVIVES dropping my hand-added backoff (pure FrameNet 0.738 > 0.190).

THE ONE REAL WALL, UNDERSTOOD AND CROSSED (exp_causal_tendency_recovery). CAUSE-vs-ENABLE for tendency-ambiguous verbs (open/move/turn) is NOT in the verb — "the key opened the gate" (ENABLE) vs "the wind opened the gate" (CAUSE), same verb. Research pinned WHY: patient tendency is world-knowledge (Wolff & Song 2003) and partly linguistically constructed (Kuhnmuench & Beller 2005); a verb lexicon is capped at 0.500 there (measured, vs a tendency-oracle 1.000). But Wolff's own force ARITHMETIC recovers it from a cue already in the sentence — a WEAK affector that still succeeds (a nudge, a breeze) means the patient contributed → ENABLE; a STRONG affector (a winch, a heave) means it overcame the patient → CAUSE. A glass-box affector-magnitude estimator lifts 0.500 → 1.000, the magnitude-shuffle twin stays at chance, and it generalizes to held-out affectors. So the wall is a buildable path, not a dead end (mechanism demonstration).

REAL-TEXT POINT ESTIMATE (exp_causal_force_dynamic_realtext). 21 verbatim McGuffey sentences, hand-adjudicated. On the mechanism's proper domain (verb in its force sense, n=12): 0.917 vs placeholder 0.333 — recovers real force-dynamic causation incl. "saved/kept X FROM Y" → PREVENT and a negated CAUSE. Key refinement: the feared polysemy-precision problem largely dissolves because the PREVENT from-construction self-disambiguates the non-force uses of kept/held/saved/stopped (7/7 correctly rejected); the residual verb-sense need is CONCENTRATED (a few frame-polysemous verbs like "upset the boat"=capsize + the "let us/let me" hortative), not pervasive.

MEASURED BOUNDS (exp_causal_force_lexicon_coverage). ENABLE is barely lexicalized (of 391 non-gold verbs, 1 is ENABLE — consistent with the "partly constructed" finding). Most narrative causation is connective-linked clause pairs (the Trabasso NETWORK level), which force dynamics LABELS but a verb lexicon doesn't type — the applicability bound.
