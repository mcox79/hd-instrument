## 1. THE BRAIN'S MECHANISM (the opening move), AND THE UPSTREAM CHAIN

**Structure: the anterior-temporal semantic HUB and its modality spokes (PINNED).** Patterson, Nestor & Rogers 2007; Binder & Desai 2011; Ralph, Jefferies, Patterson & Rogers 2017. Lexical-semantic knowledge -- a word's sense inventory, its supersense, its taxonomic parents, its argument frames, its polarity -- is STORED there and retrieved by content-addressed lookup during comprehension. Two properties of that structure are load-bearing here, and they are what the organ implements:

- **Acquisition is offline; retrieval is a read.** The store is laid down developmentally. Nothing in the comprehension loop re-derives it. A library that parses index files, builds maps and walks a graph *while reading* is the acquisition machinery running inside the read -- the wrong computation, not merely an untidy dependency. **Measured: 4,348,472 library calls on the FIRST document of a 4-document read.**
- **One store, many reads.** The hub is ONE structure read by many consumers, not 36 private lookups each re-implementing its own access. Every site now addresses the same organ; the relation types (sense inventory / supersense / taxonomy / frames / polarity) are ARMS of one store.

## EVALUATION OF THE MOST SUCCESSFUL IMPROVEMENTS -- why the signal was maximised

**The win was cracking the real chain to the top, rung by rung, and the chain here is the SUPPLY chain:**

1. **INSTRUMENT FIRST, at the object level, not the call level.** Proxying the returned `Synset`/`Lemma` objects -- not just `wordnet.synsets` -- is what turned "37 modules import nltk" into "2,006,404 distinct queries, 34 operations, and 99.1% of them come from one module rebuilding a static graph". Every later decision (what to freeze, sqlite vs JSON, freeze the graph too) fell out of that table rather than out of judgement.
2. **THE PARITY FLOOR WAS THE LIBRARY ITSELF, query for query.** Not a sample, not a summary: replaying all 2,006,404 queries is what caught the `all_synsets` POS-order difference, which no reasonable spot-check would have caught and which would have silently changed a graph node order.
3. **THE DYNAMIC HALF FOUND WHAT THE STATIC HALF COULD NOT.** Poisoning `nltk` and running the real read is what exposed the perceptron on the live path -- a defect the brief, the 09-13 flip note and my own corpus-level shim all missed. **A static audit of a dependency cannot tell you what a read does.**
4. **ISOLATING THE TWO HALVES MADE THE ONE REGRESSION ATTRIBUTABLE.** The freeze-only arm (byte-identical) is what allows "exactly one dimension moved, and the tagger moved it" instead of "the digests differ, we think it's fine".
5. **THE REPAIR CAME FROM THE CONFUSION DIRECTION, NOT FROM THE ACCURACY NUMBER.** The organ and the stand-in make the same NUMBER of VBD/VBN errors (276 vs 277). Only the per-tag breakdown showed the organ trades VBD->VBN 97 times, and only the consumer's own rule (bare VBN is dropped) explains why that costs events. The fix then wrote itself, and it needed no new knowledge: read the posterior under the licensing condition the consumer already applies.

**Rungs NOT cracked (named, with numbers):** the category organ's verb-form evidence (all-verb-form 0.8685, VBG->NN 58, VBP->VB 143, VBD<->VBN 97/73 on 40 docs) -- 22% of gold past/participial events still never reach the timeline; and the lexicon's CONTENT is still supplied rather than acquired.

## ALTERNATE PATHS -- other ways to do this read, similarly or more brain-foundational

1. **A SPARSE DISTRIBUTED STORE INSTEAD OF A B-TREE INDEX (more brain-foundational).** Structure: hippocampal/cortical content-addressable memory; computation: retrieval by overlap on a sparse code, not exact-key lookup, which buys graceful degradation and similarity-based recall for free (a misspelled or novel form still activates neighbours -- which an exact index cannot do, and which is the substrate's `content_addressable_retrieval` slot). What it would take: encode each synset packet as a sparse hypervector, retrieve by cleanup memory; the risk is that byte-identity with WordNet stops being provable, so it must be landed as a SECOND arm behind the frozen exact store, scored on the reads that currently miss (OOV forms). This is the single most promising follow-on and it is blocked only by wanting the exact-parity arm first.
2. **ACQUIRE THE STORE INSTEAD OF IMPORTING IT (the honest end state).** Structure: cross-situational statistical learning into the hub (Yu & Smith); the lexicon's supersenses and relations are learnable from reading plus the consolidation gate. What it would take: the learner program's propose-and-verify loop run against `observe()`'s demand log; why not now: it is a different problem (filed), and a freeze must be provably answer-preserving before its content can be allowed to drift.
3. **PER-SPOKE ASSETS RATHER THAN ONE STORE.** The hub-and-spoke architecture is literally modality-specific spokes feeding one hub, so a taxonomy spoke, a frame spoke and an affect spoke could each be their own asset (the entity-type spoke already is). Why not now: it fragments one structure into several organs, which is the anti-fragmentation gate's exact failure mode; the arms-of-one-organ shape is the better reading of the same neuroscience.
4. **LOAD THE WHOLE STORE INTO MEMORY (simpler, less faithful).** ~150 MB of Python objects, no per-query cost. Rejected: a reader that asks no lexical question should pay nothing, and the measured per-query cost is already a wash with nltk (252.9 us vs 243.7 us).

## KEY REALIZATIONS

- **A static audit of a dependency cannot tell you what a read does.** Poisoning the import and running the reader found a supervised model on the live path that the brief, a landed flip note and my own corpus-level instrumentation all said was gone.
- **Proxy the OBJECTS, not the entry points.** The relations a read walks are invisible if you only log `wordnet.synsets`; logging them is what revealed that 99.1% of the traffic was one module doing acquisition inside the read.
- **When a freeze and a behaviour change ship together, isolate them or you can prove neither.** The freeze-only arm is the whole reason the single changed dimension is attributable.
- **Two taggers making the same NUMBER of errors can have very different downstream cost.** The direction of the confusion, read against the consumer's own rule, is where the repair was hiding -- and the repair needed no new knowledge, only the graded posterior the organ was already producing and the consumer was already discarding.
- **A parity bar has to be the library itself, exhaustively.** 2,006,404 replays found exactly one ordering difference, in the one operation whose order a sample would never have checked.
