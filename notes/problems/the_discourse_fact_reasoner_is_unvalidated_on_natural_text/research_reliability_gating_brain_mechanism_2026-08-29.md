# Research drill: reliability-gating of a world-knowledge bridge in reference resolution — brain mechanism (2026-08-29)

Question: is the measured net-zero gate ("no observable gate beats the fact-blind 0.805 floor; oracle ceiling +0.021") a "brain CAN, we can't" fidelity gap, or INTRINSIC to the problem? Leading with biology. Each claim tagged PINNED-BY-EVIDENCE / SPECULATION.

## 1. WHEN does the brain deploy a world-knowledge bridge — discrete gate or continuous graded cue?
- **PINNED** — The dominant, best-evidenced picture is CONTINUOUS graded integration, not a discrete reliability gate. McRae, Spivey-Knowlton & Tanenhaus (1998, *JML*) quantified thematic fit off-line (norming) and showed it influences self-paced reading times *early and simultaneously* with syntactic constraints in a competition/constraint-satisfaction model — world knowledge enters as one weighted cue among many, in real time, not as a gated override.
- **PINNED** — Metusalem et al. (2012, *JML*, PMID 22711976) show generalized event knowledge is activated automatically and incrementally (reduced N400 to event-related but locally anomalous words, over and above lexical association). Event knowledge is always ON and graded by activation strength; it is not fetched only when a gate opens.
- **PINNED** — Kehler, Kertz, Rohde & Elman (2008, *J. Semantics* 25:1) and the Bayesian formalization Kehler & Rohde (2013, *Theoretical Linguistics*) model pronoun interpretation as `P(referent|pronoun) ∝ P(pronoun|referent)·P(referent)`, where world knowledge/coherence enters the PRIOR `P(referent)` and morphosyntax/salience the LIKELIHOOD. World knowledge is a soft term multiplied in, weighted by how much probability mass the event/coherence relation actually assigns — i.e., a low-reliability fact automatically gets low weight in the product.
- **Implication (SPECULATION, strongly supported):** In this framework a *low weight on an unreliable fact cue is the correct brain-faithful behavior*. Our learned logistic "gate" is a crude approximation of the brain's continuous down-weighting; its net-zero result is what a well-calibrated soft integrator should produce when the fact cue is low-validity. The net-zero is a feature of correct calibration, not a failure of the gate.

## 2. Do humans ACTUALLY resolve world-knowledge-decisive references reliably, or also default to structure?
- **PINNED** — Winograd schemas are *hand-constructed* to be world-knowledge-decisive and to defeat selectional/statistical shortcuts (Levesque et al. 2012; nyu cs.davise). Even so, the human ceiling is only ~**92%** (Bender 2015, "Establishing a Human Baseline," 407 MTurk speakers; Davis 2016 replication) — NOT ~100%. On specially-engineered decisive items humans still miss ~8%.
- **PINNED** — "Good-enough" / underspecification theory (Ferreira, Bailey & Ferraro 2002; Ferreira & Patson 2007; Christianson 2016; Karimi & Ferreira 2016) shows comprehenders routinely leave anaphora/ambiguity UNDERSPECIFIED and rely on shallow heuristics unless the task forces full resolution — especially under time/resource pressure. Humans frequently *do not* override structure with a single fact; they satisfice.
- **PINNED (corpus convergence)** — Corpus evaluations of centering/pronoun resolution (e.g., Tetreault 2001; the knowledge-aware-coref literature, Zhang et al. ACL 2019 P19-1083) treat world knowledge as a *residual* factor beyond salience/morphosyntax; genuinely world-knowledge-decisive pronouns are a small minority of natural tokens. This matches the measured ~15% sliver and the fact that structure alone reaches 0.805.
- **Implication (SPECULATION, well-supported):** Since humans neither face many such cases nor resolve the constructed ones perfectly, the +0.021 oracle ceiling reflects INTRINSIC scarcity+difficulty, not a mechanism we are missing.

## 3. The reliability signal — does the brain gate on confidence-of-comprehension / metacognition?
- **PINNED** — Routine comprehension monitoring/validation exists (Richter 2015, "validation and comprehension as two sides of a coin"; epistemic-monitoring literature), but it is a *passive, first-order plausibility check* run on the incoming stream, not a discrete confidence score attached to an extracted fact that then gates a downstream resolver.
- **PINNED** — Feeling-of-knowing / confidence judgments (metamemory literature; PMC4539452, PMC12047626) index *memory retrieval* success, not the reliability of a discourse fact for reference; there is no evidence of a metacognitive gate that says "trust this extracted fact for pronoun X."
- **PINNED / mechanistic** — Haviland & Clark (1974) bridging cost and demanded on-line relational checks are an *effort/timing* signature (extra reading time when a bridge must be built), NOT a reliability classifier. Bridging is done backward-looking when coherence breaks, not as a pre-filter that scores facts.
- **Best reading (SPECULATION, supported):** The brain's real "reliability signal" is not a separate metacognitive gate — it is the *graded activation strength of the event/prior representation itself* (McRae, Metusalem). A richly-understood event yields a strong prior that naturally dominates; a weak/ambiguous one contributes little. Confidence is *encoded in the weight*, not gated on top of it. This is exactly the term our discrete fact + hard gate does NOT possess: we have a boolean fact and must bolt on a gate, whereas the brain has a continuous prior whose magnitude IS the confidence.

## 4. Computational-level verdict — is world knowledge a low-validity cue for reference?
- **PINNED** — Competition Model (Bates & MacWhinney 1989): cue validity = availability × reliability. In English, word order / preverbal position dominate; semantic-pragmatic cues have LOW availability (rarely decisive) and moderate reliability, hence LOW validity for argument/referent assignment. A calibrated comprehender *should* weight world knowledge low and let it decide only clear selectional-violation cases (selectional-violation detection is early and automatic — Warren et al.; W18-4603 — but that is a narrow, high-precision trigger, not a general bridge).
- **Implication:** Weighting the fact cue low and letting it decide only clean cases is exactly the measured behavior (best gate ≈ floor; override correct only ~7% when it fires broadly). Our system is behaving as cue-validity theory prescribes.

## 5. BOTTOM LINE — verdict
**(A) INTRINSIC BOUND — with one mechanistic refinement.**
- World knowledge is, at the computational level, a genuinely low-validity, low-frequency cue for reference (Competition Model); the brain integrates it CONTINUOUSLY as a soft prior (Bayesian pronoun models, thematic-fit competition, event-knowledge N400), not through a discrete reliability gate; and even humans default to structure/good-enough and top out at ~92% on *engineered* decisive items. The net-zero observable gate and the +0.021 oracle ceiling are therefore the brain-faithful outcome, not a missing-mechanism failure. The organ's value is in fact-GIVEN settings (QA, where the bridge is supplied and reliable), not fact-DETECTION in the wild.
- **The refinement (do NOT read as (B)):** our architecture is unfaithful in FORM — a boolean self-extracted fact + hard override gate — whereas the brain has no gate; confidence lives in the graded activation/weight of the prior. The brain-faithful lever is therefore NOT "build a better gate" (that ceiling is +0.021 and unreachable from observables) but "make the fact cue a soft, activation-weighted prior fused into the resolver's competition." Our learned logistic gate already approximated that and returned net-zero — which is confirmatory evidence that the ceiling is real, not evidence a stronger gate exists. Recommend ACCEPT THE BOUND for fact-detection-in-the-wild; deploy the organ where facts are given.

### TLDR
The brain does not switch a world-knowledge fact on or off with a reliability gate — it blends it in continuously as a soft "prior," weighted by how strongly the event is understood, and it weights this kind of cue LOW because it is rarely decisive and often wrong. Even people, given puzzles specifically built to need world knowledge, only get ~92% and otherwise lean on grammar and recency. So our finding — no on/off gate beats just trusting sentence structure, and the best-possible gain is tiny — is what a correctly-tuned brain-like reader would do, not a broken part. The payoff for this organ is in tasks where the fact is handed to us (question answering), not in hunting for decisive facts in raw stories.
