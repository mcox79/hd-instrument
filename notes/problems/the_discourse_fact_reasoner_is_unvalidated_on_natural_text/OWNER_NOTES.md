THE PROBLEM. A landed "discourse-fact store + 2-hop bridge" resolves reference by remembering a stated fact about a character and bridging it through world knowledge ("she poured the tea ... the hostess sat" -> she = the hostess). It scored 0.998 on a CONSTRUCTED test — but with the facts handed in clean and the knowledge edges exact. The author's own #1 caveat: real-text accuracy is UNMEASURED. The task: measure it on real narrative where the reader must SELF-EXTRACT the facts and the knowledge base is patchy — beat the fact-blind reader CI-separated with the info-free twin losing, plus the graceful-degradation curve; a rigorous NEGATIVE is a full pass.

RESULT — a complete, TWO-SIDED, brain-grounded answer (all on real LitBank, 100 novels, self-extracted facts, no oracle; witness 11/11).

(1) NEGATIVE, fired blind on competitive coref. Held-out DEV sets the fact-bridge weight to ZERO; forced on it HURTS (0.68–0.78 vs the real fact-blind floor 0.805, CI-separated below). The constructed 0.998 was an idealized-extraction + exact-KG artifact: a controlled degradation curve connects 0.998 -> floor, and at real coverage (fact-decisive references ~15%, KG verb-coverage ~17%) it lands near the floor. The cue carries REAL signal (beats its shuffled twin) but is swamped by salience and too rare.

(2) I BUILT ACROSS the wall I named. The deep bottleneck is entity-side: novels rarely STATE a character's type. So I built the brain's rich-entity model — a character known by what they DO (action history, 93% coverage vs 43% for stated types). It ALSO collapses to the floor (DEV weight 0), and the reason GENERALIZES a prior insight into a law: for the hard cases (salience fails) the right answer is the LEAST-mentioned character, so ANY discourse-content bridge (type OR history) is anti-correlated with it — the brain uses the syntactic binder there, not memory.

(3) POSITIVE — the mechanism's real home, measured HONESTLY against the REAL resolver on the NATURAL candidate set. (I first tried a constructed 2-way with a hardcoded coin-flip floor, caught it as a construction proof, and WITHDREW it.) On the natural fact-present sliver (~15% of refs, gold has a self-extracted bridging fact), fusing the fact into the real resolver LIFTS 0.837 -> 0.961 CI-separated (+0.124), DEV picks weight 2, the info-free twin crashes to 0.16; on the salience-ambiguous third, 0.617 -> 0.914. THE HONEST BOUNDARY: on the complement (85%, no fact) the same cue HURTS blind (0.80 -> 0.52) — which is exactly why the unconditional weight is 0.

(4) THE GATE QUESTION, DRILLED TO GROUND. It's a gated capability, so: can an observable fact-reliability gate deploy it? I built every gate — clean-type, low-margin, salient-bridger, combinations, AND a learned logistic gate on all reliability features (held-out DEV). NONE beats the floor (best +0.001, not separated); the oracle ceiling is only +0.021 and unreachable (a bridge override of the structural pick is correct only ~7% of the time). The research drill (5 converging literatures) gives the DECISIVE verdict: (A) INTRINSIC BOUND, not a missing mechanism. The brain has NO reliability gate — it integrates world knowledge as a continuous graded Bayesian PRIOR (Kehler & Rohde 2013; McRae 1998; Metusalem 2012), so a low-validity cue correctly gets low weight; humans themselves top out ~92% on world-knowledge-decisive Winograd items and satisfice. My own data VETs the calibration: the DEV-tuned weight is 2 where facts are present and 0 across the population — the reader already weights the cue BY ITS RELIABILITY. So the net-zero gate is the CORRECT brain-faithful behavior, and a gate is the WRONG lever.
