## 1. THE OPENING MOVE: how does the brain do THIS?

**It reads the passage once, forward, and what it knows at sentence *k* is what sentences 1..*k*-1 put there.**

| what | the pin |
|---|---|
| a discourse FILE is updated **by the utterance being processed, in sequence**; the context for sentence *k* is the file as it stands after 1..*k*-1 | Heim 1982, file-change semantics (the account pri-104 already cites for the five card symbols) |
| the availability of a discourse referent is its **base-level activation**, a decaying function of the time since it was met -- so there must be a CLOCK, and it must count the passage, not the machinery | Anderson & Schooler 1991 / Anderson 2007 (ACT-R); Lewis & Vasishth 2005 for the sentence-processing case; the substrate's own `hdlab/salience_binder.actr_activation` |
| comprehension is PREDICTIVE and the higher level's belief re-enters the lower level's competition as a prior -- **a prediction is formed BEFORE the input arrives** | Rao & Ballard 1999; Kuperberg & Jaeger 2016; this is exactly the arm pri-104 built |

The third pin is the one the defect destroyed. A next-mention prior is only a prediction if it is computed from the past. **When the register has already been written with the whole document, "this string has been mentioned before" is not a prediction, it is the answer.**

So the brain-foundational form has three parts, and all three are in the diff:

1. **ONE in-order feed.** The reader is the one comprehender that advances the passage: `posterior(words, observe=True)`, at exactly one call site.
2. **Every other call is a READ, answered AS OF its sentence.** Cards carry their filing times; `symbol` counts only mentions filed strictly before sentence *k*; the current sentence's own mentions live in a transient overlay that a read throws away. A consumer that asks late gets exactly the belief the comprehender held at that sentence (witness W1: max |dP| = 0).
3. **No process-global memo of register-dependent output.** The affect path's `@lru_cache` is keyed on the PASSAGE.

## 3. WHAT WAS BUILT

Three changes, one organ, no new organ (`hdlab/lexical_categories.py` + the two call sites in `hdlab/situation_reader.py`).

**(a) The file card carries filing TIMES.** A card is now `{"ev": [(sentence, kind_seen_so_far, def_seen_so_far), ...], "pf": the sentence at which the string was first found to pluralise}`. `symbol` bisects `ev` for the mentions filed strictly before the sentence in flight. The card the old code kept (`n`, `det`, `plural`, `last`) is exactly the `as_of = now` special case, which is why the floor is reproduced to 0.0 (W9).

**(b) `posterior(words, lag=None, observe=False, sent_idx=None)`.** `observe=True` is the feed: it files the sentence's mentions and advances the clock by ONE sentence. The default is a read: `begin` resolves the sentence from the register's own `sent_key` (the lowered token tuple of each fed sentence), the read writes nothing, and `commit` throws the overlay away. Because the default is "read", **every existing caller in the substrate became a read with no edit at its call site** -- and every caller that never opens a passage (`_reg is None`) is byte-identical to before (W5).

**(c) The reader feeds the passage once, at the passage boundary**, and caches the settled belief under its own per-read key, so the consumer that re-asks for a sentence is served that pass instead of re-running forward-backward. **The feed therefore costs no extra forward-backward passes**: it replaces the first consumer's pass.

**(d) The affect memo is keyed on the passage.** `register_generation()` is bumped by `new_document`; `_affect_pos(text)` passes it into the `lru_cache`.

**(e) The offline accrual reads the passage through the SAME API** (`begin` / `note` / `commit`) instead of its own hand-rolled loop -- so the symbol computed while accruing and the symbol computed while reading are now the same function evaluated by the same code. Asserted byte-identical to the incumbent accrual (W10).

**The control kills my first explanation and hands me the real one.** My hypothesis was alphabet sparsity (13 cells instead of 9, the mechanism pri-99 documented for `UNK_JOINT_SHAPE`). `actr2_d05` has the SAME 9 symbols and the SAME 1,337 counts per cell as the floor and is **still CI-separated negative** -- so it is not sparsity, it is **the accumulation term itself**.

*Mechanistically:* summing over every presentation makes a string mentioned many times long ago look currently active. And the dose-response is monotone in the decay: as d rises the sum collapses onto the most recent term, and the loss shrinks to nothing -- unseen -0.00296 (d=0.5) -> -0.00118 (d=1.0), repeat -0.00828 -> -0.00497, neither separated at d=1.0. **The incumbent's one-bit-off-the-last-mention read is the d -> large limit of the ACT-R sum, and on THIS decision the limit is the operating point.**

*Why that is not a failure of ACT-R but a fact about the decision:* the name-vs-common-noun question is whether the referent is CURRENTLY active, not how entrenched the string is. Entrenchment -- how often the word has been met -- is already carried, far better, by the organ's lexical emission counts. The accumulation term therefore **double-counts frequency evidence the organ holds elsewhere**, and pays for it in the one thing it was supposed to add. The ACT-R sum remains the right quantity where entrenchment genuinely matters and no lexical channel carries it, which is retrieval -- and that is exactly where the substrate already uses it (`hdlab/salience_binder.actr_activation`). **Same structure, different decision, different term. It is not in the shipped diff.**

## 7. ALTERNATE PATHS -- other ways to do this read, as or more brain-foundational

1. **One forward sweep, no second pass at all** (MORE brain-foundational than what I shipped). The brain does not read a passage five times; every downstream process runs on the same incremental sweep. *Structure:* the cortical hierarchy running concurrently on one input stream. *Computation:* each level's state at word *t* is a function of the levels' states at *t*-1 and the input at *t*. *What it would take:* restructuring `SituationReader.read` from a sequence of five document-level passes into one per-sentence pipeline. *Why not now:* that is a reader-architecture rewrite, far outside this brief, and it moves every board number at once. My patch reaches the same OBSERVABLE guarantee -- every consumer gets the belief the comprehender held at that sentence (W1, W3) -- by memoising the single in-order sweep, which is a strictly smaller change with the same invariant.
2. **File cards keyed on the REFERENT rather than the surface string.** Heim's card is per discourse referent; pri-104 keyed on the string because the resolver needs a parse and the parse reads these categories. Now that the cards carry TIMES, a principled predict-then-revise second sweep (Kuperberg & Jaeger 2016) becomes expressible: sweep 1 builds the referents, sweep 2 is replayed AS OF each sentence with referent-keyed cards. *What it would take:* a `revise_passage` arm and a re-measure of pri-104's instrument. *Why not now:* it needs the entity resolver in the feed loop, which is a second organ inside the categories rung.
3. **A graded card instead of a 9-cell alphabet.** The top-down prediction is a graded distribution, not a lookup in a discrete table; the BF form is a small count-based log-linear read over (number of mentions, activation, determiner evidence). *Why not now:* it rebuilds the entity asset and it is a change to the prior's FORM, not the register's discipline -- a different problem.
4. **ACT-R base-level activation as the recency term** -- built and measured here; a CI-separated negative, understood (section 4c).
5. **An explicit sentence index at every consumer.** The patch already accepts `sent_idx=`; wiring each consumer to pass its own sentence index removes the read-miss class by construction (measured at **0** today, so it buys robustness rather than accuracy).

## 9. EVALUATION OF THE MOST SUCCESSFUL IMPROVEMENT -- what let the signal be maximised

The owner's standing observation is that the big wins come from cracking the real, mathematical, brain-foundational chain **all the way to the top**. That is what happened here, and the chain is short enough to name rung by rung.

**THE CHAIN, CRACKED:**

| rung | the brain's quantity | what was there | what it is now |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. the passage** | a passage is read ONCE, forward (Heim 1982) | five consumers each re-read it, each writing | ONE in-order feed, one call site |
| **2. the clock** | time since the referent was met, in units of the passage (ACT-R) | a count of calls into the organ, running 4.30x fast | sentences of the passage: 60 for 60 sentences |
| **3. the read** | what the comprehender knew AT THAT POINT | whatever had been written by whoever ran last | as of sentence k, by construction (max \|dP\| = 0) |
| **4. the prediction** | a prediction is formed BEFORE the input arrives (Rao & Ballard; Kuperberg & Jaeger) | **100% of first mentions were told the string comes back** | `e_first` on 0.4402 of prior reads, as in a real forward read |
| **5. the memo** | -- (an engineering artefact with no brain analogue) | a process-global cache of an output that depends on the passage | keyed on the passage |

**Rung 4 is the one that mattered, and it is the top of this chain.** Every rung below it was computing correctly; what was wrong was that the quantity being handed down was not a prediction at all. The moment the feed was made in-order, three things fell out at once and none of them was engineered separately:

- the read became repeatable (12/12), because there is nothing left for call order to change;
- the ACT-R window returned to the value pri-104 swept and shipped (5 sentences instead of an effective ~1.2), because the clock became a clock;
- `ENT_SKIP_FIRST`'s guarantee -- pri-104's published "first-mention accuracy EXACTLY unchanged, 0.8871" -- became true on the live path for the first time, arriving at **0.8871** to the digit without anything being tuned to make it.

That last one is the tell. **A number that another solver measured on a clean in-order harness reappearing, unbidden, to four decimal places on the live path is what it looks like when a chain is cracked to the top:** the live path and the clean harness stopped being two different computations.

**THE RUNGS *NOT* CRACKED, named honestly:**

- **The reader is still a five-pass reader.** I made the five passes *agree*; I did not make them *one pass*. The brain reads once and everything runs on that sweep (section 7, path 1). This is the largest remaining fidelity gap in this rung and it is an architecture change, not a patch.
- **The card is keyed on the surface string, not the referent** (pri-104's honest MODEL label, unchanged here). "Souter" and "the justice" still have separate cards.
- **The prior is a 9-cell lookup, not a graded top-down distribution.**
- **The reader's input is still lowercased** -- worth 0.4076 PROPN F1, forty times anything in this brief, and one edit away (LEAD 2).
- **The board still cannot see any of this**, because three of its loaders never open a passage (LEAD 1).
