## 1. THE PROBLEM IN PLAIN LANGUAGE
When the reader is given plain text with no answer-key markup, it never notices the pronouns. It finds the names and the ordinary nouns itself, but every 'he', 'she', 'they', 'it' was coming from the test file's markup. So on real text it cannot say who did anything when the doer is a pronoun (9 right in 52 on a sample), and it never even asks who a pronoun refers to. Giving it the pronoun positions from its own word-category organ, with no answers, takes it to 42 right in 52. Make it find them itself, every time, and never read the markup while reading.

## 2. WHY THIS ONE
It is the top of the chain (the reader's first act is to discover what it is reading about), it is a gold leak on the inference path, and the measured recovery is the largest of any item on the queue (+33 answers in 52 with our own organ and no new mechanism). The board could not see it because its rows never run the reader (pri 122).
