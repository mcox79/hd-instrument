## 1. THE PROBLEM IN PLAIN LANGUAGE
Our reader now produces, for each grammar call it uses to work out who-did-what or where a thing attaches, a trustworthy "how sure am I" number -- and that number really does tell the confident calls apart from the shaky ones. But right now NOTHING uses it: the number is attached to each answer and then ignored, so the reader still states every answer as fact, shaky ones included. The job is to build the piece that ACTS on that certainty -- a reader that, when a call is below its confidence bar, HOLDS BACK: it either declines to answer or falls back to its safe default guess, instead of asserting a coin-flip. Then measure that the reader really is more reliable when it is allowed to hold back, on modern text, and hand back the evidence that switching this behaviour on is worth its small cost. The payoff: the reader stops being confidently wrong, which is exactly what any later reasoning has to stand on.

## 3. HOW THE BRAIN DOES THIS (the opening move)
PINNED -- PRECISION-WEIGHTING / active inference (Friston 2010; Ernst & Banks 2002 inverse-variance MLE cue combination): a percept's influence on belief is weighted by its inverse-variance PRECISION -- a reliable cue drives belief, an unreliable cue is DOWN-WEIGHTED, never hard-committed. PINNED -- decision confidence GATES commitment (Kepecs et al. 2008 OFC vevaiometric; Kiani & Shadlen 2009 opt-out / decline-when-unsure): the reader commits to confident role assignments and HOLDS BACK on uncertain ones (Hale 2001 / Levy 2008 surprisal = the distribution over parses, not a single tree; Christianson et al. 2001 good-enough processing when confidence is low). The computation to replicate is a DEFER POLICY over the head-driven readout: if the arc's calibrated confidence < tau, DOWN-WEIGHT the pick toward the reader's prior (or ABSTAIN), else COMMIT -- and the risk-coverage curve (selective accuracy on the confident subset) is the measure. Applying confidence to gate the THEMATIC-ROLE readout is an architecture-level extrapolation (OUR-INVENTION-UNDER-TEST, a-priori P~0.30; upheld by the predecessor with the info-free twin flat) -- so the defer POLICY it drives must be measured the same way, with the random-confidence twin LOSING.
