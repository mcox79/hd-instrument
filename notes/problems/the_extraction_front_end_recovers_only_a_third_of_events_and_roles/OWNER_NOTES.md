Research drill (cited): event-hood is argument-structure/lexical-category based and TENSE-AGNOSTIC (neo-Davidsonian; Frankland&Greene 2015; Matchin&Hickok 2020); tense is a separable feature (Ullman 2001; Bastiaanse PADILIH), and the brain's asymmetry runs the OTHER way — PAST is the harder form, present is the default (Bastiaanse 2011). So the detector was BACKWARDS: it gated on the tense the brain finds harder and dropped the brain's default.
