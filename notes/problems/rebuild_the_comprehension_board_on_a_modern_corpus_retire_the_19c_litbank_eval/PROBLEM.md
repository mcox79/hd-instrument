We grade the reader on how well it understands stories, and we report one overall score plus a score for each skill (who a pronoun means, who did what, what happened before what, what caused what, who the main character is, how a character feels, what someone is trying to do). But the texts we grade it on are 100 novels all written before 1923 -- old-fashioned English the owner has ruled is "basically a different language," and the reader is meant to understand MODERN language. So the report card is measured on writing the reader was never meant to handle, which makes the scores mean less than they claim.
