# Research: incremental discourse mechanism for copular predication — subject resolution, salience, canonical-entity binding, integration (2026-09-02)

Deep-drill continuation of `research_copular_is_a_binding_2026-09-02.md` / `IDEAL_copular_is_a_architecture_2026-09-02.md`
(same problem, same day). That prior work named five gaps as OPEN — this drill targets exactly those, via 4 parallel
full-text-first Sonnet lit-scans. Nothing here re-derives what is already PINNED there (Higgins typing, symmetric
CA3 identity, ATL feature-overlap is-a). Calibration penalty applied throughout (deflate 0.15-0.25 from raw-abstract

## HEADLINE

**Two of the five questions come back as genuine, confirmed literature gaps, not under-search — that is itself the
finding.** (1) No study localizes a copula-locked "predication slot" that opens before the complement and gets
filled; the one directly relevant design (subject+copula chunk, then predicate) found the OPPOSITE — no
pre-complement modulation, evidence for shallow predicate-locked processing, not anticipatory copula-locked setup.
(2) No study manipulates discourse-givenness in an English equative/specificational sentence and measures an
online subject-assignment signature — three independent search passes over Birner, Birner & Ward, and Mikkelsen
confirm this is a ~30-year gap in that specific research program. Where there IS real evidence (Q3 salience
formula, Q4 identity-coref binding, Q5 integration), it **complicates rather than confirms** the simplest version
of the design: the literature-favored salience decay is power-law not exponential; canonical-entity binding is
solid for identity copulas but untested for property copulas; "integrated" architectures still show real internal
staging and lingering residue, not flat one-shot overwrite.

## Q1 — Is the predication node set up at the copula, then filled? (anticipatory/predictive evidence)

**OPEN — direct evidence NOT FOUND, and the one on-point study argues against it.**

- **Urbach & Kutas (2010), *J. Memory and Language*, "Quantifiers more or less quantify on-line."** [ABSTRACT]
  Design is the closest match on record to "subject + copula, then predicate": presents "All/No rubies are" as a
  chunk, then the predicate noun, ERP on the predicate. **Quantifier polarity (all/no) — present before the
  copula/predicate gap — had NO effect on the predicate's N400.** N400 tracked plain lexical/associative fit only.
  This is evidence AGAINST a rich predication slot being set up and modulated ahead of the complement; it argues
  for shallow, predicate-locked lexical processing at copular constructions, not anticipatory logical integration.
- **Flick & Pylkkänen (2020), *Cortex* 127:42-57**, "Isolating syntax in natural language: MEG evidence for an
  early contribution of left posterior temporal cortex." [ABSTRACT, cross-confirmed 2 sources] This IS a real,
  named "predication effect" — but it contrasts a **predication vs. modification syntactic frame** for an
  adjective (small-clause predicate vs. relative-clause modifier), not a copular "X is a ___" construction, and
  the effect (~200-230ms, left posterior temporal, not ATL) is measured **post-adjective (post-complement)**, not
  at/before it. It shows the parser distinguishes predication-structure from modification-structure once the
  predicate word has arrived — not that a slot opens at "is" first. **No second Flick & Pylkkänen paper on literal
  copular "is a" timing was found.**
- **Coulson & Van Petten (2002), *Memory & Cognition*.** [ABSTRACT] Graded N400 on the predicate noun of "X are Y"
  sentences, scaling with semantic distance (literal categorical < literal mapping < novel metaphor) — real,
  well-cited evidence of incremental semantic evaluation, but timed to the predicate word itself, not anticipatory.
- **Călinescu, Ramchand & Baggio (2025), *Language, Cognition and Neuroscience* 40(5):657-685.** [ABSTRACT] The
  most recent and most directly-labeled "predication" EEG study — but a DIRECT COMPLICATION: their ERP/alpha-beta
  signal was driven by lexical-semantic properties of the composing words, NOT by the syntactic predication-vs-
  modification distinction itself (their "predication" = noun-after-verb, not copular). Weakens any claim that
  predication has its own clean, content-independent neural signature.
- Bemis & Pylkkänen (2011) LATL composition (~200-250ms post-second-word) is the field's general two-word
  composition benchmark, [ABSTRACT] contested by a 2023 methodological critique re: what it actually indexes.

**Verdict:** treat "the predication node opens at the copula, then gets filled" as an UNTESTED, not merely
under-evidenced, hypothesis — the one experiment shaped like a direct test (Urbach & Kutas) points the other way.
Best-supported honest claim: **copular predicate composition is evaluated incrementally and gradedly once the
predicate word arrives (Coulson & Van Petten-style graded N400 on the predicate), riding on a general ~200-250ms
structural composition signature — with no evidence for rich anticipatory pre-activation keyed to the copula
itself.** P(copula-locked predication-slot mechanism, as literally framed) ≈ 0.20 after deflation.

## Q2 — Does givenness/topicality drive subject assignment online, in equatives specifically?

**OPEN for the narrow/direct claim (confirmed 30-year gap, not under-search). Real but non-transferable adjacent
evidence exists for the broader mechanism class.**

- **Direct equative/specificational subject-choice RT/ET/ERP study: NONE FOUND** across 6+ targeted query
  variants. Mikkelsen (2005) itself is theoretical/acceptability-judgment only [ABSTRACT, skim]. **Birner (1994
  dissertation), Birner & Ward (1996, *Studies in Language*; 1998 book)** are corpus/naturalistic-count studies
  only — 3 independent search passes found zero experimental (RT/rating/ET) follow-up under either author's name.
  This corroborates, rather than merely repeats, the prior drill's "essentially no online literature" flag.
- **Gernsbacher & Hargreaves (1988), *JML* 27:699-717 — Advantage of First Mention.** [ABSTRACT] Real, replicated
  probe-recognition effect (first-mentioned referent accessed faster) — but the authors explicitly report the
  advantage **persists even when the first-mentioned referent is NOT the grammatical subject**, i.e. first-mention
  accessibility is DISSOCIATED from subjecthood in their own data. This complicates, rather than confirms, a clean
  "discourse-old → subject" comprehension story; it's a real memory-accessibility mechanism, but not shown to
  drive subject choice.
- **Bock & Warren (1985), *Cognition* 21:47-67.** [ABSTRACT] The best-supported causal link in EITHER direction —
  but it is a PRODUCTION finding (conceptual accessibility → subject assignment during formulation), not
  comprehension/parsing, and not tested on equatives.
- **Clifton & Frazier (2004), *Memory & Cognition* 32:886-895** ("Should given info come before new? Yes and no")
  + **Brown, Savova & Gibson (2012), *JML* 66(1):194-209.** [ABSTRACT] Given-before-new ordering facilitates
  double-object datives but NOT NP-PP datives — the closest tested analog to a "givenness-order" processing rule
  is explicitly **construction-gated, not a universal monotonic function**. This is the single most important
  calibration signal for Q2: even where an online givenness-ordering effect IS real, it does not port cleanly
  across construction types.
- **Kaiser & Trueswell (2004), *Cognition* 94(2):113-147.** [ABSTRACT] Finnish OVS vs. SVO: comprehenders use
  non-canonical word order to *anticipatorily* predict upcoming-referent discourse-status (new) at the second-noun
  position — genuine online, predictive linkage of syntactic position to discourse status, in a case-marking
  language, not English equatives.
- **Wang & Schumacher (2013), *Frontiers in Psychology* 4:363.** [FULL-TEXT] Japanese wa/ga/o × Given/Inferred/New:
  graded N400 (New > Inferred > Given) at topic position — direct ERP evidence givenness is computed online and
  gradedly — but the authors *deliberately* avoided testing the subject-assignment link (used non-subject
  positions specifically to sidestep the confound).
- **Benatar & Clifton (2013), *JML* 71.** [FULL-TEXT] General given-before-new reading-time advantage (gaze
  duration ~194ms given vs. ~234ms new) — real and robust, but not subject-position-specific.

**Verdict:** the mechanism CLASS (rapid online givenness computation, position-status linkage) is real and
multiply attested; the SPECIFIC claim (givenness monotonically determines equative subject choice, in a way
testable online) is untested territory, and the closest tested analog (dative given-before-new ordering) is
explicitly non-monotonic/construction-gated — a caution against assuming clean transfer. P(a clean monotonic
givenness→subject rule generalizes to equatives) ≈ 0.30 after deflation.

## Q3 — The computable salience formula (Centering / Givenness Hierarchy / ACT-R) + neural grounding

**Best-supported computable form identified; neural grounding is real but narrower than hoped.**

- **Lappin & Leass (1994), *Computational Linguistics* 20(4):535-561 — the RAP algorithm.** [ABSTRACT, 3 converging
  secondary sources incl. standard Jurafsky & Martin reproduction] The only published CARDINAL (weighted-sum)
  salience formula: `Sentence-Recency=100, Subject=80, Existential-construction=70, Direct-Object=50,
  Indirect-Object/Oblique=40, Head-Noun=80, Non-adverbial=50, Role-Parallelism=+35, Cataphora=-175`; decay =
  **halve every sentence boundary** (discrete geometric/exponential, base 1/2, hand-tuned to one corpus).
- **Centering (Grosz/Joshi/Weinstein 1995; Brennan/Friedman/Pollard 1987) stays strictly ORDINAL across its whole
  lineage** — no paper found that converts the Cf role-ranking into a scored/cardinal function (search-based
  absence, not exhaustive enumeration; confidence 0.55). **Strube (1998) "Never Look Back"** [ABSTRACT] directly
  challenges over-weighting role vs. pure recency (a recency-only "S-list" matches/beats BFP on some corpora) —
  a real empirical check on how much grammatical-role weighting actually buys.
- **Givenness Hierarchy computational implementations** (Williams & Scheutz 2018 HRI; Pal et al. 2020 CogSci
  probabilistic "Cognitive Status Filter") [ABSTRACT] stay CATEGORICAL over the six GH statuses, never collapsed
  to one continuous scalar — independently confirms this project's existing caveat that GH encodes MANNER of
  accessibility, not a raw salience degree.
- **Neural grounding — mostly OPEN as discourse-specific claims:** hippocampal novelty/recency (Ranganath & Rainer
  2003; Lisman & Grace 2005), dorsal/ventral attention (Corbetta & Shulman 2002), and LIP priority maps (Bisley &
  Goldberg 2010) are all PINNED as GENERAL mechanisms but **never tested on discourse-entity/linguistic-referent
  salience** — cross-domain analogy only, capped ≤0.25 each.
- **Brilmayer & Schumacher (2021), *Frontiers in Psychology* 12:623648.** [FULL-TEXT] **The strongest direct hit:**
  P300 amplitude scales gradedly across a 4-level referential-prominence hierarchy (pronoun→pronoun > noun→pronoun
  > noun→noun > pronoun→noun) on naturalistic story stimuli, driven by exactly **recency + persistence (frequency
  of later re-mention) + syntactic/thematic role** — the same three inputs already in this project's ACT-R-derived
  `B_i(t)` formula. Corroborated by Burkhardt (2006)'s graded N400 (Novel > Inferable > Given), citation partially
  re-verified. This is genuinely discourse-specific PINNED evidence, not cross-domain analogy.
- **Anderson & Schooler (1991), *Psychological Science* 2:396-408.** [ABSTRACT] Power-law retention/practice
  functions match real environmental recency/frequency statistics across many memory paradigms — independent
  cross-paradigm support for ACT-R's `dt^-d` power-law decay, a materially different (fatter-tailed) family than
  Lappin & Leass's single-corpus-tuned geometric halving.
- **Gordon, Grosz & Gilliom (1993), *Cognitive Science* 17:311-347** (already known on this project): the
  repeated-name penalty gives independent BEHAVIORAL confirmation that subjecthood/Cb-status carries a measurable
  real-time salience premium, matching the Centering/L&L role-rank ordering.

**Synthesized recommendation (novel synthesis, capped P=0.45):** keep the existing form
`B_i(t) = ln(sum_k w_role(k)·Δt_k^-d)`. Populate `w_role(k)` with **normalized Lappin & Leass weights**
(subject=1.00, existential=0.875, direct-object=0.625, indirect-object/oblique=0.50) — the only published cardinal
instantiation of the same role hierarchy Centering assumes ordinally, now independently corroborated by
Brilmayer & Schumacher's graded neural role-weighting. Keep the **power-law decay** (`Δt^-d`), not L&L's geometric
halving — Anderson & Schooler give it cross-paradigm behavioral validation that L&L's single-corpus tuning lacks.
**A concrete, cheap, falsifiable discriminator exists:** power-law vs. exponential decay predict measurably
different long-range reactivation rates for entities mentioned 8+ sentences back — fit both forms to an existing
annotated coreference corpus's accuracy-vs-distance curve (see Cheap Decisive Test below).

## Q4 — Does a copular predicate bind to the same canonical entity node coreference resolves to?

**Identity-copula link: PINNED-adjacent (from the prior drill, reinforced here). Property-copula link: OPEN,
genuinely untested, best support is a weak analogy.**

- **Dijksterhuis et al. (2024), *Science* 385(6716):1478-1484.** [FULL-TEXT via bioRxiv preprint
  10.1101/2024.06.23.600044] Confirmed real: 22 epilepsy patients, hippocampal single-unit recording. Task is
  **pure reference** ("Shrek and Fiona..." then "He ordered a drink") — the Shrek-tuned concept cell reactivates
  when "He" resolves to Shrek, and reactivation strength predicts correct behavioral resolution. **The paper
  contains NO manipulation of property/predicate assertion at all** — Dijksterhuis is on-record (secondary
  interview source) that "how we bind meaning to words" (i.e. property binding) is her *stated next open
  question*, not something this paper answers. So: PINNED that pronoun resolution reactivates the referent's
  concept cell; explicitly SILENT on whether a copular property predicate does the same.
- **Ison, Quian Quiroga & Fried (2015), *Neuron* 87(1):220-230.** [FULL-TEXT] The best available direct
  mechanistic analog, though non-linguistic: hippocampal/MTL concept cells **rapidly (some single-trial) expand
  their tuning** to a newly-associated stimulus after novel person-place pairings. This shows the hippocampal
  concept-cell substrate IS plastically updatable by new associative information fast enough for a sentence-level
  mechanism — raises plausibility that property binding COULD recruit the same machinery, but does not test
  sentence-level predication. P(same-node property binding), capped ≤0.50 and deflated further ~0.15 for the
  analogy gap → **net ≈0.25-0.30.**
- **Entity-file / property-then-pronoun paradigm (does asserting a property speed/slow a LATER pronoun to that
  entity?): NOT FOUND** as a direct study, despite targeted search. Genuine literature gap, matching this
  project's own prior flag exactly.
- **O'Brien, Rizzella, Albrecht & Halleran (1998), *JEP:LMC* 24(5):1200-1210.** [ABSTRACT] Outdated
  protagonist-property information continues to be resonance-reactivated and interferes with later consistent
  text, even after an explicit update — property info IS bound persistently to the character's representation in
  a content-addressable memory, supporting "properties live on the entity representation" generally, but not
  specifically that coref retrieval speed is affected.
- **Du & Zhang (2023), *Brain Sciences* 13(3):387.** [ABSTRACT] Chinese-language ERP: discourse-level
  property/stereotype information about a character reverses N400 effects on a LATER critical word referencing
  that character — the single best (but single-study, un-replicated, non-English) piece of evidence that property
  assertion measurably and immediately modulates processing of a subsequent mention.
- **Ranganath & Ritchey (2012), *Nat. Rev. Neurosci.* 13:713-726**, AT/PM framework (already partially known on
  this project): the anatomical adjacency (perirhinal cortex sits between the ATL semantic hub and hippocampus) is
  real and PINNED; an explicit functional statement bridging "ATL property composition" to "hippocampal
  concept-cell entity node" was NOT found in this session (PDF fetches degraded) — the functional bridge remains
  this project's own extrapolation, only slightly de-risked by A2b + generic perirhinal-familiarity statements.

**Verdict:** claim the identity-copula-to-coref link as PINNED-adjacent (inherits directly from Dijksterhuis 2024's
pure-reference result plus the already-established CA3-symmetric-identity finding). Do NOT claim the same
confidence for property copulas ("she was a doctor" binding to the same node "she" later retrieves) — this is a
real, motivated, but empirically OPEN extrapolation, net P≈0.25-0.30.

## Q5 — Integrated single incremental pass vs. modular staged pipeline

**Middle position, moderately PINNED for early multi-constraint integration; genuine counter-evidence for literal
flat one-shot processing.**

- **MacDonald, Pearlmutter & Seidenberg (1994), *Psychological Review* 101(4):676-703.** [ABSTRACT, well-known
  architecture] Constraint-satisfaction claim: multiple weighted probabilistic constraints (lexical, syntactic,
  thematic, discourse) become available and are integrated via interactive activation AS SOON AS the relevant
  lexical item is processed, contrasted with Frazier's modular garden-path model. Discriminating empirical
  signature: a strong discourse/thematic cue available BEFORE the ambiguous region should ELIMINATE garden-path
  difficulty entirely, not just speed recovery.
- **Empirical support, PINNED, multi-paradigm:** Trueswell/Tanenhaus/Garnsey (1994) and the broader constraint-
  based-lexicalist body of work (verb-specific thematic info affects first-pass RT/eye movements immediately);
  **McRae, Spivey-Knowlton & Tanenhaus (1998), *JML* 38:283-312** (constraint-based model beat 2-stage model
  fitting observed RTs); **Altmann & Steedman (1988), *Cognition* 30(3):191-238** (discourse/referential context
  available before an ambiguous region ELIMINATES garden-path effects — a direct hit on the discriminating
  signature above); **Just & Carpenter (1980), *Psychological Review* 87(4):329-354**, the "immediacy assumption"
  — BUT their own model has **3 ordered sub-stages per word** (encoding → relational assignment → discourse
  integration), so even the canonical "integrated" account has internal seriality WITHIN a word-cycle, not literal
  flat simultaneity.
- **Zwaan & Radvansky (1998), *Psychological Bulletin* 123(2):162-185.** [ABSTRACT, PDF degraded] Situation model
  updates continuously as new information on any of five dimensions arrives (dimensional-shift cost = direct
  evidence for continuous, not batch, updating) — PINNED for general incrementality. No explicit literature claim
  was found that bundles detection+typing+binding+coref+inheritance into ONE update operation at that specific
  granularity — that decomposition is this project's own, not literature vocabulary. Treat as OPEN at that grain.
- **Genuine counter-evidence to flat full integration:**
  - **Sanford & Garrod bonding-then-resolution** (already known on this project): fast morphosyntactic
    "bonding" precedes slower situational "resolution" — a real, if fast/online, temporal staging.
  - **Frazier & Clifton (1996) *Construal*; Frazier & Fodor (1978) "The Sausage Machine," *Cognition*** — the
    classical 2-stage serial-parsing contrast case, confirmed still actively cited/debated, not a strawman.
  - **Christianson, Hollingworth, Halliwell & Ferreira (2001), *Cognitive Psychology* 42(4):368-407.** [ABSTRACT,
    but 5-experiment, well-cited] **The strongest direct counter-evidence found:** after garden-path reanalysis,
    the ORIGINAL wrong thematic-role assignment LINGERS and coexists with the corrected one — real evidence
    against naive full-overwrite integration; a purely flat architecture needs an explicit residue/trace
    mechanism to explain this.

**Verdict:** "multiple constraint types are evaluated within the SAME early time-window, not modular serial
stages blind to each other" is moderately PINNED (~0.65-0.70 confidence, multi-paradigm convergence). The
stronger claim — a literal single flat function computing detection+typing+binding+coref+inheritance atomically
per word, with no internal structure and no lingering residue — is NOT literature-tested at that granularity and
is directly complicated by Christianson et al.'s lingering-misinterpretation effect. Recommend framing as **one
shared incremental update cycle per word, with softly-ordered/mutually-informing sub-computations and an explicit
residue-tolerance mechanism**, not a hard-gated pipeline AND not a literal atomic flat function.

## Cheap decisive test

Two of the five questions (Q1, Q2) resolve to located negatives — there is no existing human data to test a
substrate hypothesis against, which is itself the actionable finding (see Design Consequences). The other two
(Q3, Q4/Q5) yield a concrete, cheap, on-project test:

**Test A (Q3 — decay functional form).** Fit BOTH candidate salience-decay forms — (a) the project's originally
proposed `count + beta·exp(-lambda·dist)` and (b) the ACT-R power-law `ln(sum_k w_role(k)·Δt_k^-d)` with
normalized Lappin & Leass role weights — to the accuracy-vs-mention-distance curve on an existing annotated
coreference benchmark (LitBank / the pronoun-resolution problem's own held-out struct-dominated bucket already on
disk). No new data collection required.

**Test B (Q4 — canonical-entity merge).** Build the already-named NEXT STEP from `SOLVED.md` ("route identity
copulas into coreference," X==Y merge) and measure "what is X" query accuracy for a coref-resolved canonical
entity vs. the current within-clause-only (nsubj-token) holder, on the existing copular gold + coref-resolved
passages.

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS (Test A):** the power-law form is CI-separated better-fitting (lower residual / higher held-out
accuracy) than the exponential form on the same distance-binned population, particularly at long range (8+
sentences) where the two forms diverge most. **HARD-FAIL:** no CI-separated difference between the two forms at
any distance bin — the functional-form choice doesn't matter at this corpus's scale, and the simpler exponential
form should be kept for engineering economy (a legitimate, informative negative, not a failure).

**HARD-PASS (Test B):** canonical-entity "what is X" read-back accuracy (resolved through coref across sentence
boundaries) is CI-separated ABOVE the within-clause-only baseline, with an info-free shuffle twin (coref-merge to
a random co-referring mention) losing CI-separated. **HARD-FAIL:** the coref-merged canonical-entity accuracy is
NOT CI-separated above baseline, OR the shuffle twin does not lose — meaning the merge adds noise rather than
signal at current coref-resolution quality, and the identity/property binding should stay within-clause pending a
coref-quality fix elsewhere.

## DESIGN CONSEQUENCES

**(a) Per-entity salience = `count + beta·exp(-lambda·dist)` for equative holder resolution.**
**COMPLICATES the functional form; CONFIRMS the general shape.** The literature does not support raw mention
`count` weighted only by exponential recency decay. Two specific corrections: (1) **decay form** — the
behaviorally cross-validated family is POWER-LAW (`dt^-d`, Anderson & Schooler 1991, validated across many
memory paradigms), not exponential; Lappin & Leass's exponential/geometric-halving form was hand-tuned to one
coreference corpus, never behaviorally validated at the functional-form level. (2) **the count term should be
grammatical-role-weighted, not raw** — Brilmayer & Schumacher (2021, full-text, naturalistic stimuli) show P300
salience is driven jointly and gradedly by recency + persistence(frequency) + syntactic/thematic role, and
Gordon/Grosz/Gilliom's repeated-name-penalty gives independent behavioral confirmation of role-weighting.
Recommend replacing (a) with the already-partially-designed `B_i(t) = ln(sum_k w_role(k)·Δt_k^-d)`, populated
with normalized Lappin & Leass role weights, and run Test A above as the cheap discriminator before committing.
Separately: Q2's finding (Gernsbacher's first-mention/subjecthood dissociation; Clifton & Frazier's
construction-gating) is a caution that even a well-validated GENERAL salience formula should not be assumed, only
tested, to transfer cleanly to the SPECIFIC equative-holder-choice decision — the two are adjacent, not identical,
problems.

**(b) Resolving the holder to a canonical coref entity.**
**CONFIRMS for identity-type copulas; COMPLICATES (OPEN, not PINNED) for property-type copulas.** Dijksterhuis
et al. (2024, full-text-verified) plus the already-established CA3-symmetric-identity result together make the
identity-copula-to-coref merge a well-motivated, PINNED-adjacent design (do this — it is Test B above and is
already `SOLVED.md`'s own next step). But do NOT claim the same evidentiary status for routing PROPERTY
predicates ("she was a doctor") into the same canonical-entity node a later pronoun retrieves — no direct test of
this exists anywhere in the literature searched; the best support is a non-linguistic plasticity analogy (Ison
et al. 2015, net P≈0.25-0.30) and one un-replicated non-English ERP study (Du & Zhang 2023). Build it as a
parsimony-motivated design choice (one entity representation is simpler than two, and nothing found argues against
it structurally) but flag it explicitly as OUR-INVENTION-under-test, not brain-PINNED, when writing this up —
this is the single biggest overclaim risk in the current design if left unflagged.

**(c) "Word-level incremental prediction is the final remaining divergence from the brain."**
**COMPLICATES this framing — real, but not "clean," and not the only remaining divergence.** Word-by-word
incrementality itself is well-supported (MacDonald 1994; Trueswell/Tanenhaus; Altmann & Steedman; Just &
Carpenter; Zwaan & Radvansky — five converging paradigms). But two findings specifically puncture the "clean, final,
one-shot" framing: (1) **Q1's negative result** — there is no evidence for anticipatory copula-locked predication-
slot pre-activation; the honest brain-faithful target is graded evaluation AT the predicate word, not prediction
BEFORE it, so a design that claims to add "true anticipatory prediction at the copula" would be reaching past what
any study has shown, not closing a documented gap. (2) **Q5's counter-evidence** — even the most integrated
accounts in the literature retain real internal sub-staging (Just & Carpenter's 3 sub-steps per word; Sanford &
Garrod's bonding-then-resolution) and show genuine LINGERING of superseded interpretations (Christianson et al.
2001, 5 experiments) rather than full atomic overwrite. A maximally brain-faithful incremental design should
therefore build in soft internal ordering and residue-tolerance, not aim for a literal zero-latency, no-history,
fully-flat per-word function — claiming that as "the one remaining divergence" understates what a truly
brain-faithful incremental mechanism requires.

## Substrate-product implications

Highest-leverage, lowest-cost next actions: (1) Test A (decay-form fit) is free — reuses existing corpus data,
directly arbitrates a real functional-form question, and either confirms or kills the power-law recommendation

## TLDR (plain language)

We asked whether real brain-science backs up five specific design choices for how the reader should understand
sentences like "Ahab was a captain." Two answers came back as genuine "nobody has ever tested this" gaps, which is
itself useful: no study shows the brain sets up a placeholder the moment it hears "is" and then fills it in later
(the one relevant experiment actually points the other way — understanding happens once the description word
arrives, not before); and no study has ever measured, in real time, whether the more-familiar person or thing
becomes the "subject" of an is-sentence where both sides could be either. Where evidence does exist, it complicates
two design choices for the better: the math for "how salient is this character right now" should decay like a
long-tailed forgetting curve, not a smooth exponential fade, and weight by grammatical importance not just how
often something was mentioned; and linking "she was a doctor" to the same mental file that "she" later points to
is solidly supported for identity statements ("she is his wife") but is an untested guess, not a proven fact, for
plain property statements. On the "does the brain do everything in one integrated pass" question: mostly yes, but
even the brain keeps some real internal staging and lets outdated interpretations linger briefly rather than
instantly overwriting them — so "one clean pass" is the right direction but should not be built as literally flat.
