## 1. THE PROBLEM IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

When we read a story, we keep a running mental map of WHERE each character/object is — who is in the room, who just
left, who is upstairs — and we update it every time someone moves. This "where is everything" track is one of the core
dimensions of the mental model of a situation. Our reader does NOT have it. The situation-model organs
(`situation_model_accumulate`, `situation_reader`, `factorized_entity_store`, `event_bundle`) bind **(entity, role,
event)** — who did what — but nothing tracks **where each entity is over narrative time**. This is not a small omission:
it is a whole PINNED dimension of the human situation model that is simply absent. It already bit us: the theory-of-mind
observation-cue front-end had to build a minimal per-entity presence tracker INLINE as a stopgap (a spaCy-parse proxy
inside `experiments/perceptual_access_ledger.py`) just to answer "was this character present when the thing changed?".
The task: build the missing organ — a per-entity LOCATION REGISTER (where each entity is, as PRESENCE INTERVALS updated
by motion events) — validate it answers "where is entity X at time T?" on real narrative beating the strongest floor
CI-separated (info-free twin losing), and make it SERVE the observation/belief front-end (and "where is X?" reading
questions) so it is wired, not islanded.
