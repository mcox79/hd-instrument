## 1. THE PROBLEM IN PLAIN LANGUAGE
Right now the reader knows a word is a noun or a verb because we trained it on a hand-labeled treebank — which is not how a person learns. A child never sees part-of-speech labels; they figure out categories purely from how words pattern in what they hear and read (words that fill the same slots and predict the same neighbors belong together). This problem builds that: a learner that induces word categories from reading alone, with no labels, reaching accuracy good enough to use, and producing a stable set of categories the rest of the reader can rely on. It is the piece that makes our whole "learn the grammar by reading" front-end genuinely brain-like instead of resting on a supervised crutch.

## 2. WHY THIS ONE — the deep root, under the HARD 100%-BF gate
The supervised tagger is one of the substrate's NOT_BF defects, and it is the ROOT of the reading-learned front-end's brain-foundationality: the arc scorer and the joint decode both key on categories, so a labels-trained category inventory undermines the whole "acquire syntax by reading" thesis. `scale_the_reading_learned_arc_scorer` names this as "THE gating link." It is squarely the project's primary direction — a neuroscientist would ask how the brain ACQUIRES categories, not how to fit labels — and it is genuinely unfiled (no category-induction/SRN folder exists).
