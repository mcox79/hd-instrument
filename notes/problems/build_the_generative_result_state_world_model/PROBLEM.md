## 1. THE PROBLEM IN PLAIN LANGUAGE
We built the reader several ways to reason about a story -- what caused what, what happened before what, how each sentence
connects to the last, and who did what to whom -- and every one of them is almost perfect when we hand it clean facts. But
on real prose they all slump in the same place, and when we drilled WHY, five separate investigations pointed at the SAME
missing piece. A good reader does not just LOOK UP whether an action serves a goal or causes an outcome; they IMAGINE it --
they run the situation forward in their head ("she picked up the umbrella... so now she is holding it, and she wanted to
stay dry, so that fits") and check whether the imagined result matches the goal or what they were expecting. Our reader
cannot imagine forward like that. It can only search a fixed list of stored links, and that list never covers the specific,
contextual thing a story actually says. Worse, our reader reads in one direction only: it decides who-did-what and what
happened, and never lets its expectation of what SHOULD happen next reach back and help it read. This job is to build the
one missing part -- a little "simulate the situation forward and check the result" engine, made of parts we already have --
and prove that it turns one of those near-misses into a real win on modern text, with a scrambled-facts version falling
apart so we know the imagined structure is doing the work. No outside AI does the imagining.

## 3. HOW THE BRAIN DOES THIS (frame -- PINNED vs OUR-INVENTION)
- **PINNED (the computation).** Comprehension is PREDICTIVE, not read-then-infer: the cortex predicts its next input
  top-down and propagates the ERROR (Rao & Ballard 1999; Friston 2010; the N400 as forward semantic-prediction-error,
  Rabovsky 2018). A reader represents a narrative as a CAUSAL NETWORK and the meaning of an action is its RESULT-STATE
  (Schank & Abelson 1977 scripts/RESULT; Trabasso & van den Broek 1985). Whether an action serves a goal is judged by
  INVERSE PLANNING -- SIMULATE the action and check whether its predicted outcome achieves the goal-STATE (Baker, Saxe &
  Tenenbaum 2009; Csibra & Gergely teleology). The forward simulation runs over intuitive-theory engines with effectively
  infinite coverage because the link is COMPUTED, not stored: intuitive PHYSICS / ToBY (Battaglia & Tenenbaum; Gerstenberg
  CSM), intuitive PSYCHOLOGY / ToMM (goal + belief + affect), over a situation model indexed on Zwaan & Radvansky's five
  dimensions (time, space, causation, protagonist, intentionality). The load-bearing property: the brain GENERATES the
  unstated result-state and uses it BOTH to fill the causal/goal link AND as the top-down expectation that constrains the
  next parse/extraction. This is exactly what every retrieval route (CSKG, event-type gate, topical relatedness) is NOT.
- **OUR-INVENTION-UNDER-TEST (sweep, don't adopt).** The state representation (which predicates a "result-state" carries --
  force-dynamic outcome, mental-state change, affect valence, possession/location); the rollout depth; how the goal-state /
  forward-expectation CHECK is scored (match / cosine / graded-necessity); the confidence gate that decides when the
  generated expectation overrides the feed-forward pick; how the rollout binds to the RESOLVED participants (the meaning
  channel); and every substrate operating-point knob. SWEEP these, adopt no number.
- **NOT brain-faithful (do NOT do).** An external LLM / learned seq2seq at inference (THE invariant); a RETRIEVAL means-end
  (CSKG/KG/fact-store) -- coverage-bounded by construction from THREE measured angles, the very wall this replaces; a
  CLASS-LEVEL-only simulator that composes force-sets + appraisal without binding to the actual participants (the causal
  U8's modest +0.030 is because it stops there); sentence-internal top-down cues (givenness / Centering / word-order) as
  the parser's third cue (built, near-zero validity, inert -- the greedy-arc SOLVED); scoring a subset/class-level win as
  the full capability; Story Cloze as causal-chain gold (affect-dominated); a 19c corpus as load-bearing gold (BANNED
