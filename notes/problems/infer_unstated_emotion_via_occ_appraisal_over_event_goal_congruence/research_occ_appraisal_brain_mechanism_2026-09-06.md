# Research verification — OCC appraisal + the two upstream generalizations (2026-09-06)

Independent literature verification (4 parallel lit-scans) of the brain-foundational basis for this problem's
mechanism. Verdict: **both upstream generalizations check out against the primary literature; corrections are
citation-precision only, no refutation.** Calibration P(faithful as stated, after corrections) ~0.65.

## G1 — Goal FAILURE / thwart status (the upstream `track_status_thwart` generalization)
- **Failed > completed > neutral accessibility is empirically real.** Probe-recognition RTs: failed-goal 1994ms <
  completed 2136ms < neutral 2387ms.
- Tightest primary sources for **failed > completed**: Dopkins, Klin & Myers 1993 (JEP:LMC 19(1):70); Huitema,
  Dopkins, Klin & Myers 1993 (JEP:LMC 19(5):1053 — readers slowed on actions inconsistent with a still-unresolved
  earlier goal, across intervening text). Lutz & Radvansky 1997 (JML 36:293) — its OWN contribution is
  **completed > neutral** (completed goals don't fully decay); it cites the failed>completed half from the 1993
  papers. Suh & Trabasso 1993 (JML 32:279) — superordinate-goal reinstatement (supporting, broader).
  Caveat: Long & Golding 1993 questions whether reinstatement is fully automatic at normal reading rates.
- **Goal-outcome status is a STRUCTURAL PRIMITIVE, not decoration.** Zwaan & Radvansky 1998 (Psych Bulletin
  123:162): the motivational+causal dimensions are the "backbone" of the situation model; "failed goal information
  is more available than completed" (p.173). Trabasso & van den Broek 1985 (JML 24:612): episode skeleton =
  Setting -> Initiating Event -> Response -> **Goal -> Attempt -> OUTCOME**; goal failure is the generative mechanism
  for subgoals; causal-chain membership explains 32-65% of recall/importance variance.
- **Implication:** the goal register's `track_status` implementing failure only on a NEGATED goal clause (not a
  thwarting event) is an INCOMPLETE realization of a pinned, backbone primitive -> the generalization is warranted.

## G2 — OCC prospect-based emotions (the `detect_prospect_sign` confirm/disconfirm generalization)
- **Mapping verified WORD-FOR-WORD** against OCC 1988 Table 1 (via Steunebrink, Dastani & Meyer 2009 reproduction;
  cross-checked Gratch & Marsella 2004 EMA):
  hope = pleased about the PROSPECT of a desirable event; fear = displeased about the prospect of an undesirable
  event; **satisfaction** = pleased about the CONFIRMATION of a desirable prospect; **fears-confirmed** = displeased
  about the CONFIRMATION of an undesirable prospect; **relief** = pleased about the DISCONFIRMATION of an
  undesirable prospect; **disappointment** = displeased about the DISCONFIRMATION of a desirable prospect. The branch
  is literally labelled "PROSPECT-BASED", sibling to a prospect-irrelevant "WELL-BEING" branch (plain joy/distress).
- **Goal-congruence-first appraisal is mainstream** (not idiosyncratic to OCC): Lazarus 1991 (primary appraisal =
  goal relevance + goal congruence/incongruence; INDEPENDENTLY defines relief as "a goal-incongruent condition that
  changes for the better"); Scherer CPM (goal relevance -> goal conduciveness = checks 3-4 of ~7); Frijda 1986
  (concerns + law of situational meaning). Barrett constructed-emotion: core affect (valence) + conceptualization
  into "goal-based categories"; best experimental cite = Lindquist & Barrett 2008 (Psych Science 19(9):898,
  "Constructing Emotion: The Experience of Fear as a Conceptual Act" — situational context causally necessary).
  [Note: the TiCS "functional architecture" paper is Lindquist & Barrett **2012** 16(11):533, a DIFFERENT paper.]
- **Automaticity nuance (Gygax et al. 2003/2004):** online reading recovers general VALENCE automatically, but the
  FINE discrete label (relief vs satisfaction) is more effortful/resource-dependent. This FAVORS computing the fine
  OCC type as deliberate glass-box composition (my two-level valence-then-type scoring reflects the coarse-fast /
  fine-deliberate split). Do NOT claim the exact online label is effortless.

## Against ontological separateness (honest caution, folded into SOLVED §8)
Dimensional/constructionist theories (Russell 1980; Barrett) predict relief and joy collapse onto the SAME core-
affect region, differentiated only by prior-expectation HISTORY, not a separate affect program. Compatible with
this architecture (we do the situational conceptualization) but: say "same core positive affect, differently
LABELLED by prior-expectation history," not "phenomenologically distinct." Unverified pointer for a future drill:
Mellers et al. 1997 decision-affect theory (whether prospect emotions have a BEHAVIORAL, not just narrative-label,
signature).
