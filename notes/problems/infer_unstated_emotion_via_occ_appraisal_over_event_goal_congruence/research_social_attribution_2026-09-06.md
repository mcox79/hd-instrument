# Research: the OCC ATTRIBUTION (gratitude/anger/pride/shame) extraction wall (2026-09-06)

Drilled after the social prototype located a NEGATIVE: the socialize RULE is exact + agency detection works, but
gratitude/anger extraction fails. Three mechanisms, primary sources, buildability verdict. Two BUILT (§9a.2).

## 1. Beneficiary-based goal satisfaction (gratitude) -- BUILDABLE (separable structural layer, no norm channel)
PINNED: naive-utility-calculus splits cost (to the ACTOR) from reward (to the GOAL-HOLDER) as architecturally
separate terms -- Jara-Ettinger, Gweon, Schulz & Tenenbaum, TiCS 20(8):589 (2016); Jara-Ettinger, Schulz &
Tenenbaum, Cognitive Psychology 123:101334 (2020). The "did B's action help A" inference = inverse planning over a
multi-agent MDP: Ullman, Baker, Macindoe, Evans, Goodman & Tenenbaum, "Help or Hinder," NeurIPS 22:1874 (2009).
Pre-linguistic primitive: Hamlin, Wynn & Bloom, Nature 450:557 (2007) (6-10mo evaluate helpers/hinderers);
Warneken & Tomasello, Science 311:1301 (2006) (14mo helping). Role structure: FrameNet Assistance
(Helper/Benefited_party). Dowty 1991 has NO beneficiary role (strict Agent/Patient); Primus 1999 adds Proto-
Recipient. Bratman shared-agency (1992/2014) does NOT apply (passive, unaware beneficiary != joint agency).
BUILD: a goal is SATISFIED when another agent does the goal HEAD (or its converse) FOR the goal-holder (benefactive
PP / recipient binding). No praiseworthiness needed -- that composes later (#3).

## 2. Implicit goal / investment / possession (anger antecedent) -- BUILDABLE (second feeder, no norm channel base)
PINNED (cognitive): Trabasso & van den Broek, JML 24:612 (1985) + Trabasso/van den Broek/Suh, Discourse Processes
12:1 (1989) -- posit an unstated Goal node by counterfactual necessity; Graesser, Singer & Trabasso, Psych Review
101:371 (1994) -- readers generate a why-question and answer via superordinate-goal inference. Effort->goal-value:
Baker, Saxe & Tenenbaum, Cognition 113:329 (2009); Liu, Ullman, Spelke & Tenenbaum, Science 358:1038 (2017)
(10mo infer higher goal-value from higher-cost actions). Possession: Friedman & Neary, Cognition 107:829 (2008);
Friedman, PB&R 15:290 (2008) (first-possession default); psychological ownership: Pierce et al. 2003; Brown et al.
2005. Appraisal side = agency+controllability ONLY: Roseman, Cog&Emo 5:161 (1991); Smith & Ellsworth, JPSP 48:813
(1985) (other-responsibility/control separates anger, NO independent norm term); Weiner, Psych Review 92:548 (1985);
Averill, Am Psychologist 38:1145 (1983) (anger ~ perceived avoidable wrongdoing). BUILD: instantiate
(agent, MAINTAIN/OBTAIN(object), strength=f(effort)) from effort/possession markers into the same goal store; a
later same-theme event by a DIFFERENT actor changing its state -> thwart -> anger. Justification-defeat -> Phase-1.

## 3. Blame/praise (deservingness) -- SPLITS
Decision-tree models: Shaver 1985 (causality->foreseeability->intent->coercion->justification); Malle, Guglielmo &
Monroe, "A Theory of Blame," Psych Inquiry 25:147 (2014). TWO outputs, not one: Cushman, Cognition 108:353 (2008)
(wrongness tracks intent; punishment tracks intent AND outcome). Bias: Alicke culpable control (1992/2000). Neural:
rTPJ intent (Young et al., PNAS 104:8235, 2007; TMS PNAS 107:6753, 2010); vmPFC value integration (Young et al.,
Neuron 65:845, 2010). VERDICT: Tier-1 (agent-causation + volitionality) ships now (Smith & Ellsworth: most of the
anger/gratitude signal) = the CONTROLLABILITY guard (built). Tier-2 (justification, coercion) needs a legitimacy
standard = Phase-1. PRIDE/SHAME cannot use Tier-1 (agent-type constant=self -> the discriminating dimension is
degenerate); differentiation is ENTIRELY a norm/standard judgement -> Phase-1. Omitting pride/shame was correct.

## Bottom line
#1 and #2 are separable structural layers, BUILT (§9a.2): the social subset rose 0/12 -> 3/12 (clean cases), rule +
agency exact, twin loses; residual = predicament-implicit-goal inference (Graesser) + theme coreference (Phase-1-
adjacent). #3 Tier-1 built (controllability); Tier-2 + pride/shame correctly deferred to the norm channel (Phase-1).
