## 1. THE PROBLEM IN PLAIN LANGUAGE
When our reader works out who did what, where a thing is, or what a sentence attaches to, it commits to one answer and states it as fact -- even when the underlying grammar call was a coin-flip. A good reader does the opposite: it leans on the calls it is sure about and holds back on the shaky ones, so it is rarely confidently wrong. Our grammar engine already produces a "how sure am I" number for each of its decisions, but nothing downstream looks at it -- it is thrown away. The job is to pass that certainty through to the parts that read off who-did-what and where, so each of them trusts its confident answers and defers (falls back, or abstains) on the uncertain ones. The payoff is not a smarter grammar engine -- it is a reader that knows what it does not know, which is exactly what any later reasoning has to stand on.

## 3. HOW THE BRAIN DOES THIS (the opening move)
PINNED -- PRECISION-WEIGHTING / active inference (Friston): the brain treats each estimate as a distribution and weights it by its inverse-variance PRECISION -- a reliable cue drives belief, an unreliable one is down-weighted, never hard-committed. PINNED -- graded lexicalist constraint satisfaction (MacDonald 1994; Bates & MacWhinney cue validity): attachment/role is a competition among cues weighted by their VALIDITY, and confidence is the competition's margin (Hale 2001 / Levy 2008 surprisal = the distribution over parses, not a single tree). The reader's arc-eager parser is a GREEDY hard-commitment decoder with an UNCALIBRATED score -- that is the exact fidelity gap. The mechanism to replicate: expose the parser's per-arc reliability as a CALIBRATED confidence (a logistic over the arc margin / competition, cue-validity style -- the parser submission prototyped this: calibration AUC 0.768 vs the greedy 0.645; the arc-FACTORED global parser's margin is even better, AUC ~0.81 for object attachment), then have each HEAD-DRIVEN reader (who-did-what, obl, space) PRECISION-WEIGHT its readout -- trust the confident arcs, and on low-confidence arcs down-weight or fall back to the reader's own prior / abstain rather than assert.
