## 1. THE DEFECT, AND HOW THE BRAIN DOES THIS

**The opening move.** A case-marked phrase licensed by a PREDICATE is an oblique **participant of an event**; one
licensed by a NOMINAL is a **property of a thing**. Those are two different objects in a situation model — the first
fills a slot in an event representation, the second modifies an entity — and the organ that names roles had **no name
for the second**. `ROLE_CLASSES` was `[SUBJ, OBJ, PASS_SUBJ, BY_AGENT, OBL, OTHER, IOBJ]`; `ROLE_TO_DEP` could emit
`nsubj / nsubj:pass / obj / iobj / obl / obl:agent / dep`. Reproduced first-hand: **gold `nmod` 0 of 489 correct even
given the gold tree** (`nmod → obl` 266, `nmod → dep` 222, `nmod → iobj` 1).

**The structure and the computation are unchanged.** Still the Competition Model (Bates & MacWhinney 1982/1989;
MacWhinney 1987): parallel cue competition, each cue carrying a learned VALIDITY (availability × reliability),
additive activation → softmax = the posterior, strengths a pure function of accrued counts. What changed is (a) the
CLASS SPACE the cues compete over, (b) four things the cues **read**, and (c) one thing about **how one cue is read**.

## 4. WHAT THE BRAIN DOES MATHEMATICALLY ALONG THIS CHAIN, AND WHAT EACH STEP BOUGHT

1. **Case marking is a top Competition-Model cue, and the marker FORM is the value** (Bates & MacWhinney 1989;
   MacWhinney 1987). pri 103 lexicalised the preposition. English's *other* case marker — the genitive, the only one it
   puts to the nominal's **right** — was invisible. Two new values of the existing `case` cue: `gen_clitic`,
   `gen_pron`. **nmod:poss 0.000 → 0.928 under gold heads (125 tokens).**
2. **The licensor is visible WITHOUT the arc.** Late Closure / Recency (Frazier 1979, PINNED) makes the nearest
   preceding lexical head the default licensor; a genitive-marked nominal's licensor is the *following* head (English
   genitives are left-branching), and a genitive to the left is skipped because it stands in determiner position (the
   DP-head rule the organ already applies in `is_arg_head`). Alone on the obl/nmod population the cue is **0.707**
   against the arc-bound gold-head rule's 0.9796 — high availability, moderate reliability, which is what a
   Competition-Model cue *is*. **It is what turns the class's obl regression into a gain: −0.0203 CI-sep → +0.0271 CI-sep.**
3. **A cue is read only where there is something for it to interpret.** Fired on every nominal, the licensor cue costs
   gold-heads CORE accuracy **−0.0185 CI-sep**; restricted to nominals that actually carry a case marker it is
   positive. A bare preverbal nominal is filling a slot, not asking who licenses it.
4. **A cue whose job is to survive a wrong arc must not be read inside the arc's configuration.** Every other cue is a
   contrast `log P(r|cfg,v) − log P(r|cfg)`, which is the right form for a cue that refines a configuration. The
   licensor cue is an *independent estimate of the configuration itself*, so it is read unconditionally,
   `log P(r|v) − log P(r)`. **gold nmod 0.7157 → 0.7423; gold core +0.0084 n.s. → +0.0135 CI-separated UP; and it is
   what recovers the causation affector.**
5. **A relative pronoun is the filler of a gap, not a case-marked phrase.** pri 103 stopped the surface case scan *at*
   a relativizer; it never stopped it *for* one, so "an area THAT will need 15,000" read `to` — which marks the
   antecedent two nouns to the left — as `that`'s own case marker. **6 relative-clause subjects recovered.**
6. **A phrasal verb is a stored lexical item** (MacWhinney's item-based constructions; pri 103's own queued alternate
   path). The lexical `prep` cue cannot tell the particle of "worked OUT a deal" from the case marker of "walked OUT
   of the room"; the cue VALUE is the verb-lemma + particle **pair**. **8 objects recovered; core +0.0169 on gold.**

## 6. EVERY NEGATIVE, UNDERSTOOD, WITH ITS MECHANISM AND ITS NUMBER

1. **Learning over the governor's head POSTERIOR instead of its MAP head — EXACTLY NULL.** Accruing each decision to
   every candidate configuration weighted by `P(head|dep)` (the E-step form: expected sufficient statistics under the
   latent-cause posterior) changed nothing: gold all-expressible 0.8394 vs 0.8394; live 0.6941 vs 0.6937.
   *Mechanism, measured:* **there is no distribution to learn over.** Mean `P(MAP head)` = **0.9884**, 96.6% of tokens
   above 0.9, mean candidates above 0.05 = **1.044**. The governor is *confidently wrong*, not uncertain — which
   sharpens pri 103's calibration finding (AUC 0.678, 74% correct at P ≥ 0.95) into a statement about the SHAPE of the
   posterior, not only its centring.
2. **A cue-convergence GATE on learning (teach only where the arc and the arc-free licensor agree about
   predicate-vs-nominal host) — NEGATIVE.** gold obl 0.8217 → 0.7585, gold all-expressible 0.8394 → 0.8171.
   *Mechanism, measured:* it discards **16,923 of 74,984 decisions (22.6%)**, and those are precisely the cases where
   the two cues CONFLICT. The Competition Model's whole content is the *relative* validity of conflicting cues.
3. **The same idea as a WEIGHT rather than a gate (β swept 0.75 / 0.5 / 0.25) — MONOTONICALLY NEGATIVE**, which closes
   the family: gold all-expressible 0.8509 / 0.8423 / 0.8399 against 0.8528 at β = 1. Softening the gate does not
   rescue it; the conflicted experiences carry signal in proportion to how much they are trusted.
4. **The PROSODIC BREAK (comma / dash / paren) as a cue value — NEGATIVE.** gold core **−0.0169 CI-sep** with gold nmod
   flat (354 → 353 correct). *Mechanism:* an intonation-phrase boundary separates appositives, conjuncts and list
   items (49 + 116 + 108 tokens here, all OTHER) just as often as it separates an `nmod:desc`, so the contrast is
   ~flat for NMOD inside the configuration while it shifts the core classes.
5. **The FIRST-NOUN (clause-initial) cue, arc-free — NEGATIVE.** gold core **−0.0337 CI-sep**, live core −0.0194
   CI-sep, gold all-expressible 0.8528 → 0.8271. *Mechanism, measured:* availability of `initial` among gold preverbal

## 15. WHY GATING OR WEIGHTING CONFLICTS DESTROYS THE EVIDENCE — with counts, and with the literature

**The Competition Model's own account.** Cue strength is learned from cue validity = availability × reliability
(Bates & MacWhinney 1989), but the model's staged prediction is explicit: learning is controlled first by
**availability**, then by overall **validity**, and **the ultimate phase is dominated by CONFLICT VALIDITY — the
reliability of a cue in exactly the less-frequent situations where cues COMPETE**. A gate that discards conflicted
experiences therefore removes the evidence that drives the *final and most discriminative* phase of cue-strength
learning. That is not an analogy; it is the mechanism the model names.

**The counts, measured.** The convergence gate keeps 58,061 of 74,984 weighted decisions and discards 16,923 (22.6%),
and it discards them **asymmetrically across the two classes in contention**:

| class | kept | discarded | share discarded |
|---|---|---|---|
| OBL | 6,712 | 2,743 | **0.2901** |
| PASS_SUBJ | 819 | 362 | 0.3065 |
| SUBJ | 11,906 | 4,274 | 0.2642 |
| OTHER | 19,427 | 6,154 | 0.2406 |
| OBJ | 8,131 | 1,727 | 0.1752 |
| **NMOD** | 10,246 | 1,524 | **0.1295** |
| IOBJ | 581 | 67 | 0.1034 |

It strips **29.0% of the OBL evidence and only 13.0% of the NMOD evidence** — i.e. it removes more than twice the
share of exactly the class the licensor cue is supposed to discriminate *against*. Hence the measured result: gold obl
0.8217 → 0.7585. Softening the gate into a **weight** (β = 0.75 / 0.5 / 0.25) is monotonically negative for the same
reason (gold all-expressible 0.8509 / 0.8423 / 0.8399 against 0.8528 at β = 1): trusting a conflicted experience less
*is* discarding part of it.

## 16. THE TWO NEW SOURCES OF GRADED LEARNING EVIDENCE THE SUPERVISOR ASKED FOR — built, measured, understood

### 16a. Sentence-level posterior ENTROPY instead of per-arc confidence — a PRIOR SHIFT, not a competence gain

Built: `w *= (1 − H)^γ` where H is the sentence's mean normalised head-posterior entropy (mean H = 0.3494). Measured,
gold heads: gold nmod **0.7423 → 0.7485 (γ=1) → 0.7546 (γ=2)** — it *does* move nmod up. But gold obl 0.8352 → 0.8239
→ 0.8217 and core 0.9207 → 0.9174 → 0.9157, and all-expressible 0.8604 → 0.8576.

*Mechanism, measured, not guessed.* The sentence-level weight is not neutral across classes:
`corr(sentence entropy, OBL share) = +0.1504`, `corr(…, SUBJ share) = +0.1699`, `corr(…, NMOD share) = +0.0328`.
Under γ=1 the **share of each class's total evidence that survives** is OBL 0.5716, SUBJ 0.5888, **NMOD 0.6340**.
So it strips 43% of the OBL evidence and 41% of the SUBJ evidence but only 37% of the NMOD evidence — **the same
asymmetric stripping as the convergence gate, by a different route.** The nmod gain is a shifted prior, not better
discrimination, which is why obl and core pay for it. Not shipped.

### 16b. The RUNNER-UP host's class, taken UNTRIMMED — informative per token, but too small a population

The round-1 posterior-accrual test used the cache trimmed at P ≥ 0.02, which would have made this a test of a weak
implementation rather than of the idea. Rebuilt with the **untrimmed top-3** and a renormalised split
`q = p2/(p1+p2)`. Among tokens that have a runner-up at all (n = 3,556 over 4,000 sentences): **mean q = 0.1625,
median 0.1134, 52.4% have q ≥ 0.1, 29.3% have q ≥ 0.25, and 43.3% of runner-ups are in a DIFFERENT host class
(predicate vs nominal).** So the runner-up IS informative per token.

Measured effect of teaching both configurations in proportion: gold nmod 0.7423 → 0.7423, all-expressible 0.8604 →
0.8599, live all-expressible 0.7041 → 0.7027. **Null.**

*Mechanism:* the informative population is tiny. Only ~6% of tokens have a second candidate at all (mean candidates
above 0.05 = 1.044, mean P(MAP) = 0.9884), and within those the runner-up carries 16% of the mass — so the accrual
re-allocated is on the order of **1% of the total evidence**. The idea is right and the arm's posterior is simply not
where the information is. This is the *fair-test* version of the round-1 negative and it reaches the same place by a
better road.

## 18. THE PREDICATE-PROXIMITY CUE — the one phase-7 BUILD, and why the literature found it and I had not

Reading the Late Closure literature to check my attribution turned up the thing I had missed: the parsing literature
does **not** treat Recency as the account. It pairs **Late Closure / Recency / Right Association** (Frazier & Fodor
1978; Kimball 1973; Gibson et al. 1996) with a *competing* principle, **Predicate Proximity** (Gibson,
Pearlmutter, Canseco-Gonzalez & Hickok 1996): *an attachment is preferred as structurally close to the head of a
PREDICATE phrase as possible*, and the balance between the two is language-modulated rather than absolute. My
licensor cue implemented **half** of that account.

Built as a second arc-free cue value — how far back the nearest preceding predicate head is, bucketed (1 / 2 / 3-4 /
5-8 / far) — available in the same situations as the licensor (i.e. wherever there is a case marker to interpret),
competing with it rather than overriding it. Measured:
