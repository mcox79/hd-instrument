## 1. THE DEFECT, AND HOW THE BRAIN DOES THIS

**The opening move.** A case-marked phrase licensed by a PREDICATE is an oblique **participant of an event**; one
licensed by a NOMINAL is a **property of a thing**. Those are two different objects in the situation model — the first
fills a slot in an event representation, the second modifies an entity — and the organ that names roles had **no name
for the second**. `ROLE_CLASSES` was `[SUBJ, OBJ, PASS_SUBJ, BY_AGENT, OBL, OTHER, IOBJ]` and `ROLE_TO_DEP` could emit
`nsubj / nsubj:pass / obj / iobj / obl / obl:agent / dep`. Reproduced first-hand: **gold `nmod` 0 of 489 correct even
given the gold tree.**

**The structure and the computation are unchanged.** This is still the Competition Model (Bates & MacWhinney
1982/1989; MacWhinney 1987): parallel cue competition, each cue carrying a learned VALIDITY (availability ×
reliability) read *within* its configuration, additive activation → softmax = the posterior, strengths a pure function
of accrued counts. What changed is (a) the CLASS SPACE the cues compete over and (b) four things the cues **read**.

**Why the class is nearly free.** The obl/nmod distinction is a pure function of the licensing host's category, and the
organ already computes the head's category as its `config` cue. No new machinery — a missing symbol, as pri 94 said.

## 4. WHAT THE BRAIN DOES MATHEMATICALLY ALONG THIS CHAIN, AND WHAT IT BOUGHT

1. **Case marking is a top Competition-Model cue, and the marker FORM is the value** (Bates & MacWhinney 1989;
   MacWhinney 1987). pri 103 lexicalised the preposition. English's *other* case marker — the genitive, the only one it
   puts to the nominal's **right** — was invisible. Two new values of the existing `case` cue: `gen_clitic`,
   `gen_pron`. **nmod:poss 0.000 → 0.928 under gold heads (125 tokens).**
2. **The licensor is visible WITHOUT the arc.** Late Closure / Recency (Frazier 1979, PINNED) says the default
   licensor of a phrase is the nearest preceding lexical head; a genitive-marked nominal's licensor is the following
   head (English genitives are left-branching), and a genitive to the LEFT must be skipped because a genitive stands
   in determiner position (the DP-head rule the organ already applies in `is_arg_head`). Measured alone on the
   obl/nmod population the cue is **0.707** against the arc-bound gold-head rule's 0.9796 — high availability,
   moderate reliability, exactly what a Competition-Model cue is, so it is added to the competition and never used as
   a rule. **This is what turns the class's obl regression into a gain: gold obl −0.0203 CI-sep → +0.0316 CI-sep.**
3. **A cue is read only where there is something for it to interpret.** Fired on every nominal the licensor cue costs
   gold-heads CORE accuracy **−0.0185 CI-sep**; restricted to nominals that actually carry a case marker it is
   **+0.0084 n.s.** and gold obl goes *up*. A bare preverbal nominal is filling a slot, not asking who licenses it.
4. **A relative pronoun is the filler of a gap, not a case-marked phrase.** pri 103 stopped the surface case scan *at*
   a relativizer; it never stopped it *for* one, so "an area THAT will need 15,000" read `to` — which marks the
   antecedent two nouns to the left — as `that`'s own case marker. Only a pied-piped adjacent preposition can mark a
   wh-word. **6 relative-clause subjects recovered.**
5. **A phrasal verb is a stored lexical item** (MacWhinney's item-based constructions; pri 103's own queued alternate
   path). The lexical `prep` cue cannot tell the particle of "worked OUT a deal" from the case marker of "walked OUT
   of the room". The cue VALUE is the verb-lemma + particle **pair**. **8 objects recovered; core +0.0169 on gold.**

## 6. EVERY NEGATIVE, UNDERSTOOD, WITH ITS MECHANISM AND ITS NUMBER

1. **Learning over the governor's head POSTERIOR instead of its MAP head — EXACTLY NULL.** Accruing each decision to
   every candidate configuration weighted by P(head | dep) (the E-step form; expected sufficient statistics under the
   latent-cause posterior) changed nothing: gold all-expressible 0.8394 vs 0.8394, live 0.6941 vs 0.6937.
   *Mechanism, measured:* **there is no distribution to learn over.** The attachment arm's marginals have mean
   P(MAP head) = **0.9884**, 96.6% of tokens are above 0.9, and the mean number of candidates above 0.05 is **1.044**.
   The governor is *confidently wrong*, not uncertain — which sharpens pri 103's calibration finding (AUC 0.678, only
   74% correct at P ≥ 0.95) into a statement about the shape of the posterior, not just its centring.
2. **Cue-convergence-gated learning (teach only when the governor's arc and the arc-free licensor agree about
   predicate-vs-nominal host) — NEGATIVE.** gold obl 0.8217 → 0.7585, gold all-expressible 0.8394 → 0.8171, live all
   down. *Mechanism, measured:* the gate discards **16,923 of 74,984 decisions (22.6%)**, and those are precisely the
   cases where the two cues CONFLICT. The Competition Model's whole content is the *relative* validity of conflicting
   cues; removing the conflicts removes the evidence that sets it.
3. **The PROSODIC BREAK (comma / dash / paren) as a cue value — NEGATIVE.** gold core **−0.0169 CI-sep** while gold
   nmod is flat (354 → 353 correct). *Mechanism:* an intonation-phrase boundary separates appositives, conjuncts and
   list items (all OTHER, 49 + 116 + 108 tokens here) just as often as it separates an `nmod:desc`, so the contrast is
   ~flat for NMOD inside the configuration while it shifts the core classes.
4. **The FIRST-NOUN (clause-initial) cue, arc-free — NEGATIVE.** gold core **−0.0337 CI-sep**, live core −0.0194
   CI-sep, gold all-expressible 0.8528 → 0.8271. *Mechanism:* availability of `initial` among gold preverbal subjects
   is only **0.595** (343 of 576), so `later` fires on 40% of real subjects and the contrast it learns actively
   suppresses exactly the embedded and postposed subjects the organ is supposed to recover. (It *does* recover the
   causation affector — see 6 below — but not at this price.)
5. **Mapping `nmod:poss` to OTHER instead of NMOD — strongly negative**, gold nmod 0.7157 → 0.4683 CI-sep, live
   0.6667 → 0.3988. *Mechanism:* it is a quarter of the class (125 of 489) and a possessor is a nominal licensed by a
   nominal like any other; the genitive is its case marker, not a different role.
6. **The causation landing doc's affector is NOT recovered by the shipped arm.** Attributed exactly, on the LIVE
   parse of 'The storm flooded the village' (the live head of `storm` is `flooded` — correct), by the SUBJ−OTHER
   margin: floor **−0.278** → NMOD class alone −0.208 → +genitive −0.209 → **shipped −0.060** → licensor-on-every-
   nominal **+0.242 (nsubj)** → **gold-head-trained oracle: `nsubj`**. So the shipped build closes **78% of the
   margin gap** and the residual is the perceived-heads learning rung, not the class. The variant that closes it costs
   core accuracy CI-separated, so it was not shipped.
7. **The reliability gate on learning was re-swept** (0.0 / 0.5 / 0.8): 0.0 and 0.5 are identical to 4 decimals and
   0.8 is worse (gold nmod 0.6667 vs 0.6851). pri 103's operating point is confirmed and saturated.

## 10. ALTERNATE PATHS — as brain-foundational or MORE so, for strategy to queue

1. **Learn the labels rung's configuration cue from a SECOND, independent estimate of the host, not from the
   governor's arc.** The finding of this session is that the labels rung's knowledge is poisoned by the heads rung at
   *accrual* time, and that the arc-free licensor is a genuinely independent estimator of the same quantity
   (0.707 alone). The stronger form is to accrue the configuration cue itself over the *pair* (arc host class,
   arc-free host class) — i.e. make the configuration a two-source convergent estimate rather than one source with a
   correction. Brain-wise this is straightforward multisensory cue combination (Ernst & Banks 2002) applied to the
   configuration rather than to a contrast. **Reach: up to the oracle gap, gold-heads all-expressible 0.8528 → 0.9219.**
2. **Consolidate during a REPLAY pass rather than online-per-sentence.** The brain does not commit a comprehension
   outcome to long-term cue validity at read time; it consolidates (hippocampal replay → cortical statistics). A second
   pass in which the organ re-reads its own earlier decisions with the whole sentence available, and accrues only what
   survives re-reading, is the brain-faithful way to filter the governor's confident errors without discarding
   conflicts the way the convergence gate did. Not built here.
3. **A learned TIME/MEASURE lexical class as a cue.** `nmod:unmarked` is dominated by temporal and measure nominals
   ("last season", "10:53 AM", "$30 an entree", "5% a year"). The organ's own witness already records this as a known

   (too sparse); the queued stronger version is a COARSE induced class. **Reach: ≤ 60 tokens, ~+0.05 on gold nmod.**
4. **Stop measuring `nmod:desc` as nmod.** "President Bush" / "Enron Corp." is a UD-2.16 annotation split, not a
