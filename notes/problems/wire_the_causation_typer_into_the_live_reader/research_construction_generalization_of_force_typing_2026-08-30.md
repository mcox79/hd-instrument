## Bottom line (verdict first)

**Yes — there is a construction-general force representation, and it is the exact triple the typer already consumes: (affector force sign, patient tendency, endstate reached).** The four remaining constructions all map onto it, and the brain recovers that mapping ROBUSTLY for the canonical (agent-first / NVN-compatible) member of each — so a weak front-end on **resultative, caused-motion, make-periphrastic, and the transitive lexical causative** is **OUR fidelity gap to build across, not a brain limit.** The only genuine bounds are (i) the **intransitive inchoative** (there is literally no affector expressed — abstaining is CORRECT, not a miss) and (ii) **role-reversed / non-canonical order** (passive-implausible, object-cleft, object-relative, middle), which is where the brain itself is fragile (good-enough processing) — a reader failing THERE is brain-faithful.

**The construction-general representation, named:** Goldberg's **argument-structure constructions** all instantiate one **caused-change schema** — "X CAUSES Y to BECOME/MOVE/REACH Z" — and the **resultative is a metaphorical extension of caused-motion** (Change-of-State AS Change-of-Location; Goldberg 1995). The **construction carries the causal relation independent of the verb**; the verb contributes only MANNER or RESULT root content. That is why the same force type is recoverable when the surface form and even the bare verb differ.

**Two convergent evidence lines that this is construction-general in the brain, not verb-driven:**
- **Bencini & Goldberg (2000), *J. Memory & Language* — the sentence-sorting study:** with 4 verbs × 4 constructions (transitive/ditransitive/caused-motion/resultative) crossed, people sort by **CONSTRUCTION, not by verb**, when grouping by overall sentence meaning. Sentence meaning tracks the construction more than the shared verb. This is the load-bearing psycholinguistic result: the caused-change meaning lives in the construction.
- **Allen, Pereira, Botvinick & Goldberg (2012), *Brain & Language* — fMRI MVPA:** argument-structure constructions have **distinct multivoxel signatures** (left IFG / peri-Sylvian) that discriminate construction identity — i.e. the construction has neural reality separable from the verb. Converges with Kemmerer's argument-structure-construction work (two-level theory: verb-root meaning vs construction/event-relational meaning are separably localized) and with the **eADM** actor-first prominence binder (Bornkessel-Schlesewsky & Schlesewsky 2006) that recovers the Actor construction-generally from animacy/case/order.

## The construction-general schema (PINNED) and where each piece comes from

| piece of the force triple | construction-general source | PINNED vs OUR-INVENTION |
|---|---|---|
| **force TYPE (CAUSE/ENABLE/PREVENT)** | the CONSTRUCTION (caused-change schema; Goldberg 1995) OR the periphrastic verb lexicon (Wolff & Song 2003: make/force/cause = CAUSE; let/allow/permit = ENABLE; block/keep/prevent = PREVENT). NOT the manner verb. | PINNED |
| **affector (antagonist/causer)** | the highest-prominence **Actor** (eADM); the matrix subject, incl. an inanimate force ("the wind", "the heat") | PINNED: actor-first binding; OUR-INVENTION: nsubj→affector parse glue |

| **endstate reached** | construction-specific (see per-construction below): result XP, path goal, complement predicate, or verb-lexicalized result | PINNED: event-structure composition; OUR-INVENTION: the parse cue detector |

## DELIVERABLE — per construction: (a) how the brain recovers the triple, (b) where the endstate comes from, (c) generalize (OUR gap) or genuine bound

### 1. RESULTATIVE — "she hammered the metal FLAT", "he wiped the counter CLEAN"
- **(a) How the brain recovers the triple (PINNED).** The verb is **MANNER** (hammer, wipe); it carries NO causation and NO result on its own. The **construction** supplies the caused-change schema "X causes Y to BECOME Z" (Goldberg 1995) — so **force TYPE = CAUSE comes from the construction, not the verb.** affector = subject (Actor), patient = object.
- **(b) Where the endstate comes from (PINNED).** From the **RESULT XP** — the secondary predicate (adjective/PP: *flat*, *clean*, *to pieces*), NOT the verb. Event-structure semantics: the resultative denotes **two subevents** — a dynamic subevent (hammer) + a result-state subevent (flat) — glued by **causation that comes from neither the verb nor the adjective alone** (Rappaport Hovav & Levin event structure; the "compositional glue is causation"). Default `endstate_reached = TRUE` (resultatives are telic — the result state obtains on completion), defeasible by progressive ("was hammering it flat") / "almost".
- **The rule the reader gets from Manner/Result Complementarity (Rappaport Hovav & Levin 2010, "Reflections on Manner/Result Complementarity"; Beavers & Koontz-Garboden):** a single verb lexicalizes EITHER manner OR result, never both. So: **if the verb is a MANNER verb and there is a secondary-predicate adjective/PP, the endstate is that RESULT XP; if the verb is a RESULT verb (break, flatten), the endstate is IN the verb.** Result verbs resist a separate redundant result phrase. This is a clean disambiguation the front-end can use.
- **(c) Verdict: GENERALIZE — OUR gap.** Resultatives are productive, frequent, canonical; endstate reading is robust. Build the detector: an `acomp`/`oprd`/`xcomp` adjective or resultative PP predicated of the object → bind as endstate, type CAUSE. (Harder sub-case, flag but don't block: **non-subcategorized "fake object" resultatives** — "talked himself hoarse", "ran her shoes threadbare", "sang the baby asleep" — rarer; the force TYPE is still CAUSE, only the endstate-binding is trickier.)

### 2. CAUSED-MOTION — "he pushed the cart INTO the barn", "the wind blew the papers OFF the desk"
- **(a) How the brain recovers the triple (PINNED).** Goldberg's caused-motion construction "X CAUSES Y to MOVE Z" — [Subj V Obj Obl_path]. **Force TYPE = CAUSE from the construction.** affector = subject (incl. inanimate "the wind"), patient = object. The **resultative is the metaphorical extension of THIS construction** (Goldberg 1995) — so they share machinery, which is why one detector family covers both.
- **(b) Where the endstate comes from (PINNED).** From the **PATH / directional PP** (the oblique), a change of **LOCATION**, not change of state. English is **satellite-framed** (Talmy 2000): the **path is in the satellite/preposition** (into/off/onto/to), the verb carries manner. The **goal of a bounded path IS the endstate** (the cart is IN the barn). Telicity = **path boundedness** (Krifka; Zwarts): *into the barn / to the wall* (bounded) → telic, `endstate_reached = TRUE`; *toward the barn / along the road* (unbounded) → atelic, endstate not asserted.
- **Key for the reader:** the endstate here is **spatial (a goal location)**, not a change-of-state adjective. If the endstate slot expects an adjective it will miss caused-motion. Treat "reached the goal location" as `endstate_reached = TRUE`.
- **(c) Verdict: GENERALIZE — OUR gap.** Highly frequent, canonical, robust. Build: directional-PP / particle (into/onto/off/to/up/out) with a caused-motion verb → bind goal as endstate, type CAUSE. **Note the symmetry with the already-built prevention-from:** "kept the toddler FROM falling" is caused-NON-motion — the from-complement is a **negated path/endstate**. Caused-motion (reached) and prevention-from (not reached) are the same family with opposite `endstate_reached`.

### 3. MAKE / HAVE / GET PERIPHRASTIC — "the heat MADE the metal expand", "the joke MADE her laugh"
- **(a) How the brain recovers the triple (PINNED).** The real predicate is in the **COMPLEMENT**; make/have/get carry the **CAUSATION**. Periphrastic causatives **lexicalize the force type** (Wolff & Song 2003; Wolff 2007): **make/force/cause = CAUSE** (affector force is sufficient; patient need not tend toward it). affector = matrix subject (heat, joke), **patient = causee = complement-clause subject** (metal, her). This is the **same causee=complement-subject binder the SOLVED already built for the LETTING causative** — extended to make/have/get, with force type CAUSE instead of ENABLE. Syntactically biclausal but processed as a single caused-event (Kemmer & Verhagen 1994, "The grammar of causatives and the conceptual structure of events" — periphrastic causatives express a two-participant force-dynamic event).
- **(b) Where the endstate comes from (PINNED).** From the **complement predicate**, and — critically — **make/have + bare infinitive are IMPLICATIVE**: "the joke made her laugh" ENTAILS she laughed. So `endstate_reached = TRUE` by default (the caused event actually occurred), unlike "tried to make her laugh". ("get X to V" is likewise implicative of the complement event.) This contrasts sharply with PREVENT (not reached) and with the defeasible resultative.
- **(c) Verdict: GENERALIZE — OUR gap, and CHEAP (reuses the existing causee binder).** Build: matrix make/have/get with a ccomp/xcomp → causee = complement subject, endstate = complement event (implicative TRUE), type CAUSE. (Nuance: *get*/*have* carry a directive/manipulative flavor; for a 3-way typer they remain CAUSE-family.)

### 4. LEXICAL CAUSATIVE vs INCHOATIVE ALTERNANT — "the wind opened the gate" (CAUSE) vs "the gate opened" (spontaneous)
- **(a) How the brain recovers the triple (PINNED).** The **causative-inchoative alternation**: same verb (open/break/melt), two frames. **Transitive:** affector = subject (Actor), patient = object, force TYPE = CAUSE (already handled by the SOLVED). **Intransitive/inchoative:** the surface **subject is the PATIENT** (unaccusative), **NO affector is expressed.** The discrimination the reader must make is a **diathesis check**: is the nsubj a CAUSER (transitive, has a dobj) or the affected THEME (intransitive, no dobj)?
- **Brain finding (PINNED, and it refines the abstain rule).** Change-of-state verbs split into **externally-caused** (break, open, melt — denote **two subevents**: cause + change) and **internally-caused** (bloom, rust — one subevent). Psycholinguistic evidence (change-of-state processing literature; Levin & Rappaport Hovav 1995 *Unaccusativity*): **externally-caused verbs take LONGER to comprehend than internally-caused, in BOTH the transitive AND the intransitive frame** — i.e. the brain represents the **cause sub-event even in the intransitive inchoative** ("the gate opened" implies something opened it), but does **not bind a specific affector**.
- **(b) Where the endstate comes from (PINNED).** From the **VERB** — open/break/melt are RESULT verbs, the result state is lexicalized (gate is open). Both frames are telic achievements → `endstate_reached = TRUE`.
- **(c) Verdict: MIXED — transitive GENERALIZE (OUR gap, already done); intransitive is a PRECISION BOUND, and abstaining is CORRECT.** For the intransitive inchoative there is **no affector in the sentence to recover** — the reader must **NOT hallucinate a CAUSE link**; record a **spontaneous change-of-state (external cause implied, affector = unknown)**. This is brain-faithful (the brain also leaves the affector unbound). The buildable win is the **diathesis DETECTOR** that prevents the reader from mistyping an inchoative as a CAUSE — a false-positive guard, not a coverage add.

## RQ4 — role-reversal / non-canonical order: what the brain robustly recovers vs where it is genuinely fragile

**Robust (agent-first / NVN-compatible → OUR gap if the reader fails):** active transitive causative, caused-motion, resultative, make/have/get periphrastic, and the **plausible passive** (recovered, though with a **P600 revision cost** — a distinct, effortful reanalysis; the ERP-of-passive-vs-active literature).

**Genuinely fragile (brain-faithful to fail — do NOT hammer):**
- **Role-reversed IMPLAUSIBLE passives** — "the dog was bitten by the man" is frequently misread as the dog biting. **N400 is often insensitive to the reversal**; plausibility/lexical association overrides the syntactic assignment (Ferreira 2003; Ferreira, Bailey & Ferraro 2002; Kim & Osterhout "semantic attraction"). Good-enough / shallow processing.
- **Object clefts** ("it was the man the dog bit") and **object relatives** — patient-before-agent; the **NVN heuristic** ("first noun = agent") drives errors, worst with implausible content.
- **The MIDDLE construction** ("the book reads easily", "these shirts wash well") — the surface subject is the patient/theme, there is **no expressed agent**, and the middle is semantically idiosyncratic (dispositional, generic). This is the hardest and least compositional; treat a middle as **not a force-dynamic caused event** (no antagonist), same abstain posture as the inchoative.
- **Refinement from the 20-years good-enough review (Ferreira lab, 2024):** even **overt morphological case** is not reliably used for role assignment (German case-marking studies) — the prominence/agent-first heuristic dominates and morphological reanalysis is fragile. This confirms: the reader should recover roles robustly for **canonical order** and is entitled to be fragile (brain-faithful) for **patient-first constructions**.

**Practical split for the wire:** canonical-order failures = OUR gap to build; patient-first (reversed passive, object cleft/relative, middle) failures = brain-faithful, report as such, do not over-engineer.

## What this means for the wire (front-end additions, each PINNED + OUR-INVENTION flagged)

1. **RESULTATIVE detector** — secondary-predicate result XP (acomp/oprd/xcomp adjective or resultative PP) predicated of the object; verb is manner; type CAUSE; endstate = result XP. *PINNED: construction-carries-meaning + endstate-from-result-XP. OUR-INVENTION: the parse detector; Manner/Result Complementarity gives the manner-vs-result verb split for free.*
2. **CAUSED-MOTION detector** — directional PP / particle (into/onto/off/to/up/out) with a caused-motion verb; type CAUSE; endstate = goal location, `reached = TRUE` iff bounded path + no defeater. *PINNED: satellite-framed path-in-PP (Talmy) + caused-motion construction (Goldberg). OUR-INVENTION: the PP/particle detector. Reuses the from-complement machinery (prevention = negated path).*
3. **MAKE/HAVE/GET periphrastic** — EXTEND the existing causee=complement-subject binder to make/have/get; force type CAUSE; endstate = complement event, implicative → TRUE. *PINNED: periphrastic verb lexicalizes force type (Wolff & Song) + implicativity. OUR-INVENTION: the ccomp/xcomp matrix-causative detector. Cheapest — the binder exists.*
4. **INCHOATIVE precision guard** — diathesis check (unaccusative intransitive → subject is PATIENT, no affector) → record spontaneous change-of-state, do NOT emit a CAUSE link. *PINNED: causative-inchoative alternation + externally-vs-internally-caused. OUR-INVENTION: the diathesis detector. A false-positive guard, not coverage.*

**One honesty caveat that constrains the headline:** the construction-general representation is real and the brain recovers it robustly for CANONICAL members — so a rigorous negative on canonical resultative/caused-motion/make-periphrastic would be a real fidelity gap. But a negative on **role-reversed / patient-first** members (reversed passive, object cleft, middle) or on the **intransitive inchoative** is **brain-faithful** and should be reported as a bound, not hammered.
