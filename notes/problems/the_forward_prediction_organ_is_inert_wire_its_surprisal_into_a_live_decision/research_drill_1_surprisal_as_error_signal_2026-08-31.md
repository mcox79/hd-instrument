# Research drill 1 (2026-08-31): forward-prediction error as a live comprehension-control signal

Dispatched via hdi_research before finalizing the decision design. Question: is "surprisal of the
reader's OWN role binding predicts a comprehension ERROR" brain-foundational, and what is the most
brain-faithful decision to drive?

## Answers (citation-backed)

**Q1 -- Is "surprisal of the reader's own binding => wrong binding" PINNED or OUR-INVENTION?**
OUR-INVENTION (a well-motivated extrapolation), label it as such. Pinned parts: prediction error is
real/graded/meaning-level (N400/surprisal; Hale 2001, Levy 2008, DeLong 2005, Michaelov 2024); readers
DO misassign roles at implausible/low-probability sites (good-enough: Ferreira 2003, Ferreira & Patson
2007, Karimi & Ferreira 2016); revision is a rational posterior (noisy-channel: Levy 2008, Gibson et al.
2013); conflict triggers reanalysis (semantic P600: Van Herten & Kolk 2005/06; Brouwer 2012). What is
NOT cleanly pinned is a mechanism separating "surprising-but-correct" from "surprising-because-mis-
bound." Closest pinned mechanism = the semantic-P600 CONFLICT MONITOR. So the honest claim is the weaker
one: a high-surprisal bound argument is at ELEVATED RISK of misinterpretation -- a RISK FLAG, not a
verdict.

**Q2 -- Which decision, ranked by neural directness?** (1) RE-READ/reanalysis -- most directly evidenced
as a control action (regressions to re-check: Levy, Bicknell, Slattery & Rayner 2009; P600 conflict).
(2) REVISE-toward-prediction (noisy-channel posterior) -- what the brain does FIRST (fast, automatic,
N400 window) but IS the good-enough error; committing it silently reproduces the brain's failure mode.
(3) ABSTAIN/withhold -- weakest as a distinct neural act, best as a precision-weighted confidence
readout. RECOMMENDATION: make the primary decision a surprisal-gated RE-READ that treats the plausible
reinterpretation as a CANDIDATE to verify (not auto-commit), with confidence as the dial.

**Q3 -- Precision-weighting as a graded signature?** Sound and literature-grounded (Federmeier et al.
2007 frontal post-N400 positivity: disconfirming a HIGH-CONSTRAINT prediction carries an extra cost;
Szewczyk 2022 reliability-weighting; Kuperberg precision hierarchy). PITFALLS: measure at the DECISION
layer (constraint modulates the late positivity, not raw N400 magnitude); keep it feature/meaning-level
(Nieuwland 2018); orthogonalize precision from cloze/correctness (the can-fail is the diagnosticity
SLOPE steepening with precision, not lower baseline surprisal).

## How it shaped the build
- INFORMATIVE gate framed as surprisal = RISK flag (AUC), OUR-INVENTION-labeled.
- Primary ACTIONABLE = surprisal-ABSTAIN (the confidence readout, drill-blessed, robust) + tested
  surprisal-gated reanalysis as the ambitious decision but treating the plausible candidate as a
  verified adoption (gap + precision gate), NOT an auto-commit.
- Precision-diagnosticity built as the graded signature (AUC by precision tercile), at the decision
  layer.

Key sources: Ferreira 2003; Ferreira & Patson 2007; Gibson, Bergen & Piantadosi 2013 (PNAS); Levy,
Bicknell, Slattery & Rayner 2009 (PNAS); Van Herten, Chwilla & Kolk 2005/06; Brouwer, Fitz & Hoeks 2012;
DeLong, Urbach & Kutas 2005; Kutas & Federmeier 2011; Federmeier et al. 2007 (f-PNP); Szewczyk et al.
2022; Kuperberg 2022 / predictive-coding N400 2024; Hodapp & Rabovsky 2021; Michaelov et al. 2024.
