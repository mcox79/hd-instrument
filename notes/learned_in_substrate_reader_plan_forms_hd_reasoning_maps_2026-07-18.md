## The architecture (each piece grounded in a landed result)
1. **Representation = HD role-filler binding (HAVE it).** The reader EMITS into HD binding, not symbolic tuples. k-parity + nesting confirmed binding holds nested structure at depth ~2-3 (brain-faithful center-embedding limit).
2. **Learned construction-induction (the core new capability = FORM the map).** Instead of hand-rules, LEARN which role-filler bindings to form from grounded text: induce sentence patterns (constructions) scored by COHERENCE (does the parse map into the known foundation / stay reasoning-coherent?), via a bootstrap loop. Reuses the surprise/KL/schema-fit math we already have (D2 spec). This is "discover the conjunctions from grounded input" — the exact gap k-parity flagged.
3. **Selection engine = the predictive/error loop (predcode).** Relevance-gate to SELECT the right binding among candidates at scale (k-parity: this is where predcode earns its keep). = "comprehend using the picture-so-far, predict the next piece of meaning, correct on surprise" = the compress-and-carry reading loop (USER [[feedback_dragonfly_is_understanding_and_responding_not_nextword_prediction_USER_2026-07-18]]).
4. **Coherence gate (don't poison the foundation).** Accept a reading only if it coheres with the situation-model-so-far (schema-fit). We saw error-amplification when bad parses leak; the gate is the comprehension analog of the reactivation/error-correction the D1 drill flagged.
5. **Base-first grounding (HAVE it).** Words pre-grounded (dictionary/WordNet = picture/teacher stand-in). The reader GROWS RELATIONS among known words, does NOT learn word meanings from text (USER [[feedback_word_meaning_from_grounding_not_grade1_text_reading_grows_relations_USER_2026-07-18]]).
