## BRAIN MECHANISM (4 findings, 1 load-bearing TENSION -- brain-check did NOT match the naive assumption)
1. **ROLE REPRESENTATION = abstract, content-independent role codes.** Frankland & Greene (eNeuro 2020 "A Model for Structured Information Representation"; AnnRevPsy 2020): the brain encodes "who did what to whom" with DEDICATED role populations -- AGENT in medial lmSTC, PATIENT in lateral lmSTC -- and CONTENT (filler) is dynamically ATTACHED via Hebbian "assembly-projection." => fixed role vectors (agent/patient/verb) with bound content = BRAIN-FAITHFUL role-filler separation.
2. **COMPGEN MECHANISM = structural scaffolding + constraint-satisfaction, NOT explicit algebra.** eLife 2024 (107162, "neural mechanism for compositional generalization of structure"): humans extract abstract relational "building blocks" from experience + reuse them as a STRUCTURAL SCAFFOLD to interpret novel input via CONSTRAINT-BASED hypothesis-narrowing / template-mapping; representational ALIGNMENT of same-structural-role stimuli (300-680ms). CRITICALLY: found NO explicit binding signature (no tensor-product, no synchrony). Plus (search-level): novel composite = LINEAR COMBINATION of familiar elements (vmPFC high-dim); low-dim DISENTANGLED abstract variables; hippocampal-entorhinal + frontoparietal.
3. **LEARNABILITY = meta-learning acquires systematicity.** Lake & Baroni (Nature 2023, MLC "meta-learning for compositionality"): a neural net reaches human-like systematic generalization via episodic/compositional meta-training. => the LEARNED front-end CAN acquire compgen; not innate-only.
4. **CLS split.** Neuron 2026 (distinct hippocampus vs neocortex in symbolic compositional generalization): hippocampus = fast novel-composition binding, neocortex = consolidated abstract structure. => our CLS case+sleep maps here.

**THE TENSION (honest brain-check, do NOT pre-assume): explicit VSA algebraic binding is NOT what the brain literally does** (eLife found no binding signature). It IS a design CHOICE that captures the brain's FACTORIZED role<->content SEPARATION (Frankland-Greene = brain-faithful) and yields the systematicity the brain exhibits. The brain's actual composition = dynamic assembly (Hebbian) + constraint-satisfaction settling (template-mapping) -> which our Kintsch-CI comprehension loop + resonator settling APPROXIMATE. So: claim "native binding captures the factorized-role property + delivers systematicity"; claim "the settling/CI-loop captures the constraint-satisfaction half"; do NOT claim "native binding = the brain's mechanism." (Softens the scour's chain-grade framing; strengthens brain-faithfulness by adding the settling half.)

## DESIGN (component #1: the glass-box compositional ENCODER)
- **LEARNED text->role assignment** (small in-substrate front-end): which token fills which role (agent/patient/verb). This is the LEARNED locus (the part that must generalize).
- **FIXED VSA role-binding** (FHRR): prop = agent(x)filler + patient(x)filler + verb(x)v = the factorized substrate (free-algebra systematicity property, 29399).
- **CONSTRAINT-SATISFACTION settling** (resonator / CI-loop): map NOVEL combinations onto the role-structure (the eLife template-mapping half; we have iterative_attractor).
- COMPGEN = factorized-code systematicity (property) + the LEARNED mapping generalizing + settling on novel combos.

## THE CAN-FAIL CELL (component #1 test = the chain-grade shot)
- COGS/SCAN-style HELD-OUT-COMBINATION split: train on a subset of (agent,verb,patient) role-filler combinations; TEST on NOVEL combinations (fillers seen in OTHER roles, combinations never co-occurring). This is genuinely non-tautological (29423 bar): held-out combos are NOT lookups.
- ARMS: LEARNED-native-bind(+settling) encoder vs a FAIR LEARNED-FLAT baseline (same training budget, same supervision, NO binding -- e.g. a bag/MLP readout). 
- **AVOID THE 29399 FREE-ALGEBRA TRAP (the load-bearing VET risk): the LOCUS must be the LEARNED encoder.** If native-bind beats flat ONLY by fixed construction (not learning), it's free-algebra not a capability. The genuine result = learned-encoder-INTO-binding-space generalizes to held-out combinations where a learned-flat model FAILS (COGS precedent: flat 16-35% held-out vs compositional ~98%). 
- MEASURE the LEARNING CURVE (held-out compgen improves as it trains/reads = the flexible/improving property). Design-gate: real baseline (learned-flat), can-fail (native-bind no better than flat on held-out, OR wins only by construction), difficulty-on (held-out combos truly disjoint), one-variable (binding on/off, same training).
- HARD-PASS = native-bind generalizes to held-out combos + beats flat + learning-curve rises + not-construction-determined => CHAIN-GRADE CANDIDATE (novel, brain-faithful-factorization, beats-baseline, flexible/improving).
