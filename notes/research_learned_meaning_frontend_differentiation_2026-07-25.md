## Biology-grounded synthesis

### Q1 — Does the brain's semantic system differentiate coarse-to-fine WITH LEARNING, and what's the signal?

**Biology-grounded, high confidence.** Rogers & McClelland's PDP model (*Semantic Cognition: A PDP Approach*, MIT Press 2004; Nat Rev Neurosci precis 2003) trains a Rumelhart-style network on an **item + relation → attribute-completion** task (e.g. input "canary" + "can" → output the correct property pattern, mirroring Collins & Quillian 1969 feature-verification data). The ONLY learning signal is **error-driven correction** (backprop cross-entropy against the true attribute vector) — there is no hand-inserted category structure. Under this single signal, coarse distinctions (animal vs. plant) are resolved by the network *before* fine ones (fish vs. bird; within our target case, hydro vs. nuclear vs. coal), reproducing the empirical developmental order (Keil 1979 conceptual development; Mandler global-to-basic categorization; Quinn infant global/basic-level).

**Biology-grounded, mechanistically exact.** Saxe, McClelland & Ganguli, *A mathematical theory of semantic development in deep neural networks*, PNAS 2019 — for deep **linear** networks trained on hierarchically-structured environmental statistics, they derive an **exact, closed-form solution** of the learning dynamics: the network resolves each "mode" of the input-output correlation structure at a time governed by that mode's **singular value** — large-singular-value modes (= coarse, shared-across-many-items structure) are learned in **few exposures**; small-singular-value modes (= fine, idiosyncratic structure shared by few items) require **many more exposures**, and each mode's error trajectory is **sigmoidal/stage-like** (long near-flat plateau, then a rapid transition), not smooth or exponential-single-timescale. This gives a mechanistic answer to "what drives the differentiation" — it's a direct, provable consequence of the **eigenvalue spectrum of the training environment's co-occurrence statistics**, not an arbitrary curriculum or hand schedule.

### Q2 — ATL hub-and-spoke: why does the learned hub discriminate where flat distributional space conflates?

**Biology-grounded.** Lambon Ralph et al. (Nat Rev Neurosci 2017 "The neural and computational bases of semantic cognition"; Rogers et al. 2004 Cogn Neuropsych hub-and-spoke; Chen & Lambon Ralph subsequent hub-circuit work) model the bilateral anterior temporal lobe (ATL) as a **nonlinear transmodal bottleneck ("hub")** with reciprocal connections to modality-specific "spokes" (visual, verbal, motor, etc.). The hub is forced, by training, to **predict across modalities/relations** — reconstruct a concept's visual features from its verbal description and vice versa. Two concepts that share SOME spokes (hydro/nuclear/coal all share the "produces-electricity", "power-plant" spoke) but differ on others ("water-source" vs "fission/waste" vs "combustion/pollution") can only be kept discriminable if the **hub's own weights** have been pushed apart by prediction error on the differing spokes — a flat, single-modality distributional vector (GloVe/WordNet co-occurrence) has no such cross-modal/cross-relation error signal forcing separation, so near-neighbor concepts that differ only on a few properties stay conflated. **Direct clinical existence-proof of the reverse:** in semantic dementia (progressive ATL atrophy), the SAME fine-vs-coarse asymmetry appears as *damage*: patients lose fine/subordinate distinctions FIRST (types of vehicle, e.g. "car" collapses toward "vehicle" collapses toward "thing") while coarse/superordinate structure is preserved far longer (Warrington 1975; Hodges, Graham & Patterson 1995; corroborated by more recent hierarchical-error studies showing coordinate-level errors precede cross-category errors) — i.e. the LAST-LEARNED / LEAST-REDUNDANTLY-CODED distinctions are the FIRST LOST, the same ordering predicted by the eigenvalue account in Q1. This cross-validates that fine content lives in the **low-variance, hub-dependent modes** — exactly the modes a frozen, single-source distributional vector under-resolves, and exactly what a trained bottleneck should recover given the right cross-relation error signal.

### Q3 — Shape of the learning curve; does it generalize to unseen combinations (systematicity)?

**Biology-grounded (shape/rate) + biology-adjacent caution (generalization).** From Q1: the curve per distinction is **stage-like / sigmoidal**, its onset-time governed by the distinction's eigenvalue/shared-variance in training statistics — coarse curves transition early, fine curves transition late, and (Rogers-McClelland) rare/low-typicality items may transition very late or not resolve at all within a finite exposure budget ("illusory correlations", never-fully-differentiated categories) — this is itself a documented, non-catastrophic failure mode, not a bug.

**Caution — systematicity is a SEPARATE, harder axis.** Plain error-driven PDP differentiation nets have **no explicit role/filler binding** — they are flat associative networks. Fodor & Pylyshyn's classic critique (connectionism lacks compositional structure) is empirically borne out by modern compositional-generalization benchmarks: COGS and similar splits show 96-99% in-distribution accuracy collapsing to 16-35% on **held-out novel combinations** for flat/non-compositional architectures, while explicitly-structured/binding architectures (AM-parser, native symbol-binding nets) hold up near 98%. This independently cross-validates our own 07-18 prior_art_scour finding: **compositional generalization requires a SEPARATE native-bind mechanism at the encoder**, not differentiation-learning alone. **Implication for this drill: differentiation-learning (this component) and compositional-binding (a different, already-scoped component) are two distinct axes.** This component should be evaluated for what it does — resolve fine content WITHIN a trained vocabulary — and explicitly NOT credited for zero-shot compositional transfer, which is out of scope here and belongs to the separate native-bind roadmap item.

### Q4 — Discriminative or generative pressure; which is brain-faithful for the substrate?

**Biology-grounded, converging.** Eleanor & James Gibson's differentiation theory (*Perceptual Learning: Differentiation or Enrichment?*, Psych Review 1955; Eleanor Gibson's subsequent program) explicitly frames perceptual learning as **differentiation driven by discrimination demands** — learning to detect the **distinctive features that tell stimuli apart for a task** — and explicitly rejects passive associative "enrichment" of impoverished input as the mechanism. The Rogers-McClelland implementation is technically a **predictive/completion task** (cross-entropy against the true attribute vector), not an explicit contrastive-with-negatives objective — but functionally it delivers a **discriminative-completion signal**: whenever two items share most of their predicted attributes but differ on a few (near-neighbor minimal pairs — hydro/nuclear/coal), the training error is concentrated *exactly on the differentiating attributes*. So the brain-faithful learning signal for the substrate's front-end is: **predict the correct fine property/relation from context, and let the error be dominated by near-neighbor minimal-pair contrasts** — this is discriminative in effect even though implemented as prediction-error, and it is NOT generic whole-passage reconstruction and NOT abstract InfoNCE-style random-negative contrastive learning (no direct brain precedent found in this scan for the latter as the primary differentiation driver; flagged speculative if used).

## Concrete can-fail cell design

**Name:** `exp_learned_meaning_frontend_differentiation_v1`

**SHAPE:** A small Rumelhart-style item+relation→attribute-vector predictor: input = FROZEN GloVe/WordNet embedding of the concept word (the current conflated baseline) concatenated with a relation-cue (one-hot over a small WorldTree relation set: SOURCEOF / REQUIRES / CAUSE / KINDOF / PROP-RESOURCES-RENEWABLE, etc., already disk-confirmed to exist for hydro/nuclear/coal per the 07-24 note); a single hidden bottleneck layer; output = predicted property-bit-vector over a small property vocabulary (fuel-source, renewability, waste-type, combustion-byproduct, water-dependency). Trained via cross-entropy error-driven backprop (Rumelhart-task-faithful). This is a genuinely LEARNED map — NOT a lookup table, NOT a hand-authored property-binding rule (the gate distinguishing this from the 07-24 hand-bound arc).

**FIXED-VSA vs LEARNED split (NVSA pattern):** LEARNED = the item+relation → hub-bottleneck → property-vector mapping (small dense net, glass-box/inspectable weights and per-property predictions). FIXED = everything downstream unchanged — the existing `hd_fact_store.py` typed REL/ARG0/ARG1 bind/bundle algebra and the unchanged combiner from 29546. The hub's bottleneck activation becomes the new CONCEPT-level vector fed into the (already-built, already-validated) selection stage, replacing the frozen SemanticHDEncoder cosine feature — isolates the change to the meaning representation exactly at the diagnosed wall.

**PLACE:** the concept-representation layer feeding the 29546 selection stage — same slot the frozen cosine currently occupies, not a bolted-on side module.

**METRIC:** discrimination accuracy — top-1 correct-property / nearest-neighbor-among-minimal-pair-contrast-set — measured vs. **#exposures** (property-prediction training examples seen), split by **coarse tier** (renewable vs. nonrenewable — high shared-variance) vs. **fine tier** (hydro vs. nuclear vs. coal specific source identity — low shared-variance). NOT cosine-to-gold (that's the metric that's already failing) and NOT raw end-to-end ARC accuracy alone (too noisy/indirect given the combiner's own ceiling at oracle=0.69) — this metric isolates the REPRESENTATION's own improvement.

**Real baseline:** frozen GloVe/WordNet cosine similarity (today's conflated state; the standing baseline across 29544/29545/29546).

**Learning signal:** discriminative-completion / property-prediction error over near-neighbor minimal-pair triples (hydro/nuclear/coal/gas power-plant facts, or entailing-vs-merely-topically-similar WorldTree fact pairs), per Q4.

**Must-fail control:** shuffled-property-label control — identical architecture and exposure count, but property-vector labels randomly permuted across items (preserves label marginal frequency, destroys the true item-property mapping). This control MUST fail to rise above chance at any exposure count. If it also rises, the "learning curve" is an artifact (frequency leakage / readout exploiting something other than real item-specific discrimination) and the whole cell is invalidated regardless of the main-arm curve.

**Compositional-generalization probe (the genuine edge — expected partial-fail, diagnostic not promissory):** hold out specific (item, relation) pairs never seen in training (e.g. train "hydro CAN produce-electricity" + "hydro HAS water-source" + "coal HAS combustion", withhold "hydro HAS renewable" entirely). Per Q3, the PREDICTION is that this component alone will show DEGRADED-BUT-ABOVE-CHANCE performance on same-item/new-relation recombination (partial credit from the item's already-differentiated bottleneck vector) but WILL NOT reliably solve true novel item x relation combinations requiring compositional binding never exposed — a clean fail here is expected and diagnostic (confirms the boundary between this component and the separate native-bind roadmap item), a full pass would be a genuine surprise worth flagging up, not assumed.

**Can-fail condition (main arm):** if the FINE-tier discrimination curve stays flat (no rise above baseline) across the full exposure budget while the COARSE-tier curve does rise, that is an informative, in-scope negative — reproduces the diagnosed wall at a controlled, instrumented scale and should trigger inspection of whether the frozen GloVe seed vectors for the minimal-pair set are themselves too close to carry any differentiable signal ("garbage-in" risk, see below) before concluding the mechanism itself fails. If NEITHER curve rises, that is a pipeline/implementation failure, not a scientific negative — must debug before drawing any conclusion.

**Named risk to pre-check before the full run:** if the frozen GloVe vectors for hydro/nuclear/coal are already near-degenerate (cosine > ~0.9 pairwise), the hub may lack enough input signal to differentiate regardless of training signal quality — a cheap 5-minute pre-check (pairwise cosine among the minimal-pair set) should run before committing to the full exposure-curve sweep.

## Falsifiable predictions (HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL, pre-registered)

1. **Coarse-before-fine ordering.** HARD-PASS: coarse-tier discrimination accuracy crosses 80%-of-asymptote at a strictly smaller #exposures than fine-tier crosses the same threshold, replicated across >=3 independent minimal-pair sets. HARD-FAIL: orderings are mixed/reversed across sets (no consistent coarse-first pattern) — would refute the eigenvalue-ordering account as applied here.
2. **Stage-like (sigmoidal) curve shape.** HARD-PASS: fine-tier curve fit is significantly better under a sigmoidal/logistic model than a linear model (e.g. AIC delta favoring sigmoid by a pre-registered margin) for >=2/3 minimal-pair sets. HARD-FAIL: curves are consistently linear or immediately-saturating (no plateau-then-transition) — would suggest the differentiation isn't following the predicted mode-resolution dynamics.
3. **Must-fail shuffled control.** HARD-PASS-precondition (gate, not itself a result): shuffled-label control stays within noise band of chance across all exposure counts. HARD-FAIL (invalidates cell regardless of main-arm result): shuffled control rises above chance by more than the pre-registered margin.
4. **Fine-content discrimination lift over baseline.** HARD-PASS: fine-tier discrimination accuracy at max exposure budget exceeds frozen-GloVe-cosine baseline by >=0.25 absolute AND clears the shuffled-control band by the same margin. HARD-FAIL: lift <0.10 absolute — the learned front-end does not meaningfully out-discriminate the current conflated baseline; treat as a negative on this specific architecture (not necessarily on the whole differentiation-learning direction — check the near-degenerate-seed risk before generalizing the conclusion).
5. **Compositional probe (diagnostic, not gating).** Expected: held-out same-item/new-relation performance sits meaningfully above chance but below in-vocabulary performance; held-out novel item x relation combinations sit near chance. A surprise full pass here should be flagged and re-examined for leakage before treating it as evidence against the separate-binding-component thesis.
