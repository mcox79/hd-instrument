## STEP 1 — Brain-drill (multiple angles, biology first)

### 1a. Tolman-Eichenbaum Machine / grid-cell structure-content factorization (carried forward from `research_brain_compositional_role_binding_structure_content_factorization_2026-07-18.md` — not re-derived, cited for continuity)

**SHAPE:** a learned, content-blind structural code `g` (entorhinal/grid-cell analog) generated from graph topology / transition structure ONLY, bound to a content code `x` via an outer-product-class conjunction to form hippocampal code `p = bind(g, x)`. **PLACE:** medial entorhinal cortex (`g`-generator, shared/frozen across environments) + hippocampus proper (`p`, re-learned per environment via Hebbian associative storage into matrix `M`). **METRIC:** `g` satisfies two trainable constraints — (i) distinct `g` at distinct graph nodes/roles, (ii) IDENTICAL `g` when the same node/role recurs with different content — trained across MANY different graphs/environments so that transfer to a brand-new environment's content is zero-shot (Whittington, Muller, Mark, Chen, Barry, Burgess, Behrens, *Cell* 2020; confirmed extending to purely conceptual/social-hierarchy domains via gridlike neural codes, Constantinescu, O'Reilly, Behrens *Science* 2016). **Honest gap (both this program's 07-18 note and this session's independent ATL lit-scan confirm it):** TEM has never been demonstrated for language-scale/symbolic role-filler binding — this remains an untested extrapolation, not a lookup of an established result.

### 1b. Rogers-McClelland PDP semantic differentiation (NEW this drill — fills a stated gap)

The PDP account (Rogers & McClelland, *Semantic Cognition*, MIT Press 2004; McClelland & Rogers, *Nat Rev Neurosci* 2003) generalizes via **coherent covariation through a shared distributed hidden layer**: every item and property passes through the SAME hidden units, so properties that co-occur across many items exert convergent pressure on shared weights, and error-driven (backprop) learning acquires the strongest cross-item covariance structure first — producing coarse-before-fine "waves of differentiation" and correct generalization to a novel item sharing that structure (the canonical "novel property of maple generalizes to all plants" demonstration). **The formal account of WHY/WHEN this generalizes vs. memorizes is a data-structure claim, not primarily an architecture claim**: Saxe, McClelland & Ganguli (*PNAS* 2019) give exact solutions for deep linear networks showing the timing and shape of differentiation is governed by the **singular-value spectrum of the training set's item-property correlation matrix** — i.e., generalization requires the training environment to actually CONTAIN diverse, hierarchically-nested covariation across many items; the same architecture fed low-diversity or non-hierarchical data will not differentiate correctly. **The known failure/memorization mode**, per Marcus (1998, *Cognitive Psychology*, "Rethinking Eliminative Connectionism"): this class of network generalizes only by **interpolation inside the convex hull of the training feature space** and cannot extrapolate a relation to structure outside it — a formal, independent statement of exactly the memorization failure this program keeps measuring. Rogers-McClelland's own model reproduces a related failure signature (**illusory correlations** — transient overgeneralization mid-training when property covariation is not yet coherent across enough items), which is a *symptom of insufficient training-set diversity*, not a different mechanism. **SHAPE/PLACE/METRIC (synthesized, not literature-native naming, flagged as such):** SHAPE = a distributed hidden code undergoing discrete coarse-to-fine splits driven by dominant covariance eigenmodes; PLACE = originally architecture-agnostic in the 2004 book, later explicitly localized to the ATL as the shared/transmodal hub (Rogers et al. 2004 *Psych Review*, semantic-dementia bridge); METRIC = coherent covariation (cross-item feature co-occurrence structure), not raw feature values. **Direct relevance to this program:** the PDP account and TEM are NOT competing — they answer different halves of the same problem. PDP says generalization needs enough DIVERSE, STRUCTURALLY-OVERLAPPING training examples for a shared representation to differentiate correctly (a DATA/CURRICULUM precondition). TEM says the representation itself must be FACTORED into structure vs. content for that shared learning to transfer to NEW content rather than only new combinations of OLD content (a REPRESENTATION precondition). **Both preconditions are missing from 29556/29557** (see Step 2) — this is not an either/or, it names two separate necessary conditions this design must satisfy jointly.

### 1c. ATL hub-and-spoke (Lambon Ralph et al.) — NEW this drill; important, honest GAP identified

Bilateral, graded anterior temporal lobe acts as an amodal, transmodal convergence "hub" atop modality-specific "spoke" regions (vision, motor, audition, verbal) — patient (semantic dementia), TMS (Pobric, Jefferies, Lambon Ralph, *PNAS* 2007), and RSA/decoding evidence (Lambon Ralph, Sage, Jones, Mayberry, *PNAS* 2010) converge on: the hub computes a genuinely SHARED, cross-modal concept code that supports generalization/graceful degradation, not per-modality lookup — mechanistically realized in the Rogers et al. 2004 PDP model as the deep shared hidden layer forced to map across modality-specific input/output layers (same mechanism as 1b, now anatomically localized). **Honest, explicitly-flagged gap (independently confirmed by this session's lit-scan, which found NO paper bridging these literatures):** the hub-and-spoke program is about amodal integration of FEATURES of a SINGLE concept — it makes no claim about role/relation BINDING or compositional/systematic recombination, and no published work connects ATL hub-and-spoke to TEM/grid-cell factorization or to Fodor-Pylyshyn systematicity. **Implication for this design:** ATL-style amodal convergence is a plausible mechanism for WHERE grounded content `x(concept)` itself might be computed (an amodal fusion of Binder-style experiential-attribute "spokes"), but it is NOT itself a candidate mechanism for the structure/role-binding half of this problem — that role is filled by TEM/grid-cell factorization + Smolensky/LISA/NVSA binding (already covered on 07-18/07-25), not by hub-and-spoke. Do not conflate the two; this note keeps `x` (content, informed by ATL-style amodal integration where grounded) and `g` (structure, TEM-style) as architecturally separate per their separate literatures.

### 1d. Systematicity / binding (carried forward, not re-derived) — the compact citation set

Fodor & Pylyshyn 1988 (the challenge); Smolensky 1990 tensor-product binding (the connectionist reply — fixed algebraic bind, content-agnostic, the direct ancestor of the substrate's native FHRR/HRR bind); Hummel & Holyoak LISA 1997 (temporal-synchrony instantiation, structural-alignment generalization, poor scaling); Hersche et al. NVSA 2022/2023 (*Nat Mach Intell*, arXiv 2203.04571) — **learned perceptual front-end + FIXED symbolic dictionary/algebra**, 87.7%/88.1% on RAVEN/I-RAVEN, the concrete engineering precedent this design copies (structure = fixed algebra + trained invariant code; content = learned front-end); Lake, Linzen & Baroni 2023 (*Nature*, MLC) — the honest counter-data-point that compositional generalization is ALSO achievable via a meta-learning TRAINING REGIME without explicit architectural binding, so if the design below fails, "the lever is curriculum, not architecture" is a live next-drill hypothesis, not a dead end.

## STEP 2 — Diagnosing the memorization gap off our OWN atoms (measured, not recalled)

Both candidate mechanisms already exist on this exact substrate. Reading their VET'd `metrics.json` directly (not from memory):

**29556 = `exp_learned_meaning_frontend_differentiation_v1`.** Mechanism: `concat(item, relation-onehot) -> nonlinear MLP hidden layer -> property`. Content = frozen GloVe/WordNet fused vector (the exact borrowed-embedding anti-pattern this program has since banned as the meaning organ). Result: `ho_lift = 0.0`, VET-confirmed STRUCTURAL (not init-noise). **What it lacks:** total entanglement — there is no separate structural code at all; the hidden layer is free to fit `(item, relation)` idiosyncratically per pair, and gradient descent does exactly that (textbook Fodor-Pylyshyn failure, independently reproduced).

**29557 = `exp_native_binding_compositional_generalization_v1`.** Mechanism: `bind(item_HV, role_HV_r) -> SINGLE LINEAR readout M -> property`, where `role_HV_r` is a **fixed RANDOM vector per relation, drawn once, never trained**. Content = same frozen GloVe/WordNet vector as 29556 (unchanged, held fixed by design). Measured verdict (`data/exp_native_binding_compositional_generalization_v1/metrics.json`): **MIDDLE** — `bind_lift=0.222` (generalizes over frozen) but `bind_over_concat=0.0` (**binding format is not the lever** — a concat+linear ablation matches it exactly) and, most importantly, **`shuffle_sep=0.111`, BELOW the pre-registered 0.15 pass bar** — meaning much of the raw held-out "lift" is majority-class base-rate (the shuffled-label control alone reaches 0.667), and genuine item-specific compositional generalization beyond base rate is thin/borderline, not a clean win. The cell's own honest conclusion: 29556's failure was the MLP's nonlinear entangling hidden layer, not the concat-vs-bind representational format — replacing the hidden layer with a single linear map helped, but the binding operator itself (with an UNTRAINED, random role vector) did none of the work.

**The precise, nameable gap (what NEITHER cell tested):**
1. **No trained structural code.** 29557's `role_HV_r` is random and frozen — it is never subjected to TEM's invariance-across-fillers / distinctness-across-roles contrastive pressure. It is "a fixed tag," not a LEARNED content-blind structural representation. TEM's actual claim requires `g` to be TRAINED across many environments/relation-instances to satisfy those two constraints; nothing in this program has built that training objective yet.
2. **No multi-hop / multi-role composition.** Both cells are single relation-instance property COMPLETION (one bind, one linear readout). TEM's and Smolensky/LISA's systematicity claims are about COMPOSING multiple structural bindings (chaining role-filler pairs across a relational graph) — this program has never composed 2+ binds and asked whether generalization survives composition, which is the sharper and more decisive test.
3. **No held-out-to-NEW-CONCEPT test.** 29556/29557's held-out split withholds (item, relation) COMBINATIONS where the item was still SEEN during training (under some other relation). Neither tests TEM's actual zero-shot claim: a concept **never seen in ANY training example**, introduced only via its relational edges at test time. This is the sharper bar the USER's framing ("generalizing to new concepts") names directly, and it has never been tested on this substrate.
4. **Borrowed content, still.** Both used the frozen GloVe/WordNet vector as content — the exact pattern now banned. This is a confound in both cells' results (any "lift" partially rides on distributional-embedding structure, not a brain-earned representation) that a redesign must remove.

## STEP 2b — Why factorization should transfer where the arc's two partial mechanisms did not (per explicit reinforcement)

The 39-cell grounding arc (`grounding_work_lookback_synthesis_2026-07-26.md`) already tried two brain-aligned mechanisms at the CHAINING problem and both partially worked on a toy domain but face a documented transfer problem. The mechanistic reason factorization is a different bet, not a repeat:

- **`exp_grounding_multihop_sr_reachability_routing_v1` (HARD_PASS on toy, mean out-degree 6.65)** works by AGGREGATING OVER MULTIPLE REDUNDANT PATHS via the resolvent `(I-γT)⁻¹`. Its own follow-on (`research_sr_chaining_arc_reasoning_design_2026-07-26.md`) pre-registers the honest prediction that this collapses at ARC's graph scale (mean degree <1, 22x sparser) because there is no redundancy to aggregate over. **This is a graph-DENSITY precondition, not a representation fix.** Factorized `g` does not depend on path redundancy at all — its generalization lever is cross-ROW contrastive invariance (many rows sharing the same relation-type), which WorldTree supplies abundantly (KINDOF alone has 2,137 rows) independent of how sparse any single node's local neighborhood is.
- **`exp_grounding_rung2_loop_closer_v1` (HARD_FAIL, grounded reach 0.057 < ungrounded 0.085, NEGATIVE lift)** fused Lancaster SENSORIMOTOR norms into a chain-traversal decision for allometry/ecological reasoning — wrong-TYPE grounding for an abstract-relational task, actively adding noise. Factorized `g` does not require grounded content to carry the reasoning signal at all: the entire compositional burden sits on the STRUCTURAL code, and content `x` can be a content-agnostic per-concept tag (see Step 3). Grounded content becomes an optional, ablatable robustness arm, never the load-bearing mechanism — this sidesteps the wrong-type-grounding failure mode by construction, matching the instruction that success must be held-out-to-new-concept generalization, not a borrowed or mismatched content signal.
- **`exp_grounding_learned_sr_heldout_reasoning_v1` (HARD_FAIL_CG_MEMORIZED_SEARCH)** is, on inspection of its own code, POST-HOC SIMILARITY SMOOTHING over a SINGLE entangled per-node vector (char-trigram + InfoNCE binding conflates a node's identity and its relational position into one vector); held-out-node reachability is recovered (or not) by kNN interpolation in that entangled space. This is architecturally the exact ENTANGLEMENT failure this whole note exists to fix — it is not a scaled-up TEM; TEM's `g` is trained to be content-blind BY CONSTRUCTION via an explicit contrastive objective, not hoped-for as an emergent side effect of a similarity-training loss built for a different purpose.
- **`exp_grounding_gated_fusion_relation_inference_mammal_v1` (HARD_PASS on toy, untested at scale)** learns to ARBITRATE between two ALREADY-COMPUTED channels. Blending two entangled signals cannot manufacture structural generalization that neither channel possesses internally — it is a downstream patch, not an upstream representation fix. Factorized `g` attacks the representation itself, upstream of any blending step.

**Net argument:** none of the four prior mechanisms ever built a content-blind structural code trained via an explicit invariance objective across many relation instances. Each instead (a) exploited path-redundancy (density-dependent, doesn't generalize to sparse target domains), (b) fused a content signal that was the wrong type for the task, (c) hoped an entangled similarity-trained encoder would happen to generalize via smoothing, or (d) arbitrated between two such channels. This design tests the one lever none of them tested. This is an argument for why the bet is DIFFERENT, not a claim that it is more likely to win — see the deflated P below, which weighs this argument against two direct on-substrate priors (29556, 29557) already showing this general architecture family struggles here.

## STEP 3 — Pre-registered can-fail cell design (design only — NOT built or dispatched)

**Proposed anchor name:** `exp_grounding_tem_factorized_heldout_concept_v1` (for `hdi_exp_dev` to author).

**Substrate:** REAL WorldTree typed-relation tables (`data/corpora/worldtree/WorldtreeExplanationCorpusV2.1_Feb2020/tablestore/v2.1/tables/`) — 81 relation tables, ~9,700 rows verified this session (`KINDOF` 2,137 rows, `PARTOF` 236, `CAUSE` 381, `HABITAT` 45, `PREDATOR-PREY` 14, plus dozens of `PROP-*` attribute tables), spanning thousands of science-domain concepts. This is both denser in ROW-COUNT-PER-RELATION-TYPE (the precondition factorization needs) and richer in RELATION-TYPE DIVERSITY than either the toy ConceptNet-allometry domain (single domain) or the 233-rule/225-node ARC derivation graph (mean degree <1, too sparse and too few rows-per-type for any contrastive invariance training) used in prior cells — a genuinely new, better-suited data substrate for this specific test, not a re-run.

**Reuse, verbatim, no reinvention:** `hdlab/binding.py::bind`/`unbind` (HRR circular convolution) or `bsc_bind`/`bsc_unbind` (bipolar elementwise, self-inverse) — pick whichever matches content dtype, do not add a third representation; `experiments/exp_teacher_free_relational_encoder_cn_subgraph_v1.py`'s `char_trigram_features`/`ProjHead`/`info_nce`/`vicreg_repulsion` training harness pattern (reused for the FLAT and SINGLE-HOP-BIND baseline reproductions); the gated-fusion cell's disjoint-split/grid-search convention (reused for any lambda-style diagnostics); `data/corpora/binder/binder2016_ratings.csv` (66 experiential-attribute Binder norms) as the ONLY candidate grounded-content source, per the standing anti-borrowed-embedding discipline — NEVER GloVe/word2vec/BERT/any distributional embedding as content.

### Mechanism (glass-box, two representations kept structurally separate from the start)

1. **Content `x(concept)`** — two arms, tested as a robustness ablation (never the primary lever):
   - **RANDOM-ID (primary/decisive arm):** a fixed random unit vector per concept, zero pretrained semantics. This is the purest test — any held-out success here is attributable ONLY to the structural code, immune to the "content-similarity shortcut" critique that would otherwise cloud interpretation.
   - **BINDER-GROUNDED (secondary/diagnostic arm):** Binder-2016 experiential-attribute vector for concepts with coverage; RANDOM-ID fallback for concepts without (realistic partial coverage — WorldTree's science vocabulary is broader than Binder's 535-concept core). Pre-registered EXPECTATION, not a forced win: per the arc's own finding that sensorimotor/experiential grounding was often redundant-or-wrong-type for abstract relational reasoning, and per this session's precedent (`exp_native_meaning_encoder_binder_grounded_v1`: relations-only signal DOES generalize toward Binder features, `p@10=0.139` vs untrained floor `0.033`, but far weaker than distributional context `0.243`), this arm may show LITTLE OR NO improvement over RANDOM-ID — that would be an honest, informative, expected result (content richness is not the lever; structure is), not a failed test.
2. **Structural code `g(relation_type, arg_slot)`** — a small learned embedding/encoder over (relation-type name, argument-slot index only — e.g. "KINDOF slot-0 (hyponym)" vs "KINDOF slot-1 (hypernym)"; "PARTOF slot-0 (part)" vs "PARTOF slot-1 (whole)") — **NEVER conditioned on filler identity**. Trained via a contrastive objective across the full ~9,700-row corpus: (i) **invariance** — pull together `g` for the SAME (relation, slot) across different rows/fillers; (ii) **distinctness** — push apart `g` across different (relation, slot) pairs. This is TEM's two constraints, translated to relational-table structure exactly as already specified in `research_brain_compositional_role_binding_structure_content_factorization_2026-07-18.md` Section 5.
3. **Bind:** `p = bind(g(relation, slot), x(concept))` via the substrate's native FHRR/HRR operator.
4. **Store/query:** per fact `(relation, arg0=A, arg1=B)`, store the association between `bind(g(relation,slot0), x(A))` and `bind(g(relation,slot1), x(B))` (Hebbian associative matrix, TEM's `M` analog — the substrate's existing bind/unbind/cleanup primitives, no new retrieval mechanism). Query: given `bind(g(relation,slot1), x_query)`, unbind against the known-concept dictionary (nearest-neighbor cleanup) to retrieve the predicted other argument.
5. **Multi-hop / multi-role composition (the explicit untested gap from 29557):** chain 2+ such retrievals across DIFFERENT relation types — e.g. `X --KINDOF--> Y`, then `Y --PARTOF--> Z` — where hop 2's query content is hop 1's RETRIEVED (possibly held-out/never-directly-seen) concept. Success requires factorization to survive composition across multiple bind operations and multiple roles, the sharper systematicity test neither prior cell attempted.

### Primary metric — TWO held-out splits (both required per the USER's explicit framing)

- **Split A (primary, decisive): HELD-OUT NEW CONCEPTS.** A set of concepts entirely excluded from `g`-training's contrastive sampling (their `x` vector, whether random-ID or Binder, is never used as a positive/negative during `g` training) — introduced ONLY at test time via their content vector plus the relation edges naming them. Success = correct retrieval using the FROZEN, already-trained `g`-machinery bound to genuinely novel content — TEM's exact "new environment, same g" test.
- **Split B (secondary, COGS/SCAN-style): HELD-OUT ROLE-COMBINATIONS.** A concept seen during training in SOME relation types is withheld specifically from ONE relation-type/slot (e.g. a concept seen in `CAUSE` and `PROP-GENERIC` during training but withheld from `KINDOF`).

Reported metric: top-1 (and top-10) nearest-neighbor-cleanup retrieval accuracy on 2+-hop chains where AT LEAST one hop's argument is held-out under Split A or Split B, against a full concept dictionary that includes held-out concepts as valid answers.

### Arms (paired; identical corpus + identical splits + identical seeds; only the encoder/mechanism differs)

- **FLAT** — reproduces 29556's mechanism (`concat(x, relation-onehot) -> nonlinear MLP -> predicted-arg`) on WorldTree data. Expected per precedent: `ho_lift ~ 0` (memorizes). The baseline to beat.
- **SINGLE-HOP-RANDOM-BIND** — reproduces 29557's exact mechanism (`bind(x, role_HV_r FIXED-RANDOM) -> single linear M`) on WorldTree data, single-hop only. Expected per precedent: thin/borderline lift, most of it base-rate (mirrors `shuffle_sep=0.111 < 0.15`).
- **FACTORIZED-g (primary CG candidate)** — the TEM-style trained-invariant `g`, native bind, chained across 2+ hops, evaluated on Splits A and B.
- **Must-fail controls (all four required; genuine structure must collapse each):**
  - `SHUFFLED_STRUCTURE` — randomly permute which concept fills which slot across facts (destroys real relational structure, preserves marginals).
  - `SCRAMBLED_ROLES` — randomly permute relation-type labels across rows (trains `g` against the WRONG identity).
  - `RANDOM_G` (necessity control, reused pattern from `exp_grounding_learned_sr_heldout_reasoning_v1`'s CODEALIAS arm) — identical bind/retrieval pipeline, but `g` is randomized per relation-instance instead of trained-invariant, isolating whether LEARNED invariance (not merely having some tag) is doing the work.
  - `CONTENT_SCRAMBLED` — `x(concept)` vectors randomly permuted across concept identities post-hoc (must collapse retrieval to chance — sanity check that content still carries identity).

### Pre-registered bands

**HARD-PASS:** `FACTORIZED-g`'s held-out top-1 accuracy (pooled across Splits A+B, on 2+-hop chains) clears FLAT by >= 15-20 percentage points (matching the 07-18 note's convention) AND clears `SINGLE-HOP-RANDOM-BIND` by a clear, separately-reported margin (isolating that TRAINED invariance — not binding format alone, which 29557 already showed is not sufficient — is the active ingredient) AND all four must-fail controls collapse to within noise of the chance/base-rate floor AND a positive learning curve: held-out accuracy improves as the number of distinct relation-type ROWS used in `g`-training increases (per Saxe et al. 2019's formal data-diversity-drives-differentiation account, Section 1b) — a flat or non-improving curve with more data is itself disqualifying even if the point estimate clears the bar.

**HARD-FAIL:** `FACTORIZED-g` ties FLAT and/or `SINGLE-HOP-RANDOM-BIND` on held-out (within ~5 points) — meaning content-blind structural factorization, even trained via explicit invariance and composed over multiple hops on real, denser, multi-relation-type data, still does not clear the memorization wall; OR any must-fail control fails to collapse (construction/leakage bug — respec, do not report as a mechanism refutation); OR the learning curve is flat/non-monotonic with more relation-type diversity (would contradict the diversity-generalization account and point to a different, unidentified bottleneck entirely, worth reporting as its own finding).

**MIDDLE_BAND:** `FACTORIZED-g` clears FLAT by 5-15 points with controls holding, or clears the accuracy bar but the Binder-grounded content arm shows no improvement over random-ID (an expected, informative, non-disqualifying outcome per Step 3.1's pre-registered expectation) — report explicitly as "structure carries the generalization, content-richness does not," which would itself be a clean, useful, product-relevant finding distinguishing this design from the arc's grounding-heavy priors.

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS prediction:** FACTORIZED-g's pooled held-out top-1 accuracy on 2+-hop chains (Splits A+B) exceeds FLAT by >=15-20pp and SINGLE-HOP-RANDOM-BIND by a clear separately-reported margin; all four must-fail controls sit within noise of chance; accuracy rises monotonically with relation-type-row diversity used in `g`-training; the RANDOM-ID content arm alone is sufficient to clear the bar (Binder-grounded content need not add anything, consistent with Step 2b's argument that structure, not content richness, is this design's lever).

**HARD-FAIL prediction (stated honestly in advance, given TWO direct priors — 29556 and 29557 — of this general architecture family struggling on this exact substrate, and TEM's own literature gap confirmed independently by both this session's lit-scans):** FACTORIZED-g ties or only marginally beats (within ~5-10pp) both FLAT and SINGLE-HOP-RANDOM-BIND on held-out new concepts specifically (Split A, the sharper bar) — meaning trained cross-row invariance, even done "correctly" per TEM's own recipe, does not by itself manufacture zero-shot content generalization when content is itself minimally informative (random-ID) and the corpus, while denser than ARC's rule graph, is still far smaller and noisier than TEM's original many-hundred-trial training regime. If this occurs, the two live next-drill candidates per the biology (Section 1) are: (a) Lake-Linzen-Baroni meta-learning-for-compositionality (a TRAINING-REGIME lever, not architecture) — genuinely untested in this program; (b) the PDP/Saxe diversity account implies the corpus itself may be too small/insufficiently hierarchical for the invariance objective to converge — worth a scaling/diversity-curve diagnostic (already built into the design above) before concluding the mechanism itself is wrong.
