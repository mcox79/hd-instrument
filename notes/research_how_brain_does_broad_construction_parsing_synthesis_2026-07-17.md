## THE BRAIN'S BROAD PARSER = ONE INTEGRATED MECHANISM (3 parts)

1. **WHAT it is -- a large LEARNED CONSTRUCTION INVENTORY (lane 1, Construction Grammar).** All linguistic knowledge = CONSTRUCTIONS (form-MEANING pairs) at every grain size: morpheme -> word -> idiom -> argument-structure schema (ditransitive/caused-motion) -> abstract schema (passive/relative). NO sharp lexicon/grammar divide -- a CONTINUUM from idiosyncratic idioms to general schemas. Broad coverage = a LARGE inventory (order low-thousands; NO hard number exists -- honestly flagged; Russian Constructicon ~2,200 as an order-of-mag anchor, NOT a per-speaker measurement). Syntax itself carries meaning (Goldberg).
2. **HOW it's acquired -- USAGE-BASED, GROWN FROM EXPOSURE (lane 1, Tomasello).** Item-based/verb-island first -> abstracted to general schemas via analogy + distributional analysis. Entrenchment + statistical preemption (empirically dissociable) do the retreat-from-overgeneralization. CONTINUOUSLY UPDATED across life: structural priming = implicit ERROR-DRIVEN LEARNING (weight changes, not transient activation). Productivity (novel-verb "pilk" studies) proves it's real schemas, not rote strings.
3. **HOW it's used -- INCREMENTAL + PREDICTIVE + BIDIRECTIONAL FEEDBACK (lane 2).** Surprisal theory (Hale 2001/Levy 2008): difficulty = -log P(word | context) over a distribution of PARSES/constructions (a probabilistic GRAMMAR, not n-grams). Surprisal is a "causal bottleneck" -- frequency, plausibility, discourse, memory all act by changing next-STRUCTURE probabilities. Prediction is GENERATIVE pre-activation (DeLong a/an; Kuperberg&Jaeger multi-level), NOT after-the-fact scoring. TOP-DOWN BIDIRECTIONAL FEEDBACK (predictive coding: predictions flow DOWN, errors propagate UP; neuroimaging confirms level-specific top-down suppression + IFC error-enhancement) -- feedback pre-narrows the ambiguity search space before the ambiguous word arrives, then error drives TARGETED reanalysis (not from-scratch reparse). Predictive coding EMERGES from ENERGY-EFFICIENCY optimization (only propagate error; signaling ~75% of brain energy). Kuperberg&Jaeger + Bornkessel-Schlesewsky (active inference): inventory + prediction + feedback = ONE integrated probabilistic-inference architecture, not 3 bolt-ons.

## ENGINEERING CONVERGENCE (lane 3): does a "classical parser" == the brain's learned inventory?
- **PLAIN TREEBANK PCFG != the brain's grown inventory** -- it is "INSTALLED, not grown": categories/bracketing are FIXED by expert-designed annotation guidelines (Penn Treebank), the "learning" is just frequency-fitting inside a pre-designed hypothesis space ("linguistic intuition is externalized into the annotations"). AND it is SYNTAX-ONLY (no lexical/semantic content) -- conflicts with CxG form-MEANING. Raw treebank PCFG ~50-60% F (too coarse).
- **DOP (Data-Oriented Parsing, Bod) = the CLOSEST single system + an EXPLICIT convergence.** Bod HIMSELF frames DOP as a computational instantiation of usage-based/constructivist theory ("Beyond Grammar: an Experience-based Theory"; "rules and exemplars are endpoints of the same distribution"; simulates child item-based->abstract development). Keeps variable-size LEXICALLY-FILLED fragments = structurally much closer to "constructions" than PCFG rules. = the strongest convergence claim in the literature (his own thesis).
- **UNSUPERVISED grammar induction (CCM Klein&Manning; compound-PCFG; Dunn "Computational Learning of Construction Grammars") = closest analog to GROW-FROM-RAW-EXPOSURE** (no pre-imposed tagset; anti-poverty-of-stimulus -- constructions learnable from distributional stats).
- **FCG / SBCG = real GLASS-BOX CxG parsers** (unification over typed feature structures, integrated form-MEANING, BIDIRECTIONAL parse+produce, no neural). FCG: ~35k lexical + ~40 grammatical constructions. But largely HAND-authored, not learned-from-corpus-at-scale.
- No single system does all 3 (unsupervised-induce + variable-grain-lexical-fragments + integrated-form-meaning); DOP is the nearest.
