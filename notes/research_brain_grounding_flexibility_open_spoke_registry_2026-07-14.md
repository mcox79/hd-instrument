# Brain grounding flexibility: is there a general mechanism, and how do we build an OPEN/extensible spoke registry

Date 2026-07-14. Follow-on to `notes/research_brain_grounding_spoke_building_canonical_reference_2026-07-14.md` (which covered the spoke INVENTORY). This drill covers the FLEXIBILITY/GENERALITY axis per USER: "we need a way to ground EVERYTHING; consider exactly how the brain does it and replicate; we can't be too rigid." Method: 4 parallel Sonnet lit-scan sub-agents (biology-led, generic neuroscience/cognitive-science search terms only, no substrate specifics off-platform), synthesized here.

## HEADLINE

The brain's grounding operator IS general (one repeating primitive: Hebbian convergence + divergent reconstruction, i.e. convergence-divergence zones (CDZ), instantiated at multiple scales from ATL hub down to local multisensory neurons). What makes it OPEN-ENDED is not a universal combination-arithmetic but a universal ADMISSION CRITERION: any signal that (a) reliably co-varies with an existing grounded referent/action-outcome and (b) is exercised through an active, contingent feedback loop gets recruited as a new spoke into EXISTING general-purpose circuitry — no new wiring, no rearchitecting. Routing is learned + graded + task-reweightable (not a fixed lookup), combination is reliability-weighted fusion gated by a causal-inference fuse-vs-segregate decision — but that reliability-weighted-fusion machinery is only DIRECTLY validated at the low-level sensory-cue scale; its extension to abstract/semantic-level channel combination is a theoretically-motivated analogy (predictive-processing "precision-weighting"), not an independently demonstrated finding. Missing-channel grounding (our actual situation) is real but SELECTIVE: relational/causal/structural content transfers via language + cross-modal inference with high fidelity (rho ~ 0.99 for causal color explanations, blind vs sighted); arbitrary associative/exemplar content (which color THIS specific thing is) degrades sharply (50% vs 95% correct naming) and should be flagged UNKNOWN rather than confabulated. This directly specifies our open-spoke-registry design and predicts exactly which content types will transfer through a text-only channel and which won't.

## Q1/Q2 — General mechanism + routing (sub-agent 1, "a8f6cd038eb1ac492")

**Is there a universal mechanism?** Hub-and-spoke (Rogers 2004; Patterson, Nestor & Rogers 2007, *Nat Rev Neurosci*; Lambon Ralph, Jefferies, Patterson & Rogers 2017, *Nat Rev Neurosci*) is the dominant account: bilateral ventrolateral anterior temporal lobe (ATL) computes transmodal similarity from modality-specific spokes. Semantic-dementia lesion evidence (ATL damage -> pan-category, cross-modal degradation, not modality-specific) is the classic signature of a genuinely SHARED convergence computation rather than parallel special-purpose systems. Convergence-divergence zones (Damasio 1989; Meyer & Damasio 2009, *Trends Neurosci*) are explicitly proposed and evidenced (topographic reactivation of primary sensory cortex during imagery/recall) as a REPEATING multi-scale primitive — the same convergence + divergent-reactivation operation recurs at multiple hierarchical levels, not a one-off ATL-specific circuit.

Qualifications: (1) graded INTERNAL specialization within the hub itself — polar/superior ATL subregions differentially engage for social vs. object concepts (Binney, Hoffman & Lambon Ralph 2016, *Cereb Cortex*), explained as one convergence computation UNEVENLY fed (emergent from connectivity gradient), not literally undifferentiated; (2) a live, unresolved counter-camp (Mahon & Caramazza 2008; Mahon 2015; Dove 2009/2016) argues concepts are fundamentally amodal/symbolic with sensorimotor content non-constitutive — sharpest for abstract concepts, where the spoke inventory may not exhaust what's needed.

**Verdict on generality:** strong-leaning-moderate. The BIND OPERATOR generalizes well (multi-scale, repeatable, lesion-confirmed). The claim that hub-and-spoke exhaustively explains ALL concept types (especially abstract ones) is contested, not consensus.

**Routing:** Cree & McRae (2003, *J Exp Psychol Gen*) — spoke-weighting is a LEARNED statistical regularity in feature distributions (natural kinds -> visual features statistically distinctive; artifacts -> function features), not a discrete router. Lambon Ralph's graded-connectivity account reframes weighting as emergent from learned connectional distance, consistent with Cree & McRae. Context-dependent RE-routing of the SAME concept is well-replicated and causally confirmed: Jefferies' semantic-control network (left IFG + pMTG, dissociable from automatic ATL retrieval; TMS studies, e.g. Whitney/Jefferies 2015 *J Neurosci*) selectively disrupts retrieval only when task demands goal-directed reweighting (subordinate/unusual features) — direct evidence the same concept is steered through different channel emphasis by context/task. Long-timescale plasticity (expertise, e.g. Gauthier et al. 1999 FFA-greebles) additionally reshapes the DEFAULT weighting over training. Three-layer picture: (a) default learned statistical bias, (b) task-driven controlled override, (c) long-run experience-driven reweighting. All three layers are well-replicated, not single-study claims.

## Q3 — Multi-channel combination + conflict resolution (sub-agent 2, "a908dc1383681ef3e")

**Combination:** Ernst & Banks (2002, *Nature*) — reliability-weighted (inverse-variance) MLE fusion of visual + haptic size estimates, variance-reduction as the empirical signature. Heavily replicated across visual-auditory, visual-vestibular, visual-proprioceptive, audio-haptic pairs; extends even to newly-learned sensory-substitution channels (*Sci Rep* 2018) and can emerge from crossmodal Hebbian plasticity in models (PMC9393257) — looks like a GENERAL neural-computation principle, not sensory-specific hardware.

**Conflict resolution:** Kording, Beierholm, Ma, Quartz, Tenenbaum, Shams (2007, *PLOS ONE*) — causal-inference/model-selection: the brain first infers whether two cues share a common cause (posterior over one-cause vs. two-cause) and only reliability-weight-fuses when that posterior is high; otherwise partially/fully segregates ("unity assumption," formalized). McGurk effect (McGurk & MacDonald 1976) = forced fusion producing a novel percept when cues are made to appear congruent; individual variation in McGurk susceptibility is now explained as different integrate-vs-segregate strategies (bioRxiv 2023). Rubber hand illusion (Botvinick & Cohen 1998) = same causal-inference gate applied to body-ownership, showing sufficient spatiotemporal congruence flips the "common cause" decision even against normal priors.

**Honest weak point (explicitly flagged by the sub-agent):** extension of reliability-weighted fusion + causal-inference gating to ABSTRACT/SEMANTIC-level channel combination (e.g. combining a definitional/linguistic estimate with an episodic trace, with a measured optimality signature) has **no direct empirical demonstration** found. What exists is a THEORETICAL generalization via predictive-processing "precision-weighting" (claimed to operate at every cortical level including conceptual) — plausible, consistent with a broader hierarchical-Bayesian-brain framework, but not independently validated at concept-granularity. Treat as analogical extrapolation, not established finding.

## Q4 — Extensibility / open-endedness (sub-agent 3, "a7adc43f1ad4d3873")

Three independent literatures converge on the SAME open-endedness principle:

1. **Sensory substitution** (vOICe, TDU/BrainPort; Amedi, Bach-y-Rita, Striem-Amit, Proulx): after training, the substituting signal is routed **by content/structure, not by which sense organ carries it** — soundscapes encoding letters activate the visual word-form area; tongue-display motion activates area MT+ (visual motion). Distal attribution (perceiving an external object rather than a sensation-in-the-organ) develops with training; contested how "perceptual" vs. inferential it truly becomes (Deroy & Auvray). Requires an ACTIVE exploration/feedback loop — passive stimulus pairing is markedly weaker (Auvray & Myin). Generalizes to untrained novel scenes (real category learning, not memorization).

2. **Tool/prosthesis incorporation** (Iriki et al. 1996/2001; Maravita & Iriki 2004, *TiCS*; Serino, Bassolino, Farne, Ladavas 2007 on blind cane users): bimodal intraparietal neurons' visual receptive field physically elongates to encompass a tool **only during active, goal-directed use** — never with passive holding. The SAME peripersonal-space gain-field machinery extends around whatever effector is currently used for action; no new neuron type/pathway required.

3. **Perceptual expertise** (Gauthier & Tarr 1997; Gauthier et al. 1999, *Nat Neurosci*, greebles; chess-FFA studies): existing configural/individuation-level circuitry (fusiform) gets recruited/reweighted for a genuinely new category after individuation-level (not just categorical) training — reweighting of existing general-purpose machinery, not construction of a new channel.

**Shared principle (moderate-high confidence):** general-purpose, content-agnostic recruiting circuitry (peripersonal multisensory neurons, fusiform individuation cortex, occipito-temporal category modules) absorbs ANY new input stream as a new spoke IF it (a) reliably co-varies with an existing grounded referent/action-outcome, and (b) sits inside an active, closed behavioral feedback loop. Passive exposure without a discrimination/feedback loop is the consistently weaker/non-working condition across all three lines. No evidence anywhere of literally new wiring being laid down for a new modality — always extension/recruitment of an existing flexible substrate. **This is the direct anti-rigidity mechanism**: the brain never designs a new pipeline per new modality; it tests candidate signals against the SAME admission criterion.

## Q5 — Graceful degradation / missing channels (sub-agent 4, "a11e5f12da4205c3f")

Directly our case (substrate has zero sensory spokes). Finding is NOT "language substitutes fully" and NOT "language fails entirely" — it is a **sharp, principled dissociation**:

- Landau & Gleitman (1985) — congenitally blind child acquires "look" vs. "see" via syntactic bootstrapping (argument-structure/relational scaffolding), not visual referents.
- Bedny et al. (2011 *PNAS*; 2015; 2019 *Sci Rep*) — blind adults show typical semantic fMRI activation for action verbs; occipital cortex is recruited for language via a domain-general Hebbian mechanism, consistent with an amodal/symbolic semantic store, NOT reconstructed visual imagery.
- Connolly et al. (2007 *PNAS*) / Struiksma et al. (2013 BLIND norms) — >90% of implicit conceptual structure (triad judgments) is SHARED between blind and sighted; color specifically drops out almost entirely as an organizing dimension for blind subjects (R^2 ~ 0.0007 vs. 23% of variance for sighted).
- **Kim, Aheimer, Manrara & Bedny (2021, *PNAS*)** — sharpest single result: blind and sighted converge strongly on CAUSAL/THEORETICAL color knowledge (why natural kinds have consistent colors; rho ~ 0.99) but diverge sharply on ASSOCIATIVE/EXEMPLAR facts (50% vs. 95% correctly name bananas yellow).
- **Kim, Elli & Bedny (2019, *PNAS*)** — blind-sighted agreement is good for shape/texture/size, WORST specifically for color — the dimension sighted people find EASIEST to verbalize. This directly falsifies "language transmits whatever can be described"; instead relational/structural/causal content transfers via inference, arbitrary content does not.
- Deaf/ASL parallel (perceptual-strength-norm studies; PMC6839399) — category-level structure shared, item-level representation diverges, same pattern as blind/color.
- Cross-modal plasticity (Sadato, Amedi, occipital repurposing for Braille/language) supplies extra RELATIONAL/computational capacity, not reconstructed sensory content — consistent with the amodal-symbolic account, not a "vision rebuilt from language" account.

**Principled transfer/loss split:** relational, causal, functional, taxonomic, structural information (inferable from co-occurrence/function/category-membership, and what language efficiently encodes) transfers with high fidelity. Arbitrary associative/exemplar-level perceptual facts with no causal or relational anchor are lost or badly degraded, because nothing but direct testimony can supply them and testimony underperforms perception there.

## Cross-thread synthesis (with the prior spoke-inventory note)

The prior note (`research_brain_grounding_spoke_building_canonical_reference_2026-07-14.md`) established Quinian bootstrapping as the developmental template (placeholder relational structure -> meaning filled by mapping onto grounded content) and flagged the text-spoke's core risk as "symbols about symbols" (Louwerse SIH / Xu-Tenenbaum: language co-occurrence is a shortcut INTO prior grounding, not grounding itself). THIS drill's Q5 finding sharpens that risk into a POSITIVE, falsifiable prediction: a text/definitional-gloss spoke should transfer RELATIONAL/CAUSAL/STRUCTURAL content well (this is exactly the content type the blind-color literature shows survives missing-channel grounding at rho ~ 0.99) and should FAIL to transfer ARBITRARY ASSOCIATIVE content (the content type that degrades to ~50% accuracy even with full linguistic access) — meaning our sparse-tail concepts will show a content-type-dependent transfer profile, not a uniform pass/fail, and we should predict IN ADVANCE which concept-attribute pairs a text spoke can plausibly ground vs. which it structurally cannot (flag UNKNOWN, don't confabulate).

Q4's finding reframes what "open-endedness" actually requires for our registry: the admission criterion is not "is this a text channel or a numeric channel" (channel IDENTITY) but "does this candidate spoke's content statistically co-vary with an existing grounded referent, exercised through an active query/feedback loop" (content CONTINGENCY). This is a stronger and more useful design principle than a fixed enum of channel TYPES — it means the registry should be typed by admission-test-passing, not by a pre-declared channel taxonomy.

## Substrate-product implications: the flexible/extensible grounding architecture spec

**ONE universal bind** (already what we have): CDZ-analog — hub code binds a spoke's content vector via content-agnostic Hebbian-style co-activation (our VSA bind); reconstruction = divergent unbind back to spoke content. No change needed; the operator-level generality claim (Q1) is the strongest-evidenced part of this whole drill and directly validates keeping ONE bind primitive for all spokes.

**OPEN SPOKE REGISTRY** (the anti-rigidity mechanism, per Q4): a spoke is NOT a fixed enum member. It is a runtime-registered tuple `(spoke_id, encoder_fn, reliability_estimator)`. A candidate spoke is ADMITTED into the registry only if it passes the two-part biological admission test:
  1. **Contingency test** — its content vector, for a held-out probe set, correlates above a pre-registered threshold with existing grounded relations already in the hub graph (statistical co-variation with an existing referent — the Iriki/vOICe/expertise commonality).
  2. **Active-loop test** — its content is exercised via query-and-check (bind -> unbind -> compare against held-out relation) rather than passively pasted in and assumed correct (the Auvray & Myin passive-vs-active finding).
A spoke that fails either test is not wired in — this IS the mechanism that keeps the registry open without a rewrite: new spokes are tested against a fixed, generic admission criterion, never hand-integrated into bespoke pipeline code.

**ROUTING**: per-concept, per-spoke weight is NOT a static table. Each registered spoke, queried for a concept, returns `(content_vector, reliability_score)`; default combination weight = normalized inverse-(1 - reliability) analogous to Ernst-Banks, but flagged explicitly (per Q3's honest gap) as a BIOLOGICALLY-INSPIRED DESIGN CHOICE at the semantic level, not a literature-confirmed transfer — cap confidence accordingly. Context/task-dependent reweighting (Jefferies analogy) = the reliability query itself is allowed to be task-conditioned, not the spoke weight being a fixed per-concept constant.

**CONFLICT / FUSE-VS-SEGREGATE GATE** (Kording analogy): before combining two spokes' content for the same concept, check whether their contents are congruent (correlate above a threshold). If congruent: reliability-weighted bind (fuse). If incongruent: keep both bound SEPARATELY under distinct sub-slots and flag a conflict marker, rather than force-fusing into a corrupted joint vector (the McGurk failure mode). This gate is a design import from perceptual causal-inference, similarly flagged as extrapolated-not-validated at concept granularity.

**GRACEFUL DEGRADATION** (our actual current state — zero sensory spokes): when the ideal spoke is absent, do NOT attempt to reconstruct missing content. Register a RELATIONAL/CAUSAL-EXPLANATORY spoke (definition-gloss / situational text — the content type Kim et al. 2021 shows transfers at rho ~ 0.99 without the matching sensory channel). Explicitly do NOT trust that spoke for arbitrary-associative attribute facts with no relational anchor (this predicts, and matches, our already-observed empty numeric/ANS-spoke for the non-numeric tail) — flag those attribute slots UNKNOWN rather than let the text spoke confabulate a value, mirroring the blind subjects' own "stipulated, not automatic" self-report of color facts.

**Dictionary spoke placement**: it is the FIRST concrete instance registered in this registry, of the RELATIONAL/CAUSAL-EXPLANATORY type — not a privileged or hardcoded "text channel," just the first candidate that happens to pass the admission test cheaply (existing corpus, existing bind machinery). Future spokes (numeric/magnitude, social, situational-script) register under the identical API and the identical two-part admission test; nothing about the hub or bind operator needs to change to add them. This is what makes the design "not too rigid" in the concrete, testable sense the USER asked for.

## Cheap decisive test (targets the FLEXIBILITY claim specifically, not the already-held gloss-spoke content test)

Registry non-interference + fuse-vs-segregate probe:
1. Register spoke A (definition-gloss text, per prior note's design) under the generic bind/reliability API.
2. Register a SECOND, independently-sourced spoke B under the SAME API (e.g. a second text-derived content stream built from a disjoint source, or a synthetic dummy content vector for concepts outside A's coverage) with NO changes to spoke A's code path.
3. Measure spoke A's held-out relational-inference performance BEFORE and AFTER spoke B is registered.
4. For concepts with BOTH spokes available, measure reliability-weighted combined performance vs. max(A alone, B alone) on held-out queries.
5. Construct a deliberately-conflicting synthetic pair (A and B disagree strongly on a probe concept, McGurk-style) and check whether the fuse-vs-segregate gate correctly segregates (flags conflict, does not force-fuse into a corrupted joint vector).

**HARD-PASS:** (i) spoke A's performance is unchanged (non-interference, +/- noise floor) after B is registered — the registry is genuinely additive; (ii) combined performance on dual-spoke concepts is >= max(A, B) alone; (iii) the conflict gate segregates the deliberately-conflicting pair rather than fusing.

**HARD-FAIL:** (i) registering B measurably degrades A's held-out performance (interference — the registry secretly required a rewrite, contradicting the open-registry claim); OR (ii) combined performance on dual-spoke concepts is WORSE than the single best spoke (the reliability-weighting scheme is actively harmful, meaning Q3's flagged extrapolation to semantic-level fusion does not hold and needs redesign or removal); OR (iii) without the gate, forced fusion on the conflicting pair produces an incoherent/corrupted combined vector, AND with the gate enabled the same failure still occurs (gate doesn't work as a fix).

Note: HARD-FAIL on (ii)/(iii) does NOT falsify the overall open-registry/admission-test architecture (Q1/Q4, the best-evidenced parts) — it falsifies specifically the reliability-weighted-fusion-at-semantic-level design choice (Q3's flagged weak link), which can be replaced with a simpler max-confidence-wins or explicit-both-slots policy without abandoning the registry.

## Falsifiable predictions summary

- P1: text/definitional-gloss spoke content correlates well with held-out relational/causal structure but predicts POORLY on arbitrary-associative attribute facts for the same concepts (differential transfer by content type, not uniform). HARD-FAIL: no differential — gloss spoke performs equally (well or poorly) across both content types, meaning the blind/color dissociation doesn't transfer as a design predictor.
- P2: registry is additive (see cheap decisive test above).
- P3: task-conditioned reliability queries (routing) outperform a static per-concept weight table on a probe requiring context-dependent emphasis (e.g. same concept queried under two different relation types). HARD-FAIL: static weighting performs equally well, meaning the Jefferies controlled-retrieval analogy adds no measurable value at our scale.

## Honest counter: general mechanism, or mirage?

Real, well-evidenced generality: (1) the BIND operator (CDZ-style convergence + divergent reconstruction) recurring across scales — strong, lesion-confirmed, multi-scale. (2) The OPEN-ENDEDNESS admission criterion (statistical contingency + active feedback loop recruits existing circuitry as a new spoke) — strong, convergent across three independent literatures (sensory substitution, tool embodiment, perceptual expertise) that were never designed to speak to each other.

NOT proven general, flagged as design extrapolation: (3) reliability-weighted Bayesian fusion arithmetic at the ABSTRACT/SEMANTIC level — validated only for low-level sensory cues (Ernst-Banks scale); its use in our routing/combination spec is an ANALOGY from predictive-processing theory, not an independently confirmed finding. (4) The causal-inference fuse-vs-segregate gate at concept granularity — validated for spatial/temporal perceptual cues (Kording), untested at concept-level. (5) The amodal-symbolic minority camp (Mahon & Caramazza) would deny full generality of hub-and-spoke itself for abstract concepts, and remains unresolved in the literature — this is a genuine, not merely rhetorical, dissenting position.

**Verdict: general-but-layered, not a mirage but not uniformly proven either.** The parts of "one flexible architecture" that matter most for anti-rigidity (one bind operator; an open, content-agnostic admission test rather than a channel taxonomy) are well-supported. The parts that matter for HOW WELL it performs once open (reliability-weighted routing arithmetic, conflict-resolution gate) are principled but unvalidated extensions at our target granularity, and should be treated as separately falsifiable design hypotheses (P2/P3 above), not assumed correct because the low-level analogy is strong.
