# research: value-based ACTION-SELECTION (basal-ganglia Go/NoGo) — brain-grounding for the Stage-4 gap — 2026-07-08

## HEADLINE

**The mechanism is not a gap — it is already built and HARD_PASS-certified.** `experiments/exp_pfc_gate_cfrpe_trained_v2.py` (2026-07-05, `data/exp_pfc_gate_cfrpe_trained_v2/metrics.json`, verdict `HARD_PASS`) is, primitive-for-primitive, a basal-ganglia Go/NoGo action-selection gate: a learned successor-representation (SR) value signal (`reach`), trained end-to-end by a TD-error that is literally the substrate's reward-prediction-error (RPE) delta-rule (`cfrpe`), feeds a winner-take-all actor (`Go_i = w_manifold*manifold_i + w_goal*goal_sim_i + w_reach*reach_i`; `gate = argmax_i Go_i`) that beats a static additive baseline by closure=0.661 at a fair regime (V1200, decision_depth=4; GONOGO=0.653 vs ADD=0.053 vs ORACLE=0.962, cv=0.037, sign_p<0.0001, reach_rank_test=0.690). This is the substrate analog of Frank & O'Reilly's PBWM Go/NoGo model with the SR (Dayan 1993; Stachenfeld, Botvinick & Gershman 2017) playing the "critic"/state-value role and the TD-error playing the dopaminergic RPE training signal (Montague, Dayan & Sejnowski 1996). **So tonight's premise ("action-selection is the next unaddressed gap") is only half right**: the *existence* of a working substrate value-based action-selection primitive is proven, not open. The genuine remaining gap — confirmed by grepping the follow-on cells in this same lineage — is **composition and scale**: (1) this Go/NoGo gate has never been wired to receive its goal/context signal from the just-certified attention-routing gate (`exp_substrate_gen_lm_combinedgate_recency_content_v8`, whose own docstring explicitly scoped OUT "value-based/PBWM gating" as future work) rather than from a ground-truth goal fed directly to it; and (2) it is already known, from three follow-on cells in the same corpus, to degrade with decision depth (compounding error) and to fail at self-discovering multi-step subgoal decomposition. That intersection — compose the two independently-proven gates and measure the composition tax — is the one concrete, non-duplicate cell this note proposes.

## Ranked mechanisms (neuroscience signature -> substrate primitive; scour-first result)

**1. Basal-ganglia direct/indirect (D1 Go / D2 NoGo) pathway, three-factor RPE-gated Hebbian rule (Frank, Loughry & O'Reilly 2001, *Cognitive, Affective & Behavioral Neuroscience* 1(2):137-160; Frank 2005, *J Cogn Neurosci* 17(1):51-72; O'Reilly & Frank 2006 "PBWM," *Neural Computation* 18(2):283-328) — HIGHEST likelihood, and already CONFIRMED on this substrate.**
Mechanism: Δw ∝ pre × post × dopamine-signed-RPE, with D1 ("Go") units strengthening on positive RPE (DA burst) and D2 ("NoGo") strengthening on negative RPE (DA dip) — opposite-sign gating between the two pathways over the SAME candidate-action population; net output = disinhibition (via SNr→thalamus) of whichever action's Go population wins the competition.
Substrate analog (already built, already HARD_PASS): `cfrpe_trained_gonogo` arm in `exp_pfc_gate_cfrpe_trained_v2.py`. `reach_i` (SR-value) plays the role of the learned Go-drive; `argmax_i Go_i` is the WTA disinhibition step; the training signal (`cfrpe` delta-rule, adaptive-LR-clamped outer-product update on TD(0) error) is a direct substrate implementation of the three-factor RPE rule. **This differs from the biased-competition attention gate (v5-v8) in exactly the way the task asked**: the attention gate's admission weights are a function of STIMULUS similarity/recency (content-cue match or positional prior) computed fresh each instance with NO training signal tied to task reward; the Go/NoGo gate's weights (`w_reach`, and M itself) are LEARNED across training rollouts from a scalar RPE that only exists because of a defined goal/task — i.e. one is competition-over-inputs-by-stimulus-match, the other is competition-over-outputs-by-learned-value. Confirmed distinct computations, confirmed both already work in isolation.

**2. Actor-critic / dopamine-RPE framework (Montague, Dayan & Sejnowski 1996, *J Neurosci* 16:1936-1947; Schultz, Dayan & Montague 1997, *Science* 275:1593-1599; O'Doherty, Dayan, Schultz, Deichmann, Friston & Dolan 2004, *Science* 304:452-454, dissociating ventral-striatum-critic from dorsal-striatum-actor) — same substrate primitive as #1 under a different citation lineage, not independent.**
The substrate's SR-transport matrix M (Dayan 1993, *Neural Computation* 5(4):613-624: `V(s) = M(s,:).R`, M learned by TD(0) as discounted expected future state-occupancy, independent of reward) trained by the cfrpe delta-rule is literally the "critic" (state-predictive value features); the Go/NoGo argmax actor built on top of `reach = cos(E[cand]@M, E[goal])` is the "actor." Lit-scan confirms O'Reilly & Frank (2006) explicitly frame PBWM this way — mechanisms #1 and #2 are the SAME thing in the literature, not two candidates to choose between. Rank-2 only because it's the same substrate cell viewed through a different lens, contributing no NEW primitive to test.

**3. Softmax/argmax over learned Q-values with explicit exploration (indirect pathway as a suppression/exploration control) — a real but currently UNUSED substrate degree of freedom.**
The existing `gate = argmax_i Go_i` is a hard, deterministic WTA — there is no temperature/softmax exploration term and no explicit NoGo-as-suppression-of-alternatives signal separate from the Go drive itself (D1/D2 are currently folded into one scalar `Go_i`, not modeled as two competing pathways with independent learning). This is a genuine, cheap, ADJACENT extension (per [[feedback-dont-dismiss-adjacent-methods]]) but is not the highest-priority next cell — it targets exploration/robustness, not the composition gap the director flagged. Noted as a rank-3 candidate for a future cycle, not designed further here.

**Convergence point:** the brain literature (Chatham & Badre 2021, *PLOS Computational Biology*, "Analogous computations in working memory input, output, and motor gating: electrophysiological and computational modeling evidence" — lit-scan-verified) explicitly argues that PFC/BG gating uses the SAME Go/NoGo RPE-trained computation for INPUT gating (what enters a maintained representation — this is what v8's attention gate does), OUTPUT gating (what gets read out for use — what cfrpe's Go/NoGo does), and MOTOR gating (what action gets executed). That means the substrate has, by accident of two separately-motivated research threads, already built BOTH loci of what the literature calls one recurring primitive — and never tested whether they compose the way the theory predicts. That composition test, not a new from-scratch mechanism, is the highest-value next cell.

## Cheap decisive test / cell proposal (the one candidate)

**Working name:** `exp_pfc_bg_composed_attention_value_gate_v1` (exp_dev to finalize naming/versioning on pickup).

**Extends:** `experiments/exp_pfc_gate_cfrpe_trained_v2.py` (E encoder, W_ops, SR-transport M trained by the cfrpe delta-rule, Go/NoGo WTA actor — reuse unchanged) + `experiments/exp_substrate_gen_lm_combinedgate_recency_content_v8_n8192_gpu.py` (the combined content+recency admission gate: `combined_logit_j = content_rel_j/GATE_TAU + recency_bias_j`, softmax over K candidate slots — reuse unchanged).

**Task:** pin the regime to the ALREADY-KNOWN-GOOD spot (do not re-discover a fair regime; reuse the one both parent cells independently validated): V=1200, decision_depth=4, K in {4,6} (v8's own K sweep). At each decision hop, the Go/NoGo actor's goal/context signal is not given directly — it must be read out from a K-slot scrolling window holding several past goal-cues, mixed across v8's own ALIGNED / CONFLICT / CUE_ABSENT sub-regimes (so the correct goal-slot is sometimes the most recent, sometimes flagged by content, sometimes ambiguous). Task correctness requires BOTH stages: attention must find the right goal representation, AND the value-gate must select the right action given it. Neither existing gate alone can solve this task (attention gate has no notion of "action"; Go/NoGo gate has no goal without attention).

**Arms (paired, same E/W_ops/M/test chains per seed x regime):**
- `ORACLE_GOAL_GONOGO` — ceiling; true goal fed directly (reproduces cfrpe v2's own FAIR-regime numbers in-cell as a replication/positive-control).
- `V8_GATE_GONOGO` — **THE TEST**: goal representation = v8's combined-gate soft-pooled output over the K candidate goal-cue slots, fed into the unchanged trained Go/NoGo actor.
- `RAW_UNIFORM_GONOGO` — goal representation = uniform pooling over K slots (attention-blind control) fed to Go/NoGo — isolates whether attention is load-bearing at all.
- `V8_GATE_SCRAMBLED_GONOGO` — **telemetry-sensitivity guard**: v8's admission weights computed, then permuted before pooling (same weight magnitude/entropy, wrong slot assignment). Must collapse toward `RAW_UNIFORM_GONOGO`.
- `V8_GATE_ADDITIVE` — attention correct, but action-selection reverts to the static `ADDITIVE_BASELINE` (not Go/NoGo) — isolates whether the value-based actor still adds anything once attention alone is solved (the real neuroscience question: PFC gates WHAT enters; BG separately decides HOW to act on it — these are not redundant even when both work).
- `w_reach==0` null-reduction control — inherited unchanged from cfrpe v2; confirms GONOGO reduces exactly to ADDITIVE with the value term off.

**New discriminators (beyond cfrpe v2's existing closure/gonogo_lift/dynamics_lift family, reused unchanged):**
- `att_lift = V8_GATE_GONOGO - RAW_UNIFORM_GONOGO` (attention necessity).
- `composition_tax = ORACLE_GOAL_GONOGO - V8_GATE_GONOGO` (cost of imperfect attention propagating into the value-based decision — the actual cortico-striatal information-bottleneck measurement).
- `scramble_gap = V8_GATE_GONOGO - V8_GATE_SCRAMBLED_GONOGO` (**mandatory telemetry-sensitivity guard** — given tonight's two metric-tautology incidents, this arm is not optional. A tautological/pinned discriminator would show `scramble_gap ~ 0`, because it would be rewarding "having admitted something," not "having admitted the right thing." Threshold reused from the already-validated content-gate HARD-PASS band, 0.30, rather than invented fresh.)

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS:** at V1200_d4, `V8_GATE_GONOGO` closure >= 0.25 (cfrpe v2's own bar) AND `att_lift` >= 0.15 absolute AND `scramble_gap` >= 0.30 AND `composition_tax` <= 0.20 (attention imperfection does not blow up the value decision — composition is graceful) AND all inherited cfrpe anti-tautology guards hold (dynamics_lift>0.05, sign_p<0.05, reach_rank_test>0.30, oracle>=0.90).

**MIDDLE-BAND:** `att_lift` >= 0.15 (attention genuinely load-bearing) but EITHER `composition_tax` > 0.20 (attention error compounds badly into the downstream value decision) OR closure misses 0.25 OR `scramble_gap` lands in [0.15, 0.30) (present but weak).

**HARD-FAIL:** `att_lift` < 0.15 (composition adds nothing — the two independently-proven gates do not combine, even though both work in isolation; a real, informative negative about serial composability of gating primitives) OR `scramble_gap` < 0.15 (discriminator is NOT telemetry-sensitive — same failure class as tonight's two incidents; report as `INCONCLUSIVE_TAUTOLOGICAL_METRIC`, not as a negative mechanism result).

**Explicit scope discipline:** this cell pins `decision_depth=4` deliberately — it is NOT re-testing the already-diagnosed compounding-depth-error or self-discovered-decomposition failures (those are separately HARD_FAILed in `autonomous_waypoint_discovery_v1` / `waypoint_rescue_coarse2fine_verify_v1`; re-running them here would conflate two different open questions). This cell isolates ONLY the attention-into-value-gate composition question, at the one regime where both parent mechanisms are independently known to work.

- The known HARD_FAIL boundary conditions (compounding depth-error, cannot self-discover decomposition, no cerebellar consumer) are real constraints on how FAR this composed system can be pushed later — they are cited here as the reason this cell pins depth=4 rather than as reasons to avoid the composition test.

## Substrate-product implications

If HARD-PASS: gives the glass-box substrate a two-stage, fully-inspectable cortico-striatal-style decision pipeline — an input gate that selects context by stimulus competition (legible: recency-prior + content-cue weights, no opaque attention matrix) feeding an output gate that selects actions by learned value (legible: a small trained SR matrix + three scalar mixture weights) — directly serving the M3 cortex-layer / agentic milestone, which needs BOTH "what to attend to" and "what to do about it" as separately-inspectable, separately-debuggable primitives rather than one opaque end-to-end policy net. If HARD-FAIL (genuine, non-tautological): informative in its own right — it would mean two independently-correct gating mechanisms do not compose serially without an explicit shared bridge, pointing to a concrete, brain-grounded fallback (PFC-BG top-down modulation is known to be bidirectional/interactive in the literature — the value/goal signal should modulate WHICH slot gets attended, not just passively consume whatever the attention stage outputs) as the next cell, rather than a stall.
