## HEADLINE

**1. Is the dominant residual constructional-not-worldknowledge? YES, deflated P=0.45** — for creation verbs
specifically (build/make/bake/carve), the created-object-vs-locative-adjunct ambiguity is dominantly a
STRUCTURAL/CONSTRUCTIONAL fact (bare-NP-direct-object vs PP-headed-by-a-locative-preposition), the SAME class of
missing mechanism already scoped and precedented in the sibling motion-verb drill (subcat-frame-frequency +
directional-PP diagnostic) — generalized here to creation verbs, which structurally lack the competing
locative-alternation reading that placement verbs (put/load/spray) have. But a genuine MINORITY world-knowledge
residue survives: prepositions that are surface-identical across a locative reading and a material/instrument
reading ("built OF brick," "carved IN marble") are NOT resolvable by preposition-class alone — that slice needs a
small lexical-ontology check (place vs. substance), not a bigger structural rule.

**2. Does carry-context or a learned reader escape the 0.557 ceiling, or hit the SAME WALL? SAME WALL, for this
specific residual.** The established discourse-context literature (referential theory, situation models) resolves
REFERENT-SET-CARDINALITY ambiguity robustly and in real time — but there is no positive evidence, and some
active counter-evidence (a 2022 failed replication of the "discourse proactively shapes syntax" claim; the
good-enough-processing literature), that a compressed discourse gist reaches down to fine-grained THEMATIC-ROLE
assignment when the referent is already unique. This specific residual (b) is not a referent-identity problem —
both "huts" and "the stream" are already unambiguous, unique referents in a single clause — so carry-forward
context has no established mechanism to arbitrate it. A learned reader gets no special leverage here either: it
would need to learn the SAME preposition/construction cue the symbolic parser is missing. The learned reader's
real, literature-supported advantage lies elsewhere (light/report-verb construction-coverage gaps; referent-set
disambiguation across sentences) — not on this per-instance role-assignment residual.

**3. Highest-value next build: a small, glass-box PP-CLASS structural feature added to the EXISTING symbolic
front-end** (generalizing the sibling drill's directional-PP diagnostic from motion verbs to creation verbs: a
locative/instrumental-preposition-headed PP carries a negative prior on patient-hood, keyed by verb-class), tested
with a scramble/degrade must-fail control — NOT a jump to the full learned-reader rebuild. This reopens a cheap
structural lever inside the symbolic front-end that the "0.557 is a proven ceiling" framing had prematurely
closed for this sub-class. The learned-reader pivot remains the correct long-game (per USER 07-18 authorization)
but its first increment should target coverage gaps and cross-sentence referent disambiguation, not re-litigate
this specific per-instance ambiguity, which the literature says a comprehension-only fix (structural feature) —
not a bigger, more integrated architecture — actually resolves.

## Angle 1 — Is this genuinely constructional? (construction grammar / FrameNet / UD)

**Established:** Goldberg's Argument Structure Construction framework (1995, 2006) treats bare-NP direct objects
as licensed by ordinary transitive argument structure; Levin's (1993, ch. 26) "Verbs of Creation and
Transformation" class does not exhibit the true competing alternation that placement/spray-load verbs have
(there is no reading of "built the stream with huts" meaning "built huts by the stream") — this asymmetry is
WHY the bare-NP/PP-adjunct split should be a cleaner, more reliable cue for creation verbs specifically than for
verb-attached PPs in general. FrameNet's core-vs-peripheral distinction (Ruppenhofer et al., *FrameNet II*) is
frame-dependent, not category-fixed — Place-type elements CAN be core for inherently spatial frames (Event-type
predicates), but for creation-type frames the Created_Entity is standardly core and Place peripheral, mirroring
the syntactic split (moderate confidence — this specific frame-element listing rests on secondary sourcing this
session, the live FrameNet page fetch failed).

**Established, and directly cautionary:** Universal Dependencies' own documentation explicitly declines to
commit to a clean universal argument/adjunct split (calls it "sufficiently subtle and hard to apply
consistently" cross-linguistically), folding true oblique arguments and adjuncts into one `obl` label. A
paper explicitly named creation verbs (build/make/paint) as a documented hard case for this split (summary-sourced,
flagged lower-confidence, not independently re-verified this session).

**The genuine residual, independently converged on by two of the four sub-scans:** prepositions that are
surface-identical between a LOCATIVE reading and a MATERIAL/INSTRUMENT reading ("built OF brick," "carved IN
marble," vs. "built BY/NEAR the stream," "carved IN the cave" [locative]) cannot be resolved by preposition-class
or dependency-label alone — this requires knowing the ontological type of the PP-object noun (place vs.
substance/material), a small, targeted lexical-semantic check, not general selectional-typicality modeling.

**Countervailing general-population evidence (from the computational-ceiling scan, Angle 4 below):**
Merlo & Esteve Ferrer (2006) find a real ~12-point gap between preposition-alone (67.9%) and full
syntax+semantic-class features (79.8%) for verb-attached PP argument/adjunct classification IN GENERAL —
but their corpus is dominated by placement/locative-alternation verb types (put/place/set-class), which DO have
the competing alternation creation verbs lack. Their general number should NOT be read as directly transferring
to the narrower creation-verb sub-case; it is the right caution against over-claiming syntax-only sufficiency in
general, but the creation-verb sub-class is plausibly the easier tail of that distribution, not the
representative case.

**Verdict, Angle 1 (deflated P=0.45, novel-synthesis-capped):** for creation verbs specifically, the
created-object-vs-locative-adjunct split is dominantly resolvable by preposition-class structural marking
(generalizing the sibling drill's directional-PP diagnostic), with a real but MINORITY exception
(locative/material-instrument preposition homonymy) that genuinely needs a small lexical-type check. This is
NOT the same as claiming syntax-alone solves argument/adjunct classification in general — it is a narrower,
better-supported claim about this specific verb class.

## Angle 2 — Does carry-forward discourse context resolve fine-grained role ambiguity? (situation models / referential theory)

**Established (P~0.85, direct replicated evidence):** referential theory (Crain & Steedman 1985; Altmann &
Steedman 1988) shows prior discourse robustly and immediately shifts syntactic-ambiguity resolution online — but
in essentially every study, the disambiguating cue is REFERENT-SET CARDINALITY (how many candidate referents of
a type the discourse has introduced), not a general situation/gist fact. Situation-model literature (van Dijk &
Kintsch 1983 CI; Zwaan & Radvansky 1998 event-indexing) is well-established for facilitating integration of
CONGRUENT material and flagging updating cost on dimension shifts (protagonist/space/time/causation) — but no
study was found showing these mechanisms causally REASSIGN a thematic role in a later structurally ambiguous
sentence; this is a genuine evidentiary gap between two literatures that don't cross-cite this exact claim.

**Direct counter-evidence, load-bearing for the "same wall" verdict:** Dempsey et al. (2022, *J. Memory &
Language*) is a Bayesian failed replication of Grodner, Gibson & Watson's (2005) "Strong Interactive Mental
Models Hypothesis" — discourse coherence did NOT proactively project/facilitate syntactic structure as
originally claimed. Combined with the good-enough-processing literature (Ferreira & Patson 2007; 2024
20-years-later review), which shows comprehenders often retain shallow/wrong parses even when context COULD
have corrected them, the honest read is that a compressed discourse gist is a real information-loss risk for
exactly the fine-grained detail (which NP fills which role) this residual needs.

**Verdict, Angle 2 (deflated P=0.30 that carry-context reaches this granularity in general; P=0.60-0.70
specifically IF the ambiguity happens to reduce to referent-counting, which case (b) does not — both "huts" and
"the stream" are already-unique referents):** carry-forward discourse context is NOT a plausible mechanism for
THIS residual class. It remains a plausible, separately-motivated lever for the coreference/referent-identity
residual class the sibling drill explicitly flagged as OUT OF SCOPE and a distinct next-drill candidate — do not
conflate the two.

## Angle 3 — Is comprehension (map-building) and reasoning (composing over maps) the same substrate?

**Established, strongest single line of evidence:** hippocampal relational-binding (Cohen & Eichenbaum; Duff &
Brown-Schmidt) implicates the SAME system in forming NEW cross-clause entity-relations during discourse AND in
domain-general relational/transitive inference — but specifically for establishing NEW, arbitrary relations, not
for core within-sentence syntactic/thematic parsing, which survives hippocampal damage intact. This is a real,
direct, but NARROWER convergence than "comprehension and reasoning are the same machinery" — it says the
DISCOURSE-INTEGRATION layer and general relational memory share machinery, not that core sentence parsing does.

**Established, formal-currency level:** VSA/HRR/Semantic-Pointer architectures (Smolensky, Plate, Eliasmith) were
explicitly designed as a shared binding formalism spanning symbolic compositionality needed for BOTH syntax and
relational inference; Eliasmith's Spaun is the strongest existing single implemented system doing both language
structure and general reasoning/working-memory tasks from one substrate — but this is a representational-format
unification (shared "physics" of binding), not direct neurobiological proof of one literal circuit.
LISA/DORA (Hummel & Holyoak) similarly generalize synchrony-based binding across analogy, thematic-role
assignment, and hippocampal binding as a claimed domain-general mechanism, but the sentence-comprehension
application is thinner/more theoretical than the analogy application.

**Countervailing evidence:** rostrolateral PFC (RLPFC) is recruited ADDITIONALLY for explicit relational
integration/analogy, scaling with relational complexity in ways core sentence comprehension does not — this
argues reasoning ADDS machinery on top of a shared binding core rather than reusing it wholesale. Predictive
coding (Friston; Pickering & Garrod) is applied to both domains but only as a shared COMPUTATIONAL PRINCIPLE
(hierarchical prediction-error minimization), not a literal shared circuit claim in the literature.

**Verdict, Angle 3 (deflated P=0.30-0.35 for literal shared machinery; P=0.60-0.65 for the more conservative
separable-but-analogous view):** the evidence supports the USER's factorization-core framing at the level of a
SHARED ABSTRACT BINDING PRINCIPLE/FORMALISM (which is exactly what the structure-content factorization already
is) — reused across comprehension and reasoning as the common substrate for ROLE-FILLER BINDING — but does NOT
support literal single-circuit identity for ALL of reasoning; complex relational reasoning plausibly adds
machinery beyond the shared binding core (an RLPFC-like extra layer), consistent with treating the factorization
core as the reusable asset and both the reader-front-end and any heavier reasoning layer as consumers built on
top of it, not as the same layer.

## Angle 4 — Computational ceiling: how much does syntax-only buy, cheaply?

Merlo & Esteve Ferrer (2006): verb-attached PP arg/adjunct classification, preposition-alone = 67.9%, full
syntax+semantic-class features = 79.8% (noun-attached PPs are far easier: 93.3% preposition-alone). Abend &
Rappoport (2010) unsupervised/distributional: ~70% ceiling even with quasi-semantic distributional signal added.
Nikolaev & Padó (2023) frame this as an unsolved theoretical problem via minimal pairs (same preposition "on,"
different argument-hood driven by verb semantics) — but their example verb ("put") is a placement-alternation
verb, not a creation verb, so it does not directly refute Angle 1's narrower creation-verb claim. No
Construction-Grammar computational system (ECG, FCG) was found with a reported accuracy number on this specific
ambiguity class; these remain small hand-built demonstrations, and even their argument-structure constructions
key off verb SEMANTIC class, not pure syntax — architecturally consistent with Angle 1's residual carve-out
(preposition-class-only is necessary but not fully sufficient; a small verb/noun-class lexical check is still
needed for the homonymy tail).

**Verdict, Angle 4 (deflated P=0.15 that a syntax/construction-ONLY fix with zero new semantic-class information
recovers >=15pts on the GENERAL verb-attached-PP population; this drill's own claim is narrower and higher,
P=0.45, specifically for the creation-verb sub-class where the competing-alternation confound is structurally
absent):** treat Merlo & Ferrer's 12-point general gap as the honest prior for how much of ANY residual needs
semantics beyond pure preposition/dependency features — and expect the creation-verb sub-class specifically
(this drill's actual target) to sit nearer the favorable end of that distribution, not the unfavorable end,
because it lacks the confound that drives most of Merlo & Ferrer's error mass.

## Cheap decisive test

**Step 1 (free, do first, mirrors the sibling drill's Step 1):** re-triage the reader's coherent-but-wrong FPs on
creation/transformation verbs (Levin ch.26 class: build/make/bake/carve/construct/create + near-synonyms) into
(a) locative-preposition-headed PP wrongly filling patient (target class — expect majority), (b)
material/instrument-preposition-headed PP wrongly filling patient (the flagged homonymy residual — expect
minority, should NOT be fixed by Step 2 below), (c) genuinely contested/gradient cases (spot-check against
PropBank/FrameNet-disagreement style criteria, per the sibling drill's category (c)).

**Step 2 (build, small, glass-box):** add ONE new structural feature to the existing LCCP design — a
preposition-CLASS diagnostic (locative-class prepositions: by/near/at/beside/along the {place} = negative prior
on patient-hood; material/instrument-class prepositions: of/with/from + {substance-type filler} = requires the
small lexical-ontology check, NOT covered by the structural prior alone) — generalizing the sibling drill's
directional-PP diagnostic from motion-verb constructions to creation-verb constructions. This is a small addition
to an existing, already-committed design (per the sibling drill's Step 4 construction-row pattern), not a new
architecture.

**Step 3 (falsification-critical):** scramble/degrade the preposition-class tagging on a held-out subset. The
structural prior's measured contribution to patient-exclusion MUST drop toward zero on the scrambled subset.
Separately, confirm the fix produces ~0 gain on the triaged (b) homonymy subset specifically — a nonzero gain
there would mean the "fix" is accidentally absorbing signal it shouldn't have access to (construction-determined
result, re-examine).

## Falsifiable predictions (HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL)

**Prediction 1 — the preposition-class structural feature reduces coherent-but-wrong FPs on the creation-verb
locative-adjunct subset (Step 1 category (a)).** P=0.45 (deflated; best-precedented claim in this drill,
generalizes an already-validated design pattern to an adjacent verb class with a structurally more favorable
confound profile). **HARD-PASS:** >=15-point FP-rate reduction on category (a), holding categories (b) and (c)
out of the pass/fail count. **HARD-FAIL:** <5-point reduction — would indicate the creation-verb sub-class does
NOT enjoy the favorable structural profile Angle 1 predicts, and Merlo & Ferrer's general-population 12-point
semantic-necessity gap applies here too; escalate to a semantic-class feature before re-testing.

**Prediction 2 — the fix does NOT meaningfully reduce FPs on the material/instrument-homonymy subset (category
(b)).** P=0.55 (deflated; this is closer to established literature — the two independent sub-scans converging on
the same preposition-homonymy carve-out strengthens this specific claim). **HARD-PASS:** <5-point FP reduction on
category (b) with the structural-only fix. **HARD-FAIL:** the structural-only fix ALSO recovers >=15pts on
category (b) — surprising positive, would mean the preposition-class feature is doing more (or something
different) than the intended locative-adjunct-exclusion job; re-examine for a confound before trusting Prediction
1's mechanism story.

**Prediction 3 (must-fail control, mechanism-validity).** Scrambling the preposition-class tagging on a held-out
subset must measurably collapse the structural prior's patient-exclusion contribution toward zero. P=0.55.
**HARD-PASS:** measured contribution on the scrambled subset drops to within noise of a no-feature control.
**HARD-FAIL:** contribution unchanged under scrambling — vacuous/construction-determined result, do not trust
Prediction 1 even if it nominally HARD-PASSes.

**Prediction 4 — carry-forward discourse context (a compress-to-gist situation model) gives ~0 gain on category
(a)/(b) (unique-referent, role-assignment) residuals specifically, while giving a real, positive, and separately
attributable gain on referent-set-cardinality-style ambiguities elsewhere in the corpus (a different, already
out-of-scope residual class).** P=0.35 (deflated; the discourse literature's positive evidence is real but
narrower than the granularity needed here, per Angle 2). **HARD-PASS:** measured gain on category (a)/(b)
residuals from adding carry-forward context is statistically indistinguishable from zero, AND a positive,
nonzero gain is measured on a held-out referent-cardinality-ambiguity slice (confirms carry-context is a real,
working mechanism, just not applicable to THIS residual). **HARD-FAIL (either direction is informative):** if
carry-context gives a real gain on category (a)/(b) too, that is a genuine, surprising positive result — flag for
follow-up, do not dismiss as noise, because it would mean the discourse gist model retains more fine-grained
structure than the general literature predicts. If carry-context gives ~0 gain even on the referent-cardinality
control slice, the carry-forward mechanism itself may be mis-implemented and this predicts nothing about the
role-assignment question either way — re-run the control before drawing any conclusion about Prediction 4's main
claim.
