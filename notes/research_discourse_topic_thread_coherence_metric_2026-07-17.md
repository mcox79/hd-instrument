# Research (3x depth): discourse "state of mind" beyond pronouns — topic/thread/situation coherence as the testable value, and a chain-grade metric

**Date:** 2026-07-17. **Filed by:** research (Sonnet lit-scan x3 parallel, Sonnet synthesis). **Trigger:** Director's realignment brief — repeated attempts to demonstrate discourse-state value via PRONOUN resolution accuracy have not clearly beaten a recency baseline on natural text (centering-theory literature itself: Tetreault 89% vs. BFP-Centering 79% on newspaper text, roughly tied on fiction — recency-adjacent baselines are already strong competitors in the pronoun task specifically), and the strongest non-recency signal (verb-semantics/implicit-causality, Garvey & Caramazza) is too rare to test at corpus scale (n=0 in 100 LitBank books, per Director's brief). ## HEADLINE

**The pronoun-accuracy struggle is a metric-choice problem, not a mechanism problem — and the literature independently converges on a DIFFERENT metric family that is (a) more directly what "state of mind" is FOR biologically, (b) glass-box feasible with existing rule-based precedent, and (c) testable at corpus scale with ZERO new human annotation.** Biologically, situation-model maintenance is not chiefly in the anaphora-resolution business at all — its best-evidenced functions are causal-integration-driven recall/importance (Trabasso & van den Broek), online comprehension-monitoring/inconsistency-detection (Otero & Kintsch), and constructive integration beyond verbatim memory (Bransford & Franks) — pronoun resolution is a surface SYMPTOM of situation-model bookkeeping, not its purpose. Computationally, the literature already has a 20-year-old, still-current, purely classical (rule/statistics, no neural network required) task+metric family built for EXACTLY this: Barzilay & Lapata's entity-grid coherence model, validated on a **self-supervised, zero-annotation-cost discrimination task** (can the model tell an original document from a randomly-shuffled version of itself?) that is DIRECTLY correlated with human-rated readability (DUC03/05-07 summarization studies) — not just a self-referential shuffle-detection artifact. This task family isolates the exact design choice the substrate's already-scoped Tier-1 (Cf-ranked, grammatical-role-ordered) discourse memory makes — does tracking WHO IS SUBJECT/OBJECT across sentences (not just WHO IS MENTIONED) carry real coherence information? — and it can be run TODAY on real prose already in the pipeline, no gold-label construction required, with a real non-trivial baseline (entity co-occurrence without role information) rather than an abstain-all floor.

Second-ranked, and the natural NEXT cell once entity-grid signal is confirmed: ProPara-style entity-STATE tracking (create/move/destroy/change-location) has a genuine existence-proof of glass-box feasibility — a published RULE-BASED baseline ("ProComp," Dalvi et al. 2018) with reported numbers competitive with early neural baselines on the coarser sub-tasks — and it directly operationalizes the WSM note's Tier-2 five-dimension situation content (protagonist/space especially), but at real (not zero) annotation cost.

## (a) Biology — what the state-of-mind is FOR, beyond pronouns

Assume as background (not re-derived here) the situation-model STRUCTURE literature already covered in `research_discourse_state_of_mind_situation_model_2026-07-17.md`: Zwaan & Radvansky's 5 monitored dimensions, Kintsch's Construction-Integration textbase/situation-model distinction, Gernsbacher's Structure Building Framework, Zacks & Tversky's Event Segmentation Theory. This lane targeted FUNCTION: what does a well-maintained situation model actually buy a reader, and how is that benefit measured WITHOUT reference to pronoun accuracy?

**A1. Causal-network centrality predicts recall/importance, independent of surface form (Trabasso & van den Broek 1985, *J. Memory & Language*; van den Broek's causal-network model).** Deriving a causal-network representation of a narrative (which propositions causally enable/motivate which later propositions) and counting each proposition's causal-connectivity predicts immediate recall, delayed recall, summarization-inclusion, and human importance ratings — with substantial unique variance beyond story-grammar category or textual position. Eye-tracking follow-ups show longer fixation on causally-central statements. This is the cleanest FUNCTIONAL claim in the whole scan: integration into a causal network, not referent-tracking per se, is what predicts what a reader keeps. Direct evidence, well-replicated classically; caveat: original studies used small hand-coded narratives, and corpus-scale causal-relation annotation is itself a nontrivial extraction problem (the substrate has no causal-relation extractor yet) — flagged as a real cost, not assumed away.

**A2. Comprehension-monitoring / inconsistency-detection is a direct behavioral signature that a situation model is being actively checked against new input (Otero & Kintsch 1992; Otero 2002).** Readers frequently FAIL to detect text-internal contradictions unless the contradicting propositions are close together in the situation model/working memory; detection rate (or reading-time slowdown at the contradiction) is a measurable proxy for whether cross-sentence integration is actually happening, and inconsistency-detection-and-repair scores independently predict comprehension-test performance and discriminate skilled/less-skilled readers. This is the strongest candidate for a corpus-scale, TEXT-ONLY (no eye-tracking/EEG hardware) functional test: inject a synthetic contradiction (e.g. swap an entity's stated location/property between two sentences of a passage) and measure whether a discourse-tracking mechanism flags it, against a baseline mechanism with no cross-sentence state (which cannot detect it even in principle). Direct evidence; the "distance in the model" parameter varies across studies (not fully standardized) but the core paradigm (inject → detect) is mature.

**A3. Constructive integration beyond verbatim memory (Bransford & Franks 1971).** Subjects who heard sentence FRAGMENTS of complex ideas later falsely, confidently "recognized" full INTEGRATED sentences they never heard — and recognized these more readily than the actual input fragments. Direct behavioral proof comprehenders build a representation that EXCEEDS the literal input; measurable via false-alarm rate to novel-but-consistent probes vs. novel-and-inconsistent probes. Classic, well-replicated, but corpus-scale automation requires hand-authored probe sentences (a genuine construction cost, not free).

**A4. Surface-form/textbase/situation-model triple dissociation by recognition-memory DECAY RATE (Sachs 1967; Fletcher & Chrysler 1990, *Discourse Processes*).** Verbatim recognition decays fastest, paraphrase (textbase) recognition next, situation-model-consistent-but-never-stated-inference recognition persists LONGEST. This is the paradigm-level template for "does situation-model quality predict later recall/recognition independent of word-level memory" — present text, delay, probe three ways, measure differential persistence. Direct, classic, well-replicated; but automating requires hand-authored inference probes per passage (same construction cost as A3).

**A5 (documented failure mode, useful as a discriminating design, not a positive result).** The Moses illusion (Erickson & Mattson 1981; Shafto & MacKay 2000's Armstrong illusion) shows readers systematically FAIL to detect a knowledge-conflicting substitution ("Moses" for "Noah") even when instructed to look for errors — shallow lexical/semantic overlap can carry comprehension through without full situation-model verification. Domain experts show REDUCED illusion rates on their own domain (richer situation-model content = better detection) — this gives a graded, inverse-proxy design (illusion-susceptibility rate as a measure of situation-model engagement depth) but argues the state-of-mind's checking is often NOT engaged by default, an honest caveat against overclaiming universal comprehension-monitoring.

**A6. Event-boundary prediction-error and reading-time slowdown (Zacks and colleagues; Kumar 2023's Bayesian-surprise-predicts-segmentation).** Text reading slows at event boundaries and speeds when the transition is made predictable by editing — a causally-manipulated result (not just correlational) that situation-model-driven prediction is doing real work. Weaker fit for pure-text corpus-scale testing than A1/A2 (the strongest causal-manipulation studies are film/activity-segmentation, not text), but the reading-time analogs exist and this is the direct link to the WSM note's already-registered Prediction 2 (discontinuity-triggered consolidation).

**Ranking by direct text-only corpus-scale measurability:** A1 (causal centrality) > A2 (inconsistency detection) > A3 (Bransford-Franks integration) > A4 (Sachs/Fletcher-Chrysler decay dissociation) > A5 (Moses-illusion, inverse probe) > A6 (event-boundary prediction, weaker text-only evidence base). Note none of these is "resolve the pronoun correctly" — that is conspicuously ABSENT from the literature's own account of what a situation model is measured to be FOR. This is the direct evidentiary support for the realignment.

## (b) Computational glass-box discourse-coherence models and their task+metric

**B1. Entity Grid coherence model (Barzilay & Lapata, ACL 2005 / *Computational Linguistics* 2008, "Modeling Local Coherence: An Entity-Based Approach") — the single strongest candidate found.** Mechanics: an *n × m* grid, rows = sentences, columns = discourse entities (coreference chains); each cell holds the entity's grammatical role in that sentence (S/O/X/absent). The grid reduces to a fixed-length vector of local role-TRANSITION probabilities (bigrams/trigrams down each column, e.g. "S→O", "O→–→S"). Task: **sentence-order permutation discrimination** — given an original document and ~20 randomly-shuffled versions of itself, can the model rank the original above the shuffles? Reported classical (no neural network) accuracy: roughly 83-90% depending on grid variant and corpus (Earthquakes/Accidents aviation-report corpora), with one replication citing 90.4% on Accidents. **Critically, this is the ONE candidate in the entire scan with a documented DIRECT correlation to human judgment beyond self-referential shuffle-detection**: the same entity-transition feature vectors, used in summary-coherence scoring, correlate with human-rated readability in DUC03/05-07 summarization evaluations. Fully reproducible with POS/coreference statistics alone — genuinely glass-box; a simple ranking rule (or even a hand-set scoring formula) can substitute for the original paper's SVM ranker without losing the mechanism.

**B2. Content-model HMMs (Barzilay & Lee 2004, "Catching the Drift").** Same permutation-discrimination and extractive-summarization-content-selection tasks, using an unsupervised topic-sequencing HMM instead of entity-role grids. Classical/statistical, no neural components; reported as beating prior methods but exact percentages not independently re-confirmed this pass. Moderate cost (HMM training per domain).

**B3. Topic/lexical-cohesion segmentation (Hearst 1997 TextTiling; Choi 2000 C99) + Pk/WindowDiff metrics.** Cheapest of all candidates computationally — pure block-cosine-similarity valley detection, no ML at all. Pk/WindowDiff are exact, simple counting-based evaluation metrics against human-annotated topic boundaries (Pevzner & Hearst 2002 gives the precise definitions; WindowDiff fixes Pk's known false-positive/false-negative asymmetry). Weaker link to comprehension VALUE directly — validated against human-drawn BOUNDARIES, not against comprehension/coherence ratings — a proxy, not a direct correlation study. This is the natural metric for the WSM note's already-registered Prediction 2 (event-boundary/consolidation trigger timing), not for the "does tracking add value" question this drill is chasing.

**B4. RST discourse parsing (Mann & Thompson 1988; Marcu's rule-based/decision-tree parsers).** Classical (decision-tree, no neural net), evaluated via Span/Nuclearity/Relation F1 against gold RST-Treebank trees — a structural-agreement metric, not a downstream comprehension/coherence metric. No direct evidence found linking RST-tree correctness to human coherence/comprehension judgment. Weakest fit of the four for THIS drill's purpose (though potentially useful later for causal/rhetorical-relation extraction feeding A1's causal-centrality function).

**Ranking (validation strength x cheapness x human-judgment correlation):** B1 (entity grid) >> B3 (Pk/WindowDiff segmentation, cheapest but weaker value-link) > B2 (content-model HMMs) > B4 (RST parsing, weakest here).

## (c) Entity-state-tracking and comprehension-QA task families (existence-proof of glass-box feasibility)

**C1. ProPara (Dalvi et al. 2018, NAACL, arXiv:1805.06975) — real existence-proof of a non-neural baseline.** Process-paragraph corpus (81k datapoints) with gold entity-state annotations (created/destroyed/moved, at each step). Three question categories: Cat-1 (was entity X created/destroyed/moved — binary), Cat-2 (WHEN — step localization), Cat-3 (WHERE — location span). Verified numbers (paper Table 3): majority baseline Cat-1=51.0; a genuinely **rule-based system ("ProComp") scores 57.14/20.33/2.4 (macro 26.62)** — competitive with contemporary neural baselines (QRN macro 26.26, EntNet macro 26.07) on the coarser categories, weak specifically on location-span (Cat-3); human ceiling is 91.67/87.66/62.96 (macro 80.76). This is a genuine, citable, non-neural existence proof — directly operationalizes the WSM Tier-2 five-dimension content spec (protagonist state, space/location especially). Cost: requires gold entity-state annotation per passage — real, not zero, construction cost; the dataset itself is reusable off-the-shelf if the substrate's register can be approximated to it, or a small hand-built analog is needed for the curriculum register specifically.

**C2. bAbI task suite (Weston et al. 2015) — cheap but low discriminating power.** Location/possession-tracking tasks ("Where is X?" across a short synthetic narrative) are the discourse-relevant subset. Symbolic solvability is STRUCTURALLY GUARANTEED by the deterministic story-generating simulator (widely noted critique of bAbI) — no specific citation found reporting an exact rule-based accuracy number per task (this is inference from task design, not a directly-cited baseline, weaker existence-proof than C1). Cheapest/most scalable (fully synthetic, zero annotation cost) but a system passing this task by construction is NOT informative about whether the substrate's specific mechanism (vs. a trivial lookup table matched to the simulator's grammar) is doing real work — good as a regression/smoke guard, not a decisive test.

**C3. Verification/recognition-probe paradigms as a TASK-DESIGN template, not an off-the-shelf benchmark.** Fletcher & Chrysler's three-way probe design (verbatim / paraphrase / situation-consistent-but-never-stated) is the single most DIRECT isolation of "was an integrated model built" in the entire scan — but no non-neural NLP system has ever been scored against it; it would need to be built from scratch as an automated corpus-scale task (hand-author probe sentences, no annotation precedent to reuse). High construction cost, best directness. Deprioritize for a FIRST cheap test; revisit once B1/C1 land.

**C4. NarrativeQA (Kočiský et al. 2018) — weakest fit.** Generative QA over books, evaluated via BLEU/ROUGE (n-gram overlap metrics known to poorly reflect situation-model correctness — a surface-overlap confound), no classical/rule-based baseline found, expensive human-annotated corpus. Deprioritize.

**Ranking (existence-proof of glass-box feasibility x directness x construction cost):** C1 (ProPara, real rule-based baseline exists) > C2 (bAbI, cheap but low-power) > C3 (probe-design template, best directness but must be built from scratch) > C4 (NarrativeQA, weakest).

Directly isolates the substrate's own Tier-1 (Cf-ranked-by-grammatical-role) design choice against a real, non-trivial baseline (entity co-occurrence without role information). **TOP PICK — see falsifiable spec below.**
2. **ProPara-style entity-state tracking, register-adapted (C1).** Strong second: directly operationalizes WSM Tier-2's five-dimension content (especially protagonist state/location), real rule-based existence-proof from the literature. Requires building or adapting gold entity-state annotations for the curriculum register — real, nonzero cost. Natural SECOND cell once (1)'s signal is confirmed (or independently informative if run in parallel, since it targets a different tier).
3. **Injected-inconsistency detection (A2, operationalized as a corpus task).** Strong biological grounding (Otero & Kintsch), cheap to construct (synthetic contradiction injection needs no human annotation — just swap a property/location between two sentences of a real or curriculum passage and check whether the tracking mechanism flags the clash), and it directly tests the WSM Tier-2 discontinuity-check machinery already sketched in the prior WSM note. Ranked third rather than first because the corpus-scale AUTOMATED version of this paradigm is less mature/standardized in the literature than B1's permutation-discrimination task (Otero & Kintsch is a human-subjects paradigm, not an NLP benchmark) — genuinely promising, but requires more design-from-scratch than B1.
4. **Causal-centrality-predicts-recall (A1), as a summarization-content-selection task.** Highest biological load-bearing value in the whole scan (predicts recall/importance directly) but currently the most EXPENSIVE to operationalize — requires a causal-relation extractor the substrate does not yet have. 5. **Topic-segmentation Pk/WindowDiff (B3).** Cheap, well-validated METRIC, but weaker direct link to comprehension value (validated against human-drawn boundaries, not comprehension scores) and doesn't isolate entity/role tracking specifically. Useful later, paired with WSM's already-registered Prediction 2 (event-boundary/consolidation-trigger timing) — not the right tool for THIS drill's "does tracking add value" question.
6. **bAbI-style synthetic location/possession QA (C2).** Cheap regression/smoke guard, NOT a decisive test — passes by construction, low discriminating power for whether the substrate's specific mechanism (vs. a trivial simulator-matched lookup) is doing real work.
7. **Fletcher-Chrysler/Bransford-Franks integrated-probe recognition (A3/A4/C3).** Best THEORETICAL directness in the entire scan (literally designed to isolate situation-model construction from fact memorization) but must be built entirely from scratch (hand-authored probes, no reusable corpus/metric precedent) — defer until cheaper candidates (1)-(3) are run and have either succeeded (motivating further investment) or localized a specific residual question this paradigm is uniquely suited to answer.
8. **RST discourse parsing / NarrativeQA (B4/C4).** Weakest fit on both counts (structural-agreement-only metric; surface-overlap QA metric respectively) — deprioritize.

## Cheap decisive test: entity-grid role-transition coherence, top-ranked candidate

**Task:** Take a set of real prose passages (>=6-8 sentences each) already handled by the substrate's existing parser — reuse whatever natural-text sample motivated the "struggle" framing (LitBank-style or curriculum-adjacent natural prose), not the closed-vocabulary curriculum corpus alone, since the coherence-tracking claim needs to be tested on text with genuine topic/entity variety. For each passage, generate K (>=10) random full-shuffle permutations AND, separately, K adjacent-sentence-swap-only permutations (the HARDER discrimination case per Barzilay-Lapata's own reported easier-on-far-permutations-than-near-permutations finding — this is the difficulty-on knob).

**One variable under test:** does GRAMMATICAL-ROLE information in the entity-transition sequence (the substrate's Tier-1 Cf-ranking design choice: WHO is subject vs. object across sentences) carry coherence-discriminating information BEYOND plain entity co-occurrence (WHO is merely mentioned, no role)? This isolates exactly one design decision, not the whole four-tier WSM architecture.

**Candidate mechanism (A):** entity-grid-style score built from the parser's own already-extracted role-tagged entities per sentence — for each discourse entity, the sequence of {S, O, X, absent} across sentences; score = a coherence measure over local role-transition patterns (e.g., count of role-CONTINUITY transitions — same entity keeps appearing as subject across adjacent sentences — minus role-DISCONTINUITY transitions, or a log-frequency-weighted transition score if enough passages exist to estimate transition frequencies).

**Baseline 1 (non-trivial, NOT abstain-all):** entity co-occurrence overlap score — Jaccard/overlap of the SET of entities mentioned in adjacent sentences, discarding grammatical role entirely. This is the real comparison: does role information add anything over mere co-mention?

**Baseline 2 (sanity floor):** random score — expected 50% pairwise discrimination accuracy.

**Metric:** pairwise discrimination accuracy — fraction of (original, permuted) pairs where the original scores higher than its permuted counterpart. This is the exact Barzilay-Lapata task and metric, independently validated and correlated with human readability judgment (not invented for this test).

**HARD-PASS:** role-transition score (A) achieves >=70% pairwise discrimination accuracy on full-shuffle permutations (deflated well below the literature's 83-90% band, appropriate for a much smaller/simpler corpus and register) AND beats Baseline 1 (co-occurrence-only) by >=8 percentage points on full-shuffle permutations AND beats Baseline 1 by >=4 points on the harder adjacent-swap permutations (a smaller but nonzero margin is the honest expectation on the harder case, per the literature's own near-vs-far asymmetry).

**HARD-FAIL:** role-transition score (A) is within 3 points of Baseline 1 on full-shuffle permutations — meaning grammatical-role tracking adds no real discriminating information beyond bag-of-entities co-occurrence, falsifying the specific value of the Tier-1 Cf-ranking design (not the whole WSM architecture, just this one design choice) — OR overall discrimination accuracy for candidate (A) is <=60% (barely better than the random floor), meaning the substrate's entity/role extraction is too sparse/noisy at this register to support ANY coherence signal, a more basic problem than the role-vs-co-occurrence question.

**MIDDLE (partial signal, report which failure class dominates):** beats Baseline 1 on full-shuffle but the margin collapses to near-zero on adjacent-swap permutations (role information helps detect gross scrambling but not fine-grained local incoherence) — informative either way, not a wasted test.

**Difficulty-on knobs (for follow-up, not the first cell):** increase K permutations per passage; restrict to adjacent-swaps-only (the harder case, built into the first cell's second condition above rather than deferred); increase passage length; vary corpus register (curriculum-simple vs. natural LitBank-style prose) to test whether the signal is register-dependent.

**Can-fail check:** yes on both directions — the test can HARD-FAIL informatively (role information genuinely adds nothing at this register/scale, a real localization of where the Tier-1 design choice does NOT pay off) and it is not saturating-vacuous (Baseline 1 is a real, non-trivial competitor already capturing most of the "same topic mentioned nearby" signal a naive test might mistake for coherence-tracking).

## Substrate-product implications

1. **The product claim shifts from "resolves pronouns correctly" (a narrow, recency-competitive, hard-to-differentiate task) to "tracks what a passage is about well enough to detect when it's been scrambled" (a broader, literature-validated, human-judgment-correlated claim).** This is a more defensible and more informative capability story, and it is testable TODAY with zero new annotation.
2. **The entity-grid test (candidate #1) is nearly free to run**: it reuses already-extracted role-tagged entities, needs no gold labels (self-supervised via permutation), and gives an immediate, real-non-trivial-baseline-referenced pass/fail signal on whether the Tier-1 Cf-ranking design choice is earning its keep.
3. **Do not treat a HARD-FAIL on candidate #1 as evidence the whole WSM architecture is wrong** — it would specifically falsify "grammatical-role information beats co-occurrence for coherence-detection AT THIS SCALE/REGISTER," which is a narrower and more useful localization than a global verdict on discourse-state value.
4. **Candidate #2 (ProPara-style entity-state tracking) and candidate #3 (inconsistency injection) are the next-tier investments once #1 lands** — they cost more (annotation or from-scratch task design) but target genuinely different situation-model content (state/location tracking; active discontinuity-checking) that #1 does not test.
5. **Causal-centrality (candidate #4) is the highest-ceiling but currently ungated capability** — it has the strongest biological evidence of any function reviewed (predicts recall/importance directly) but requires a causal-relation extractor that does not yet exist; flag as a longer-horizon build, not a near-term test.
