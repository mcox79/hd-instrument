## Brain-grounding (lead with biology)

### How the brain earns fine ABSTRACT/DEFINITIONAL distinctions (not sensorimotor)

**Already established (reused verbatim from the 29556 note, high-confidence, convergent across 5+ literatures, not re-derived here):**
- **Rogers & McClelland PDP** (*Semantic Cognition*, MIT Press 2004) — item+relation -> attribute-completion, pure error-driven correction, no hand-inserted category structure, reproduces coarse-before-fine developmental ordering.
- **Saxe, McClelland & Ganguli** (PNAS 2019) — exact closed-form eigenvalue-ordering theory: each "mode" of item-relation correlation structure resolves at a time governed by its singular value; coarse/shared modes resolve in few exposures, fine/idiosyncratic modes need many more, each mode's trajectory is sigmoidal/stage-like.
- **Lambon Ralph et al.** (Nat Rev Neurosci 2017) — bilateral ATL as a nonlinear transmodal hub forced by cross-modal/cross-relation prediction error to keep near-neighbor concepts (that share most spokes) discriminable on the FEW spokes they differ on; semantic-dementia lesion data (Warrington 1975; Hodges, Graham & Patterson 1995) cross-validates the SAME ordering in reverse (fine/subordinate distinctions lost first).
- **Gibson & Gibson** (Psych Review 1955) — differentiation-not-enrichment: learning targets the distinctive features that resolve a DISCRIMINATION DEMAND, not passive associative enrichment.

**NEW grounding for this drill, specific to why the ABSTRACT/PROPOSITIONAL case (solute vs solvent, photosynthesis PRODUCES sugar) is a genuinely different regime from the concrete-object case the 29556 note was built around (hydro vs nuclear vs coal power plants):**

- **Complementary Learning Systems, updated** (McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly 1995, *Psych Rev*; Kumaran, Hassabis & McClelland 2016, *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 20(7):512-534, "What learning systems do intelligent agents need? Complementary learning systems theory updated") — two systems: hippocampus = sparse, pattern-separated, FAST encoding of individual episodes (specific facts, specific concept-relation pairs); neocortex = distributed, overlapping, SLOW interleaved learning that gradually extracts latent SHARED structure across many episodes, generalizing only after sufficient interleaved exposure. Kumaran-Hassabis-McClelland's update adds that recurrent REPLAY of hippocampal traces can itself support some generalization (a middle path, not purely binary). **This maps EXACTLY onto the already-built MDL gate**: `KEEP_EPISODIC` = hippocampal-style per-item retention (the model as a lookup table of specific episodes); `compression_ratio > 1` / promote-a-rule = neocortical-style extraction of a shared rule that transfers to new items. v2's `compression_ratio = 1.1706` result is, in CLS terms, **partial/early-stage neocortical consolidation — real but not yet strongly generalizing structure**, exactly the CLS-predicted middle regime before sufficient interleaved exposure, not evidence the mechanism is wrong.
- **Words As social Tools (WAT) theory** (Borghi, Binkofski et al. 2013; Borghi & Binkofski 2014; Borghi, Barsalou, Binkofski et al. 2019) — abstract concepts depend MORE heavily on LANGUAGE and RELATIONAL/PROPOSITIONAL structure than concrete concepts do, because much of their defining content is not perceptually groundable at all (you cannot see "solute-ness"; you can only state the relation "X dissolves in Y"). Borghi & Cimatti (2009) specify this as conceptual content captured by relationships BETWEEN linguistic/propositional representations, not perceptual features. Barsalou, Santos, Simmons & Wilson's Language-And-Situated-Simulation (LASS) extension similarly treats abstract-concept meaning as anchored in situational/introspective/relational content more than in sensorimotor content.
- **Product of these two literatures for THIS drill:** for solute/solvent/photosynthesis/condensation-class concepts, the RIGHT feature space to differentiate on is **exactly the WorldTree typed-relation graph** (propositional/relational, matching WAT's prediction for abstract concepts) — NOT a richer sensorimotor/grounding signal (which the Binder-features arc already tried and found data-bound at this exact ARC/science decision seam, per atom 29571, `cert cc0045e42`, "Binder direct-supply data-bound at ARC-science decision seam"). This is an important, decisive piece of convergent evidence: **two independent lines (Binder direct-supply closing structurally + WAT's theoretical prediction that abstract concepts lean on relational/linguistic structure, not perceptual features) both point AWAY from perceptual grounding and TOWARD relational/propositional structure as the right feature space for exactly this concept class** — which is what build B already proposes to use (WorldTree typed relations), reinforcing rather than changing the design.

### SHAPE + PLACE + METRIC build B must match

- **SHAPE:** error-driven, interleaved (not blocked-by-concept) exposure over item+relation -> discriminating-role-filler pairs; CLS-consistent dual regime (fast per-item retention available as a fallback/floor, slow cross-item rule-extraction as the target signal, MDL two-part code as the formalization of the trade-off — already built, reused verbatim from v2).
- **PLACE:** the concept-representation layer feeding the reasoner's tie-break seam — literally the same slot build A's `ARM_DEF_AUTO` currently fills via direct trust-gate lookup. B replaces "recover the profile by direct fact-store retrieval" with "predict the profile from the concept's OTHER relations, for concepts where the discriminating relation itself is held out."
- **METRIC:** held-out-to-NEW-concept discrimination accuracy on the SPECIFIC discriminating role-filler slot (not a generic property-prediction accuracy across all relations, and NOT raw end-to-end ARC accuracy, which is too indirect given the reasoner's own combiner ceiling) — measured against build A's own MFV/GloVe/BGE baselines recomputed on THIS narrower concept set (v1's frequency-saturation lesson requires this preflight, see below), plus the CLS-diagnostic `compression_ratio` gate carried over unchanged from v2.

**Proposed name:** `exp_composed_differentiation_loop_v3_defmatch_feedb_v1` (hdi_exp_dev's call on final naming; this note is the design-of-record, not the implementation).

- v2's fairness gates G1-G6 verbatim: MFV baseline, shuffled-relation control, apples-to-apples GloVe/BGE recompute, no-leak/non-circular assertion, ablation battery (relation-only, CE-only, CE-no-learner), coverage-honesty reporting.
- v2's `build_ce()` / `ce_features_for_heldout()` CE-prototype quantization bridge (fixes the description-length blowup that caused v1's false MEMORIZES verdict).
- Build A's own reused arm machinery is NOT re-touched — B is a separate cell that CONSUMES A's `feed_b` metrics field as a JSON input, it does not import A's reasoner-arm code.

**What is NEW (the retarget):**
1. **Concept universe = build A's tie-relevant concepts**, read directly from A's full-run `metrics.json["feed_b"]["concept_role_slot_profiles"]` keys (NOT a fresh WorldTree scrape). This is what makes B's held-out yardstick mean something specific: success on B is success on the EXACT concepts the reasoner's ties are about.
2. **Train/held-out split = build A's own `feed_b["concept_heldout_side"]`**, consumed literally (not re-drawn by B with a fresh random seed). This is the single most important design choice: it means B's "held-out-to-new-concepts" claim is verified on concepts A itself defines as novel/unseen for the tie-resolution task, closing the loop the task explicitly asks for ("B learns to PRODUCE that representation for a held-out concept from its relations").
3. **Prediction target = the SPECIFIC discriminating role-filler slot**, not a generic bag-of-relations property. Concretely: for each tie unit, build A's `concept_role_slot_profiles[concept]` is a dict of `{relation_type: [fillers]}` (e.g. `{"SOURCEOF": ["energy"], "KINDOF": ["animal","plant",...]}`). Build B's target is the ONE relation-filler pair that build A's def-match scoring actually used to break that specific tie (recoverable from A's per-tie trace/`per_tie` metrics field) — e.g. for solute-vs-solvent, the target is which of `{SOURCEOF, PART-OF, PROP-*}` role-filler correctly aligns with the stem's causal direction, not the whole profile. This is a NARROWER, harder-but-more-decisive target than v2's "predict any relation's value" — it is chosen specifically because it is the one thing the reasoner's tie-break actually needs, avoiding the risk (flagged honestly) that a generic multi-relation prediction task could pass while never actually helping the seam it is meant to feed.
4. **Input features = the concept's OTHER (non-target) relations only**, explicitly excluding the target relation from a concept's own input (v2's G4 no-leak assertion, re-applied at the finer per-slot grain rather than per-relation-table grain — this is a STRICTER leak check than v2's, since the target is now a specific slot within a profile that could otherwise trivially co-occur with other slots of the same relation type).
5. **Curriculum ordering (closes v2's flagged gap):** v2 explicitly flagged its own limitation — "batch accumulator = ORDER-INVARIANT, so it cannot express Rogers-McClelland curriculum (coarse->fine) LEARNING DYNAMICS." Build B v3 should test an INCREMENTAL variant of the MDL learner (present KINDOF/coarse relations first, PROP-*/SOURCEOF/fine relations second, matching the Saxe-McClelland-Ganguli eigenvalue-ordering prediction) alongside the existing batch variant as a SEPARATE diagnostic arm — not gating the main verdict, but directly testing the coarse-before-fine SHAPE prediction the biology makes, which no prior cell in this arc has actually tested (both v1 and v2 report only a static coarse-vs-fine SIGNATURE, not a dynamic curve).

**Data-integrity preflight (mandatory, before headline accuracy — v1's own lesson):**
- Recompute MFV **fresh on build A's specific concept set** (not reuse v1's 0.9665 or v2's ~0.5-0.6 figures — those were measured on different, generic slices). Given build A's concepts are DELIBERATELY the confusable tie set (near-synonym lures by construction), MFV is expected to be LOWER and the task LESS frequency-saturated than v1's generic slice — this should be verified, not assumed, before any other number is trusted.
- Per-concept relation-count density: held-out concept is scorable only if it has >=2 non-target relations available (reuse v2's density gate verbatim).
- Relation-label precision proxy per table (reuse v2's curated-column high-precision audit).

**Must-fail controls (mandatory):**
1. **Shuffled-relation control** (reuse v2's `_shuffle_relation_targets` verbatim) — held-out accuracy MUST collapse to MFV/chance.
2. **Shuffled-target control** (NEW, sharper than #1): shuffle ONLY the specific discriminating-slot labels across concepts while leaving all other relations intact and correctly attached. This isolates whether the learner is exploiting the FINE, SPECIFIC signal the tie-break needs, versus exploiting gross relation-table co-occurrence structure that #1 alone would not distinguish. This is the control the task explicitly calls for ("shuffled relations, shuffled targets") and is a genuine addition beyond v2's control battery.
3. **Frozen GloVe/BGE baseline, recomputed on this concept set** (reuse v2's G3 apples-to-apples machinery) — the standing content-thin baseline this whole arc measures against.
4. **Oracle ceiling comparison against build A's own `ARM_DEF`/`ARM_DEF_AUTO`**: substitute B's LEARNED/PREDICTED profile into build A's def-match scoring function in place of the trust-gate-retrieved oracle profile, and re-run the reasoner's tie-break with B's predictions. This directly answers the task's framing ("B learns to PRODUCE that representation... so a held-out new concept's fine distinction GENERALIZES") at the level that actually matters — not just "does B's accuracy beat a baseline in isolation" but "does substituting B's learned profile for A's oracle lookup preserve tie-resolution performance."

## Falsifiable predictions (HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL, pre-registered)

**Precondition gate (must clear before B is dispatched at all):** build A's FULL/POWERED run (n=128, same protocol as the already-VET'd `powered_v1`, atom 29573) clears its own pre-registered HARD-PASS band: `(ARM_DEF vs ARM1 McNemar p<0.05 AND arm_def>arm1) OR (ARM_DEF_AUTO vs ARM_DEF_SCR McNemar p<0.05 AND arm_def_auto>arm_def_scr)`, WITH `gold_only` preserved at 1.00 and the positive control reproducing `powered_v1` exactly. Build A's own smoke run currently trends toward its HONEST_NEG band (`def_vs_arm1` p_exact=1.0, 2 wins vs 3; `defauto_vs_defscr` p_exact=0.6875, 2 wins vs 4 — i.e. the scrambled control currently WINS more often than the def-structure arm at smoke scale) but this is **explicitly noise-dominated** (n=15 tie units, single-digit discordant pairs) and NOT a decision. **If build A's full run HONEST_NEGs: build B should NOT be dispatched.** The logic is structural, not merely cautious: B can at best learn to approximate what oracle/direct retrieval of the definitional-structure profile already gives; if even the ORACLE (perfect-recall) version of that profile fails to beat the standing GloVe-oracle match, no amount of learning to approximate that same profile can do better — B would be optimizing toward a ceiling that has already been measured as insufficient. This is the single most important honest gate in this design.

1. **Held-out discriminating-slot accuracy vs recomputed MFV/GloVe-BGE baseline.** HARD-PASS: B's held-out accuracy (on build A's own held-out concepts, predicting the specific tie-discriminating slot) exceeds max(fresh-MFV, GloVe-oracle-recompute, BGE-0.228-cited-bar) by >=0.10 absolute, AND clears both must-fail controls (#1 and #2 above) by the same margin. HARD-FAIL: lift <0.05 absolute over the strongest baseline — a third replication of the v1-HARD_FAIL/v2-MIDDLE pattern, now on the narrower, harder-but-more-decisive target; would be informative (see Cross-thread synthesis) rather than merely negative.
2. **CLS-consistent generalization gate.** HARD-PASS-precondition (gate, not itself the result): `compression_ratio > 1` (chosen != `KEEP_EPISODIC`). HARD-FAIL (invalidates the cell regardless of accuracy number): `compression_ratio <= 1` — pure per-item lookup, no shared rule extracted, the CLS-predicted transition to neocortical-style structure never occurred at this data scale.
3. **Shuffled-target control (the sharper, task-mandated control).** HARD-PASS-precondition: shuffled-target-label arm stays within noise of MFV/chance across the full exposure range. HARD-FAIL (invalidates regardless of main-arm result): shuffled-target arm rises above chance by more than the pre-registered margin — would mean any apparent lift is leakage/frequency artifact, not genuine slot-specific learning.
4. **Oracle-substitution transfer (the task-relevant yardstick).** HARD-PASS: substituting B's predicted profile for build A's oracle-retrieved profile in the reasoner's tie-break preserves >=70% of the gap between `ARM_DEF`(oracle) and `ARM1`(GloVe-oracle) established by build A's full run. HARD-FAIL: substitution collapses tie-resolution performance back to `ARM1`/`ARM3global`-scramble levels — B's learned profile, even if it scores acceptably on an isolated held-out metric, does not actually carry the seam-relevant signal when plugged back into the real decision.
5. **Coarse-before-fine curriculum diagnostic (exploratory, not gating).** Expected (per Saxe-McClelland-Ganguli): the incremental/ordered-exposure variant shows a measurably earlier crossing of an 80%-of-asymptote threshold for coarse (KINDOF-carried) slots than for fine (PROP-*/SOURCEOF-carried) slots. A null result here (no ordering effect) is diagnostic, not disqualifying — it would mean the MDL batch learner's order-invariance genuinely caps what this architecture can show about curriculum dynamics, redirecting future work toward a genuinely sequential/online learner rather than a batch one.
