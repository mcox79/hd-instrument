# Canonical reference: how the brain builds the grounding SPOKE (all concept types, abstract-led)

Date 2026-07-14. Synthesis of a 3-part literature scan (abstract-concept grounding; developmental bootstrap; binding-mechanism + spoke inventory) into our canonical reference, with the transfer lens = realize one spoke glass-box in VSA on our existing relation-graph HUB. Frame: brain grounds via HUB (amodal semantic hub, anterior temporal lobe = our reusable relational scaffold) + SPOKES (modality-specific grounded content bound on); different concepts weight different spokes. Our substrate has the hub, ZERO spokes; the numeric spoke is near-empty for our non-numeric commonsense tail -> build the spoke that MATCHES (text/situational).

## Q1 - THE SPOKE INVENTORY (channel -> locus -> what it carries)
- VISION - fusiform/ventral visual stream. ACTION/MOTOR - premotor + parietal affordance. AUDITION - superior temporal gyrus. TOUCH - S1/S2. GUSTATORY-OLFACTORY - piriform/OFC/insula.
- MAGNITUDE/NUMBER - intraparietal sulcus: Approximate Number System + spatial "mental number line". (This is the spoke our join test found empty for our tail - fires only for genuinely magnitude concepts.)
- INTEROCEPTIVE - anterior insula, a predictive-coding COMPARATOR (top-down visceral prediction vs ascending afferent = error). Distinct from generic affect.
- AFFECTIVE/VALENCE - OFC/amygdala, distributed (VAD: valence-arousal-dominance norms measure it).
- SPATIAL - hippocampal place cells + entorhinal GRID cells (modules, ~1.42 dorsoventral scale ratio). NB the "original" relational map later REUSED for abstract/social maps = the same grid machinery grounds physical AND conceptual space (direct support for our scaffold-reuse thesis).
- SOCIAL - a genuinely separate channel (Borghi "Words as Social Tools"): social interaction / norm-following / linguistic negotiation as its own indirect-embodiment pathway.
- LANGUAGE/SITUATIONAL - word-word statistical structure + situated scenes (our next buildable; see Q2/Q5).
- HUB is GRADED (Lambon Ralph): a hub subregion's modality-weighting = its topological/connectional DISTANCE from each spoke. So "weighting" is not a separate module - it emerges from (a) learned per-concept feature statistics (Cree & McRae: natural kinds lean visual, artifacts lean motor, because those are the statistically distinctive features) riding on (b) the fixed graded-connectivity gradient. No dedicated attention/gain weighting mechanism found.

## Q2 - ABSTRACT CONCEPTS (the lead; our tail)
**No single dominant account. Field converged on GRADED, HETEROGENEOUS, MULTIPLE-REPRESENTATION views** (Borghi et al. 2017 Psych Bulletin 143:263-292 "The challenge of abstract concepts"; "Ultimate Grounding of Abstract Concepts: A Graded Account", J Cognition). Every abstract concept draws on a WEIGHTED BLEND of channels; the blend differs systematically by SUBTYPE (Crutch, Troche/Reilly, Villani cluster analyses find emotion / social-institutional / quantity-math / mental-state subcategories, each a different feature mix). Abstract concepts organize THEMATIC/associative; concrete organize TAXONOMIC.
The channels (all real, combine per-subtype; none suffices alone):
- SITUATED SIMULATION (Barsalou 2009) = the UMBRELLA + the CONSENSUS mechanism for EVENT/procedural abstracta. Meaning = re-enacted SCENE/SCRIPT binding object+setting+action+goal. This is the literature's clear answer for our "getting hair styled" / "create electricity" tail (event simulators; Matlock: even metaphorical-motion verbs trigger motor simulation).
- EMOTIONAL/interoceptive (Vigliocco/Kousta 2011) = affect as internal referent. CONTESTED (Paivio 2013 reply; anti-affective-embodiment memory studies) and holds only for a SUBSET (evaluative/affect-laden abstracta), NOT general. A special case nested inside Barsalou (affect = one modality a simulator binds).
- LANGUAGE-STATISTICS SHORTCUT (Louwerse Symbol Interdependency Hypothesis) = word co-occurrence carries REDUNDANT TRACES of the perceptual world (language evolved to describe a structured world), usable as a fast "good-enough" retrieval index. **CRITICAL: SIH explicitly does NOT claim language suffices - it is a shortcut INTO prior grounding, a way a grounded system economizes; it does not let an ungrounded system get meaning from co-occurrence alone.** (This is our symbols-about-symbols guardrail - see Q5 counter.)
- SOCIAL (Borghi Words-as-Social-Tools) = separate channel for social/institutional/moral abstracta -> "justice" grounds here (norm-following + linguistic negotiation), not in perception.
- CONCEPTUAL METAPHOR (Lakoff) = map abstract onto concrete/spatial.

## Q3 - HOW THE SPOKE IS BUILT / ACQUIRED (development) -- the design goldmine
- Concrete-before-abstract = GRADUAL overlapping shift, NOT clean stages; some abstract (emotion/mental-state) acquired early <3; concreteness advantage GROWS with age (Kuperman AoA norms; Brysbaert concreteness norms).
- **QUINIAN BOOTSTRAPPING (Carey 2011) = THE mechanism, and a striking structural match to us:** children first acquire PLACEHOLDER SYMBOLS whose initial meaning is defined ONLY by relations to EACH OTHER (e.g. count-list order), semantically impoverished; meaning is then FILLED IN via analogical mapping / abduction / induction that maps the placeholder RELATIONAL STRUCTURE onto a target domain. Proposed DOMAIN-GENERAL (Gentner "Bootstrapping the Mind" 2010; Nature Human Behaviour 2023 computational model). Contested vs Fodorian core-combination. **=> our substrate IS a bag of placeholder symbols defined only by their mutual relations (the graph = the count-list analog); grounding = filling in meaning by mapping the placeholder structure onto grounded content. This is the developmental template for what we are building.**
- XU & TENENBAUM Bayesian cross-situational learning: statistics does real work ("suspicious coincidences") BUT the effect ONLY fires under a KNOWLEDGEABLE-TEACHER assumption; self-sampled data kills it -> pure co-occurrence UNDER-DETERMINES meaning without a grounding assumption about the SOURCE. (Reinforces our DPI / active-sampling / exogenous-referent findings: statistics alone insufficient.)
- NELSON SCRIPT THEORY / Generalized Event Representations: a SCRIPT (who / roles / objects / temporal-causal sequence) is generated from a SINGLE experience, refined with repetition. Event-structure abstraction, mechanistically distinct from object-concept learning (which is functional/perceptual-similarity clustering + taxonomic extension). The acquisition mechanism for our verb-phrase/event tail.
- SPELKE CORE KNOWLEDGE = the minimal innate SEED the rest bootstraps off: 5 systems - OBJECTS, AGENTS, NUMBER, GEOMETRY/SPACE, SOCIAL. Answers "what is the minimal grounded core": these five.

## Q4 - BINDING MECHANISM + ANTI-SMEARING
- **CONTENT-AGNOSTIC BIND = CONVERGENCE-DIVERGENCE ZONES (Damasio)** - the closest existing model to our content-agnostic bind: a CDZ does NOT store sensory content; it stores the CONJUNCTION via Hebbian co-activation and RECONSTRUCTS content by DIVERGENTLY re-activating the original spoke regions. Same convergence/Hebbian machinery regardless of which modalities bind (vision->hub and taste->hub are identical operations, different wiring). (= our VSA bind: hub code binds a spoke's content, reconstruct by unbinding.) Also: binding-by-SYNCHRONY (phase-locked gamma; domain-general "same operator any modality" candidate) and Feature Integration Theory (attention-gated, FALLIBLE -> illusory conjunctions = binding is a distinct operation from feature detection).
- **ANTI-SMEARING = DENTATE GYRUS PATTERN SEPARATION** - sparse, heavily-inhibited granule cells transform overlapping inputs into near-ORTHOGONAL patterns = capacity protection / anti-interference. **= the brain's version of our CORRELATION-HURTS-CAPACITY finding (decouple store-codes to near-orthogonal).** Semantic-hub analog less quantified (ATL damage -> feature-INTEGRATION collapse, respond on single features; no quantified ATL capacity curve = a literature GAP / novel-synthesis opening).

## Q5 - TRANSFER: minimal glass-box VSA text/situational spoke (+ honest counter)
DESIGN (Quinian template): we already HAVE the placeholder relational structure (the graph). The text/situational SPOKE = bind each concept's DEFINITION-GLOSS + SITUATIONAL-RELATION content onto its relational-graph code via the content-agnostic VSA bind (CDZ analog), MATCHED to concept subtype (event concepts -> situation/script content a la Nelson; social/moral -> social-relation content; emotion -> valence content). Glass-box: the gloss tokens / situational slots are inspectable. First cheap experiment = attach a definition-gloss vector (bundle of the gloss lemmas' codes, or a structured who/action/object/goal situation frame) to each sparse-tail concept and test whether it improves HELD-OUT relational inference on the non-numeric tail where the graph alone is starved. Must-fail: scrambled-gloss must NOT help; a gloss that is just the concept's own neighbors re-encoded must NOT beat the graph (that would be symbols-about-symbols, not a spoke).
**HONEST COUNTER (the trap to design against): Louwerse SIH + Xu-Tenenbaum both warn that language co-occurrence is a SHORTCUT INTO prior grounding, NOT grounding itself.** A text spoke built from our OWN corpus co-occurrence risks being "symbols about symbols" (the DoQ trap) - re-encoding more graph structure, not adding exogenous referent. The brain's language channel works only because it indexes REAL prior sensorimotor grounding. => a genuine text spoke must carry EXOGENOUS content (external definitional/situational text the graph did not already contain), and the must-fails above are exactly the test that it does. ## HEADLINE + CALIBRATION
- The brain grounds by ROUTING each concept to the spoke(s) that fit it; abstract concepts = a graded BLEND (situated-simulation umbrella + social + emotion-subset + metaphor + language-shortcut), acquired by QUINIAN BOOTSTRAPPING (placeholder relational structure -> meaning filled by analogical mapping onto a grounded core of objects/agents/number/space/social). Bind = CDZ (content-agnostic conjunction + divergent reconstruction); anti-smear = pattern separation.
- STRONGEST TRANSFER (P_deflated 0.40): the Quinian-bootstrapping frame legitimizes our exact architecture (placeholder graph + fill-by-mapping) AND dictates the first spoke = event/situational + social text content bound glass-box onto the graph, with the symbols-about-symbols must-fail as the fairness gate.
- SINGLE BEST NEXT EXPERIMENT: minimal text/situational-spoke cell (above) - HELD until pinpoint confirms tail = DATA-limited (confirm-bottleneck-first).
- HONEST COUNTERS: (1) abstract-grounding accounts are CONTESTED/heterogeneous (no clean division of labor); (2) affect-embodiment holds only for a subset; (3) the text spoke can be a symbols-about-symbols trap unless it carries exogenous content; (4) Quinian bootstrapping itself is contested (Fodorian rival). Nothing here shows a spoke CANNOT be built glass-box - the CDZ/pattern-separation mechanisms are explicitly content-agnostic and inspectable-in-principle.
