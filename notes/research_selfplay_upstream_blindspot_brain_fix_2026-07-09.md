# Research: brain-first fix for the shared-upstream blind spot in self-play Speaker/Listener differentiation

Filed by: research (Sonnet lit-scan x4, synthesized by research/Opus)
Date: 2026-07-09
Trigger: VET-flagged 5x-negative-drill on confirmed structural negative (see `notes/research_selfplay_shared_estimator_independence_speaker_listener_2026-07-09.md` and the downstream landed-VET audit on disjoint-data cross-fit, B1: blind-spot overlap 0.77 -> 0.39, grounding ~0.60, highest code entropy, still ~2x the independence bar).

## HEADLINE

Across four independent literatures (CLS/hippocampus-neocortex, predictive-coding/active-inference, precision-weighting/arbitration, dentate-gyrus pattern separation + its failure modes), the brain overwhelmingly locates the fix for a shared-upstream blind spot **upstream of the split, in the CODE, not downstream in reconciliation or dynamic trust-weighting.** Precision-weighting/arbitration is a *consumer* of independence (it presupposes decorrelated error signatures already exist) and cannot manufacture them. Reconciliation/replay (wake-sleep, hippocampal replay, TMR) is a *content-transfer/stabilization* channel between two already-differently-coded systems, not the source of their independence, and risks re-correlating them if applied carelessly. The load-bearing mechanism is **pattern separation**: a sparse random-expansion + competitive-sparsification transform applied to the shared representation *before* it is split, decorrelating at the source. Failure-mode evidence (folie a deux, confabulation, incomplete split-brain) directly confirms the negative's structural diagnosis: wherever a corrupting/shared channel survives upstream, downstream systems converge on the same wrong answer no matter how differentiated their downstream processing is.

The minimal buildable fix for B1 (disjoint-data cross-fit) is therefore not a smarter downstream gate or a reconciliation loop, but a **second, independent, per-branch decorrelating transform inserted at the shared-representation boundary** -- structurally the same move dentate gyrus makes on entorhinal cortex input before CA3 ever sees it, and structurally the same move CLS makes by giving hippocampus and neocortex genuinely different *coding statistics* (sparse-orthogonal vs. dense-overlapping) on the *same* sensory stream, not just different data.

## Cross-thread synthesis (four lit-scans)

**Thread 1 -- CLS wake-sleep reconciliation** (McClelland/O'Reilly/Norman CLS 1995/2014; Buzsaki hippocampal replay/SWR; Hinton/Dayan/Neal wake-sleep 1995, Ikeda 1998 convergence proof; TMR literature).
- Hippocampus vs. neocortex independence comes from **divergent coding statistics on the same input** (sparse+orthogonalized vs. dense+overlapping) -- an architectural/representational split, not a downstream trick. LOAD-BEARING for independence.
- Replay/SWR consolidation and the wake-sleep algorithm are **one-directional, gated, content-transfer channels between untied weight sets** -- they stabilize/teach without merging parameters. NOT the source of independence; orthogonal to it. Wake-sleep's convergence guarantee specifically depends on the two passes being untied, and only holds when the true posterior is realizable by the recognition family (a caution: naive symmetric reconciliation loops can break down outside this regime).
- Secondary flag from this thread: dentate gyrus pattern separation decorrelates entorhinal input *before* it even reaches the hippocampus-vs-cortex split -- foreshadowing Thread 4's finding that this is the truly primary mechanism.

**Thread 2 -- predictive coding / active inference / exogenous ground** (Rao & Ballard 1999; Friston FEP; active inference/enactivism; Harnad 1990 symbol grounding + Searle robot-reply counter; 2025/2026 LLM "thin grounding" argument; self-supervised blind-source-separation multi-encoder decorrelation literature).
- Exogenous/external grounding is HELPFUL and, for genuine *semantic* grounding in the strict Harnad sense, arguably NECESSARY.
- But for the narrower question of breaking *correlated statistical error* between two estimators specifically, the strongest counter-evidence is that **purely internal architectural asymmetry + a mutual-disagreement-penalizing self-supervised loss (blind-source-separation / decorrelating-ensemble literature) CAN decorrelate two estimators sharing an upstream input, with no exogenous signal at all** -- PROVIDED the estimators already have differentiated inductive biases/coding transforms. If they share both the blind spot AND the same architecture/coding transform, internal-only restructuring has no leverage and an external channel is the only tie-breaker.
- Since B1 already holds grounding at an acceptable ~0.60 (the exogenous/referential axis is not currently the failing axis), this thread argues the missing piece for B1 specifically is the internal-architectural-asymmetry ingredient (a differentiated coding transform), not a new exogenous-grounding channel -- exogenous grounding is the fallback needed only if internal asymmetry proves insufficient (see Sharpest open question, S3).

**Thread 3 -- precision-weighting / neuromodulatory arbitration** (Friston/Feldman precision-weighting; Aston-Jones & Cohen adaptive gain; Yu & Dayan ACh/NE uncertainty; Daw/Niv/Lee model-based vs. model-free arbitration; basal ganglia/cerebellar gating).
- Consistent verdict across all four sub-threads: precision-weighting and dynamic arbitration are **consumers, not producers, of independence.** Every case examined computes each system's reliability signal from *that system's own, already-differentiated* error statistics, then routes trust dynamically. None of this literature shows a precision/gating mechanism reaching "behind" a genuinely shared blind spot to find independent information that structurally is not there.
- Direct implication: a dynamic trust-gate layered onto B1 with no added decorrelation stage is predicted to do nothing to corr(failmask) -- it would be arbitrating between two still-correlated estimators. This is a ruled-out candidate, not a promising minimal fix (see Falsifiable predictions).

**Thread 4 -- dentate gyrus pattern separation + brain failure modes** (Leutgeb et al. Science 2007; PMC2829853 human fMRI; J Neurosci 36:7569; Marr 1971 / O'Reilly & McClelland 1994 theoretical mechanism; folie a deux PMC10479191/PMC11806268; Schnider confabulation/reality-monitoring theory; split-brain/callosotomy literature).
- Dentate gyrus is a **primary, load-bearing, anatomically upstream decorrelation stage**: entorhinal cortex input (~10^5-scale) is expanded onto a much larger, sparser granule-cell population (~10^6-10^7, ~1-4% active) via largely random connectivity, before ever reaching the CA3 autoassociative comparator. Output spiketrains are measurably more decorrelated than the EC input feeding them -- decorrelation happens AT DG, not merely inherited.
- Theoretical mechanism (Marr 1971; O'Reilly & McClelland 1994): random expansive projection into a much higher-dimensional space + competitive/lateral-inhibition sparsification (k-winner-take-all) provably reduces overlap between similar inputs. This is a general-purpose, architecture-agnostic decorrelation primitive, not tied to any one brain area.
- Failure-mode confirmation (the negative-side check requested by the drill): folie a deux dissolves when the shared corrupting relationship/channel is removed, directly showing shared-upstream-input causes shared-wrong-conclusion and that separation restores independence. Confabulation arises specifically because the reality-monitoring "checker" shares the same corrupted generative substrate as the content-generator it is supposed to check -- an exact biological instance of "the shared blind spot lives upstream of the split, so downstream differentiation can't remove it," matching this drill's confirmed negative almost verbatim. Split-brain patients retain correlated (non-independent) processing precisely in proportion to how much shared subcortical/extra-callosal channel survives disconnection.

## (S1) Which brain mechanism is load-bearing

Ranked by evidence strength across all four threads:
1. **Upstream pattern separation (DG-style random expansion + competitive sparsification) -- PRIMARY / load-bearing.** Directly targets "blind spot lives upstream of the split." Confirmed both structurally (Thread 4) and by the CLS coding-statistics finding (Thread 1), which is the same move restated at a different anatomical scale.
2. **Internal architectural/coding asymmetry as a general enabling condition -- secondary but necessary precondition.** Thread 2's BSS counter-evidence shows internal-only decorrelation works, but only once the two branches already process the shared signal through different transforms -- i.e., pattern separation IS the mechanism that supplies this asymmetry cheaply.
3. **Reconciliation/replay (wake-sleep, hippocampal replay, TMR) -- tertiary, orthogonal.** A gated, one-directional content-transfer channel for stabilization/knowledge-sharing between already-differentiated, untied systems. Useful for keeping grounding coherent over time, but not itself a decorrelator, and risky if applied as a naive symmetric reconciliation loop (can re-correlate).
4. **Precision-weighting / dynamic arbitration -- NOT load-bearing for independence.** Confirmed across three separate sub-literatures as a pure consumer mechanism. Ruled out as the minimal fix.
5. **Exogenous grounding -- necessary for the grounding axis and as a backstop only when internal architectural asymmetry is degenerate/absent** (both branches share identical coding transforms). Not currently the failing axis for B1 (grounding already ~0.60), so it is not the prescribed *next* addition, but is the prescribed addition IF a pattern-separation stage fails to move corr(failmask) (see S3).

## (S2) Minimal buildable mechanism to layer onto disjoint-data cross-fit (B1)

**Add:** a fixed (non-learned), independently-seeded, sparse random-projection expansion + top-k competitive sparsification stage ("DG-style pattern separation"), applied to the shared upstream representation separately for the Speaker branch and the Listener branch, immediately before each branch's own downstream computation -- layered ON TOP OF the existing disjoint-data cross-fit (not replacing it).

Concretely:
- Two fixed random matrices, `P_speaker` and `P_listener`, each drawn from an independent seed, projecting the shared representation into a higher-dimensional sparse code.
- A k-winner-take-all (or lateral-inhibition-style) sparsification step per branch, keeping only the top-k active dimensions per sample.
- Zero new learned parameters -- the projection/sparsification is fixed structure, matching DG's largely non-plastic, randomly-wired expansion layer. Cheap CPU cell: this is matrix-multiply + top-k, no additional training loop.
- Applied in addition to (not instead of) disjoint-data cross-fit, since Thread 1/4 both frame CLS-style coding-asymmetry and data-disjointness as complementary, not substitutable, decorrelation axes.

**What this is NOT:** a reconciliation/replay loop (Thread 1/3 rule this out as the wrong layer -- it's a stabilizer, and misapplied risks re-correlating the branches), and NOT a dynamic precision-weighting gate (Thread 3 rules this out as a consumer, not producer, of independence -- it would have nothing to arbitrate on if the branches are still correlated).

## (S2 cont.) Falsifiable predictions -- HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL thresholds

Baseline anchors from the confirmed negative: naive mirror corr(failmask) ~= 0.77; B1 corr(failmask) = 0.39; B1 grounding ~= 0.60; independence bar implied at ~0.19-0.20 (B1 is stated as "~2x the independence bar").

**B1+PS (primary arm):**
- HARD-PASS: corr(failmask) <= 0.20 (at or below the independence bar) AND grounding >= 0.50 (no more than ~0.10 absolute drop from B1) AND code entropy stays within ~10% of B1's (pattern separation should not collapse the code).
- MIDDLE-BAND (worth one sparsity-level sweep before concluding, not a full re-drill): corr(failmask) in (0.20, 0.35) with grounding intact.
- HARD-FAIL, case (a) -- representation-level fix insufficient: corr(failmask) >= 0.35 (no material improvement over B1's own 0.39). This would falsify "the blind spot is fixable by a representation-level transform" and REDIRECT to Thread 2's exogenous-referent mechanism (shared held-out exogenous data both branches must independently reconstruct, trusted only where they agree) as the next candidate -- i.e., this HARD-FAIL is itself diagnostic, not a dead end.
- HARD-FAIL, case (b) -- over-aggressive sparsification: grounding drops below ~0.40 even if corr(failmask) improves. This would indicate the sparsification stage is stripping referential/semantic content along with correlated noise, and the fix is to restrict pattern separation to a non-semantic subspace (a scoped variant, not an abandonment of the mechanism).

**B0+gate (negative control):** predicted HARD-FAIL to move corr(failmask) below ~0.70 (i.e., a precision-weighting gate alone barely dents the naive-mirror correlation). If this control instead HARD-PASSES, treat as a major surprise requiring re-scan of Thread 3's literature (possible confound: gate may be leaking information about branch identity that acts as an accidental decorrelator, not genuine arbitration).

**B1+naive-reconcile (negative control, optional):** predicted to move corr(failmask) back up toward >= 0.55 (partial re-correlation) relative to B1's 0.39, confirming reconciliation loops are only safe when applied as a one-directional, untied-weights, gated transfer (per wake-sleep's convergence conditions), not as naive symmetric fine-tuning.

## (S3) Sharpest open question

The brain evidence gives **two different candidate locations** for "upstream," and the drill's confirmed negative does not yet distinguish them:
1. **Representation-level**: the blind spot lives in the shared *encoder/coding transform* itself -- fixable by inserting a DG-style decorrelating transform between the shared representation and each branch (this is the B1+PS proposal above).
2. **Distribution/objective-level**: the blind spot lives in the shared *training signal or data distribution* the two branches are ultimately both fit against -- no per-branch representational transform can fix this, because both branches are still chasing the same target with the same systematic errors baked into what "correct" means. This would require Thread 2's exogenous-referent mechanism instead (an outside-sample check neither branch's own output can satisfy).

The B1+PS HARD-FAIL case (a) above is the direct empirical test that distinguishes these two hypotheses: if pattern separation on the representation does NOT move corr(failmask), the blind spot is very likely distribution/objective-level, and the next drill should go straight to designing the exogenous-referent screen rather than tuning pattern-separation hyperparameters further.

P_deflated: brain-literature consensus that upstream pattern separation is the primary independence-producing mechanism (vs. reconciliation or precision-weighting): 0.55-0.60 (four independently-dispatched threads converged without being prompted toward this conclusion -- unusually strong agreement for a lit-scan, but still deflated per calibration discipline since no thread found a case testing this exact self-play/shared-substrate setup). P that the specific B1+PS translation (fixed random-projection + k-WTA layered on B1) clears the HARD-PASS bar on corr(failmask) while preserving grounding: 0.30-0.35 (novel-synthesis cap applied, further deflated for translation risk from biological circuit to the current cross-fit setup). P that precision-weighting/dynamic-gate alone would work (i.e., B0+gate HARD-PASSES): 0.10-0.15 (should fail per lit; included as a real falsification opportunity, not a strawman).
