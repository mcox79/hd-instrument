# Negative-drill: why real "interaction" domains came back additive-capturable, and where genuine non-additivity actually concentrates

Date: 2026-07-15
Dispatch: research (synthesis) + 4 parallel Sonnet lit-scan sub-agents (quantitative-genetics variance decomposition / ranked genuine-interaction domains / measurement-vs-aggregation-artifact / brain dendritic-integration mode)
Trigger: three real-data negatives — chemical-mixing hazard, LLM-narrated genetic epistasis, and the fair real-Costanzo-epsilon test — all came back additive-capturable; even epistasis (the purest expected interaction case) was main-effects-dominated at the readable scale. This drill asks WHY and WHERE the exception pockets are.

## HEADLINE

Genuine strong non-additivity is real but **concentrated in a structured minority**, not uniformly rare, and not simply a world-level fact independent of measurement scale — it operates on **two different axes simultaneously**, and both point the same direction:

1. **World-level (why broad/genome-wide measurement is additive-dominant):** Hill, Goddard & Visscher (2008, PLoS Genet 4:e1000008) give the mechanistic reason additive variance dominates population-level measurement even when the underlying molecular biology is saturated with epistasis: under the standard neutral allele-frequency spectrum (U-shaped, most alleles near 0 or 1, not near 0.5), the "average effect of allele substitution" statistically absorbs almost all of the nonlinear functional form into the additive axis. This is not a claim that molecular epistasis is rare — it's a claim that **population-level regression cannot see it** except at intermediate allele frequencies. Directly explains why the real-Costanzo-epsilon genome-wide test and bulk SAR-nonadditivity measurements came back additive-dominant: they are exactly the population-average / bulk-aggregate measurement regime this theorem describes.
2. **Measurement/aggregation-level (why curated subsets look different):** independent of world-level rarity, pooling a minority of genuinely-interacting pairs with a majority of non-interacting pairs mathematically dilutes the aggregate signal (Simpson's-paradox-class aggregation bias). The lit-scan found **direct, reproducible evidence that restricting to high-confidence, mechanistically-coupled subsets reveals substantially stronger non-additivity than genome-wide averages**: protein-complex-internal gene pairs show 28% interaction enrichment over chance, strengthening (not weakening) as confidence stringency increases (Bellay et al. 2011, PLoS Comput Biol); "monochromatic" (purely aggravating or purely buffering) modular epistasis is a real, if partial, structural regularity (Segrè et al. 2005, Nat Genet); and pathway-restricted drug-synergy screens find synergy essentially confined to same-pathway compound triads while genome/screen-wide synergy looks mostly additive (Frontiers 2021).

**Both are true and compatible: genuine non-additivity is genuinely rare when measured as a population-average, AND that same genuine non-additivity is measurement-hidden inside identifiable high-confidence pockets that bulk/genome-wide analysis washes out.** The three prior negatives all measured at the bulk/population-average scale — consistent with, not contradicting, this theory. The fix is not "abandon these domains," it's "stop averaging across them" — restrict to the specific structurally-defined subsets below.

**Top domain where the interaction term genuinely and provably dominates: synthetic lethality / paralog-buffered AND-gate gene pairs** — cases where both single-gene perturbations are statistically indistinguishable from wild-type fitness (main effect ≈ 0 by direct measurement, not inferred) while the double perturbation is severely deleterious or lethal. This is the cleanest real-data case because the "additive model looks fine" escape hatch (allele-frequency projection, Hill-Goddard-Visscher) structurally cannot apply — full knockouts are not segregating alleles at intermediate frequency, there is no population-genetic averaging in effect, the nonlinearity is measured directly in the fitness function.

## (a) How much of nature is genuinely non-additive vs additive (variance-fraction evidence)

| System | Additive variance fraction | Interaction/epistatic variance fraction | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK Biobank, 70 human traits, n=254,679 | h²(additive) mean = 0.208 | Dominance ≈0.001 (negligible); additive×additive epistasis ≈0.055, 95% CI [-0.010, 0.119] — not significantly different from zero | Pazokitoroudi et al. 2021, Nat Commun (PMC8205999) |
| Yeast cross, 46 quantitative traits | Additive QTL ≈43% of phenotypic variance; median 88% of narrow-sense heritability captured by detected loci | QTL×QTL interactions ≈9% of phenotypic variance on average (range ~0-50% trait-to-trait; one QTL pair explained 71% of the broad-vs-narrow-sense gap for a single trait) | Bloom et al. 2013 Nature 494:234; Forsberg et al. 2017 Nat Genet |
| *Mimulus guttatus*, 4 floral traits | 60-80% of genetic variance (bias-corrected) | Epistasis strongly modulates the MAGNITUDE of additive variance (context-dependent allele substitution effects) even though additive variance remains majority | PLOS Genetics 2015 (pgen.1005201) |
| *Drosophila*, stress/behavioral traits | -- | 46-70% of trait-associated SNPs participate in >=1 significant epistatic interaction (70% starvation resistance, 67% chill-coma, 46% startle response) — high LOCUS participation, but per-pair effect sizes still individually small | Huang, Mackay et al. 2012, PNAS 109:15553 |
| Chemistry SAR (matched molecular pairs) | -- | Only ~0.18% of double-transformation cycles clear a 5-sigma noise-adjusted nonadditivity threshold in noisy public data; 5-13% clear a property-specific noise floor for cleaner physchem endpoints (LogD, solubility, permeability, clearance) | Kramer et al. 2015 JCIM (PMC4372821); ACS Omega 2022 (PMC9352238) |

**Reading:** at the bulk/population/genome-wide scale, additive variance is the large majority (43-80%+) and interaction variance is a small minority (single digits to ~10%) almost everywhere measured this way — this generalizes across human complex traits, yeast, plant floral traits, and chemistry SAR. The one partial exception (Drosophila locus-participation rate) is about how many loci show SOME detectable interaction, not how much variance that interaction carries — consistent with "many weak interactions, dominated in aggregate by additive main effects," not "genuinely large interaction variance is common."

## (b) Ranked domains where the interaction term genuinely dominates (additive provably fails)

1. **Synthetic lethality / paralog-buffered AND-gate pairs (highest confidence).** Both single-KO fitness effects measured directly at ≈0; double-KO lethal or severely deleterious. Costanzo et al. 2016 yeast SGA: ~23M double mutants, ~550K negative + ~350K positive interactions genome-wide, threshold |ε| > 0.08-0.12 for the strongest calls. Paralog-buffering screens (enCas12a multiplex, Dede et al., PMC7558751) show functional buffering is "systematically absent" from single-gene (monogenic) screens — the single-KO signal is provably ~zero, only the combinatorial perturbation reveals the effect. BRCA-PARP is the clinical exemplar: BRCA-mutant alone viable, PARP-inhibition alone tolerated, combination up to ~200x killing increase. **Why additive provably fails here and not in genome-wide averages:** full knockouts fix both alleles, there is no allele-frequency projection (the Hill-Goddard-Visscher escape hatch requires segregating variants near intermediate frequency) — the nonlinearity is measured directly, not inferred from population variance.
2. **Allosteric/cooperative binding, Hill coefficient >> 1.** A Hill coefficient n_H > 1 is an ALGEBRAIC (not empirical) proof of non-additivity — the binding polynomial is dominated by the fully-liganded term, non-decomposable into independent single-site terms by construction. Hemoglobin n_H ≈ 2.8-3; tarantula hemocyanin n_H = 6-11 (highest reported for any biomolecule, JBC); bacterial flagellar motor switching reported among the highest Hill coefficients of any allosteric complex. Ranked below synthetic lethality for our purposes because it is a continuous single-molecule binding-curve phenomenon, not a discrete pairwise/combinatorial data shape — harder to map onto a relation-pair reasoning task.
3. **Threshold/bistable switches (ultrasensitivity, MAPK cascades, lac operon).** Bistability requires >=3 roots of the steady-state equation (2 stable + 1 unstable) — a topological/qualitative phase change, not a graded deviation. Purely additive/linear dynamics provably cannot produce multistability at all (linear ODEs have exactly one fixed point) — any observed bistable switch is a constructive proof the governing dynamics contain a dominant nonlinear term. Real but describes a dynamical regime, not a static pairwise data table.
4. **XOR-like logic in gene circuits.** Engineered XOR gates (quorum-sensing NOR-composite circuits, recombinase-based logic) achieve near-ideal truth tables because they are explicitly selected for it. Natural incoherent feed-forward loops are the closest natural analog but compute a graded, time-dependent pulse/fold-change-detector function, not a clean static XOR — "impure," confounded with dynamics/timing. Ranked last: real in engineered systems, only approximate in natural ones.

**Common thread distinguishing rank 1-3 from genome-wide population measurement:** all three directly manipulate/measure fixed molecular or genetic states (full knockouts, saturating ligand titration, dynamical bifurcation) rather than inferring effects from variance across a segregating, allele-frequency-biased population — there is no "epistasis converts to additive variance" escape hatch available in any of them.

## (c) Measurement-vs-reality verdict

**Primarily MEASUREMENT/AGGREGATION-HIDDEN, with a real but smaller genuine-rarity component underneath it (both operating together, not competing explanations).**

Evidence for measurement/aggregation-hiding:
- Protein-complex-internal gene pairs: 63% of observed genetic interactions vs 49% expected by chance (28% enrichment, p<1e-5), and this enrichment **strengthens under stricter confidence filtering**, not weakens (Bellay et al. 2011, PLOS Comput Biol).
- "Modular"/"monochromatic" epistasis (Segrè et al. 2005, Nat Genet): within-complex interaction consistency (MCI 0.71-0.82) is far higher than between-complex (0.46-0.60) — the signal concentrates exactly where physical/functional coupling is independently confirmed.
- Trigenic interaction mapping (Kuzmin et al. 2018, Science, ~200K triple mutants): interactions concentrate among functionally-related gene sets; averaging across unrelated triples would mask this.
- Chemistry: Kramer's own explicit noise-floor quantification (SD ≈0.4 log units from propagated assay error; 5-sigma ≈2.0 log-unit threshold needed) means the overwhelming majority of "additive-looking" cycles in typical noisy public data are statistically indistinguishable from measurement error, not proven-additive.
- Drug synergy: a systematic antifungal chemogenomic screen found strong synergy essentially confined to same-pathway compound triads (fenpropimorph/miconazole/cerivastatin) — a curated mechanism-specific subset reversing a "mostly additive" bulk-screen impression.
- General mechanism: Simpson's-paradox-class aggregation bias / non-collapsibility is well-established statistical theory for exactly this dilution pattern (a minority interacting subgroup pooled with a majority non-interacting subgroup attenuates the aggregate estimate).

Evidence for a genuine (not just hidden) rarity floor underneath:
- Hill-Goddard-Visscher's mechanism is a real population-genetic fact, not an artifact of poor measurement — it correctly predicts why STANDING VARIATION in outbred populations is additive-dominant regardless of measurement quality, because most segregating alleles genuinely sit at extreme (not intermediate) frequency.
- Even within curated high-confidence subsets, only a fraction show monochromatic/pure interaction structure (~10% of GO bioprocesses overall per one quantitative follow-up) — curation raises the yield substantially but does not make interaction-dominance universal even there.

**Net verdict: the "additive captures everything" result from the prior three drills is a predictable consequence of measuring at the bulk/genome-wide/population-average scale. Zooming into the identifiable high-confidence subsets named above (protein-complex-internal pairs, paralog-buffering candidate lists, physically-interacting-protein pairs, same-pathway-restricted synergy sets) is expected — on existing published evidence, not speculation — to expose materially stronger non-additivity than the genome-wide average the prior drills tested against.**

## (d) Brain implication

**"Additive-with-rare-conjunction" is a defensible, evidence-supported characterization of brain computation** — not merely a convenient analogy to the genetics/chemistry finding, but structurally the same pattern at the neural level:

- **Dendritic integration:** Cash & Yuste (1998/1999) found most paired dendritic inputs sum linearly ("active linearity," NMDA-boost/K-shunt cancellation). Polsky, Mel & Schiller (2004) and Poirazi, Brannon & Mel (2003) refine this: genuine supralinear (NMDA-spike) summation is spatially conditional — confined to inputs clustered within ~40um on the same thin branch — while cross-branch summation at the soma is approximately linear. This is literally a two-layer "nonlinear locally, additive globally" architecture, a minority-condition-triggered nonlinearity, not a tree-wide default (London & Häusser 2005 review).
- **Normative/efficient-coding rationale:** Ujfalussy et al. (2016, eLife) show linear dendritic integration is information-optimal when inputs are statistically independent, and nonlinear integration becomes necessary/beneficial specifically when inputs are correlated/clustered — an explicit cost-benefit argument that nonlinear machinery should be recruited only where input statistics demand it. Attwell & Laughlin (2001) and Lennie (2003) establish the metabolic-cost backdrop (spiking dominates the brain's energy budget, forcing sparse ≤~15%-active codes) that makes "run expensive nonlinear circuitry everywhere" energetically infeasible — directly predicting selective, not universal, deployment of conjunctive machinery.
- **Population-level quantification:** Parthasarathy et al. (2022, eNeuro) directly measured the fraction of neurons carrying genuine nonlinear (not just linear-mixed) selectivity: 8.7% of dlPFC neurons, 4.4% of PPC neurons. **>90% of neurons in both regions did not show detectable genuine nonlinear conjunctive tuning.** This is a striking numerical parallel to the ~5-10% interaction-variance fractions found in the genetics/chemistry literature in (a) above — the brain's own allocation of nonlinear/conjunctive resources sits in the same rough regime as the world's genuine interaction-variance fraction.

This is consistent with (not merely analogous to) the world-level finding: if the brain's environment is itself mostly additive with a genuine-but-minority interaction-rich substructure, then a nervous system built under a metabolic cost constraint would be expected to evolve exactly this allocation — cheap linear/additive integration as the default, expensive nonlinear conjunctive coding reserved and spatially/functionally localized for the identifiable minority of cases (correlated/clustered inputs, disambiguation-critical memories) where it earns its cost. The brain is not a counter-example to "genuine interactions are a minority" — it is architected as though it already knows this.

## Cheap decisive test

Re-run the Costanzo-epsilon test from the prior real-data drill (`notes/drill_real_nonadditive_experimental_datasets_for_conjunction_modules_2026-07-15.md`), but **change the subset selection, not the dataset**: instead of a random or |ε|-threshold-only sample of the ~23M pairwise measurements, restrict to the structurally-defined high-confidence pocket —
(i) protein-complex-internal gene pairs (per Bellay et al. 2011's complex-membership annotation, available via the same yeast complex databases used in that paper), or
(ii) paralog-buffering candidate pairs where BOTH single-mutant fitness values are within noise of wild-type (near-zero main effects by direct measurement, not inferred) yet the double-mutant epsilon is large.
Compute the same additive-baseline-vs-conjunction-module comparison used in the prior drill, but ALSO compute and report the interaction-variance-fraction (interaction term variance / total variance) separately for (a) this curated subset and (b) a matched-size random subset of all pairs, as the primary discriminating statistic.

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS** (measurement/aggregation-hiding hypothesis confirmed, genuine pocket exists and is learnable): the curated subset (protein-complex-internal pairs, or paralog-buffering near-zero-singles pairs) shows an interaction-variance-fraction >=3x the matched random-subset baseline, AND the conjunction module achieves >=30% relative held-out error reduction over the additive baseline specifically on this curated subset (same threshold as the prior drill's Kramer test, for comparability across domains).

**HARD-FAIL** (genuine rarity dominates even in the best-available pocket; measurement-hiding hypothesis does not rescue this domain): the curated subset's interaction-variance-fraction is statistically indistinguishable (within noise) from the random-subset baseline — i.e., even the most theoretically-favored pocket (complex-internal, near-zero-singles pairs) does not show materially more non-additivity than the genome-wide average. Also HARD-FAIL if fewer than ~50 clean paralog-buffering pairs (both singles within noise of WT, double significantly deleterious) exist in the available Costanzo/SGA data at sufficient statistical power — insufficient N to test the hypothesis at all, in which case escalate to the enCas12a paralog-buffering compendium (Dede et al., PMC7558751) or the Benchmarking-GI-Scores harmonized compendium (already identified in the prior drill) as a higher-N fallback with the same subset-restriction logic applied.

**Additional falsifiable check (Hill-coefficient pocket, secondary):** if a discrete pairwise dataset can be constructed from allosteric/cooperative-binding literature (e.g., converting ligand-titration curves for high Hill-coefficient proteins into a binary "cooperative vs non-cooperative site-pair" table), the interaction-variance-fraction on that table should be an order of magnitude higher than either the random-subset or curated-genetics baseline, given Hill coefficient's algebraic (not merely empirical) non-additivity guarantee. HARD-FAIL if it is not — would indicate the "algebraically guaranteed" cooperativity argument does not survive translation into a discrete relation-pair data shape, a genuine domain-shape mismatch worth knowing before investing further.

## Substrate-product implications

If the HARD-PASS test above lands, this converts the prior three negatives from "conjunction modules don't help on real data" into a scoping result: "conjunction modules help exactly on the structurally-identifiable interaction-rich pockets (complex-internal pairs, near-zero-singles paralog buffering, and analogous curated subsets in chemistry/pharmacology), not on domain-wide averages" — which is a MORE useful and MORE actionable finding for the PIVOT's foundation-content sourcing than either "conjunctions are everywhere" (refuted) or "conjunctions don't exist in real data" (would have been the wrong conclusion to draw from the prior three negatives alone). It also directly derisks the brain-recommended architecture change (learned bilinear bind, ranked #1 in the 07-14 brain-grounding drill): that architecture is precisely the "expensive machinery reserved for a genuine minority" design the brain itself uses, and this drill supplies the real-data selection criterion (curate to complex-internal/near-zero-singles pockets) for feeding it training signal that actually contains the interaction structure it's meant to learn, rather than diluted genome-wide averages where even the brain-aligned architecture would correctly find nothing to learn.

If HARD-FAIL: the finding narrows further to "genuine rarity dominates even in the best-available real pockets we can currently access" — meaning the interaction-reader's proven synthetic-benchmark capability may only be exercisable on constructed/synthetic data or requires assembling a purpose-built dataset (e.g., converting Hill-coefficient titration curves into discrete pairwise tables) rather than any existing published bulk resource, which is itself a useful scoping result for what NOT to keep re-testing against.
