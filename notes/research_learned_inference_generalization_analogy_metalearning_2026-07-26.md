## HEADLINE

**VSA bind/unbind STORES and RECALLS a fact once it has been written to associative memory (with any consistent key, trained or random) -- it structurally cannot INVENT a fact that was never written.** The substrate's one demonstrated compositional capability is CHAINING already-stored single-hop facts (29578/29579's `hop2_top1=0.467`, composing two stored binds, well above the `0.0003` base-rate floor but far below single-hop 0.836) -- a real, separation-driven, moderate capability, but categorically different from inferring an edge that was never stored in any form. Genuine zero-shot relational generalization -- "concept C, never told it KINDOF D, correctly inferred as KINDOF D because C's OTHER edges resemble a concept E that IS KINDOF D" -- requires a mechanism that does not touch the missing fact's storage slot at all. The literature names exactly two brain-relevant candidates for this: **structure-mapping analogy** (Gentner/SME, neurally instantiated by Hummel-Holyoak LISA/DORA, PFC/parietal-localized) and **meta-learning-for-compositionality** (Lake-Linzen-Baroni MLC, a training-regime account). This note brain-drills both, finds they answer **different sub-problems** (analogy = zero-example projection from a structurally similar known case; MLC = a trained skill for FAST few-example in-context binding, not zero-example invention), picks **structure-mapping analogy as the primary, more directly-matching lever** for this specific ask (no support examples of the missing fact are given), and designs a can-fail cell that excludes the queried edge from ALL storage -- the exact fix the VET named -- with a store-recall floor and a flat-MLP baseline as must-clear controls proving the exclusion is real (no leakage) before any analogy or meta-learning number can be trusted.

## STEP 1 -- Brain-drill (biology first, both candidate mechanisms)

### 1a. Structure-mapping analogy (Gentner SMT; SME; Hummel-Holyoak LISA/DORA; Penn-Holyoak-Povinelli; RLPFC/parietal relational integration)

**Core claim (Gentner 1983, *Cognitive Science*):** analogy is driven by alignment of RELATIONAL STRUCTURE between a base (familiar, well-understood) and a target (novel), not by surface/attribute similarity. The **systematicity principle** prefers importing a predicate embedded in a deeply interconnected, higher-order relational system over an isolated matching predicate -- this is the proposed SELECTION mechanism for which structure transfers, not an independently proven neural fact.

**The exact mechanism this design needs -- candidate inference:** once a structural alignment satisfying parallel connectivity and one-to-one correspondence is found between base and target, statements TRUE OF THE BASE but not yet stated of the target are projected as **candidate inferences** -- conjectural, defeasible claims imported via the established correspondence (Gentner 1983; Falkenhainer, Forbus & Gentner 1986/1989, *Artificial Intelligence*, the Structure Mapping Engine/SME). This is precisely "C has edges like E; E KINDOF D; therefore C KINDOF D" -- SME's candidate-inference output is a standard, measured phenomenon in both human analogy experiments and the computational model, not a hoped-for side effect.

**SME's algorithm (Falkenhainer/Forbus/Gentner 1989):** takes predicate-logic structured representations of base and target (attributes, relations, higher-order relations), builds local pairwise matches, combines them into a globally consistent structurally-parallel mapping (greedy, no backtracking) scored by systematicity, emits unmapped base statements as candidate inferences on the target. **Known, well-documented limits:** requires hand-coded, ALREADY-PARSED relational representations for both domains (cannot extract structure from raw/unstructured input); early versions did not scale to human-sized representations (this motivated corpus-based successors like the Latent Relation Mapping Engine specifically to remove the hand-coding requirement -- a tacit admission of the boundary condition).

**Neural instantiation -- LISA (Hummel & Holyoak 1997, 2003, *Psychological Review*):** role-filler bindings (e.g. agent=John in loves(John,Mary)) are represented via **temporal synchrony** -- filler and role units fire in synchronized phase, distinct propositions desynchronized -- solving the binding problem without a symbolic tag while keeping structure-sensitivity; mapping/correspondence emerges from synchronized-firing self-organization between base and target analogs. **Capacity limit (specific, quantitative, treated as a genuine architectural prediction, not a fitted parameter):** synchrony-binding supports roughly **2-3 simultaneous propositions / 4-6 role-bindings**; larger analogies must be mapped serially, and mapping ORDER strongly affects the final outcome (order-dependence is itself a documented failure mode). **Successor -- DORA (Doumas, Hummel & Sandhofer 2008, *Psychological Review* 115(1):1-43):** extends LISA to model DISCOVERY of relational concepts (e.g. "larger than") from unstructured object-property comparisons rather than assuming pre-existing roles -- the one partial answer to "where does the structured representation come from," still requiring structured object-property inputs, not raw perception.

**Neural basis (converging fMRI/lesion evidence):** Waltz, Knowlton, Holyoak et al. (1999, *Psychological Science*) -- prefrontal-lesion patients show a SELECTIVE, catastrophic deficit in relational integration on both deductive and inductive tasks while anterior-temporal-lesion patients are intact -- direct causal evidence PFC (not temporal cortex) is necessary specifically for integrating multiple relations. Christoff, Bunge, Wendelken et al. localize the mapping/integration step to **left rostrolateral PFC (RLPFC)**, distinguishing it from simpler relational judgment. Green, Kraemer, Fugelsang, Gray & Dunbar (2006, 2010, *Cerebral Cortex*) show **frontopolar cortex activity scales with semantic distance/abstractness** of the analogy. **Halford's Relational Complexity theory** (Halford, Wilson & Phillips 1998, *BBS*) formalizes complexity as the number of variables that must be bound simultaneously, capping unstructured human relational processing at **~4 variables**, with RLPFC activation/network dynamics scaling with this count -- structurally the same kind of capacity claim as LISA's synchrony limit, from an independent (fMRI, not connectionist-modeling) literature.

**Penn, Holyoak & Povinelli (2008, *BBS*, "Darwin's Mistake"):** the human/nonhuman cognitive discontinuity is specifically in capacity for HIGHER-ORDER, SYSTEMATIC, ROLE-GOVERNED relational structure (relations-between-relations) -- nonhuman animals perceive first-order relations/similarities but lack (or severely limit) this capacity, which the authors argue underlies human analogy, causal reasoning, and language broadly. **Contested:** this is an intensely debated BBS target article (adversarial-commentary format guarantees pushback); specific named counter-arguments beyond the general characterization could not be verified this session -- flagged UNVERIFIED.

**Is this validated zero-shot relational inference in humans?** Yes, with severe, well-documented boundary conditions: (i) surface-similarity interference -- humans and unstructured retrieval are strongly biased toward surface-similar (not structurally-similar) matches under naturalistic/uncued retrieval; (ii) capacity limits -- LISA's ~2-3 propositions and Halford's ~4 variables both hard-cap simultaneous relational integration; (iii) **the most important caveat for this design**: SME, LISA, and DORA (partially) all require both base and target already be cast into PRE-PARSED, role-structured relational representations -- the mechanism does not itself extract structure from raw input. "Zero-shot" here means zero-shot GIVEN a pre-existing structured description of the novel entity's OTHER relations, not zero-shot from raw unstructured encounter. This maps directly and favorably onto the substrate's actual situation: WorldTree facts ARE already typed-relation triples (pre-parsed by construction), so this specific SME/LISA boundary condition is already satisfied by the corpus, not a gap this design has to close.

**SHAPE:** a mapping `M: base-relations -> target-relations` that maximizes structural consistency (one-to-one correspondence, parallel connectivity) and systematicity (prefers deeply interconnected relational subsystems); candidate inferences = base statements connected to the mapped system, projected onto the target where not yet known. **PLACE:** SME = symbolic constraint-satisfaction algorithm (no claimed neural locus); LISA/DORA = synchrony-based binding, drawing on left RLPFC/frontopolar cortex for the relational-integration/mapping step (Christoff/Bunge/Wendelken; Green et al.) and implicating parietal cortex in relational representation more broadly; Halford's RC theory gives an independent fMRI-network account of the same capacity limit. **METRIC:** structural/relational overlap (systematicity-weighted isomorphism quality of the mapping), NOT feature/attribute similarity; capacity-gated at ~2-4 simultaneously-integrated relations (LISA and Halford converge independently on this order of magnitude).

### 1b. Meta-learning-for-compositionality (Lake-Linzen-Baroni MLC, *Nature* 2023; Lake-Baroni SCAN, ICML 2018; Lake meta-seq2seq, NeurIPS 2019)

**Training procedure (well-established):** MLC embeds a standard seq2seq/Transformer in a meta-learning OUTER loop. Each episode presents a small "study" set that arbitrarily (re)defines novel pseudo-word-to-meaning mappings plus a few compositional examples using them, then a "query" set testing composition of those SAME novel words. Because the mappings differ every episode, the network cannot memorize fixed word meanings -- it can only succeed by learning a general-purpose, FEW-SHOT, IN-CONTEXT procedure for compositional inference. This is optimization-for-a-skill, not an architectural primitive.

**Empirical result:** a genuine controlled human behavioral experiment (same novel-word stimuli given to human participants and to models). Reported item-accuracy (secondary-source figures, primary Nature text paywalled, flagged as approximate): humans ~=80.7%, MLC ~=82.4%, correlation r=0.79 (p=0.007) between human and model PER-ITEM accuracy -- MLC reproduced the human ERROR PATTERN, not just aggregate accuracy. Vanilla seq2seq and standard Transformer baselines (no meta-training) failed to generalize systematically on the same task; ChatGPT/GPT-4 reportedly performed far worse than both (~5% on one secondary figure).

**SCAN (Lake & Baroni 2018):** vanilla seq2seq RNNs trained on "jump," "run," "run twice," etc. but never "jump twice" fail catastrophically to compose "X twice" onto the held-out primitive, despite near-perfect in-distribution accuracy. Follow-up (Lake 2019, NeurIPS, meta seq2seq) established that purely ARCHITECTURAL fixes (attention, more data, alternative RNN cells, later Transformers) do NOT resolve this -- corroborated by later replications. Resolution required changing the TRAINING REGIME: embedding ordinary seq2seq training inside a meta-learning loop over many support/query task pairs.

**Is MLC a model of human learning or an ML trick? (contested, both readings live in the literature):** Lake's own framing explicitly claims developmental plausibility -- direct quote: *"We're really just trying to train language models in a way that is more like a child learning a new word."* The Nature paper explicitly targets Fodor & Pylyshyn's 1988 systematicity challenge and presents human-error-pattern matching as evidence of cognitive plausibility, not mere performance. **Counter-critique (two 2025-2026 preprints, live dispute):** "Fodor and Pylyshyn's Systematicity Challenge Still Stands" (arXiv:2606.14512) and "Fodor and Pylyshyn's Legacy: Still No Human-like Systematic Compositionality in Neural Networks" (arXiv:2506.01820) both argue MLC demonstrates only surface-level recombination CONTINGENT ON in-context support exemplars supplied at test time, not intrinsic algebraic/systematic compositionality -- i.e., MLC learned HOW TO ADAPT via few-shot exemplars, not a structure-sensitive symbol system.

**The decisive fact for this design -- what MLC requires at test time:** every novel primitive/mapping is introduced via a HANDFUL OF STUDY/SUPPORT EXAMPLES within an episode that itself follows the trained episode structure. MLC has never been shown to generalize to a primitive introduced with ZERO support examples via structural analogy alone -- that scenario sits outside its tested regime. This is precisely why MLC and explicit-binding/analogy approaches are NOT competing on the same claim: MLC buys a general skill for FAST few-example in-context binding (learnability from raw exposure diversity); explicit-binding/analogy approaches buy composition/candidate-inference guaranteed by algebra or structural alignment, with zero support examples of the missing fact, at the cost of requiring pre-structured representations and hitting a hard relational-integration capacity ceiling.

**Developmental plausibility of the "many varied episodes" training regime as a model of human acquisition:** Elman (1993, *Cognition*, "starting small") shows exposure-order/curriculum effects on grammar learning -- conceptually adjacent but not about compositional word-learning specifically and never run through anything resembling MLC's episodic optimization. Tomasello's usage-based/construction-grammar account (*Constructing a Language*, 2003) documents children moving from item-specific "verb islands" to abstract constructions via exposure to many varied usage instances -- a genuine, well-evidenced developmental phenomenon ANALOGICALLY similar to MLC's claim, but not computationally formalized as MLC's episodic training, and no cited work directly tests whether children's trajectory quantitatively matches MLC's dynamics. **Honest assessment: this specific link is speculative** -- suggestive at the conceptual level, not directly evidenced.

**SHAPE:** not a representational primitive -- a TRAINING CURRICULUM: an outer loop sampling many episodes, each redefining meaning, forcing an inner seq2seq/Transformer to learn a general few-shot compositional-inference PROCEDURE. Compositionality lives in learned weights, not an explicit bind operator. **PLACE:** engineering framing = optimizer over episodic loss (MAML-class/in-context learning); Lake's OWN framing = a hypothesis about human developmental acquisition via exposure-diversity, explicitly claimed but NOT neurally localized (no claimed brain region, unlike the analogy literature's RLPFC/parietal evidence) and now actively disputed as to whether it demonstrates genuine systematicity at all. **METRIC:** episodic few-shot accuracy on novel primitive-meaning mappings never seen in ANY training episode -- but the novel item is ALWAYS introduced via a few in-episode support examples; the skill measured is fast adaptation-from-support, not literally zero-example invention.

### 1c. Pick, and why they compose rather than compete

**For THIS specific ask** -- infer a held-out relational edge about a concept with NO support examples of that edge given, not even a few -- **structure-mapping analogy is the directly-matching mechanism** and MLC, as literally specified, is not: MLC's demonstrated capability requires a study/support set for every novel mapping, which is precisely the thing this design must NOT supply (supplying a few examples of the missing KINDOF edge would make the test trivial and would not be the "genuine generalization" fix the VET named). Analogy's candidate-inference mechanism, by contrast, is architecturally zero-example BY CONSTRUCTION: it projects from a DIFFERENT, already-known base concept's structure, never from examples of the target fact itself. Analogy's own boundary condition (pre-parsed relational representation required) is ALREADY satisfied by WorldTree's typed-triple format, so this is the more tractable lever on this substrate today, not just the more brain-true one for this specific ask.

**Where they could compose (a live secondary hypothesis, not this cell's primary bet):** MLC's actual contribution is a TRAINING REGIME that teaches a general skill from exposure diversity, not a representational primitive. Nothing in principle stops using that regime to TRAIN the analogy machinery itself -- i.e., meta-learn the alignment-scoring/candidate-inference weights (which base-concept to project from, which candidate inferences to trust) across many synthetic held-out-edge episodes drawn from WorldTree, rather than hand-tuning a fixed cosine-alignment rule. This would be "meta-learning as the training regime for structure-mapping's selection mechanism" -- a genuine composition of both literatures, not a fallback. This is named as the STEP 2 pre-registered fallback below, not built first, because the primary analogy mechanism should be tested in its simplest, most brain-faithful (hand-specified alignment metric) form before adding a trained selector on top of it.

### 2.1 The fix: exclusion, not just novelty

29578/29579's Split A withheld a concept from `g`-TRAINING but still WROTE its edges into the associative memory `M` at test time (code-confirmed, lines 609-611). This design's Split A' withholds a concept's edge under one SPECIFIC (relation, slot) from **every stage that could store it**: it never enters `train_facts`, never enters `M`, never enters any bind/store call, for that one (relation, slot, concept) triple. The concept MAY still appear in OTHER stored relations (as Gentner's mechanism requires -- analogy needs the target to have SOME known relational structure to align on; a concept with literally zero known edges cannot be analogized to anything, which matches the honest brain-true boundary condition named in Step 1a, not a design shortcut).

### 2.2 Mechanism (analogy arm, primary)

1. **Relational profile** `P(concept)` = the bundle (VSA-style sum, or a sparse feature vector -- decided at build time by `hdi_exp_dev`, NOT this note) of all `(relation_type, slot, neighbor)` edges the concept participates in, EXCLUDING the held-out (relation, slot) under test. This is a per-concept vector built ONLY from stored, non-excluded edges.
2. **Alignment scoring:** for query concept `C` (missing its `R`-slot-`s` edge), score every OTHER known concept `E` by structural similarity of `P(C)` to `P(E)` computed ONLY over relation-types OTHER than `R` (excluding the tested relation entirely from the alignment score -- this is the "align on everything else, project the one thing that's missing" move that IS Gentner's candidate-inference mechanism, and also the leak-proofing: the alignment score must never see the relation being predicted).
3. **Candidate inference:** among the top-K structurally-aligned `E`'s that DO have a stored `R`-slot-`s` edge to some `D`, project `C --R--> D` as the candidate answer (majority vote or highest-similarity single source, both reported).
4. **No bind/unbind used for the inference step itself** -- this is a similarity-and-projection mechanism over relational profiles, not a store/retrieve operation. (Binding primitives are reused ONLY inside the store-recall FLOOR arm below, to reproduce what "the substrate already knows how to do" looks like on this exact excluded-fact split.)

### 2.3 Mechanism (meta-learning arm, secondary/fallback per Step 1c)

A small learned scorer (replacing step 2.2's fixed cosine alignment with a trained weighting over profile-feature-dimensions) trained via an MLC-style episodic regime: sample many synthetic held-out-edge episodes from the OTHER 3,568 WorldTree triples (excluding the actual test triples entirely), each episode = (query concept's partial profile, candidate base concepts' profiles, which base's edge is correct) -- train the scorer to pick the correct base across episodes. Evaluated on the SAME final excluded-edge test set as the analogy arm. This directly operationalizes "meta-learning trains the analogy selection mechanism" from Step 1c, and is the fallback path if the fixed-metric analogy arm undershoots (Step 1c's honest composition hypothesis, tested not assumed).

### 2.4 Required floor and baseline arms (must be run, and must land where predicted, before analogy/meta-learning numbers can be trusted)

- **STORE_RECALL_FLOOR:** identical bind/unbind/cleanup pipeline as 29578/29579's SINGLE_HOP_RANDOM_BIND arm, but run on THIS cell's exclusion-enforced split (the fact truly never stored). **Predicted: near the base-rate floor (~0.0003, i.e. 1/n_dict), NOT 0.836** -- this is the single most important number in the whole cell: if this floor comes back anywhere near 29578/29579's 0.836, the exclusion was not correctly enforced (a leakage bug, respec before reporting anything else). If it lands near chance, that is the clean confirmation that "bind cannot invent" and the direct empirical contrast this whole design exists to produce.
- **FLAT-MLP baseline:** `train_flat` reused verbatim (29556's mechanism) on this cell's exclusion-enforced `train_facts`. **Predicted: `ho_lift ~ 0`** (matches 29556's own prior result; the concat+MLP has no mechanism for inference beyond its training distribution either).
- **Must-fail controls (analogy arm):**
  - `SCRAMBLED_ANALOGY_SOURCE` -- randomly reassign which base concept's edge is projected (destroys the alignment, preserves marginal relation-type frequencies). Must collapse to base-rate/relation-frequency floor.
  - `SHUFFLED_PROFILE` -- randomly permute which concept owns which relational profile before scoring. Must collapse.
  - `RANDOM_ALIGNMENT` (necessity control) -- replace the cosine/structural similarity score with a random score, keeping everything else identical. Isolates whether the ALIGNMENT quality (not just "having some candidate-inference machinery") is doing the work -- this is the exact control that caught 29578/29579's degenerate result, reused here on purpose.
- **Must-fail controls (meta-learning arm, if built):** `SHUFFLED_SUPPORT_EPISODES` -- train the scorer on episodes with randomized correct-base labels. Must collapse the trained scorer to the RANDOM_ALIGNMENT floor.

### 2.5 Primary metric

Top-1 / top-10 accuracy predicting the value of a held-out relational edge for a `(concept, relation, slot)` triple that was excluded from EVERY storage/training stage (not just `g`-training as in 29578/29579), against the full concept dictionary (~3,431 concepts) as the candidate pool, reported separately for: STORE_RECALL_FLOOR, FLAT-MLP, ANALOGY (primary), META-LEARNED-SCORER (secondary), and all must-fail controls. Also report, as a secondary diagnostic (not gating): accuracy broken down by the queried concept's OUT-DEGREE in the non-excluded relations (per Gentner's own boundary condition, a concept with richer known structure should analogize better -- a positive degree-accuracy correlation would itself be informative confirmation of the mechanism, a flat one would suggest something other than structural richness is driving any observed lift).

### 2.6 Pre-registered bands

**HARD-PASS:** ANALOGY (or META-LEARNED-SCORER) top-1 clears STORE_RECALL_FLOOR by a decisive margin -- specifically **>=10x the base-rate floor in absolute terms, or >=15 percentage points, whichever is larger** -- AND clears FLAT-MLP by a comparable margin AND all must-fail controls (SCRAMBLED_ANALOGY_SOURCE, SHUFFLED_PROFILE, RANDOM_ALIGNMENT) collapse to within noise of the STORE_RECALL_FLOOR AND STORE_RECALL_FLOOR itself lands at/near the base-rate floor (confirming no leakage -- a non-collapsed floor invalidates the whole cell's comparison regardless of the analogy number) AND accuracy shows a positive relationship with queried-concept out-degree (Gentner's own predicted boundary condition, checked not assumed).

**HARD-FAIL:** ANALOGY ties STORE_RECALL_FLOOR and FLAT-MLP (within ~5 points, both near the base-rate floor) -- meaning structural-profile alignment, even implemented correctly with leak-proof exclusion, does not manufacture genuine relational invention on this corpus; OR any must-fail control fails to collapse (construction/leakage bug -- respec, do not report as a mechanism refutation); OR STORE_RECALL_FLOOR itself comes back elevated (near 0.8, matching 29578/29579) -- meaning the exclusion enforcement itself failed and the ENTIRE cell must be re-specified before any conclusion is drawn.

**MIDDLE_BAND:** ANALOGY clears the floor and FLAT-MLP by 5-15 points with controls holding -- a real but modest signal, informative and reportable ("structural alignment carries SOME genuine inference signal on WorldTree's relation diversity, short of a clean win") -- or ANALOGY beats the floor decisively but the accuracy-vs-out-degree relationship is flat/absent (would contradict Gentner's own predicted mechanism even if the raw number looks good, and should be reported as a possible confound -- e.g. relation-type base-rate skew rather than genuine structural matching -- worth a follow-up diagnostic before claiming the mechanism).
