## HEADLINE

1. **Metacognition/abstain: three components are cheap-buildable NOW, all closed-form.** Chow's 1970 reject rule (threshold on calibrated posterior) + post-hoc OOD scores (margin/entropy/Mahalanobis, all portable from any similarity list) + Fleming's meta-d'/M-ratio (as a GRADING metric, generator-agnostic) are all ADOPT-tier, zero-training. SelectiveNet's training mechanism is NOT-APPLICABLE (needs backprop); its risk-coverage/AURC evaluation framework is ADOPT. Kiani-Shadlen's "iterations-to-converge as an independent confidence cue" (orthogonal to margin) is a genuinely NEW candidate signal, ADAPT-tier, contingent on the substrate's inference loop having exploitable convergence-time variance (unverified this cycle).
2. **`hdlab/conformal.py` is correct but INCOMPLETE as a production abstain path -- not "ready" as shipped.** The split-conformal quantile formula is textbook-correct (glass-box, closed-form). Three cheap additions are needed and none require a neural net: (a) Mondrian/class-conditional per-partition quantiles (group-by + quantile, floor ~10 calibration points/partition), (b) Gibbs-Candes adaptive online alpha-update for distribution drift (`alpha_{t+1} = alpha_t + gamma*(alpha - miscoverage_indicator)`), (c) an explicit singleton/empty/multi set-size-to-abstain-decision rule (standard practice, not novel, currently absent from the code). Verdict: **conformal is the right FRAMEWORK, not yet the ready PATH** -- ship the 3 additions before calling this the abstain mechanism.
3. **Attention/salience: the two strongest zero-training candidates are Kalman-gain-style precision-weighting (`K = sigma_prior^2/(sigma_prior^2+sigma_obs^2)` from moving-window empirical variance) and Reynolds-Heeger divisive normalization (`R(x) = [E(x)*A(x)]/[sigma+S(x)]`, a closed-form competition-plus-gain formula).** Both need only per-candidate scores the substrate should already compute (a drive/error score + a reliability/gain estimate). Sparsemax (closed-form Euclidean projection onto the simplex) is a clean sparse-selection layer to sit on top of either. All three: buildable cheap NOW, no learned network.
4. **The substrate's existing "surprise" ingest signal (already load-bearing per USER 07-16, ALL-3-INGEST-SIGNALS) is mathematically the SAME FAMILY as IDF/self-information and Itti-Koch center-surround contrast** -- i.e., a version of item-level salience is probably already implemented. The scour identifies TWO likely-missing pieces: (a) a source-reliability multiplier (Kalman-gain / precision-weighting) that down-weights salience from noisy/unreliable channels -- currently the surprise signal is probably unweighted by source-reliability; (b) an explicit hard-gate stage (sparsemax or top-k) to actually cap downstream processing -- currently likely a fixed threshold rather than an adaptive competitive selection. This is the actionable delta, not a rebuild.

## Cheap decisive test

**Test A (metacognition/conformal -- pure CPU, synthetic, no corpus needed).** Extend `hdlab/conformal.py` with (a) a per-partition quantile function (group calibration scores by partition label, floor-fallback to global quantile below N=10/partition), (b) an online ACI alpha-update function per the Gibbs-Candes formula, (c) a `decide_abstain(prediction_set)` helper returning {ANSWER, ABSTAIN_UNKNOWN, ABSTAIN_AMBIGUOUS} from set cardinality. Synthetic smoke: generate calibration nonconformity scores from distribution A; generate a test stream that SLOWLY DRIFTS from distribution A to distribution B over T steps (a controlled, known distribution-shift schedule). Compare realized coverage of (i) vanilla static split-conformal vs (ii) ACI-adaptive over the drift window.

**Test B (attention/salience -- pure CPU, synthetic, no corpus needed).** Build a 3-stage pipeline: item-level surprisal/salience score -> Kalman-gain reliability multiplier (estimated from a moving window of recent per-channel error variance) -> sparsemax hard-gate to select the top sparse subset. Synthetic smoke: multiple candidate "channels," some with planted HIGH variance/noise (superficially surprising but unreliable) and some with planted LOW variance (reliable). Ground truth: a planted "truly important" subset independent of noise level. Measure precision@k of the selected subset under (i) raw-surprisal-only ranking vs (ii) reliability-weighted ranking.

## Falsifiable predictions (HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL)

**Test A:**
- HARD-PASS: ACI-adaptive realized coverage stays within [1-alpha-0.03, 1-alpha+0.03] throughout the drift window, while vanilla static split-conformal's realized coverage drifts outside that band by >=0.05 by the drift's end. This demonstrates the adaptive addition is load-bearing (not decorative) and confirms the "silent coverage decay under drift" risk flagged by the lit-scan.
- HARD-FAIL: ACI-adaptive shows equal-or-worse coverage stability than vanilla static conformal across the same drift schedule. If this happens, do NOT add the online-adaptive layer -- fall back to periodic (scheduled) recalibration instead, which is simpler and the lit-scan found no evidence ACI beats it absent an actual non-stationary target.
- Mondrian per-partition test: HARD-PASS if per-partition realized coverage is within [1-alpha-0.05, 1-alpha+0.05] for partitions with >=10 calibration points, AND the fallback-to-global rule keeps small partitions from blowing the band by more than the same margin. HARD-FAIL if per-partition quantiles are UNSTABLE (coverage variance across repeated resamples > 2x the marginal case) even above the N=10 floor -- would mean the floor needs raising before shipping.

**Test B:**
- HARD-PASS: reliability-weighted salience improves precision@k for the planted "truly important" subset by >=10 percentage points over raw-surprisal-only ranking, under injected channel noise, at k matched to the planted subset size.
- HARD-FAIL: reliability-weighting shows <=2 percentage points improvement (noise) over raw surprisal-only ranking. If this happens, the Kalman-gain machinery isn't earning its complexity -- ship raw-surprisal + sparsemax hard-gate only (2-stage, not 3-stage), and defer precision-weighting until real noisy-corpus telemetry shows an actual reliability gradient across sources.

## Substrate-product implications

- The abstain layer is the direct enabler of the auditable-AI positioning: a system that can say "I don't know" with a distribution-free coverage GUARANTEE (not just a soft confidence number) is a defensible, explainable claim to make to an auditor. The Mondrian/conditional-coverage addition specifically lets the product make PER-CATEGORY guarantees ("for queries of type X, we are wrong at most alpha% of the time"), which is a much stronger auditor-facing claim than a single marginal number.
- The adaptive/online conformal addition (Gibbs-Candes) is the direct answer to "what happens when real users start asking different kinds of questions than the calibration set anticipated" -- this is the single most likely failure mode of a static-calibration abstain gate in production, and the fix is ~10 lines of update rule, not a retraining cycle.
- The salience/attention layer is highest-risk-once-real-corpora-land per the framing brief; the good news from this scour is that the two strongest candidates (Kalman-gain reliability weighting, sparsemax hard-gate) are both closed-form and can be smoke-tested on synthetic noisy-channel data BEFORE any real corpus ingestion, derisking the "attention risk" ahead of the actual ingestion milestone.
- Both elements keep the glass-box invariant fully intact: nothing in either recommended build requires an external LLM or an opaque trained network at runtime; every formula is closed-form given inputs the substrate should already be computing (similarity scores, partition membership, recent error variance, pairwise similarity for centrality).
