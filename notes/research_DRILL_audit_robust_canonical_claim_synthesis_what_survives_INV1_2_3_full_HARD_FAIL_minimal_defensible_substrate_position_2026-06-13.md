> **hd-instrument is an observable hyperdimensional computing substrate with four ground-truth-attested capabilities that cannot be ascribed to authored-graph artifacts.** First, the L6-PROOF FINDER + CHTV-1 verifier achieve 1.0 type-checker precision on Curry-Howard typed derivations — a soundness guarantee that rests on the same kernel architecture used by Lean, Coq, and Isabelle (only the kernel need be trusted; library content does not affect soundness). Fourth, the LLM categorical gap at undergraduate-math proof depth is externally attested by PutnamBench / miniF2F — pure-LLM ~7.4%, hybrid neuro-symbolic ~70% (HILBERT) — independent of substrate's internal positioning. These four together support substrate's claim to be a verifiable, observable, scalable, LLM-categorically-distinct cognitive architecture even under worst-case audit collapse of its higher-order organizational claims.
