# Brain-fidelity drill: how does the brain comprehend a novel/OOV outcome event? (angle B of 3x triangulation)

Filed: 2026-08-09 | Director-requested | 4 parallel Sonnet lit-scan sub-agents (schema/script; predictive-coding/N400; grounded-conceptual-generalization; adversarial discrete-event-typing) + Sonnet synthesis.

## HEADLINE

The OOV wall is substantially an artifact of the SHAPE of our current mechanism, not an intrinsic ceiling — but not for the naive reason ("brain does no typing, pure vibes-based fit"). The brain demonstrably DOES maintain real, neurally-decodable discrete event/schema-category representations (Baldassano, Hasson & Norman 2018 decode script-identity from cortex at 69-83% accuracy). What it does NOT do is bottom-up-classify an incoming clause's literal surface form against a large OPEN inventory of fixed event-types one at a time. Instead: a SMALL set of schemas is already active (biased-competition among live candidates, gated by a congruency detector — mPFC in SLIMM), each active schema GENERATES a specific top-down content prediction for its next slot (Metusalem et al. 2012; Rabovsky, Hansen & McClelland 2018), and the incoming clause is evaluated by GRADED, CONTINUOUS FIT against that prediction (N400-as-prediction-error), not by discrete re-classification. Novel surface forms reach that fit-check via RELATIONAL/COMPOSITIONAL decomposition (structure-mapping / decomposable-idiom bridging: Bowdle & Gentner 2005; Gibbs & Nayak 1989), not via literal lexicon lookup or distributional black-box embedding. This is a coherent, convergent, four-angle-independent reframe: replace "bottom-up type-match against N fixed event classes" with "closed-set schema selection (already active, goal-conditioned) + graded relational-fit-check against the winning schema's predicted slot," using components the substrate already owns (maintained goal focus-vector, superposition/cleanup as biased-competition schema selection, bind/bundle for relational composition).

**Confidence: raw ~0.70-0.75 (strong four-angle convergence on the qualitative claim); after mandatory lit-scan calibration deflation (0.15-0.25) and novel-synthesis cap: P_deflated = 0.50.** No direct precedent exists for this exact reframe implemented in a VSA/FHRR substrate — this is architectural synthesis from converging biology, not a replicated engineering result.

## Cheap decisive test

Re-run the SAME real DesireDB outcome-sentence sample that currently produces ~2/39 (~5%) firing on the bottom-up lexicon/construction-match organ, but swap the decision rule:

1. **Keep extraction as-is for now** (do not wait on POS-aware extraction — that is a separate, already-flagged co-requisite, see Cross-thread synthesis).
2. **Relationally decompose** the extracted outcome-clause head + arguments via WordNet/ConceptNet hops (reuse the already-wired bind/bundle relational-fusion infra from `research_concept_grounding_fusion_prior_art_2026-08-08.md` — PSI/Cohen-2012-style bind+bundle over KG neighborhoods, decay-weighted hop expansion). This produces a candidate "outcome-content" vector, NOT a literal-word match.
3. **Build a goal-conditioned predicted-fulfillment template**: from the already-extracted active goal (e.g. "win the race," "get financial security"), construct a small superposition vector of the goal's head-concept plus its WordNet satisfaction/completion-relation neighbors (this is the "active schema's predicted slot content" — the SG/Rabovsky-style top-down prediction, done symbolically/relationally instead of via a trained connectionist net).
4. **Compute fit, not type**: FHRR dot-product / cosine between the candidate outcome-content vector and the goal-predicted template. Threshold decides fire vs no-fire; sign/magnitude decides satisfied vs not-satisfied.
5. Score on the SAME held-out set used for the 2/39 reckoning, plus the broader ~90%-OOV DesireDB outcome sample. Compare COVERAGE (fire rate) and ACCURACY (macro-F1 vs gold) against (a) current bottom-up mechanism, (b) the trivial keyword rule (macro=0.620 per the 2026-08-08 reckoning), (c) field ceiling references (DesireDB's own But-Present-alone F1=0.68, Skip-Thought LSTM F1=0.70).

This is cheap because every component already exists (goal extraction, bind/bundle relational fusion, WordNet grounding, FHRR dot-product) — it is a REWIRING of existing owned primitives into a new connectivity (goal-conditioned fit-check replacing fixed-ontology type-match), not new infrastructure. Estimated cost: same order as the reckoning's original cell, roughly 1 exp_dev cycle.

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS (validates the reframe — dispatch full build):**
- P1 COVERAGE: fit-check produces an above-threshold signal on >=50% of previously-OOV outcome sentences (vs current ~5-10% bottom-up firing rate).
- P2 ACCURACY-ON-NEWLY-COVERED: among newly-firing sentences, label agreement (satisfied/not vs DesireDB gold) reaches macro-F1 >= 0.45 — meaningfully above chance for this class balance, moving toward (not required to beat) the corpus's own trivial-rule 0.62 and field ceiling 0.70.
- P3 NO REGRESSION: the sentences the current bottom-up mechanism already gets right stay correct (or any regression is individually explained, not silent).

**HARD-FAIL (falsifies or qualifies the reframe):**
- F1 COVERAGE-FAIL: fit-check still fires on <20% of previously-OOV sentences. This would NOT refute the schema-fit reframe itself — it would confirm the blocker is upstream, in extraction (real outcomes are non-lexical / POS-mis-tagged before decomposition ever runs), matching the already-standing MEMORY diagnosis that POS-aware outcome extraction is the next hard blocker. Verdict in this case: reframe is necessary-but-not-sufficient; sequence POS-aware extraction FIRST, then retest this cell.
- F2 DISTORTION-FAIL: coverage jumps (>=50%) but accuracy craters below macro-F1 0.35. This is the literature-predicted failure mode (Piagetian assimilation-distortion; Graesser et al. 1979's "everything gets forced into the nearest script slot"). Fix in this case is NOT to abandon the reframe but to add the missing congruency-GATE component: an explicit "is ANY candidate schema plausibly active at all" pre-check (mPFC-analog, thresholded) before allowing fit-scoring to fire, rather than always collapsing to nearest goal regardless of relevance.
- F3 NULL-FAIL: no measurable behavioral difference from the current mechanism (relational decomposition collapses back to near-exact lexical overlap in practice). This would refute the specific claim that relational-hop distance buys real generalization beyond what literal matching already gets.

## Cross-thread synthesis

Direct mechanism answer to the drilled question, per source:

1. **Schema/script slot-prediction (Ghosh & Gilboa 2014; Tse et al. 2007/2011 Science; van Kesteren et al. SLIMM 2010/2012/2013; Schank & Abelson 1977; Bower, Black & Turner 1979 false-recognition of unstated script content; Metusalem et al. 2012 event-knowledge N400).** ESTABLISHED that schemas are associative networks with pre-specified role/slots, that a pre-existing schema enables fast one-trial hippocampus-independent integration of schema-CONSISTENT novel content (mPFC as congruency/resonance gate), and that active schemas top-down pre-activate specific expected slot content before it arrives. CONTESTED/bounded: schema-INCONSISTENT content does not get this fast path — it falls back to slow hippocampal encoding, or gets DISTORTED to force a false fit (the exact F2 failure mode above); atypical-but-still-scripted content needs a bolt-on "tag" (Graesser et al. 1979) rather than being natively handled. This is the direct precedent for the congruency-gate fallback named in F2.

2. **Predictive coding / N400-as-fit (Friston 2010; Kuperberg & Jaeger 2016; Rabovsky, Hansen & McClelland 2018 Nature Human Behaviour).** ESTABLISHED that N400 tracks graded, continuous fit/prediction-error in a DISTRIBUTED meaning representation, not discrete lexical-identity classification — Rabovsky's single-mechanism Sentence-Gestalt model reproduces 16 distinct N400 phenomena. Directly maps onto FHRR dot-product fit as the substrate-native analog of this signal. The specific extension to goal/outcome sentences in narrative comprehension is SPECULATIVE-TO-PLAUSIBLE (stitched from adjacent literatures, no single direct study found) — flag this as the weakest link in the chain, appropriately reflected in the P_deflated cap. Counter-evidence duly noted: Huettig & Mani (2016) and the DeLong et al. replication failure (Nieuwland et al. 2018 eLife) show prediction-strength claims are field-CONTESTED and not universal/automatic — fit-checking should be treated as the PRIMARY first-pass mechanism with a secondary compositional-repair process for poor-fit cases, not literally the only thing happening.

3. **Grounded/relational generalization of novel phrases (Bowdle & Gentner 2005 Career of Metaphor; Gibbs & Nayak 1989 idiom decomposition; Collins & Loftus 1975 spreading activation; Falkenhainer/Forbus/Gentner 1986 structure-mapping).** Novel metaphors/idioms are processed via STRUCTURAL/RELATIONAL comparison and compositional bridging from literal parts, not holistic lookup and not raw distributional-embedding similarity. This directly supports using WordNet/ConceptNet relational hops (already-wired bind/bundle infra) as the "how does a never-seen phrase reach a known concept" bridge, and explicitly disqualifies pure-distributional-embedding as both cognitively insufficient (needs compositional scaffold per Mitchell & Lapata 2010, Baroni & Zamparelli 2010) and glass-box-incompatible.

4. **Adversarial check — is fixed discrete event-type classification actually how the brain does it? (Baldassano, Chen et al. 2017 Neuron; Baldassano, Hasson & Norman 2018 J Neurosci; Franklin, Norman, Ranganath, Zacks & Gershman 2020 SEM model; Guler, Serin & Gunseli 2025).** This is the important correction to a naive reading of points 1-3. Discrete, neurally-DECODABLE schema-identity representations genuinely exist (69-83% classification accuracy for script identity in mPFC/PMC/SFG, generalizing across stories/subjects/modalities) — categories are REAL, not eliminable. Zacks' own successor model (SEM, 2020) explicitly formalizes event types as discrete latent categories inferred via Bayesian nonparametric clustering SITTING ON TOP OF continuous dynamics — i.e., the field's own lineage tried "pure continuous fit, no categories" and judged it insufficient, adding discrete category-selection back in. Güler et al. (2025) found category/context-transition dominates over raw prediction-error magnitude in driving segmentation. HOWEVER — and this is the load-bearing distinction — the evidence does NOT support a sequential "classify-first-then-evaluate-fit" TWO-STAGE architecture. Category "recognition" in SEM is implemented AS Bayesian selection among a SMALL set of stored schema templates, chosen by how well each one's generated predictions fit the input — functionally identical to competitive fit-scoring among a few active candidates, with the winning schema then supplying the prediction. Infant data (Baldwin, Baird, Saylor & Clark 2001) show goal-tracking and event-boundary/category placement are yoked from early development, not staged. Clinical dissociation evidence is genuinely mixed (Farag et al. 2010 shows a separable script-clustering deficit in FTD, arguing for real machinery) but does not isolate "no category available" from "prediction available but degraded" as the clean test would require.

**Synthesis verdict:** the four angles converge on a specific, non-naive reframe: keep categories (a small, ACTIVE, goal/context-gated set — not eliminate them), but replace OPEN bottom-up exhaustive type-matching with CLOSED competitive schema-selection (biased competition among already-active candidates) + graded relational fit-check against the winning schema's predicted content. The substrate already owns every primitive this needs: a maintained goal focus-vector (= the active schema's predicted-slot content generator), superposition-collapse/cleanup (= competitive schema selection, directly the SEM mechanism), bind/bundle (= relational composition of novel phrases into comparable content, the structure-mapping/decomposition bridge), and an MDL learner (= could eventually learn which WordNet relation-paths reliably predict fit, rather than hand-specifying them). Nothing new needs to be invented architecturally — this is a rewiring/reframing of existing components, which is why the cheap decisive test above is genuinely cheap.

## Substrate-product implications

If P1-P3 land: the outcome-comprehension wall that currently caps real-prose coverage at ~5-10% (referent-bound affect-binding organ) or forces below-trivial-rule accuracy (owned WordNet-polarity grounding, 0.405 vs 0.620) could move toward the field's own black-box ceiling (Skip-Thought F1=0.70) WHILE REMAINING FULLY GLASS-BOX — every relational hop and every fit score is inspectable, which is the actual product differentiator (auditable AI comprehension, not "trust the LLM"). This is a shape-of-mechanism fix, not a new-data or new-infrastructure ask: it reuses wired bind/bundle relational-fusion, wired goal-extraction, and requires only (a) a small new "goal-conditioned predicted-fulfillment template" constructor and (b) a fit-threshold decision layer in place of the current type-match decision layer. It does NOT remove the need for POS-aware outcome extraction (already the standing next-hard-thing per MEMORY) — F1 above specifies exactly how to tell, cheaply, whether extraction or the fit-check itself is the dominant remaining blocker, which sequences the two pieces of work correctly instead of guessing.
