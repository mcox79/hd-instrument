## (a) HEADLINE

**The brain has the exact same problem, and the biology gives a precise, three-way-split answer — not a flat yes or a flat no.** (1) **IDENTITY/individuation** — is this new relation a coherent, real, non-noise category, distinguishable from another new relation — is achievable glass-box, and the mechanism is NOT "argument-type signature" (which the literature explicitly and repeatedly shows is too coarse — Gleitman's own worked cross-linguistic example is two verbs sharing an identical argument frame with different meanings) but **repeated structural comparison across multiple exposures of the same new relation**, empirically validated as sufficient for schema induction with no external grounding event (Doumas & Hummel's DORA model; Kotovsky & Gentner's progressive alignment). (2) **CLASS-level meaning** — roughly what KIND of relation a new verb denotes (eating-like vs. locative-like vs. aggressive-like) — is also achievable glass-box, via structure-mapping/systematicity alignment to the schemas of already-known relations (Gentner's SME; the RoleMap model), which is the direct computational analog of what syntactic bootstrapping itself gives children: a coarse semantic **class**, not a full meaning. (3) **Fine-grained/idiosyncratic MEANING** — the exact content that distinguishes one same-class new verb from another (why "grims" specifically means whatever it means, as opposed to any other same-class relation) — **is a genuine, literature-confirmed structural bound, not an engineering gap**: Pinker's event-structure/root-meaning split and Levin's systematic/idiosyncratic split both explicitly state that syntactic/distributional/structural evidence asymptotes at class membership and categorically cannot recover idiosyncratic content, no matter how much of it accumulates; closing that residue in children requires a categorically different evidence type (situated event observation, joint attention, intention-reading, or explicit definition) that a no-LLM, no-sensorimotor substrate structurally lacks. **This means the honest verdict is split, not "fundamental limit" full-stop**: relation IDENTITY and relation CLASS are legitimately achievable glass-box upgrades over the current construction-grade state (worth building); relation MEANING-VERIFICATION-in-full is a real wall, matching the brain's own wall, and should be labeled as needing external grounding rather than chased further with pure structural machinery.

## 1. The brain's own verb-learning problem (full detail)

**Syntactic bootstrapping gives coarse structure, explicitly and by design, not identity.** Gleitman's core mechanism: because many verb-denoted events are not directly, unambiguously observable, children use the syntactic frame (transitivity, argument count, sentential complements) to narrow the hypothesis space for a verb's meaning — the "zoom-lens hypothesis" (Gleitman & Gillette): syntax narrows an otherwise huge candidate set to a smaller set of scene-construals, an online interpretive procedure, not a direct lookup. Pinker's (1994) formal decomposition makes the ceiling explicit: verb meaning = **event structure** (largely recoverable from syntax: causation, participant count, path/goal) + **root meaning** (idiosyncratic content — what specifically differentiates *crying* from *laughing*, both intransitive with identical event structure). **No source in this cycle's lit-scan claims argument-structure signature alone can distinguish two novel verbs sharing an identical frame** — this is a well-established, explicitly acknowledged limit, illustrated by a genuine cross-linguistic minimal pair (Wilkins' Mparntwe Arrernte "arrerne"/put vs. "are"/see, sharing an identical three-argument frame) and by Landau & Gleitman's (1985) study of blind children struggling specifically with *look* vs. *see* — a case where frame-based syntax underdetermines the distinction.

**Why verbs specifically, not just "harder in general": relational individuation is structurally different from object individuation.** Gentner's Natural Partitions Hypothesis: objects are "cohesive, bounded, temporally stable percepts" that exist pre-linguistically as candidate units a word can be pointed at; relations have no such pre-given unit — a verb "selects" from available relational information in a way that varies across languages and even across speakers describing the identical event (*chase* vs. *flee*, *buy* vs. *sell* — same event, different argument-perspective lexicalization). Gentner calls this "Relational Relativity": there is categorically more freedom in how relations get carved up and named than in how objects do. This is NOT eliminated by verb-friendly typology (Korean/Mandarin show earlier/heavier verb acquisition than English, per Tardif 1996 and Choi & Gopnik, driven by pro-drop syntax and verb-heavy caregiver input) — even in those languages, relational category learning remains comparison-dependent and developmentally effortful at the conceptual level; typology shifts WHEN verbs are learnable, not WHETHER structural individuation is inherently harder than object individuation.

**Dowty's proto-roles and Levin's diathesis-alternation classes both narrow to a CLASS, with an explicit, named ceiling below that.** Dowty's Proto-Agent/Proto-Patient entailment clusters do real compositional work (X causes a change of state in Y -> transitive-causative, roughly which argument is subject) but many distinct verbs share an identical proto-role profile (*break, shatter, crack, snap, smash*). Levin's own stated conclusion about her 80+ diathesis-alternation verb classes: they identify "class-defining meaning components... but will not provide an analysis for the idiosyncratic aspects of the meaning of a particular verb" — *punch* vs. *slap* (same `hit`-class alternation behavior) are structurally identical and differ only in idiosyncratic manner content invisible to syntax/alternation evidence.

**The residual gap is closed in children by a categorically different evidence type, not more of the same.** Convergent citations: Tomasello's social-pragmatic theory (joint attention + intention-reading — grounding in the SPEAKER's communicative intent, often non-ostensively); situated cross-situational tracking of the actual event (Scott & Fisher 2012; Yurovsky et al.) which resolves SOME residual ambiguity but presupposes access to the situated event itself, something a no-sensorimotor substrate lacks; and explicit definition. **The honest synthesis across all three lit-scan lanes: verb/relational meaning is not evidence-QUANTITY-limited (accumulate more text/structure and it resolves), it is evidence-TYPE-limited (structure asymptotes at class; only situated/intentional/definitional evidence closes the remaining, structurally irreducible gap to individual identity).** This is the single most load-bearing, most convergently-corroborated finding across all three lanes.

## 2. What DOES work glass-box — structural comparison for identity, structural alignment for class

**Identity/individuation without any known-relation match: DORA-style repeated structural comparison, empirically validated.** Doumas, Hummel & Sandhofer's DORA model (2008, *Psychological Review*; tested Doumas & Hummel 2013, *PLOS ONE*) discovers and individuates genuinely NEW relational concepts via iterative comparison across repeated, un-pre-specified instances — no matching to a pre-existing known relation required, and notably, labeling/language played no demonstrated role in the empirical test; comparison-driven structural alignment alone sufficed. Kotovsky & Gentner's progressive-alignment work independently shows the same: comparing multiple instances of a still-unlabeled relation, especially starting with high-surface-similarity pairs and moving to more disparate ones, abstracts a common relational schema from what the instances share — schema INDUCTION from comparison, distinct from mapping onto a known category. This is a **real, literature-validated, glass-box-implementable mechanism** — the current cell's `_relation_args_coherent` already does a primitive version of this (checking a common arg-type slot across exposures) but only along the arg-type dimension; the lit-scan's contribution is confirming that widening the comparison to richer relational structure (order/symmetry, co-occurrence context) is the theoretically-motivated next step, not a speculative addition.

**Class-level meaning without full identity: structure-mapping/systematicity alignment to known-relation schemas.** Gentner's Structure-Mapping Theory (1983) and its computational instantiation, the Structure Mapping Engine (Falkenhainer, Forbus & Gentner 1989), plus the RoleMap model (Petkov & Petrova 2019) built specifically for classification, formalize "which known relational category does this new instance's structure align best with" as a constraint-satisfaction competition scored by the **systematicity principle** — preferentially mapping onto whichever known schema shares the most interconnected, higher-order relational structure, not just isolated attribute matches. This gives a real classification mechanism, with an honest, literature-flagged failure mode: systematicity only RANKS candidates, it doesn't guarantee a unique fit, so ambiguity remains when multiple known relations align comparably (Gentner & Colhoun) — and a distinct failure mode, "mere-appearance match" (surface/object overlap without true relational overlap — Gentner & Markman's comparison taxonomy), where a new relation could be misclassified by superficial argument-type resemblance alone. Both failure modes are directly testable in the proposed cell (section b, point 4) by including a genuinely ambiguous new relation in the held-out battery.

**Both mechanisms are the SAME architectural move already validated for entities**, just requiring richer feature comparison than raw arg-type. This is not a new subsystem — it is the existing `type_profile`/coherence-checking machinery in `exp_read_grow_openvocab_fastmap_v1.py` widened along two axes (symmetry/order, co-occurrence-with-known-relations) for identity, plus one new lightweight scoring step (systematicity-weighted argmax over known-relation schemas) for class.

## 3. Verdict

**Not a flat "fundamental limit" and not a flat "fully achievable" — a precise three-tier split, directly evidenced by the biology:**

- **Tier 1 (Identity — is this a real, coherent, distinct new relation): ACHIEVABLE glass-box.** Mechanism: DORA-style repeated structural comparison (widen the existing arg-type coherence check to include order/symmetry + co-occurrence-with-known-relations). Recommend building this — it directly fixes the exact gap flagged in the current cell's docstring ("any two new verbs with the same arg-types are indistinguishable").
- **Tier 2 (Class — roughly what kind of relation): ACHIEVABLE glass-box.** Mechanism: structure-mapping/systematicity alignment against known-relation schemas (argmax class label). Recommend building this as new, real, class-level semantic content — currently entirely absent from the relation track.
- **Tier 3 (Fine-grained/idiosyncratic meaning verification): FUNDAMENTAL LIMIT, matching the brain's own documented ceiling.** Do not pursue further with purely structural/distributional glass-box mechanisms — the literature (Pinker's root-meaning residue, Levin's idiosyncratic-component ceiling, Tomasello's intention-reading requirement) consistently and convergently states this specific residue requires a categorically different evidence type (situated event access, joint attention, or explicit definition/external anchor) that the current architecture structurally lacks. This is a real, citable structural bound, not an unexplored engineering avenue — willing to and here DOES conclude fundamental limit for this specific tier, per the drill's own contract to follow the evidence.

**Recommended cell:** the decisive test in section (b) — extend the existing new-relation track with (i) a same-arg-type structurally-distinct distractor relation to test Tier-1 identity separation via richer structural signature vs. the current arg-type-only ablation (expected to show the ablation FAILING and the richer mechanism succeeding — this negative-then-positive pairing IS the discriminator), and (ii) a small held-out battery of synthetic new relations authored against known classes to test Tier-2 classification accuracy. Do NOT include a Tier-3 meaning-verification arm — there is no available ground truth to test it against, and the biology has already answered why.
