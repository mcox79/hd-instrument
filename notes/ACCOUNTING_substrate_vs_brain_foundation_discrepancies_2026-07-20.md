## PART 3 — THE LOAD-BEARING DISCREPANCIES (ranked)

1. **The learning signal (item 9) — the missing organ, now DOUBLE-flawed.** Non-contrastive (memorizes) AND single-scalar-learning-rate (no DA/ACh/NE gating). Even a fixed contrastive loop would still lack a second independent gating axis. This is THE root the whole session converged on. Fix = the redesign axes (held-out surprise + structured codes + order-sensitive target + correctness-isolating contrast) — and design homeostatic scaling in from the start (Hebbian unstable without it).
2. **Binding + structural code (items 1,5) — stipulated vs derived-from-data.** The substrate's bind + FHRR maps are hand-built once and reused; the brain DERIVES its structural code from a predictive objective over experience (even the scaffolded half is only partially innate). We have no analog to the learned-derivation half. (This is the "free-by-construction, not learned" root, item-9-adjacent.)
3. **Comprehension (item 7) — a DOUBLE gap.** Rule-not-predictive (the 0.557 ceiling) AND flat-not-hierarchical (no multi-timescale/discourse integration). NEWLY identified: even a fixed predictive reader at one flat level can't integrate narrative-scale coherence.
4. **Three total-gap overlooked components:** metacognition/confidence (highest product relevance), attention/selective-gating (highest risk on real noisy corpora), hierarchical prediction (structurally separate from the contrastive fix).

## PART 5 — BUILD-PRIORITY IMPLICATIONS (integrated)
- **#1 (standing): the contrastive learning loop + structured codes, TOGETHER** (the crux redesign; homeostatic scaling designed in). Gated on validating structured-codes-generalize (held-out true-vs-random > chance) FIRST.
