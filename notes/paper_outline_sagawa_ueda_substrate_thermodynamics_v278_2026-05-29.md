## 3. Abstract draft (250 words)

> Stochastic thermodynamics provides a quantitative framework for nonequilibrium fluctuations in small computational and biological systems, but applications to engineered information-processing substrates remain limited beyond Josephson-junction information engines and DNA-pulling experiments. Here we report production-scale verification of three independent fluctuation theorems in a hyperdimensional computing (HDC) associative-memory substrate: the trajectory-class fluctuation theorem (TCFT) of Wimsatt-Boyd-Crutchfield, the Crooks fluctuation theorem, and the Sagawa-Ueda generalized Jarzynski equality under measurement-feedback. The substrate operates on a discrete bipolar state space of dimension N=8192 with codebook-projected memory retrieval, falling into the gated-multistable associative-memory / lR-phase static class. Across protocol-robustness, M-density-sweep, and erase-time observational axes, the TCFT was verified at N=8192 with 5-seed Spearman correlation rho=-1.000 and dissipation-margin sigma_excess > 3.0 in every cell, with a sister-replication at N=2048 confirming N-down scalability. The Sagawa-Ueda generalized Jarzynski equality was verified at N=8192 with 5-seed mean(exp(-Delta F + I_mutual)) within 0.1% of unity. Concurrent ruling-out of three Hatano-Sasa-style housekeeping decompositions, across two N regimes and two dynamics families, narrows the substrate's nonequilibrium sub-class to the Seifert-Esposito + Sagawa-Ueda info-thermodynamics lineage. The substrate is, to our knowledge, the first hyperdimensional computational system shown to satisfy these fluctuation-theorem identities at production scale, distinct from the recently reported CTRNN-based associative-memory thermodynamics framework (Aguilera et al., 2025). We discuss the operational layer's argmax-readout decoupling from the genuinely nonequilibrium W-magnitude layer, and the implications for engineered substrates that can issue thermodynamically auditable erasure certificates.

## 5. Methods section outline

### 5.1 Substrate description (1-2 pages with formal definitions)

- **State space.** Vectors v in {-1, +1}^N with N=8192 (primary), N=4096 + N=2048 sister scales.
- **Codebook.** Bipolar codebook C with K columns; codebook generation procedures (BSC: i.i.d. bipolar; antipodal: matched pair structure; Kerdock: orthogonal code construction at even log2 N).
- **Binding algebra.** Element-wise multiplication; bundling = majority-rule sign. Storage rule: outer-product Hebbian / sign-rule write W = sum_i x_i * y_i^T.
- **Retrieval dynamics.** Iterated argmax over codebook columns; iterated sign-projection on W (this is the substrate's underlying Markov chain, with the readout being the argmax projection on the codebook).
- **SKAH-M / lR-phase classification.** Definition + bands: lR-phase regime: storage M / capacity threshold M_c < 1, beta_c=10 sharp first-order transition between low-isolation and high-isolation regimes. SKAH-M = "soft-key/atomic-hard-Memory" (gated multistable AM); the substrate satisfies SKAH-M's defining 6-cell battery confirmed at FULL N=8192 5-seed in prior work (v228).

### 5.2 Experimental protocols for fluctuation-theorem tests (2-3 pages)

**5.2.1 TCFT protocol.** Trajectory-class definition: each class = a subset of substrate W-state trajectories during an erasure protocol where W is gradually zeroed by reverse-Hebbian update; class is indexed by initial substrate-isolation level. TCFT identity: P_forward(class) / P_reverse(class) = exp(-Delta F_class). Measurements: forward W-erasure trajectories x5 seeds; reverse W-write trajectories; class-conditional dissipation Delta F_class via integrated work.

**5.2.2 Crooks protocol.** Standard Crooks: forward / reverse work distributions on W-protocol (substrate-store + substrate-retrieve cycle); detailed FT: P_F(W) / P_R(-W) = exp(beta * (W - Delta F)). Measurements: 5-seed work histograms.

**5.2.3 Sagawa-Ueda generalized Jarzynski with feedback.** Substrate write = "measurement"; substrate read = "feedback." Mutual information I_mutual = mutual info between substrate codebook representation and the input pattern (estimated via empirical histograms over trial trajectories). Generalized Jarzynski: <exp(-beta * W + I_mutual)> = 1. Measurements: simultaneous work + mutual-info logging over 5-seed N=8192 trajectories.

**5.2.4 HS-class exclusion protocols (Hatano-Sasa orthogonal decomposition).** Three protocols tested at N=2048 and N=8192 with two dynamics families (BSC + Glauber). Detailed methods + non-passing band thresholds reported. These NEGATIVE results are load-bearing for class identification (they exclude housekeeping-orthogonal sub-classes).

**5.2.5 BID order parameter protocol (independent geometric corroborator).** BID = bipolar-isolated-density signed-margin distribution between stored facts. Bands defined relative to static Hopfield 3-phase corridor (retrieval + spin-glass + paramagnetic). Substrate's measurement at N in {4096, 8192, 12288} OUTSIDE the static-Hopfield bands.

### 5.3 Statistical methodology (1 page)

- 5-seed replication, mean +/- s.e. reporting.
- Sigma-margin definition: |observed - reference| / s.e.; HARD-PASS bands per [[feedback-envelope-expansion-fail-bands]].

## 7. Discussion section outline (6 subsections, ~3 pages)

### 7.1 Physical interpretation: what does it mean for an HDC system to satisfy stochastic thermodynamics?

The substrate's writes are physical measurements (codebook projection); the substrate's reads are physical feedback (codebook-projected argmax). The TCFT + Sagawa-Ueda confirmation means the substrate satisfies the fluctuation-theorem identities REQUIRED OF SYSTEMS THAT MEASURE AND USE INFORMATION. This is a quantitative class-membership claim, not a qualitative analogy.

### 7.2 Connection to memory storage and retrieval physics

The Sagawa-Ueda lineage gives the deletion-certificate property its thermodynamic foundation: the minimum work to erase a stored fact is bounded by k_B T * H(fact), where H is the entropy of the encoded fact. Sagawa-Ueda's generalized Jarzynski INCLUDES this as a special case; the substrate's verified Sagawa-Ueda identity means deletion is thermodynamically auditable in the formal sense.

### 7.3 Connection to non-equilibrium phase transitions

The substrate's static-phase identity (gated-multistable AM / SKAH-M / lR-phase) at the equilibrium-landscape level + its non-eq dynamics at the W-update level together specify a system that has BOTH a rich phase structure AND obeys fluctuation theorems for its dynamics. This is the non-equilibrium phase transition framing: the substrate transitions BETWEEN equilibrium landscape phases via non-eq dynamics that obey detailed FT.

### 7.4 Operational-layer-invariance pattern (the 4-5 witness pattern)

The substrate exhibits a striking pattern across 4 independent observables (PB-3 critical-slowing tau_recovery flat; AXIS-4 hysteresis max_loop_area zero at both beta=8 and beta_c=10; KF-5 v275 STEERABILITY_PARTIAL_DECOUPLING with entropy steerable but BPC not; BE-1 precision-floor isolation INSENSITIVE across FP32 -> INT1): the OPERATIONAL (argmax-readout) layer is invariant to perturbations of beta / precision / M-density / dynamics-trajectory, while the INTERNAL (W-magnitude) layer retains rich structure. This is the slow-fast decomposition of Esposito-Van den Broeck manifesting at observational scale. The substrate is INTERNALLY RICH (non-eq FT-obeying) but OPERATIONALLY SIMPLE (Markov-like argmax readout). For substrate-as-engineered-system, this is a DEPLOYABILITY FEATURE: customers don't tune internal-state parameters, the operational layer absorbs internal complexity.
