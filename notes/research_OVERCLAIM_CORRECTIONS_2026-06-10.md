## CORRECTION 1: "Universal Tier 1 = categorical translation win"

**Over-claimed:** N-languages linear scaling via universal Tier 1 primitives; categorical advantage over LLM N² training pairs.

**Honest:** ~30-35 NSM/Wolff primitives (logic, causation, taxonomy, basic mental) genuinely cross-linguistically stable. BUT spatial relations, tense systems, evidentiality, color terms, kinship are NOT universal — vary radically across language families (Mandarin vs English vs Tzeltal).

**Revised architecture:**
- **Tier 0:** ~30-35 universal primitives (truly language-independent)
- **Tier 1:** per-language-family extensions for spatial/tense/evidentiality/etc.
- **Tier 2:** per-language grammatical constructions
- **Tier 3:** per-language lexicon

**Revised commercial framing:** Substrate has structural advantage on Tier-0 content (logic/causation/taxonomy). LLMs still better on Tier-1+ language-specific content. Translation is NOT categorical win; structural advantage on subset.

**P_deflated:** 0.35 joint cross-language + cross-domain; 0.60 IsA-only universality.

## CORRECTION 2: "Continual learning matches biology"

**Over-claimed:** Substrate continual learning = biological CLS architecture; "this is what humans do."

**Honest:** Substrate = hippocampal fast-store ONLY. Full Complementary Learning Systems (McClelland 1995) requires TWO systems: hippocampus + cortex. Substrate has the hippocampus analog; cortical slow-generalizer + frequency-selectivity NOT in substrate natively.

**What's confirmed:**
- Avoids McCloskey-Cohen catastrophic forgetting (P=0.90)
- Hippocampal fast-store analog real

**What's gaps:**
- Capacity exhaustion at M>M_c (substrate fills up; what then?)
- Schema preservation empirically UNCONFIRMED for correlated real-encoder outputs
- Frequency selectivity ARCHITECTURALLY ABSENT without reinforcement writes
- Cortical generalizer alongside fast-store NOT in substrate
- Full biological CL equivalence: P_deflated=0.10 (very low)

**Engineering implication:** "Continual learning at production scale" claim requires:
1. Cortical-analog slow generalizer alongside fast substrate
2. Reinforcement-write mechanism for frequency selectivity
3. Empirical validation of schema preservation under correlated inputs

## CORRECTION 3: "Substrate could BEAT LLMs at aesthetics"

**Over-claimed:** Substrate aesthetic wins via novelty (anomaly margin) + skill (composition quality) + coherence + form-function; LLMs are mid.

**Honest:** LLM aesthetic advantage is REAL AND LARGE. P_deflated=0.12 for open aesthetic parity.

**Why my claim was wrong:**
- 100 years of formal aesthetics has NO consensus formula
- Birkhoff/Berlyne/Schmidhuber/NIMA all describe weak signals (10-30% variance)
- Novelty ≠ beauty (max anomaly margin = random noise = zero aesthetic value)
- CreativityPrism (2025): GPT-4-class matches/outperforms human writers on fluency + coherence
- LLM "mid" baseline is mid only by AI standards, not human standards

**Substrate's defensible aesthetic position:**
- Schema-fit (PP-265) for formal genres ONLY
- Compositional coherence across long documents (real structural advantage)
- Entity-binding consistency (no context-window drift)

**Commercial niche (HONEST):** regulated documents, structured brand content, cross-document KBs. NOT open creative writing. NOT a creative-writing LLM competitor.

**P_deflated by niche:**
- Open aesthetic parity: 0.12
- Constrained schema-governed quality: 0.35
- Genre-criteria enforcement: 0.45
