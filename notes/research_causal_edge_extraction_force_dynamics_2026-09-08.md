## (A) The pinned force-dynamics edge computation — Wolff's criteria, and why it cannot be the fix

**Wolff & Song (2003), *Cognitive Psychology* 47:276-332 (already PINNED on disk, reused not re-derived).** The
force-vector model classifies a causal interaction along three dimensions, computed from the verb + its
arguments (subject=affector, object=patient):

1. **Tendency** — does the patient have an intrinsic disposition toward or away from the endstate (or none)?
2. **Concordance** — does the affector's applied force align with (concordant) or oppose (discordant) the
   patient's own tendency?
3. **Result** — is the endstate actually reached?

This produces the categorical read: **CAUSE** = patient has no tendency (or tends away) + affector's force
alone produces the result ("the wind moved the boat"). **ENABLE** = patient already tends toward the endstate,
affector's force is concordant / removes an obstacle, result reached ("the sergeant let the soldiers eat").
**PREVENT** = patient tends toward the endstate, affector's force opposes it, result NOT reached ("the lock
kept the door closed"). Wolff & Song's five experiments show this model predicts human causal-VERB CHOICE
better than counterfactual-dependency or Cheng's covariational model — the categories are psychologically real
and verbs genuinely carry force-vector information (PINNED).

**Two already-established limits (from this project's own 2026-08-29/08-30 drills, reused not re-run):**

- **Tendency is not always lexically fixed.** For tendency-ambiguous verbs ("open"), the SAME verb yields
  CAUSE vs ENABLE depending on world-knowledge about the patient's disposition (jammed gate vs. spring-loaded
  gate) — a verb-lexicon-only typer is capped at chance (measured on-disk: 0.500 vs a tendency-oracle's 1.000).
  Kuhnmuench & Beller (2005, *Cognitive Science* 29(6):1077-1090, PINNED) independently show CAUSE-vs-ENABLE
  judgments track linguistic/task FRAMING cues, not a framing-independent mental model — the distinction is
  partly linguistically CONSTRUCTED, not purely a verb property.
- **Scope mismatch, not weakness.** Force dynamics presupposes a force-transmission PREDICATE already links two
  arguments of the SAME clause. It has no mechanism for judging whether two INDEPENDENTLY DESCRIBED events (no
  shared causative verb, different clauses/sentences) are causally linked AT ALL — this is a category error to
  fix, not a gap to patch. This project's own 2026-08-30 drill already measured this directly: the force typer
  fires on ~16% of MAVEN-ERE causal-annotated edges (the explicit-physical-predicate slice); the remaining ~84%
  (implicit, connective-less, cross-sentence narrative causation — **exactly the case this task is about**) is
  structurally outside the theory's domain, because there is no force-dynamic verb between the two events for
  it to type.

**Answer to Q1: force dynamics is PINNED as the correct model of "given a causal predicate, what TYPE of
force-relation does it encode" — it is NOT applicable, by scope, to "does a causal edge exist between these two
events at all," which is the parent's actual open problem.** Consistent with the on-disk finding that "the
grounded PHYSICAL chain on narrative" (a force-dynamics-driven composer) already sits below the info-free twin
on narrative text (from the earlier `generate_dont_retrieve...SOLVED.md`, finding 6). It does not discriminate
causation from co-occurrence for the unmarked majority because it was never designed to try.

## (B) Type-conditioned reasoning — brain-foundational architecture, but the routing step is the same wall

**YES, at the architecture level**, and this project already has independent neuroimaging support pinned
(2026-08-30 WALL 2 drill, reused): a domain-general fronto-parietal/insula network detects causal STRUCTURE for
both physical and social causation, but social/intentional causation ADDITIONALLY and specifically recruits the
mentalizing network (TPJ, mPFC/dmPFC — Saxe & Kanwisher 2003), which selectively fails in ToM-impaired
schizophrenia patients (a genuine double dissociation).

**Newly verified this cycle, and it sharpens the architecture claim:**

- **Gerstenberg & Tenenbaum (2017, ch. 28 in Waldmann's *Oxford Handbook of Causal Reasoning*, pp.515-548,
  PINNED at abstract/secondary-source level — full chapter PDF unparseable).** Explicit architecture: **ONE**
  inference engine (counterfactual simulation / Bayesian inference) applied to **different domain-specific
  generative models plugged in per domain** — a physics-like simulator for physical events, an
  inverse-planning/rational-agent model for intentional behavior. Corroborated by the broader
  counterfactual-simulation-model (CSM) line: the same simulate-and-compare machinery predicts causal judgments
  for physical collisions/omissions AND social responsibility judgments (helping/hindering) — same algorithm,
  swapped generative model. This is **type-first-then-type-appropriate-generative-model**, not a uniform
  max-union and not a genuinely different algorithm per domain.
- **Gopnik & Wellman (2012, *Psychological Bulletin* 138(6):1085-1108 — corrects the task's citation, which
  named the wrong venue).** Their actual claim runs the OTHER direction from naive domain-pluralism: the
  INFERENCE MECHANISM (causal Bayes nets) is domain-**general**; physics and psychology differ in
  content/priors (ontology + causal-relation types), not in the learning algorithm. This reconciles cleanly
  with Gerstenberg-Tenenbaum's "same engine, different generative model."
- **Wellman & Gelman (1992, *Annu. Rev. Psychol.* 43:337-375, PINNED via convergent secondary sources)** and
  **Carey (2009, *The Origin of Concepts*, PINNED via convergent secondary sources)** independently confirm
  physics (contact-causation), psychology (goal-directed self-propulsion evaluated via rationality), and
  biology are separate early-emerging core-knowledge systems with distinct causal-explanatory content.
- **Baker, Saxe & Tenenbaum (2009, *Cognition* 113:329-349)** frames inverse planning explicitly as the
  domain-specific generative model for intentional causation, inverted via the SAME general Bayesian toolkit
  used elsewhere — consistent, not contradictory, with the shared-engine reading above. This is already the
  landed `hdlab.theory_of_mind` organ's mechanism.

**Caveat (my synthesis, flagged as such per calibration policy):** no single cited source states a literal
"discrete classify-type-THEN-dispatch" two-stage gate verbatim — this is a defensible bridge across
Carey/Wellman-Gelman (domain-distinct content) and Gerstenberg-Tenenbaum/Baker (domain-general engine,
domain-specific model), not a directly-evidenced single claim. **Cap this synthesis at P=0.50.**

**The practical problem, grounded in this project's own on-disk audit, not speculation:** even granting the
architecture, the FIRST step — classifying WHICH type of causation a candidate edge represents — is itself a
contextual word-sense/event-typing problem. This project's own `generate_dont_retrieve...SOLVED.md` AUDIT
UPDATE already flags `event_type` routing as **"MFS [most-frequent-sense], not contextual WSD"** — a known,
independently-diagnosed secondary cap (consistent with the standing MEMORY finding that the WSD wall is
contextual representation, not grounding). Type-conditioning does not sidestep the extraction/binding wall
already localized as the dominant signal-loss point (component I1 in the `BRAIN_FOUNDATIONAL_ANALYSIS`
component audit) — it adds a NEW classification step that is itself subject to the same wall.

## (C) The pinned weighted-integration rule — real, but a reweighting of existing signals, not a new one

**Kehler & Rohde (2013, *Theoretical Linguistics* 39:1-37).** Genuinely Bayesian top level: P(referent|pronoun)
proportional to P(pronoun|referent) x P(referent), where the next-mention prior P(referent) is decomposed BY
coherence relation (Result/Explanation=causal; Parallel; Contrast; Occasion), each relation carrying its own
cue-driven referent bias (implicit-causality verb semantics drives the causal-relation bias). This is a real
multi-cue Bayesian combination at the relation level — but the actual cue combination WITHIN a relation's
likelihood is empirically/regression-fit (log-linear/logistic — additive in log-odds), not the hard-gate/max
this project already refuted, and not naive independent-product either.

**Kintsch (1988, *Psychological Review* 95:163-182).** Literal recurrent linear-algebra relaxation: activation
vector A(t+1) = A(t)·C, C = signed connection-strength matrix over shared-argument compatibility, iterated with
negative-clip + renormalize toward a fixed point. Qualitatively different from both a static weighted sum (it's
mutual reinforcement across ALL active nodes, multiple passes) and from max/argmax (competing propositions
retain graded nonzero activation after settling — a soft, not eliminative, outcome).

**Rehder (2003, *JEP:LMC* 29:1141-1159), noisy-OR causal-model theory.** P(effect=1|causes) = 1 - product over
active causes of (1-w_i) [+ optional leak term]. The standard principled multi-cause combination: bounded in
[0,1] without renormalization, sub-additive for redundant cues (unlike linear sum), still lets multiple weak
cues jointly exceed any single cue (unlike winner-take-all). **Caveat, independently verified:** Rehder &
Burnett (2005, *Cognitive Psychology*) found human judgments deviate toward MORE additive over-integration than
strict noisy-OR predicts — people integrate somewhat more liberally than the "principled" model, not less.

**The important negative finding: the specific mechanism the task named (Graesser-Singer-Trabasso 1994
"convergence + working-memory activation threshold") has no formula in the original paper** — confirmed this
cycle (search failed to surface one; GST94 is qualitative/verbal theory only). **The actual formalized versions
of that idea are separate, later papers — the Landscape Model (van den Broek, Risden, Fletcher & Thurlow 1996)
and the Resonance Model (Myers & O'Brien 1999)** — both literally sum weighted activation from text-based,
coherence-reinstatement, and background-knowledge sources per reading cycle and threshold the result. **This
matters directly: "resonance" is already in the task's own list of tried-and-promiscuous proxies.** The one
mechanism that actually operationalizes "convergence across sources + activation threshold" with a real formula
has ALREADY been implemented in this project and already found promiscuous — this is not a fresh untried idea,
it is a re-labeling of something already tied.

**No direct head-to-head comparison of max/competition vs. weighted-sum vs. noisy-OR vs. CI-settling on causal
COHERENCE judgment specifically exists in the literature searched** — a genuine gap, not a located negative for
that comparison itself.

**Net for Q3:** of the four candidate combination rules, this project has already tried something equivalent to
two of them (competition/max-union — the "PRECISE"/"BROAD multi-engine competition" cells, both tied; and
additive-resonance — already promiscuous). **Noisy-OR over type-gated, independently-scored cues is the one
genuinely untried, well-motivated combination rule left.** Rehder & Burnett's finding that humans over-integrate
relative to noisy-OR suggests today's competition-style combination may be over-firing on precision — noisy-OR
is a plausible, cheap, worth-running precision fix. It is **not** expected to add new discriminative information
beyond what the individual cues already carry (see D and the calibration below): combination-layer changes
cannot fix an input-signal problem.

## (D) Causal-power / intuitive-theory edge inference — structurally undefined for a single narrative instance

**Griffiths & Tenenbaum (2005, *Cognitive Psychology*; 2009, *Psychological Review* 116(4):661-716) — causal
support.** PINNED (verified via secondary restatement; primary PDF equations not independently confirmed
verbatim this cycle): causal support = log[P(D|Graph-with-link)/P(D|Graph-without-link)], a Bayes-factor
log-likelihood-ratio between two small candidate causal graphs, using a **noisy-OR** generative likelihood with
strength parameters integrated out under a uniform Beta(1,1) prior. This is small-enumeration Bayesian
structure learning — tractable via closed-form beta-integral algebra or trivial numerical quadrature, **no
training required, genuinely LLM-free-computable.**

**Cheng (1997, *Psychological Review* 104(2):367-405) — Power PC.** PINNED: generative causal power p =
[P(e|c)-P(e|~c)] / [1-P(e|~c)]; analogous preventive-power formula. Also LLM-free arithmetic.

**The decisive limit, independently confirmed by the lit-scan (searched specifically, found none, not just
under-searched): both formulas are DEFINED ON REPEATED-TRIAL CONTINGENCY TABLES** — P(e|c) and P(e|~c) are
frequencies across MANY observed instances of the same cause-type/effect-type pair. **Neither has a documented
single-narrative-instance extension** (one story, one candidate cause, no repeated trials to build a contingency
table) in the primary literature or in citing NLP work — the one text-causal-narrative system found
(Heddaya et al. 2024, arXiv:2410.05252, "Causal Micro-Narratives") uses LLM classification, not a Cheng/G&T
computation, and does not substitute corpus base-rates for the missing contingency table. Waldmann's
causal-model theory (Waldmann & Holyoak 1992; Waldmann 1996; Waldmann, Hagmayer & Blaisdell 2006) adds a
complementary, not alternative, piece: prior structural/mechanism knowledge should constrain WHICH covariation
counts, before the power/support computation runs.

**The only way to make Cheng/G&T applicable to a single narrative edge is to substitute a corpus-derived base
rate** (how often does an event of TYPE X precede/produce an event of TYPE Y, across many DIFFERENT stories) as
a proxy contingency table. **This is not a new idea for this project — it is the formalized version of the
co-occurrence/associative-similarity channel already tried across six operationalizations** (content
co-occurrence, conceptual, entropy, script, generative-telic, retrieved-CSKG — per the earlier SOLVED.md's KEY
REALIZATION #2), all of which tie the info-free twin on exactly the unmarked residual that matters. Dressing the
same co-occurrence signal in Bayesian causal-support notation does not add new information; it is very likely to
inherit the same ceiling. **Waldmann's mechanism-gating idea is the one place D and B combine non-redundantly:**
a type-conditioned structural prior (physical contact vs. intentional-goal structure) could GATE which
event-type pairs are even eligible for a corpus base-rate estimate, rather than computing one undifferentiated
co-occurrence statistic over all pairs — a refinement worth noting, but of an already-6x-refuted channel, so
expect modest-to-no lift, not a decisive fix.

## (E) Honest calibration — external benchmarks + internal triangulation

**External F1 ceilings, verified this cycle (not previously on disk for this project):**

- Best fine-tuned-supervised system on the best-behaved benchmark: **DPJL (Shen et al. 2022, COLING), 67.9% F1
  on EventStoryLine** (RoBERTa + causal cue-word prompts). Causal-TimeBank tops out lower (CATENA 2016, 62.2%
  F1; most 2020-21 neural systems 50-53% F1). On the LARGER, HARDER, MORE REALISTIC MAVEN-ERE (document-level,
  multi-relation-type — closest in character to this project's own gold) **the original paper's own RoBERTa
  baseline is only 31.5% F1.**
- **LLMs zero-shot do WORSE than fine-tuning, specifically on causal reasoning** (Gao et al. 2023, EMNLP
  Findings, arXiv:2305.07375): GPT-4 41-42% F1 on EventStoryLine (vs. fine-tuned 67.9%); only 11.5% F1 on
  Causal-TimeBank. Explicit finding: "ChatGPT... has been comprehensively outperformed by baseline methods based
  on fine-tuned small PLMs." This task does not reward scale/world-knowledge alone; it requires dataset-specific
  supervised calibration to reach even the mid-60s F1.
- **Implicit-only causal extraction is dramatically harder** — a verified 49-point F1 gap (explicit 88.0% vs.
  implicit 39.2%) in one domain (PubMedCausal, arXiv:2605.28363) — directly relevant since this project's own
  problem is exactly the unmarked/implicit majority.
- **Human inter-annotator agreement itself is protocol-dependent, not a fixed ceiling**: kappa swings from ~0.2
  (RED protocol) to 0.78 (EventStoryLine forced-choice protocol) on the SAME underlying causal-judgment task
  (EventFull, arXiv:2412.12733, NAACL 2025) — a meaningful share of "the ceiling" is not extractable from text
  at all, because human graders do not agree on one ground truth.

**Internal evidence (already on disk, reused):** three independently-built formal selection computations over
the SAME candidate edges — pairwise sufficiency (forward event-transition, construction-validated d=0.68 but
task-insensitive), pairwise necessity (counterfactual LOO, ties, reduces to lexical set-membership), and global
causal-network structure (connectivity/main-chain/structural-necessity, UNDERPERFORMS base, structure=shuffle
twin) — **all tie or underperform, and none beats its info-free/structure twin.** The diagnosis already reached
independently by the solver ("the ceiling is DEFINITIVELY the causal-EDGE-correctness... not the selection
computation over them") is corroborated, not contradicted, by this cycle's literature: switching the
combination/typing LAYER cannot fix an input-signal problem, and the external literature confirms the
input-signal problem (edge correctness) is hard even with full supervised training on labeled causal corpora.

**Calibrated P (per mandatory lit-scan calibration policy: deflate 0.15-0.25, cap novel-synthesis at 0.50):**
Pre-deflation, treating "force-dynamic typing + inverse-planning + type-conditioning + weighted (noisy-OR)
integration, assembled together" as one candidate system: ~0.30-0.35 (most of the ingredients are re-labelings
of already-tried, already-tied mechanisms; only the noisy-OR combination rule is genuinely untried, and it is a
precision-only lever, not a new information source). **Deflated: P = 0.15-0.20 that this system MATERIALLY beats
the promiscuous proxies on full-population causal-edge correctness.** This is a LOCATED-NEGATIVE-SHAPED
calibration, not a crossable-wall calibration. Be blunt, as asked: **the honest read is a 12th confirmation in

## Cheap decisive test (pre-registered, HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL)

Build the ONE genuinely untried combination: a **noisy-OR integration of TYPE-GATED, independently-scored
cues** — force-dynamics CAUSE/ENABLE/PREVENT score when it fires (explicit physical predicate present);
inverse-planning / means-end score when the candidate is goal-typed (reuse the landed ATL-hub means-end engine,
already measured at me2 0.5326 on the goal subset); associative/co-occurrence score as the residual fallback
when neither type-specific engine fires — versus the CURRENT competition/max-union baseline, on the SAME
MAVEN-ERE / TellMeWhy populations already loaded by the existing cells (`exp_multistep_causal_network_v1.py`,
`exp_multistep_meansend_chain_v1.py`), holding extraction/binding fixed, with an info-free twin (shuffle which
cue fires, matched marginals) and the shared-participant-confound subset already defined in this problem's prior
cells.

- **HARD-PASS** (license one more round of combination-layer work): noisy-OR beats the current
  competition/max-union baseline CI-separated on **precision on the confound subset** (the cases where topical/
  participant-sharing already fools the max-union baseline) by >= 0.05, with the info-free twin losing. This
  would confirm Rehder & Burnett's over-integration finding predicts a real, exploitable precision gain from
  switching combination rules — a genuine, if narrow, win.
- **HARD-FAIL** (close the combination-layer family; do not build further weighted/noisy-OR/Bayesian-coherence
  variants over these same cues): noisy-OR ties or underperforms competition, OR does not beat its info-free
  twin. This would be the 12th triangulation and the decisive one for this specific family of ideas — the
  remaining honest conclusion is that no re-weighting of these particular input cues can materially move the
  needle, because none of the individual cues (force-dynamics fire-rate ~16%, forward-transition construction-
  validated-but-task-insensitive, means-end hub, temporal precedence, associative co-occurrence) is shown to
  individually discriminate true causal-edge existence from topic/participant sharing at the base rate needed.
- **Pre-registered P(HARD-PASS) = 0.20** (deflated from the 0.30-0.35 pre-deflation estimate above, per
  calibration policy — this specific test isolates the ONE genuinely untried ingredient, so it deserves a real
  shot, but the weight of 11 prior triangulations plus the external F1-ceiling evidence argues for a low prior).

## Substrate-product implications

A user-facing reading/summarization product that relies on any combination-layer causal-edge extractor built
from these ingredients would inherit the same ~30-68% F1 ceiling documented in the external literature (worse
on implicit/unmarked causation, which is the majority of real narrative text) — regardless of how cleverly the
existing cues are typed or combined. The one credible remaining lever, already named by the solver's own
CONTEXT_HANDOFF and independently unblocked (not contradicted) by this cycle's literature, is NOT a better
combination rule but a **story-specific, online-learned generative model** (the recurrent loop's forward
transition learned from THIS story's own events as they are read, rather than a general corpus/ATOMIC prior or
a corpus base-rate substituted into a causal-power formula) — the one mechanism class this project has not yet
built and the literature does not refute. Cost of the one recommended test here (noisy-OR type-gated
combination) is small (reuses three already-built, already-scored engines); cost of concluding the combination-
layer family is closed and moving fully to the story-specific online generative model is the honest, larger
next commitment.

## TLDR (plain English)

We asked: is there a smarter way to figure out "did event A cause event B" in a story, using ideas from how the
brain tells physical causes (pushing/forcing) apart from mental causes (wanting/trying) and combines many weak
clues into one judgment — without any outside AI? Answer: those brain mechanisms are real and well-documented,
but every one of them turns out to answer a narrower question than the one we actually need. The "pushing/
forcing" mechanism only works once a sentence already uses a force word like "pushed" or "let" — it has nothing
to say about two sentences placed near each other with no such word, which is most of real storytelling. The
"combine many clues" mechanisms are genuine upgrades over what we already tried, but they only reweigh signals
we already have — they do not supply a new fact the story didn't already give us. And the formal "how strong is
this cause" math from psychology needs many repeated examples of the same cause-and-effect pair to work at all;
it doesn't have an answer for "did THIS happen once in THIS story," so the only way to use it here is to
fall back on "how often does this kind of thing happen in general" — which is the same general-knowledge
shortcut we already tried six different ways and found doesn't work on the hard cases. Outside evidence backs
this up: even the best modern AI systems, trained specifically on labeled examples, only get causal links right
about 55-68% of the time on the easiest test sets, and much worse (about 31%) on harder, more realistic ones —
and giant general AI models actually do WORSE than smaller specially-trained ones on this exact task. So the
honest conclusion is: this is very likely one more confirmation that the real bottleneck is telling a genuine
cause from a coincidence in the first place — not which clever method sits on top once you have candidate
causes. There is one small, cheap test still worth running (a more principled way to combine the clues we
already have, which might trim some false alarms), but nobody should expect it to be the breakthrough.
