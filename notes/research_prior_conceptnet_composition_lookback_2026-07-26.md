# Prior ConceptNet composition/multi-hop look-back: does banked work already validate the "compositional redundancy not density" reframe?

Filed by: research sub-agent (read-only look-back). No cells built, no dispatch, no bank writes, no push.

## HEADLINE

**The reframe is NOT validated by prior work — the most directly relevant, already-VET-banked prior test found the opposite and says so explicitly on-disk.** `exp_substrate_conceptnet_kg_inference_transfer_cpu_v1` (T3, provenance_quality=CERT_CHAIN_GRADE) is a genuine held-out-edge inheritance/composition test on real ConceptNet, and its banked atom's own description reads: *"Coverage-completion-not-reasoning **replicated multi-corpus** (WordNet Item-1/M1/HYP-5 -> ConceptNet)."* The substrate's composition mechanism (cf-RPE + graph-BFS) underperforms a frozen-BGE single-hop cosine baseline on ConceptNet (Hits@10 0.451 vs 0.502) — the SAME wall as WordNet, not a "fire." A second attempt (`exp_conceptnet_semantic_seeded_beam_composition_v1`, semantic-seeded beam) also HARD_FAILs against BGE and even against random search. A third, closest-to-a-win result (`exp_conceptnet_rerank_parity_multiseed_v1`, random-code substrate-native beam) reaches only a **statistical tie** with BGE — and VET downgraded that from the cell's claimed "win" to MM_STANDARD (parity, not beat) because the substrate's run-to-run variance fails the pre-committed chain-grade tie gate. The two apparent HARD_PASSes named in the dispatch (`glass_box_micro_loop_conceptnet_multihop_v1`/`_SCALE_v1`, `substrate_role_tagged_compositional_generalization_on_concept_KG_v1`) do **not** test held-out-edge inference at all — one tests a retrieval-architecture (WM gate+requery) chaining **already-known** stored facts with no FREQ/embedding baseline in the comparison, and the other is an **unvetted, confounded synthetic-toy** cell whose 1.000 result is carried almost entirely by a concept-encoder change applied only to the winning arm, not by the role-binding/composition mechanism under nominal test.

**Bottom line: my exact "composition/inheritance beats a real baseline on ConceptNet held-out edges" test has already been run twice (graph-BFS+cfRPE, semantic-seeded beam) and both are clean, VET-banked HARD_FAILs replicating the WordNet wall. Re-running that exact test is redundant. The one genuinely open, non-redundant gap is different: the ONE mechanism that IS robustly CHAIN_GRADE on real ConceptNet topology (the gated WM-requery loop) has never been pointed at a genuine never-ingested held-out-edge task with a FREQ/BGE bar — it has only ever been asked to re-chain facts already in its own store.**

## Direct synthesis answer

**Does prior banked work already establish that composition/inheritance FIRES on redundant ConceptNet where it tied floor on curated WordNet/WorldTree?**

No. Prior banked work establishes close to the opposite, and does so explicitly:

1. The one cell that is a genuine, apples-to-apples held-out-edge inheritance/composition test on real ConceptNet (`exp_substrate_conceptnet_kg_inference_transfer_cpu_v1`) is VET-banked at CERT_CHAIN_GRADE provenance and its own banked description says the WordNet "coverage-completion-not-reasoning" wall (Item-1/M1/HYP-5) **replicated** on ConceptNet — the substrate loses to a frozen-embedding baseline, it does not beat it. This is the single strongest piece of on-disk evidence directly bearing on the reframe, and it points the other way.
2. A second, independent composition attempt on the identical held-out split (`exp_conceptnet_semantic_seeded_beam_composition_v1`, semantic-seeded beam) is also a clean, VET-confirmed HARD_FAIL, and diagnoses a mechanism (correlation-hurts-capacity) for why semantic signal actively hurts composition on ConceptNet rather than helping it.
3. The best composition-alone result against a real baseline on ConceptNet (`exp_conceptnet_rerank_parity_multiseed_v1`, deliverable B, random-code beam) is a **statistical tie**, and VET explicitly declined to call it a win.
4. The two cells that look like clean ConceptNet composition HARD_PASSes from their headline verdicts (`glass_box_micro_loop_conceptnet_multihop_v1`/`_SCALE_v1`, `substrate_role_tagged_compositional_generalization_on_concept_KG_v1`) do not actually test the claim: the first tests whether an iterative retrieval architecture beats single-shot lookup at re-chaining KNOWN facts (never held-out, never compared to a FREQ/embedding baseline), and the second is unvetted, on synthetic toy data, and confounded by a concept-encoder swap applied only to the winning arm.
5. The one genuinely positive, VET-clean signal that composition/binding "fires" on real ConceptNet content (`exp_role_filler_factorization_conceptnet_cg_v1`) answers a narrower question — factored-vs-flat generalization to a held-out filler in a KNOWN relation/role slot — and VET explicitly ruled out reading the real-ConceptNet apparent-headroom as evidence about "redundant text" or "reading axis"; it is a native-superposition capacity effect that also appears on synthetic data of matching shape.

So: **"reframe already validated by prior work" — NO. "My exact composition-inference-beats-FREQ-on-ConceptNet test is still required" — also NO, because that exact test (cf-RPE+BFS vs BGE, and separately semantic-seeded-beam vs BGE) has already been run twice on real ConceptNet with a real non-trivial baseline, both HARD_FAIL, both VET-banked, both explicitly cross-referenced to the WordNet wall.** Running a third near-identical variant would be redundant per the discipline this program already applies to itself (the semantic-seeded-beam cell's own VET note frames itself as a can-fail alternative to the graph-BFS cell, not a fresh question).

**What is a genuine, non-redundant gap:** none of the failing composition attempts (cf-RPE+BFS, semantic-seeded beam) used the ONE mechanism that is independently, robustly CHAIN_GRADE on real ConceptNet topology at scale — the gated WM-requery loop (glass_box_micro_loop family). That mechanism has only ever been asked to re-derive answers already present in its own store (a retrieval-architecture question), never redirected at a firewalled, never-ingested held-out-edge split with a frozen-BGE / closure-style bar the way the two failing cells were. Whether a WM-gated iterative-requery architecture — as opposed to a single-shot cf-RPE completion or a semantic-seeded beam — can beat BGE on the SAME held-out ConceptNet split used by the two HARD_FAIL cells is an open, specific, gap-filling question that has not been asked. It is a plausible reason the prior HARD_FAILs might not be the substrate's ceiling: both failing mechanisms were single-shot-style completions (cf-RPE / one-shot beam), and the one mechanism independently shown to matter on real ConceptNet topology (iterative gate+requery, not just "more redundant data") has never been given this specific test.

## Recommendation

Needs a **specific gap-filling test**, not a re-run of the reframe's implied hypothesis and not a repeat of either already-failed mechanism:

- Do NOT re-test "does composition beat a baseline on real, redundant ConceptNet" with the cf-RPE/graph-BFS or semantic-seeded-beam mechanisms — both are already clean, VET-banked HARD_FAILs on this exact question, explicitly cross-referenced to the WordNet wall. Re-running them (or a third minor variant of the same single-shot-completion family) would burn a cycle re-answering an answered question.
- If pursuing this line further, the open question is narrower and different in kind: point the CHAIN_GRADE-certified gated-WM-requery loop (not a new mechanism — reuse the certified glass_box_micro_loop mechanism verbatim) at the SAME firewalled, never-ingested held-out split and frozen-BGE bar used by `exp_substrate_conceptnet_kg_inference_transfer_cpu_v1`, so the comparison is apples-to-apples against the existing HARD_FAIL. That test does not exist yet on disk.
- Separately flag for skunkworks: `exp_substrate_role_tagged_compositional_generalization_on_concept_KG_v1` is sitting unvetted with a confounded design; it should either be sent through landed-VET with the missing ablation (label-driven encoder x ORTHO/CLUSTERED roles, independently) noted as a prerequisite, or explicitly down-weighted in any cap_map / synthesis reasoning until that ablation exists. Its current HARD_PASS_CHAIN_GRADE framing should not be treated as evidence for anything about role-binding composition until that confound is resolved.

## Cheap decisive test (for the gap identified above, if the Director wants to close it)

Redirect the certified `glass_box_micro_loop_conceptnet_multihop_v1` retrieve->gate->WM-requery->commit loop (commit `200b66c3f`, mechanism reused verbatim, no new code) at the firewalled held-out split already built for `exp_substrate_conceptnet_kg_inference_transfer_cpu_v1` (466 eval rows, 233 with-path/233 without, transitive rels IS_A/PART_OF/HAS_A/MADE_OF/DERIVED_FROM/AT_LOCATION), scored against the same frozen-BGE and closure bars already computed there. Cheap because: the loop mechanism, the held-out split, and both baselines already exist on disk — this is a re-application, not a new build.

## Falsifiable predictions

- **HARD-PASS:** WM-gated iterative requery, applied to the never-ingested held-out ConceptNet split, achieves Hits@10 >= frozen-BGE (0.502) + 0.05 AND nontrivial-subset lift >= 0, with scramble/always-requery controls behaving as they do in the certified base cell (scramble collapses, always-requery breaks easy) — i.e., the architecture that wins at chaining KNOWN facts also wins at inferring UNSEEN ones.
- **HARD-FAIL:** WM-gated requery on this split performs no better than the existing cf-RPE+BFS HARD_FAIL (Hits@10 <= 0.451 +/- noise) or fails to clear frozen-BGE — this would confirm the wall is genuinely about held-out generalization/inference per se (the WordNet-replicated "coverage-completion-not-reasoning" bound), independent of which retrieval architecture is used, and would close this line rather than open it.

## Substrate-product implications

If the gap-filling test HARD-FAILs (the more likely outcome given the WordNet-replication precedent and that the loop's own certified scope note says it works on graph TOPOLOGY, not semantic inference), that is a strong, cheap piece of evidence that the "redundant density helps composition" story is wrong in general — the wall is inference-over-unseen-structure, and no amount of redundant real-world KG topology fixes it with the mechanisms tried so far. That would redirect effort away from "more/redundant text-derived KG data" and toward the structural fixes already flagged elsewhere in this program (grounded/learned meaning, TEM-style factorization at the encoder level per `research_structure_content_factorization_generalizing_meaning_2026-07-26.md`) rather than toward more ConceptNet-composition cells of this family.
