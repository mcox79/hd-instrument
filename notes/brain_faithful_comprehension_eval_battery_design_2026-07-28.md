## 1. Neuroscience frame: "knowing" is assessed by USE, not placement

The brain does not certify that a concept is known by checking whether its neural code sits near related codes in some geometric sense — it is assessed **behaviorally, by what the system can DO with the concept**: verify its properties, generalize a property to a novel instance of its category, detect when something about it is anomalous, and map its relational role onto a structurally analogous situation while ignoring surface resemblance. This is the Rogers & McClelland (*Semantic Cognition: A Parallel Distributed Processing Approach*, MIT Press 2004) account, already cited in the audit note's eval-metric row: property verification / categorization / naming are the behavioral probes of "knowing," not a static similarity score.

Four converging human-literature paradigms, per this session's lit-scan (`notes/research_comprehension_eval_battery_lit_grounding_2026-07-28.md`, Part 1, all VERIFIED-LIVE unless flagged):
- **Property/category verification & typicality** (Rogers & McClelland 2004) — diagnostic because typicality gradients and coherent covariation of property errors only emerge from represented shared feature structure, not an arbitrary label pointer.
- **Category-based induction to a NOVEL instance** (Osherson, Smith, Wilkie, Lopez, Shafir, *Psychological Review* 97:185-200, 1990) — generalizing a property to a conclusion category never paired with that property at learning time is diagnostic of a learned **intension**, not an exemplar lookup table. This is the closest human-literature analog of "memorization-proof."
- **Relational/structural vs. surface similarity** (Gentner & Markman structure-mapping; Markman & Gentner 1990 cross-mapping; Gick & Holyoak 1980 analogical transfer) — cross-mapped stimuli pit a surface-featural match against a relational/structural match; choosing the structural match under cross-mapping is the signature of genuine relational comprehension.
- **N400 semantic-violation ERP paradigm** — a graded, real-time integration-cost signal to congruent vs. incongruent completions, standardly used as a comprehension probe distinct from a recognition-memory probe. Caveat (honest, per lit-scan): the *specific* claim "N400 is more memorization-resistant than a recognition-memory ERP component" was **not independently confirmed live** this session (flagged RECALLED-UNVERIFIED in the source note) — treat the N400 sub-test below as literature-inspired-by-mechanism, not literature-proven-immune-to-shortcuts. It earns its place on construction grounds (degree/frequency-matched distractors), not on citation alone.

No single named "shortcut-proof" paradigm exists in the human literature (there is no reason to build one — humans can't be probed with a frequency table). The NLP field, however, had to solve exactly this problem when benchmarks turned out to be gameable, and independently converged on the same three-part answer.

## 2. The NLP-side convergent pattern (why this is the fix for v6, not a new idea)

Per lit-scan Part 2 (VERIFIED-LIVE with numbers):
- **Peng et al. 2020** ("Learning from Context or Names?", EMNLP 2020, arXiv:2010.01923) is the paradigm v6 explicitly ported (`exp_prop_extraction_selfteach_v6.py` L102-106 cites it by name) — entity/name-identity shortcuts let relation-extraction models skip reading context entirely. v6 built the right DIAGNOSTIC (B0 identity-only baseline) but not yet the right FIX to the task construction underneath it.
- **Poliak, Naradowsky, Haldar, Rudinger, Van Durme, "Hypothesis Only Baselines in Natural Language Inference"** (\*SEM 2018): 69.0% SNLI hypothesis-only vs. 34.28% majority-class. **Gururangan, Swayamdipta, Levy, Schwartz, Bowman, Smith, "Annotation Artifacts in Natural Language Inference Data"** (NAACL 2018): ~67% SNLI / ~53% MultiNLI hypothesis-only. The field's standard **pass/fail gate**: strip content that should be logically necessary; if the ablated baseline stays well above chance, the benchmark is contaminated and must be reconstructed, not just flagged.
- **Gardner et al., "Evaluating Models' Local Decision Boundaries via Contrast Sets"** (Findings of EMNLP 2020, arXiv:2004.02709): minimal, label-flipping perturbations that force genuine content-sensitivity — models drop up to 25pp on contrast instances vs. the original test set. This is the construction TECHNIQUE for building distractors that neutralize an identity shortcut, not just a diagnostic for detecting one after the fact.
- **HellaSwag** (Zellers et al., ACL 2019) and **WinoGrande**: adversarial-filtering (AF) selects hard distractors that a shallow/identity classifier scores well on, specifically to defeat that shortcut — contemporaneous SOTA drops to <48% on AF-filtered HellaSwag while humans stay >95%. This is the **dynamic-range** fix: hard, tunable, k-way forced-choice pools compress the identity-only baseline toward chance while leaving room for genuine comprehension to separate — the direct opposite of relational-AUC's ~0.05 range problem.
- KB-completion literature (TransE/RotatE-era Hits@k/MRR, full-pool ranking) additionally supplies the standard **transductive vs. inductive** split distinction (unseen triple among seen entities, vs. unseen entity/relation entirely) — the precedent for design requirement #1's "new concepts AND new concept-pairs AND new relations" tiering below.

**The synthesis, stated once:** a valid comprehension sub-test = (a) an identity-only ablated baseline that MUST land near chance, PLUS (b) a hard, adversarially-constructed distractor pool sized for real dynamic range, PLUS (c) held-out-NEW at three tiers (new concept, new concept-pair, new relation-type). v6 had (nothing of a); this design fixes that at the construction level, not just the diagnostic level.

## 3. Design requirement #5 first, since it's the crux: the anti-Peng construction

Diagnosing v6's B0=0.754 precisely explains *why* it failed and *how* to fix it: CSKG's relation distribution is itself majority-skewed per object (`/r/LocatedNear` is 60% of all train edges; a given object like "kitchen" has an overwhelmingly dominant relation almost regardless of subject). A frequency table over (object -> majority relation) alone recovers 75% of the answer with **zero reading**. Any 22-way or 16-way full-relation classification task built on this graph inherits that skew.

**The fix is not a better classifier — it's a different candidate pool, per-item, adversarially constructed against B0 itself (the HellaSwag/Gardner method applied to this graph):**

For each held item (subject, true-relation, object, sentence):
1. Compute B0's per-relation score for THIS object from the train-only frequency table (already built in v6, `_b0_pred` logic).
2. Build the forced-choice candidate pool = the **top-K relations by B0 score for this specific object**, always including the true relation. This guarantees B0's own top guess sits inside the confusable pool as a distractor — by construction, B0 cannot out-perform ~1/K on the pool (it can no longer default to "always guess the object's global majority relation," because that majority relation is now competing against 2-3 other relations B0 itself rates almost as plausible for that object).
3. K tunable (start K=4, matching mAFC signal-detection-theory precedent that k-way forced-choice gives more efficient discrimination than pairwise); require the true relation's B0 rank to vary across items (not always rank-1) so the pool isn't trivially "true answer is always B0's top pick, avoid it."
4. Held-out tiers (requirement #1, applying the KB-completion transductive/inductive distinction): **Tier A (new concept)** — subject never seen in ANY train edge. **Tier B (new concept-pair)** — subject and object each seen individually in train, but never together (catches memorized-triple lookup vs. generalized relational reading). **Tier C (new relation-type)** — an entire relation type (e.g. `/r/MadeOf`, small-count in the train table: 233 instances) zero-shot withheld from training exposure entirely, tested via the sentence text alone. Reuse `build_split`'s freq-stratified sha256-ranked partition machinery directly, just re-keyed to also stratify by relation-type-holdout for Tier C.
5. Concept-identity-preserving control (requirement #1's second half): word-scrambled sentence AND wrong-relation-slice control (already built in v6: `extraction_acc_wrong_rel_slice`, subject's own dominant relation excluded) — both retained, both MUST land near the new (lower, K-way) chance rate for a passing test, not just below the old flat majority-baseline band.

This single construction fix (per-object adversarial-pool over the existing B0 table) is a few dozen lines on top of already-built, already-landed v6 infrastructure — no new asset, no new model, no new training run. It is the cheapest, most load-bearing item in this whole design.

## 4. The sub-test menu (4 chosen + 1 stretch)

| # | Sub-test | Brain analog | Construction (anti-memorization) | Identity-only baseline (must be ~chance) | Dynamic range |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Relational cloze v7 (PRIMARY, build first)** — given subject+object+sentence with relation masked, K-way forced choice among B0-confusable relations | Property/relational verification (Rogers & McClelland); the exact task class Peng 2020 targets | Per-item adversarial pool = top-K B0-scored relations for THIS object (Section 3); 3-tier held-out (new concept / new pair / new relation); word-scramble + wrong-dominant-relation controls carried over from v6 | B0 frequency table restricted to the SAME K-way pool -> forced toward ~1/K by construction (currently 0.754 on the unconstrained 16-22-way task; this redesign directly targets that number down to chance) | Chance = 1/K (25% at K=4) vs. an uncompressed accuracy ceiling — order of magnitude wider than relational-AUC's 0.05 |
| 2 | **Property inference (grounding norms)** — from the learned text-rep alone, forced choice among K quantized sensorimotor/VAD archetype profiles (k-means over Lancaster+Warriner+Brysbaert vectors) for a held-out concept | Property verification / feature-listing (Rogers & McClelland) — infers an UNSTATED attribute, the textbook "knowing" probe | Distractor archetypes = nearest-medoid profiles (hard, not random) at matched concept-frequency; concept's true profile never shown at train time (encoder never trains on grounding norms directly — norms are eval-only truth here, mirroring WordNet-lexname's existing eval-only role) | Predict archetype from concept SURFACE-FORM statistics alone (string length, character n-grams, frequency bucket) — sensorimotor profile is not recoverable from spelling, so this should already be near chance; verify empirically before trusting the sub-test | Chance = 1/K over archetype clusters (tune K to grounding_testbed's natural cluster structure, likely K=6-8) |
| 3 | **Category discrimination (WordNet supersense), hard-distractor redesign** — reuse `semantic_eval`'s existing same-lexname infra but replace random-negative AUC with K-way forced choice over CONFUSABLE lexnames (co-occurrence-adjacent categories, e.g. noun.artifact vs noun.body for tool-adjacent words) | Category verification / typicality (Rogers & McClelland) | Adversarial-filtering-style distractor selection: choose lexnames with HIGH marginal co-occurrence overlap with the true lexname's context words (not uniform random draw — the current source of compression) | Predict lexname from unconditional lexname frequency or suffix/morphology cues alone, no context read | Currently compressed (relational-AUC-shaped problem); K-way hard-pool redesign should open this to a real chance/ceiling gap the same way HellaSwag's AF filtering did for SWAG |
| 4 | **Anomaly/violation detection (N400 analog)** — reuse relational_eval's degree-matched pos/neg construction, but score via the encoder's OWN masked-slot predictive probability (true object vs. a plausible-but-false object) instead of static cosine placement | N400 semantic-violation paradigm (congruent vs. incongruent completion, cloze-probability-graded) — literature backing flagged partial (Section 1 caveat) | Distractor object = degree/frequency-matched false completion (same construction as `relational_eval`'s negative sampling, already leak-proof); score = model's predictive log-prob margin, true minus false | Predict true-vs-false purely from OBJECT marginal frequency (unconditional popularity), ignoring subject/relation/sentence — degree-matching already neutralizes this by construction, but must be checked empirically | Continuous margin score, not just accuracy — richer signal; also **doubles as a live instrument for the encoder-objective fix** (audit row #2, predictive-coding direction), so this sub-test pulls double duty |
| 5 (stretch) | **Analogy / cross-mapping** — given a source relation instance, complete a target under the SAME relation but a different concept-pair, forced choice between the relationally-correct completion and a surface-similar-but-relationally-wrong distractor | Gentner structure-mapping / cross-mapping (Markman & Gentner 1990) — the most direct relational-vs-surface dissociation in the literature | Cross-mapped distractor = a concept superficially/co-occurrence-similar to the correct answer but under the WRONG relation-structure; requires constructing matched relation-pair quadruples from CSKG, higher asset-construction cost than 1-4 | Predict from surface/co-occurrence similarity alone (the literal distractor-construction target) — should fail by construction if cross-mapping is built correctly | Binary forced-choice per item, chance=50%, but the CONSTRUCTION difficulty is the real cost — deferred to phase 2, not in the first build |

Sub-tests 1-3 share one property that matters for reuse: they only need a **static concept-rep matrix** (`ground`/`text`/fused arms, exactly `relational_eval`'s and `semantic_eval`'s existing signature) — no live model forward pass. Sub-test 4 needs the live model (masked-slot predictive probability), which is a materially different harness shape and should be wired as an optional second entry point, not force-fit into the static-rep interface.

## 5. Reusable harness spec (requirement #4)

One module, `eval_battery.py` (name only — exp_dev's to build), callable identically for any encoder/readout/consolidation variant:

- **Interface A (static-rep sub-tests 1-3):** `run_battery(rep_matrix, universe, split, adj, deg, seed) -> dict` — same `universe`/`split`/`adj`/`deg` objects `relational_eval`/`semantic_eval` already consume from `exp_scale_meaning_learn_arc_heldout_v2.py`; any variant just needs to produce a `[K, d]` rep matrix, nothing else changes. This is the "drop-in" property requirement 4 asks for.
- **Interface B (predictive sub-test 4):** `run_anomaly_probe(model, tok, held_instances, seed) -> dict` — takes the live model + tokenizer, reuses `relational_eval`'s degree-matched negative-sampling helper directly (already leak-proof, already on disk).
- **Determinism:** every distractor draw is `np.random.default_rng(seed + fixed_offset)`, matching the existing convention in both `relational_eval` (`seed + 71`) and v6 (`seed + 401`) — no new nondeterminism source introduced.
- **Leak-proofing:** reuse `_zero_overlap_witness` (exp_scale_meaning_learn_arc_heldout_v2.py L1066-1069) as-is for the text-corpus side; add an analogous zero-overlap witness for the CSKG-edge side specific to Tier B (new-pair) and Tier C (new-relation) holdout, since those are new split axes this design introduces.
- **Assets, all already on disk, no new acquisition:** `data/cskg_foundation_v1/` (edges, 16 kept relations, sentence-bearing subset), `data/grounding_testbed/` (Lancaster, Warriner, Brysbaert, AoA), `data/wordnet_lexname_cache_v1.json` (existing WordNet lexname cache). Sub-test 5 (analogy) would need a new derived asset (matched relation-pair quadruples) — this is exactly why it's deferred.
- **Cost:** sub-tests 1-3 are pure vector-math over pre-computed reps + one train-only frequency table — smoke-cheap, no GPU needed beyond producing the rep matrix once. Sub-test 4 needs one forward pass per held item at the masked slot — comparable cost to v6's existing FULL run (1241s measured).
- **Cardinality/verdict shape:** carry v6's existing band-pattern forward per sub-test — `MAJORITY_BASELINE_MAX`, `B0_HARD_PASS_MAX` / `B0_HARD_FAIL_MIN`, `COMPREHENSION_MARGIN`, `MIN_HELD_N` — re-derive numeric thresholds per sub-test's own K and chance rate rather than reusing v6's literal numbers (those were tuned for the old unconstrained 16-22-way task).

## 6. Recommendation: cheapest first

**Build sub-test 1 (relational cloze v7, anti-Peng adversarial-pool redesign) first, as the new PRIMARY metric.** Reasons: (a) it is a direct, minimal, load-bearing fix to an experiment that already ran FULL and already HARD_FAILed for exactly the reason this design targets — the fastest possible falsification-or-confirmation of the whole gating hypothesis; (b) it reuses ~95% of already-built, already-verified v6 infrastructure (B0 table, held/train split, scramble control, wrong-relation-slice control, band/verdict machinery) — the only new code is the per-item adversarial candidate-pool construction in Section 3; (c) it requires no new asset acquisition and no new model architecture; (d) a positive result here directly unblocks judging the readout/binding-key fix (audit row #2, v6's stuck iteration) since that row's blocker was explicitly named as "a broken EVAL not a broken mechanism."

Sub-tests 2 and 3 are the next-cheapest (static-rep interface, existing assets, existing eval-only-truth pattern for WordNet already proven), and should follow once sub-test 1 validates the harness shape. Sub-test 4 (N400 anomaly) is worth building in the same wave given its dual role as an instrument for the encoder-objective fix, despite needing the live-model interface. Sub-test 5 (analogy/cross-mapping) is the most brain-faithful of the five but the most expensive to construct (new derived asset) — defer to phase 2.
