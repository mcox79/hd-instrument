## HEADLINE

**No single textbook mechanism is "the loop" — four independently-converging literatures (Construction-
Integration, constraint-based sentence processing, schema/knowledge-based comprehension, and
macrostructure/working-memory theory) describe the SAME four-part cycle, and it maps directly onto
components already scoped in this arc.** (1) Construction generates an over-inclusive candidate set
without pre-filtering by correctness (Kintsch's CI construction phase = the LCCP's unchanged Step 1);
(2) discourse/situation-model context is integrated **immediately, in parallel, as one weighted
constraint among several** — NOT as a late two-stage rerank — per the constraint-based-lexicalist and
referential-theory literatures (directly resolves the task's Angle-2 question: top-down constraint is
interactive, not post-hoc repair, with an honest, literature-documented exception where strong local
verb-argument cues can legitimately resist override); (3) the carried reference for that constraint is
NOT the full accreted situation model but a macrorule-COMPRESSED gist plus a small set of LTWM-style
retrieval cues (Ericsson & Kintsch) — the carry-forward bound is *relocated*, not lifted, onto a small
addressable structure, checkpointed at discontinuity/event boundaries (Zwaan/Gernsbacher MAP-vs-SHIFT,
already wired in the WSM note to the ingest gate's PE signal); (4) whether this produces a genuine
**within-single-document** compounding curve is, honestly, an OPEN EMPIRICAL QUESTION even in the human
literature — the Matthew effect (Stanovich) and schema-priming (Bransford & Johnson) are well-established
but at DEVELOPMENTAL/cross-session grain, and no cited study directly measures a text-specific
comprehension-accuracy curve rising across one continuous document. This is not a source of the design's
weakness but the reason the FAIR test below is a genuinely novel measurement, not a confirmatory replay of
known human data.

**Ranked brain mechanism (name it): SITUATION-MODEL-GUIDED CONSTRUCTION-INTEGRATION with
MACROSTRUCTURE-COMPRESSED CARRY** — over-inclusive candidate construction, settled via a constraint
network biased by a compressed, cue-addressable carried situation model, checkpointed at discontinuity
boundaries. Deflated P=0.32 for the six-way composed design (see Calibration) — this is the AND of three
already-capped novel-synthesis components (WSM, coherence-gate, LCCP) plus two new literature angles, one
of which (within-document compounding) has no direct empirical precedent in either direction.

## Angle 1 — Kintsch Construction-Integration as a computational LOOP (mechanism + citation + implication)

**Mechanism.** Per cycle (roughly one clause/sentence): **construction** is bottom-up and deliberately
indiscriminate — weak associative/lexical activation spreads to related propositions including irrelevant
or contextually wrong ones, with no context-gating at this stage. **Integration** is a constraint-
satisfaction/spreading-activation settle over a proposition-connectivity matrix (built from argument
overlap and text-to-inference fit): mutually consistent propositions reinforce each other via excitatory
links, inconsistent ones are inhibited toward zero — "wrong" constructions are pruned purely by fit within
the activated network, not by a separate correctness rule. Only a small subset survives to seed the next
cycle: Kintsch & van Dijk's (1978) **leading-edge strategy** keeps the propositions highest in the text's
*macrostructure* (the gist hierarchy), not everything recently mentioned — if the next sentence needs an
antecedent that has dropped out of this buffer, the model falls back to a costlier reinstatement search
through long-term episodic text memory. Van den Broek's **Landscape Model** adds two reactivation sources
across cycles: passive resonance (any string-overlap match, relevance-blind) and strategic
coherence-based retrieval, triggered specifically when a coherence-break is detected against the reader's
own coherence standard.

**Citations:** Kintsch (1988, *Psychological Review*); van Dijk & Kintsch (1983); Kintsch & van Dijk (1978,
"Toward a Model of Text Comprehension and Production"); Miller & Kintsch (1980, leading-edge strategy,
referenced not independently re-verified this session); van den Broek et al., Landscape Model papers
(*Behavior Research Methods*; exact author list not independently confirmed); Myers & O'Brien Resonance
Model (title confirmed, year/authorship not independently re-verified); a *Journal of Mathematical
Psychology* paper analytically characterizing CI's integration-step convergence (verified prior session,
cited in the sibling coherence-gate note).

**Implication for the loop:** (1) generate an over-inclusive candidate set with no pre-filter (matches
LCCP Step 1, unchanged); (2) settle via constraint satisfaction BIASED by the carried context vector — this
is the missing document-coherence reranking mechanism the task asks for; (3) compress the settled state
down to a small macrostructure-top subset before carrying forward, not everything recent; (4) fall back to
a costlier broad search only on demand (this is exactly the WSM's Tier-0/1 cheap-buffer-first,
Tier-3-reinstatement-on-demand structure, independently re-derived here from a different literature).

## Angle 2 — Top-down / predictive discourse-context effects on local parsing disambiguation

**Mechanism.** Two literatures converge on the SAME answer to the task's explicit question ("does
discourse context act as an early interactive constraint, or only late reranking/repair?"): **early and
interactive, with one honest exception.** Referential theory (Crain & Steedman 1985; Altmann & Steedman
1988) holds that candidate parses are evaluated in parallel by how well each satisfies discourse-level
presuppositions (e.g., a definite NP requiring a prior contrast set); the analysis requiring fewest new
discourse entities wins immediately — not as post-hoc repair. Constraint-based lexicalist models
(MacDonald, Pearlmutter & Seidenberg 1994; Trueswell & Tanenhaus 1994) go further: lexical-frequency/
verb-bias, semantic plausibility, and discourse constraints are integrated as a SINGLE weighted parallel
process, not staged syntax-then-semantics correction. Visual-world eye-tracking (Spivey-Knowlton,
Trueswell & Tanenhaus 1993; the classic "apple on the towel" referential-scene paradigm, Tanenhaus et al.)
shows FIRST-PASS fixations already reflecting the discourse/scene-consistent parse — direct evidence for
immediate use, not late correction. **The honest exception:** Mitchell, Corley & Garnham (1992) and Britt
(1994) show discourse context can FAIL to override strong local (obligatory-argument-verb) syntactic
biases — some mis-attachments resist context correction in humans too — and the field's own measurement
window (parse-commitment effects can lag context by 2-3 words) leaves room for a partial late-repair
component; the debate over pure-interactive vs. staged-with-fast-repair is not fully closed.

**Citations:** Crain & Steedman (1985); Altmann & Steedman (1988, *Cognition*); MacDonald, Pearlmutter &
Seidenberg (1994, *Psychological Review*); Trueswell & Tanenhaus (1994); Spivey-Knowlton, Trueswell &
Tanenhaus (1993); Tanenhaus, Spivey-Knowlton, Eberhard & Sedivy (1995, *Science*, visual-world paradigm,
not independently re-confirmed this session but well-known/high-confidence); Mitchell, Corley & Garnham
(1992); Britt (1994).

**Implication for the loop (this is the sharpest, most load-bearing design decision this drill
contributes):** the document-coherence/situation-model signal should be wired in as **ONE MORE WEIGHTED
FEATURE inside the LCCP's existing Step 2/3 parallel cue-competition scorer** — not as a separate
post-hoc reranking pass over already-committed candidates. This directly resolves the task's Angle-2
question in favor of design candidate (a) over a two-stage generate-then-rerank architecture, while the
honest exception (strong local cues can legitimately resist override) argues for a WEIGHTED, learnable
feature (consistent with the LCCP's construction-level weight vector and the coherence gate's Friston-style
precision-weighting) rather than an absolute veto.

## Angle 3 — Compounding / Matthew effect: mechanism strong, within-document evidence genuinely absent (honest gap)

**Mechanism.** Bransford & Johnson (1972) established that a relevant schema must be active DURING
encoding, not just available at retrieval — providing context before a cryptic passage raised recalled
idea units from ~3.6 to ~8; partial or post-hoc context gave almost no benefit. Anderson & Pearson's
schema-theoretic account specifies WHY, mechanistically: an active schema provides assimilation slots,
biases importance-judgments (what to encode vs. discard), drives inference-generation to fill gaps, and
structures retrieval — six moment-to-moment functions, not one retrieval aid. The strongest real-time
mechanism evidence is Miller, Cohen & Wingfield (2006, *Memory & Cognition*): giving readers a passage
TITLE measurably sped their reading (1623ms vs. 1817ms/segment) AND their concurrent secondary-task
reaction time (459ms vs. 490ms) **during comprehension itself** — direct evidence that active context
frees working-memory resources online, not merely post-hoc, with the effect amplified for low-WM-span
readers. Stanovich's (1986) Matthew effect proposes a reciprocal causal loop (vocabulary/knowledge -> more
successful reading -> more vocabulary/knowledge) — but this is explicitly a **developmental, years-scale**
account, and empirical support is genuinely mixed (a cited meta-analysis titled "Matthew Effects in Reading
Comprehension: Myth or Reality?" reports the predicted fan-spread pattern often fails to appear in observed
cohorts).

**The honest gap:** no study found in this scan directly measures a text-specific comprehension-
speed/accuracy learning curve rising across the course of a SINGLE continuous document, isolated as a
controlled variable. The closest analogs are all confounded: cross-sectional expert-vs-novice comparisons
(prior knowledge brought TO the text, not built up DURING it), multi-text spontaneous-reactivation studies
(across separate texts/sessions, not one document), and the Miller/Cohen/Wingfield result (title-present
vs. absent, not early-vs-late portions of the SAME passage). **This within-single-document compounding
curve is an open empirical question, not a settled human finding, in either direction.**

**Citations:** Bransford & Johnson (1972); Anderson & Pearson (schema-theoretic view, via Reading Rockets/
ERIC summary); Miller, Cohen & Wingfield (2006, *Memory & Cognition*, PMC2812925 — verified primary text);
Stanovich (1986, *Reading Research Quarterly*); a "Matthew Effects in Reading Comprehension: Myth or
Reality?" meta-analytic source (title-verified, full citation details not independently confirmed);
Cunningham & Stanovich (1997/1998); "Learning from texts: activation of information from previous texts
during reading" (PMC4875937).

**Implication for the loop:** mechanism-level support is strong for WHY schema-active-during-encoding
should change moment-to-moment parsing cost/accuracy (nothing in the cited mechanisms is intrinsically
tied to a multi-year timescale — the requirement is an ACTIVE schema, and a situation model growing across
a document IS an active schema by the time enough of the document is read). But because no direct human
precedent exists for the SPECIFIC within-document curve, this design's compounding claim must be treated as
a genuinely NEW, testable hypothesis — not an import of an established finding — and the cheap decisive
test below is the first direct measurement of it, which is itself a contribution regardless of outcome.

## Angle 4 — Compress-and-carry / macrostructure (mechanism + citation + implication)

**Mechanism.** Van Dijk & Kintsch's three macrorules compress a sequence of microstructure propositions
into a superordinate macroproposition (the gist): **deletion** (drop locally-irrelevant detail not
presupposed by later content), **generalization** (replace a set with their shared superordinate, e.g.
"dog, cat, canary" -> "pets"), **construction** (replace a cause-act-consequence sequence with the single
global fact it constitutes, e.g. "went to station, bought ticket, boarded train" -> "took a train") — all
governed by a schema representing the reader's goals, not applied blind bottom-up. The 1983 "strategic"
reframing treats this as ONLINE: tentative macropropositions form cycle-by-cycle as top-down-plus-bottom-up
hypotheses, revised as more text arrives, and feed back to bias interpretation of new local propositions
(medium-confidence citation flag: this specific "cycle-by-cycle hypothesis-and-revision" characterization
is a secondary-source synthesis this session, primary-text extraction failed on a corrupted PDF stream).
Ericsson & Kintsch's (1995) **long-term working memory (LTWM)** explains how the carried state stays
bounded: true active STM only holds the current cycle plus a small prior-cycle carry-over buffer (matching
Angle 1's leading-edge strategy exactly); skilled readers extend far beyond this narrow buffer via durable,
task-specific LTM **retrieval structures**, addressed by small retrieval CUES kept active in STM — the
bound is *relocated onto pointers*, not lifted. Zwaan/Radvansky's event-indexing model and Gernsbacher's
Structure-Building Framework give the trigger: a discontinuity on one or more of the five dimensions
(protagonist, time, space, causation, intentionality) below threshold gets folded into the current
situation model (**MAP**, cheap, in-place update); a discontinuity on MULTIPLE simultaneous dimensions
triggers **SHIFT** — the current substructure is checkpointed/compressed, its activation suppressed (not
deleted — a deactivated branch, retrievable later), and a fresh substructure opens.

**Citations:** Kintsch & van Dijk (1978); van Dijk & Kintsch (1983, full-text located but extraction
failed on a corrupted stream — secondary-source-only this session); Ericsson & Kintsch (1995,
*Psychological Review*, "Long-Term Working Memory"); Zwaan, Langston & Graesser (1995, *Psychological
Science*); Zwaan, Radvansky, Hilliard & Curiel (1998); Zwaan (2025, *Perspectives on Psychological
Science*, 25-year retrospective — not yet read closely, flagged for a possible future drill); Gernsbacher
(1990, *Language Comprehension as Structure Building*; 1995 framework paper).

**Implication for the loop:** carry forward NOT the raw accreted situation model but (i) a small set of
retrieval CUES/pointers into a structured durable store, refreshed each cycle, and (ii) a compressed gist
produced by macrorule-style deletion/generalization/construction at each SHIFT checkpoint — this is the
concrete mechanism (independently re-derived here from a third literature) for exactly the "compress before
carry" discipline the WSM note already wired to the ingest gate's PE signal (Tier 2 -> Tier 3 consolidation
at event boundaries). The genuinely NEW contribution versus the 07-17 WSM note: the compression itself
should be a macrorule pass (delete/generalize/construct), not a bare threshold-triggered dump of the raw
bundle, and the Tier-3 store should hold retrieval CUES (LTWM-style pointers), not full re-encoded content.

## Angle 5 — THE DESIGN VERDICT: a glass-box loop built around the ranked mechanism

**Ranked brain mechanism, restated:** SITUATION-MODEL-GUIDED CONSTRUCTION-INTEGRATION with
MACROSTRUCTURE-COMPRESSED CARRY — over-inclusive candidate construction (unchanged LCCP Step 1),
document-coherence integrated as ONE weighted parallel feature (Angle 2, not a late rerank), a carried
reference that is macrorule-compressed and cue-addressable rather than raw-accreted (Angle 4), checkpointed
at PE-detected discontinuity boundaries (already wired to the WSM's Tier 2->3 consolidation trigger), with
an honestly-unproven-but-mechanistically-motivated compounding property (Angle 3).

### Concrete design: the Compress-and-Carry Loop (CCL), wired directly into the LCCP

**Composes with, does not replace:**
- WSM Tier 2 (activated N/16-32 bundle over the 5 Zwaan dimensions) IS the carried situation model — no
  new store.
- WSM Tier 3 (Frontier-2 exact-record paging, entered via the already-VET'd ingest-gate PE/unexpectedness
  signal) IS the durable, cue-addressable LTWM store.
- The coherence gate's Score 1/Score 2/settling/DEFERRED machinery is reused verbatim for the flagged
  middle-band case, extended to include the Tier-2 node.
- The LCCP's Step 2 (cue-feature extraction) and Step 3 (learned linear scoring) are extended by ONE new
  feature, not restructured.

**Step 2b (new) — document-coherence feature:** for each LCCP candidate binding, compute a bound-vector
overlap (cosine/dot-product, the same operation the WSM note already specifies for its MAP-vs-SHIFT
discontinuity check) between the candidate's bound role-fillers and the CURRENT Tier-2 bundle, across the
5 Zwaan dimensions independently (protagonist, time, space, causation, intentionality). This is a
DIFFERENT signal from the sibling coherence gate's Score 1 (which checks a relation's fit against a
static/growing centroid at the RELATION level) — it specifically checks whether THIS candidate's
protagonist/causal-role assignment is consistent with who-has-been-doing-what across the DOCUMENT so far,
which is exactly the class of error the task names (show/you vs. show/way — locally plausible, wrong given
the wider story).

**Step 3 (extended) — the document-coherence feature enters the LCCP's existing per-construction linear
scorer as one more weighted dimension**, per Angle 2's finding: integrated in parallel with cue-competition
features, not as a categorical veto — a candidate with strong local verb-argument support can still win
even against a moderate document-coherence penalty, respecting Angle 2's honest exception (some
mis-attachments resist context override in humans too, and should be allowed to resist it here).

**Settling (reused, extended):** when the document-coherence feature and the sentence-local coherence-gate
score disagree sharply (one accepts, one rejects), route to the coherence gate's existing bounded
CI-style settling network — now including the Tier-2 bundle as an additional fixed high-confidence node the
network settles against, not just same-sentence neighbor relations. Converges high = accept; low = reject;
oscillates = DEFERRED (the existing third state, unchanged).

**Compress-and-carry checkpoint (new machinery, the core contribution of this drill):** reuse the
already-VET'd ingest-gate PE/unexpectedness signal (already established in the WSM note as the MAP-vs-SHIFT
trigger). Below threshold (routine continuation): **MAP** — fold the new clause's role-fillers into the
CURRENT Tier-2 bundle, cheap, no compression pass. Above threshold (multi-dimension discontinuity, a real
scene/event change): **SHIFT** — (i) apply a macrorule pass (deletion of locally-irrelevant detail,
generalization of repeated entity mentions, construction of cause-act-consequence sequences into single
gist propositions) to the just-closed Tier-2 bundle; (ii) commit the compressed gist to Tier 3 as a small
set of retrieval CUES (pointers, not the raw bundle — the LTWM discipline), (iii) open a fresh, empty Tier-2
bundle for the new scene. This directly prevents the raw bundle from exceeding the already-measured
N/16-32 crosstalk ceiling (per the WM note) as a document grows — compression is not cosmetic, it is what
keeps the carried reference usable at all past a certain document length.

**Why this raises precision past the LCCP's 0.50 ceiling:** the document-coherence feature is the only
signal in the whole stack that looks OUTSIDE the current sentence/relation — neither the LCCP's
construction-level cue-weights nor the sentence-local coherence gate can see a candidate that is locally
plausible but wrong given who/what has been established across the document. This is precisely the
within-frame-coherent-but-document-incoherent failure class the task names.

**Why this compounds (mechanistically motivated, not a replay of an established human curve — see Angle
3's honest gap):** the document-coherence feature is literally a similarity score against a reference
vector (Tier 2, backed by Tier 3) that accumulates more confirmed content as the document is read. As that
reference grows, the mechanical separation between right- and wrong-candidate scores should increase —
independent of whether human comprehension research has directly measured this specific curve. This is why
the test below is a genuinely novel measurement of the mechanism's own behavior, not a confirmatory replay.

## Falsifiable predictions — HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL

**Prediction 1 (precision raise on the targeted failure class).** P=0.30 (deflated; composes three
already-capped novel-synthesis components). HARD-PASS: on a curated subset of within-frame-coherent-but-
document-incoherent mis-attachments (independent gold, never self-graded), the full loop (Arm 3, below)
reduces the FP rate by >=15 points vs. the LCCP sentence-local-only baseline (Arm 1). HARD-FAIL: <5-point
reduction — would mean the document-coherence feature is too weak/noisy relative to the local cue features
it competes against in Step 3, even with the full compress-and-carry discipline; the honest fallback per
the LCCP note's own brain-check is to consider a stronger auxiliary signal (small spot-checked gold seed
set) rather than pure situation-model coherence.

**Prediction 2 (compounding — the within-document learning curve, a genuinely novel measurement per Angle
3's honest gap).** P=0.25 (deflated further than Prediction 1 — no direct human precedent exists in either
direction for this specific curve, so there is no literature-agreement floor to anchor confidence on).
HARD-PASS: a statistically significant POSITIVE slope in precision (or in the document-coherence feature's
discriminative margin between correct/incorrect candidates) across document-position bins for Arm 3, while
Arm 1 (no carried state at all) shows a flat/no-slope curve — the construct-validity control that rules out
a generic order-effect artifact. HARD-FAIL: flat or negative slope for Arm 3 — a live, non-strawman possible
outcome given Angle 3's evidentiary gap, not a surprising failure if it occurs.

**Prediction 3 (compression specifically, not just "having a situation model," drives the compounding —
isolates the Angle-4 contribution).** P=0.30 (deflated). Arm 2 = document-coherence feature added WITHOUT
macrorule compression (Tier-2 bundle accretes flat/uncompressed or is naively truncated); Arm 3 = full loop
with PE-triggered MAP/SHIFT compression. HARD-PASS: Arm 3 beats Arm 2 specifically on LONG documents (where
uncompressed Tier-2 approaches/exceeds the already-measured N/16-32 crosstalk ceiling), with Arm 2 ~= Arm 3
on short documents — the clean dissociation signature that compression, not mere state-carrying, drives the
compounding win. HARD-FAIL: Arm 2 and Arm 3 perform identically regardless of document length — would mean
the specific compress-and-carry machinery is not load-bearing (though a cruder "any carried state" design
might still work, a separate, less interesting finding).

## FAIR can-fail test (full specification)

**Real baseline:** the LCCP exactly as already pre-registered (sentence-local coherence-gate signal only,
no situation-model feature, no compression checkpoint) — Arm 1, the system this arc was already building
toward, not a strawman.

**Can-fail (both directions):** the loop genuinely can hurt, not just fail to help — an over-weighted
document-coherence feature can suppress a TRUE candidate that happens to be a legitimate scene-change or
genuinely surprising plot development (a real risk the coherence-gate note already flags for its own
Score-1 upgrade: real narrative content has legitimately diverse fillers). Symmetrically, an
under-compressed or badly-triggered checkpoint could let stale Tier-2 content bias scoring after a real
scene change has occurred (a false-continuity error, the MAP/SHIFT mistake in the wrong direction).

**One variable per arm:**
- Arm 1: LCCP baseline, sentence-local only (Prediction set already specified in the 07-19 LCCP note).
- Arm 2: + Step 2b document-coherence feature, no compression/checkpointing (isolates the situation-model
  feature alone).
- Arm 3: + PE-triggered MAP/SHIFT compression and LTWM-cue carry (isolates the compress-and-carry
  discipline specifically).

**Held-out:** (verb, construction) pairs never jointly seen during training (reused from the LCCP note's
split) CROSSED with a multi-scene document corpus carrying independent human-placed scene-boundary labels
(reused from the WSM note's Prediction 2 design) — this lets the same corpus serve both the precision test
and the compounding/checkpoint-quality test.

**Measured, not just accuracy:** (a) precision/recall on the curated within-frame-coherent-but-document-
incoherent subset specifically, not overall precision (this loop targets one failure class, and a gain
there could mask a loss elsewhere — report both); (b) the document-position-binned learning curve
(required, per the task); (c) MAP-vs-SHIFT trigger agreement with human-placed scene boundaries (reused
metric from the WSM note, informs whether the checkpoint fires at the right times, which gates whether
Prediction 3's dissociation is even measurable).

## Brain-check (outcome not pre-assumed)

**Situation-model-guided, compounding comprehension IS a real brain capability** — skilled readers
demonstrably use discourse context to resolve local ambiguity online (Angle 2's visual-world/referential-
theory evidence) and demonstrably get better within a genre/domain over long exposure (Stanovich's Matthew
effect, Angle 3). This is not a case of the brain lacking the capability.

**Where the brain-check reveals a real, shared structural bound (same-limit, accept):** CI/Landscape
settling can confidently converge on a wrong reading (the garden-path lingering-misinterpretation finding,
already flagged in the sibling coherence-gate note) — this loop's own settling step inherits that exact
limit, which is precisely why the DEFERRED state is mandatory rather than optional. Separately, Ericsson &
Kintsch's LTWM finding is itself a same-limit result: the carry-forward bound is *relocated* onto small
cues, not lifted — our own Tier-2 bundle is subject to the SAME N/16-32 crosstalk ceiling biology also hits;
unbounded uncompressed accretion is not a free substrate-native escape hatch (though the EXPLICIT,
glass-box compression discipline could in principle be tuned more precisely than a biological schema — a
plausible, untested substrate-native edge, not asserted as fact).

**Where document-context may be genuinely irreducible (accept-bound, not a design failure):** per Angle
2's counter-evidence (Mitchell, Corley & Garnham 1992; Britt 1994), some mis-attachment classes resist
discourse-context correction even in human wetware when a local obligatory-argument cue is strong — the
design's weighted-feature choice (not an absolute veto) is meant to respect this, and the FAIR test should
expect some residual mis-attachments even under a fully-working loop. This is an honest, not smoothed-over,
ceiling.

**The one genuinely open honest risk (not a same-limit or brain-fails-the-same-way case, but a genuine
unknown):** per Angle 3, NO direct human study — in either direction — measures a within-single-document
comprehension-accuracy curve. If Prediction 2 HARD-FAILs, this would not contradict an established human
finding (none exists to contradict); it would mean the mechanistically-motivated hypothesis (a growing
