# Research: the brain's multi-source information environment, and a circularity-free arena for surprise / schema-fit / recurrence

**Filed by:** research (Opus synthesis over 4 parallel Sonnet lit-scans)
**Date:** 2026-07-16
**Trigger:** USER drill — current single-static-KG arena makes schema-fit (graph-structural placement) and recurrence (node-degree proxy) near-copies of the reachability label that defines "derivability," so both signals collapse onto the label and each other. Need to understand WHY a real environment keeps these independent, then design an arena that inherits that independence.

## HEADLINE

In a real environment, surprise/schema-fit/recurrence are independent **because they are computed over three structurally different substrates**: surprise is a *temporal/predictive* quantity (violation of an online forward model built from redundant multimodal time-series), schema-fit is a *static structural/associative-overlap* quantity (mPFC-mediated match against a consolidated semantic network, computed with no reference to time or source), and recurrence/corroboration is a *cross-source epistemic* quantity that is only informative when sources are **conditionally independent** (Bayesian cue-combination / truth-discovery condition) — a raw repetition or degree count is NOT recurrence, it is a repetition-count that truth-discovery literature explicitly shows produces false confidence when sources are copies of each other. A single static knowledge graph has only ONE generative process (the edge-adding process itself), so all three "signals" measured on it are different linear projections of that one process — they are mathematically forced to collinearize. The fix is not a better formula, it's an arena with three genuinely separate generative processes: a temporal arrival stream, a pre-existing structural schema built by a DIFFERENT process than the claim stream, and a source-reliability/dependence graph that is hidden from the gate and must be inferred.

## Part 1 — The brain's world as an information environment

The literature converges on four structural facts about the real environment that make each signal computable and separable:

**(a) Surprise requires temporal redundancy to build a forward model against.** The hippocampal CA1 comparator (Lisman & Otmakhova 2001; Lisman & Grace 2005, *Neuron*) works because CA3 continuously generates a predicted next-state from stored associations, and CA1 compares it against actual EC3 input — a genuine prediction-vs-outcome computation, confirmed by Kumaran & Maguire's (2007, *J Neurosci*) associative-mismatch paradigm (hippocampus activates on violated sequence predictions, not on unfamiliarity per se). This is only possible because natural multimodal streams carry strong temporal and cross-modal autocorrelation (efficient-coding literature: the retina/LGN "whitens" exactly this redundancy because it's what makes prediction possible at all). Mismatch negativity (MMN) work shows this generative model is *hierarchical* — dissociable local (tone-to-tone) vs global (sequence-level) regularity violations — meaning surprise is computed **relative to a nested, schema-conditioned predictive model**, not as a flat global anomaly flag. This is a load-bearing cross-check on the 07-15 foundation-builder v4 finding (surprise must be schema-CONDITIONED, not whole-relation-presence) — MMN's local/global dissociation is the direct biological analog and independently confirms that framing.

**(b) Schema-fit is a static structural-overlap computation, evaluated with no reference to time or source.** Tse et al. (2007, *Science*) and the SLIMM model (van Kesteren, Fernández, Henson, Ruiter 2012, *TINS*) show mPFC/vmPFC rapidly integrates schema-congruent material via associative match against an existing consolidated cortical network — fast, low-prediction-error route — while hippocampus is recruited for incongruent material via a genuinely separate (surprise-driven) pathway. Schema-fit is thus mechanistically dissociable both from surprise (temporal) and from source-corroboration (evidentiary) — it answers "does this slot into the associative network I already have," a question with no time index and no notion of "who told me."

**(c) Recurrence is source-independence-weighted evidence accumulation, not a tally.** Three converging strands: (i) hippocampal replay/SWRs are *prioritized* by novelty/reward/prediction-error, not a flat function of raw exposure count (Carr/Jadhav/Frank; Ambrose/Pfeiffer/Foster on reward-biased replay) — replay converts episodic traces into cortical knowledge at a rate gated by salience, not frequency; (ii) statistical/regularity learning (Saffran, Aslin & Newport 1996) shows recurrence-as-regularity is extracted online from a raw co-occurrence stream via transitional-probability tracking — a live estimation process, not a lookup on a pre-built structure; (iii) Bayesian/drift-diffusion evidence accumulation (Gold & Shadlen 2007) weights each observation by its reliability/precision (log-likelihood-ratio) before summing — a handful of high-reliability observations can outweigh many low-reliability ones. None of these are compatible with "count the edges."

**(d) The three axes are separable in the real world because natural/social environments are only *partially* correlated across time, structure, and source — not because they are perfectly independent.** Barlow's efficient-coding hypothesis (redundancy reduction in sensory coding) supplies the ecological argument: natural signals carry correlational structure across many axes simultaneously, and organisms evolve near-factorial codes precisely because the environment doesn't hand you one axis at a time. The same logic (Part 4 below) applies to social/testimonial environments.

## Part 2 — Source reliability: how the brain (and formal epistemology) handles differing reliability

**Source/reality monitoring.** Johnson, Hashtroudi & Lindsay's (1993, *Psych Bull*) source monitoring framework: memories are not literally tagged with a source label; source is inferred at retrieval from qualitative trace properties (perceptual detail, contextual detail, amount of "cognitive operations" evidence). mPFC/anterior PFC tracks self-generated-vs-external judgments (Simons et al. 2008, *J Neurosci*; causal TMS confirmation, Fujiwara et al. 2020); parietal cortex separately tracks recollection-confidence vs familiarity-confidence. This is a real dissociation: WHO/HOW a claim was acquired is tagged by different circuitry than WHAT the claim's content is.

**Epistemic vigilance / testimony calibration.** Sperber et al.'s (2010) "epistemic vigilance" frames trust calibration as evaluating a source's competence AND honesty, updated by track record. Developmentally, Koenig, Clément & Harris (2004, *Psych Science*) show 3-4 year-olds track individual informants' accuracy history and preferentially trust previously-reliable sources for NEW claims — trust is a running reliability estimate, carried forward, not re-derived per claim.

**Cross-source integration requires conditional independence.** Ernst & Banks (2002, *Nature*) MLE cue-combination: inverse-variance weighting of cues is only optimal, and only reduces uncertainty at all, when cue noise is uncorrelated. This generalizes to social testimony: "wisdom of crowds" aggregation gains from independence, not headcount — correlated errors (shared source, common bias) make aggregation amplify systematic error instead of cancelling noise. Bovens & Hartmann's Bayesian epistemology formalizes this with explicit penalties for "hidden circularity" (shared causal ancestry among reports) versus genuine positive relevance (independent convergence).

**Discounting non-independent/unreliable sources.** Truth-discovery / data-fusion literature (Dong, Berti-Équille & Srivastava — arXiv:1503.00310 and related; Bayesian truth-discovery variants like DEPEN) makes this operational: algorithms jointly infer per-source reliability AND detect source dependence/copying (shared idiosyncratic values across "independent" sources reveal copying), because naively pooling N corroborating sources that are actually 1 source echoed N times produces false confidence. This is the direct real-world basis of "corroboration ≠ node-degree" — degree in a graph has no notion of whether the edges came from independent evidence or from one generative process replicated.

## Part 3 — Recurrence as true evidence-integration (not a static statistic)

Spacing/testing effects show repetition value is gated by *retrieval difficulty*, not raw count (Bjork's storage-strength/retrieval-strength framework: a repetition after a hard, effortful retrieval adds far more durable strength than an easy one) — mechanistically, study-phase retrieval (each later exposure re-triggers and re-encodes the earlier trace) dominates over simple re-exposure. Complementary Learning Systems theory (McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly 1995; O'Reilly et al. 2014) explains WHY repetition-via-replay matters at all: hippocampus stores fast, pattern-separated episodic traces; slow interleaved cortical replay extracts generalized statistical structure across many episodes without catastrophic interference. Replay rate is *prioritized* (novelty/reward/prediction-error weighted, Ambrose/Pfeiffer/Foster; Carr/Jadhav/Frank), not flat. The net picture: recurrence-as-reliability is a **precision-weighted, prioritization-gated, online accumulation process across time and occasions** — categorically different from counting static graph edges.

## Part 4 — How the three axes concretely decorrelate (and the environmental conditions required)

Three formally distinct statistical properties must vary **semi-independently** across a real environment for the axes to separate:

1. **Temporal/contextual predictability** (does this violate my moment-to-moment forward model) — requires a temporally-extended arrival stream with learnable autocorrelation.
2. **Associative-structural overlap** (does this slot into my existing consolidated semantic network) — requires a pre-existing schema built by a *different, prior* process than the current claim stream, so structural fit isn't definitionally entangled with how the claim arrived.
3. **Inter-source error correlation** (are the corroborating reports drawn from independent evidence, or copied) — requires an actual population of distinct sources with a hidden dependence graph among them.

A single static knowledge graph fails all three preconditions at once: there is no temporal arrival process (so "surprise" degenerates to a structural anomaly, indistinguishable from schema-fit); the schema IS the same graph the claims are drawn from (so schema-fit is definitionally entangled with the claim-generating process); and there are no sources at all, only edges, so "corroboration" can only be operationalized as degree — which is exactly a repetition-of-one-generative-process count, the thing truth-discovery literature identifies as a **false-corroboration artifact**, not evidence. This is the precise mechanism of the collapse the USER observed.

**Uncertainty flag (explicit, per sub-agent reports):** the neuroscience literature on novelty/surprise does NOT itself model "number of independent corroborating sources" as a hippocampal-circuit signal — that concept lives entirely in social-epistemology / testimony / truth-discovery literature, a separate field. The synthesis connecting these three literatures into one 3-axis framework is **ours**, not an established joint model in any single paper. Treat Part 4's decorrelation argument as a well-supported synthesis across independently-corroborated sub-literatures, not a citation of one unified source.

## Part 5 — Arena design translation

### Minimal ingredients

1. **Ground-truth world model**, generated by a process kept separate from both the schema-graph and the source population — each candidate claim has a TRUE/FALSE (or graded-truth) label assigned by this hidden generator, not derivable from schema-graph structure or corroboration count by construction.
2. **A pre-existing consolidated schema** (an embedding/associative-graph structure built BEFORE the claim stream starts, from a disjoint seed corpus) — schema-fit = structural/associative distance between an incoming claim and this pre-built structure, computed with no reference to time-of-arrival or source identity.
3. **A population of >=5-6 sources**, each with:
   - an independent reliability parameter (accuracy rate, e.g. 0.5-0.95),
   - a scope/specialization (only asserts claims in some schema sub-region, mirroring real source specialization),
   - a hidden **dependence graph**: some sources are genuinely independent (separate underlying evidence); others are correlated "echoes" (copy another source with added noise) — this must NOT be visible to the gate; it must be inferable only from claim-content correlation patterns, per truth-discovery method.
   - a temporal arrival schedule (claims emitted over discrete time steps, not all at once), with autocorrelated content (some topical persistence) so an online forward-model/transitional-probability estimator has structure to learn.
4. **Conflict injection**: a controlled fraction of claims contradict either ground truth or each other, at varying source-reliability levels, so "conflicting testimony" is a real, present condition, not an edge case.
5. **Full factorial / stratified sampling of the evaluation set**: explicitly balance claims across a grid of {surprising vs expected} x {schema-fit vs schema-violating} x {high independent-corroboration vs low/echo-only corroboration} x {true vs false ground truth}, keeping true/false roughly balanced within every cell. This is the direct fix for circularity — by construction, no single signal alone can perfectly predict the ground-truth label, because the label was assigned independently of all three signals.

### Circularity-free eval metrics

- **Arena-validity precondition (must pass BEFORE testing the gate):** measure pairwise correlation among the three raw signal scores across the claim stream. Target |r| < 0.3 pairwise. If the constructed arena still produces |r| > 0.6 between any pair, the arena itself hasn't achieved independence — fix the arena, not the gate.
- **Partial/conditional predictive power:** report each signal's conditional mutual information with the ground-truth-derived "should assimilate" target, CONDITIONAL on the other two signals — not marginal correlation. A signal only earns credit if it retains predictive power after the other two are conditioned out.
- **Copying stress-test (the discriminating cell):** construct matched pairs of claims with IDENTICAL raw assertion-count but different source-dependence structure (one claim corroborated by N independent sources, one by N copies of one source). A genuine corroboration signal must score the independent case higher; a degree-proxy will fail this by construction — this is the direct test that a fixed single-graph arena cannot express at all (no notion of source-independence exists there).
- **Schema-conditioned vs global surprise check:** verify surprise is computed WITHIN the local schema-region (per MMN's local/global dissociation), not as a flat whole-relation-presence flag — carries over and operationalizes the 07-15 foundation-builder v4 finding.
- **Within-cell ground-truth recovery:** compute gate-decision-vs-ground-truth accuracy ONLY within each stratification cell (not marginally) — this is the actual "does the gate work" metric, immune to the marginal-correlation circularity that broke the single-graph test.

### HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL thresholds (pre-registered)

**HARD-PASS:**
- Arena validity: pairwise |r| among raw signals < 0.3 (all three pairs), measured before any gate logic runs.
- Copying stress-test: gate assigns >=1.5x higher corroboration weight to matched independent-source claims vs copied-source claims (p<0.05).
- >=2/3 signals retain nonzero conditional mutual information with ground truth after conditioning on the other two.
- Gate's within-cell classification accuracy beats the best single-signal baseline by >=15% relative error reduction (genuine multi-signal value-add, not redundant collapse).

**HARD-FAIL:**
- Pairwise |r| remains > 0.6 for any signal pair despite the stratified/multi-source redesign (arena still degenerate — same failure mode as the single graph, root-caused elsewhere).
- Gate cannot distinguish independent from copied corroboration better than chance on the stress test (confirms it's still a degree-proxy in disguise).
- Removing any one signal from the gate changes decision accuracy by <2% (that signal was doing no independent work — it was a redundant copy of another, exactly the failure mode this redesign targets).

## Cheap decisive test

Before writing any substrate arena code: run the **arena-validity precondition as a pure simulation** (spreadsheet/toy-Python generative model, no substrate cell, no dispatch) — construct a small synthetic claim population (~200 claims, 5-6 sources with declared reliability + a hidden 2-cluster dependence structure, a schema-embedding built from a disjoint seed set, and a discrete-time arrival schedule with autocorrelated topics) and directly compute the three signals' pairwise correlations using the simplest possible proxies (surprise = deviation from a simple online transitional-probability tracker; schema-fit = embedding distance to the pre-built schema; corroboration = naive vs. dependence-corrected independent-source count). If even this toy simulation can't get pairwise |r| below 0.3, the stratification design itself needs rework before any substrate-side cost is spent. This is cheap (no cell, no GPU/CPU queue) and decisive (kills or greenlights the whole arena concept at near-zero cost).

## Substrate-product implications

- The fix for the collapse is structural (build a genuinely multi-source, temporally-extended arena), not a formula change on top of the existing single-graph test — no reweighting of the current signals will decorrelate them, because the underlying generative process is single-source by construction.
- A source-dependence/copying detector (truth-discovery style) is a standalone, buildable, independently-valuable substrate primitive beyond the gate itself — useful for auditable provenance and conflict resolution across ingested sources generally, not just for this arena.
- An online transitional-probability/regularity tracker (Saffran-style) is a different component than the existing static schema-graph placement score and should be built as its own module — conflating them was part of what caused the original collapse.
- This design gives the "brain-faithful" racing arm (07-16 session thrust) an actual fair environment to run in; without it, brain-faithful vs learned-weights vs hybrid cannot be meaningfully distinguished because the current test environment can't express the independence the brain-faithful mechanism depends on.
