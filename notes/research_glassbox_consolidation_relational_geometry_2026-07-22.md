## HEADLINE

**Split verdict, and the split is itself the answer.** The literature contains TWO distinct biological consolidation mechanisms, and they behave oppositely on the crux question:

1. **Flat prototype/overlap reshaping (Lewis & Durrant 2011 iOtA; classic "schema gist" abstraction)** — mechanistically IS a frequency/overlap-weighted similarity statistic. It is glass-box (Hebbian, inspectable) but it does **not** escape the similarity-collapse wall. This matches — independently, from the outside — our own atom 29440 refutation (linear atomize+sleep reduces analytically to fixed-similarity kNN). **NO-GO for this variant.** The weaker behavioral literature claiming sleep produces abstract *rule* generalization (Ellenbogen et al. 2007 transitive inference; Gómez/Werchan infant grammar generalization; Lau/Alger/Fishbein radical generalization; Cai & Mednick creativity) is CONTESTED/unreplicated where tested rigorously (Morgan & Stickgold 2017, Matorina & Poppenk 2021, Berens & Bird 2022 all failed to reproduce Ellenbogen's sleep-specific effect; Werchan & Gómez 2016 found *wake*, not sleep, drove generalization at 2.5y) and where explicable, explicable by ordinary feature-overlap/associative-network abstraction, not a new relational axis. Honest read: this branch gives **no credible evidence** of relational-feature escape.

2. **Sequential/generative replay as compositional graph traversal (Kurth-Nelson, Economides, Dolan & Dayan 2016, *Neuron*; Liu, Dolan, Kurth-Nelson & Behrens 2019, *Cell*; Schwartenbeck, Baram, Liu, Kurth-Nelson, Behrens et al. 2023, *Cell*)** — this is a **documented, well-evidenced exception**. Replay here does not average overlapping episodes into a shared prototype; it **samples/rolls out sequences over a previously-learned LOCAL transition graph**, producing paths that were never directly experienced (novel shortcuts, rule-implied orders that diverge from the scrambled orders actually presented). Liu et al. 2019's Study 2 explicitly disrupted pairwise transitions by design so that correct replayed sequences "could only be inferred using structural knowledge" — this rules out a pure pairwise-similarity account for that output. This mechanism is still glass-box: sequential sampling/rollout over a locally-learned edge set requires no backprop. **GO, but for a qualitatively different mechanism than flat geometry-reshaping**: the escape from similarity comes from **multi-hop composition over a relational graph**, not from reshaping a single embedding/similarity space in one pass.

The CLS-theory literature itself (McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly 1995; Kumaran, Hassabis & McClelland 2016) is honest-but-uncomfortable background: the *original* neocortical "slow structure extractor" was cast in the backprop-connectionist lineage (opaque), and the 2016 update doubled down on the CNN-as-neocortex analogy rather than moving toward inspectability. The one clearly Hebbian/glass-box, well-replicated piece of CLS-adjacent evidence (Tse et al. 2007/2011 schema-consistent one-trial consolidation, NMDA-receptor-dependent LTP in prelimbic mPFC) is real and glass-box — but it describes **assimilation of a new item into an already-existing relational scaffold**, not the **creation of a new relational scaffold from raw unstructured cases**. That creation step is exactly what's missing from the glass-box literature reviewed here, and is the honest residual gap.

## Cheap decisive test

Reuse the validated 29443 real-text agreement testbed (subject-verb agreement, head-noun vs linearly-nearest-noun feature, held-out lexeme/distance split). Do **not** build another flat atomize+prototype-reshape arm (already refuted, and now independently non-viable per the biology). Instead:

1. **Atomize** each training sentence into a small set of LOCAL TYPED RELATIONAL EDGES (e.g. `(candidate_noun_i, IS_STRUCTURAL_HEAD_OF, verb)`, `(candidate_noun_j, IS_LINEAR_ATTRACTOR_OF, verb)`), extracted per-case — this is the atomize step, unchanged in spirit from the existing pipeline, but the atom now stores a typed local relation, not just a flat bound vector.
2. **Replay stage = compositional traversal, not prototype averaging.** Sample chains across many atomized cases to build a generalized local-relation template: e.g. accumulate, per relation-TYPE (not per lexeme), how that type predicts the number-agreement outcome, regardless of linear position — analogous to Kurth-Nelson/Liu's rollout composing locally-learned edges into paths never co-presented.
3. **At test on held-out combinations**, answer by traversing from the verb along the `IS_STRUCTURAL_HEAD_OF` edge-type (wherever it resolves in the novel sentence), not by flat vector similarity to stored training cases.
4. **Content-dependence discriminator (the signature-flip test, same family as atom 29437/fork-B):** scramble the relation-TYPE labels (decouple which edges are labeled `IS_STRUCTURAL_HEAD_OF` from actual syntactic structure) and re-run. This should **severely hurt** the graph-traversal mechanism (it has nothing else to fall back on) — the opposite of 29437's fixed-FHRR signature, where scrambling geometry *helped* or was neutral.

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS** (glass-box relational generalization confirmed, no gradient-trained component needed):
- Held-out structurally-novel combinations (head-attractor distance/count unseen at train) resolve correctly via edge-type traversal at accuracy well above the flat-similarity/positional-heuristic baseline already measured in 29443.
- Scrambling relation-type labels collapses accuracy toward chance (large effect, not noise-level) — confirming the mechanism is content/structure-dependent, not secretly keying off position.
- Every step (edge extraction from a case, edge-type frequency accumulation, traversal path selection) remains individually inspectable — no black-box scoring function anywhere in the loop.

**HARD-FAIL** (the glass-box invariant does not actually hold, or this is not a real escape):
- (a) **Edge-extraction regress:** without an oracle parse providing clean relation-type labels, the "local relation learner" that must infer `IS_STRUCTURAL_HEAD_OF` from raw token co-occurrence itself collapses back into a linear/frequency/positional heuristic (the same wall as 29440/29443, just relocated one layer down into the edge-extraction step) — meaning the traversal step didn't actually buy anything; the real bottleneck (learning *what counts as a structural-head relation* from raw text) still needs something gradient-trained-like to work reliably.
- (b) Scrambling relation-type labels does **not** hurt performance (near-identical accuracy) — meaning the mechanism is secretly still using position/count, not the labeled relation, and the graph-traversal framing is cosmetic.
- (c) The traversal mechanism generalizes only as well as memorized edge-type statistics per lexeme (doesn't transfer to genuinely novel lexeme+distance combinations) — i.e., it's prototype-averaging wearing a graph costume, collapsing back to Branch-1's wall.

**MIDDLE (inconclusive, needs a harder split):** traversal beats flat-similarity baseline but the scrambling ablation shows only a small effect — power issue, not yet a clean signature.

## Cross-thread synthesis

This directly resolves "THE GLASS-BOX CRUX" flagged at the end of `literature_scan_leap_learner_binding_must_be_learned_content_dependent_2026-07-22.md`. That note asked whether glass-box consolidation can match the gradient-trained MLC/contrastive-encoder results found in the machine-learning literature. The biological answer is: **not via the mechanism our own atoms already tried (flat atomize + prototype/geometry reshaping) — that variant is now independently non-viable both from our own refutation (29440) and from the biology (iOtA-style abstraction IS a similarity statistic, and the behavioral "sleep enables abstract rules" literature is contested/non-robust where it would matter).** But there IS a second, distinct, well-evidenced biological mechanism (sequential/generative replay as multi-hop graph traversal) that provably escapes pairwise similarity, remains glass-box, and has NOT been tried in our program. This reframes the leap-learner target: not "a better geometry-reshaping consolidation," but "an edge-typed relational-graph consolidation + compositional-traversal retrieval," which is architecturally different from both the refuted flat-prototype design and the MLC/contrastive gradient-trained design the ML literature offers. It also sharpens (does not resolve) the open fork-B question from the prior drill: MLC-style meta-learning and graph-traversal replay are BOTH candidates for "content-dependent, escapes free-algebra" — they are not mutually exclusive, and the graph-traversal candidate is the one that keeps the glass-box invariant, at the cost of pushing the hard problem down into edge-extraction (see HARD-FAIL (a)).

Consistent with the MEMORY.md refinement discipline (do not assume the brain-check outcome; when the brain fails the same way, the fix is substrate-native, not brain-faithful): here the brain-check is genuinely two-sided — it FAILS the same way as our substrate on the naive/flat mechanism (iOtA, contested transitive-inference literature), which validates treating that path as closed rather than re-litigating it; but it SUCCEEDS via a qualitatively different, still-biological, still-glass-box mechanism (compositional replay/traversal) that we have not yet tried. That is the actionable delta.
