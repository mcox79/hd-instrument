## HEADLINE

Five independent human literatures on reading (strategy-instruction pedagogy, expert "how-to-read-well" craft, reading-science synthesis, early-childhood comprehension pedagogy, and image/grounding theory) **converge strongly on the comprehension loop and coherence-monitoring gate we already built** (this is the single highest-value confirmation — see Convergence Check below), and **independently surface four candidate levers we do NOT yet have**: (1) a document/corpus-level *preview pass* before linear chunk-by-chunk reading (Adler's inspectional/syntopical reading — genuinely new timescale, payoff concentrated in structured/expository text, i.e. deferred but real); (2) *knowledge-dependency-ordered curriculum* for what to read and in what sequence (Hirsch/Core Knowledge + basal-reader skill sequencing — directly actionable for the "read more text" thrust, ties to the already-drilled compounding-threshold finding); (3) a *self-generated question-difficulty ladder* as a training/ingestion signal (dialogic reading's CROWD prompts + QAR taxonomy — a strategy humans need live scaffolding to execute, but a machine has no motivation problem and can just always run it — a genuine "must-be-taught -> can-be-built-in-directly" case); (4) a *feedback-scored compression objective* for the compress step itself (Kintsch's own Summary Street — the theory's author's own instructional application of Construction-Integration). A fifth finding is a **real, literature-identified structural gap, not yet actionable**: dictionary-only grounding is specifically thin for concrete/imageable vocabulary (Harnad's symbol-grounding problem + dual-coding + VLM concreteness results converge), which strengthens but does not yet trigger the optional vision-track case. Finally, the literature contains a live, unresolved **field tension** (Willingham's knowledge-first critique vs. strategy-instruction/Kintsch's Summary Street) directly relevant to our own strategic fork: it argues, with real but contested evidence, that generic taught "strategies" have a low, fast-saturating ceiling while cumulative knowledge/curriculum-ordering compounds — a soft steer toward the more-data/curriculum-ordering fork over further structural-strategy hunting, though our "structural levers exhausted" finding is about extraction, not knowledge growth, so the analogy is partial and flagged as such.

## Deliverable 1c — images/multimodal grounding (USER scope extension, two layers kept separate per instruction)

**(a) Extractable now — pedagogical principle, pixel-free:**
- **Dual-coding theory** (Paivio 1971/86/90): concrete/imageable concepts get a second, independent nonverbal code; abstract concepts rely mainly on the verbal code. The **concreteness effect** (concrete > abstract recall/recognition) is one of the most robust findings in verbal-learning research, though the *mechanism* is contested — rival accounts (Context Availability Theory/Schwanenflugel; Barsalou's grounded/embodied cognition) explain the same effect without requiring a literal "imagery code." Notably, the concreteness effect **persists in congenitally blind subjects** — evidence against a strictly visual-sensory account of the "second code."
- **Harnad's symbol grounding problem** (1990): dictionary-only grounding (defining words via other words) is, in Harnad's own framing, **not a solution** to the grounding problem — it is the diagnosed failure mode ("dictionary-go-round") unless the base vocabulary bottoms out in *some* non-symbolic representation. Critically, Harnad's fix does not require that channel be *vision specifically* — any non-symbolic sensory/categorical transduction qualifies.
- **Actionable principle (buildable now, no pixels needed):** concrete and abstract vocabulary plausibly need **structurally different grounding treatments** — this is a real, literature-identified bifurcation, independent of modality. It licenses auditing the current dictionary-grounded lexicon and flagging concrete/imageable items as "grounded but structurally thin" (dictionary-only, no independent non-symbolic anchor), distinct from abstract/relational items where dictionary/co-occurrence grounding is plausibly adequate on the literature's own account.
- **Picture-walks / picture-book pedagogy**: previewing illustrations before the linear text-read to activate vocabulary/schema is a real, if less rigorously tested (mostly practitioner-literature-backed), instance of "build a referent-level anchor before linear verbal processing" — the same **timing** principle as Bransford & Johnson's schema-before-encoding finding, and the same **sequencing** logic as Adler's inspectional-reading skeleton-first strategy (candidate lever #1). This is a genuine convergence point across three unrelated literatures (schema theory, picture-book pedagogy, expert-reading craft) on "orient before you process linearly," strengthening confidence in lever #1 as a real principle, not just an artifact of Adler's prescriptive framing.

**(b) Future-input only, requires actual vision channel — NOT actionable now:**
- Recent vision-language-model literature genuinely converges that visual grounding **disproportionately helps concrete/imageable vocabulary** with little-to-contested impact on abstract/relational vocabulary (multiple 2024-2026 papers cited by the sub-agent scan, including at least one paper titled to signal a contested/negative concreteness-judgment result — flag this literature as live and unsettled, not a clean win). This is the closest empirical analog to "would image-grounding add signal above dictionary-only grounding for concrete vocab" — **and it can only be demonstrated with real pixel-trained models.** It does NOT establish that our system should ingest images now. It DOES strengthen the case that IF the optional, not-yet-built vision track is ever turned on, its highest-value target is the concrete/imageable subset of the lexicon specifically — a scoped, falsifiable future milestone, not a present lever.
- **Honest boundary:** our grounding is currently dictionary-based (functioning as a teacher/definition stand-in, not a picture stand-in). Whether that dictionary channel is already "good enough" for concrete nouns, or whether it has the specific Harnad-diagnosed gap, is an **open, testable question** the moment we have both a labeled concrete/abstract vocabulary split and a held-out grounding-quality metric — this is flagged as a candidate CHEAP TEST below, entirely independent of vision.

## Convergence Check (Deliverable 2 — the highest-value output)

**Does human reading pedagogy independently arrive at our compress-and-carry loop + coherence-gate?** Yes, on the loop's two best-established components, and the convergence is a genuine cross-validation (five independently-developed literatures — clinical/developmental psychology's error-detection paradigm, K-12 strategy-instruction pedagogy, expert-craft advice, early-childhood dialogic-reading research, and cognitive-science-of-reading synthesis — did not copy each other's frameworks but landed on compatible structures):

- **Compress step <-> "summarize"**: reciprocal teaching's summarize step and Kintsch's own Summary Street both frame gist-compression as the trainable, measurable comprehension operation — matches our compress step almost exactly, including Kintsch's OWN endorsement (he is literally the author of the theory we already credited).
- **Coherence-gate <-> "clarify" / monitoring**: this is the **strongest convergence in the whole scan**. Markman's 50-year error-detection paradigm shows human readers — especially children and poor comprehenders — routinely FAIL TO NOTICE planted contradictions even when explicitly told to check. This is not a minor footnote: it means **monitoring does not emerge reliably from mere reading experience**, it has to be explicitly present as a check. Combined with the N400/P600 neuroscience already drilled (`research_coherence_schema_fit_gate_brain_drill_2026-07-19.md`), this is now a **triple-cross-validated** design choice (neuroscience + clinical error-detection paradigm + classroom strategy-instruction pedagogy all independently say: build the gate, don't wait for it to emerge). This is the single most confidence-boosting finding of this scan for a design decision already made.
- **Predictive-coding <-> "predict"**: reciprocal teaching's predict step (use current gist to anticipate the next segment) matches our predictive-coding step; convergence is present but thinner — the pedagogy literature treats predicting as a taught habit for engagement/purpose-setting, not explicitly as a construction-integration mechanism the way our loop does. Partial convergence, not independent re-derivation of the mechanism.
- **Query-the-map <-> "question-generate" / QAR**: this is where the literature **diverges usefully**. Reciprocal teaching's question-generating step and Raphael's QAR taxonomy both name an operation — actively posing and answering questions against the text — that our loop does **not** currently have as a first-class step. This is a genuine missing-lever signal, not just a relabeling of something we have (see candidate lever #3).
- **Schema-activation timing <-> carry-forward**: Bransford & Johnson's finding that schema must be given BEFORE encoding (not after) and Anderson & Pichert's finding that encoding and retrieval-schema should be decoupled (the same encoded material supports different fact-recovery under different schemas) both **harden** rather than merely restate our carry-forward design: they argue for (i) always making prior gist/context available before processing the next chunk (already how carry-forward works) and (ii) keeping the stored representation richer than any single query's use of it, so a later different "schema"/question can recover different facts — this is a genuine independent argument FOR the structure-content factorization already banked (atoms 29332-29335: content-blind structural code + native bind), since that factorization is precisely what keeps encoding decoupled from any one downstream use.
- **Adler's inspectional/syntopical reading <-> nothing in our current loop**: this is the cleanest **divergence** — Adler names a document/corpus-level pre-pass (build the whole-thing skeleton before any linear processing) that operates at a different TIMESCALE than within-passage comprehension, and it is *not* redundant with the coherence-gate or compress step. It is corroborated independently by scientist paper-reading order (abstract->conclusion->figures before body) and Wineburg's historian sourcing-before-reading practice — three unrelated expert-reading traditions converging on the same "orient first" principle. **This is the strongest candidate for a genuinely missing lever**, precisely because nothing in our already-built machinery does it.

**Brain-check discipline applied (per USER 07-15 mandate, do not pre-assume outcome):** for each divergence, ask whether the brain does the operation spontaneously or whether it must be taught. Result: monitoring/error-detection is a case where the brain **fails to do it spontaneously** (Markman) — this is the "brain fails the same way, fix is native/built-in" pattern already flagged in memory (`feedback_dont_assume_brain_check_outcome...2026-07-17`), and it is exactly why we already built a gate rather than hoping for emergent monitoring — good retrospective validation of that call. Question-generation (dialogic reading's CROWD ladder, reciprocal teaching's question step) is a **second instance of the same pattern**: children need live adult scaffolding (PEER/CROWD) to reliably generate good questions against text — it is explicitly a TAUGHT behavior, not spontaneous. For a machine with no motivation problem, this converts cleanly into a **candidate substrate-native affordance**: always run a self-generated question-difficulty ladder during ingestion, with no fade-out risk (Rosenshine & Meister's finding that reciprocal-teaching's classroom benefit decays to near-null (d=+0.09) at scale is a HUMAN-specific problem — motivation/fidelity decay across many classrooms/teachers — that plausibly does not apply to a system that simply always executes the procedure). This is the single cleanest "must-be-taught -> can-be-built-in-directly" case in the whole scan.

## Deliverable 3 — direct implications for the two active thrusts

**(a) Cross-document compounding / consolidation-correctness:**
- Hirsch's Core Knowledge argument and Wineburg's corroboration-before-accepting-new-source practice both **independently converge on the already-drilled Kendeou knowledge-revision requirement** (`research_cross_document_compounding_consolidation_viability_2026-07-19.md`): new information must be checked against/integrated with an existing, presumed-correct model, not merely accumulated. This is a third independent literature (historians' craft practice) landing on the same gate-before-merge structural requirement as clinical knowledge-revision psychology — reinforces, does not introduce new risk to, the already-identified three-way gate (correctness / decorrelation / threshold).
- Willingham's knowledge-first critique of strategy-instruction is a genuine, if contested, argument that **cumulative knowledge compounds while taught strategies fast-saturate** (his cited claim: ~10 sessions to ceiling, no further gain from 50). If even partially true for a computational system (the caveat is his claim is about human motivation/habituation-based saturation, which may not transfer directly — flagged honestly, not asserted), it is a soft steer: given the memory's own finding that "clean structural levers [are] EXHAUSTED" on the reading axis, the literature's independent claim that knowledge/curriculum growth has a longer runway than strategy-hunting is a real, if imperfect, analogy supporting the compounding/more-data fork over continued deep structural-lever hunting — NOT a strong claim, a soft directional one.

**(b) What/how/in-what-order to read to grow (the "read more text" thrust):**
- Hirsch/Core Knowledge sequencing + basal-reader multi-factor complexity gradients (Fountas & Pinnell) directly answer "what order": sequence exposure by **knowledge-dependency and structural complexity together** (not token-length/vocabulary-frequency alone), so that earlier texts build the background the later texts' comprehension leans on. This is directly actionable NOW as a corpus-selection/ordering policy for the more-data thrust, independent of any new model component.
- Dickinson & Tabors' decontextualized-talk finding argues for prioritizing **narrative/non-present-referent text** over here-and-now description in the corpus, since that is what differentially predicts later comprehension in the human developmental literature — consistent with (does not contradict) the already-established narrative-first, defer-expository finding.
- Wolf's slow-print-before-fast-skim sequencing argument is a weak additional cross-validation of the same narrative-first-then-expository ordering already adopted, from a different (neuro-cultural) angle.

## Deliverable 4 — honest filter: what will NOT transfer

Explicitly flagged, not laundered into false levers:
- **Reciprocal teaching's dialogic turn-taking / peer roles** — a social delivery format, not an information-processing operation.
- **Gradual Release of Responsibility's live teacher-modeling-then-receding-support** — requires a live human sensing a live learner's demonstrated competence in real time; the "competence-gated curriculum" reframe (reduce explicit scaffolding once held-out checks pass) is a real but much weaker analog, not the same mechanism.
- **Pressley's "motivation to use strategies independently"** — the load-bearing durability factor in his account has literally no computational analog, because a machine has no motivation problem; reframed positively above (no fade-out risk once built in).
- **Dialogic reading's live Evaluate/Expand/Recast of a child's own novel utterance** — requires an actual utterance from a live learner to correct; no analog absent a self-generated-answer-scoring loop, which is a stretch, not a direct mapping.
- **Read-aloud caregiver attachment / joint attention / social praise** — purely social-affective, explicitly out of scope (system has no motivation/affect per task framing).
- **KWL's K/W self-elicitation steps** — self-assessment/motivation scaffolding for a human learner, no computational counterpart.
- **Wolf's biliteracy print-vs-digital code-switching curriculum** — protects a slow-reading circuit from *competing* skim-mode interference; the system has no competing skim mode to protect against, so the specific mechanism doesn't transfer (the general narrative-first sequencing conclusion does, separately, per 3b above).
- **Actual pixel ingestion** — explicitly future-only per the images angle above; nothing in the current scan licenses ingesting images now.
- **QAR and think-alouds as literally taught human techniques** — their *taxonomic content* transfers (query-routing categories; interpretability/tracing value) but not "teaching a technique to a learner," since there is no learner being taught, only a system being designed.

## Candidate levers, ranked (deflated; novel-synthesis cap P<=0.50 applies)

1. **Coherence-gate as substrate-native affordance (retrospective validation, not new)** — P(design choice correct) ~0.70 deflated from a would-be-higher literature-convergence read; already built, this scan raises confidence it was the right call, does not itself need testing.
2. **Curriculum ordering by knowledge-dependency (Hirsch + basal-reader gradient) for corpus growth** — P(this meaningfully improves later comprehension/compounding if applied to our corpus-selection policy) ~0.40, deflated for cross-domain translation uncertainty (human classroom curricula vs. machine exposure ordering); actionable now, cheap to pilot (a corpus-ordering policy change, not a new model component).
3. **Self-generated question-difficulty ladder as an ingestion-time training/monitoring signal** (dialogic reading's CROWD ladder + reciprocal teaching's question-generation + QAR taxonomy) — P(this adds real signal beyond what compress+coherence-gate already extract) ~0.35, genuinely novel, cap applies; the strongest "must-be-taught -> build-in-directly" candidate but entirely unbuilt and untested.
4. **Document/corpus-level preview pass (Adler inspectional/syntopical reading)** — P(would help, if built, on CURRENT narrative corpus) ~0.25 (narrative children's text lacks the paratextual structure — headings/TOC/abstracts — inspectional reading depends on); P(would help on expository/textbook text, when we get there) ~0.45. Payoff is real but timescale-deferred, consistent with the already-established expository-is-the-frontier finding — do not build now, but do not discard either.
5. **Feedback-scored compression objective (Summary Street pattern) for the compress step** — P(this improves compress-step quality if implemented as a learning signal) ~0.40; concrete, theory-author-endorsed, technically the cheapest of the four unbuilt levers to prototype (needs a coverage/relevance/redundancy scorer against source content, which our existing structure-content vectors could plausibly serve as a substitute for Summary Street's original LSA scorer).
6. **Word-sense-consistency-within-document tracking ("coming to terms")** — P(meaningfully extends current coref beyond referring expressions to polysemous content words) ~0.30; smallest, most incremental of the six.
7. **Concrete-vocabulary dictionary-grounding gap** — P(this is a real, identifiable limitation of current grounding for a specific vocabulary subset) ~0.55 (literature convergence is genuinely strong here — Harnad + dual-coding + VLM-concreteness results independently agree), but P(fixable without a vision channel) ~0.20 — mostly a future-milestone strengthener, not an action item; the one PRESENT-day action is auditing/flagging concrete-vs-abstract lexicon items with a grounding-quality metric (see Cheap Decisive Test below), independent of vision.

## Cheap decisive test

Two independent, cheap, non-experiment-design-committing checks that would sharpen the ranked list above before any build decision:

1. **Preview-pass / lookahead micro-test (lever #4, narrative-scope check).** On the existing ~163-sentence grade-2/3 narrative eval set, check whether a trivial lookahead (peeking at the last sentence of the current paragraph/scene before processing the first sentence) changes coreference/quotative-attribution precision at all, using the machinery already in place. **HARD-PASS:** >=0.02 absolute precision gain with zero recall cost (replicating the same zero-recall-cost pattern as the two landed CGs) -> the preview-pass principle has near-term payoff even without paratextual structure, worth prioritizing sooner than expository text. **HARD-FAIL (expected, and still informative):** no signal / no exploitable structure in flat narrative prose -> confirms the lever's payoff is genuinely concentrated in structured/expository text as literature predicts, defer without abandoning.
2. **Concrete-vs-abstract grounding-quality audit (lever #7, present-day, vision-independent).** Split the existing dictionary-grounded lexicon into concrete/imageable vs. abstract/relational using an off-the-shelf concreteness norm list (e.g. Brysbaert et al. concreteness ratings — a public, generic academic resource, not a system-specific query), then compare any existing held-out grounding-quality or downstream-task metric across the two splits. **HARD-PASS (gap exists):** concrete items show measurably worse grounding-quality/downstream performance than abstract items of matched frequency -> confirms the literature's predicted gap is present in OUR system specifically, strengthens (without triggering) the future vision-track case, and flags exactly which vocabulary subset to prioritize if/when vision is turned on. **HARD-FAIL (no gap):** dictionary grounding is already adequate for concrete vocabulary in our system -> the Harnad-style concern doesn't apply here, vision-track motivation weakens to "general capability", not "a compensating fix for a known grounding hole."

Both tests are analysis-only (no new cells, no new model components) and can be run against data/metrics already on disk plus one public generic norm-list download — genuinely cheap.
