**The substrate is a trust-layer knowledge engine for trust-critical AI applications.**

- **Bottom: HDC math (hyperdimensional / FHRR computing)** -- well-established 2024-2025 research area (HDReason, WSDM-2025 link prediction, ConformalHDC, frontiers HDC encoder work). Not the differentiator.

- **Answer** -- composed from HDC (LLM-like compositional fluidity)
- **Provenance** -- cite the cert-grade record-IDs backing it (KG-like structure)
- **Honest bound** -- explicit "we can answer this for X; not Y" (neither LLMs nor vector DBs give this)
