**Filed by:** research (Opus synthesis over 3 parallel Sonnet lit-scans)

## HEADLINE

A ready-made real corpus that instantiates all four arena ingredients simultaneously **already exists and is downloadable today**: the Weather dataset from Dong, Berti-Équille, Hu & Srivastava (VLDB 2010, "Global Detection of Complex Copying Relationships Between Sources") — 18 real forecast websites, conflicting predictions, actual-outcome ground truth, AND (uniquely among the corpora surveyed) a hand-built gold/silver standard for which sources copy which other sources. Below that tier, three complementary real-data classes fill in what the synthetic arena still has to invent by hand: (1) KBs with documented — sometimes literal in-data — provenance/lineage (UMLS's per-atom source tag, VIAF's per-cluster source-record retention, Hetionet's provenance table) for the schema/structural-overlap side; (2) a genuine three-tier reliability contrast in the wild, with the LLM-generation tier now independently and quantitatively confirmed to be the correlated/non-independent "copying" case the arena is designed to test (Kish effective-sample-size ≈2.2 out of 9 "independent" LLM judges; TruthfulQA's shared-misconception structure); (3) the full classic truth-discovery benchmark hub (Book/Stock/Flight/Weather/Restaurant/Population, all still live at lunadong.com) plus Snorkel/WRENCH's explicitly-modeled source-correlation weak-supervision framework, which is the field's own purpose-built answer to exactly this arena's requirements.

## Ranked shortlist

### Tier A — purpose-built truth-discovery benchmarks (closest structural match, cheapest to pilot)

**A1. Weather dataset (Dong/Berti-Équille/Hu/Srivastava, VLDB 2010) — TOP PICK**
- Provenance/reliability/independence signal available: 18 real forecast-website sources, sampled every 45 min over one day (March 2010) for 30 US cities; per-source reliability is estimable from disagreement-vs-outcome; **a hand-built gold+silver standard for the copying/dependence graph itself** — this is the one corpus in the entire survey where "which sources copy which" is a labeled artifact, not something the arena has to synthesize or the gate has to infer blind.
- Maps to arena ingredients: reliability (yes, derivable) / hidden dependence graph (yes, LABELED — a genuine drop-in for the arena's "hidden copying structure that must be inferred") / conflicting claims (yes, real forecast disagreement) / ground truth (yes, actual weather outcome).
- Ground truth: actual observed weather per city/day.
- Licensing/access: free direct download (`weather.zip` at lunadong.com/fusionDataSets.htm), academic-research use, no login/contact required.
- Gap vs the arena spec: no temporal-arrival/autocorrelated-topic stream beyond the single-day 45-min sampling (the arena's Part 5 spec wants a longer discrete-time arrival schedule with topical persistence) — usable as the source-population + dependence-graph substitute, not as a full temporal-forward-model substrate on its own.

**A2. Book / Book-Author dataset (Yin/Han/Yu 2008 TruthFinder; re-released Dong/Berti-Équille/Srivastava 2009-2010)**
- ~880-895 online booksellers, conflicting author-attribution claims, gold (100 books) + silver (500+ books) ground truth, largest and most battle-tested source count of any classic benchmark.
- Maps to arena ingredients: reliability (derivable per-seller) / dependence graph (NOT labeled — only inferable, same limitation the arena gate itself has, making this a fair "blind" test rather than a labeled one) / conflicting claims (yes, real) / ground truth (yes, gold+silver).
- Licensing/access: free (`book.zip` + `book_golden.txt`/`book_silver.txt`, also mirrored on 2+ GitHub repos, e.g. `nawshad/Truth-Discovery-Algorithms`, `totucuong/spectrum`).
- Best used as the "no cheat sheet" companion to A1: pilot the gate on Weather (where copying is labeled, for calibration/debugging) then re-run blind on Book (no copying labels, the real test of whether the gate's inferred-dependence signal works without help).

**A3. Restaurant dataset (Dong/Berti-Équille/Srivastava, VLDB 2009 — the seminal copying-detection paper)**
- 12 sources, weekly tracking of Manhattan restaurant listings, ground truth established by manually phoning 467 restaurants to verify closure status. Smaller and older than A1/A2 but is literally the paper that introduced copying detection as a method — useful as the "canonical reference implementation" baseline to compare the gate against.
- Licensing/access: free (`restaurants.zip`, `restaurants_golden.txt`).

**A4. Stock / Flight / Population datasets (same VLDB 2013 / SIGMOD 2014 lineage)**
- Stock: 55 financial sites, 1,000 tickers, NASDAQ-official ground truth. Flight: 38 tracking sites, 1,200+ flights, airline-official ground truth. Population: 4,264 sources, ~50K claims, statistical-agency ground truth (best "many low-quality outlier sources" stress test).
- None of these three have a labeled copying graph (same inferred-only limitation as Book) but all are free, downloadable now from the same lunadong.com hub, and give scale/domain diversity if a multi-domain pilot is wanted later.

**A5. Snorkel / WRENCH weak-supervision benchmarks**
- Reframes "sources" as labeling functions with learned accuracy AND explicitly modeled pairwise correlation (Snorkel's generative label model is functionally a live-inferred dependence graph, not a fixed dataset) — closer to a candidate *method* to borrow than a corpus to ingest. WRENCH (NeurIPS 2021 D&B track) packages 22 real weak-supervision tasks with source-correlation structure baked in and is actively maintained (`github.com/JieyuZ2/wrench`), free and open-source.
- DAFNA's synthetic-dataset generator (10 tunable params incl. presumably dependence structure) is a middle path: not a real corpus, but a validated generator built by the truth-discovery field itself for exactly this purpose — worth comparing against the arena's own synthetic generator design before assuming ours needs to be invented from scratch.

### Tier B — documented-lineage KBs (best for the schema/structural-overlap side, not the copying/dependence side)

**B1. UMLS Metathesaurus — strongest literal in-data provenance**
- 200+ source vocabularies (SNOMED CT, MeSH, RxNorm, ICD, NCI Thesaurus, etc.); every atom carries an explicit `SAB` (source-abbreviation) field — provenance is a literal queryable data column, not just documented in a paper. Structural-inconsistency papers exist with counts (81,512 concepts with inconsistent semantic-group assignment; documented cycles from conflicting source hierarchies).
- Maps to arena ingredients: this is a schema-fit / structural-overlap instantiation (Part 5 ingredient 2: "pre-existing consolidated schema"), not a source-population-with-hidden-dependence instantiation — UMLS documents WHICH vocabulary asserted a fact but not whether vocabularies copy from each other.
- Licensing/access: free via UMLS Terminology Services (UTS) account/license; some constituent vocabularies (SNOMED CT, CPT) carry usage restrictions even within a UTS license. Large (multi-GB), biannual releases, actively maintained.

**B2. VIAF (Virtual International Authority File) — best real-world "corroboration across sources" analog**
- ~25M source records from ~37-50+ national library agencies, merged into ~20M per-entity clusters; each cluster literally retains and links the original per-library records, so "which library asserted which name-form" is inspectable. A documented linkage study (LC/DNB matching) reports ~70% auto-link rate with <1% error — a rare case with an actual measured accuracy figure for the cross-source merge process itself.
- Maps to arena ingredients: reliability (partially, via per-agency linkage accuracy) / dependence graph (NOT labeled as copying, but the merge structure is a genuine multi-source-corroboration structure) / conflicting claims (yes — differing birth/death precision, name variants, occasional nationality disagreement across libraries) / ground truth (no formal benchmark subset found for this).
- Licensing/access: free full dumps (viaf.org), ODC-BY (attribution required, not CC0).

**B3. Hetionet / PharMeBINet — provenance-as-data-property**
- 29 (Hetionet) / more (PharMeBINet) integrated bioinformatics databases; a published table (eLife 2017 Table 4) ties every edge/node type to its source resource plus license metadata carried as literal graph properties. Documented shared-upstream overlap (DrugBank/ChEMBL/DrugCentral/BindingDB all feeding the same compound-binding edge type) confirms real redundancy among nominally-independent sources — a genuine, if modest, real-world "hidden copying" case.
- Licensing/access: free (GitHub `hetio/hetionet`), static 2017 snapshot; PharMeBINet (2022) is the maintained successor.

**B4. Wikidata/Freebase/DBpedia lineage** — most narrative documentation (a full WWW 2016 conference paper on the Freebase migration) but the weakest literal in-data provenance signal surviving to today's live Wikidata items (approved Freebase-derived statements got normal Wikidata references, not a "source=Freebase" tag) — useful for understanding HOW large-scale KB-to-KB migration/curation gates work, less useful as a ready-to-ingest dataset with per-claim source labels.

### Tier C — reliability-tier contrast corpora (validates the arena's 3-tier provenance framing empirically)

**C1. Instrument-measured baseline.** CODATA fundamental-constants adjustments publish the raw per-lab inputs (value + uncertainty + lab + year) behind each consensus constant — a ready-made "N independent instruments measuring the same fact" table, including documented real disagreement (proton-radius ~7σ discrepancy between muonic and electronic measurements, later resolved). Gaia/SDSS/DES cross-match catalogs give a continuous distribution of independent-instrument disagreement (photometric offsets, ~0.1% positional mismatch rate) rather than one anecdote. Near-independence is real but NOT perfect — even GenBank shows a documented case of one misidentified/recombinant genome propagating through multiple "independent" depositions (a copying-risk case embedded even in the instrument tier).

**C2. Expert-curated tier.** MeSH inter-indexer consistency studies give a direct, decades-deep quantified base rate for "how independent are two expert curators really" (historical range 10-60% raw agreement; modern kappa/alpha studies in the 0.3-0.6 range) — this is the single most citable tier-2 calibration number found. The ICD-9-to-ICD-10 coding transition is a real, dated, systemwide example of a guideline change producing a correlated shift across independently-operating hospital coders nationwide (47-70% of examined code clusters showed abrupt correlated shifts at the transition point) — a genuine "shared upstream bias" instantiation using public administrative claims data.

**C3. LLM-generated tier — the copying-risk case, now independently quantified.** This is the strongest and most directly relevant finding of the whole drill. "Nine Judges, Two Effective Votes" (arXiv 2605.29800) measures 9 frontier LLMs across 7 families on ChaosNLI (which conveniently ALSO carries 100 independent human annotations per item as a genuine-independence baseline sitting right next to the LLM outputs) and finds an effective sample size (Kish n_eff) of only ~2.2 out of 9 nominal "independent" judges (95% CI 2.07-2.31, i.e. 24.2% of nominal), mean pairwise error correlation phi-bar = 0.39, validated against a Condorcet null model (p<10^-4). "Correlated Errors in Large Language Models" (arXiv 2506.07962) independently confirms error correlation INCREASES with model capability and shared lineage — the opposite of what independence would require. "Shared Imagination" (arXiv 2407.16604) shows 13 LLMs converge on the SAME fabricated answer to impossible questions 54-96% of the time depending on condition — LLMs literally hallucinate alike. TruthfulQA (arXiv 2109.07958) is a mature, public, purpose-built dataset where wrong answers are specifically the shared-misconception type (not random noise), with documented inverse scaling (bigger same-family models are LESS truthful because they more faithfully reproduce shared training-corpus misconceptions). Together these four are a ready, citable, quantitative confirmation that "LLM-generated" is exactly the correlated/non-independent tier the arena's design assumed it would be — not a hypothesis needing further lit support.

## What each corpus concretely fills in vs. what still has to be built

| Arena ingredient | Best real-data source found | What's still missing / has to stay synthetic |
|---|---|---|
| Per-source reliability | Stock/Flight/Book (source-vs-gold-standard accuracy is computable) | none — this is fully covered |
| Hidden source-dependence/copying graph | **Weather (A1) — the only one with a labeled gold+silver copying standard** | Book/Stock/Flight/Restaurant have real copying but it's uninstrumented (must be inferred, same as the gate would have to do) |
| Conflicting claims | All of Tier A, natively | none — fully covered |
| Ground truth | All of Tier A, natively (gold/silver standards, official sources) | none — fully covered |

| Temporal arrival stream w/ autocorrelation | Weather's 45-min sampling is the closest (one day only) | full multi-day/week autocorrelated arrival stream — no real corpus surveyed has this at the granularity the arena spec wants; likely stays synthetic or gets built by resampling/interleaving a real corpus's timestamps |

## Falsifiable predictions (HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL)

Framed around the actual next decision this drill informs: should we pilot a real corpus as a drop-in for one arena source-tier, and which one?

**HARD-PASS (real-corpus pilot is worth building):**
- On the Weather dataset (A1), a naive corroboration-count/degree proxy computed on the labeled-copying claims disagrees with the gold copying-graph-corrected reliability estimate in >=30% of source pairs (i.e., the same "degree != independence" failure mode the synthetic arena predicted is empirically reproducible on REAL data, not just a designed-in synthetic artifact).
- Pairwise correlation among {reliability, schema-fit-analog, corroboration} computed on a Book+UMLS pairing (real claims + real schema) lands in a similar regime to the synthetic arena's target (<0.3 achievable, or at minimum not collapsed at >0.9), showing the independence property the arena was designed to create is also present in real assembled data, not an artifact of the synthetic generator's design choices.
- The C3 LLM-correlation numbers (Kish n_eff ~2.2/9, phi-bar 0.39) replicate in direction (not necessarily magnitude) when substituting the arena's own gate's corroboration signal for the Kish-style estimator on a small held-out slice of LLM-generated claims — confirms the arena's copying-detection logic isn't just fitting a synthetic-generator artifact.

**HARD-FAIL (real corpora don't de-risk the synthetic path — stay synthetic-only for now):**
- The Weather dataset's real copying graph turns out too sparse/small (18 sources, single day) to give a statistically meaningful stress-test comparison (e.g., <10 genuinely-labeled independent-vs-copied claim pairs after filtering) — insufficient N to validate against.
- Pairwise correlation among the three signals on any real-corpus pairing exceeds 0.7 regardless of which schema/claim-stream combination is tried — i.e., real assembled data reproduces the SAME collapse the original single-KG arena had, meaning the independence property is a genuinely hard-won synthetic-design achievement that real off-the-shelf data doesn't hand you for free (this would argue for keeping the arena synthetic and treating real corpora only as later validation, not as a replacement source-tier).
- Licensing/access friction on the most-needed corpus (UMLS, if the schema-side pairing is attempted) blocks reproducible automated pulls (UTS login/account gating breaks a fully-scripted pipeline) — downgrades UMLS from "pilot now" to "pilot later with manual setup."

## Cheap decisive test

Before any arena-build cell or real-data ingest cell is authored: download the Weather dataset (`weather.zip`, free, ~one HTTP GET, no cell/GPU/CPU queue involved) and, in a throwaway script (not a substrate cell), directly recompute the three arena proxy signals — reliability (source accuracy vs. actual-weather ground truth), corroboration (naive count vs. copying-graph-corrected count using the released gold/silver copying labels), and a placeholder schema-fit (skip, or substitute a trivial city-climate-normal baseline as the "prior structure") — on the real claims. Compare the resulting pairwise correlation and copying-stress-test gap directly against the synthetic arena's toy-simulation numbers from the same-day design note. This is cheap (no infra, no GPU, a few hours of script work) and decisive: it tells us in one pass whether real assembled multi-source data actually reproduces the independence property the synthetic arena was carefully engineered to have, which is the load-bearing open question for the whole synthetic-to-real transition.

## Substrate-product implications

- The single cheapest, most consequential move available right now is NOT building a new synthetic-arena cell — it's a few hours of throwaway script work pulling the Weather dataset and re-running the arena's own validity precondition on real data, which either confirms the synthetic design generalizes (strong validation, cheap) or reveals the independence property was synthetic-generator-specific (equally valuable, cheap negative result, redirects effort toward better synthetic engineering rather than premature real-data ingestion).
- If the Weather pilot passes, the natural roadmap is: Weather (labeled copying, calibration) -> Book (unlabeled copying, blind test) -> a UMLS/Hetionet-paired schema for the structural-overlap signal -> only THEN consider building genuinely novel real-data ingestion tooling, in that order of cheapness and de-risking value.
- The LLM-generated-tier confirmation (Kish n_eff ~2.2/9, TruthfulQA shared-misconception structure) is independently useful beyond this arena: it's direct evidence against ever treating multiple LLM-generated "sources" as if they were independent corroborating evidence anywhere else in the pipeline (e.g., any future move to LLM-generate synthetic conjunction-module content, per the 07-15 foundation-sourcing pivot, should NOT count multiple generations from the same or related models as independent corroboration of a claim's truth).
- None of the surveyed real corpora supply the arena's temporal-autocorrelated-arrival-stream ingredient at the scale the design wants — this piece will likely stay synthetic (or be built by resampling a real corpus's timestamps into a denser synthetic schedule) even if the other three ingredients migrate to real data.
