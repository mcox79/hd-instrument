## HEADLINE / VERDICT

**NO GENUINE EDGE.** Glass-box VSA/HD reasoning (native bind + fixed codebook + no gradient-trained embeddings at runtime) does NOT have a demonstrated edge on real incomplete-KG link prediction or conjunctive-query answering, in either the external published literature or — more decisively — this project's OWN already-landed in-house experiments, which ran exactly this comparison on real data (CSKG, FB15k-237) and got a consistent, converging answer: **this is neural-embedding turf.** Not "only for conjunctive queries" either — that sub-case turns out to be the same generic-math trap as tonight's two negatives (answer-set shrinkage, not learned structure). Gap-filling (novel relational inference from statistical regularities) IS a genuinely learned, experience-dependent brain capability — but the brain's own mechanism for it is neocortical slow interleaved statistical-regularity extraction, i.e. the biological analog of training an embedding, not glass-box symbol algebra on a fixed random codebook. **Verdict: PIVOT to path (B), extraction/grounding.** Path (A) is closed as a chain-grade target; the reading/comprehension arc this session already re-converged on is the only genuine one.

## 1. The load-bearing strategic question, answered directly

### (a) Has anyone shown glass-box VSA competitive with neural KGE on FB15k-237-class benchmarks?

Only marginally, and only on the easiest sub-task. External scan found one 2026 paper (arXiv 2606.24948, "Holographic Memory for Zero-Shot Compositional Reasoning in Knowledge Graphs") testing real-valued HRR and phase-only FHRR + modern-Hopfield cleanup on FB15k-237: **filtered MRR 0.358/0.350 for single-hop link prediction — genuine parity with RotatE (0.337-0.338)**, above TransE (0.294) and ComplEx (~0.247), though below NBFNet (0.415, a trained GNN). This is the single strongest external data point in the substrate's favor. But the SAME paper's central finding is negative: **zero-shot two-hop composition collapses to chance across all cleanup temperatures**, mechanistically localized to capacity/interference in the superposed memory — not a bind/unbind-algebra failure, a storage-capacity failure. Every OTHER competitive VSA-adjacent KGE result found (HDReason, HolE) trains its vectors by gradient descent, disqualifying it as "glass-box" in the substrate's sense.

**This project's own in-house history says the same thing, more starkly.** A multi-week arc (`research_to_skunkworks_PREREG_KG_fb15k237_batched_TIER2...2026-06-20.md`, `research_composed_reasoning_kg_architectures_2026-06-18.md`, the ANCHOR_COMPOSE / crux-engine / native-KGStore threads through 2026-07-14) already built and ran the exact head-to-head comparison on real data:
- **Pure native VSA store** (fixed-atom high-dim bipolar codebook, multiplicative Hadamard bind, one-shot Hebbian outer-product write, bilinear readout — the actual glass-box-no-gradient-training regime): measured native oracle ceiling **MRR 0.0231** on real CSKG (`native_representational_ceiling_levers`, 2026-07-13), diagnosed as write-rule-limited (~0.14N Hopfield-class capacity), not dimension-limited.
- **A separate pure-VSA attempt directly on FB15k-237** (`exp_crux_engine_fb15k237_vsa_gt_v1`, FHRR bind/unbind + resonator decode, GPU FULL, 2026-07-10): went through multiple HARD_FAIL iterations (decode-side fix falsified, candidate-recall ceiling ~0.207 single-shot at best, resonator capacity walls) — never reached a stable competitive result.
- **SGD-trained low-dim additive-geometry map** (ANCHOR_COMPOSE, k=24 additive TransE-style, entity composition training-free/inductive but relation operators SGD-fit): **held-out MRR 0.1282, Hits@10 0.278** on CSKG (N=25752, 12-core) — 5.5x the native-VSA ceiling, and calibrated at ~60-70% of trained inductive-KGE literature (InductivE MRR ~0.18-0.21).
- **Symbolic rule-induction (AnyBURL-style, glass-box in a DIFFERENT sense — no vectors at all, explicit logical rules)** directly on real FB15k-237 (`exp_gt_induction_fb15k237_dense_v1`, 2026-07-10): **MRR 0.212, Hits@10 0.288, Hits@1 0.171** — roughly on par with untuned TransE, below RotatE/ComplEx, but a genuinely competitive non-embedding result.

The pattern across BOTH literature and this project's own experiments is identical: **whenever the substrate used pure glass-box VSA superposition/bind with no gradient training, it measurably underperformed by 5-9x. Whenever it used either gradient training (even a small additive map) or symbolic rule-mining (a different non-VSA glass-box family), it reached 60-85% of neural-KGE-literature performance.** This is not a hypothesis to test — it is an already-measured result, confirmed twice independently (in-house and external lit), and should not be re-litigated by building a third version of the same test.

### (b) Is there a conjunctive-query type where structured (box/beta-style) VSA reasoning genuinely beats single-vector embeddings AND the transparency is a real value-add, not just parity?

Structured representations DO show a genuine, mechanism-level (not just parameter-count) advantage over point vectors for intersection: Query2Box beats point-vector GQE by ~24-25% relative Hits@3 on FB15k/FB15k-237/NELL995, and ablations show the ATTENTION-BASED ("soft") intersection specifically drives the gain. BetaE's per-query-type MRR on FB15k-237 (1p=39.0, 2p=10.9, 3p=10.0, 2i=28.8, **3i=42.5**, pi=22.4, ip=12.6) shows something important that inverts the framing in tonight's prompt: **accuracy does NOT degrade as more constraints are conjoined — 3i beats 2i** — because each added constraint shrinks the valid answer set, making ranking easier even though the representational job looks nominally harder. The real degradation driver is sequential PATH LENGTH (2p/3p collapse to ~10-11 MRR), not conjunction count.

This is the substrate's OWN "constraints-brought-to-bear" resolution law (1->7 constraints, 0.05->0.99) reproduced almost exactly in the external KG literature — and Scan 1 of the prior brain-drill note (`research_brain_reasoning_readout_multihop_constraint_chaingrade_target_2026-07-19.md`) already flagged this precise thing as a CAUTION: "the constraints->resolution gain is GENERIC MATH (multiplicative evidence combination / answer-set shrinkage), not a learned capability." **A chain-grade built on "our VSA gets better with more conjoined constraints" would be a THIRD instance of the exact same construction-determined trap as tonight's two negatives** — the win is answer-set arithmetic, not learned inference. And even setting that aside: Query2Box/BetaE are themselves gradient-trained structured embeddings, not glass-box — so a substrate win here (if it existed) would still only be a TRANSPARENCY differentiator at best, not an accuracy edge, and no evidence anywhere shows the substrate's native (untrained) version reaching BetaE/Query2Box-level accuracy in the first place. **Verdict: no-edge, not "only-for-conjunctive-queries" as a live exception — the conjunctive case collapses into the same generic-math trap.**

### (c) Is link-prediction-under-incompleteness fundamentally a frequency/embedding problem?

Yes, on all evidence gathered. Symbolic/rule-based reasoning (AnyBURL, SAFRAN) shows a real but narrow, scale-bounded edge — competitive with or beating embeddings on SMALL KGs, losing on large ones (a 2025 comparative benchmark, Delplanque et al., reports this scale-dependence explicitly, though exact numbers weren't extractable from the scan — flagged as attested-by-abstract only). The consistent, well-supported selling point of symbolic methods is interpretability and training speed, not a decisive accuracy edge at scale. This matches the substrate's own 2026-06-15 finding (`research_synthesis_3x_DRILL...AnyBURL_proposer_CHTV_verifier...`): the substrate's genuine literature-gap differentiator was never claimed to be raw link-prediction accuracy — it was the CHTV logical-verifier's structural defense against semantic drift (a soundness/trust property), explicitly positioned as complementary to, not competitive with, embedding-based completion.

## 2. Biology: is gap-filling a learned brain capability, and by what mechanism?

**Two mechanistically distinct things get colloquially called "filling in gaps," and only one of them is what real-KG link prediction actually is.**

- **(A) Pattern completion** (CA3 autoassociative recall of a partial/degraded cue) is recall of something ALREADY encoded — a stored engram, now partially cued. The literature (Kesner/Rolls-style CA3 reviews, Nakazawa-lineage work) treats this purely as retrieval, never as the mechanism for predicting a never-observed fact.
- **(B) Novel relational inference** (predicting a fact that was NEVER directly observed, purely from statistical/structural regularities extracted across many OTHER facts — what a real incomplete-KG link-prediction task actually demands) is attributed to a DIFFERENT mechanism: hippocampal recombination of separately-retrieved episodic memories into an on-the-fly novel inference (Eichenbaum's relational-memory-network view — pattern completion here is only a subroutine fetching the components, not the inference itself), plus neocortical statistical-regularity extraction via slow interleaved learning (Complementary Learning Systems, Kumaran/Hassabis/McClelland 2016). The Tolman-Eichenbaum Machine makes the split architecturally explicit: a factorized structural code (transferable across contexts) is built SLOWLY and conjoined with content to generalize to novel conjunctions.

**Is (B) learned and experience-dependent?** Yes, strongly. Developmental-amnesia case studies (Hoskin/Vargha-Khadem lineage) show children with early hippocampal damage retain intact pairwise/semantic facts but FAIL transitive/relational inference specifically — the inference capacity, not raw storage, tracks hippocampal integrity. Schema studies (Tse et al., summarized in McClelland's CLS reviews) show the SPEED of assimilating new schema-consistent facts scales with how much relevant structure has already accumulated through prior experience — directly analogous to a network generalizing better after more training.

**Can any local/biologically-plausible (non-gradient-descent) synaptic rule close the gap to gradient-trained generalization?** No, and this is the load-bearing negative for path (A). Classic Hebbian one-shot caps at ~0.14N (Amit-Gutfreund-Sompolinsky) — this is exactly the substrate's OWN measured ceiling story (0.0231 MRR, write-rule-limited). Delta-rule/pseudoinverse rules raise raw STORAGE capacity but do not improve generalization to unseen combinations. Modern/dense Hopfield networks (Krotov-Hopfield) get exponential capacity via higher-order energy functions, formally linked to transformer attention — but the GENERALIZATION power in that lineage comes from the gradient-trained embeddings feeding into the retrieval step, not from the Hopfield step alone. Predictive coding is the one local rule proven to converge to exact backprop gradients on arbitrary graphs — but it does this by locally approximating the SAME global multi-layer credit-assignment computation backprop performs, and still empirically underperforms backprop on real benchmarks. **The gap between glass-box/Hebbian-class mechanisms and gradient-trained generalization appears to require something functionally equivalent to global gradient-based training — no known biological or artificial local rule substitutes for it.**

This directly explains, mechanistically, why the substrate's own native-Hebbian KGStore measurably lost to its own SGD-trained additive-map sibling by 5.5x on the same real data: it is not an implementation bug, it is the expected consequence of a well-characterized capacity/generalization theorem that both the brain and the substrate obey.

## Substrate-product implications

Do not market "beats neural KG embeddings on link prediction" — the evidence, both external and in-house, says the opposite at the representation level the substrate would need to stay glass-box. The one legitimate, still-standing substrate-product claim from this territory is the CHTV-verifier soundness/anti-drift property (a trust/auditability differentiator versus NELL-class statistical self-bootstrapping), which is orthogonal to raw MRR and does not require winning the embedding race. For anything requiring genuine novel-fact inference over incomplete real-world relational knowledge at good accuracy, the honest, product-safe framing is: pair a trained (or literature-standard) statistical completion component with the substrate's transparent, verifiable reasoning layer on TOP of correctly-extracted facts — not "the substrate infers missing facts itself, glass-box, from scratch." This is consistent with, and reinforces, the current pivot to extraction/grounding (path B) as the sole genuine chain-grade thread.
