This is **exactly** the input the 07-24 note's Prediction 2 (do-calculus routing test) needs, and it removes that note's own flagged risk sub-cause: "(a) extraction of clean triples from ARC_Corpus/ConceptNet text is too noisy/low-coverage." There is no extraction step. The triples already exist, typed, in the tablestore.
2. **`questions/questions.{train,dev,test}.tsv`** carries the **gold explanation chain** for every one of ~4400 WorldTree questions as a UID:ROLE list (verified count: 2206 train-split questions have a non-empty explanation field, mean **5.75 facts per explanation**, max 22). This is not new fact content — it is a human-verified CO-OCCURRENCE graph over the existing small fact set, telling you which facts compose together for which question type.
3. **`inference-patterns/patterns-tsv/` (356 files) and `patterns-edges/` (356 files)** — the corpus's own authors already pre-mined these gold chains into per-topic multi-hop "inference patterns" (e.g. `[EARTH HUMIMP] Fossil Fuels - Burns.tsv`, `[CEL CYCLES] Earth Rotation.tsv`). Inspected directly: the edges files are a raw lexical-overlap connectivity matrix (word-stem co-occurrence positions between fact pairs), a byproduct of the pattern-mining tooling, not a clean rule format — lower-value than items 1-2, useful only as a connectivity-graph input if item 2's cleaner explanation-chain mining is insufficient.

None of this adds new *coverage* over ARC content (the 0.06 number stands — this is structure ABOUT the same ~9k tablestore facts, not more facts). But it is free (zero acquisition cost, sitting unzipped already), it is maximally trustworthy (human-authored, CC-BY-SA, cited resource), and it directly, immediately operationalizes the causal-routing experiment the previous research cycle already designed and pre-registered.

**Blunt assessment: this is not a coverage fix. It is a de-risking of an already-queued experiment, plus a sharpened Part-A ranking for where a genuine coverage fix would have to come from (external corpora, not more mining of WorldTree itself).**

## PART B — rule-chaining vs. model-based reasoning: the deeper fork

**This drill does not re-run the 07-24 note's lit-scan (42 sources, three-way split already established: perceptual/dynamic simulation [Battaglia/Hamrick/Tenenbaum, Hegarty] vs. Pearl-style do-calculus over known structure [Gopnik/Sloman/Waldmann-Hagmayer] vs. cached covariation recall for taught facts [diSessa's p-prims, Chi/Vosniadou's conceptual-change literature]).** That verdict stands. This drill adds one new piece: **does the model-based path actually escape the coverage wall, or does it just move the wall?**

**New finding (this drill's own targeted search, today): model-based reasoning has ITS OWN coverage bottleneck — but it is a smaller, differently-shaped one, and that difference is the whole argument for building it.**

Forbus's Qualitative Process Theory and Compositional Modeling literature (Forbus 1984 *AI*; 2011 *WIREs Cog Sci*; de Kleer & Brown 1984) is explicit that representing a domain compositionally requires "domain theories that are orders of magnitude larger than today's theories... at several levels of granularity... incorporating multiple perspectives," plus "techniques for determining which subset of knowledge to apply for a given task" (compositional modeling's own knowledge-acquisition literature, confirmed via direct search today). **Model-based reasoning is not knowledge-free.** It substitutes a library of reusable *process/model fragments* (generic schema-types: rate-coupling, threshold behavior, conservation, phase-change, cyclic process) for a library of *specific facts* (this dam, this planet, this rock). The claim that (B)/(C) sidesteps the coverage wall rests entirely on whether that schema-fragment vocabulary is a much SMALLER, faster-saturating closed set than the combinatorial space of ARC's specific entity-facts — and today's disk find gives a concrete, checkable existence-proof that it plausibly is: **WorldTree's own COUPLEDRELATIONSHIP table (254 rows) is, by inspection, exactly this schema library** — generic monotonic-relationship templates ("as roughness of a surface increases, friction will increase"; "as the temperature of a liquid increases, that liquid will evaporate quicker") that are reused across many different specific entities, not one row per entity. 254+509+382 = 1145 SCHEMA rows is a much smaller ask than comprehensively covering ARC's actual entity-fact space (dams, nuclear plants, coal plants, specific rock types, specific organisms) — which is exactly the axis where WorldTree's raw fact tables (KINDOF, PROP-THINGS, etc., tens of thousands of rows) are still only 6% coverage. **The schema/structure axis and the entity/fact axis genuinely have different scaling behavior; this is the honest mechanism, not a hand-wave.** Confidence in this specific claim: capped P=0.40 (deflated, novel synthesis — no direct study measures ARC-item schema-type saturation vs. entity-fact saturation; this is this drill's own inference from the disk evidence + Forbus's coverage caveat, not a demonstrated result).

**Retrieval-augmented-reasoning literature (search today, ML side, not neuroscience) gives a consistent, if generic, warning that reinforces routing rather than replacing retrieval wholesale:** one-hop/local-neighborhood retrieval alone tends to induce overly-cautious "don't know" behavior when the local graph lacks support — the fix in that literature is combining a reasoning/structure-propagation component WITH retrieval, not abandoning retrieval. This matches the 07-24 note's own routing design (retrieval stays for single-hop taught facts; do-calculus/schema-propagation is added ONLY for the multi-variable coupled-relationship subclass) rather than motivating a full replacement of the fact-retrieval pipeline.

### Verdict on (A) rule-DB / (B) model-based / (C) hybrid

**(C) HYBRID wins, unchanged from 07-24's conclusion, now with a sharper mechanism for WHY it sidesteps the wall rather than just asserting that it does.** Comprehend the question -> retrieve the FEW entity-level facts needed to bind variables (still uses the existing SemanticHDEncoder retrieval, unchanged, for the shallow "what kind of thing is a dam" facts) -> match the relationship pattern against the SMALL, already-on-disk schema library (COUPLEDRELATIONSHIP/IFTHEN/CAUSE, 1145 rows, no extraction needed) -> propagate via the EXISTING do-calculus primitive (PP-270/307) -> read off the predicted direction and match against MC choices by sign, not lexical similarity. This is (C), not pure (B): it still needs a few retrieved facts to instantiate the schema's variables (which entity plays which role), it just does NOT need a comprehensive fact-DB that states the causal link for every entity pair explicitly — the schema supplies the causal link generically, retrieval supplies only which entities are in play.

**SHAPE:** a routing branch inside `the_climb`, gated by a schema-pattern-match classifier (does this question's needed relationship match a COUPLEDRELATIONSHIP/IFTHEN/CAUSE row's predicate/slot signature?) — not a rebuild of the answering pipeline.
**PLACE:** between retrieval and answer-selection; retrieval is reused unchanged for entity-binding and for the complementary single-hop/taught-fact subclass (Prediction 3 in the 07-24 note, expected to stay retrieval-only).
**METRIC:** inherits the 07-24 note's own pre-registered gate exactly — do not re-derive a new one. Prediction 1 HARD-PASS: >=5% of ARC-Easy or >=8% of ARC-Challenge items are genuinely a >=2-variable monotonic-covariation class. Prediction 2 HARD-PASS (the central claim): on that subset, schema+do-calculus routing beats flat retrieval by >=10pp absolute. Both thresholds are now LOWER-RISK to clear than when pre-registered, because the schema source is confirmed clean (no extraction noise to blame a null result on).

**Does (C) sidestep the coverage wall? Partially, and honestly bounded: it sidesteps the wall for the SCHEMA/STRUCTURE axis (a plausibly small, near-saturable set, 1145 rows already on disk) but NOT for the ENTITY/FACT axis (still needs retrieval to correctly identify what kind of thing "a dam" or "a nuclear reactor" is, which is exactly the SELECTION-gate problem this whole ARC arc has already spent multiple cycles on and found hard — cap_map's own selection-gate history, referenced in the memory context, found sel_gold_precision 0.12 vs oracle 0.97 on a similar retrieval-selection task).** The schema library removes the need to store "dam causes electricity" as a fact; it does NOT remove the need to correctly retrieve/select "dam is-a barrier-across-flowing-water" as an entity fact when the pool contains lexically-similar distractors (nuclear/coal/gas plant facts, per the memory context's own most-recent frontier finding). **This drill's honest addition to the fork: the coverage wall splits into two independently-scaled sub-walls, and (C) only closes one of them.** The entity-fact selection wall is the SAME wall the meaning-representation frontier (per MEMORY.md's current-frontier block) is already targeting from a different angle (richer/grounded content meaning) — this is a genuine, useful cross-thread convergence, not a new problem: both threads point at the same underlying gap (entity-level content discrimination), approached from two directions (schema-routing here, richer meaning-representation there).

## Falsifiable predictions (HARD-PASS + HARD-FAIL)

**Prediction 1 (schema-match rate — does a non-trivial ARC subset match the now-confirmed-clean 1145-row schema library).** P=0.42 (deflated; slightly above 07-24's own 0.40 because the extraction-noise risk that note flagged is now removed).
**HARD-PASS:** >=5% of ARC-Easy or >=8% of ARC-Challenge items pattern-match a CAUSE/IFTHEN/COUPLEDRELATIONSHIP row signature (predicate + slot-type match, not full lexical match).
**HARD-FAIL:** match rate is near-zero (<2%) — meaning even the clean, hand-authored schema library doesn't have PATTERN coverage of ARC's relationship-types (a genuinely different, more severe failure than a noisy-extraction failure would have been, since there is no extraction step left to blame).

**Prediction 2 (the central claim — schema+do-calculus routing beats flat retrieval on the matched subset).** P=0.42 (deflated, still capped below the general 0.50 novel-synthesis ceiling — the combination itself, ARC MC-QA answered by do()-propagation over WorldTree's own hand-typed schema rows, has never been run).
**HARD-PASS:** >=10pp absolute accuracy lift over Arm A on the Prediction-1 subset.
**HARD-FAIL:** no lift or a negative lift — and because extraction noise is ruled out as an excuse, a HARD-FAIL here is a stronger, more informative negative than 07-24 anticipated: it would mean either the do-calculus propagation step itself doesn't transfer to real ARC item phrasing (a genuine primitive-scope finding, distinct from PP-307's synthetic-composition success), or the MC-choice direction-sign matching step is the actual bottleneck (a scoring-mechanism failure, not a knowledge or reasoning failure) — these must be reported separately, not blended.

**Prediction 3 (QASC connectivity+selectivity gate — the Part-A external-source probe, run only if Prediction 1 shows the schema library itself is coverage-starved for relationship TYPES, not just entity-instances).** P=0.35 (deflated; genuinely uncertain whether QASC's fact graph has a real causal core or is CSKG's hub-promiscuity problem again under a different name).
**HARD-PASS:** after the same de-biased hub-capping filter used to kill CSKG, QASC's fact graph retains non-trivial connectivity to ARC content specifically through non-hub, science-typed edges.
**HARD-FAIL:** connectivity collapses to ~0 after hub-capping, same as CSKG — confirming this is a structural property of large auto-extracted fact corpora generally (promiscuous-hub bridging, not genuine causal density), not specific to ConceptNet/CSKG, and redirecting further external-source search toward smaller, hand-typed resources (Aristo TupleKB, rank 4) or the LLM-built+vetted path (rank 5) instead of larger auto-extracted corpora.

**Cross-check.** If 1 and 2 both pass: ship the routing branch, scoped to the matched subset (exactly 07-24's own cross-check). If 1 passes, 2 fails: the schema-library-as-model-fragments idea is real but the propagation/scoring mechanism needs rework — do not conclude model-based reasoning is wrong, conclude this specific wiring is. If 1 fails: the schema/structure axis is ALSO coverage-starved (not just the entity/fact axis) — this would be the most consequential possible negative, since it would mean the "structure saturates faster than facts" claim this note's Part B rests on is FALSE for WorldTree's own schema set, and would redirect Part-A investment toward extracting a bigger schema library (from QASC/GenericsKB's causal-cue-tagged sentences) rather than routing logic.
