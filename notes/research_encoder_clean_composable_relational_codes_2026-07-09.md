## HEADLINE

The brain and the math of sparse recovery agree on the same story from two independent directions: (1) **sparsity is the dominant lever** that suppresses cross-talk/interference in composable codes (hippocampal DG: capacity ~ 1/(a*ln(1/a)), a = fraction active -- Treves & Rolls), and (2) **recovery fidelity as a function of sparsity/redundancy is a sharp phase transition, not a gradual curve** (Donoho-Tanner: transition width shrinks as ~1/sqrt(N)). A role-recovery recall of 0.282 is consistent with sitting deep on the FAILURE side of such a transition -- which predicts that small parameter nudges (a little more sparsity, a little more dimension) will NOT produce gradual improvement; you have to cross the boundary. Two further mechanisms are needed to make the crossing durable rather than a one-off: an explicit BINDING-CONSISTENCY training objective (bind at encode time, not read time -- the single most strongly-quantified lever found: Lake & Baroni's MLC moves SCAN systematic accuracy from near-0% to 99.78% via a training-objective change alone, no architecture change), and an iterative CLEANUP/RESONATOR readout stage (modern Hopfield / resonator-network-style joint factorization) specifically because it is the one mechanism in the literature built to stop per-hop error from compounding across a multi-hop chain -- which is exactly the failure mode observed (reach stops at 1 hop).

## S1 -- brain/VSA mechanism ranking (which is load-bearing)

Four candidate levers, ranked by strength of evidence found:

1. **Sparsity level (fraction of active units), not just dimensionality/expansion** -- PRIMARY lever, per Treves & Rolls (Network 1991/1994): autoassociative storage capacity scales as **alpha_c ~ 1/(a*ln(1/a))** as sparsity a -> 0, i.e. superlinear capacity gain from sparsification, while dentate gyrus expansion (~5-10x more granule cells than EC inputs, empirically ~1-5% active per IEG studies, e.g. Chawla-type Arc studies, PMC3726960) provides the *dimensional room* for very sparse codes to remain informative, but sparsity itself is what suppresses crosstalk. Aimone et al.'s neurogenesis models treat sparsity as "the key determinant of pattern separation," with expansion as enabling infrastructure, not the direct interference-suppressing mechanism. **Caveat**: the exact a*ln(1/a) formula and 5-10x expansion ratio were confirmed via secondary-source cross-referencing, not independently re-derived from a fully parsed primary equation this session -- flag as literature-consistent, not hand-verified.

2. **Explicit binding-consistency / structural training objective at encode time** -- second-strongest, and the MOST CLEANLY QUANTIFIED lever overall. Lake & Baroni's Meta-Learning for Compositionality (*Nature* 2023) shows a training-OBJECTIVE change alone (episodic compositional-skill optimization, generic transformer architecture unchanged) takes SCAN systematic-generalization accuracy from near-chance to >=99.78%. Tensor Product Representation lineage (Smolensky 1990; TPR-RNN, Schlag & Schmidhuber 2018) shows explicit role x filler factorization trained WITH a structural binding objective outperforms post-hoc algebra on frozen entangled embeddings on multi-hop bAbI tasks (qualitative result solid; exact percentages flagged unverified from PDF extraction). McCoy et al.'s "RNNs Implicitly Implement TPRs" is notable: ordinary RNNs that succeed at systematic tasks are FOUND, post-hoc, to have converged on TPR-like factorized internal codes -- meaning the structure is discoverable as a byproduct of the right training signal even without hard-wiring, but hard-wiring (explicit objective) is what reliably gets multi-hop chains right rather than "mostly right."

3. **Cleanup / attractor denoising stage after unbind** -- largest SINGLE-mechanism SNR multiplier found in the VSA literature specifically: HRR-lineage sources report cleanup memory raising recoverable superposition capacity from ~2 items (no cleanup) to ~14 items (with cleanup) at fixed recovery threshold in d=100 -- roughly 7x (flagged secondary-sourced, PDF extraction failed on primary formula). Modern/dense Hopfield networks (Krotov & Hopfield 2016; Ramsauer et al. "Hopfield Networks is All You Need" 2020) give this an exponential-capacity theoretical backing. Critically, **resonator networks** (Frady, Kent, Olshausen & Sommer) generalize single-pass cleanup to ITERATIVE JOINT factorization across multiple unknown bound factors simultaneously -- this is qualitatively different from one Hopfield pass, and is the literature's answer to exactly the multi-hop problem: each hop's noisy estimate gets corrected via cross-consistency with the other factors in the loop, instead of propagating and compounding unchecked into the next hop. Sparse block-coded VSA variants are specifically what make resonator search tractable (bounds the combinatorial search space).

4. **Orthogonalizing the role/basis vectors** -- structurally clean (TPR-style exact orthogonal roles give in-principle zero interference via projection) but does not scale to unbounded roles the way random/quasi-orthogonal HRR/BSC vectors do. Best used as a bounded-role-set design choice (small closed set of relation types), not a general solution.

**Convergent read**: sparsity and the training objective are the two mechanisms that determine whether clean composable codes get LEARNED in the first place; cleanup/resonator readout is what determines whether a noisy-but-not-hopeless code SURVIVES multi-hop chaining without compounding. All four are complementary, not substitutable -- consistent with why single-mechanism partial fixes (e.g., just adding more dimensions) are predicted by the compressed-sensing framing (S below) to fail to cross the phase boundary alone.

## S1b -- the phase-transition framing (why 0.282 specifically, not "somewhat noisy")

Compressed-sensing / sparse-recovery lit-scan (Donoho-Tanner phase diagram, AMP state-evolution, RIP/coherence bounds, dictionary-learning capacity) converges on one point: recovery fidelity as a function of (sparsity level, redundancy/dimension, number of superposed items) is a **sharp phase transition**, not a smooth curve. Transition-zone width shrinks as ~1/sqrt(N) -- at realistic scale this is close to a step function. RIP-style guarantees require **m >~ C*k*log(N/k)** measurements/redundancy for a k-sparse recovery target; below that threshold, no gradual-improvement regime exists -- you are simply on the failure side.

Practical read for the 0.282 number: a point deep in the failure region does not creep upward with modest sparsity/dimension nudges. It stays near its floor until the combined parameter shift is large enough to cross the boundary, at which point fidelity should jump rapidly toward the high-recovery regime within a narrow band, not climb gradually. **This reframes the fix from "tune harder" to "cross the boundary with a coordinated multi-lever push"** -- consistent with S1's four-mechanism, not one-mechanism, prescription.

## S2 -- concrete buildable encoder-architecture changes

Ordered cheapest/most-diagnostic first:

1. **Cheap decisive test (no retraining)**: apply post-hoc k-sparse thresholding (top-k / k-WTA, target ~1-5% active per DG-analog) directly to the EXISTING encoder's learned relational codes, then re-run the same role-recovery-fidelity eval that measured 0.282. This isolates whether sparsity ALONE recovers a meaningful fraction of the gap (cheap fix, no retrain) or whether the entangled/dense representation itself is the bottleneck because it was never trained with a structural signal (requires retrain with a new objective -- the more expensive S2.3 below). Near-zero cost, CPU-only, decisive on the S1 mechanism ranking's #1 vs #2 fork.

2. **Sparse expansion recoding layer**: DG-inspired -- expand the relational-code dimensionality (~5-10x) and enforce k-sparse activation (top-k or L0/L1-penalized) as an explicit layer between raw encode and the bind/unbind operator, rather than relying on the existing (likely denser) code.

3. **Explicit binding-consistency training objective** (the strongest single lever found, per MLC): add a loss term requiring bind(role, filler) -> unbind(role) to recover filler with high fidelity DURING TRAINING, not just relying on emergent structure from a generic objective. This is a training-time intervention on the encoder, not a post-hoc algebra change -- "bind at encode time with a structural signal," which every literature thread in S1/S1b that measured it found clearly beats "bind at read time on frozen embeddings."

4. **Orthogonalized/structured role basis**: if the relation-type set is bounded (a small closed vocabulary of role types), initialize and regularize role vectors toward mutual orthogonality (penalize off-diagonal Gram-matrix mass) rather than leaving them fully random/learned-entangled.

5. **Resonator-style iterative cleanup at readout**: replace single-shot correlation-based unbind with an iterative joint-factorization cleanup stage (resonator-network-style), specifically to arrest per-hop error compounding across chained hops -- this is the mechanism most directly targeted at the OBSERVED failure mode (fails past 1 hop specifically, not "noisy everywhere").

6. **Curriculum**: train 1-hop (primitive relation) recovery before multi-hop composed recovery, per Dekker et al. 2022 (PNAS) -- their strongest finding was that DIMENSIONAL ALIGNMENT of training structure (not blocked-vs-interleaved ordering per se) produced large compositional-generalization gains in both humans and a matched network model (52/63 vs 32/61 aligned-vs-misaligned grid condition; 52/58 vs 8/56 for polar mapping) -- apply as: order training examples so relation-type and filler-identity vary along separated (not entangled) axes.

**Measurable target**: if per-hop role-recovery fidelity is f, and 2-hop chaining requires the first hop's (possibly noisy) output to serve cleanly as the second hop's input, a naive independent-compounding model gives 2-hop success ~ f^2. At f=0.282, f^2=0.079 -- consistent with the observed "does not extend past 1 hop" result. To get reach>=2 reliably functional (not just barely above chance), you need f high enough that f^2 clears a working threshold (e.g. f=0.9 -> f^2=0.81; f=0.95 -> f^2=0.90). Given the phase-transition framing in S1b, the realistic target is NOT "nudge f from 0.282 to ~0.5" (still deep in failure region, f^2~0.25, likely still unusable) -- it is a combined-lever push to **f >= 0.85-0.90 single-hop fidelity**, which the phase-transition literature predicts should arrive as a comparatively sharp jump once the combined intervention crosses the boundary, not a gradual climb.

**The test**: run the S2 items as an ablation ladder -- (0) baseline 0.282; (1) sparsity-only (cheap test, no retrain); (2) sparsity + orthogonal role basis (no retrain, cheap); (3) full retrain with binding-consistency objective + sparse expansion (expensive); (4) full stack + resonator cleanup readout, measured separately for single-hop fidelity AND 2-hop chained-reach recall (not just single-hop, to directly test the compounding-suppression claim of the cleanup/resonator mechanism).

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS**: the cheap sparsity-only test (S2.1, no retrain) raises single-hop role-recovery fidelity by >=0.10 absolute (0.282 -> >=0.38) OR the full combined stack (S2.2-2.5) raises single-hop fidelity to >=0.85 AND 2-hop chained-reach recall to >=0.50 (well above the ~0.08 predicted by naive f^2 compounding of the 0.282 baseline) on the SAME eval protocol that measured 0.282.

**HARD-FAIL**: the cheap sparsity-only test moves fidelity by <0.03 absolute (i.e., sparsity alone is not load-bearing -- points to S1 lever #2, the training objective, as necessary not optional) AND the full combined stack fails to clear 0.45 single-hop fidelity (i.e., the phase boundary is not crossed by this specific lever combination, or the diagnosis that interference/crosstalk is the bottleneck is itself wrong -- something else, e.g. insufficient training signal density or a capacity ceiling elsewhere in the encoder, is the real limiter).

**MIDDLE_BAND** (informative, not decisive): sparsity-only test moves fidelity 0.03-0.10 -- sparsity is a partial but not sufficient lever; full combined stack needed, proceed to S2.3-2.5 without expecting the cheap test alone to close the gap.
