## HEADLINE

1. **Biology strongly supports "evaluate-and-gate," much more weakly supports "rewrite-the-schema."** Tse/Morris (rat mPFC lesion-and-IEG studies) and van Kesteren (human fMRI, SLIMM model) give convergent, quantitative evidence that a fast congruency-detection circuit (mPFC-vs-hippocampus) determines HOW FAST and via WHICH route new information consolidates — schema-consistent items can become hippocampus-independent in one trial (vs. weeks normally). But the "schema gets edited by repeated violation" half (true accommodation, not just flagging an exception) is thin — mostly theoretical framing (Kumaran/Hassabis/McClelland 2016), not demonstrated dose-response data.
2. **The prize-critical claim — sleep GENERATES new inferential reach — is real but modest, and gated by how well the item was learned in the first place, not de novo creation.** Transitive-inference-after-sleep (Ellenbogen 2007 + independent replications, PMC10547378) is the most reproduced result in this space: sleep groups score reliably above chance on never-directly-trained inference pairs (~0.56-0.62 vs ~0.50-0.53 for wake), moderated strongly by pre-sleep encoding strength (interaction beta=-0.31, p<.001) — sleep AMPLIFIES existing learning, it does not manufacture inference from nothing. The flashier "sleep inspires insight" result (Wagner 2004, 59% vs 23%) is contested: the SAME lab's own follow-up (Yordanova 2008) only got 31% vs 19%, Verleger (an original co-author) failed to cleanly replicate the flagship number, and no version controlled for experimenter expectancy.
3. **Sleep-specific necessity is itself seriously contested at the current evidentiary bar.** Properly-powered replications of the flagship interference-protection effect (Ellenbogen 2006, original d=3.07) mostly come back null (Bäuml 2014 eta^2=0.001, Bailes 2020 eta^2=0.004, Pöhlchen 2020 eta^2=0.001-0.006); only one adequately-powered study (Abel 2023, n=120) found a protective effect. Awake quiet-rest hippocampal replay (Karlsson & Frank 2009; Schapiro 2018, Nat Commun) produces consolidation-like benefits with NO literal sleep. One direct study found a relational-generalization benefit that tracked elapsed TIME, not sleep specifically (PMC4410790). **What transfers to the substrate is "a dedicated non-interleaved offline window," not anything requiring simulated sleep stages** — this matches and reinforces the 07-08 note's own Rank-2 design (discrete offline phase-gate), now with the added honesty that "offline" is the necessary ingredient, not "sleep" per se.
4. **The SAME mechanism that produces true relational generalization also produces false-positive schema-congruent confabulation — this is not two separate failure modes, it is one mechanism with two tails.** Payne et al. 2009: a night of sleep increased BOTH true recall and DRM-paradigm false recall of gist lures relative to wake (though a later meta-analysis, Newbury & Monaghan 2018, found the overall main effect is inconsistent, moderated by recall-vs-recognition test type). Alba & Hasher (1983) is the foundational demonstration that schema-driven memory is reconstructive, not reproductive — the same congruency-detection process that fast-tracks true schema-consistent learning (finding 1) also fabricates plausible-but-never-observed schema-congruent details. **Any consolidation mechanism that generalizes toward a category/schema will, by the same mechanism, sometimes hallucinate schema-congruent relations that are not true** — this must be pre-registered as an expected cost, not treated as an incidental bug to be tuned away.
5. **Biology supports a genuine labile-to-stable CONFIDENCE/PERMANENCE gradient, but it is 3-4 mechanistically distinct processes at different timescales, not literally one scalar.** Systems consolidation (weeks-years, hippocampal-to-cortical locus transfer, classic temporally-graded retrograde amnesia / Ribot's law), reconsolidation (Nader/Schafe/LeDoux 2000 — RETRIEVAL re-labilizes an already-stable memory, requiring fresh protein synthesis to re-stabilize; this is the direct biological precedent for "confidence can drop and a settled relation can be revised on contradiction"), synaptic tagging-and-capture (Frey & Morris 1997, minutes-hours, early-LTP tag decays in <3h unless a coincident salient signal "captures" it into protein-synthesis-dependent late-LTP), and metaplasticity (BCM sliding threshold — a synapse's own history changes its future plasticity rate, a genuine intrinsic "harder to move once moved" mechanism) are four different mechanisms. Precision-weighting / Bayesian Kalman-gain accounts (Friston-style: update magnitude proportional to prediction-error times precision, i.e. inversely proportional to current certainty) give the correct NORMATIVE shape for a single-scalar engineering approximation, but the literature does not license treating it as literally unified.
6. Deflated P for "an offline replay+confidence-reweight pass measurably improves held-out relational inference for content-placed cold entities, beyond a static one-shot fit, with graded and revisable (not permanently-locked) confidence": **P_deflated = 0.30** (novel-synthesis territory — no prior art found combining relation-level confidence/permanence scalars with offline consolidation on a KG/VSA substrate; deflated below the course-C note's 0.20-0.25 for the bare "does replay change generalization" claim would suggest a LOWER number, but the confidence/precision-weighting principle itself is much better independently grounded (Bayesian/Kalman precision-weighting is a mature, well-evidenced normative framework, not itself novel-synthesis) than the bare replay-changes-generalization claim, so the COMBINED claim is held slightly above that floor at 0.30, capped per novel-synthesis-cap=0.50 regardless).

## Q3 — reconciling a new item's provisional relations with existing schema

**Strongest evidence (Tse, Morris et al., *Science* 2007, 316:76-82; *Science* 2011, 333:891-895):** rats trained over many sessions on a hippocampal-dependent flavor-place paired-associate task develop a stable schema (accuracy significantly above chance from ~session 10). Once the schema exists, NEW pairs consistent with it can be learned in as little as ONE TRIAL and become hippocampus-independent within 48 hours — vastly faster than the normal weeks-to-months systems-consolidation timescale. The 2011 follow-up shows schema-consistent new learning drives immediate-early-gene up-regulation specifically in prelimbic mPFC, and mPFC inactivation blocks BOTH new schema-consistent learning AND recall of already-remote memories (causal, not epiphenomenal).

**Human corroboration (van Kesteren, Fernandez, Henson, Ruiter — SLIMM model, *J. Neurosci.* 2010, 30:15888; *TINS* 2012, 20:512):** mPFC-vs-MTL engagement at encoding tracks congruency monotonically in OPPOSITE directions (mPFC up / hippocampus down as congruency rises), and behavioral memory strength rises monotonically with congruency too — a graded congruency-detection-and-routing circuit, not a binary switch.

**Direction of update for schema-VIOLATING items (Bein, Reggev & Maril 2018, *Learn. Mem.* 25:352, PMC6049394):** congruent items recognized far better than incongruent (t(30)=2.95, p=.006, d=0.53); incongruent items were often statistically indistinguishable from unrelated/novel items on plain recognition — the deficit traces to IMPAIRED INTEGRATION AT ENCODING (more fixations, harder to bind), not accelerated forgetting after the fact. Associative/backward-recall tasks showed incongruent items partially outperforming unrelated ones (d~0.5) — a genuine but SMALL "assimilate as flagged exception" effect.

**Honest gap:** the "does the schema itself get REWRITTEN given enough repeated violation" question (true accommodation, not just tagging an exception) has no clean quantitative dose-response study behind it in this lit-scan. Kumaran, Hassabis & McClelland (2016, *Trends Cogn. Sci.* 20:512) is the closest citation and it is an organizing/conceptual synthesis for AI-agent design, not measured data. **Treat "novel relations get absorbed as flagged exceptions, easily distinguishable from the parent schema, fast" as well-supported; treat "the parent schema's own content changes from repeated child-entity counterevidence" as a plausible extrapolation, not a demonstrated mechanism** — this matters directly for design (Section below): build the "exception flag" path first; do not assume schema-level rewrite for free.

## Q4 — THE PRIZE-CRITICAL CLAIM: does consolidation generate new inferential reach?

**Transitive inference (Ellenbogen, Hulbert, Jiang, Stickgold 2007, *Curr. Biol.*; independent replication PMC10547378):** premise pairs A>B, B>C, C>D, D>E trained to criterion, tested on untrained inference pairs (esp. the hardest, most "distant" pair B>D). Sleep groups performed reliably above chance on inference pairs; immediate/wake groups were at or near chance. Independent replication numbers: 12h-delay sleep group M=0.62 above chance (t(35)=2.53, p=.008) vs wake M=0.53 n.s. (t(35)=0.62, p=.271), n~24; a second experiment (27h incl. sleep vs 3h wake): sleep-hierarchy M=0.56 (t(69)=1.80, p=.038) vs wake M=0.53 n.s., n~70. **Effect is distance-graded** (strongest for the hardest/most remote inference pairs, absent for easy/near pairs) and **strongly moderated by pre-sleep encoding strength** (interaction beta=-0.31, p<.001) — sleep amplifies what was already learnable, it is not creating inference ability from nothing. Status: replicated across independent labs; modest effect sizes, conditional on encoding strength and item difficulty.

**"Sleep inspires insight" (Wagner, Gais, Haider, Verleger, Born 2004, *Nature*):** number-reduction task with a hidden shortcut rule. Original: sleep 59% (13/22) gained insight vs wake controls 22.7-25% (5/22, 5/20), chi^2=10.2, p=.03. **Contested/mixed, not a clean replication record:** Yordanova et al. 2008 (same lab) — early-night sleep 31% (9/29) vs late-night 19% (5/26), markedly weaker than the original 59%; Lang et al. 2006 single-session control got 23% (6/26) with NO sleep manipulation at all; **Verleger et al. 2013 — an original co-author — failed to cleanly replicate the high post-sleep solution rate**; Debarnot et al. 2017 replicated only in young adults, not older adults; a 2024 preregistered replication targeting a related N1-sleep-insight claim (Lacaux et al. 2021) found no N1 effect, only a partial N2 benefit. No version of this paradigm controlled for experimenter-expectancy effects.

**Gist/statistical-regularity extraction (Djonlagic et al. 2009 weather-prediction task; systematic review "Sleep and Extraction of Hidden Regularities"):** sleep improved extraction of implicit probabilistic category structure in some studies, but the review concludes benefits are inconsistent and task/temporal-rule dependent. **Directly relevant counter-finding:** "Time- but not sleep-dependent consolidation promotes emergence of cross-modal conceptual representations" (PMC4410790) — generalization emerged with elapsed time regardless of whether that time contained sleep, for that specific paradigm.

**A large, adequately-powered, direct 2026 test found NO effect:** a bioRxiv preprint titled "No evidence for sleep-dependent memory generalization in a large online sample" is a large-N, well-powered direct test of exactly this question (full text unavailable at fetch time, 403; flagged because most originals in this literature are n=20-70 and thus underpowered by modern standards, and this is one of the few adequately-powered direct tests located).

**Calibrated verdict:** transitive inference is genuine and replicated, but small and conditional (sleep amplifies existing learning, not de novo inference). "Sleep inspires insight" is likely overstated (flagship number not cleanly reproduced even by an original co-author). Gist/statistical generalization is mixed, with at least one direct study attributing the effect to elapsed time rather than sleep specifically. **Overall: YES, offline consolidation measurably extends inferential reach beyond directly-trained items — for transitive/relational inference specifically — but the effect is modest, gated by how well the base facts were learned, and the "sleep-specific" framing (vs. mere offline/time-elapsed) is itself contested (see Q6).**

## Q5 — TRANSFER: the minimal glass-box VSA experiment

### The confidence/permanence design (coordinator-added scope)

**What already exists on the substrate (verified on disk):** `hdlab/kg_traversal.py::refuse_gate_calibrate` computes a global accept/refuse confidence threshold `tau` from graded retrieval-time scores (in-KB vs OOD calibration split) — this is a RETRIEVAL-TIME, GLOBAL confidence gate. It is not a per-relation, persistent, updatable confidence/permanence scalar. Stored relations currently carry `metadata={}` on 100% of edges (no numeric attributes) — the provisional-to-reinforced/replaced lifecycle has no home to live in yet. This is the concrete gap the design below targets.

**Honest design tension (native magnitude vs. explicit scalar) — three options, one recommendation:**
- (a) **Native bundle-magnitude-as-confidence**: reinforcement = re-superpose the same relation vector (its coefficient/magnitude in the bundle grows); contradiction = subtract or bind-cancel. Cheapest, reuses existing machinery, no new storage.
- (b) **Explicit persistent per-relation confidence scalar**, stored alongside the (now populated) metadata field, decoupled from the vector's own norm.
- (c) **Hybrid**: retrieval reads off native magnitude (already the mechanism driving readout, unchanged), but the substrate ALSO writes an explicit confidence/permanence scalar for BOOKKEEPING and to GATE future update rate (precision-weighting: learning-rate ~ 1/confidence).
- **Recommendation: (c).** The substrate has independently discovered, and CG-certified, that overloading one scalar (magnitude/norm) for two different jobs creates crosstalk it keeps re-paying for (`reference_correlation_hurts_associative_store_capacity_decouple_from_retrieval_2026-07-08` — decorrelated codes for the store, correlated codes for retrieval, is the same "don't overload one representation for two jobs" lesson). Pumping a relation's magnitude up to signal "high confidence" also changes its weight in bundling/interference math, for reasons unrelated to confidence. Biology itself keeps these separate: Kindt/Soeter propranolol-reconsolidation studies show an emotional-STRENGTH scalar can be reduced for up to a month while the explicit/declarative CONTENT trace is left intact — a real dissociation between "how strongly does this affect me" and "what is the content," which is precedent for NOT collapsing confidence into the content-vector's own norm.

### The minimal experiment

**Setup:** synthetic relational KB with parent/category structure. Cold entities receive content-based-placement: inherited/provisional relations copied from the parent category, tagged LOW initial confidence. Some inherited relations are correct (parent-consistent, evidence will confirm), some are wrong (entity-specific exceptions, evidence will contradict), and the entity has its own genuinely novel relations never present in the parent (analogous to schema-inconsistent new content, Q3). Directly-observed/asserted relations start at HIGH initial confidence (ground truth, not inherited guesses).

**Consolidation pass (offline, per Rank-1/Rank-2 design of the 07-08 note — reuse, do not re-derive):** replay stored edges + accumulated evidence; for each provisional relation, update confidence via a precision-weighted rule — update magnitude proportional to (1 - current_confidence) x prediction-error-sign, i.e. confirmed evidence raises confidence toward an asymptote (harder to move further once high, mirroring metaplasticity/BCM's history-dependent threshold), contradicted evidence lowers it (and, if a stable/high-confidence relation is strongly enough reactivated-under-mismatch, it must be able to RE-LABILIZE and revise — the direct reconsolidation analog, non-negotiable per Finding 5 below).

**Must-fail controls (both required, not optional):**
1. **Random-replay-order control**: same replay volume, no confidence-aware ordering or gating — tests whether prioritization/gating adds anything over generic noise-averaging.
2. **Confidence-blind fixed-rate update control**: every relation updates at the SAME fixed learning rate regardless of current confidence (no precision-weighting) — tests whether the "established relations resist single-shot corruption" property is real or decorative.
3. **Static one-shot baseline**: content-placement guess, never revised — the floor.

**HARD-PASS (all five required):**
1. Confirmed provisional relations: confidence rises measurably above its initial low value AND held-out inference accuracy on that relation improves >=15% relative over the static baseline.
2. Contradicted provisional relations: confidence drops and the wrong inherited relation is corrected/pruned/replaced at a rate measurably above chance and above the static baseline.
3. **Single-shot-corruption protection test**: a single noisy/contradicting observation does NOT flip an already-high-confidence (directly-observed) relation under the confidence-gated design, but the SAME single contradicting signal DOES revise a low-confidence provisional relation — the explicit stability-plasticity dissociation, directly modeled on precision-weighted/Kalman-gain learning-rate scaling.
4. The confidence-gated + prioritized replay arm beats BOTH must-fail controls on the composite metric (correct-reinforcement rate + correct-correction rate + held-out inference gain).
5. **Revisability check (non-negotiable, directly from reconsolidation biology)**: given REPEATED, strong (not single-blip) contradiction, even a high-confidence established relation eventually revises. A design that makes high-confidence relations permanently unbreakable has smuggled in an unfalsifiable lock the biology explicitly does NOT support (reconsolidation shows even fully consolidated memories reopen under reactivation-plus-mismatch) and would risk the substrate ossifying wrong inherited guesses forever.

**HARD-FAIL (any one kills the mechanism as tested):**
1. Confidence trajectories for confirmed vs. contradicted provisional relations are statistically indistinguishable (evaluating-against-evidence step does nothing beyond noise).
2. Random-replay-order matches or beats the confidence-gated arm (confidence machinery is decorative; plain replay-averaging does all the work).
3. Confidence-blind fixed-rate update matches or beats confidence-gated update on the single-noisy-observation protection test (precision-weighting adds no real protection).
4. A single contradicting observation flips an established high-confidence relation even under the gated design (the confidence signal is not load-bearing for resistance).
5. High-confidence relations never revise even under repeated strong contradiction (over-fit to permanence at the cost of required revisability — matches the exact failure shape the reconsolidation literature warns against).
6. The rare/cold-entity inference win disappears once checked against inverse/duplicate-relation or Cartesian-product-relation leakage in the eval set (Sun-et-al-2019-style audit, reused from the 07-10 prior-art note — mandatory, not optional).

## Q6 — HONEST COUNTER

**Does consolidation genuinely ADD inferential power, or just reorganize?** Both, inseparably, per the same mechanism. Payne et al. (2009, *Psychol. Sci.*) found a night of sleep increased BOTH veridical recall AND false recall of DRM critical lures relative to daytime wake; while true-item memory decayed across both conditions, gist/false memories were preferentially PRESERVED by sleep (though Newbury & Monaghan's 2018 meta-analysis, *Psychon. Bull. Rev.*, found no consistent overall main effect — moderated heavily by recall-vs-recognition test format). Alba & Hasher (1983, *Psychol. Bull.* 93:203) remains the foundational demonstration that schema-driven memory is RECONSTRUCTIVE: schema activation improves memory for schema-consistent material but also produces false recognition of plausible-but-never-presented schema-congruent details. **The mechanistic reading: the same congruency-detection/consolidation process that fast-tracks true schema-consistent learning (Q3) is what fabricates false schema-congruent content — there is no way to get one without risking the other with the same mechanism.** This must be pre-registered as an expected cost of the design above (HARD-FAIL #6, leakage-style audits) rather than discovered as a surprise later.

**Is sleep/offline actually NECESSARY, or would an online update do as well?** Seriously contested at current evidentiary standards. Properly-powered direct replications of the flagship "sleep protects against interference" result (original Ellenbogen et al. 2006, d=3.07) mostly return null or near-null: Bäuml et al. 2014 (eta^2=0.001-0.0003), Bailes et al. 2020 (n=97, eta^2=0.004), Pöhlchen et al. 2020 (eta^2=0.001-0.006) — all null; only the adequately-powered Abel et al. 2023 (n=120, need n>=79/group per modern power estimates, almost no earlier study met this) found a protective effect (d=1.28). Vertes (2000, *Behav. Brain Sci.*) argued REM sleep's necessity for consolidation specifically is not well-supported — REM-deprivation studies are "about equally divided" between showing and not showing deficits, and pharmacological/lesion elimination of REM produces no clear cognitive deficit. Awake hippocampal replay during quiet rest (Karlsson & Frank 2009; Schapiro et al. 2018, *Nat. Commun.*) independently improves memory and prioritizes weakly-learned items WITHOUT any sleep at all. **Verdict: sleep is not demonstrated to be mechanistically necessary, distinct from an equivalent-duration OFFLINE (non-interleaved-with-new-encoding) window. What transfers to the substrate is "a dedicated offline pass, structurally separated from live ingest" — already the 07-08 note's Rank-2 design — not anything that needs to model sleep stages, circadian timing, or REM/NREM distinctions as first-class substrate mechanisms.**

## Cross-thread synthesis

Five threads (schema-reconciliation, transitive-inference/prize-claim, false-memory/distortion counter-evidence, confidence/permanence-gradient biology, plus the already-on-disk mechanics/degree-invariance notes) triangulate on ONE actionable shape: **consolidation should be modeled as a graded, PRECISION-WEIGHTED update over an EXPLICIT per-relation confidence/permanence scalar (kept separate from the vector's own bundling magnitude), gated by evidence-reliability, revisable under repeated strong contradiction (never permanently locked), and honestly expected to produce SOME rate of schema-congruent false positives as the necessary cost of the same mechanism that produces true relational generalization.** Every one of the five literatures independently arrived at a version of "verify the reliability/independence premise before trusting an update," matching the same discipline already flagged in the 07-10 prior-art note (predictability-gating, anti-collapse instrumentation, degree/leakage audits) — this drill adds the confidence/permanence AXIS and the honest replication-status audit of the biology's own prize claim, which those notes did not cover.

## Substrate-product implications

- The cold-entity content-placement problem (provisional relational profile from parent category) has a direct, biology-precedented fix: tag inherited relations LOW-confidence at placement time, run them through the SAME offline consolidation pass as directly-observed relations, and let the precision-weighted update do the sorting (confirm -> strengthen toward permanence; contradict -> weaken/prune/replace). This requires populating the currently-empty `metadata` field with an explicit confidence/permanence scalar per relation (Option (c) above) — a genuinely new storage requirement, not free from existing machinery.
- Expect and PRE-REGISTER a nonzero false-positive/schema-congruent-confabulation rate as a cost of this mechanism, not a bug to eliminate — Finding 4/Q6 says this is mechanistically inseparable from the true-generalization benefit. Report the true/false split explicitly, per-eval, rather than assuming it can be tuned to zero.
- Do not build "sleep" as a first-class simulated concept (no need for NREM/REM staging, circadian gating, or nightly-budget mimicry beyond a simple finite-per-cycle-budget scheduler) — the load-bearing biological requirement that survived scrutiny is "discrete, non-concurrent-with-ingest offline phase with graded, revisable confidence updates," not sleep physiology per se.
- This composes directly with, and should be built as an extension of, the already-designed Rank-1/Rank-2 consolidation pass (07-08 note) and the replay/consolidation map-builder design (07-10 course-C note) — NOT a new parallel mechanism. The confidence/permanence scalar is the missing piece that turns "replay reconciles two codes" into "replay evaluates and revises a specific claim."
