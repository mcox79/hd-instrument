## HEADLINE

The brain's answer to "how do I tune myself while running" is NOT one controller -- it is (at least) SIX separable scalar channels, each solving a DIFFERENT, provably-unsolvable-by-a-fixed-knob problem (NE=gain/interrupt, ACh=encode-vs-retrieve gate, 5HT=adaptive time-horizon, tonic-DA=vigor/effort-per-decision, ACC/EVC=marginal-cost-benefit halting, homeostasis+sleep=offline renormalization+consolidation) -- and the strongest algorithmic precedent (Aston-Jones/Cohen gain-as-hyperparameter, Niv average-reward vigor, Shenhav EVC-as-meta-RL, Hinton wake-sleep) shows each channel is a ONE-SCALAR-IN, ONE-SCALAR-OUT feedback loop, decoupled from content and from each other, that composes ADDITIVELY on top of an already-working action/value/attention core rather than requiring a new bespoke architecture. The minimal substrate-implementable self-manager is therefore a small bank of 5-6 independent scalar meta-controllers sitting ABOVE the value-gate + attention-gate + envelope-predictor already built, not a new module that replaces or re-derives them.

**P_deflated (self-manager-as-modulating-layer-over-existing-core, general thesis):** 0.45 (capped near novel-synthesis ceiling; individual mechanism-level claims below run 0.30-0.55 per channel; see per-channel bands).

## 1. Brain mechanism per channel (with citations)

### NE / locus coeruleus -- GAIN + INTERRUPT

Aston-Jones & Cohen's adaptive gain theory: tonic LC firing tracks task utility; phasic LC bursts (locked to decision outcomes) sharpen unit responsivity (gain), raising signal-to-noise ratio without changing the signal itself; when utility declines, sustained tonic firing drives disengagement/exploration [1]. This was directly instantiated as a literal scalar "gain" hyperparameter on unit activation functions in a connectionist model two decades earlier (Servan-Schreiber, Printz & Cohen 1990) -- catecholamine effects on signal-detection reproduced by varying ONE gain parameter [2]. Sara's "network reset" hypothesis: phasic LC bursts evoked by unexpected/novel events act as a global interrupt that discontinuously disengages the current network configuration, distinct from a smooth gain anneal [3].

### ACh / basal forebrain -- ENCODE vs RETRIEVE mode-switch

Hasselmo: high ACh suppresses recurrent/feedback synapses (biasing toward new-input encoding); low ACh releases that suppression (biasing toward pattern-completion/retrieval); encoding and retrieval are further scheduled onto different theta phases to minimize mutual interference [4][5]. Complementary Learning Systems (McClelland, McNaughton, O'Reilly 1995) frames the same conflict architecturally: a single shared substrate cannot simultaneously write-orthogonally and retrieve-associatively, so encode/retrieve must be either time-gated (ACh) or structurally separated (hippocampus vs cortex) [6].

### 5HT / dorsal raphe -- adaptive time-horizon (patience)

Doya's meta-learning framework assigns serotonin to the TD discount factor gamma -- low tonic 5HT steepens discounting (impulsive), high 5HT flattens it (patient) [7][8]. Miyazaki et al. confirm behaviorally: phasic dorsal-raphe 5HT activation prolongs waiting for delayed reward, and the effect is maximal exactly when reward probability is high AND timing uncertain -- i.e. when patience is decision-theoretically rational, not merely costless [9][10][11]. OFC projections carry the effect; NAc projections do not, implicating a value-computation locus not a motor-suppression one [12].

### Tonic dopamine -- vigor / opportunity-cost

Niv, Daw, Joel & Dayan formalize free-operant behavior as an average-reward SMDP: optimal response vigor is a monotonic function of the long-run average reward rate rho, proposed to be encoded by tonic (not phasic) striatal dopamine -- the opportunity cost of time itself [13][14][15]. Cools extends this to cognitive effort: DA modulates willingness to expend deliberation effort as a function of expected value of control; computational work separately fits a phasic "choice" (RPE) component and a tonic "vigor" component from the same behavioral data [16][17][18].

### ACC -- expected value of control (effort allocation)

Shenhav, Botvinick & Cohen (EVC theory, 2013): dACC computes payoff-minus-cost over candidate control-allocation intensities and selects the maximizer -- a genuine cost-benefit optimization over a continuous control-effort variable, not a binary flag [19][20]. A 2025 extension reframes ACC as meta-RL: it tracks a Bayesian-surprise (KL-divergence-like) signal and uses it to meta-learn how much control to deploy, unifying "monitoring" and "control execution" [21]. The original conflict-monitoring model (Botvinick et al.) computes a trial-by-trial conflict signal and feeds it forward as a gain on next-trial control -- producing the Gratton conflict-adaptation effect, empirically DISSOCIABLE from a separate post-error-slowing channel [22][23][24].

### Homeostasis + sleep -- offline renormalization and consolidation

**Synaptic homeostasis hypothesis (SHY, Tononi & Cirelli):** wakeful learning drives net synaptic potentiation across cortical circuits; sleep (via slow-wave activity) globally down-scales/renormalizes synaptic strength, restoring baseline before the next wake cycle [28][29][30]. **Allostasis (Sterling):** classical homeostasis = fixed setpoint + reactive negative feedback; allostasis = the setpoint itself is shifted PREDICTIVELY ahead of anticipated demand via a feed-forward/inference layer coupled to the feedback loop [31][32][33]. **Sleep as offline consolidation (CLS + sharp-wave-ripple replay):** McClelland/McNaughton/O'Reilly's Complementary Learning Systems -- hippocampus is a fast, sparse, pattern-separated one-shot buffer; neocortex is a slow, distributed, interleaved-learning store; systems consolidation transfers hippocampal traces to cortex via offline replay, physically instantiated as hippocampal sharp-wave ripples (compressed ~20x sequence reactivation during rest/sleep) [34][35][36]. **Direct algorithmic precedent (strongest of the whole drill):** the wake-sleep algorithm (Hinton, Dayan, Frey, Neal, Science 1995) alternates a "wake" phase (bottom-up recognition weights trained on real data) with a "sleep" phase (top-down generative weights train recognition weights on the model's OWN self-generated/fantasized samples) -- a literal, content-decoupled offline re-tuning phase, not just a biological metaphor [37][38]. Brain-inspired continual-learning replay (van de Ven et al. 2020; Tadros et al. 2022) operationalizes SWR-style unsupervised reactivation as a discrete offline phase between tasks that recovers old-task performance [39][40].

## 2. What each buys computationally (vs. a fixed/static knob)

| Channel | Static-knob failure mode it fixes | Formal character |
|---|---|---|
| NE gain/interrupt | Fixed temperature picks ONE point on the precision/flexibility tradeoff for the whole episode; cannot respond in real time to utility decline, and cannot implement a discontinuous "reset" (categorically different from a smooth anneal) | multiplicative gain on unit responsivity + separate discrete interrupt channel |
| ACh encode/retrieve | A single always-on recurrent substrate used for both writing and reading suffers catastrophic interference -- the SAME parameter setting that gives high-fidelity retrieval (strong recurrence) actively corrupts new writes, and vice versa | binary/graded gate on recurrent-connection strength, NOT on input drive |
| 5HT time-horizon | A fixed discount factor is either too myopic (fails whenever delay/value contingencies are long) or too far-sighted (overweights noise when the environment is actually short/volatile); optimal gamma is a moving target under nonstationary delay statistics | scalar gamma adapted online from {realized delay, hit/miss, uncertainty-over-delay} |
| Tonic DA vigor | A fixed effort/speed setting cannot track the SMDP-optimal result that vigor should scale with the environment's average reward rate rho -- provably wrong in both rich (too slow) and sparse (too effortful) regimes | scalar effort/speed multiplier driven by EMA(reward/compute-time) |
| ACC/EVC halting | Always-max-effort wastes compute on easy/uncontested trials; always-fixed-low systematically fails on the harder subset where extra iteration would flip the answer -- this is the compute/accuracy tradeoff under an UNKNOWN, per-instance difficulty distribution | marginal-cost=marginal-benefit halting rule on search depth/retries, driven by a disagreement/entropy proxy |
| Homeostasis+sleep | A system that only ever potentiates/writes without renormalizing saturates its dynamic range and loses discriminability; a system that learns new things immediately on the SAME weights it uses for old retention suffers catastrophic forgetting | offline (or slow-timescale) renormalization + replay pass, triggered by drift telemetry |

**The unifying computational claim (own synthesis, not literature-derived):** every channel is solving a variant of the SAME underlying problem -- a single fixed global parameter cannot simultaneously satisfy two regimes that place OPPOSITE demands on it (explore vs exploit; encode vs retrieve; patient vs impulsive; effortful vs economical; write vs preserve). The brain's solution is never "pick the average" -- it is always "add a fast, cheap, content-free feedback loop that swings the SAME knob to whichever regime's setting is currently load-bearing." This is the single design principle worth carrying into the substrate.

## 3. Minimal substrate-implementable version (per channel)

Per channel, the MINIMAL controller strips away all cellular/neurotransmitter detail and keeps only the (telemetry-in -> knob-out) feedback loop:

1. **NE-analog (gain/interrupt):** telemetry = rolling utility-decline rate + input-novelty/surprise score (distributional mismatch of incoming code vs. running mean). Knob = one multiplicative scalar on selection-temperature/gain applied network-wide, PLUS a distinct discrete channel that hard-resets working-context state when novelty crosses a threshold (NOT the same axis as the smooth gain -- this is the load-bearing distinction the lit-scan surfaced: an anneal and an interrupt are different mechanisms wearing the same "NE" label).
2. **ACh-analog (encode/retrieve gate):** telemetry = a binary/graded "is this a write-request vs a read-request" signal (already implicit in the substrate's own operation -- ingest vs query). Knob = one scalar down-weighting RECURRENT/compositional-store connection strength during writes, restored during reads. Note this is a DIFFERENT axis than NE gain (recurrent-only, not global).
3. **5HT-analog (time-horizon):** telemetry = realized-delay / hit-miss / uncertainty-over-delay for whatever multi-hop or deferred-credit process is running. Knob = one scalar controlling how many future steps get non-trivial credit weight (an effective gamma or effective lookahead-depth), adapted from the telemetry via a simple EMA-style update, not fixed at config time.
4. **DA-analog (vigor/effort):** telemetry = EMA of reward-per-unit-compute (or accuracy-per-unit-compute if no external reward exists). Knob = one scalar effort/speed multiplier applied to compute-budget-per-decision (search depth, sample count, early-exit threshold).
5. **ACC/EVC-analog (halting):** telemetry = disagreement/entropy among currently-competing candidates (margin between top-2 hypothesis scores, or KL between successive belief updates) -- must be CHEAP relative to the resolution step it gates. Knob = search depth / retries / acceptance threshold, set via a marginal-cost-crossing rule rather than a fixed cutoff; the SLOW-timescale version of this same signal (running average of "has extra effort paid off lately") is the Gratton-style conflict-adaptation layer sitting on top.
6. **Homeostasis+sleep-analog (renormalization/consolidation):** two sub-knobs, corresponding to the two mechanisms the lit-scan separated cleanly: (i) SHY-style RENORMALIZE -- telemetry = global write-density / cumulative weight-norm growth since last pass (a scalar counter, content-free by construction); knob = a multiplicative/subtractive global rescale of accumulated weight toward a fixed band. (ii) CLS-style CONSOLIDATE -- telemetry = buffer fill-level / time-since-last-consolidation (queue length, not item identity); knob = a replay-and-reweight pass that resamples stored high-salience patterns INTERLEAVED with background statistics before writing them into the more stable/compositional store (the wake-sleep algorithm's own-generated-sample replay is the direct engineering precedent for "replay against your own model," not just against a stored buffer).

**Cross-cutting design point:** every one of the six is a SCALAR in, SCALAR (or small ordered set) out. None require a new W matrix, a new embedding space, or new representational machinery. This matters directly for substrate-fit: the self-manager is cheap by construction if built this way, and becomes expensive/risky the moment any channel is implemented as "train a new sub-network to predict the knob" instead of "compute a simple running statistic and map it through a fixed monotone function."

## 4. Composition with the 3 certified/near-certified substrate pieces

| Existing piece | What it is | How the self-manager composes |
|---|---|---|
| `exp_pfc_bg_composed_attention_value_gate_v1` (value-gate x attention-gate composition) | A basal-ganglia-style Go/NoGo value-gate (reach = cos(E[cand]@M, goal)) wired to read its goal from a v8 attention-routing gate | The self-manager sits ABOVE both, unchanged: (a) the ACC/EVC halting knob would gate HOW MANY hops/retries this composed pipeline gets before accepting an answer (currently DD is a fixed constant -- an EVC-style controller would make it adaptive to the composition_tax/scramble_gap telemetry the cell ALREADY computes); (b) the NE-gain knob would scale GATE_TAU / the softmax sharpness of the v8 admission gate in real time based on novelty of the incoming goal-cue, instead of the fixed a-priori GATE_TAU=0.05 currently hand-set. Neither requires touching the reach_value/cleanup/Hebbian-W internals -- this is a genuine MODULATING LAYER, not a new mechanism. |
| `exp_substrate_gen_lm_combinedgate_recency_content_v8` (attention-routing arbitration gate) | Parameter-free biased-competition softmax(content_rel/tau + recency_bias) | The self-manager's ACh-analog and NE-analog are both directly expressible as knobs on THIS gate's two existing free parameters (GATE_TAU, RECENCY_GAP_TARGET) -- currently fixed a priori. A self-manager would make GATE_TAU track an NE-style novelty signal (sharper when the incoming cue is reliable, softer when noisy) and would make RECENCY_GAP_TARGET track a 5HT-style horizon signal (how far back should recency-prior reach). The gate's OWN architecture (softmax arbitration) does not change at all -- confirms the "modulating layer over a solid core" framing empirically, not just theoretically, because this gate's free parameters are ALREADY the right shape (temperature-like scalars) for a neuromodulatory knob to land on. |
| `exp_reasoning_depth_capacity_provisioning_monitor_loop_v1` (self-improvement envelope predictor) | MONITOR-NOT-CONTROL: observes provisioning-vs-depth telemetry, fits a survival law, PROPOSES a safe max reasoning depth; explicitly never self-applies (USER-LOCKED) | This is the missing REAL-TIME half's closest sibling and clarifies the boundary: the envelope-predictor answers "what is my SAFE ENVELOPE" (a slow, offline, propose-only law fit over many runs); the self-manager answers "given telemetry from the LAST FEW STEPS, what should my knobs be RIGHT NOW" (a fast, online, auto-applying loop). They are NOT the same machinery and should NOT be merged: the envelope-predictor is explicitly monitor-only by USER lock, while at least the ACC/EVC halting knob and the NE gain knob are naturally auto-applying (per-instance halting decisions and per-instance gain are not "self-modifying the substrate," they are normal inference-time control flow, same category as choosing a search depth per query). The self-manager's DA-vigor and 5HT-horizon knobs, however, if they update slowly and persist across sessions, WOULD cross into the same "structural self-modification" territory the envelope-predictor's monitor-not-control lock was designed to fence off -- these should inherit the same monitor-not-control discipline (propose, log, require sign-off before persisting) rather than the fast per-instance auto-apply behavior of the gain/halting knobs. |

**Answer to "does the self-manager sit ABOVE them or need its own separate machinery":** ABOVE, as a set of scalar taps on parameters that already exist in the two gates (GATE_TAU, RECENCY_GAP_TARGET, DD/hop-count) plus one genuinely new machinery item -- the disagreement/entropy telemetry computation for ACC/EVC halting, and the write/read binary-flag telemetry for the ACh-analog gate, neither of which exists as an explicit substrate signal today (though the ingredients -- top-2 margins, scramble_gap-style discriminators -- are already computed inside the existing cells and just need to be EXPOSED as a live signal rather than a post-hoc metric).

## 5. Cheap first test (pre-reg-style, HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL bands)

**Cell name candidate:** `substrate_acc_evc_adaptive_halting_v1`

**Why this channel first:** of the six, the ACC/EVC halting knob is the cheapest to test because (a) the telemetry (disagreement/entropy among top candidates) is ALREADY computed as a byproduct of existing cleanup/argmax operations in the composed value-gate cell, (b) the knob (number of hops/retries before accepting an answer) is already an explicit parameter (DD in `exp_pfc_bg_composed_attention_value_gate_v1`) that is currently FIXED, and (c) it directly extends a cell already in the Store rather than requiring new corpus/task design.

**Design (paired arms, reuse `exp_pfc_bg_composed_attention_value_gate_v1`'s corpus/harness unchanged):**
- ARM_FIXED_DEPTH: current cell behavior, DD fixed at the cell's existing constant for all instances.
- ARM_EVC_ADAPTIVE: per-instance halting -- after each hop, compute a disagreement signal = normalized margin between top-2 Go/NoGo scores; if margin > threshold, HALT early (accept current hop's answer at less-than-DD depth); if margin stays below threshold, continue up to a raised depth ceiling (DD+2).
- ARM_RANDOM_DEPTH: per-instance depth drawn uniformly from {DD-1, DD, DD+1} independent of any telemetry (controls for "does variable depth alone help, absent the EVC signal" -- isolates whether the DISAGREEMENT signal is load-bearing or whether any depth variation would do).

**HARD bands (pre-reg):**
- HARD_PASS: ARM_EVC_ADAPTIVE beats ARM_FIXED_DEPTH on accuracy-per-unit-compute (accuracy / mean hops used) by >= 15% relative AND beats ARM_RANDOM_DEPTH by >= 10% relative (proves the disagreement signal specifically, not just depth variance, is doing the work) AND mean hops used by ARM_EVC_ADAPTIVE is >= 10% lower than ARM_FIXED_DEPTH on the ALIGNED/easy sub-regime (proves it is actually economizing, not just always running longer).
- MIDDLE_BAND: beats ARM_FIXED_DEPTH on accuracy-per-compute by 5-15% but does not clear the ARM_RANDOM_DEPTH margin (disagreement signal present but weak).
- HARD_FAIL: ARM_EVC_ADAPTIVE within +/-5% of ARM_FIXED_DEPTH on accuracy-per-compute, OR fails to beat ARM_RANDOM_DEPTH (adaptive halting adds nothing beyond noise).
- Telemetry-sensitivity guard (mandatory per house discipline): a SCRAMBLED-disagreement control arm (disagreement signal replaced by random noise of matched scale) must collapse toward ARM_RANDOM_DEPTH -- if it does not, the discriminator is analytically pinned, not genuinely reading the disagreement signal.

**Cost:** re-uses the existing composed-gate cell's corpus/harness entirely; adds one halting-rule variant and one control arm. Estimated ~20-30 min GPU at smoke-preview scale (same N/V ratio discipline as the parent cell).

**P_deflated:** 0.40 (brain-existence-proof tilt + very close mechanical fit to an already-certified cell's existing free parameter raises raw estimate to ~0.55-0.60; lit-scan calibration penalty -0.15 to -0.20 for the "no direct precedent explicitly wiring ACT-style early-exit to an ACC/EVC frame" gap the lit-scan flagged; capped under 0.50 novel-synthesis ceiling).

## 6. Does the corpus support building this as a MODULATING LAYER over an already-solid core (evolutionary-order design prior), rather than a new bespoke module?

**Own grounded opinion (not just describing the prior):** YES, and the evidence for this is stronger than the lit-scan alone suggests once you look at WHERE the free parameters already live in the two certified gates. `exp_substrate_gen_lm_combinedgate_recency_content_v8`'s GATE_TAU and RECENCY_GAP_TARGET, and `exp_pfc_bg_composed_attention_value_gate_v1`'s DD (hop depth) and w_reach, are ALL scalar knobs that were hand-set a priori and then FROZEN for the cell's entire run. That is exactly the "fixed-knob" failure mode every neuromodulatory channel in Section 2 is a documented fix for. The fact that these knobs already exist, are already scalar, and are already sitting at the exact interface points (softmax temperature, recency-bias gap, halting depth) that the neuromodulatory literature targets, is itself evidence FOR the modulating-layer thesis: the substrate's own certified architecture independently re-derived the SAME parameter shapes the brain uses, before this drill ever looked for them. That is a much stronger argument than "the brain has a layered architecture, therefore we should build one too" -- it is "the parameters we would need to modulate already exist in our own certified cells, unused as dynamic knobs."

This also directly answers the evolutionary-order framing: the value-gate (basal-ganglia-style Go/NoGo) and the attention-routing gate (biased-competition/cortical-style arbitration) are the ANCIENT action-selection core and the general-purpose cortical layer, respectively, per the brain's own evolutionary order -- and they were built and chain-graded FIRST, independently, for unrelated reasons, and only wired together this session. The self-manager is a THIRD, later-evolving layer (neuromodulatory nuclei + ACC are phylogenetically not the oldest structures either) that in the brain does not replace or duplicate the basal-ganglia/cortical machinery -- it tunes the GAIN, THRESHOLD, and HORIZON parameters those circuits already expose. Building the self-manager as new bespoke machinery (e.g., a learned meta-controller network with its own weights trained end-to-end) would be the WRONG evolutionary and computational move: it would duplicate what a scalar feedback loop already does more cheaply, and it would risk exactly the kind of representational entanglement the "correlation hurts associative capacity" and "CRT/residue helps-clean-hurts-noisy" cross-cell laws already warn against (mixing a control signal into the same representational space as content). The literature's strongest algorithmic precedents (Servan-Schreiber's gain-as-hyperparameter, Niv's vigor-as-scalar, the wake-sleep algorithm's alternating-phase-not-new-architecture design) all point the same direction: neuromodulation in the successful computational analogs is ALWAYS a thin scalar layer on an existing architecture, never a network large enough to need its own training regime. Recommend building the self-manager exactly that way -- 5-6 independent scalar taps, each computed from a running statistic already available or one hop away from an existing cell's telemetry, with NO new learned weights, mapped onto the free parameters that already sit unused-as-dynamic-knobs inside `v8` and `pfc_bg_composed`.

**Risk flagged for honesty:** the ACh-analog (encode/retrieve gate) is the channel where "thin scalar layer over existing architecture" is LEAST proven in the literature (Hasselmo's mechanism is validated at circuit level but has weak direct precedent as a single artificial scalar gate -- CLS's answer is architectural separation, not a scalar knob, which cuts slightly against the pure-modulating-layer thesis for this one channel specifically). The homeostasis+sleep channel is, by contrast, the BEST-evidenced of all six for direct algorithmic (non-bio-only) precedent -- the wake-sleep algorithm is a 1995 Science paper that IS a thin alternating-phase control loop over an unchanged generative/recognition architecture, not a new bespoke module, which is the strongest single piece of evidence for the modulating-layer thesis in the whole drill. Recommend the cheap first test (Section 5) target the ACC/EVC channel specifically BECAUSE it has both strong algorithmic precedent (meta-RL reframing, EVC-as-explicit-optimization) and the cleanest fit to an existing frozen parameter (DD) already sitting in a certified cell -- de-risk there first, then extend to the homeostasis+sleep renormalize/consolidate pair (second-best evidenced), leaving the ACh encode/retrieve gate for last since it is the one channel where a bespoke architectural answer (CLS-style separate store) may be genuinely more brain-faithful than a scalar gate.
