## 1. THE MECHANISM (how the brain assigns patienthood)

- **Patienthood = a cluster of entailments, not an atom (Dowty 1991, proto-roles).** Proto-Patient entailments: undergoes CHANGE OF STATE (incl. coming-into/out-of existence), INCREMENTAL THEME (affectedness tracks the event progressively, "eat the sandwich"), CAUSALLY AFFECTED by another participant, stationary relative to another's movement, existence-dependent on the event. Argument-Selection Principle: the argument with the most Proto-Patient entailments lexicalizes as object. This is a MATTER OF DEGREE (family resemblance) — which is why one syntactic slot hosts semantically heterogeneous fillers (things eaten/noticed/pleased/moved).
- **Force-dynamics is the embodied primitive under it (Talmy 1988).** Causer/affected are DERIVED from which entity's intrinsic force-tendency is overridden/redirected — a kinesthetic/proprioceptive primitive, not a discourse or purely-syntactic notion.
- **Syntax supplies MORE role signal than the raw scene (Gleitman syntactic bootstrapping; Human Simulation Paradigm, Gillette et al. 1999).** Muted-video (scene alone): verbs ~15% vs nouns ~45%. Syntactic FRAME alone (argument count/structure, verb nonce-replaced): ~51.7% — HIGHER than scene alone. Syntax+nouns ~75%. => the ARGUMENT-LINKING PATTERN (how many participants, how organized) carries the role assignment; raw perceptual co-occurrence underdetermines it (Quine's problem generalized). NUANCE: the frame condition disambiguates VERB IDENTITY more than it isolates WHICH-NP-is-patient, and it rides on argument NOUNS that were grounded first (word-to-world scaffolds structure-to-world).

## 2. REQUIRED-COMPONENT LIST (for "assign patienthood correctly AND keep improving with experience")

From sub-scan a4073192 (neural), consolidated:

| # | Component (region) | Function | Necessity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | pSTS (bilateral, esp. R) | biological-motion / goal-directed action detection; causally necessary (TMS/lesion) | strict — for event perception from vision |
| 2 | LOTC | encodes the action TARGET (agent/patient/object distinction) — closest neural correlate of patient-role coding | strict — for visual patient ID |
| 3 | V5/MT+ + intraparietal sulcus | motion/spatiotemporal analysis feeding the causal-impression (Michotte launching) | required for causal-impression FROM VISION |
| 4 | causal-perception mechanism | contact/motion -> "X caused Y" impression, precursor to agent/patient | required; bottom-up-vs-top-down locus CONTESTED |
| 5 | anterior temporal lobe (ATL) | amodal semantic hub; early combinatorial composition (~200-250ms); supplies plausibility constraints | required for SEMANTICALLY-guided role assignment |
| 6 | posterior IFG (Broca) / L perisylvian | builds/holds SYNTACTIC structure (movement, case, order) that fixes roles when semantics is ambiguous | required specifically for SYNTAX-driven role assignment |
| 7 | angular gyrus / pMTG / TPJ ("thematic hub") | represents verb-argument structure, binds fillers to abstract thematic roles incrementally | required for sentence-level role binding |
| 8 | WM episodic buffer (+ verbal/reading span) | holds partial role-filler bindings active during parsing/reanalysis | required but capacity-GRADED (a resource, not a module) |
| 9 | movement/trace (or equivalent structural) rep | links a moved filler to its role (Grodzinsky TDH); its loss forces a LINEAR-ORDER default heuristic | CONTESTED (TDH=syntax-specific vs WM-capacity account) |
| 10 | dopaminergic RPE / general error-based learning | domain-general teaching signal (Schultz); in language = error-based predictive weight-update (Chang-Dell-Bock "Becoming Syntactic") | plausibly required for improvement-with-experience; RPE<->thematic-learning link is a THEORETICAL BRIDGE, not a landed finding |
| 11 | corrective/negative environmental input (e.g., caregiver reformulation, Chouinard-Clark) | supplies the error signal #10 operates on | required in principle for error-driven learning |

**The load-bearing clinical mapping (Caramazza & Zurif 1976 + Grodzinsky TDH):** agrammatic patients who lose the syntactic/trace representation (#9) comprehend semantically-constrained sentences fine but FAIL semantically-REVERSIBLE non-canonical sentences (passives) — they fall back to a **"first NP = Agent" linear-order heuristic** and produce thematic-role REVERSAL errors. **Our ~0.557 reader, leaning on positional + per-verb-frequency cues, IS this agrammatic fallback** — it handles the easy semantically-constrained cases and fails exactly the reversible/structural ones (build/huts vs build/stream; they=men not flowers). This is the single sharpest brain-map of our reader's residual.

## 3. SUBSTRATE-VS-LIST EVALUATION (what we HAVE vs what's REQUIRED)

- **HAVE (validated):** role-filler BINDING algebra (the #7 binding operation, lossless), a learned similarity-structured content CODEBOOK (semantic-hub-like content codes, CG), WORKING MEMORY two-layer buffer (#8, settled), METACOGNITIVE abstain/confidence (a self-monitoring proxy, CG). So the BINDING + REPRESENTATION + WM + confidence layers are present.
- **PARTIAL / MIS-IMPLEMENTED:** SYNTACTIC structure (#6/#9) — the reader uses positional heuristics, NOT a movement/trace-like structural representation; this is the agrammatic gap. SEMANTIC plausibility (#5, ATL) — present as content codes but not wired as a graded plausibility constraint on role assignment (the graded-fit cell af19aa9d showed class-typicality alone doesn't carry it).
- **GENUINELY MISSING:** all PERCEPTUAL/event components (#1-#4: pSTS/LOTC/MT+/causal-impression) — we read TEXT ONLY, zero grounding. And the LEARNING/CORRECTION signal (#10/#11: RPE + corrective input) — there is NO error signal that tracks patient-correctness; this is the missing self-correcting learning loop (root cause of every negative this arc).
- **THE VERDICT (grounding debate, sub-scan a0cfb7d4, ~75-80% consensus, contested at edges):** grounded/perceptual experience is necessary for ACQUIRING the concept of patienthood/affectedness for genuinely novel referents; text distribution recovers STRUCTURAL REGULARITIES AMONG ALREADY-GROUNDED concepts, not new grounding (Harnad symbol-grounding; Barsalou perceptual symbols). Live counter-evidence: thematic-fit modeling (rho ~0.3-0.5) + LLM syntactic-bootstrapping (arXiv 2508.12482) recover REAL role-structure from text alone — so "text predicts nothing" is FALSE, but the hardest case (novel referent, in-context only) still needed grounded argument nouns doing indispensable work. => our text-only patient-selection failure is BRAIN-CONSISTENT for the genuinely-novel/reversible cases; the fix is grounded or weak-supervised.

## 4. RANKED TEXT-COMPUTABLE CANDIDATE SIGNALS (to design-gate at corr>0.2 with gold-patient-correct)

From sub-scan ad668495, with the fairness distinction folded in:

1. **Dowty Proto-Patient entailment signal via Levin/VerbNet causative-inchoative CLASS (top pick).** The ONLY candidate giving a near-DETERMINISTIC, VERB-SPECIFIC patient signal (change-of-state/causative-inchoative class -> the non-subject argument IS the Patient), directly targeting the affectedness entailments that DEFINE patienthood — not a noisy correlate. DISTINCT from the 6 failed signals (they were distributional-semantic or objecthood; NOT alternation/entailment-structure). **FAIRNESS CAVEAT (load-bearing, matches our USER fairness guards):** VerbNet/Levin is a HAND-BUILT lexicon = "text-adjacent scaffolding, not text-internal." Two framings must be reported separately: (a) LEXICON-LOOKUP version = weak-supervision (allowed per the foundation-from-external-tools pivot, but label it honestly as curated-knowledge, not learned-from-raw-text); (b) TEXT-INTERNAL version = re-induce the signal via Levin's OWN PROCEDURE = corpus-ALTERNATION detection (does this verb appear both transitively AND intransitively/passively with the same NP as subject? = causative-inchoative diagnostic, purely distributional-structural, no lexicon imported).
2. **Silverstein animacy (as a CO-FEATURE, not alone).** Cheap, robust (~16pp effect even in neural models), WordNet/co-occurrence-taxonomy lookup. Value = COMBINED with #1 (low-animacy argument under a change-of-state verb = very likely Patient). Animacy-alone already bounded here (feature-design bound, 29357/density VET) — so ONLY as a co-feature.
3. **Resnik-style selectional preference / distributional thematic fit.** Fully computable (corpus+WordNet) but validated human norms only moderately reconstructed (rho ~0.3-0.5) = a real ceiling; and object-typicality already HARD_FAILed at patient-slot (af19aa9d) — so LOW prior unless framed as affectedness not typicality.
4. **Implicit-causality bias from corpus re-mention stats.** Directionally real but weak/noisy sub-LLM-scale, and closer to causal-locus/blame than physical patienthood (wrong-construct risk).
5. **Unsupervised role induction (Lang-Lapata).** Induces role STRUCTURE from text but doesn't solve WHICH-cluster-is-Patient without seed knowledge — a structural aid, not a standalone proxy.
6. **HSP "syntax-alone" signal.** Dramatic for VERB-IDENTITY (15%->51.7%) but validated for verb-ID, not argument-role assignment — last as a standalone patient proxy.

**HONEST META-CAVEAT (sub-scan ad668495, load-bearing):** all six share a vulnerability — they are either (a) weak/noisy correlates needing scale/norming (2,3,4) or (b) a hand-built lexicon in a costume (1,4-VerbNet). If the bar is strictly self-supervised-from-raw-text, the genuinely-text-internal best bet = **animacy (co-occurrence taxonomy) + corpus-alternation detection (Levin's procedure)** — but this is a HYPOTHESIS TO VET AT THE DESIGN-GATE (is the alternation signal actually present + non-trivial at our corpus scale? does it correlate with patient-correctness > chance?), NOT a bankable win, given the track record of plausible-sounding text-only signals dying on contact with real data.

## 5. DESIGN-GATE RECOMMENDATION (feeds revival cell ab46d65c)

MEASURE corr(signal, gold-patient-correct) BEFORE any loop, for at least: (A) Dowty proto-patient entailment count via VerbNet causative-inchoative class [lexicon-lookup framing]; (B) corpus-alternation-detection [text-internal framing, Levin's procedure]; (C) animacy-x-alternation combined co-feature. Do NOT re-test object-typicality / selectional-coherence (closed: af19aa9d graded-fit, a5f2c3d0 SCV). Report each candidate's correlation + which framing (curated vs text-internal). HARD-PASS = a candidate clears corr>~0.2 AND a subsequent loop moves held-out patient-precision. HARD-FAIL across all = the signal-absence pivot DEEPENS to "patient-selection needs true grounding or gold weak-supervision" — which the 75-80% brain consensus PREDICTS, and which is a legitimate, brain-consistent bound (converges on the foundation/grounding investment, Frontier-1-blocked / Frontier-2-native). Either outcome is informative; the design-gate makes it cheap and decisive.
