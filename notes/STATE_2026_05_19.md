## Substrate (what we're building)

No-backprop hyperdimensional memory substrate:
- **Carrier**: BSC bipolar +-1, default N=4096
- **Binding**: elementwise multiply (byte_atom * pos_atom)
- **Bundle**: sum then sign
- **Training**: delta rule on W (no backprop) + classical pool with
  softmax retrieval
- **Decomposition**: resonator network with multi-restart
- **Capacity envelope** (matches Frady-Sommer 2020 baseline):
  - Vector dim N: validated to 262,144 (lossless)
  - Codebook K_codebook at N=4096: cliff between K=2048 (100%) and
    K=2304 (10%); hard ceiling K/N ≈ 0.55
  - Bundle size B at N=4096, K=32: 100% to B=128; 76-92% at
    B in {256..2048}
  - 50x capacity rescue available via Karunaratne-Langenegger 2024
    ACF protocol (untested, queued)

## Confirmed headlines (strong positives with statistical power)

### 1. R10 — concept-fusion beats retrieval-alone at noisy context
Lippl-Stachenfeld 2025 redundancy theorem holds at K=4 but **provably
fails at K>=8**. 5-seed multi-seed trajectory:

| K | post-shift gap | t-stat |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | -0.115 | (concepts hurt) |
| 16 | +0.008 | 5.1 |
| 32 | +0.048 | 29.3 |
| 64 | +0.106 | 61.5 |
| 128 | +0.139 | 95.3 |
| 256 | **+0.193** | 88.9 |

Gap is monotone in K, every level p << 0.001. At K=256 the
concept-fusion advantage is **~2x the entire C3-factored headline**.
This is publication-grade.

### 2. R3 — concept-as-readout-bias at gamma=0.5 [REVISED MAGNITUDE]
**Settled 2026-05-19 PM**: original +0.154 was ~80% broken-normalizer
artifact. True R3-Laplace-alone effect at K=4 = **+0.032 bpc** (3 seeds,
SD 0.005). Still real, still positive, but ~5× smaller than originally
measured. R3 does not compound with replay+R10 (substitution-not-
orthogonality, over-determined by 4 independent arguments). The
methodological contribution is the framing: "two interventions on
different pipeline stages do not compound when they consume the same
evidence base, regardless of normalization." See
`wave14b_r3_laplace_synthesis_research.md`.

### 3. Random replay during Phase B — BWT recovery (NEW HEADLINE)
Plain uniform random replay (50% interleave) from 1024-entry pool:
- K=4: +0.66 bpc BWT recovery (3-seed mean +0.65, SD ~0.01)
- **K=32: +0.73 bpc BWT recovery** (3-seed mean, from triple_compound)
- Even 10% replay captures +0.61 at K=4

**This is the dominant single mechanism for continual-learning BWT
recovery.** Random replay alone gets the substrate from -1.58 BWT (no
intervention) to -0.85 BWT at K=32 — closing ~46% of the catastrophic
forgetting gap. Cheapest, strongest mechanism we have.

### 4. C3 factored — compositional retrieval (the original headline)
Per-position factored retrieval kernel: post-shift **+0.098 bpc**
over C1 classical pool. Now superseded by R10 K=256's +0.193 but
remains the best-tested K=4 result.

### 5. Tier-1 capabilities — substrate uniqueness
- Memory editing: 100% bit-exact (decompose -> modify -> rebundle)
- Memory recomposition: 100% bit-exact (pieces from N memories)
- Interpretability: 12.4% of pool entries activate >=1 PPMI concept
  (acceptable; byte 4-grams are mostly unique)

## Critical compound-test findings (2026-05-19 ~14:00)

**r10_r3_combined** (K=16, K=32, 3 seeds): R3 at K=16 vanishes
(gain ~+0.005, null). R3 only helped at K=4. R10 grows with K; R3
shrinks with K. **They don't compound — R10+R3 is slightly worse
than R10 alone.** Mechanisms interfere.

**triple_compound** (K=32, 3 seeds, 2^3 conditions): The "triple
compound" hypothesis is FALSIFIED. Random replay alone DOMINATES.

| Condition | BWT @ K=32 |
|---|---|
| baseline | -1.58 |
| R3 only | -1.58 (null) |
| R10 only | -1.08 (+0.50) |
| R10+R3 | -1.21 (R3 hurts) |
| **Random replay alone** | **-0.85 (+0.73)** ← BEST |
| Replay+R3 | -0.89 |
| Replay+R10 | -0.88 |
| Triple | -1.02 (worse than replay alone) |

The three mechanisms are NOT orthogonal — they're **substitutes** that
all correct the same W-drift-during-Phase-B problem. Adding R10/R3
on top of random replay does nothing or hurts slightly.

**Practical implication**: for deployment, use random replay alone.
R10 is the fallback when replay isn't available (privacy / streaming).
R3 is K=4-specific and shouldn't be used at higher K.
