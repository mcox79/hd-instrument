## HEADLINE

The developmental-psychology literature gives a real, citable **order + bootstrapping mechanism** for grounding (concrete/basic-level/sensorimotor first, abstract concepts bootstrap off concrete anchors via metaphor/simulation — Barsalou, Lakoff, Harnad, Mandler), but gives **no clean "how much is enough" number** — every quantitative scale claim in that literature is contested or a research-proxy, not a sufficiency threshold. On the field/data side, there is a genuinely large, free, no-LLM-required supply of bulk MEASURED numeric data (Materials Project ~150k, AFLOW ~3.9M compounds, OQMD ~1.4M, PubChem ~110M+, Wikidata quantity-typed claims, USDA FoodData ~6-300k), classical no-LLM entity-resolution tooling to join it onto an existing symbol graph (exact-ID crosswalk via InChIKey/CAS/QID first, Fellegi-Sunter/Splink for the residual long tail), and an established KG-embedding sub-field (LiteralE, KBLRN, TransEA, MT-KGNN) that fuses numeric literals into entity vectors with **real, measured, double-digit-percent link-prediction gains** (LiteralE: DistMult MRR 0.241 -> 0.317 on FB15k-237 with numeric literals, ~31% relative gain). The specific claim that content-injection rescues degree-based cold-start entities is separately and directly evidenced (ConMask open-world completion beats structure-only DKRL/TransE by large margins specifically in the low/no-structure regime). What is **not** established anywhere in the literature is the percolation/k-core framing of "how much added connectivity is needed to cross the reasoning threshold" applied to knowledge graphs specifically — that mapping is a novel analogy this memo is proposing, not a transferred result, and must be flagged as such (P capped at 0.50, deflated further below).

Net: the deep lever (grounded ingest at scale) is well-supported as a *mechanism* (structural analogs exist and work), under-supported as a *sizing/threshold* claim (no one has told us the coverage fraction we need), and the substrate already has the wiring seam to attach it (`AdditiveKGMap`'s swappable `coord_source`, and the existing bulk-ingest dispatcher pattern in `director_kb_bio_sources.py` / `director_kb.py`).

## Cheap decisive test

Before building the full pipeline: pick ~500 sparse-tail (0/1-support) entities already in the symbol graph that plausibly have a Wikidata QID or an obvious lexical match to a Materials Project / PubChem / USDA entry (e.g. concrete nouns: elements, common foods, common materials). Attempt EXACT-ID entity resolution only (QID/CAS/InChIKey crosswalk, no fuzzy matching) against Wikidata's quantity-typed claims dump (P2067 mass, P2046 area, etc., freely bulk-downloadable as JSON dumps) and/or USDA FoodData Central bulk CSV. Measure: (a) join-hit-rate — what fraction of the 500 tail entities get at least one numeric literal via exact-ID join alone, (b) resulting degree delta — for a hit entity, does gaining 1 numeric-literal edge change its `k-core` number (i.e. did it need >=2 edges to move from isolated to reachable, or does 1 suffice under the current graph's k-core definition). This is a few hours of scripting (bulk-download + string/ID match, no training run) and answers the two load-bearing unknowns (is there enough coverage overlap with OUR tail, and does 1 grounded edge actually change reachability class) before committing to the full literal-fusion embedding build.

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS** (justifies building the full literal-fusion + AdditiveKGMap wiring):
- Exact-ID join-hit-rate on the 500-entity tail sample is **>= 15%** (i.e. grounded bulk sources have real overlap with our actual sparse tail, not just with generic Wikidata averages), AND
- For >= 50% of hit entities, adding the numeric-literal edge moves the entity from 0/1-support to >= 2-support (i.e. it's not just decorating already-connected nodes).

**HARD-FAIL** (do not build the literal-fusion pipeline yet; re-scope):
- Join-hit-rate < 5% (our tail is dominated by entities with no measured-data analog — proper nouns, rare taxonomic leaves, abstract relations — and bulk MEASURED sources don't reach them; would need a different content channel, e.g. dictionary-definition/gloss text rather than numeric literals), OR
- Hit entities are already high-degree (the join accidentally targets well-connected nodes, not the tail) — a data-plumbing bug, re-check the sampling.

**MIDDLE-BAND** (5-15% hit rate): partial coverage — proceed but scope the first production ingest to the specific sub-domains that DID hit (e.g. materials/chemistry/food nouns only), not a general claim of "the tail is fixed."

## Substrate-product implications

- If the cheap decisive test HARD-PASSes: the product story becomes "the substrate isn't just a symbol graph, it's a symbol graph anchored to real measured properties of the world" — a concrete, verifiable differentiator (every grounded entity has a citation-traceable numeric fact, not a hallucinated one), and directly attacks the sparse-tail reasoning wall named as tonight's bottleneck.
- If it HARD-FAILs on general bulk sources: the honest fallback is that our tail is dominated by lexical/taxonomic long-tail (proper nouns, rare IS_A leaves) that measured-numeric databases don't cover — the next grounding channel to test is gloss/definition text (WordNet/Wiktionary glosses, still no-LLM: bag-of-words or classical embedding of definitions) rather than numeric literals. Either outcome is informative and cheap to get.
- Do NOT oversell the percolation framing in any product narrative yet — it is a structurally apt but **unattested** analogy (no paper found applying k-core/percolation theory to KG completion specifically); treat "how much grounding is enough" as an open empirical question to be answered by our own k-core measurement on our own graph, not by citing outside literature that doesn't exist for this specific claim.

## Sequenced grounded-ingest-at-scale build plan

**Phase 0 — Cheap decisive test (above).** Cost: hours. Confidence in outcome: genuinely unknown (that's the point of the test) — do not skip.

**Phase 1 — Exact-ID bulk join (no fuzzy matching, no LLM).**
- Sources (ranked by ease of bulk access + numeric density): Wikidata quantity-claims JSON dump (broadest lexical coverage, includes P2067 mass / P2046 area / hundreds of other numeric properties) > PubChem bulk SDF/FTP (chemistry) > Materials Project bulk API (materials) > USDA FoodData Central bulk CSV (food) > AFLOW/OQMD (materials, larger but narrower vocabulary overlap with a general symbol graph).
- Join mechanism: exact match on existing cross-reference IDs already embedded in these sources (Wikidata QID, CAS number, InChIKey, PubChem CID) against symbol-graph entity labels resolved to QIDs first (most symbol-graph entities are common nouns already present in Wikidata's label index). Classical tools if scale demands it: Splink (Fellegi-Sunter, scales to hundreds of millions of records) for the residual fuzzy tail; reserve fuzzy matching for entities that fail exact-ID join.
- Data-scarcity flag: exact figures for "how many symbol-graph entities have a Wikidata-QID-resolvable label" are unknown until measured — this is Phase 0's job, not assumed here.

**Phase 2 — Literal-to-vector encoding (no LLM, classical numeric encoding).**
- Adopt the LiteralE/KBLRN pattern: encode each numeric literal (after per-property z-score or log-scale normalization — numeric literals span wildly different units/ranges: mass in kg vs band-gap in eV vs mass in Daltons) through a small literal-encoder (a plain MLP or even a fixed radial-basis discretization, consistent with the "measure->discretize->attribute-table" bulk-KB pattern), then gate-combine with the entity's existing coordinate via a learned or fixed gating function.
- Reported effect size to calibrate expectations against: literal fusion methods in the general KG literature show ~10-30% relative MRR gains over literal-blind baselines (LiteralE's ~31% on FB15k-237 is the concrete anchor number) — treat this as the ballpark expectation, deflated, not a promise, since none of these papers tested a graph shaped like ours (extreme taxonomy skew, 71k SYNONYM + 62k IS_A dominance, near-100% metadata-empty).
- Data-scarcity flag: normalization strategy per numeric-property type needs its own small design pass; don't reuse one global normalizer across incompatible units.

**Phase 3 — VSA wiring (coexist with symbol graph, reach the tail specifically).**
- Attach as a second `CoordinateSource` (or a literal-gated pre-processing step feeding the existing `LearnedSGDCoordinateSource`) in `hdlab/additive_map.py`'s `AdditiveKGMap` — the seam already exists (`coord_source: Optional[CoordinateSource]`), so this is additive, not a parallel system, and coexists with the multiplicative `KGStore` per the endgame plan already on file.
- Specifically target sparse-tail entities: the join in Phase 1 should be FILTERED or at minimum FLAGGED by current node degree, so the pipeline explicitly measures "did this fix the entities we built it to fix" (0/1-support), not just average-case entities that were already fine. This is the fairness/weak-point-localization discipline (per `feedback_fairness_plus_weak_point_localization_first_class_every_cell`) applied to an ingest pipeline: a degree-stratified before/after k-core measurement is mandatory, not optional.

**Phase 4 — Scale target and honest deflation.**
- Scale target for a first production pass: NOT "ingest all of Wikidata/PubChem" — start with the intersection of (symbol-graph sparse-tail entities) x (bulk sources that hit in Phase 0's cheap test), likely low thousands to tens of thousands of entities, not millions. Scaling further is warranted only after the Phase 0/HARD-PASS bar is cleared and the degree-stratified measurement in Phase 3 shows real reachability-class movement.
- Deflation per phase: Phase 1 (join-hit-rate) — genuinely unknown, could be anywhere from <5% to >50% depending on how lexically "generic" our tail is; do not assume high overlap. Phase 2 (embedding gain) — deflate the literature's 10-30% MRR-gain figures by the standard lit-scan calibration penalty (0.15-0.25) since our graph shape (near-total metadata absence, taxonomy-dominated) is unlike any tested benchmark (FB15k-237/YAGO/LiterallyWikidata all have partial baseline literal coverage already; ours has ~0%). Phase 3 (percolation/reachability claim) — cap P at 0.50 per novel-synthesis rule; this is the least-attested link in the chain (no KG-percolation paper found) and should be treated as a hypothesis this pipeline itself will test, not a result being applied.

## First buildable step (hand to exp_dev)

**Phase 0, exactly as scoped above**: sample ~500 current 0/1-support entities from the symbol graph, attempt exact-ID join against a Wikidata quantity-claims bulk dump (and/or USDA FoodData CSV for the food-noun subset), measure join-hit-rate and k-core-class movement. No embedding training, no LLM, no fuzzy matching — pure bulk-download + ID-join script. This is the single cheapest artifact that converts "grounded ingest at scale" from an aspiration into a measured go/no-go number, and it directly determines whether Phase 1-3 above are worth building at all.
