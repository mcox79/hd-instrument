## HEADLINE

**The framing is subtly but importantly wrong, in exactly the way worth stopping for.** "Did they
get what they wanted" is not one brain computation — it is the collapsed readout of at least THREE
dissociable systems (a mentalizing/goal-as-utility-function inference, a continuous appraisal/value
trajectory, and an event-boundary-triggered verdict crystallization), which the current bottom-up
architecture (goal-state token vs outcome-state token, same/opposed class relation) flattens into a
single symbolic match check computed once at end-of-text. The mentalizing leg (2) is not
partially-built and unwired like the force-dynamics/discourse-connective gaps found in the 2026-08-08
drill — **it is entirely absent from the architecture.** Separately, and just as consequential: a real
chunk of the "45% hard residual" is not a comprehension gap at all. DesireDB's own published
inter-annotator numbers (Krippendorff's α ≈ 0.63, 66% three-way agreement) show HUMANS disagree on
this exact task at a rate that, by Krippendorff's own reliability guidelines (α > 0.8 for confident
conclusions, 0.67-0.8 "tentative," < 0.67 "unreliable"), sits BELOW the tentative-reliability
threshold. Chasing 100% accuracy against a majority-vote label with a ~0.63-α ceiling baked in is
chasing an artifact, not comprehension. **Recommendation: neither "stay the course" nor "just go
top-down" — extend the current reuse-heavy pipeline with two specific missing pieces (a goal-as-
inferred-preference-structure leg, and a continuous graded congruence scalar in place of the binary
class-relation), AND recalibrate the success bar to inter-annotator agreement, not gold-label
accuracy.** Confidence in this overall verdict: MED-HIGH (individual literatures are HIGH-confidence
verified; the synthesis connecting them to this specific architecture is capped at P=0.50 per
novel-synthesis discipline).

## Q1 — Is a discrete Fulfilled/Unfulfilled verdict a natural brain computation, or a task artifact?

**Both, at different levels — and the current architecture picks the wrong level to be discrete at.**

At the **affective/value level**, the evidence is one-sided toward continuous, incrementally-updated
computation. Scherer's Component Process Model treats goal/need conduciveness as a continuum,
re-evaluated recursively as an event unfolds — a trajectory, not a snapshot (Scherer 2001, 2009,
*Cognition and Emotion* 23(7); Moors, Ellsworth, Scherer & Frijda 2013, *Emotion Review* 5(2) —
HIGH). The reward/value literature is even more direct: dopaminergic reward-prediction-error is
recomputed continuously as predictions update (Hollerman & Schultz 1998, *Nat. Neurosci.*; Schultz,
Dayan & Montague 1997, *Science*), human ventral striatum/OFC BOLD tracks the TD-error term
specifically (O'Doherty, Dayan, Friston, Critchley & Dolan 2003, *Neuron* 38(2)), and OFC neurons
encode graded, continuous, menu-invariant economic value (Padoa-Schioppa & Assad 2006, *Nature* 441)
— all HIGH confidence, all describing an incremental running signal, not a one-shot endpoint verdict.
Barrett's constructed-emotion account and Russell's circumplex model (Russell 2003, *Psych. Review*
110(1) — HIGH) further argue discrete verbal labels ("fulfilled") are ad hoc conceptual categories
applied at report-time on top of continuous core affect (valence×arousal); Nisbett & Wilson (1977,
*Psych. Review* 84(3) — HIGH) independently show verbal reports on mental states are frequently
constructed at the moment of report via implicit causal theory, not read directly off the underlying
process. A directly narrative-relevant computational precedent exists: Reagan, Mitchell, Kiley,
Danforth & Dodds (2016, *EPJ Data Science* 5(1), arXiv:1606.07772) operationalize exactly a
valence-over-narrative-time trajectory for stories and find it decomposes into six recurring shapes
— evidence a continuous arc representation is both principled and tractable, not merely theoretical.

Counter-evidence the program should not ignore: at the **causal/structural narrative-comprehension
level**, discrete Goal/Attempt/Outcome NODES are real, well-established psycholinguistic constructs
(Trabasso & van den Broek 1985, *J. Memory & Language* 24(5); Trabasso, van den Broek & Suh 1989,
*Discourse Processes* — HIGH). So discreteness at the STRUCTURAL level (there IS a Goal node and an
Outcome node in the causal graph) is defensible and matches the current architecture's extraction
step. What is NOT defensible is the current architecture's treatment of the LINK between those two
nodes as a binary match/mismatch: Trabasso & Sperry (1985, *J. Memory & Language* 24) and van den
Broek & Trabasso (1986, *Discourse Processes* 9) both show narrative importance/recall is driven by
a GRADED causal-connectivity count (number of direct causal connections), not a binary flag — HIGH
confidence, directly contradicts the class_relation same/opposed binary check currently gating the
main decision path.

**Reconciliation (the actually load-bearing finding for Q1):** Event Segmentation Theory says event
boundaries are generated by transient prediction-error spikes in an ongoing situation model (Zacks &
Swallow 2007, *Curr. Dir. Psych. Sci.*; Zacks, Speer, Swallow, Braver & Reynolds 2007, *Psych. Bull.*
133 — HIGH), that boundaries are hierarchical with SUPERORDINATE GOAL shifts producing the largest
boundaries (Zacks, Tversky & Iyer 2001, *JEP:General* — HIGH), and that Bayesian surprise
specifically predicts segmentation in story LISTENING (Kumar et al. 2023, *Cognitive Science* 47 —
MED-HIGH). Combined with Suh & Trabasso's finding that an unresolved goal stays MORE activated in
working memory than a satisfied one (1993, *J. Memory & Language* 32 — HIGH) and Gerrig & Bernardo's
"Suspense as Online Problem-Solving" model of continuously re-estimated solution-path likelihood
(1994, *Poetics* 22 — HIGH), the honest picture is: a discrete verdict-like state DOES crystallize —
but AT THE IN-NARRATIVE RESOLUTION MOMENT (a goal-shift event boundary), as a THRESHOLDING of a
continuously-updated trajectory, not as a single computation applied once at arbitrary end-of-text.
**This is the precise sense in which the current architecture's framing is a task artifact:** it is
discrete at the wrong TIME (whole-document, post-hoc) and the wrong REPRESENTATION (binary
class-match instead of thresholded continuous scalar), not wrong to output a discrete label per se.

Confidence: HIGH on each individual citation; MED on the reconciliation synthesis (own inference
connecting independently-verified literatures, capped at P=0.50 per novel-synthesis discipline).

## Q2 — Is goal-fulfillment-about-a-character a mentalizing/ToM computation the architecture is missing?

**Yes — this is the sharpest, most actionable finding in this drill, and the honest answer is that
the current architecture has NO analog of this leg at all, not a partial/unwired one.**

The mentalizing network (right temporo-parietal junction/RTPJ, medial PFC, precuneus/posterior
cingulate, posterior STS) is reliably and selectively recruited for reasoning about another agent's
beliefs/desires, distinct from reasoning about physical states (Saxe & Powell 2006, *Neuropsychologia*
— HIGH; Fletcher et al. 1995, *Cognition* — HIGH, first neuroimaging evidence specifically from
STORY comprehension). Frith & Frith (2003, *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B*) describe mPFC's role as a
"decoupling mechanism" — explicitly holding a represented-as-wanted state APART from the actual
state of the world. That is a structurally different computation from comparing two extracted text
spans for equality: it is holding two separate models (what the agent wants vs. what is true) and
reasoning about the relationship between them. Denny, Kober, Wager & Ochsner's meta-analysis (2012,
*J. Cogn. Neurosci.* 24(8)) found a ventral (self/value) vs. dorsal (other/mentalizing) mPFC gradient
— real but not a clean split (their own caveat) — so treat "two systems" as two ends of a gradient
with real functional specialization, not two fully separate modules; MED confidence on how cleanly
that separates in practice.

Double-dissociation evidence, while not from one single decisive study, converges from two
directions: ASD/right-TPJ-linked deficits impair ToM while sparing value-based decision-making —
Torralva et al. (2012, *Psychiatry Research*) found impaired ToM but INTACT Iowa Gambling Task
performance in Asperger syndrome, uncorrelated (HIGH). vmPFC/OFC lesions classically impair
value-based decision-making (Bechara/Damasio somatic-marker literature — HIGH, well-established) and
Shamay-Tsoory et al. (2007, *Neuropsychologia*) found VM-lesion patients specifically impaired on
AFFECTIVE ToM (faux pas, irony) while COGNITIVE/false-belief ToM was relatively spared (HIGH on the
finding; MED on how completely this maps to "fully intact"). Net: goal-*representation* (whose goal,
what would satisfy it) and outcome-*valuation* (was it good) are dissociable computations, supporting
the case for two separate mechanisms rather than one merged comparator — MED-HIGH.

**The concrete, glass-box-implementable mechanism this leg is missing:** Baker, Saxe & Tenenbaum
(2009, *Cognition* 113(3), "Action Understanding as Inverse Planning") formalize ToM as Bayesian
inversion of a rational-planning generative model — infer a POSTERIOR over the agent's utility
function from their observed actions, via explicit Bayes rule over a prior + likelihood (rational
planner). Jara-Ettinger, Gweon, Schulz & Tenenbaum (2016, *TICS*, "Naive Utility Calculus") extend
this to jointly infer costs and rewards as an explicit, formalizable latent utility function — HIGH
confidence, both directly verified, both standard glass-box Bayesian/POMDP formalisms with no LLM
requirement (WebPPL/probabilistic-program implementations are standard in this literature). Directly
on-point: Chandra, Chen, Grand, Pinto, Wu, Kleiman-Weiner, Tenenbaum & Andreas (2024, *Topics in
Cognitive Science* 16, "Storytelling as Inverse Inverse Planning") apply this exact apparatus to
NARRATIVE comprehension — modeling audience goal-inference over a character's actions, with irony
and dramatic tension arising from gaps between the audience's inferred goal-model and the character's
true goal — MED-HIGH (title/abstract-verified as directly on-topic; full-text depth on "fulfillment
judgment" specifically not independently confirmed this session, flagged UNVERIFIED on that
sub-point).

**Why this matters architecturally, not just theoretically:** the current `_class_relation` approach
represents a character's goal as a single desired-CLASS token and checks same/opposed against the
outcome's actual-class token. The inverse-planning literature says the brain-faithful representation
of "what this character wants" is closer to an inferred UTILITY/PREFERENCE FUNCTION — a (possibly
partial, multi-attribute, graded) scoring function over possible outcomes — and "fulfillment" is
EVALUATING the actual outcome under that function, not equality-testing two symbolic tokens. This
reframes the hand `CLASS_REGISTRY` ceiling (diagnosed 2026-08-08 as an OOV/coverage problem) as
ALSO a representational-mismatch problem: even with perfect coverage, a same/opposed class check
cannot express "this partially satisfies what she wanted" or "this satisfies it via an unexpected
route" the way a scored-utility-function evaluation can. Per the 2026-08-08 hard-residual drill,
lane D explicitly found no existing computational system solving the avoidance-polarity-to-outcome
matching problem — this inverse-planning/utility-function leg is a plausible, PRINCIPLED candidate
for that gap, not just for coverage.

Confidence: HIGH on all individual citations; MED-HIGH on the double-dissociation synthesis; the
architectural mapping recommendation is capped at P=0.50 (novel synthesis).

## Q3 — Where does the DesireDB ground truth come from, and is the residual irreducible?

**Partially irreducible — and DesireDB's own numbers make this a documented fact, not a hypothesis.**

DesireDB (Rahimtoroghi, Wu, Wang, Anand & Walker 2017, SIGDIAL, arXiv:1708.09040 — corrected
citation per the 2026-08-08 drill, Hobbs is NOT a co-author) used 3 MTurk annotators per instance,
majority vote for gold. Reported Krippendorff's α ≈ 0.63 (pairwise), 66% three-way total agreement,
per-label total-agreement of 75% (Fulfilled) / 67% (Unfulfilled) / only 41% (Unknown) — MED
confidence, numbers from AI-mediated secondary extraction (PDF resisted direct parse), internally
consistent across independent checks but not hand-verified against the primary PDF this session.
By Krippendorff's own published reliability guideline (α > 0.8 for confident conclusions, 0.67-0.8
"tentative," below that "unreliable"), α ≈ 0.63 sits at or below the tentative-reliability floor —
this is a MODERATE-agreement dataset by the standard the metric's own author set, not a clean gold
standard.

Critically, the disagreement is not random: when two annotators agreed Fulfilled, the dissenter
picked Unknown (not Unfulfilled) 64% of the time; for Unfulfilled-majority items, the dissenter
picked Unknown 49% of the time (MED confidence, same caveat). That is the textbook signature of
Signal Detection Theory (Green & Swets 1966 — HIGH, well-established framework) — a continuous
internal evidence variable plus a per-rater decision criterion, producing discrete responses whose
DISAGREEMENT concentrates at the low-confidence boundary rather than flipping polarity. Pavlick &
Kwiatkowski (2019, *TACL* 7, "Inherent Disagreements in Human Textual Inferences" — HIGH) show the
same pattern for NLI and argue explicitly that models should target the human judgment
*distribution*, not a collapsed majority label; Aroyo & Welty (2015, *AI Magazine* 36(1), "Truth Is a
Lie") make the general case that single ground-truth labels for subjective tasks are a methodological
fiction.

Two further findings sharpen this. First, Searle's satisfaction-conditions semantics (*Intentionality*,
1983 — HIGH, well-documented) gives the current match-architecture a genuine philosophical warrant:
a desire is satisfied iff the world comes to match its propositional content — this is NOT an
arbitrary target, it is a real formal semantics, and defends keeping SOME version of state-comparison
in the architecture. Second — and this is the finding that complicates any clean "pick architecture
A or B" answer — Chaturvedi, Goldwasser & Daumé III's companion dataset (2016 AAAI, "Ask, and Shall
You Receive?") reportedly used the CHARACTER'S OWN EMOTIONAL STATE as an annotation cue (MED
confidence, from secondary/abstract synthesis, not independently confirmed from full text). That
means real human annotators are not doing pure Searlean world-state matching; they are blending
objective outcome-matching WITH a mentalizing/simulated-affect read of the character. **The ground
truth itself is already a hybrid of the state-comparison and mentalizing computations this note is
asking the architecture to add** — meaning neither a pure bottom-up matcher nor a pure top-down
utility-inference engine will fully explain the label; both legs are needed BECAUSE both legs are in
the human judgment that produced the label.

Schank & Abelson script theory (1977) and Bower, Black & Turner (1979, *Cognitive Psychology* 11)
establish that comprehension routinely fills gaps with reader-variable world/script knowledge — HIGH
— consistent with some fraction of disagreement being genuine interpretive variance rather than pure
noise. No paper decomposing DesireDB's disagreement into "annotator error" vs. "genuine
world-knowledge variance" was found (searched directly — UNVERIFIED/absent), so the exact split is
not quantifiable from published work; the qualitative claim (SOME real fraction is irreducible) is
well-supported, the precise proportion is not.

**Bottom line for Q3:** treating 100%-vs-gold-label as the success criterion is demonstrably wrong —
DesireDB's own annotators do not achieve anything close to 100% agreement with each other. The
honest, defensible success bar is inter-annotator agreement (≈0.63 α / 66% three-way, or whatever the
equivalent is for the eval corpus currently in use), and continuing to invest build cycles chasing
performance meaningfully ABOVE that ceiling is chasing noise in the label, not missing comprehension.

Confidence: HIGH on the general SDT/disagreement-as-signal framework and on Searle's semantics; MED
on the exact DesireDB numbers (secondary extraction); MED on the Chaturvedi emotional-state-cue
claim (unconfirmed from primary text) — flagged accordingly, but the qualitative conclusion (labels
are not clean, part of the residual is irreducible) holds even discounting the specific numbers.

## Q4 — The hard verdict: which architecture direction is right?

**Ranked recommendation, brain-foundational justification:**

| Rank | Option | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **(c) deeper reframe — but scoped as a targeted 2-piece EXTENSION of the current reuse-heavy pipeline, not a rebuild** | RECOMMENDED |
| 2 | (d) partially right — the discrete gold label should not be the success criterion (fold into rank-1's calibration step) | ADOPT AS SUB-CLAUSE, not standalone |
| 3 | (b) top-down goal-conditioned reframe alone | INSUFFICIENT unless it explicitly includes rank-1's two additions |
| 4 | (a) stay the course (binary class-relation as the main decision path) | REJECTED — contradicted by Trabasso/Sperry graded-connectivity evidence AND by the entirely-missing mentalizing leg |

**Why not (a):** the causal-connectivity literature (Trabasso & Sperry 1985; van den Broek & Trabasso
1986) directly contradicts binary same/opposed matching as the psycholinguistically correct
mechanism for goal-outcome linkage strength — and per Q2, there is no representation at all of the
goal as an evaluable utility/preference structure, only a class token. Two independent literatures
say this is wrong, not just incomplete.

**Why not (b) alone:** "top-down goal-conditioned interpretation" could mean using the extracted goal
to guide extraction/disambiguation of the outcome text (a genuinely useful, defensible fix — it's
consistent with situation-model literature showing goal-relatedness biases online processing, Zwaan
Langston & Graesser 1995). But if the FINAL decision step remains a binary class-relation check on
better-extracted tokens, it inherits the same two failures: no graded congruence signal, no
utility-function evaluation. The reframe is necessary infrastructure, not sufficient on its own.

**Why (c), scoped:** per the 2026-08-08 formalize note, the pipeline is already reuse-heavy (part-1
goal-recognition, situation-model register, did-it-happen occurrence-gate are owned and working).
The two genuinely missing/wrong pieces, both independently lit-supported:

1. **Replace the binary `_class_relation` same/opposed check with a graded congruence scalar** —
   causal-connectivity-weighted (count/strength of causally-connected supporting vs. opposing
   clauses between goal and outcome nodes, reusing the existing situation-model register's bind
   structure) or appraisal-goal-conduciveness-scored. This is a natural extension of the ALREADY
   half-wired Channel-B force-dynamics primitive (2026-08-06/2026-08-08 notes) from categorical to
   continuous output — small code-surface change, strong lit support (Trabasso & Sperry; Scherer
   CPM), addresses Q1's finding directly.
2. **Add a minimal goal-as-preference-structure evaluation leg** — even a simplified,
   glass-box-compatible version of Baker/Saxe/Tenenbaum inverse planning / Jara-Ettinger Naive
   Utility Calculus: decompose the stated desire into a small set of attribute-predicates (what
   would count as satisfying THIS goal — full/partial/via-unexpected-route), and SCORE the extracted
   outcome against that set rather than requiring exact class-token equality. This is the leg found
   ENTIRELY ABSENT in Q2 — highest-novelty, highest-risk (no existing computational system solves the
   avoidance-polarity case per the 2026-08-08 lane-D search), but the most theoretically grounded new
   direction this drill surfaced. Chandra et al. 2024's direct narrative application is the concrete
   prior-art anchor to study before implementing.

**Fold in (d):** recalibrate what "success" means. Per Q3, target inter-annotator agreement on the
eval corpus (compute or estimate it if not already known), not 100%-vs-gold. Decompose the current
"45% hard residual" into (i) the portion attributable to genuine irreducible interpretive/script
variance (bounded above by the annotation-disagreement ceiling — not fixable by any architecture)
and (ii) the portion attributable to the two missing pieces above (fixable). Continuing to chase
(i) with more architecture is the single highest-risk way to burn cycles on an artifact.

## Cheap decisive test

Two cheap, script-only reanalysis probes over already-extracted structures — no new architecture
needed to run either:

**Probe 1 (graded vs. binary congruence).** On the existing hard-residual test cohort (2026-08-08's
4 failure types) and/or the 96%-abstain modern-narrative cohort (formalize_narrative_part2), for
every item where `_class_relation` currently ABSTAINS or gets it WRONG: hand-score (or script from
the already-extracted situation-model register) a simple graded signal — count of causally-connected
supporting clauses minus opposing clauses between the goal node and outcome node. Check whether the
SIGN of that graded scalar predicts the correct verdict direction better than the current binary
check.

**Probe 2 (utility-function proxy vs. class-token match).** On the same abstain/wrong cohort: for
each item, decompose the stated desire into 2-4 simple attribute-predicates by hand (e.g. "win the
race" → {finish-first, beat-opponent}), and check whether the extracted outcome text satisfies ANY
predicate in that set (not requiring exact class match). Compare recovery rate against the current
class-token-equality check.

Both probes are pure reanalysis of data already on disk — cost is a few hours of scripted/manual
scoring, no new extraction pipeline, no new training.

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS (Probe 1 — graded congruence):** the causal-connectivity-sign scalar correctly predicts
the verdict direction on ≥50% of the items where the current binary class-relation check abstains or
errs, using ONLY structure already present in the situation-model register (no new extraction).
Confirms Q1's claim that discreteness-at-the-wrong-level, not missing information, is (part of) the
bottleneck.

**HARD-FAIL (Probe 1):** the graded scalar does no better than chance (≈50/50 direction match) on the
same cohort — signals the wall is genuinely extraction-coverage/OOV (as previously diagnosed
2026-08-07/08), not a matching-vs-scoring architecture problem, and the graded-congruence build
should be deprioritized in favor of the already-identified VerbNet/Levin and PDTB-connective
extraction fixes.

**HARD-PASS (Probe 2 — utility-function proxy):** the attribute-predicate-satisfaction check recovers
correct verdicts on ≥40% of items where class-token equality currently fails specifically due to
novel/OOV outcome vocabulary (not due to genuinely ambiguous/hard-residual cases) — confirms Q2's
claim that a preference-structure evaluation, not just better coverage of the SAME class-token
mechanism, is the right fix.

**HARD-FAIL (Probe 2):** predicate-satisfaction recovery is statistically indistinguishable from
simply expanding the hand CLASS_REGISTRY with more synonyms — signals the "missing mentalizing leg"
diagnosis is over-theorized and the mundane coverage-backoff work already in the pipeline
(2026-08-07 VerbNet/Levin) is sufficient; the utility-function build should be shelved, not built.

**HARD-FAIL on the calibration claim (Q3, independent of Probes 1/2):** if a future eval shows the
current OR reframed architecture scoring meaningfully BELOW the DesireDB inter-annotator agreement
ceiling (well under ~0.60-0.63-equivalent, with a large gap, not a small one) — that would falsify
this note's claim that the residual is substantially annotation-ceiling-bounded, and would mean the
architecture gap is larger than this note estimates. Conversely, if performance is already CLOSE to
that ceiling, further architecture investment aimed at closing the LAST few points is the
artifact-chasing this note warns against — that comparison should gate any decision to keep building
past whatever the current macro-F1/accuracy is.
