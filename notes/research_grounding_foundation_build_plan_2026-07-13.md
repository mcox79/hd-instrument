## HEADLINE

The theory is settled enough to build on without further lit-scanning: grounding requires (1) a channel that is not derivable from the existing relational structure, (2) a check that can be surprised/refuted by that channel (a verifiable oracle), and (3) every claimed-grounded node must be finitely composition-reachable back to a node holding such a channel value, or it is a closed relational island (Bender-Koller's "octopus," formally reconfirmed this week via percolation/reachability audit design). The NEW finding this drill adds that most sharpens the "what to bake in first" decision: real, bulk-downloadable, MEASURED (not rated, not encyclopedic) numeric physical-property sources for genuinely common-vocabulary concepts are much thinner on the ground than the psycholinguistic-norms literature already tapped — only USDA FoodData Central and PubChem clear the bar of (freely bulk-downloadable AND large AND commonsense-vocabulary-overlapping); YCB and the new CLAMP haptic dataset are excellent-quality but small or access-unverified; NIST/MatWeb/engineering tables are high-trust but narrow or gated. This means the "real measured physical attribute" bake-in is real and buildable, but at everyday-object scale it is closer to a few-hundred-to-low-thousand-entry curated core than a sweeping tens-of-thousands ingest — reinforcing, from a completely different angle (data-source scarcity, not theory), the same conclusion the prior notes reached from spanning-basis and percolation theory: **start with a small, clean, well-measured core, not a broad thin one.** On the wiring side, fractional power encoding (FPE) gives a mature, glass-box, decades-old (Plate) plus recently-formalized (Frady/Kleyko/Sommer "Vector Function Architecture") mechanism for binding a continuous measured value into the same algebra as discrete symbol binding, with a known, controllable precision/spread tradeoff — this is the missing wiring piece the prior notes did not specify at the hypervector level.

## Cheap decisive test (pre-registered — this is the FIRST buildable cell)

**Recommended FIRST STEP: the periodic-table cross-channel grounding-mechanism cell**, already designed at the decision level in `grounding_anchor_design_first_testbed_2026-07-10.md` but not yet wired to a concrete VSA encoding — this drill supplies that missing piece (Section 3 below) and is what makes it dispatch-ready.

- **Channel A (relational, native):** group, period, bonds-with, same-family edges — a small graph (~118 nodes), exactly the substrate's native symbolic form.
- **Channel B (measured, exterior, BAKE-IN):** atomic mass, electronegativity, atomic radius, ionization energy, melting point — genuinely measured physical constants, real numbers with units, freely available (IUPAC/NIST/standard periodic-table reference data, a few hundred datapoints, trivially bulk-obtainable, no license risk, no LLM).
- **Encoding (the new wiring piece, Section 3):** each numeric property FPE-bound (fractional power of a fixed base hypervector, per Plate/Frady-Kleyko-Sommer) into a per-element attribute vector, bundled with the element's existing relational/symbolic binding — inspectable, not learned.
- **Oracle (the push-back):** periodic law — properties trend lawfully across periods/down groups. Predict a held-out element's property from its consolidated neighbors; check against the TRUE measured value.
- **Decisive ablation:** consolidate on relational Channel A alone (no numeric binding) vs A+B. HARD-PASS(grounding-is-real-and-VSA-wireable) requires A+B beats A-alone AND beats a mean/degree baseline AND the FPE-decoded prediction correlates with the true value at a rate that decays smoothly with periodic-table distance (the FPE similarity-preserves-distance property, independently checkable) AND is degree-invariant (rare elements as well-placed as common ones). HARD-FAIL: A-alone ties A+B (numeric channel not load-bearing), or FPE-decoded predictions show no smooth distance-similarity relationship (the encoding, not the grounding claim, is broken — a different failure to distinguish from a real grounding negative), or the oracle check is at chance.

This is unchanged in substance from the prior grounding_anchor_design note's decision — what's new is that Section 3 below makes the VSA encoding concrete enough to actually implement, closing the one open dependency (how does a measured scalar become a hypervector that composes with the existing bind/bundle algebra) that was previously undetermined.

## Falsifiable predictions (HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL, consolidated)

**HARD-PASS (mechanism proven, proceed to Phase 2):**
1. A+B consolidated geometry beats A-alone on held-out numeric-property prediction by a nontrivial margin, not just at the mean but on the degree-stratified (rare-element) tail.
2. FPE-decoded similarity between two elements' encoded values degrades monotonically (or near-monotonically) with true numeric distance — confirms the encoding itself is doing real metric work, not just serving as an opaque tag.
3. Ablating channel B collapses prediction to the mean/degree baseline — proves the exterior channel, not the relational structure alone, carries the information.
4. Difficulty-stratified: held-out interior elements (smooth periodic-law regions) AND held-out boundary/isolated elements (harder cases) both clear the bar — not just the easy interior (guards against the prior note's Risk #2, oracle-leak-via-smoothness).

**HARD-FAIL (do not proceed to Phase 2/3 until diagnosed):**
1. A-alone ties or beats A+B — numeric channel is not exogenously load-bearing; the mechanism, not just this domain, needs rework before any broader bake-in effort is worth funding.
2. FPE similarity does not decay with numeric distance — the wiring is broken (fix the encoding, e.g. wrong base-vector spread/precision tradeoff per Section 3, before concluding anything about grounding itself).
3. Held-out boundary/isolated elements fail while interior elements pass — oracle-leak via local smoothness, not genuine grounding; report as a leak diagnosis, not a mechanism failure.
4. **(New, per Section 3's bundling-degradation finding, arXiv:2412.00488)** raw FPE decode fails to recover a usable value after this cell's actual bundle size (5 numeric properties + relational bindings per element) — this is a DECODE/CLEANUP failure, not a grounding failure; the required diagnostic is to re-run with a cleanup step (CSim-style resonator cleanup, or fall back to level-hypervector encoding) before concluding the numeric channel itself is uninformative. Do not conflate "FPE decode degraded under this bundle size" with "grounding doesn't work" — they are different claims and the cell must report which one occurred.

## 3. VSA wiring — how a measured numeric attribute becomes a hypervector (new this drill, sub-agent-verified)

**Mechanism: fractional power encoding (FPE) / Vector Function Architecture (VFA)**, established by Plate (*Holographic Reduced Representations*, 1994/2003, continuous-value chapters; PDF: redwood.berkeley.edu) and formalized/extended by Frady, Kleyko, Kymn, Olshausen & Sommer, "Computing on Functions Using Randomized Vector Representations," arXiv:2109.03429 (NICE 2022, ACM DL 10.1145/3517343.3522597). A fixed random base hypervector **b** is raised to a real-valued fractional power v — in FHRR this is literal complex exponentiation of per-dimension phase angles (θᵢ → θᵢ·v) — encoding scalar v as **b**^v. Verified properties:
- **Glass-box / inspectable, not learned**: **b**^v is a closed-form function of v and a fixed random base vector — no training, fully deterministic, matching this project's conventions (explicit dtypes, passed-in generators, inspectable representations).
- **Similarity IS a designed kernel, not merely "smooth"**: FPE is mathematically equivalent to Random Fourier Features (Bochner's theorem) — cosine similarity between **b**^v1 and **b**^v2 equals the Fourier transform of the distribution the base vector's per-dimension frequencies are drawn from, evaluated at (v1-v2). This is a rigorous, not approximate, result, empirically confirmed on regression/density-estimation/image tasks in the VFA paper. **The spread/precision tradeoff is exact, not a vague caveat**: a wider frequency distribution gives a narrower (higher-resolution, less-reach) kernel; a narrower frequency distribution gives a broader, lower-resolution kernel. This is a chosen kernel bandwidth, calibrate on Phase 1's known property ranges, not swept blind.
- **Composes with the existing discrete bind/bundle algebra with no separate code path**: VFA papers explicitly frame FPE outputs as ordinary VSA vectors — they bind (circular-convolution/complex-multiplication) to a role vector (e.g. HAS-ATTRIBUTE(density)) and bundle into an entity's existing relational binding using the SAME operators already used for discrete role-filler structure (Kanerva's own "Dollar of Mexico" record, AAAI Fall Symposium 2010, is the original published precedent for exactly this kind of mixed continuous+discrete role/filler record).
- **A concrete, real, MUST-FLAG risk that changes the Phase-1 risk register: FPE-continuous bindings degrade fast under bundling, empirically measured, not theoretical.** A 2024 follow-up, "Improved Cleanup and Decoding of Fractional Power Encodings" (arXiv:2412.00488), directly measured this: after only **two** bundling/query steps, the original scalar can no longer be recovered by naive cleanup at 1024-dim, bundle sizes 5-13 — phase-error distributions broaden fast with bundle size. Their corrective method (CSim resonator-style cleanup) restores reliability but is an ADD-ON, not automatic. **This means Phase 1 must include a cleanup/decode step as a first-class design element, not assume raw FPE decode works out of the box** — this is new information this drill surfaces that none of the prior five notes had (they designed the periodic-table test at the mechanism-decision level, before this specific empirical degradation result existed in scope).
- **Alternative, coarser mechanism for comparison**: level-hypervectors / thermometer codes (Kanerva's own record examples; standard practice in EEG/biosignal HDC classifiers, e.g. arXiv:2205.07654 seizure detection) quantize a scalar range into bins with monotonically-decreasing similarity by bin-distance — simpler, more robust to bundling degradation (coarser resolution, no phase-wrapping risk), but similarity decays roughly piecewise/linearly rather than following a designed kernel. A 2023/2026-era survey (arXiv:2308.00685, "Learning from Hypervectors") catalogs level-codes and thermometer-quantization (TQHD) as the hardware-friendly, degradation-robust alternative. **Given the newly-surfaced FPE bundling-degradation risk, Phase 1 should smoke BOTH encodings side by side** (FPE for its exact kernel/precision, level-code as the robustness fallback) rather than commit to FPE alone before the empirical comparison is run on this substrate's actual bundle sizes.
- **Capacity/crosstalk at mixed discrete+continuous scale — genuinely open.** No source found stress-tests a large discrete symbolic store contaminated with a SMALL number of FPE-bound numeric attributes at production scale; the 2412.00488 degradation data (5-13 bundle sizes) is the closest empirical proxy, not a direct measurement of our regime. Measure directly in Phase 1's smoke.

**What this proves vs. the pure-symbol baseline, concretely:** the pure-symbol ConceptNet-style graph has no mechanism at all for representing "electronegativity = 3.44" as anything other than an opaque unstructured metadata field (which the zero-grounded-data finding already established is empty everywhere). FPE (with level-code as a robustness fallback, and CSim-style cleanup if bundling degradation bites) gives a specific, closed-form, decades-precedented way to make that number a first-class hypervector that (a) is inspectable, (b) has an exact, designed metric structure, and (c) binds into the same algebra already used for `IS_A`/`SYNONYM` edges — this is the concrete mechanism answer to "how do grounded attributes bind into a high-dim VSA substrate," which none of the five prior notes this week specified at this level, and it now comes with a known, empirically-measured failure mode (bundling degradation) that Phase 1 must design around rather than discover the hard way.

## Honest gaps / deflation

- The FPE mechanism is mature and well-precedented FOR SINGLE/LOW-DIMENSIONAL continuous encoding; it has not been validated in THIS substrate's specific FHRR/HRR configuration, at THIS project's specific N, mixed with THIS project's specific discrete-symbol capacity regime. Treat Phase 1 as the validating smoke, not an assumed-safe extension.
- The spread/precision hyperparameter is a real, unresolved calibration question — cheap to sweep, but unswept.
- Numeric physical-property source coverage at everyday-object scale is genuinely thinner than the psycholinguistic-norms literature (FoodData Central and PubChem are the only two confirmed free-bulk-large-commonsense-overlapping sources found; several promising leads — CLAMP, MatWeb — are access/format-unverified or gated).
