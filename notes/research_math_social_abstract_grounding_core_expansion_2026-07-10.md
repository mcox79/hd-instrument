# Research: why math is anti-grounded to a sensorimotor core, and how to extend the core to span math/social/abstract

Date: 2026-07-10
Mode: self-executed (no sub-agent fan-out per explicit dispatch constraint). WebSearch only, generic public-domain cognitive-science/psycholinguistics terms — no substrate-novel mechanism names, configs, or numbers went off-platform.

## HEADLINE

The VET's per-domain cosine result (EMOTIONAL +0.48, PHYSICAL +0.27, SOCIAL +0.015, MATHEMATICAL -0.43) is exactly what the cognitive-science literature predicts from a **sensorimotor-only** core: brains ground perceptual/action concepts through direct sensorimotor engagement, but ground number, social relations, and other abstractions through **structural/relational mechanisms that are not sensorimotor at all** — an independent magnitude sense (Approximate Number System / mental number line), metaphorical structure-mapping onto spatial-motor image schemas (Lakoff & Nunez's "grounding metaphors" for arithmetic), a dedicated social-relational coordinate system (power x affiliation, hippocampal-entorhinal "social space"), and introspective/affective simulation (Barsalou) for abstraction generally. A sensorimotor-norms-only core has **no channel that these mechanisms could load onto**, so orthogonal-to-negative cosine is the expected signature, not a decoder failure and not a fundamentally ungroundable domain. The fix is a **core-expansion**, not a decoder fix: add measured MAGNITUDE/ORDINALITY and SOCIAL-RELATIONAL (power x affiliation) attribute channels alongside the existing sensorimotor + affective channels (same architecture pattern as the existing Lancaster/VAD spokes), and reserve an explicit METAPHOR-MAPPING structural bridge (concrete image-schema source -> abstract target, as a graph edge type, not a scalar channel) for the residual higher-abstraction concepts (justice, causation, infinity) that the literature itself flags as resisting scalar-channel grounding.

## 1. Why is math ANTI-grounded to a sensorimotor core — brain-first

**The core observation the literature converges on: sensorimotor grounding and mathematical grounding are TWO DIFFERENT MECHANISMS, not the same mechanism at different strengths.** A sensorimotor-strength core (vision/audition/touch/taste/smell/interoception + mouth/hand/foot/head/torso action strength, the Lynott & Connell Lancaster Sensorimotor Norms design) is measuring "how much do I *perceive or physically act on* this referent." Numbers and mathematical objects have essentially zero content on that axis by construction — you do not see, hear, taste, or manipulate "seven" or "the derivative." A near-zero-to-negative cosine against a purely perceptual/action core is the textbook expected signature for a domain that is grounded through a *different* channel entirely, not evidence the domain resists grounding altogether. Four independent literatures converge on what that different channel is:

1. **Embodied-metaphor grounding of mathematics (Lakoff & Nunez, *Where Mathematics Comes From*, 2000).** Lakoff and Nunez's central claim is that abstract mathematical structure arises from **conceptual metaphor** — a structure-preserving mapping ("grounding metaphor") from a well-understood embodied source domain onto a mathematical target domain, not from any direct perceptual content of the target itself. They identify four specific grounding metaphors for arithmetic: **Object Collection** (numbers = collections; magnitude = collection size), **Object Construction** (numbers = wholes built from parts), the **Measuring-Stick metaphor**, and **Motion Along a Path** (the latter two both ground the number line: position = magnitude, forward motion = addition). Critically, what gets preserved across the mapping is *inferential structure* (path-motion's ordering and composition properties), not sensory content — this is why math loads onto **spatial/motor image-schema** structure (containment, path, iteration, part-whole) and not onto raw visual/tactile/auditory strength. Lakoff and Nunez extend the same mechanism to higher math (sets grounded in the container schema, infinitesimals grounded in perception of continuous motion). [Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Where_Mathematics_Comes_From), [PMC7667247 — "Metaphor and the Philosophical Implications of Embodied Mathematics"](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7667247/), [4 grounding metaphors overview](https://xqsolution.wordpress.com/2010/09/15/lakoff-and-nunezs-4-gs/)

2. **The Approximate Number System (ANS) and mental number line (Dehaene).** Humans (and many non-human animals) possess an evolutionarily ancient, pre-linguistic "number sense" — a primitive for estimating and comparing magnitudes (subitizing for small sets; Weber-fraction-scaled discrimination for larger sets), instantiated in the intraparietal sulcus (IPS). Dehaene's **Triple-Code Model** treats *analog magnitude* as a representational format in its own right, structurally parallel to (but distinct from) the verbal and Arabic-symbolic codes, with its own dedicated neural substrate — it is functionally "a sixth sense," not a derivative of vision/touch/audition. The mental number line is logarithmically compressed and its precision improves through childhood to ~15% adult Weber fraction. This is the direct evidence that **magnitude is a grounding primitive that stands alongside, not underneath, the classical sensorimotor modalities** — a sensorimotor core that omits a magnitude channel is missing exactly the primitive that grounds number. [Wikipedia — ANS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Approximate_number_system), [Mental number line overview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/psychology/mental-number-line), [Grokipedia — numerical cognition](https://grokipedia.com/page/Numerical_cognition)

3. **SNARC (spatial-numerical association of response codes).** Numbers are spatially mapped (small=left, large=right in left-to-right cultures) not because of any physical resemblance, but because of their **ordinal position on a mental number line** — recent work (Casasanto & Pitt) argues it is specifically ordinality, not cardinal magnitude, that drives the spatial mapping, and cardinal vs. ordinal SNARC effects appear to rely on partially dissociable mechanisms. This reinforces that number's "spatial grounding" is a **relational/structural** code (rank order embedded in space), not a perceptual-content code — consistent with mechanism (1) and (2), and further evidence against expecting math to correlate with a perceptual-strength core. [Royal Society Open Science — "One and only SNARC?"](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rsos/article/12/1/241585/93023/One-and-only-SNARC-Spatial-Numerical-Associations-are-not-fully-flexible-and-depend-on-both-relative-and-absolute-number-magnitude), [PMC11709453](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11709453/)

4. **Barsalou grounded cognition — abstract concepts generally.** Barsalou's Perceptual Symbol Systems framework, extended to abstract concepts, holds that abstraction is grounded via **simulations of introspective experience, affect, and situated social/event structure** — not perceptual simulation of the concept's "appearance." Abstract concepts are reported to be *more* strongly associated with emotion, social cognition, and internal/mental states than concrete concepts are, and multimodal simulators represent "objects, events, actions, introspections, and relational properties" as a unified mechanism, of which sensorimotor content is only one component. Pecher & Zeelenberg's "Boundaries to grounding abstract concepts" adds an important caution: grounding is context-dependent and optional even for concrete concepts, and some abstract concepts (their examples: truth, justice) may lack *any* consistent embodied correlate across contexts — language and convention do genuinely independent representational work that no embodied channel captures. This is the literature's own falsifiable boundary claim (folded into Section 4 below as a legitimate HARD-FAIL path, not a reason to expect the whole domain to close). [Barsalou 2010, Topics in Cognitive Science](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1756-8765.2010.01115.x), [Grounded Cognition, Annual Reviews](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev.psych.59.103006.093639), [Boundaries to grounding abstract concepts, PMC6015828](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6015828)

**Is abstract grounding STRUCTURAL/RELATIONAL rather than perceptual? Yes — this is the single load-bearing conclusion across all four threads.** Math is grounded via structure-preserving metaphorical mapping onto spatial-motor schemas (not raw sensory content); number specifically has an independent, non-sensorimotor magnitude primitive; and abstraction generally is grounded via introspective/affective/social simulation, not perceptual simulation. None of these mechanisms would be expected to produce positive cosine against a purely perceptual-strength core — negative-to-zero is the predicted signature, and the observed MATHEMATICAL -0.43 / SOCIAL +0.015 pattern is consistent with (not contradicting) this literature.

## 2. What measured/structural signal grounds the missing domains

**MATH (quantity/magnitude/ordinality):**
- **Troche, Crutch & Reilly (2013), "Abstract Conceptual Feature (ACF) ratings"** — 12-dimension rating scheme for ~400 abstract/concrete concepts including a dedicated **quantity** dimension explicitly capturing "size, amount, or scope" (7-point Likert, "I relate this word to size, amount, or scope"), alongside social interaction, morality, time, space, emotion, and thought. Hierarchical clustering found quantity to be one of three emergent factors (alongside perceptual salience and emotion/social cognition), i.e., empirically distinct from the perceptual dimensions. [PMC3662089](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3662089/)
- **A computable (not crowd-elicited) magnitude/ordinality signal for the numeral/quantity vocabulary itself.** Unlike every other missing domain, MATH has a special practical advantage: for the sub-vocabulary of number words and quantity terms (seven, dozen, million, few, several, first, second, most), magnitude and ordinal rank are **formally definable, not merely rateable** — no norming study is needed to know that "million" > "dozen" or that "third" follows "second." This is a near-zero-cost channel to seed before any human-norm elicitation.
- **Binder et al. (2016) 65-feature brain-based componential semantics** includes explicit non-perceptual dimensions (Cognition, Causal, Time) that partially cover magnitude-adjacent structure for non-numeral abstract concepts, though it does not isolate "quantity" as cleanly as the ACF ratings. [arXiv:1711.05516](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.05516)

**SOCIAL (relational, not just affective):**
- **Tavares, Schafer, Constantinescu, Behrens et al. (Neuron 2015, "A Map for Social Navigation in the Human Brain")** and its grid-code follow-up (Park, Constantinescu, Behrens, eLife 2021, "Distance and grid-like codes support navigation of abstract social space") establish that the hippocampal-entorhinal cognitive-map system encodes social knowledge along **two orthogonal continuous axes: power/dominance and affiliation/warmth**, jointly forming a 2D "social space" that people use to locate others relative to themselves — directly analogous to spatial grid coding, but for social relations, not physical space. [PMC9527473](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9527473/), [PMC11178359](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11178359/), [Science 2026 — hippocampal social coding in bats](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adk9385)
- This maps directly onto a **much older, already-instrumented psychometric structure: the Interpersonal Circumplex** (Leary 1957; Wiggins) — the vertical axis is dominance/control/power, the horizontal axis is warmth/affiliation/solidarity, together spanning eight "octants." Standardized rating instruments already exist for this space (Interpersonal Adjective Scales, IPIP-IPC) and could supply a ready-made word/trait-level power x affiliation coordinate — the SOCIAL-domain analog of VAD norms, with the advantage of being a mature, decades-validated 2D space rather than a novel construct. [Interpersonal circumplex overview](https://grokipedia.com/page/Interpersonal_circumplex)
- Binder et al.'s **Social** and **Human** feature dimensions provide a second, already-collected proxy across 535 concepts, with neuropsychological double-dissociation evidence (social vs. non-social abstract word processing) supporting sociality as a genuinely distinct organizing dimension in abstract semantic space. [Nature Sci Reports 2021 — social/emotion dimensional organization](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-02824-9), [PMC8651696](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8651696/)

**ABSTRACT (general, beyond math/social):**
- **Warriner, Kuperman & Brysbaert (2013) VAD norms** (valence, arousal, dominance for 13,915 English lemmas) — the large, mature, freely available affective-norm dataset that operationalizes Barsalou's affective/introspective grounding channel; already identified as a candidate channel in the companion same-day note on ATL-hub multi-attribute fusion (`notes/research_multi_attribute_grounding_fusion_ATL_hub_2026-07-10.md`). [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13428-012-0314-x)
- **Binder et al.'s Cognition, Emotion, Drive, Attention, Causal, Time** dimensions target the introspective/situational-simulation grounding Barsalou's model predicts.
- **Metaphor-mapping resources as a structural (not scalar) dataset**: the Master Metaphor List / MetaNet project (Lakoff's group) and metaphor-identification corpora (VU Amsterdam Metaphor Corpus, MIPVU) catalog explicit concrete-source -> abstract-target conceptual-metaphor mappings and could seed an explicit structural bridge table for concepts whose grounding is a specific mapping (e.g. TIME IS SPACE, ARGUMENT IS WAR) rather than a graded attribute value.

## 3. How to extend the spanning core — attribute channels vs. metaphor-structural bridge

**Both mechanisms are needed, but for different parts of the residual gap, and they are not interchangeable:**

**(A) ADD MEASURED ATTRIBUTE CHANNELS** — the cheap, well-precedented, architecture-compatible move. Widen the exterior-attribute vector with:
- a **MAGNITUDE/ORDINALITY** channel (ACF quantity ratings for general abstract vocabulary; formally-computed magnitude/rank for the numeral sub-vocabulary — near-zero cost there)
- a **SOCIAL-RELATIONAL (power x affiliation)** channel, from Interpersonal Circumplex-style trait/word ratings or Binder's Social/Human features — two dimensions, not one, matching the brain's actual 2D social-space code
- confirm/extend the **affective/introspective** channel (VAD norms + Binder Cognition/Emotion/Drive/Attention) already flagged as a fusion candidate in the companion ATL-hub note

This is architecturally identical to how the existing sensorimotor (Lancaster) channel was added — literally three more "spokes" feeding the same hub-and-spoke convergence structure already chosen for the multi-attribute-fusion cell. It requires no new graph-edge machinery, reuses the existing diffusion/consolidation pipeline, and is directly, cheaply testable against the same cosine-reach metric that exposed the gap. **This should ship first.**

**(B) METAPHOR/ANALOGY STRUCTURAL BRIDGE** — reserved for the residual concepts whose grounding is not a graded "how much does this concept engage domain X" scalar but a specific **structure-preserving mapping** to a named concrete source schema: higher mathematics (limits, infinity, complex numbers — Lakoff/Nunez's extended grounding metaphors), abstract logical/causal relations (causation, implication, justice), and any concept the literature explicitly flags as lacking a consistent embodied correlate (Pecher & Zeelenberg's "truth," "justice" examples). Mechanically this is a **new edge type in the graph** (target concept --GROUNDED_VIA_METAPHOR--> source image-schema node: PATH, CONTAINER, FORCE, COLLECTION), not a new scalar attribute value, reusing the graph-diffusion machinery the same way the ATL-hub note's "literal hub node" option (3) does, but with metaphor-source schemas as some of the hub's spokes. A flat scalar channel would flatten away exactly the relational content that makes this grounding mechanism work (addition maps to path-motion, not to "some quantity of path-ness") — this is why (B) cannot be collapsed into (A) for this residual set.

**Recommended sequencing**: ship (A) first — it is the lower-risk, same-architecture, directly-comparable-to-the-existing-cosine-test move, and per Section 1 it should already resolve MATH's basic quantity/ordinality gap and most of SOCIAL's gap. Scope (B) specifically to whatever MATH/ABSTRACT residual remains after (A) — expect a genuine nonzero residual per Pecher & Zeelenberg, and treat that residual as a bounded finding (Section 4 HARD-FAIL), not a bug to keep chasing indefinitely.

## 4. Fair test — HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL (extends the existing grounding-reach test's per-domain + narrow-core-must-fail controls)

**Cheap decisive test (pre-registered):** add the MAGNITUDE/ORDINALITY channel (ACF quantity ratings + computed numeral magnitude) and the SOCIAL-RELATIONAL power x affiliation channel (Interpersonal Circumplex-style or Binder Social/Human) to the existing spanning core, then re-run the identical per-domain grounding-reach cosine test (same probe sets, same methodology that produced EMOTIONAL +0.48 / PHYSICAL +0.27 / SOCIAL +0.015 / MATHEMATICAL -0.43) on MATHEMATICAL and SOCIAL domains, held out from whatever data fit the new channels.

**HARD-PASS (channel-expansion fix is real) — require ALL of:**
1. MATHEMATICAL domain reach moves from -0.43 to positive, clearing a bar set below the weakest already-reaching domain (PHYSICAL +0.27) to reflect calibration-penalty discipline: **>= +0.15** on held-out probes.
2. SOCIAL domain reach improves materially from +0.015 to **>= +0.15** on held-out probes.
3. **Scrambled-attribute control** (magnitude/social channel values randomly permuted across concepts before the same diffusion/cosine pipeline) stays at or below 0 for both domains — rules out the gain being dimensionality inflation rather than genuine channel content.
4. **Per-channel ablation**: zeroing the new magnitude channel alone collapses MATH's gain by a nontrivial amount (>=50% relative), and zeroing the new social-relational channel alone (while KEEPING the affective/VAD channel) collapses SOCIAL's gain by a nontrivial amount (>=50% relative) — this specifically rules out SOCIAL's gain being relabeled EMOTIONAL reach (affect and social-relational content are known to correlate per Binder's social/emotion covariance; this ablation is the required disambiguation).
5. Result holds on the median across seeds, not a single lucky seed (per the seed-fragility discipline already established in the companion ATL-hub fusion note).

**HARD-FAIL (channel-expansion does not close the gap, or a genuine bounded finding) — ANY of:**
1. MATHEMATICAL reach remains <= 0 after adding the magnitude/ordinality channel with the scrambled control correctly failing (i.e. the channel itself is well-behaved but insufficient) — this is evidence the residual math gap requires mechanism (B), the metaphor-structural bridge, not a channel-count fix, and should trigger a scoped follow-up rather than repeated channel-count escalation.
2. SOCIAL reach fails to clear the bar even after the power x affiliation channel is added and passes ablation cleanly — evidence the SOCIAL domain's probe set contains concepts requiring the metaphor-bridge or a channel not yet identified (e.g. theory-of-mind / mentalizing content distinct from trait-dominance-affiliation).
3. The scrambled-attribute control ALSO reaches positive (fails to fire negative) for either new channel — the apparent gain is dimensionality inflation, not real grounding signal; reject the specific channel operationalization and re-derive.
4. Per-channel ablation shows <10% relative degradation for the domain's own dedicated channel (magnitude for MATH, social-relational for SOCIAL) — the reach gain is being carried by a different, already-present channel (most likely affect), not the new one; the new channel is not doing real work.

A HARD-FAIL on math/abstract specifically (item 1) is a **legitimate, literature-consistent bounded finding**, not a failure of the overall grounding-core program — Pecher & Zeelenberg's own boundary claim predicts some abstract concepts (justice, truth, causation, potentially higher-math objects like infinity) will not close from any fixed scalar-channel core and require the explicit metaphor-structural bridge (B), or may retain an honest residual gap that language/convention alone carries. Report that outcome as a bounded finding, not as evidence the grounding-reach methodology is broken.

## Substrate-product implications

A spanning grounded core that reaches MATH and SOCIAL domains, not just PHYSICAL/EMOTIONAL, is what would let downstream consumers rely on cosine-to-core as a general relevance/grounding signal across a broad concept vocabulary rather than only for perceptually-concrete words — this directly extends the core's practical coverage without requiring per-domain special-casing, using the same fusion architecture already adopted for other channels. The metaphor-structural-bridge mechanism (B), if needed, is a bounded, well-scoped addition (one new edge type, reusing existing diffusion) rather than a new subsystem — keeping the overall core-expansion cheap even in the HARD-FAIL branch. None of this claims the substrate replicates ATL/hippocampal-entorhinal function; it borrows the *measured structure* those literatures established (magnitude as a distinct primitive, social space as 2D power x affiliation, metaphor as structure-preserving mapping) as concrete, testable channel/edge designs.
