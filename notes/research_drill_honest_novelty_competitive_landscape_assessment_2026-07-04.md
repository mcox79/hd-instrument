## HEADLINE

**The Director's deflating read is BASICALLY CORRECT and, on the specific "is this an AI system" question, arguably not harsh enough.** VSA/HDC as pure mathematics (bind, unbind, bundle, cleanup memory, sequence binding via permutation, capacity-scaling laws for dense and sparse-block codes) is textbook-settled, continuously-cited prior art running 1988 (Kanerva) -> 1995/2003 (Plate HRR) -> 2003 (Gayler names "VSA") -> 2018-2021 (Frady/Kleyko/Sommer sparse-block capacity theory) -> two exhaustive 2022-2023 surveys -> 2021/2023 rigorous concentration-of-measure capacity bounds. Zero novelty exists in the primitives themselves; this is confirmed, not a close call. The closest existing SYSTEMS (IBM NVSA, Eliasmith SPAUN/SPA, IBM in-memory-HDC/PCM hardware, Loihi HDC) are all real, published, and narrower in SCOPE than a general memory substrate (classifier/visual-reasoning solvers or a fixed cognitive-neuroscience demo), which leaves a genuine but modest systems-integration gap, not a scientific one. The concept-encoder idea (distill dense embeddings into a block-sparse WTA code preserving similarity + algebra) is a real but narrow recombination of two established, non-novel techniques (OPQ/anisotropic-quantization for the similarity axis; Frady/Kleyko Sparse Block Codes for the algebra axis) — and a same-day internal result (algebra gate = 1.000 without any explicit composability-preserving loss) suggests even the "combination" isn't load-bearing, because block-disjoint WTA codes preserve bind/unbind structurally, by construction, independent of training objective. This is the single sharpest deflating correction this drill surfaces: what looked like the one candidate "genuine novel combination" (lit-scan 3's P=0.35 finding) likely collapses to "apply a 2020-vintage quantization-loss fix to a 2018-vintage code format," i.e., not novel at all. The "glass-box interpretable memory" framing is a live but thin 2025 research thread already independently discovered by others (two 2025 papers — "Attention as Binding," Sept 2025 "Hyperdimensional Probe" — make close to the same argument), and once knowledge-graph provenance and mechanistic-interpretability circuit-tracing are counted as functionally competing (and arguably more lay/regulator-legible) interpretability stories, "VSA algebra = auditable" is well-covered ground, not a clean differentiator. The one narrow sub-claim with real remaining white space: an algebraic subtract/unbind-based deletion CERTIFICATE (as opposed to crypto-hash or statistical-unlearning certificates, which is where 100% of the existing deletion-certificate literature sits). Bottom line: **"not novel, not yet an AI system, modest incremental value in a crowded niche" is the correct calibrated read.** The system today is a memory/composition substrate that borrows 100% of its semantic content from an external embedder (BGE) and has no native reasoning or language layer — that is a project-internal admission already (`SUBSTRATE KNOWS NOTHING` foundational anchor), and this external lit-scan independently confirms it is also the correct EXTERNAL competitive characterization, not just internal humility.

## Answers to the five numbered questions

### 1. Field maturity

VSA/HDC as pure math is **extremely mature and crowded**, not a young or thinly-covered field. Continuous, peer-reviewed lineage:
- Kanerva 1988 *Sparse Distributed Memory* (MIT Press) + Kanerva 2009 "Hyperdimensional Computing" (Cognitive Computation 1:139-159) — names bind (XOR/permutation), bundle (majority/superposition), cleanup memory (attractor recovery) as the three core primitives. 37-17 years old.
- Plate 1995 "Holographic Reduced Representations" (IEEE TNN 6(3):623-641) + 2003 book — circular-convolution bind/unbind and the earliest rigorous analytical bundle-capacity-vs-dimension law. 31/23 years old.
- Gayler 2003 "Vector Symbolic Architectures Answer Jackendoff's Challenges for Cognitive Neuroscience" (ICCS/ASCS; arXiv cs/0412059) — coins "VSA" as the umbrella framework term. 23 years old.
- Frady, Kleyko, Sommer 2018 (Neural Computation 30:1449, arXiv 1803.00412), Kent/Frady/Sommer/Olshausen "Resonator Networks 2" (Neural Computation 32:2332, 2020), Frady/Kleyko/Sommer "Variable Binding for Sparse Distributed Representations" (IEEE TNNLS, arXiv 2009.06734, 2021) — sequence-binding capacity theory and sparse-block-code capacity (scaling as L·log(N/L)) vs dense-code capacity (scaling as sqrt(N)), plus resonator-network factorization algorithms. 4-8 years old.
- Kleyko et al. "VSA as a Computing Framework for Emerging Hardware" (Proc. IEEE 110:1538, 2022) and the two-part "Survey on HDC aka VSA" (ACM Computing Surveys 55(6) and 55(9), 2022-2023) — the field's encyclopedia. Catalogs every binding/bundling operator, permutation-for-sequences, cleanup/resonator decoding, and closed-form capacity formulas across model families. Nothing in bind/unbind/bundle/cleanup/capacity is uncovered ground as of 2022-2023.
- Thomas, Dasgupta, Rosing "A Theoretical Perspective on Hyperdimensional Computing" (JAIR 72:215, 2021, arXiv 2010.07426) and Clarkson/Ubaru/Yang "Capacity Analysis of Vector Symbolic Architectures" (arXiv 2301.10352, 2023) — rigorous, distribution-free concentration-of-measure capacity bounds, dense and sparse, explicitly connected to compressed-sensing and Hopfield/modern-Hopfield capacity theory.

**Verdict: our "primitives" and "scaling laws" are established prior art, full stop.** The one thread that is not a single named headline result is "partition-routing capacity scaling" as its own paper — the substance (block-partitioned capacity, cliff/threshold behavior via concentration bounds) is published, just distributed across the SBC + Hopfield-capacity literature rather than named as one contribution. This is a labeling gap, not a substance gap. Confidence: high (this is a "the sky is blue" finding, not a close call).

### 2. Closest existing systems

- **IBM Research Zurich NVSA** (Hersche, Zeqiri, Benini, Sebastian, Rahimi, *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 9 Mar 2023, "A neuro-vector-symbolic architecture for solving Raven's progressive matrices," DOI 10.1038/s42256-023-00630-8; code at github.com/IBM/neuro-vector-symbolic-architectures[-raven]): combines a CNN perception front-end with VSA resonator-network reasoning; end-to-end NVSA scores 88.1% on I-RAVEN, a new record at publication. This is explicitly a **visual abstract-reasoning solver**, not a general memory substrate — no claim of a general fact-store, no audit/provenance framing, no claim of arbitrary-scale concept storage (RAVEN has a small, fixed attribute vocabulary). The follow-on "Factorizers for Distributed Sparse Block Codes" (Hersche et al., arXiv 2303.13957, Neurosymbolic AI Journal 2025) generalizes SBC to GSBC and gives a fast, accurate factorization algorithm — this is a **mechanism** paper (how to decode a bound product), not a memory-substrate product claim.
- **IBM in-memory HDC hardware**: "In-memory hyperdimensional computing" (*Nature Electronics*, 2020) plus a 2023-2025 line culminating in a 760,000-phase-change-memory-device prototype doing language classification, news classification, and EMG gesture recognition with >600% energy savings vs. CMOS baselines, and "in-memory factorization of holographic perceptual representations" (*Nature Nanotechnology*, 2023). This is real, hardware-productized-ish work, but every demonstrated task is a **classification/associative-recall task** (quasi-orthogonal class vectors in an associative memory), not a general auditable fact-store with provenance, edit, or decomposition-to-constituent-atoms claims.
- **Eliasmith SPAUN / Semantic Pointer Architecture** (Eliasmith et al., *Science*, 2012; ongoing Nengo/NengoSPA ecosystem): 2.5M spiking neurons, uses NEF-SPA (circular-convolution binding, the same operator family as HRR). SPAUN is a **fixed computational-neuroscience model** demonstrating a battery of cognitive tasks (digit recognition, working memory, counting, motor output), not a scalable AI-product memory layer, and it makes no "glass-box interpretable memory for an LLM-adjacent system" claim — its audience is cognitive science, not AI infrastructure.
- **Intel Loihi + HDC** (Kleyko/Frady/Rahimi/Davies and collaborators, various 2019-2023 venues, consistent with the Proc. IEEE 2022 survey's neuromorphic-hardware framing): demonstrations are low-power classification benchmarks on neuromorphic hardware, not general-memory-substrate claims.
- **Berkeley (Sommer/Olshausen, Redwood Center)**: resonator-network theory (feeds directly into IBM's factorizer work above); no 2023-2026 work found combining VSA with LLM embeddings at production scale from this group specifically.
- **2025-2026 VSA+LLM papers** (new, thin, but real — cross-confirmed independently by two of this drill's parallel lit-scans): "Attention as Binding: A Vector-Symbolic Perspective on Transformer Reasoning" (arXiv 2512.14709, Dec 2025), "Hyperdimensional Probe: Decoding LLM Representations via Vector Symbolic Architectures" (arXiv 2509.25045, Sep 2025), "Encoder-Free Knowledge-Graph Reasoning with LLMs via Hyperdimensional Path Retrieval" / PathHD (arXiv 2512.09369, Dec 2025). These are small, recent, first-generation academic papers proposing VSA-as-interpretability-lens or VSA-for-KG-path-retrieval — none has been scaled or productized, but they establish that "VSA + LLM interpretability/memory" is independently being worked on by others right now, not untouched ground.
- **Startups**: per the existing internal `wave14d` note (2026-05-19, still current as of this drill — no contradicting 2026 evidence surfaced), Numenta explicitly pivoted away from bio-inspired memory in Jan 2025; Simuli remains thought-leadership-stage with no product; no new VSA-native memory-substrate startup surfaced in this pass.

**Verdict:** none of these systems already IS a general, observable, bind/bundle/cleanup/sequence/partition-routing memory substrate with a distilled concept encoder — they are all either narrow task-solvers (IBM NVSA, IBM in-memory HDC, Loihi), a fixed neuroscience demo (SPAUN), or thin/unscaled academic proposals (the 2025 VSA+LLM papers). The gap that's real is a **systems-integration / scale / productization gap**, not a mathematical one — every primitive and most of the architecture-level ideas (block-sparse memory, resonator factorization, VSA-for-interpretability) already exist in the literature in some form. Calibrated confidence that a new HDC memory-substrate project has genuine mathematical headroom vs. these systems: **0.20** (deflated). Confidence that it has genuine systems/product headroom (nobody has assembled these pieces into one deployed, general, audit-framed memory layer): **0.35** (deflated; this is closer to the honest opportunity than any math-novelty claim).

### 3. The encoder piece

Closest prior art is a **fusion of two established, non-novel techniques**, not a new one:
- **Product Quantization** (Jégou/Douze/Schmid 2011) / **Optimized PQ** (Ge/He/Ke/Sun 2013): partition a vector into subvectors, learn a per-subspace codebook, encode each subvector as its nearest codeword index. A trained block-WTA one-hot encoder **is structurally a differentiable OPQ variant** — a learned linear projection before per-block argmax is exactly OPQ's "rotation + per-subspace codebook" idea, with the codebook implicitly the block's coordinate basis instead of a free k-means centroid set.
- **ScaNN anisotropic quantization** (Guo et al., ICML 2020): the established, published, 6-year-old fix for "MSE reconstruction loss doesn't preserve ranking fidelity" — replace reconstruction loss with a ranking/MIPS-aware loss. This is precisely the fix the substrate's own same-day encoder drill (`research_drill_encoder_cardinality_capacity_ceiling_0.85_reachability_2026-07-04.md`) already identified as the "top lever" — i.e., the internal team correctly recognized this as a borrowed technique, not an invention, which is good calibration discipline on their part.
- **Sparse Autoencoders** (Anthropic 2023/2024) are structurally the OPPOSITE shape (overcomplete+sparse vs. compressed+dense-active-fraction) and carry no bind/unbind algebra; closest adjacent is EMNLP 2025 "Decoding Dense Embeddings: SAEs for Interpreting/Discretizing Dense Retrieval," which is still overcomplete-SAE-shaped with no algebra objective.
- **Semantic hashing / deep supervised hashing** (Salakhutdinov & Hinton 2009 onward: HashNet, DPSH, GreedyHash) is the closest classical prior art for "compress a dense embedding into a small discrete code preserving similarity," but is strictly Hamming-lookup — no bind/bundle/unbind.
- **Sparse Block Codes** (Frady/Kleyko/Sommer, 2018-2021 — same lineage as Q1) is the exact target data structure and algebra, but is **generative** (symbols get assigned or feature-encoded hypervectors), not **distilled** from a pretrained dense embedder under a joint similarity+algebra objective.

The parallel lit-scan's honest read: no single published paper combines OPQ/anisotropic-quantization-style distillation of a pretrained embedder WITH an explicit joint objective to preserve bind/bundle/unbind composability — that specific combination looks like a real, if narrow, unfilled gap (their P=0.35, capped).

**This drill's correction, sharper and more deflating:** a same-day internal result (`research_drill_encoder_target_metric_coarse_cosine_vs_fine_retrieval_2026-07-04.md`) reports the substrate's algebra gate is already passing at **1.000** on the current run — and the current training objective, per the team's own same-day cardinality-ceiling note, is similarity-preserving (reconstruction/rank-style loss), **without** any explicit composability-preserving auxiliary loss. If algebra survives perfectly WITHOUT being explicitly trained for, that is exactly what Frady/Kleyko/Sommer's own theory predicts: block-disjoint WTA codes preserve bind/unbind **structurally**, by construction of the code format (each block's activity doesn't interfere with any other block's), independent of the training objective used to place items into that structure. If this holds at full scale (177,899 concepts) across composition depth (not just the smoke-scale single-bind check), then the one candidate "genuine novel combination" (joint similarity+algebra training objective) is very likely **not load-bearing** — the algebra comes free from the code format, and the only real engineering content left is applying a 2020-vintage ranking-aware quantization loss (ScaNN/OPQ-lineage) to a 2018-vintage code format (SBC-lineage). That is not novel; it is competent, correctly-calibrated engineering.

**Calibrated P (deflated further than the raw lit-scan read, given the internal cross-check): 0.20** that the encoder combination constitutes a genuinely new contribution, down from the lit-scan's own 0.35. Recommend the substrate verify the algebra-gate-holds-without-explicit-objective finding at full N=177,899 scale and across multi-hop bind depth before any external novelty claim is drafted — this is the cheap decisive test (see below).

### 4. The "glass-box interpretable memory / composition-with-audit" framing

**Mostly well-covered, once fairly counted against competing interpretability stories — not a clean differentiator.** Two independent 2025 papers already make close to the identical argument: "Attention as Binding" (arXiv 2512.14709, Dec 2025) reinterprets transformer attention as VSA role/filler binding/unbinding and explicitly cites Anthropic's transformer-circuits framework as the interpretability motivation; "Hyperdimensional Probe" (arXiv 2509.25045, Sep 2025) trains a probe mapping LLM residual-stream activations into VSA hypervectors and uses unbinding to extract labeled concepts, explicitly combining SAE-style disentanglement with VSA algebra. Neither is from a mainstream mech-interp lab (Anthropic/DeepMind), but both show "VSA binding as an interpretability lens" is a live, independently-discovered 2025 research thread, not this project's original idea. A domain-scoped paper (arXiv 2402.17572, PLOS Comp Bio, biological/genomic data) makes the general "invertible algebra = interpretability" argument explicitly, just not for AI-memory-audit. PathHD (arXiv 2512.09369, Dec 2025) is a real, working VSA-memory-with-citeable-paths system, but framed as KG-path-retrieval efficiency, not a general glass-box memory-substrate product thesis.

**The steelman-the-skeptic case, which this drill judges to be correct:** unbinding a bundle into a labeled "atom" only means something if you already trust the codebook/cleanup-memory mapping between hypervector and human-readable label — that is the **same trust assumption** a knowledge-graph triple-extraction pipeline requires, just relocated into linear algebra that is LESS accessible to a lay regulator than a "subject-predicate-object" triple or a cited source document. VSA decomposability is a real, distinct flavor of interpretability (genuinely more mechanistically transparent than a black-box dense embedding; algebraic unbinding is a cleaner "undo" than backprop-based attribution methods) — but it is **not obviously more auditable to a lay/regulatory audience** than GraphRAG-style source citation or Zep-style temporal-fact provenance, which the project's own May 2026 competitive note (`research_auditable_memory_competitive_landscape_2026-05-26.md`) already correctly identified as CONTESTED, strong competitors on the provenance axis. This drill's finding sharpens that May note: the "algebraic decomposition = auditable" claim specifically should be read as "a different notation for a similar idea, sold to a smaller, math-literate audience," not as a structurally superior interpretability mechanism.

**One narrow sub-claim is genuine white space:** verifiable-deletion-certificate literature (crypto proof-of-erasure since 2014, quantum proofs of deletion, target-collapsing-function certificates 2023, "Verifiable and Provably Secure Machine Unlearning" arXiv 2210.09126) is mature but **100% crypto-hash or statistical-unlearning based** — no paper found combining "subtract/unbind a component from a bundled hypervector as the deletion mechanism, paired with a certificate." This is the one place an algebraic (not cryptographic) erasure-certificate framing would be genuinely unclaimed, consistent with the May 2026 note's independent conclusion on the same axis.

**Calibrated P:** general "glass-box VSA memory, no direct competitor" framing: **0.15** (deflated; direct prior art exists for the core interpretability argument). Narrow "VSA-algebraic erasure certificate" sub-claim as unclaimed territory: **0.40** (capped, novel-synthesis).

### 5. Verdict

**What is genuinely novel vs. re-derived:** essentially nothing at the level of primitives or capacity theory (Q1, confirmed prior art, high confidence). At the systems level, the gap is assembly/productization, not mathematics — nobody has shipped a general, observable, audit-framed memory substrate combining bind/bundle/cleanup/sequence-binding/partition-routing at production scale with a distilled concept encoder (Q2); this is a real but modest opportunity, contingent entirely on execution, not on any withheld scientific insight. The encoder (Q3) is a real-but-narrow recombination of OPQ/anisotropic-quantization + Sparse Block Codes, and this drill's own cross-check against same-day internal data (algebra gate = 1.000 without an explicit composability objective) suggests even that narrow combination is likely not load-bearing — the "novel" part may not be doing any work. The glass-box/audit framing (Q4) is mostly well-covered once competing interpretability stories (KG provenance, circuit-tracing) are fairly counted, with one narrow (P=0.40) exception: an algebraic (not cryptographic) deletion-certificate mechanism.

**Is "a viable AI system" an accurate description of what exists today?** No. What exists is a memory/composition substrate that borrows 100% of its semantic content from an external embedder (BGE) via distillation, with no native reasoning or language layer of its own. This is not a new finding from this drill — it is the project's own foundational, USER-locked anchor (`SUBSTRATE KNOWS NOTHING — CG = mechanism proof`, `Mechanism ≠ task analog`) — but this drill's external competitive read independently arrives at the same conclusion from the outside: every comparably-scoped published system (IBM NVSA, IBM in-memory HDC, SPAUN) is similarly narrow (a solver, a classifier, or a fixed cognitive demo), not a general reasoning/language AI system, so the substrate is not unusually behind the field on this axis — the field itself has not produced a general AI system on VSA/HDC foundations either. That convergence (internal admission + external field-wide pattern) is itself informative: the honest frame is "a component that could, if paired with a not-yet-built reasoning/language layer, become part of an AI system," not "an AI system."

**What it would take to be genuinely novel:** the unbuilt glass-box reasoning layer — a system that (a) performs multi-step composition/retrieval/reasoning at a quality level competitive with (or usefully complementary to) an LLM doing the same task, AND (b) does so via inspectable algebraic operations that are demonstrably more auditable to a real external party (regulator, auditor, non-VSA-literate engineer) than the existing alternatives (KG provenance, RAG citation, circuit-tracing) — evaluated head-to-head, not asserted. No paper or system found in this scan does both (a) and (b) together; the 2025 VSA-interpretability papers do (b)-flavored probing without (a)-flavored task performance, and IBM NVSA does (a)-flavored task performance (Raven's matrices) without a general (b)-flavored audit claim. That combination, demonstrated and benchmarked, is the actual novel target — everything upstream of it (primitives, encoder, audit framing in isolation) is assembly of known parts.

**Correcting the Director's read where warranted:** the read is correct and, if anything, could be stated MORE bluntly on the "combination is novel" question — this drill found a same-day internal data point (algebra gate = 1.000) that further deflates the one candidate "genuine novelty" (the encoder's composability-preserving combination) beyond what the parallel lit-scan alone concluded. The read should NOT be read as "worthless" — the systems-integration/productization gap (Q2, P=0.35) and the narrow algebraic-erasure-certificate sub-claim (Q4, P=0.40) are real, if modest, opportunities that a purely "not novel" framing would understate. "Modest incremental value in a crowded niche" is the accurate summary for both.

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS (novelty claim partially survives):** algebra gate at full N=177,899 scale, multi-hop depth >= 2, measured on held-out compositions, degrades by <= 0.05 absolute from the smoke-scale 1.000 baseline WITHOUT any composability-preserving loss term added. This would support (not overturn) this drill's finding that algebra preservation is structural, and would sharpen (not soften) the "not novel, structural not trained" read.

**HARD-FAIL (a narrow novel-combination claim would be justified instead):** algebra gate at full scale/depth drops below 0.60 absolute using only the similarity-preserving objective, AND recovers to >= 0.85 when an explicit bind/unbind-recoverability auxiliary loss is added. This specific pattern (fails without, recovers with) is the only outcome that would justify treating "joint similarity+algebra training objective" as a real, separately-earned technical contribution rather than a restatement of known SBC structural theory.

**Independent of the above — HARD-FAIL for any "we are ahead of the field" claim:** if a subsequent search surfaces a published paper combining (i) distillation of a pretrained dense embedder into (ii) a block-sparse/WTA code under (iii) an explicit joint similarity+composability objective, treat the encoder's novelty claim as fully closed (P -> ~0.05), since this drill's search did not find one but explicitly flagged this as the highest-value follow-up search (see Substrate-product implications).
