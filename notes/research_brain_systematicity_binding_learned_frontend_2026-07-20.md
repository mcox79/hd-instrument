## HEADLINE

The brain's systematic-generalization trick is NOT one mechanism, it's a **dual code plus a fixed algebraic operator**: (1) high-dimensional conjunctive/mixed-selectivity coding for capacity+flexibility, running **alongside** (2) a low-dimensional factorized/disentangled geometry that behaves exactly like a Smolensky TPR / Plate HRR binding operator — invertible, uniform across all role-filler pairs, so novel pairs decode correctly **by construction**, not by having been observed. The learned-front-end + fixed-composition division of labor (NVSA / NS-CL pattern) has real neural precedent (canonical cortical microcircuit as fixed primitive; Kymn et al. 2024's hippocampal-entorhinal model is a near-literal match: fixed structured binding + learned heteroassociative sensory mapping). The strongest counter-evidence is Lake & Baroni's MLC (*Nature* 2023): a **fully learned**, non-symbolic meta-seq2seq net reaches human-level SCAN-style systematicity with no fixed operator at all — so "fixed composition" is the right *default engineering hypothesis*, not a proven necessity. The single most load-bearing risk for a chain-grade claim is **construction-determinism** (Greff, van Steenkiste & Schmidhuber 2020, "On the Binding Problem in ANNs"): if the binding operator is hand-designed to match the test's combinatorial structure, the win is stipulated, not learned — this is exactly the failure mode that already killed our own structure-derivation attempt (atom 29369) in-house, so the guard below is not hypothetical, it is empirically confirmed against our own prior work.

## (1) The binding mechanism

**Temporal synchrony / gamma binding** (von der Malsburg 1981; Gray & Singer, *Nature* 1989) has real support for *segmentation* ("these features belong to one object") but **no demonstrated mechanism for role assignment** ("this filler is agent, that filler is patient"). Shadlen & Movshon (*Neuron* 1999, "Synchrony Unbound") and later inconsistent replications (Thiele & Stoner, Dong et al.) leave synchrony-as-role-binding unresolved at best. Do not lean on synchrony as the systematicity mechanism — it's a grouping signal, not a binding-to-role signal.

**Conjunctive coding**: Rigotti, Barak, Warden, Wang, Daw, Miller & Fusi (*Nature* 2013) show PFC mixed-selectivity conjunctive coding is necessary for flexible linear readout of task variables, but pure conjunctive/high-dimensional codes generalize poorly to unseen conjunctions — a lookup-table problem. The resolution is Bernardi, Benna, Rigotti, Munuera, Fusi & Salzman (*Cell* 2020, "The Geometry of Abstraction in the Hippocampus and Prefrontal Cortex"): PFC/hippocampal populations carry **both** a high-dimensional conjunctive/entangled component **and** a low-dimensional disentangled/factorized geometry simultaneously — the factorized component is what lets a linear decoder generalize to novel condition combinations. This is the single most important empirical finding for the binding-systematicity claim: the brain does not choose flat-vs-structured, it runs both codes in the same population.

**Indirection/pointer schemes**: Kriete, Noelle, Cohen & O'Reilly (*PNAS* 2013, PBWM) model PFC/basal-ganglia binding via indirection (a pointer/index in one WM slot references content held elsewhere) rather than literal conjunction. Hummel & Holyoak's LISA (*Psych. Review* 2003) hybridizes synchrony-as-indirection with role-filler binding for analogy. Eliasmith's Semantic Pointer Architecture / Spaun (*How to Build a Brain*, 2013; Crawford et al. 2016) is the concrete existence proof: HRR-style circular-convolution binding, superposition, and correlation-based unbinding are all realized in ~2.5M spiking neurons via the Neural Engineering Framework — vector-symbolic binding is neurally implementable at scale, not just a convenient abstraction.

**PFC + hippocampus**: Eichenbaum's relational memory theory (multiple reviews, *Nat. Rev. Neurosci.* 2014) — hippocampal pattern separation/completion binds arbitrary co-occurring elements into relational structure supporting flexible novel-combination inference at retrieval. Badre's hierarchical PFC gating framework (Bhandari & Badre, *Cognition* 2018) shows people transfer gating **policies** (role/rule structure) independent of specific stimulus content — evidence of a factorized control architecture, not per-instance rote learning.

**Formal account (Smolensky 1990 TPR; Plate 1995 HRR)**: binding(role, filler) = a single fixed bilinear/convolutional operator defined once for *all* possible role/filler vectors. Because it's uniform and (approximately) invertible, unbinding is literally the algebraic inverse of the same operator — **any novel role-filler pair is correctly encoded/decoded automatically; systematicity is a proved mathematical consequence of the operator's structure, not an empirical property that had to be learned case-by-case.** This is the precise property flat/associative distributed representations lack: nothing forces a plain trained embedding space to factor into independent role and filler dimensions, so a never-seen combination has no guaranteed representation.

Credit: von der Malsburg, Gray & Singer, Shadlen & Movshon, Rigotti & Fusi, Bernardi/Benna/Rigotti/Fusi/Salzman, Kriete/Noelle/Cohen/O'Reilly, Hummel & Holyoak, Eliasmith, Eichenbaum, Badre, Smolensky, Plate.

## (2) Learned front-end + fixed composition division of labor

**NVSA** (Hersche, Zeqiri, Benini, Sebastian, Rahimi, *Nat. Mach. Intell.* 2023, arXiv:2203.04571): learned CNN front-end classifies visual attributes into probability mass functions; these are bound into **fixed** VSA hypervectors via non-learned binding/bundling operators; reasoning (rule inference over Raven's matrices) is fixed algebraic abduction, not gradient-trained. Generalization to novel attribute combinations follows because the algebra is distributive/compositional by construction — the back-end never needs to have seen a given combination, only its atoms. 87.7-88.1% on RAVEN/I-RAVEN, largest margins specifically on OOD attribute-combination regimes.

**NS-CL** (Mao, Gan, Kohli, Tenenbaum, Wu, ICLR 2019): learned object-concept embeddings + a **fixed** symbolic program executor (Filter/Count/Compare/Relate). The **CLEVR-CoGenT ablation is the single cleanest piece of causal evidence in this entire drill**: freezing the attribute operators after training on split A (cubes gray/blue/brown/yellow, cylinders red/green/purple/cyan) preserves ~98.8%/98.9% accuracy on both splits A and B (novel color-shape pairings); re-learning attributes end-to-end causes color-shape entanglement and **collapse on split B**. This directly shows fixing the composition/attribute-operator layer, while letting only the perceptual mapping vary, is *causally* what buys the held-out generalization — not incidental.

**Neural evidence for the division**: Douglas & Martin's canonical cortical microcircuit (reviewed via Bastos et al., *Neuron* 2012, predictive-coding mapping) is a repeated, largely genetically/developmentally fixed laminar motif — a plausible fixed computational primitive. Frankland & Greene (*Annu. Rev. Psychol.* 2020) find reusable, position-invariant agent/patient role codes in amPFC/hippocampus/lmSTC. **Most directly on point: Kymn et al. (NeurIPS 2024, "Binding in hippocampal-entorhinal circuits enables compositionality in cognitive maps")** models hippocampal-entorhinal binding as a fixed, structured (residue-number-system-like) vector-binding operation, with the heteroassociative mapping from sensory input to those codes as the learned component — this is close to a literal computational-neuroscience instantiation of the NVSA/NS-CL pattern. Meanwhile DiCarlo-lab work shows ventral-stream (IT) object representations are substantially reshaped by unsupervised experience — the perceptual "atoms" side is genuinely plastic, in contrast to the more canonical binding machinery.

**Dissent — the load-bearing caveat**: Lake & Baroni's MLC (*Nature* 623, 2023) reaches **human-level** SCAN-style systematicity with a fully learned, non-symbolic meta-seq2seq network — no fixed binding operator anywhere. Russin et al. (arXiv:1904.09708) show a softer architectural prior (separating syntax/structure from semantics) also helps without a hard symbolic operator. DreamCoder (Ellis et al., PLDI 2021) shows composition itself can be grown inductively (wake-sleep library learning) rather than fixed a priori. Honest synthesis: **"fixed composition" is the right default engineering hypothesis and has real neural analogs, but it is not proven necessary — the more defensible claim is "fixed on the timescale of a single task/dataset, itself the product of a slower learning/evolutionary process," not "never learned, ever."**

Credit: Hersche/Zeqiri/Benini/Sebastian/Rahimi (NVSA), Mao/Gan/Kohli/Tenenbaum/Wu (NS-CL), Johnson et al. (CLEVR), Douglas & Martin, Bastos et al., Frankland & Greene, Kymn et al., Lake & Baroni (MLC), Russin et al., Ellis et al. (DreamCoder).

## (3) Why flat models fail + the construction-determinism guard

**The canonical failure**: Lake & Baroni (ICML 2018, SCAN) — RNNs succeed via mix-and-match memorization on near-i.i.d. splits, fail on splits requiring genuine recombination. Loula, Baroni & Lake (2018) show the failure is graded (some recombination types are learnable, deeper novel structural positions are not). Kim & Linzen (EMNLP 2020, COGS) sharpen this to structural role generalization specifically: models handle new words in familiar structural slots but fail on familiar words in novel structural roles — this is squarely the role-filler binding failure, not a vocabulary problem. Keysers et al. (ICLR 2020, CFQ) formalize it with **compound divergence (DBCA)**: holding atom distribution fixed while maximizing compound (combination) divergence produces a smooth, universal accuracy degradation across every architecture tested — a real, measurable gradient, not a binary bug. Hupkes et al. (JAIR 2020) attribute the deficit to three compounding causes: no discrete slots (superposed, not bound, representations), no inductive bias favoring reuse, and an MLE training objective that never *forces* a systematic solution even when one is reachable.

**What helps, and the crucial complication**: equivariant architectures (Gordon et al., ICLR 2020), meta seq2seq (Lake 2019), GECA data augmentation (Andreas, ACL 2020), and neuro-symbolic hybrids (Chen et al., NeurIPS 2020) all show real gains. But **Patel, Bhattamishra, Blunsom & Goyal (ACL 2022)** is the single most important methodological finding of this drill: plain seq2seq models reach near-perfect SCAN one-shot-primitive performance merely by **tweaking the training distribution — no architecture change at all**. This means both the original "failures" and several claimed "architectural wins" in this literature can be artifacts of incidental training-distribution construction rather than genuine representational limits or genuine fixes. Furrer et al. (2020) similarly found pretrained Transformers close much of the CFQ gap with no explicit compositional mechanism, undercutting "specialized architecture is necessary" claims.

**The construction-determinism critique** (Greff, van Steenkiste & Schmidhuber, 2020, "On the Binding Problem in Artificial Neural Networks") is the direct external analog of our own in-house finding: any architecture with an explicit, hand-designed binding/segregation operator risks **answering the question by construction** — the binding mechanism *is* the systematic-generalization capability, stipulated rather than learned, so a benchmark built around exactly that structure is close to tautological. This is precisely the failure mode of our own structure-derivation kill (atom 29369): deriving/stipulating structural-address codes to fit the data was WRONG; random, task-agnostic role codes were RIGHT (matching the brain-consistent dentate-gyrus pattern-separation prior). **This external critique independently confirms our in-house lesson and should be read as directly load-bearing for the design below, not a generic caution.**

Credit: Lake & Baroni, Loula/Baroni/Lake, Kim & Linzen, Keysers et al., Hupkes et al., Gordon et al., Lake (meta seq2seq), Andreas (GECA), Chen et al. (stack machines), Patel/Bhattamishra/Blunsom/Goyal, Furrer et al., Greff/van Steenkiste/Schmidhuber, Fodor & Pylyshyn (1988, systematicity vs productivity taxonomy).

## Design sketch: learned front-end + fixed native binding vs flat learned baseline

**Setup** (borrowing directly from the best-validated external design, CLEVR-CoGenT, adapted to role-filler binding rather than visual attributes):

- Define R roles (e.g., 4-6: agent, patient, instrument, location, ...) and F fillers per role-type (e.g., 8-12 filler identities), i.e. a compositional attribute space analogous to CLEVR's shape x color grid.
- Training split: restrict each role to a SUBSET of its possible fillers (e.g., roleA seen only with fillers 1-4, roleB only with fillers 5-8) — mirrors CLEVR-CoGenT's split A. Match filler-identity FREQUENCY across roles so atom distribution is uniform (per Keysers et al.'s compound-divergence discipline: only the (role, filler) COMPOUND should differ between train and test, not the marginal atom counts).
- Held-out test: the swapped/novel (role, filler) pairings never seen in training (roleA paired with fillers 5-8, etc.) — this is the compound-divergence-maximizing split.
- Query task: given a bound role-filler scene/sequence, answer a query that requires correctly recovering a specific role's filler (or a relation across 2+ bound pairs) — forces genuine role-conditioned retrieval, not just filler detection.

**Arm 1 (hybrid, NVSA-pattern)**: a learned front-end (small classifier/embedding net) maps raw filler observations to filler-ID codes, trained ONLY on the train-split role-filler pairings (never trained or tuned on held-out combinations). These codes are bound to role codes via the substrate's existing FIXED FHRR bind operator (native circular-convolution/complex-Hadamard binding, task-agnostic role codes assigned at random/init per the codebook-gate mechanism already validated in-house — NOT derived or stipulated to fit the data, per the atom-29369 lesson). Readout is the fixed algebraic unbind (correlation/inverse), not learned.

**Arm 2 (flat baseline)**: identical front-end capacity/tuning budget, but role+filler information is fed into an MLP/small Transformer trained END-TO-END on the same query task, with NO explicit bind/unbind operator — composition must be learned from data. This must be a genuinely capable learner (same parameter budget, same training compute, tuned learning rate/schedule), not a strawman.

**Measurements**:
1. **Learning curve** on the in-distribution dev set (held-out examples from the SAME train-split pairings) for both arms across a sweep of training-set sizes — this is the sanity check that the flat baseline is not undertrained/memory-starved (if it can't match the hybrid in-distribution, it's a strawman and the result is invalid regardless of held-out numbers).
2. **Held-out generalization gap**: accuracy on held-out novel (role, filler) pairings, for both arms, at the training-set size where in-distribution accuracy is closely matched between arms.

**Pre-registered HARD-PASS**: hybrid maintains held-out accuracy within ~10 percentage points of its own in-distribution accuracy, AND the flat baseline's held-out accuracy is at least 30 percentage points below its own in-distribution accuracy, AND both arms' in-distribution accuracies are within ~5 points of each other (rules out capacity/tuning confound).

**Pre-registered HARD-FAIL / kill conditions**:
- Flat baseline ALSO generalizes near-perfectly to held-out combos (gap < 10pp) — no advantage from fixed binding; this would replicate the Patel et al. (2022) refutation risk (a capable learner with the right training distribution already solves this without explicit binding) and should be treated as a genuine null, not re-run with a harder split until it "works."
- A trivial non-compositional shortcut baseline (majority-filler-per-role, nearest-neighbor lookup, position/frequency heuristic) matches the hybrid's held-out score — the split is not discriminative; redesign for higher compound divergence before re-testing, do not credit the hybrid.
- The front-end classifier was tuned, selected, or early-stopped using ANY signal from held-out-combination performance — invalid by leakage, must re-run strictly blind.
- Flat baseline's in-distribution accuracy is far below the hybrid's (capacity/tuning mismatch) — the comparison is invalid regardless of held-out numbers; fix the baseline's budget/tuning and re-run before drawing any conclusion.

## Construction-determinism guards (explicit, in priority order)

1. **[HIGHEST CONFIDENCE] The binding/role-code assignment must NOT be hand-designed or fit to match the held-out test's specific combinatorial structure** (Greff, van Steenkiste & Schmidhuber 2020). Role codes should be random/orthogonal at init or produced by a task-agnostic procedure applied uniformly to all roles — never selected, tuned, or derived post-hoc so that the specific held-out pairs happen to decode well. This is not a generic caution: it is the exact mechanism that already falsified our own structure-derivation attempt (atom 29369), where deriving/stipulating codes to fit data was wrong and random role codes were right.
2. Match atom (role-identity, filler-identity) frequency across train/test splits; only let the (role, filler) COMPOUND diverge (Keysers et al. 2020 compound-divergence/DBCA) — otherwise an apparent "compositional" win may just be ordinary distribution-shift robustness.
3. Include a non-compositional shortcut baseline (majority-filler-per-role, template lookup, frequency heuristic) in every run — if it matches the hybrid's held-out score, the split is not discriminative and must be redesigned before the hybrid can be credited.
4. Verify the split cannot be solved by a rule-based oracle with access to the generative scheme used to build the dataset — if it can, the benchmark measures grammar/template-matching, not learned generalization.
5. The learned front-end must be trained/frozen strictly on the train-split pairings and evaluated blind on novel combinations — never let filler/role learning see any combination-level supervision from the held-out set (this is the exact CLEVR-CoGenT lesion: re-learning attribute operators end-to-end on the full mixture causes entanglement and collapse on the swapped split).
6. Before crediting any architectural difference, test whether a training-distribution tweak alone (no architecture change) closes the gap for the flat baseline (Patel et al. 2022) — if it does, the original "failure" and any claimed "fix" may both be artifacts of incidental data construction, not a structural property.
7. Report systematicity and productivity as separate claims (Fodor & Pylyshyn 1988) — this design tests systematicity (recombination of known atoms) only; do not claim productivity (unbounded novel structure) from the same result.
8. Flat baseline must be given equal parameter budget, equal training compute, and its own tuned learning-rate/schedule, with a demonstrated in-distribution learning curve comparable to the hybrid's — an under-tuned or under-capacity flat baseline invalidates the entire comparison.

## Substrate-product implications

If the design above lands HARD-PASS with all 8 guards satisfied, it would establish a concrete, product-relevant capability: **a component that learns to recognize/classify inputs from data (the learned front-end) while inheriting systematic generalization to novel combinations "for free" from the substrate's native fixed binding algebra** — i.e., a customer-facing text/structured-input feature that generalizes to combinations of known concepts it was never explicitly trained on, without needing combinatorially many training examples. This is a capability claim (glass-box, no external LLM at inference), not a publication claim. If it HARD-FAILS per guard-checked conditions above, the informative null is that fixed binding alone does not buy systematicity over a well-tuned flat learner in this regime — which would redirect effort toward the MLC-style (Lake & Baroni 2023) fully-learned meta-learning path instead, a materially different (and currently un-scoped) build direction.

## Falsifiable predictions summary (HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL)

- HARD-PASS: hybrid held-out gap <=10pp from its own in-distribution accuracy; flat baseline held-out gap >=30pp from its own in-distribution accuracy; in-distribution accuracies matched within 5pp between arms.
- HARD-FAIL: flat baseline held-out gap <10pp (no binding advantage); OR a trivial shortcut baseline matches hybrid held-out accuracy (non-discriminative split); OR in-distribution accuracies are not matched (invalid comparison, fix and re-run before concluding).
