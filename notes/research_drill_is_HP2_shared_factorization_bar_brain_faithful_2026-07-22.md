## HEADLINE

**HP2 conflates two independent axes that the biology answers differently, and is PARTIALLY MIS-SPECIFIED.** The brain does NOT use one common cross-role binding operator applied identically to every role: Frankland & Greene (eNeuro 2020; AnnRevPsy 2020) find DEDICATED, anatomically separate populations for AGENT (medial lmSTC) and PATIENT (lateral lmSTC) -- structurally, this is ROLE-SPECIFIC, not shared-factorization, and sits closer to our TIED control (role-specific embeddings + fixed bind) than to native's single shared learned operator. eLife 2024 (107162) independently found NO explicit binding signature at all -- no evidence of a literal shared algebraic bind, TPR-style, anywhere in cortex. TEM's `g`-space (Whittington et al. 2020) and Bernardi et al. 2020's joint-population/multi-lens-readout finding DO show a shared underlying vector substrate -- but the role-SPECIALIZING operation on it (per-relation transition matrices `Wa`, per-role linear projections) is itself role-differentiated, not one shared function. So: shared CONTENT/SEMANTIC GEOMETRY (yes, brain-real -- distributional/hub-and-spoke semantic space, e.g. Lambon Ralph ATL-hub, Huth 2016 semantic maps) + ROLE-SPECIFIC BINDING SCAFFOLD (yes, brain-real, F&G) is closer to what the TIED control literally implements than to native's "one shared factorization for all roles."

**BUT** the free-algebra rebuttal (29399) was never really about shared-vs-role-specific per se -- it was about LEARNED-vs-FIXED as the locus, to block "compositionality for free from baked-in geometry, not actually acquired." On THAT axis the brain evidence cuts the other way: Lake & Baroni (Nature 2023, MLC) show systematicity is ACQUIRED via (meta-)learning, not innate-only; eLife 2024's relational scaffolds are explicitly "extracted from experience," i.e. learned developmentally, not fixed/given. HP2's literal wording bundles "shared" onto the learned side and "role-specific+fixed" onto the control side -- but biology decouples them: role-specific structure can still be LEARNED (F&G populations are presumably tuned through development/experience, not innate lookup tables), and a control's role-specificity does not by itself make it a free-algebra trap. The free-algebra concern is specifically about the control's FIXED (non-learned, at-test-time) binding operator riding on pre-existing filler geometry -- that concern survives untouched by this drill and should not be discarded.

## THEREFORE

HP2 as literally stated ("must locate the capability in a SHARED cross-role factorization") is MIS-SPECIFIED against the biology, on the same pattern as the difficulty-invariance bar (`research_drill_is_compgen_decode_coupling...2026-07-22.md`): it demands a structural property (one common operator across roles) that the best available evidence (Frankland & Greene; eLife 2024's null binding-signature finding) says the brain does NOT have. The brain's actual solution -- role-specific scaffold (learned through development, not innate) applied to a shared, itself-learned, semantic-distributional content space -- is structurally MUCH closer to our TIED control's architecture (role-specific embeddings + fixed bind + distributional geometry) than to native's single shared operator. If 29432/29433's TIED control ties native at hard difficulty via exactly this recipe, that tie may be showing a brain-faithful mechanism succeeding, not native's capability failing to be genuine.

**A REFRAMED bar, honestly narrower than "shared wins," separating the two conflated axes:** a genuine chain-grade must show the capability is LEARNED (not free-algebra/fixed-geometry-only) -- test this DIRECTLY, not via the shared-vs-role-specific proxy. Concretely: (i) ablate/randomize the control's distributional geometry (fillers get RANDOM, non-semantic embeddings instead of trained/distributional ones) -- if the TIED control's held-out performance collapses to chance while native's does not, the control WAS riding on free geometry and native's advantage is genuine and LEARNED, regardless of shared-vs-role-specific structure; (ii) if the TIED control still generalizes reasonably with randomized filler geometry (i.e., its role-specific+fixed-bind alone carries it), that is evidence the control itself has learned/structural power independent of geometry, and native's tie is a real tie, not an artifact of an unfair advantage. This is the disentangling test HP2 skipped by bundling "shared" with "learned."

## Falsifiable predictions

- **HARD-PASS (re-adjudication candidate, native's tie was NOT a free-algebra artifact):** TIED-random's held-out accuracy collapses toward chance/frequency-floor (say, >=50% relative drop from TIED-real-geometry) while native's held-out accuracy is materially unaffected by any equivalent geometry stress-test on its own filler representations (or native has no comparable free geometry channel to begin with). This would show TIED-real-geometry's tie with native was substantially propped up by pre-existing semantic geometry, exactly the free-algebra concern 29399 raised -- meaning native's LEARNED locus IS doing real, non-trivial work, and the "shared" qualifier in HP2 was the wrong proxy for that (the right test is the ablation, not shared-vs-role-specific), reopening 29432/29433 for adversarial VET as a brain-faithful CG candidate (role-specific-scaffold-style, not TPR-shared-style).
- **HARD-FAIL (HP2 stands, MM verdict is right as originally reasoned, independent of the shared-vs-role-specific question):** TIED-random still generalizes comparably to TIED-real-geometry (no material collapse) -- meaning the control's held-out success is NOT primarily geometry-riding, it is genuine role-specific structural capability that ties with native on its own merits. In this case native's design offers no demonstrated advantage over role-specific+fixed structure at hard difficulty, MM stands, and the "shared" framing turns out to be moot (both loci work, native is not uniquely better).
- **MIDDLE:** partial collapse (TIED-random degrades but stays well above chance) -- locates a graded contribution of geometry vs structure in the control; report as bounded, not a clean re-adjudication either way.
