## (b) Cheap decisive test

**Substrate-Cross-Disc-PILOT-1** (CPU-only, < 4 hr):

1. **Compute** algebra-vec cosine matrix on all atom pairs (a, b) where partition(a) != partition(b). Cost: ~5 min on 786 atoms.
2. **Flag** pairs with cos > 0.85 AND semantic-vec cos < 0.40 (high mechanism overlap + distinct domain). Pre-registered expected count: 50-150 pairs.
3. **Categorize-overlap filter**: keep only pairs where algebra-category(a) intersects algebra-category(b) on at least 1 of 13 (math taxonomy) ↔ 13 (science taxonomy) categories. Brings count to 20-60.
4. **Manual validation** (Research, ~30 min): for each flagged pair, score 0/1/2 on "productive analogue" rubric (0 = noise, 1 = trivial surface match, 2 = substrate-novel mechanism transfer).
5. **HARD-PASS:** >= 5 pairs score 2 (productive analogue). **HARD-FAIL:** zero pairs score 2 -> algebra-vec cross-partition cosine alone is insufficient signal.

The 5 enumerated triangle hypotheses (Q3) are pre-registered positive controls -- if pipeline ranks any of them in top 20, that's structural validity confirmation.

## (f) Top 5 cross-disc analogue search strategies (ranked)

Ranking criterion: productivity x feasibility x substrate-product-leverage (cost-adjusted)

### Strategy 1 (RANK 1): Algebra-vec cosine cross-partition cascade
**Implementation:** Pre-compute algebra-vec cosine matrix on all atom pairs across partitions. Flag pairs (algebra-cos > 0.85 AND semantic-cos < 0.40). Cascade through (i) category-overlap filter (13-category math <-> 13-category science) (ii) slipnet-relation triangle confirm (Strategy 4) (iii) productivity rubric.
**Expected analogue surfacing:** 20-60 candidates after gate; 5-15 productive after rubric. PRIMARY channel.
**Brain analogue:** MAC/FAC (Forbus-Gentner). Hippocampal MAC stage = semantic-vec coarse retrieval; PFC FAC stage = algebra-vec structural pass. Direct 30-year cognitive-science precedent.
**Tier-5 measurement value:** HIGHEST. Every productive analogue becomes a candidate substrate-product capability + Tier-5 surface signal.
**Cost:** ~5 min compute + ~30 min Research rubric per cycle.
**P_deflated (>= 5 productive / 30d):** 0.55 (lit-precedent strong; novel synthesis is the typed-relation gate).

### Strategy 2 (RANK 2): Multiplex community detection on cross-partition graph
**Implementation:** Build multiplex graph G where layers = (algebra-vec edges, semantic-vec edges, slipnet-relation edges, content-reference edges) and nodes = atoms across all partitions. Run k-clique percolation (Lehmann 2008 + Afsarmanesh 2016) for overlapping communities. Cross-partition communities = analogue clusters.
**Expected analogue surfacing:** 5-20 cross-partition COMMUNITIES (not pairs); each community = mechanism class + N atoms across M partitions. Captures HIGHER-ORDER analogues (triangle + quad) Strategy 1 misses.
**Brain analogue:** Default Mode Network exploration mode (Anticevic + Yu) -- DMN finds patterns across distant cortical regions; multiplex k-clique = computational DMN.
**Tier-5 measurement value:** HIGH. Communities of 4+ atoms across 3+ partitions = strong Tier-5 signal (literature rarely curates 4-way analogues).
**Cost:** ~20 min compute + ~1 hr Research community-quality audit.
**P_deflated:** 0.45 (multiplex community detection is mature; novel synthesis is layer-weighting calibration).

### Strategy 3 (RANK 3): Substrate ABC discovery (Swanson-style on typed-relation edges)
**Implementation:** For each atom A in partition P1, enumerate B atoms in partition P2 (algebra-vec adjacent). For each such B, enumerate C atoms in partition P3 (algebra-vec adjacent to B but NOT adjacent to A directly). Surface (A, B, C) triples where B is bridge concept. Filter by typed-relation chain (A --is_implementation_of--> B --is_instance_of--> C; the chain itself must type-check on 13-category algebra).
**Expected analogue surfacing:** 100-1000 candidate ABC triples raw; 10-30 pass type-check; 3-10 productive after rubric.
**Brain analogue:** Hippocampal pattern-completion + relational binding (Eichenbaum). Hippocampus links A-B and B-C without A-C direct experience.
**Tier-5 measurement value:** HIGHEST POTENTIAL but lower yield rate. ABC triples are the canonical Swanson hypothesis-generation pattern; productive ones are publishable.
**Cost:** ~1 hr compute + ~2 hr Research rubric (more candidates to triage).
**P_deflated:** 0.35 (Swanson lit is mature but high noise; substrate's typed-relation gate is novel).

### Strategy 4 (RANK 4): Slipnet-typed triangle confirmation (always-on gate)
**Implementation:** For any candidate analogue pair (a, b) from Strategy 1/2/3, REQUIRE: there exists a third atom c such that (a, c) and (b, c) share the SAME slipnet relation type (e.g., both are `implements_binding`, or both are `minimizes_energy`). If no such c exists OR the c relations are DIFFERENT types, REJECT pair as surface noise.
**Expected analogue surfacing:** Not a standalone strategy -- it's a GATE that runs on Strategy 1/2/3 outputs. Cuts false-positive rate >= 50% (P4 prediction).
**Brain analogue:** SME relation-matching (Gentner) + Companion Cognitive Architecture relational alignment. Brain analogue: anterior temporal lobe convergence -- abstracts the relation type then confirms across exemplars.
**Tier-5 measurement value:** MEDIUM (gate, not generator).
**Cost:** ~10 min per candidate.
**P_deflated:** 0.55 (gate efficacy is the novel claim; relation-typing is mature lit).

### Strategy 5 (RANK 5): Content-reference back-substitution analogue inference
**Implementation:** Per substrate_two_axes -- content-references axis captures atoms LITERALLY named in body. For any atom A that references atoms (B, C, D, ...) across partitions in its body, B/C/D are EXPLICITLY analogous in A's framing. Surface (A, B), (A, C), (A, D) as low-confidence analogues; then promote any pair via Strategy 4 gate.
**Expected analogue surfacing:** 50-200 candidates from content-references; 5-15 promoted via Strategy 4.
**Brain analogue:** Working memory + hippocampal-PFC online binding -- when WM holds (A, B, C, D), the brain encodes pairwise associations even without explicit comparison.
**Tier-5 measurement value:** LOW-MEDIUM (relies on Research seed authoring; not pure self-discovery; complementary to Strategy 1-3).
**Cost:** ~5 min compute + ~30 min Research rubric.
**P_deflated:** 0.40 (mechanical; productivity depends on authoring discipline; lower Tier-5 leverage).

## Productivity rubric (Q5 operationalized)

For each surfaced analogue, score 0/1/2 along 4 axes (max 8 per analogue):
1. **Mechanism specificity** (0 = abstract, 1 = named mechanism, 2 = mechanism with quantitative equation)
2. **Cross-partition genuineness** (0 = same partition under disguise, 1 = different partitions but topic overlap, 2 = different partitions AND distinct topic clusters)
3. **Substrate-product leverage** (0 = no capability implied, 1 = capability suggested, 2 = cell-test predicate emerges)
4. **Literature novelty** (0 = lit has explicit term, 1 = lit has component pieces but not full analogue, 2 = lit lacks the analogue)

**Productive analogue** = score >= 5/8 (rubric tuned to admit 5-15 productive per week from Strategy 1 cascade).
**Tier-5 candidate** = score >= 7/8 AND >= 1 cell-test predicate.

Per [[methodology-rule-8-substrate-content-sources-us-or-substrate]]: Research authors rubric; substrate-eval can auto-extend score via composite-C on cell-test result (BOTH-AND).

Per [[feedback-literature-is-not-oracle]]: literature-novelty=0 does NOT auto-disqualify; ANY substrate-validated mechanism that empirically lifts a capability is productive REGARDLESS of lit precedent.
