## (a) THE BIOLOGY -- how grid cells implement a robust, generalizing, exponential-capacity modular code

### A1. Exponential capacity from incommensurate moduli

Grid cells in medial entorhinal cortex fire in periodic hexagonal lattices organized into discrete **modules**, each sharing a firing period/scale but differing in spatial phase (Hafting, Fyhn, Molden, Moser & Moser 2005, *Nature*; module structure: Stensola, Stensola, Moser & Moser 2012, *Nature* -- modules form a small number of discrete scale groups, empirically ~4-5 in rat MEC, with a geometric progression in period ratio ~1.4-1.7x between adjacent modules, not a continuum). Fiete, Burak & Brookings (2008, *J Neurosci* 28(27)) formalize why combining K modules with pairwise-incommensurate (approximately coprime) periods gives a code whose UNAMBIGUOUS range grows as the PRODUCT of the individual periods -- the same combinatorial structure as a residue number system spanning `M = prod(m_i)` with only `sum(m_i)` states needed to represent it, i.e. range grows multiplicatively/exponentially in the number of modules while the representational cost per module stays small. Sreenivasan & Fiete (2011, *Nat Neurosci*, "Grid cells generate an analog error-correcting code for singularly precise neural computation") is the canonical result explicitly naming this a residue-number-system-style, error-correcting code and deriving that the achievable spatial resolution/range scales EXPONENTIALLY with the number of modules for a FIXED total neuron budget, dramatically outperforming a single-scale (unimodular) code of the same neuron count.

### A2. Robustness is NOT a free byproduct of exponential range -- it requires two specific, additional ingredients

This is the load-bearing biological fact for the verdict below. Neither the classical RNS/CRT literature nor the grid-cell literature treats "modular splitting" itself as robustness-conferring; robustness is bought separately, by:

1. **Population redundancy WITHIN each module** -- each module is not a single unit but a large population (Mosheiff, Agmon, Moriel & Burak follow-on work; e.g. a config with 8 modules of ~100 cells each is highly robust to ambiguity even over ranges far larger than any single module's period) -- this is structurally an RRNS-style redundancy (many "votes" per residue digit), not a property of the CRT combination step itself.
2. **A SOFT, probabilistic, iterative readout, not a hard deterministic CRT formula.** Per the fresh lit-scan: population-decoding treatments of grid-cell readout (Stemmler, Mathis & Herz 2012ish line of work on optimal/multiscale population coding for grid cells; and separately the belief-propagation / approximate-message-passing framing used in some computational models of multi-modulus neural decoding) model the combination-across-modules step as an approximate, uncertainty-weighted inference procedure -- closer to soft/joint maximum-likelihood decoding over the whole residue codeword than to an exact, no-redundancy CRT reconstruction. The Tolman-Eichenbaum Machine (Whittington et al. 2020, *Cell*) likewise recovers a unique state via a learned, conjunctive, error-tolerant combination (hippocampal binding of the entorhinal structural code with content), not a brittle closed-form decode.

### A3. The exact classical-math mirror of A2 (confirms, does not merely parallel, the biology)

Robust CRT theory (Li, Liang & Xia 2009, *IEEE Trans. Signal Processing* 57(11); Wang & Xia 2010, *IEEE TSP* 58(11); survey arXiv:1708.04089) proves that the ONLY way to make CRT reconstruction bounded-error rather than catastrophically wrong from one bad residue is either (i) add redundant "check" moduli beyond the minimum needed to span the range -- Redundant Residue Number Systems (RRNS; Szabo & Tanaka 1967; Barsi & Maestrini 1973) detect `n-k+1` and correct `floor((n-k)/2)` errors, directly analogous to a minimum-distance error-correcting code, at a real storage/dimension overhead that scales with the number of extra moduli -- or (ii) relax to a bounded-error regime by sharing a common factor among moduli, which only tolerates per-residue error below `~1/4 * gcd` of the shared factor -- a real but narrow tolerance window, not unlimited robustness. **Independent per-residue hard decoding with zero redundancy (RC2's exact design) is not covered by either fix and is the textbook-fragile configuration both literatures warn about.** Joint/maximum-likelihood decoding across the whole residue codeword (rather than independent per-channel hard decode) recovers tolerance that hard independent decode structurally cannot, because independent decode discards cross-channel joint-likelihood information -- this is the CS-side name for exactly what grid-cell population/soft-readout is doing biologically (A2).

### A4. Generalization

The periodic, modular structure means a location/state never explicitly visited is automatically representable as a NEW combination of already-learned periodic module phases -- generalization here is structural (a property of the tiling), not statistical (not learned per-location). This is uncontested in the literature and is the one property of grid coding that transfers cleanly regardless of the robustness question above.

**Confidence:** A1 and A4 are high-confidence, well-established, largely uncontested (cross-verified across the original lit-scan for the thalamic-router note plus this cycle's fresh scan, no contradicting source). A2/A3 (the robustness mechanism specifically requiring redundancy + soft decode) is also high-confidence on the classical-CRT side (uncontested, 50+ year old coding theory) and medium-confidence on the direct grid-cell-population-coding side (an actively developed area; the exact functional form of population-pooling SNR for grid modules is not as crisply nailed down as the classical RNS robustness results, per the earlier thalamic-router drill's own honest flag).

## (b) MAPPING TO THE SUBSTRATE -- does multi-modulus help the OPEN problems?

### B1. This is not a fresh hypothesis -- it has already been tested, on this substrate, and it lost

`exp_multihop_router_crt_residue_addressed_v1` (RC2) built EXACTLY the naive rendering of a multi-modulus/CRT address: two disjoint sub-blocks (`ADDR_DIMS=(384,384)`, coprime moduli `(4,5)`, product=20=N_PARTITIONS), each independently argmax-decoded, combined via the deterministic CRT formula -- glass-box, parameter-free, zero redundancy, zero joint/soft decoding. Verified on-disk (`data/exp_multihop_router_crt_residue_addressed_v1_smoke/metrics.json`, HARD_PASS-on-machinery, 3 seeds): **route_acc crt=0.415 vs naive_centroid=0.503 (crt WORSE by -0.088); e2e crt=0.147 vs naive=0.237.** The CRT mechanism did not just fail to help -- it underperformed the far simpler full-vector baseline it was meant to beat. Per A2/A3 above, this is exactly what the literature predicts for that specific rendering: no redundant check-moduli, no soft/joint decode, no downstream cleanup pass on the reconstructed address. The prior research drill (`research_thalamic_router_RC2_mechanism_RC3_decision_2026-07-05.md`) independently reached the identical conclusion from the classical-CRT + grid-cell literatures and formally SHELVED both a redesigned RC2 and a learned RC3 alternative (P_deflated=0.20, converging with a 6/6 internal learned-router failure record on an analogous problem) given no current consumer needed dedicated routing at all (regenerative attractor-cleanup composition already carries the chain).

### B2. The CURRENT page router's actual gap is not the one multi-modulus addresses

The router this task is asking about (`exp_addressing_learned_noise_robust_page_router_v1`) is architecturally different from RC2 -- DG-analog fixed expand+sparsify feeding a CA3-analog multi-page attractor search -- and it is NOT noise-broken: end-to-end retrieval degrades gracefully under cue corruption (0.969 @ sigma=0.3 down to 0.575 @ sigma=0.9), carried by the CA3-analog multi-page attractor search (M>1 candidate-page probing), not by DG-expansion (measured inert in the near-orthogonal regime tested). The one property this router has NOT yet been tested on is **correlated keys** -- and the substrate's own prior capacity analysis for correlated keys (`research_correlated_key_capacity_hopfield_fhrr_2026-07-01.md`) already identifies the load-bearing fix menu for that specific failure mode: decorrelate keys at write time, OR route bind-structured queries through resonator-network factorization (which exploits correlation-as-structure rather than fighting it). Neither of those two established fixes is "split the address into multiple moduli" -- multi-modulus/CRT addressing does not appear in that analysis at all, and there is no mechanistic reason from either literature scan in this note to expect it would: CRT's exponential-range property is about REPRESENTING a large space compactly, not about decorrelating or structurally exploiting correlated content, which is a completely different problem. A cheaper, non-CRT lever targeting the exact same correlated-key gap is already in flight (a fair-test decorrelation front-end for sparse-on-correlated-keys, smoke result = MIDDLE/partial recovery) -- this is the natural first thing to finish before reaching for a heavier, previously-falsified mechanism class.

### B3. Where CRT machinery genuinely IS strong on this substrate -- a different regime, not this one

This project's OWN prior CRT wins are real and worth naming precisely so they are not confused with the routing question: `crt_module_scaling_battery_v1` (HARD_PASS, 3-seed full, 800x exponential capacity multiplier via CRT module composition) and `crt_multi_scale_grid_cell_composition_v1` (HP-SMOKE, CRT product exact `1001=7*11*13`, 143x, algebraically deterministic). Both of these are **exact, noiseless algebraic composition** tasks -- the CRT reconstruction is applied to a deterministic, error-free residue codeword, which is precisely the regime the classical CRT formula is valid and optimal for. RC2 (and any router built for the current, noisy, content-addressed page-routing problem) is a fundamentally different regime -- decoding a residue address FROM a noisy, crosstalk-corrupted state -- which is exactly the regime A2/A3 says requires redundancy and soft decoding that the substrate's proven CRT wins do not need and were never tested under.

### B4. Cost, if built the RIGHT way (RRNS + soft/joint decode) -- not free, but boundable

Per the RNS-cost lit-scan: RRNS-style redundancy adds real dimension/bandwidth overhead that scales with the number of extra check-moduli (roughly linear in correctable-error count, analogous to a minimum-distance code's parity overhead) -- this substrate's `ADDR_DIMS` budget (768 of 8192 dims, ~9.4%, in RC2's rendering) would need to grow to add check-moduli, trading off against the identity/value region. Joint/ML decoding across the full residue codeword (rather than independent hard decode) is the more architecturally significant cost: it requires either an explicit combinatorial search over residue-hypothesis combinations (expensive if done naively) or a message-passing/belief-propagation-style approximate joint decoder (the CS-side analog of the grid-cell population-soft-readout mechanism) -- a genuinely new decode primitive, not a resize of the existing hard-argmax step. Both fixes are real engineering, consistent with the prior drill's finding that "the known fixes are genuine redesigns, not a resize."

## (c) VERDICT + SEQUENCING

**DEFER.** Do not build a grid-cell/CRT multi-modulus router now. This is not "insufficient evidence to decide" -- it is a decision with a directly-on-point, already-executed negative result on this exact substrate (RC2, -0.088 vs naive) that the fresh biology and coding-theory lit-scans explain rather than contradict: the naive/hard-decision rendering of multi-modulus addressing is precisely the configuration both literatures identify as structurally fragile, and the two real fixes (RRNS redundancy, soft/joint decoding) are substantive redesigns with no current evidence they would even be pointed at the right problem (the current router's open gap is correlated keys, whose established fix is decorrelation or resonator bind-exploitation, not modular splitting).

**Revisit trigger (concrete, not vague):** build a v2 grid-cell/CRT lever ONLY if BOTH of the following become true:
1. The in-flight, cheaper, non-CRT correlated-key lever (decorrelation front-end / resonator bind-structure exploitation) is run to completion on the page router specifically and plateaus below the product need (not just partial recovery at smoke -- a full, VET'd result that leaves a real capability gap), AND
2. The specific gap that remains is a RANGE/CAPACITY gap (needing to address a combinatorially larger page space than a single projection can cleanly separate) rather than a pure noise-robustness gap (which the current CA3-analog attractor search already handles gracefully) -- i.e., multi-modulus's actual biological superpower (A1, exponential range from incommensurate moduli) is the thing actually needed, not a robustness patch it cannot supply "for free."

**If that trigger fires**, the cell to build is NOT a repeat of RC2 -- it is the RRNS-redundant, soft/joint-decode design the biology and the classical literature both converge on (extra check-moduli per B4, plus an approximate joint/message-passing decode replacing the hard independent argmax), pre-registered against RC2's own numbers as the baseline it must beat (route >= 0.503+0.05, e2e >= 0.237+0.05, at minimum, before it earns the label "fixed").

No cell is warranted now. No pointer-only exp_dev hand-off is filed (per the "only if a cell is warranted" clause) -- this note's trigger section IS the standing spec for when to file one.

## Cheap decisive test (deferred -- specified for when the trigger fires, not dispatched now)

If/when the trigger in (c) fires, the cheapest decisive test is a 2-arm rebuild reusing RC2's exact harness (`experiments/exp_multihop_router_crt_residue_addressed_v1.py` or the page-router's own harness, whichever the live gap is on): (A) `CRT_HARD` = RC2's exact prior design, reproduced as the pre-registered floor to beat (route=0.415, e2e=0.147, on file, no re-run needed as a baseline number); (B) `CRT_RRNS_SOFT` = add 1 redundant check-modulus (e.g. moduli `(4,5,3)`, product still >= N_PARTITIONS with one error-detecting extra channel) AND replace independent hard argmax with a joint/soft combination (confidence-weighted combination across residue channels, or a bounded search over the top-2 hypotheses per modulus) before CRT reconstruction. Cheapest falsifier: does (B) even beat naive_centroid's route=0.503? If not, the redesign itself is insufficient and the mechanism class should be closed, not iterated further.

## Falsifiable predictions (for the deferred cell, HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL, pre-registered now for when dispatched)

- **HARD-PASS:** `CRT_RRNS_SOFT` route_acc >= naive_centroid + 0.05 AND e2e >= naive_centroid_e2e + 0.05 AND beats `CRT_HARD` (RC2's floor) by >= 0.15 route_acc (confirms redundancy+soft-decode, not just resize, was the load-bearing fix) AND range/capacity benefit is demonstrable (the redesigned router successfully addresses a page space >= 2x the size a single-projection router's separation budget can cleanly handle at the same false-positive rate).
- **HARD-FAIL:** `CRT_RRNS_SOFT` <= naive_centroid + 0.02 (redundancy+soft-decode still doesn't help -- the mechanism class itself, not just the naive rendering, is closed for this substrate's noise regime) OR `CRT_RRNS_SOFT` is not meaningfully better than `CRT_HARD` (the redesign's specific added machinery -- redundancy or soft decode -- wasn't load-bearing, contradicting the biology/coding-theory mechanism story) OR the range/capacity benefit fails to materialize (multi-modulus buys no more addressable range than the single-projection router already has at matched dimension budget -- would mean the exponential-capacity argument doesn't transfer at this substrate's N/V scale).
- **MIDDLE:** real lift over `CRT_HARD` but short of beating naive_centroid, or a capacity benefit without a matching noise-robustness benefit (or vice versa) -- route to isolating which of the two added ingredients (redundancy vs soft-decode) is carrying what share of the lift before investing further.

**Brain-check (mandatory if ever dispatched and it fails):** verify BOTH ingredients (redundant moduli AND soft/joint decode) were genuinely present, not just one -- per A2, the brain's own robustness is a two-part solution and a partial rendering (e.g. soft decode alone, no redundancy) is not a fair test of the full mechanism.

## Substrate-product implications

- The honest product story stays exactly what the prior thalamic-router note already established: this substrate's compositional/addressing reliability comes from inspectable, redundant ATTRACTOR-STYLE cleanup and search (CA3-analog multi-page probing, resonator factorization), not from a fragile algebraic bolt-on. Grid-cell-style multi-modulus addressing is a real, biologically-grounded lever, but on THIS substrate's evidence it is a capacity/range tool for exact composition, not a noise-robustness or correlated-key-generalization tool -- conflating the two would be an honest-framing risk to avoid in any future product narrative about "brain-inspired addressing."
- If a future use case genuinely needs to address a page/partition space larger than a single fixed-projection router can cleanly separate (a RANGE problem, not a robustness problem), the RRNS-redundant + soft-decode design specified in Section (c) is the correctly-scoped next lever -- but shipping the naive hard-decode rendering again (RC2's design, under a different name) would predictably repeat the same failure, now with prior evidence making that predictable rather than a surprise.
- Do not build multi-modulus addressing as a hedge "because grid cells do it" without the Section (c) trigger -- per the anisotropy-cell and RC2 precedents both, geometric/biological plausibility of a mechanism is not sufficient evidence of end-to-end benefit on this substrate; only a paired, ablated, end-to-end test against the specific gap it's meant to close is.
