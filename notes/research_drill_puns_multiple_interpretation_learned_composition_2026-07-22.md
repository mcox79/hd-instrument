## (1) Brain mechanism: multiple meanings + what values the rare one

Lexical access is **exhaustive, not context-gated**: Swinney (1979) cross-modal priming showed BOTH meanings of an ambiguous word activate within ~200ms regardless of biasing context; Tanenhaus, Leiman & Seidenberg (1979) confirmed but showed frequency orders the activation (dominant meaning strongest/fastest -- "reordered access," not exclusive access). Duffy, Morris & Rayner (1988) eye-movement data give the "subordinate bias effect": when context demands the rare sense, readers show LONGER fixations/regressions -- direct behavioral evidence the dominant sense is tried FIRST even when wrong, and correction is effortful, not free. Giora's graded salience hypothesis (2003) generalizes this: the most SALIENT (frequent/prototypical, not necessarily context-fit) meaning is always accessed first; humor/puns specifically require retrieving the salient-but-suppressed subordinate sense once the dominant one fails. Coulson & Kutas (2001) show the ERP signature of this repair directly: joke punchlines elicit an N400 (integration difficulty under the wrong sense) followed by a frontal positivity resembling reanalysis/P600, i.e. a two-stage detect-then-repair process, not graded reweighting.

**The mechanism that VALUES the rare reading is therefore not upfront weighting -- it is a downstream INCOHERENCE-DETECTION that triggers RE-SEARCH over senses already (exhaustively) activated.** This is exactly the two-channel ACC-conflict + coherence-signal escalation mechanism already brain-drilled in-house (research_drill_metacognitive_calibration_escalation_trigger_2026-07-21) and partially built (atom 29411's contradiction-flag). The reader's frequency-wall is not a valuation failure per se -- it is the ABSENCE of an escalation trigger that fires when the MFS-bound reading is incoherent.

## (2) Brain mechanism: composition learning -- grammar-like, not meaning-like

Usage-based/construction-grammar acquisition (Tomasello 2003; Goldberg 1995/2006) is item-based-to-abstract: children first produce fixed verb-island utterances tied to specific lexical items, then GENERALIZE slot-and-frame schemas across many witnessed instances (type-frequency drives productivity, not innate rule-triggering). This is structurally the SAME generalize-across-cases-into-an-abstract-rule pattern already validated in-substrate twice (breadth read-drives-knowledge loop, atom 29424, ask-rate declines as cases accumulate; the meaning-correction case+sleep loop). Lake & Baroni's MLC (Nature 2023) is the computational analog: meta-training over many compositional episodes yields a genuinely acquired (not stipulated) systematic-generalization capacity -- exactly the "learned, not algebra-free" signature the CG program has been chasing (per the 2026-07-22 adversarial drill).

**Honest verdict: composition-learning is grammar-like (generalizes to novel structure), NOT meaning-like (per-item, non-generalizing) -- the USER's bet holds** at the behavioral/developmental level. Caveat (do not over-claim): this is corpus/behavioral evidence, not a neural mechanism confirmation equivalent to Frankland & Greene's role-population data; the same absence-of-neural-binding-signature caution flagged in the HP2 drill applies here too.

## (3) Design: glass-box atomize+sleep composition cell, puns as gate

Apply the SAME loop already proven for breadth (atomize specific cases -> periodic sleep-consolidation into a general rule) to COMPOSITION: (a) ATOMIZE specific sentence-glue instances encountered in real text as inspectable atoms -- each atom = (parse-frame, selected sense, role-binding, resolved-coherent or not); (b) SLEEP-CONSOLIDATE = periodically abstract recurring glue patterns across many atoms into a general construction schema (e.g. "NP-V-NP with class-X verb -> patient-bind," generalizing Tomasello's item-to-schema step), analogous to the existing case+sleep meaning-correction loop.

**Puns as the can-fail gate:** build a set of sentences (leverage the already-flagged polysemous VerbNet predicates -- leave/meet/turn/get/fall/pull -- plus WordNet homograph pairs) where context selects the SUBORDINATE sense. Frequency-guess (MFS) FAILS by construction -- this is the real, non-vacuous baseline the frequency-wall predicts, unlike natural running text where MFS wins almost everywhere (the exhausted WSD axis).

**The sign-flip CG discriminator (the actual novel test):** if the composition/gluing operator genuinely USES meaning-structure to resolve the pun (escalate on incoherence, re-glue to the coherent subordinate sense), then DESTROYING meaning-structure (scrambling selectional-fit information / disabling the coherence check) should HURT it -- real-beats-random, the opposite sign from 29437's free-algebra signature (random-beats-real under fixed FHRR binding). If scrambling does NOT hurt (or helps), that is the SAME free-algebra signature restated on a new task -- honest MM, not CG.

## (4) Cheapest can-fail first experiment

Do NOT build atomize+sleep infrastructure first. Cheapest gate: on a few dozen hand-curated pun/subordinate-sense sentences (matched controls = monosemous/dominant-sense sentences, must NOT regress), test whether the ALREADY-BUILT escalation channel (atom 29411 contradiction-flag OR a VerbNet selectional-restriction violation check, reusing the WSD frame-drill's design) can override MFS at all, with zero new learning machinery. This is the prerequisite-viability probe: if the single-instance escalation mechanism can't get traction on clean puns, the atomize+sleep generalization loop has nothing to consolidate and should not be built yet.

**HARD-PASS (proceed to full atomize+sleep + sign-flip cell):** escalation overrides MFS correctly on >=60% of held-out subordinate-sense sentences, with <5% false-escalation on monosemous controls.
**HARD-FAIL (do not build the loop on this premise):** escalation fires <20% of the time on true puns, or false-escalates >20% on controls -- the incoherence detector isn't sharp enough yet; the frequency-wall persists even with the added machinery, and the deficit is signal-quality (selectional-restriction coverage), not the composition-learning loop itself.
