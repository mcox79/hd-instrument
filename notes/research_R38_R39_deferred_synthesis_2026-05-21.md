## PART 1: R38 V2 SUBSTRATE HYPERBOLIC SYNTHESIS

### Pass 1 lit-scan key findings (subagent `a40a958048ff3bf87`)

**Hyperbolic geometry has reached experimental tractability**:
- Boettcher et al. arXiv:2105.01087 (PRB 2022) — "Crystallography of
  hyperbolic lattices"; establishes hyperbolic Bloch theory via
  Fuchsian groups; reduces simulation to finite irrep equations.
  GAME-CHANGER for hyperbolic-tight-binding tractability.
- Kollár group arXiv:1802.09549 (Nature 2019) — circuit-QED hyperbolic
  spin platform (≈100 qubits, cryogenic, quantum).
- Lenggenhager-Dey-Bzdušek-Maciejko arXiv:2407.09601 (PRL 135 076604,
  2025) — hyperbolic spin liquids on {8,3}.

**Bethe lattice / hierarchical structure**:
- Okunishi-Takayanagi arXiv:2310.12601 (PTEP 2024) — Bethe-lattice Ising
  → holographic RG; bulk correlations exponential, boundary correlations
  power-law. **Substrate-applicable framework if substrate is re-
  architected.**
- Okunishi-Nishino arXiv:2407.14689 (PTEP 2024) — extension via CTMRG.
- Akcan-Gündüç cond-mat/0407062 — dilute Hopfield: capacity per
  synapse INCREASES as graph becomes sparser. Strongest existing
  hyperbolic-style capacity argument.

**Critical NEGATIVE findings**:
- **No 2024-2026 paper gives explicit α_c for Hopfield on {p,q}
  hyperbolic tiling** — must infer via dilute-Hopfield literature
- **Hyperbolic associative memory hardware is unprecedented**: field
  bifurcated (hyperbolic = quantum QEC; AM = Euclidean / fully-connected)
- **Modern dense AM exponential-capacity** (Lucibello-Mézard PRL 2024;
  Chen 2026 ICLR; Hu 2024 NeurIPS spherical-code) is in Euclidean
  space. Hyperbolic would need to beat exponential — no paper suggests
  this is possible.
- **Moreira et al. WACV 2024** (arXiv:2309.10013): "hyperbolic ≈ Euclidean
  for few-shot when fairly tuned." Negative result that haunts the
  V2 hyperbolic roadmap room.

### Pass 2 substrate-applicability assessment (R38)

**5 substrate-product failure modes** (per subagent):

1. **"Scale-free already won" trap**: scale-free random graphs (γ≈2)
   capture most of the per-synapse capacity gain hyperbolic geometry
   promises, without the simulation cost.

2. **Loss of analytic Bloch structure on finite patches**: Boettcher
   tractability theorem assumes INFINITE Fuchsian symmetry; a finite
   open-boundary {5,4} patch loses it. Substrate would pay simulation
   cost without analytic gain.

3. **Hierarchy mismatch**: hyperbolic embeddings excel when data is
   hierarchical. VSA codes are deliberately FLAT / uniformly-distributed.
   Forcing onto curved geometry imposes structure data doesn't have.

4. **Dense-AM landslide**: modern AM is EXPONENTIAL-capacity already in
   Euclidean. Hyperbolic substrate would need super-exponential — no
   paper suggests this is possible.

5. **Engineering hyperbolic associative-memory hardware is
   unprecedented**: all physical hyperbolic platforms are quantum
   (Kollár). Classical hyperbolic-graph ASIC is a research project,
   not engineering project.

## PART 2: R39 CONTINUOUS BURGERS-FIELD SUBSTRATE SYNTHESIS

### Pass 1 lit-scan key findings (subagent `a7eff37ecf28c9e9d`)

**Continuous Burgers-vector field foundations (Kröner/Acharya)**:
- Acharya arXiv:2502.15003 (2025) — Field Dislocation Mechanics gold-
  standard with explicit Burgers vector conservation as a-priori
  constraint. **Requires 3D Euclidean reference body — substrate-
  incompatible.**

**Bera et al. 2025 arXiv:2505.23069 deep reading** (closest substrate-
applicable analog):
- Continuous Burgers vector b_i = -∮_L (∂u_i/∂x^k) dx^k on closed loop L
  around Eshelby-like plastic event in 2D molecular-dynamics glass
- Uses REAL-SPACE NON-AFFINE DISPLACEMENT FIELD — requires 2D/3D
  embedding with reference configuration. **No analog exists for
  substrate (N bipolar spins σ ∈ {±1}^N without spatial coordinates).**
- Bera et al. DO NOT claim Burgers vector conservation in glasses —
  it's a plasticity DIAGNOSTIC, not a conserved current
- Restricted to 2D in published work; 3D analog is "hedgehog defects"
  (Wu et al. Nat. Commun. 2025) — different topological charge

**Critical NEGATIVE findings**:

**3 independent obstructions** (subagent verdict):

1. **No spatial embedding**: Hopfield is graph + ±1 states; continuous
   Burgers requires continuous displacement field on metric reference
   body. Inventing one is not coarse-graining — it's choosing a target
   geometry, unconstrained by substrate dynamics.

2. **No translational symmetry to break**: Burgers vectors physically
   encode broken continuous translational symmetry. Z_2 spin Hamiltonians
   break only Z_2; natural defect invariants are domain walls (π_0),
   NOT vector charges (π_1).

3. **No conservation in disordered media**: even Bera 2025's 2D glass
   Burgers vector is NOT conserved — plasticity indicator. "Topological
   protection" framing would import a property parent literature
   EXPLICITLY DISCLAIMS.

**Closest adjacent work (all NEGATIVE for substrate)**:
- Lewenstein et al. persistent-homology / TDA — only Betti-number
  invariants, no vector charge
- Graph de Rham cohomology (arXiv:2003.09704) — discrete 1-forms but
  no plastic-distortion analog
- Zhang cond-mat/0703405 — "topology" means graph topology, not field-
  theoretic

### Pass 2 substrate-applicability assessment (R39)

**Substrate-product value analysis**:

**Topological protection (substrate noise-immunity)**: NO
