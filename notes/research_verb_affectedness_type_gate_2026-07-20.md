## HEADLINE

**Affectedness is not a discrete category the field supports splitting into 3 hard buckets — it is a GRADED SCORE, and every serious framework treats it that way.** Dowty's (1991, *Language* 67:547-619) Proto-Patient entailments are explicitly a prototype/family-resemblance cluster: the Argument-Selection Principle counts how many of 5 entailments (change of state, incremental theme, causally affected, stationary relative to another's motion, existence-dependent) a candidate satisfies — a **count**, not a class label — and Dowty is explicit that this produces degrees of patienthood, not a clean partition. Beavers (2011, *NLLT* 29:335-370) operationalizes this as an explicit **monotonic affectedness SCALE** (quantized change > non-quantized/scalar change > unspecified change/state > no change), with entailment-strength tests (e.g. "X V-ed Y" vs. "X V-ed AT/ON Y" conative-alternation felicity) that place a verb's TYPICAL affectedness at a scale POSITION, not a category. Tsunoda (1985, *J. Linguistics* 21:385-396) draws case-marking cut-lines across a continuum (effective-action > perception > pursuit > knowledge > feeling > relationship > ability) but the cut-lines are language-particular and pragmatic, not evidence the underlying dimension is itself discrete — it is a coarse CUT over a continuum, imposed by grammar for marking purposes, not a claim that affectedness itself comes in 3 or 7 natural kinds. On the light-verb end, Grimshaw & Mester (1988, *LI* 19:205-232) frame "argument transfer" (the light verb borrows its theta-grid from the associated noun) as also gradient in practice — "take the book" (literal, full patient) to "take a walk" (fully light, no patient) sit on a lexicalization CLINE with intermediate cases, not a clean binary; PropBank's own `.LV` roleset split and the PARSEME shared-task's `LVC.full`/`LVC.cause` categories are the field's practical, still-imperfect attempt to draw a workable line across that cline for annotation purposes. **Net: the task brief's proposed hard `{affected-patient / target-not-affected / no-patient / abstain}` 4-way categorical gate is linguistically over-confident as stated — the honest design is a graded Dowty/Beavers-style score with a threshold-defined ABSTAIN band in the middle, not a forced discrete classifier.** This is the single biggest revision this drill makes to the task brief, and it is the headline.

## Cheap decisive test

Build a **verb-level graded affectedness score**, not a 3-way classifier: for each verb (or VerbNet class), compute a scalar in a bounded range from three glass-box, static, license-navigable sources, reusing the single-edge lookup-store-recall-generalize machinery already validated at atom 29390:

1. **Change-of-state end (high score):** VerbNet class membership in a causative-inchoative alternation class (`break-45.1`, `other_cos-45.4`, etc.) — verified via the alternation DIAGNOSTIC itself where possible (does the verb occur both transitively and intransitively with the same NP as subject in-corpus: "the vase broke" / "X broke the vase"?), which is the TEXT-INTERNAL framing already flagged as candidate #1(b) in `research_brain_patienthood_affectedness_grounding_2026-07-20.md` — distinct from VerbNet SELRESTRS (already coverage-blocked/closed at atom 29392; SELRESTRS encodes semantic TYPE restrictions like `+animate`/`+concrete` on a role, not CLASS membership / alternation behavior, which is what this score needs).
2. **Target/theme end (low-but-nonzero score):** Levin's search/perception/cognition verb classes (`search-35.2`, `sight-30.2`, etc. — no alternation, prepositionally-marked or oblique object, no entailed change) score near the bottom of Beavers' scale but above zero (an entity IS involved, just not changed).
3. **No-patient end (near-zero score):** PropBank `.LV` roleset flag (where it exists) + a static PARSEME-style light-verb-construction list (do/take/make/give/have/get/pay + noun) as a lookup table — verbs/collocations on this list score near zero.

**Design gate (per feedback-experiment-design-gate, verify at smoke before full run):**
1. **Real baseline** = current behavior: label the grammatical object as patient always (no gate, no score) — the same baseline framing as the task brief, run on the identical item set.
2. **Discriminator can-fail:** the score must be able to fail to separate classes — construct the eval so verbs with sparse/absent VerbNet or PropBank `.LV` coverage are explicitly tracked as a COVERAGE-FAILURE bucket, separate from a DISCRIMINATION-FAILURE bucket (same "diagnose sparsity before declaring the mechanism refuted" discipline already used for the sibling type-lookup anchor, given atom 29392's coverage-block precedent).
3. **Difficulty actually on:** the eval MUST include, alongside clean change-of-state and clean target verbs, (a) genuinely gradient cases — surface-contact verbs ("touch", "hit" — some but debatable change), and locative/spray-load alternation items (the "holistic effect" ambiguity, Anderson 1971) — and (b) light-verb/no-patient items, since none currently exist in the project's construction gold.
4. **One variable differs:** only the addition of the graded score (as a confidence-weighting input, not a hard filter — see wiring below) on top of the existing patient-assignment vote; same items, same gold, same scoring.

**Test corpus, honestly scoped:** `data/gold_construction_argstruct_ewt_v1/gold_construction_argstruct_ewt_v1.json` (136 items, 8 constructions x 16-24 each) supplies TWO of the three needed buckets directly: `prep_governed_patient` (16 items — "What are you searching for?", "Peter is looking...for a job" — currently mislabeled as "patient" in the gold schema's own field name, which is exactly the target-not-affected class this gate should score low) and most of `passive_by_agent`/`locative_alternation`/`simple_svo` (mixed, but containing genuine change-of-state verbs — approve, fill, break-class). **It does NOT contain a light-verb/no-patient bucket** — the corpus-build metadata explicitly excludes stative possession/relation verbs from the cue-conflict slice and light-verb constructions were never a modeled construction category at all. Scoring the no-patient end of the scale requires a **small new slice** pulled from PropBank `.LV`-tagged sentences or the PARSEME MWE corpus — a real, modest, un-avoidable data-gap this drill surfaces (not present in the task brief's framing, which assumed the existing gold covered all three classes).

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS:** the graded score, thresholded at design-gate-chosen cutpoints, (a) places the median `prep_governed_patient` item's score materially below the median change-of-state item's score (>= 0.15 absolute gap on a normalized [0,1] scale) with the coverage-failure bucket excluded and reported separately; (b) places light-verb/no-patient test items (from the new PropBank `.LV`/PARSEME slice) at or near the bottom of the scale, below the `prep_governed_patient` median; (c) the ABSTAIN band (score falling between the target-cluster and change-of-state-cluster) captures gradient/contact/spray-load items at a MATERIALLY higher rate than it falsely captures clean high- or low-score items (a specific false-abstain-rate check, not just "abstain a lot"). This would mean: a real, glass-box, verb-class-level affectedness signal exists and can feed the self-monitoring/confidence layer as a graded weight, without needing a forced discrete label.

**HARD-FAIL:** either (i) the score fails to separate the `prep_governed_patient` and change-of-state clusters by a meaningful margin (< 0.05 gap) after excluding coverage-failure items — meaning the target-vs-patient distinction is NOT actually recoverable from verb-class-level Dowty/Beavers/alternation features, and the whole graded-score approach collapses to noise; or (ii) coverage on the actual gold verb set is too sparse (VerbNet class + alternation-diagnostic + PropBank `.LV` jointly resolve < 50% of the 136-item verb set) — a coverage failure distinct from (i), inheriting the same risk atom 29392 already demonstrated for SELRESTRS and that may generalize to class-membership lookup too; or (iii) the abstain band swallows the majority of ALL items (> 50%), making the gate useless regardless of whether it discriminates correctly in principle; or (iv) this result turns out to be a straightforward re-run of atom 29373's already-closed finding ("curated Levin/Dowty proto-patient class signal redundant with structural signal") under a new name — this drill's position is that the FUNCTIONAL ROLE is different (a graded confidence/abstain-weight feeding the self-monitoring vote, not a standalone per-instance patient disambiguator, which is what 29373 tested), but this distinction is asserted, not yet independently verified, and must be checked explicitly before claiming this isn't just 29373 again.

**Must-fail control:** a randomly-permuted verb-to-score mapping must not separate the change-of-state/target/no-patient clusters; if it does, the eval harness has a leakage/construction problem independent of the hypothesis.

## Cross-thread synthesis

### 1. Brain/linguistics: Dowty as the operational core, confirmed graded not categorical

The lit-scan sub-agent confirms, with citation-level detail, what `research_brain_patienthood_affectedness_grounding_2026-07-20.md` already established qualitatively and adds the missing quantitative/structural texture: Dowty's Proto-Patient entailments are a **prototype cluster concept with degrees of membership**, not a taxonomy — the Argument-Selection Principle is explicitly a comparison ("the argument with the GREATEST NUMBER of entailments"), which only makes sense if entailment-count is the operative representation, not a class label. Beavers' scale is explicitly **monotonic** (each tier entails everything below it), constructed from entailment-strength diagnostics rather than a taxonomy of verb types, and is the field's most direct operationalization of "how affected" as a number. Tsunoda's hierarchy is a **coarse cut over the same continuum** for a different purpose (case-marking typology), not independent evidence for discreteness — languages draw the case-marking line at different points on the SAME underlying scale, which if anything argues AGAINST there being a small number of natural discrete affectedness kinds. The sub-agent explicitly flags gradient cases the field itself treats as genuinely in-between: surface-contact verbs ("touch", "hit" — debatable/partial change) and the locative/spray-load alternation's "holistic effect" (Anderson 1971 — "load hay onto the truck" vs. "load the truck with hay": which argument counts as wholly-affected depends on which is realized as direct object, a construction-level not lexical-level fact) — these are not edge-case noise, they are the field's own textbook illustrations that a hard cutoff is unmotivated.

### 2. Light-verb/no-patient end: resources exist, but the cline problem repeats

Grimshaw & Mester's argument-transfer account gives the linguistic warrant for treating a light verb's surface object as NOT a patient of the light verb itself (the light verb's theta-grid is borrowed/underspecified; the real predicate — and its patient, if any — belongs to the associated noun). The sub-agent confirms PropBank maintains dedicated `.LV` rolesets for a meaningful subset of common light verbs (splitting literal "take.01" from light-verb senses) and that PARSEME's verbal-MWE shared task annotates `LVC.full`/`LVC.cause` as distinct categories from ordinary transitive uses — both are genuine, machine-readable, glass-box-legal, already-existing static resources (same legal status as the VerbNet/PropBank/ATOMIC precedents established in the sibling notes). The honest caveat, consistent with the categorical-vs-graded headline: neither resource treats light-verb-hood as fully binary either — coverage is a a curated LIST (necessarily incomplete against the productive, cline-like nature of light-verb-hood in running text), and boundary cases (partially-bleached uses) exist. This is the same shape of imperfection VerbNet SELRESTRS showed at atom 29392 (coarse, incomplete, but real where it applies) — the recommendation is to treat the `.LV`/PARSEME lookup as a real but partial-coverage near-zero-score anchor, not a complete no-patient detector.

### 3. Connection to prior closures — what this drill is and is NOT re-litigating

- **29375 (text-derivation, per-instance):** stays closed. This drill is verb-class-level (a TYPE fact about the verb), not a per-instance disambiguator between two candidate NPs — same scope discipline already established for the type-level lookup drill.
- **29392 (VerbNet SELRESTRS coverage-blocked):** stays closed for what it tested — semantic-TYPE restrictions (`+animate`/`+concrete`) on an already-identified role. This drill's primary signal is a DIFFERENT VerbNet-derived fact — class membership via the causative-inchoative alternation — which the brain-patienthood note already flagged as its #1-ranked, structurally distinct candidate. The coverage-sparsity RISK likely generalizes (VerbNet is the same underlying resource, "coarse and incomplete" was the 29392 verdict), so this drill inherits that risk honestly rather than assuming a fresh resource escapes it.
- **29373 (curated Levin/Dowty signal, "redundant with structural signal"):** the least resolved connection. This drill's position — that a graded score feeding a CONFIDENCE/ABSTAIN weight is functionally different from a standalone per-instance patient-disambiguation signal — is a reasonable distinction but is ASSERTED here, not independently verified; the design-gate for any future cell built from this note should explicitly re-check whether this is genuinely new signal or the same closed finding re-packaged, per HARD-FAIL condition (iv) above.

### 4. Wiring into the Step-2 integrated vote

Given the graded verdict, the score should NOT be implemented as a hard pre-filter that blocks or forces a branch before patient assignment (as the task brief's `{affected-patient / target-not-affected / no-patient / abstain}` framing implied). Instead: the score is a **confidence/weight input INTO the existing Step-2 vote**, run first (cheap, static lookup, verb-class-level, no per-sentence cost beyond a table lookup), that (a) down-weights or discounts the "grammatical-object-is-patient" vote in proportion to how far the verb sits from the change-of-state end of the scale, and (b) when the score falls in the pre-registered gradient/abstain band, contributes toward the ALREADY-BUILT metacognitive abstain/confidence output (the CG'd self-monitoring layer) rather than adding a new discrete decision branch. This reuses existing machinery (single-edge lookup at 29390-shape, the confidence/abstain layer already validated) instead of introducing new architecture, consistent with the standing recombination-not-novel-research posture.

## Substrate-product implications

- Cheap, glass-box, build-time-only: three static lookup sources (VerbNet class+alternation, PropBank `.LV`, PARSEME LVC list), no runtime LLM, reuses the validated single-edge lookup pipeline and the existing confidence/abstain machinery — no new architecture required even if HARD-PASS.
- The headline finding (graded, not categorical) is itself a genuine course-correction on the task brief's proposed design, delivered BEFORE any cell is built — this is exactly the value the design-gate discipline is meant to catch, at zero compute cost.
- If HARD-FAIL on discrimination (condition i): this would be a THIRD independent line of evidence (alongside 29375's per-instance text-derivation closure and 29392's SELRESTRS coverage-block) that verb-class-level lexical-semantic lookup, however operationalized, keeps hitting the same wall on this corpus/verb-set — a meaningfully strengthened, not weakened, closure signal.
- If HARD-FAIL on coverage (condition ii): this is a data/resource problem, not a mechanism problem — distinguishes cleanly from (i) and points at a different fix (broader lexical resource, not abandoning the graded-score idea).
- The missing light-verb bucket in the existing construction gold is a concrete, small, well-scoped follow-on data task (pull a slice from PropBank `.LV` or PARSEME) — flagged here rather than assumed away.

## P (deflated per lit-scan-calibration; novel-synthesis capped at 0.50)

- **P(the graded score meaningfully separates target-not-affected from change-of-state verbs on the construction-gold verb set, i.e. HARD-PASS condition (a)/(b)): 0.40.** Built up from: Dowty/Beavers give a real, well-founded operational rubric (high prior on the underlying linguistic distinction being real) — discounted for (i) the alternation-diagnostic's TEXT-INTERNAL form being unverified at this corpus's scale, (ii) VerbNet coverage/coarseness risk directly inherited from 29392, (iii) no paper found quantifying this exact separation on a comparable item set.
- **P(coverage is NOT the dominant failure mode, i.e. >= 50% of the gold verb set resolves via VerbNet class + PropBank `.LV`): 0.45.** Genuinely uncertain; 29392 already showed VerbNet coverage is a real risk on "core verbs," and this drill's verb set (UD-EWT web text) skews toward exactly the informal/colloquial verbs (search-for, look-at, take-care-of) least likely to have clean VerbNet/PropBank entries.
- **P(the abstain band is usefully sized, i.e. HARD-FAIL condition (iii) does not fire): 0.55.** Moderate confidence — gradient cases (touch-class, spray-load) are a known-real but relatively small slice of ordinary verb tokens per the lit, so a well-tuned threshold should avoid over-abstention, but the actual threshold has never been measured on this corpus.
- **P(this drill's functional-role distinction from 29373 holds up, i.e. HARD-FAIL condition (iv) does not fire): 0.45.** The single most honestly uncertain number in this note — the distinction (confidence-weight-feeding-abstain vs. standalone-per-instance-disambiguator) is real IN PRINCIPLE but has not been independently tested; flagged as the second-biggest risk after the graded-vs-categorical headline.
- **P(the headline reframing itself — graded not categorical — is the linguistically correct characterization, not an artifact of this cycle's search): 0.70.** Reasonably high confidence: three independent, well-established frameworks (Dowty, Beavers, Tsunoda) converge on graded/scalar/continuum framings from three different methodological angles (entailment-counting, alternation-diagnostics, cross-linguistic case-marking typology), and the light-verb literature (Grimshaw & Mester's argument-transfer + PropBank/PARSEME's own imperfect-boundary practice) independently shows the same cline pattern at the opposite end of the scale.

## Substrate-product implications addendum — biggest risk (explicit, per task instruction)

**The single biggest risk this drill flags is exactly the headline: if a hard 3-way categorical gate had been built as the task brief originally proposed, it would have been fighting the field's own evidence** — Dowty, Beavers, and Tsunoda all converge on graded/scalar representations, and light-verb-hood is independently a cline (Grimshaw & Mester; PropBank/PARSEME's own imperfect boundary-drawing). The honest output of any verb-affectedness component should be a **graded score feeding abstain**, not a forced categorical label — consistent with, and reusing, the substrate's already-built metacognitive confidence/abstain layer rather than adding a new discrete-decision architecture.
