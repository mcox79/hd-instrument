# research: content-dependent context gating (brain-grounding for the CONTEXT_GATE follow-on) — 2026-07-08

## HEADLINE

Tonight's CONTEXT_GATE headline (`exp_substrate_gen_lm_contextgate_depth_v5_n8192_gpu`, MIDDLE_BAND/on-HARD_PASS-line) learned a **positional/recency** gate: it is a single per-slot-index weight vector `g_j`, identical for every sequence, that happens to concentrate on the most-recent slot because in a 1st-order Markov corpus the most-recent slot IS always the relevant one. That is index-selection, not content-selection — it is mathematically guaranteed to fail the moment the relevant slot's *position* varies from instance to instance. Three independent brain-literature lit-scans (biased-competition/top-down attention, PFC-basal-ganglia PBWM gating, hippocampal CA3 pattern-completion) converge on the same answer for what a genuinely content-dependent gate looks like: **similarity-weighted (query-key) admission**, i.e. per-instance softmax over cosine-match between a top-down cue/template and each candidate slot's content — which is also, structurally, the same computation as modern continuous-Hopfield attention (Ramsauer et al. 2020, "Hopfield Networks Is All You Need," ICLR 2021: the Hopfield energy-descent update *is* `softmax(QK^T/sqrt(d))V`). This gives a single, substrate-implementable primitive (`enc_gate` generalized from a fixed `g[j]` vector to a per-instance `g[j](query)` computed via cosine-match to a content cue) that is directly testable against the existing recency-gate on a corpus purpose-built so recency provably cannot win.

## Ranked mechanisms (neuroscience signature -> substrate primitive)

**1. Biased competition / top-down attentional template (Desimone & Duncan 1995; normalization-model formalization: Reynolds & Heeger 2009) — HIGHEST likelihood of a working content-dependent gate.**
Mechanism: a top-down "template" (goal/target representation held in PFC) biases the competition among simultaneously-active stimulus representations in V4/IT, so the population whose tuning best MATCHES the template wins representational dominance — independent of the stimulus's spatial position or arrival order. Reynolds & Heeger's normalization model formalizes this as multiplicative gain modulation proportional to template-match, i.e. structurally a similarity-weighted (soft, not winner-take-all) admission — the same computation as query-key attention (this is the link the modern-Hopfield-networks-as-attention literature makes explicit: Ramsauer et al. 2020 prove the classical/modern Hopfield retrieval update is exactly `softmax(beta * Q K^T) V`, i.e. biased-competition-style similarity-weighted pooling is mathematically identical to transformer attention).
Substrate analog: exactly the CONTENT_GATE cell below — `g_j = softmax(cos(key_j, top_down_cue)/tau)`, applied as a per-slot multiplicative admission weight before binding, generalized from v5's fixed `g[j]` to a per-instance, content-computed weight.
Honest caveat (lit-scan): the classical experiments are almost all SPATIAL/SIMULTANEOUS (multiple objects present at once in a visual scene), not SEQUENTIAL/TEMPORAL (selecting among K *past* context slots held in a scrolling window). The literature does not directly test the temporal-slot case. This is a **structural extrapolation, not a behavioral one** — the risk is low because the underlying computation (bias competition among a fixed set of candidate representations by similarity to a template) does not depend on whether the candidates are laid out in space or in time; nothing about the biased-competition math requires simultaneity. But it has not been directly demonstrated in a temporal-memory-slot setting in the primate literature, so this is flagged, not assumed.

**2. PFC/basal-ganglia PBWM gating (Frank & O'Reilly 2006 "PBWM"; O'Reilly & Frank 2006 dopamine-trained Go/NoGo gating) — best BEHAVIORAL precedent that content/task-relevance beats recency specifically in temporal/sequential tasks.**
Mechanism: basal-ganglia Go/NoGo pathways, trained by dopaminergic reward-prediction-error (a three-factor Hebbian/RL rule), learn WHICH incoming item to gate into a PFC working-memory "stripe," based on its LEARNED task-relevance — not its recency. The signature task in this literature (12-AX, and delayed-non-match designs with intervening distractors) is explicitly temporal/sequential: the gate must ignore a recent, task-irrelevant distractor and continue holding an earlier, task-relevant cue. This is the closest literature to "select the relevant slot from several intervening, more-recent, irrelevant slots" — directly analogous to what our corpus below is designed to force.
Substrate analog: the same softmax-admission gate, but LEARNED via a reward/predictive-error signal (Hebbian relevance measured against next-token prediction error, as v5's `learn_gate` already does) rather than a fixed structural template.
Honest caveat: PBWM's canonical formalization is typically a DISCRETE Go/NoGo decision per incoming item (categorical: admit this one new item, or don't), evaluated one slot at a time — not a CONTINUOUS softmax weighting simultaneously over K already-present candidate slots. Treating it as a continuous relaxation (softmax over K, rather than K independent binary Go/NoGo decisions) is a reasonable and common generalization in later "gated working memory" RL literature, but is a step beyond the original PBWM formalization. Medium extrapolation risk (lower than pure novel synthesis, since gated-WM-RL literature already makes this generalization).

**3. Hippocampal CA3 pattern completion / content-addressed recall (Marr 1971; McNaughton & Morris 1987; Treves & Rolls 1994; O'Reilly & McClelland 1994 pattern-separation/completion; Teyler & DiScenna hippocampal indexing theory) — same underlying math, weaker fit to the K-slot gating framing.**
Mechanism: CA3's recurrent auto-associative network retrieves the stored attractor whose basin best matches a partial/noisy cue — retrieval strength is a function of pattern DISTINCTIVENESS/basin depth and cue-overlap, not recency of storage. This is mathematically the substrate's own `iterative_attractor`/CA3-cleanup module (already built and already tested as an "antidote" in v4/v5 — and already shown, at v4/v5, to make the noise-compounding problem WORSE, not better, when applied as a cleanup-after-the-fact operation).
Honest caveat: classical hippocampal-indexing models retrieve ONE best-matching pattern from a LARGE associative store via iterative settling dynamics (multiple attractor-convergence steps) — a different capability (retrieve-the-best-match-from-many-stored-patterns) than the target capability here (weight/admit among a SMALL number, K, of simultaneously-held candidate slots, in one shot, before binding). The one-shot-similarity-lookup reduction of hippocampal indexing theory (treat each of K slots as a mini-attractor, gate by cue-similarity, no iterative settling) is a faithful simplification of the *content-addressing* idea but drops the recurrent-dynamics part entirely. Ranked third because it is the most already-tried-and-already-failed-as-an-antidote path in this program (CLEANUP arm in v4/v5), even though its underlying math is sound.

**Convergence point (why #1 is top-ranked over #2/#3):** all three literatures reduce, when formalized, to the SAME computation — similarity/dot-product-weighted (soft) selection among a candidate set — which is also mathematically identical to transformer/query-key attention (Ramsauer et al. 2020). The substrate does not need three different mechanisms; it needs ONE (`softmax(cosine-match/tau)`-gated admission) applied per-instance with a CONTENT cue (biased-competition framing) rather than a fixed per-index weight (recency framing) or a fixed prediction-error-trained-but-still-per-index weight (v5's PBWM-flavored but still positional implementation).

## Cheap decisive test / GPU-testable cell proposal

**Extends:** `exp_substrate_gen_lm_contextgate_depth_v5_n8192_gpu.py` (commit 4692cd9cc). Same scaffolding (codebook, roll-bind context slots, Hebbian readout, seed-checkpointing, GATE_ARMS pattern) — new corpus + one new encoder function (`enc_content_gate`), reusing `enc_raw`/`enc_gate`/`learn_gate`/`scramble_gate` machinery almost unchanged. No existing cell (checked: `exp_pfc_gate_*`, `exp_wave14_moe_attention_routing_v1`, `exp_query_margin_gate_smoke_v1`, `exp_substrate_working_memory_multi_bank_routing_v1`, `exp_cortex_attention_binding_router_*`, `exp_surprise_gated_pool_charlm`) tests a per-INSTANCE content-match gate over temporal context slots — the closest, `exp_wave14_moe_attention_routing_v1`, does query-key soft attention but over STATIC expert-prototype keys for MoE routing, not over a scrolling K-slot temporal window with an instance-varying relevant slot. This is a genuine gap, not a duplicate.

**Corpus (the regime where recency-gating provably fails):** a variable-lag flagged-dependency stream over the same VOCAB=70 codebook. Reserve one token id as `FLAG`. At each position, emit `FLAG` with small probability `p_flag`, immediately followed by a `VALUE` token drawn from a small sub-vocabulary. All other positions are i.i.d. `FILLER` tokens carrying zero predictive signal (pure noise, by construction — same "provable noise" property v5 relied on for RAW). Periodically (every `K` steps) emit a `QUERY` marker; the target for the next-token prediction at a QUERY position is the VALUE that followed the MOST RECENT `FLAG` before it. Critically: the number of FILLER tokens between that FLAG+VALUE pair and the QUERY is drawn **uniformly at random from `{0, ..., K-2}`** per instance — so the informative slot's absolute index within the K-slot context window varies instance-to-instance. This makes the relevant slot's *position* carry **zero information** (by construction, uniform over indices) while its *content* (preceded-by-FLAG) is a deterministic, always-present signal.

**Arms:**
- `RAW` — bind all K slots uniformly (v5's `enc_raw`, unchanged).
- `RECENCY_GATE` — v5's existing mechanism unchanged: ONE fixed per-index weight vector `g_j`, learned once from the corpus (predictiveness of slot-index j for the next token, averaged over instances), applied identically to every instance. Analytic ceiling: because the true informative index is uniform over `{0,...,K-2}` by construction, ANY single fixed-index policy can be correct on at most `1/(K-1)` of instances in expectation — this is a closed-form, provable failure bound, not an empirical hope.
- `CONTENT_GATE` (new) — per-instance gate: `r_j(instance) = cos(cb[tok_at_slot_{j-1}], cb[FLAG])` (checks, per slot, whether the immediately-preceding token in that slot's window was FLAG); `g_j(instance) = softmax(r_j/tau)`. This is the biased-competition/query-key primitive: a content cue (the FLAG code) biases per-instance admission, structurally identical to `enc_gate` but with `gate` recomputed per training/eval batch from instance content instead of fixed once from the corpus.
- `CONTENT_GATE_SCRAMBLED` — firing control: same weight spectrum, but the cue is matched against a random non-FLAG code (or `r_j` computed against a permuted token index within the same instance), destroying the content-correctness of the match while preserving weight magnitude/entropy. Must NOT recover recall — isolates "genuine content-match" from "any peaked admission weight helps by variance-reduction alone."

**Metric:** top-1 recall of the correct VALUE token at QUERY positions (chance = `1/|VALUE sub-vocab|`), plus bpc as in v5 for continuity.

**Pre-registered bands:**
- **HARD-PASS:** `CONTENT_GATE` recall >= 0.70 at K=6 AND `RECENCY_GATE` recall <= 0.30 (near its analytic `1/(K-1)~=0.20` ceiling, confirming recency-gating provably fails on this corpus, not just empirically underperforms) AND `CONTENT_GATE` beats `CONTENT_GATE_SCRAMBLED` by >= 0.30 absolute recall (selection is genuine content-match, not a renormalization/variance artifact — same firing-control logic v5 already validated for its scramble arm).
- **MIDDLE-BAND:** `CONTENT_GATE` beats `RECENCY_GATE` by >= 0.15 recall but misses the 0.70/0.30 pair, OR the scramble separation is positive but < 0.30.
- **HARD-FAIL:** `CONTENT_GATE` recall - `RECENCY_GATE` recall < 0.15 (content gate gives no real lift over a fixed-index gate on a corpus explicitly built so index carries zero information — would mean the query-key primitive fails to transfer from the (simultaneous, spatial) biased-competition literature to the (sequential, temporal) slot-selection setting), OR `CONTENT_GATE_SCRAMBLED` matches `CONTENT_GATE` (no genuine content-selection, just admission sharpening).

## Substrate-product implications

If HARD-PASS: gives the glass-box substrate an inspectable, content-addressed context-admission primitive — a legible alternative to opaque transformer attention, directly relevant to the M3 cortex-layer gap (attention-routing / action-selection) and to the encoder-adjacent goal of native, non-recency-biased context handling for generation. If HARD-FAIL: the negative is itself informative — it would mean the substrate's existing similarity/cosine machinery cannot cleanly separate "content match" from "any sharpened admission," which would redirect attention to a harder representational fix (e.g. explicit slot-tagging/indexing rather than post-hoc content-matching) rather than a pure gating fix. Either outcome is a real answer, not a stall.
