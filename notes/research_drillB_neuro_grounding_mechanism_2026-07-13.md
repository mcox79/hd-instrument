## HEADLINE

The literature converges on one precise, well-established, and directly portable answer to "what is the minimal mechanism that turns a floating symbol into a grounded one": **the symbol must be bound to a stored trace from a real exogenous episode, then on future invocation the system generates a PREDICTION from that trace, compares the prediction against a NEW real (independently measured) episode, and the resulting error/mismatch is itself the signal that (a) is reportable as the concept's content and (b) drives representational update.** This is not a metaphor for grounding — it is the literal, decades-replicated mechanism the brain uses for the concrete example asked about: perceived "heaviness" of a lifted object is not a readout of a raw force sensor at all, it is derived from the MISMATCH between the cerebellum's anticipatory (efference-copy) grip-force prediction and the actual load-force feedback (Flanagan/Wolpert-line forward-model literature; Sensorimotor Memory Biases Weight Perception During Object Lifting, PMC4689183) — "heavy" means heaviness because the symbol is coupled to a real, re-testable, error-correctable sensorimotor prediction loop, not because it is merely linked to the word "weight" in a graph. Active inference / predictive coding (Friston) is the domain-general formalization of the identical mechanism (prediction, exogenous comparison, precision-weighted error, representational update) and — unlike sensorimotor-contingency theory, which strictly requires a live perturbable body — **is substantially portable to a bodyless glass-box VSA system**: the "sensory" side of the comparison can be a bulk downloadable measured-numeric database, and the "action" side can be an internal composition/query operation, as long as the predicted value is actually checked against an independently measured value and the residual is used, not merely logged. This is, concretely, what the already-designed periodic-table Phase-1 cell (predict a held-out element's property from consolidated neighbors, check against the true measured value) already structurally IS — this drill's contribution is to show that cell is a literal implementation of the brain's forward-model/active-inference grounding mechanism, and to specify two concrete, cheap, brain-motivated refinements to it (explicit residual-binding, precision-weighted channel fusion) that the prior design did not include. For abstract concepts, the drill adds independent empirical corroboration — not just philosophical argument — that pure relational/co-occurrence structure among abstractions is a genuine circularity risk in real language data (Naumann et al. 2018: abstract words co-occur predominantly with OTHER abstract words), strengthening the case for the already-adopted composition-reachability audit (Phase 0) as a hard requirement, not a formality.

## Cheap decisive test (pre-registered — an ADDITION to the already-designed periodic-table Phase-1 cell, not a new cell)

The existing Phase-1 cell (`research_grounding_foundation_build_plan_2026-07-13.md`) already runs the core forward-model test: consolidate on relational structure + FPE-bound numeric attributes, predict a held-out element's property from its neighbors, check against the true measured value, A-vs-A+B ablation. This drill adds a specific sub-test derived from the neuro mechanism:

- **Policy (a) baseline** (current design): bind the PREDICTED attribute value only.
- **Policy (b) residual-augmented** (new, brain-motivated): additionally bind the (predicted - true) RESIDUAL as a second explicit vector component at training/consolidation time wherever ground truth is available, per the "the mismatch itself becomes the percept" finding (weight perception is derived from correction magnitude, not raw sensing).
- **Precision-weighted fusion** (new, brain-motivated): when combining the 5 numeric channels (mass, electronegativity, radius, ionization energy, melting point), weight each channel's contribution by its own cross-validated reliability (inverse-variance), per active-inference precision-weighting / Ernst-Banks MLE cue combination (already independently surfaced via the multisensory-integration literature in the ATL-hub note — this drill provides the active-inference-specific formalization of the identical principle).
- **Recalibration pass** (new, brain-motivated): analogous to boundary-vector-cell drift-reset in path integration, periodically re-check any propagated/diffused (inferred, not directly measured) attribute values against newly available true measurements during consolidation, rather than propagate-once-and-freeze.

**HARD-PASS** (prediction-error-as-signal mechanism is real and load-bearing for this substrate, not just a neuro curiosity): residual-augmented policy (b) beats value-only policy (a) on held-out numeric-property prediction, with the improvement concentrated in the harder (boundary/isolated, low-degree) held-out elements — matching the brain's inverse-effectiveness pattern (biggest relative gain where the single-channel signal is weakest, already established as real on this exact literature in the ATL-hub note) AND precision-weighted fusion beats naive equal-weight concatenation AND a recalibration pass measurably reduces error accumulation over successive consolidation rounds relative to a no-recalibration control.

**HARD-FAIL** (any of, each independently diagnostic, do not conflate):
1. Residual-augmented policy (b) ties or underperforms policy (a) — prediction-error carries no additional information beyond the point estimate in this regime (this substrate's diffusion/consolidation may already implicitly encode what the residual would add; or the numeric channels are too well-behaved/low-noise for the mismatch signal to matter — a real, informative negative, not a mechanism failure).
2. Precision-weighting ties naive equal-weighting — either channel reliabilities are genuinely near-equal here (uninformative but not falsifying), or the weighting estimate itself is unreliable/overfit on a small validation set (must disambiguate via the same overfitting gate already specified in the ATL-hub note's Pitfall #2 before concluding either way).
3. The recalibration pass does not reduce drift or introduces oscillation/instability — diagnostic that this substrate's diffusion-with-restart dynamics do NOT behave like linear vector path-integration (a real, useful negative bounding how far the grid/place-cell analogy can be pushed onto a PageRank-style propagation mechanism, rather than a literal continuous-integration one).

## Falsifiable predictions — full cover across the three lit-scan threads

1. **Forward-model/prediction-error mechanism (heaviness, most load-bearing finding).** PREDICTS the periodic-table oracle test's existing A-vs-A+B ablation IS already exercising this mechanism (point 1-3 above sharpen it). HARD-FAIL: no measurable difference between value-only and residual-augmented policies at any held-out difficulty tier — would mean this substrate's specific consolidation math already saturates whatever the residual would add, a genuinely useful negative that caps how far the "explicit residual" refinement is worth pursuing further.
2. **Active-inference precision-weighting.** PREDICTS reliability-weighted fusion beats naive fusion, generalizing the already-adopted "late fusion, reliability-weighted" recommendation from the ATL-hub note with an independent theoretical route (Bayesian precision, not just MLE cue-combination psychophysics — two separate literatures converging on the identical weighting rule is corroborating, not novel, evidence). HARD-FAIL threshold: reliability-weighted and equal-weight fusion produce statistically indistinguishable held-out prediction error across all 5 seeds.
3. **Boundary-vector-cell-style recalibration for diffusion drift.** PREDICTS periodic re-anchoring against ground truth reduces cumulative propagation error in a multi-round consolidation loop, the same way boundary cells reset path-integration drift on landmark re-encounter. HARD-FAIL: recalibration makes no measurable difference to propagation error, OR destabilizes convergence (oscillation) — diagnostic that diffusion-with-restart's mathematics differ enough from linear path-integration that the analogy does not transfer as-is (report which, do not conflate).
4. **Abstract-concept reachability/circularity (independent corroboration, not new claim).** Naumann et al.'s empirical finding (abstract words in real corpora co-occur predominantly with other abstract words) PREDICTS that any relational/co-occurrence graph built without an explicit reachability check will show a nontrivial fraction of abstract nodes whose entire neighborhood is other unggrounded abstractions — this is precisely what the already-designed, not-yet-run Phase-0 percolation/reachability audit (`research_grounding_percolation_reachability_cskg_audit_2026-07-11.md`) is built to detect. HARD-FAIL for the underlying worry would be: the reachability audit finds near-zero closed-relational-island nodes even without any exogenous-anchor bake-in yet — would suggest the graph's existing structure is already accidentally well-anchored (surprising, worth flagging loudly if it happens, but do not expect it given the "SUBSTRATE HAS ZERO GROUNDED DATA" foundational finding).
5. **Sensorimotor-contingency-as-active-query (speculative, most novel).** PREDICTS that framing an internal composition/query operation as the "action" side of an active-inference loop (in place of a literal body) — i.e., treating "sweep a parameter and observe how a downstream measured signal changes" as the substrate's analog of self-generated sensorimotor action — could in principle extend the grounding mechanism to concepts with no direct numeric attribute but a queryable derived relationship (e.g., a concept only knowable via its effect on other measured quantities). No cheap test is proposed for this yet; it is flagged as a promising unconsidered avenue (below), not a falsifiable prediction ready to pre-register.

## Replicate this (proven mechanism) vs. promising unconsidered avenue

**Replicate — proven, well-established, high-confidence, directly maps to an already-designed or already-running cell:**
- Forward-model / prediction-error as the grounding signal (Flanagan/Wolpert/cerebellar grip-force literature, decades-replicated, lesion+TMS-confirmed) — the periodic-table Phase-1 cell's predict-vs-truth oracle check already structurally IS this mechanism; residual-binding and precision-weighting are cheap, low-risk amendments, not a new architecture.
- Precision-weighted multi-channel fusion (active-inference formalization, independently convergent with the already-adopted MLE-cue-combination late-fusion design) — reinforces, does not change, the ATL-hub note's existing recommendation.
- Composition-reachability as a hard requirement for abstract-concept grounding (Reinboth-Farkaš graded account, converging with Harnad/Taddeo-Floridi/Bender-Koller already adopted, now independently corroborated by Naumann's corpus measurement) — reinforces, does not change, the already-designed Phase-0 reachability audit's priority.

**Promising unconsidered avenue — genuinely new to this program, higher uncertainty, not yet in any build plan:**
- Explicit residual/prediction-error vector as a first-class BOUND representational component (not merely a pass/fail gate) — the "mismatch itself becomes the percept" idea. Untested on this substrate; cheap to add to the already-queued Phase-1 smoke (Cheap decisive test above).
- Boundary-vector-cell-style periodic drift recalibration for the diffusion-with-restart consolidation loop — genuinely novel mechanism transfer, uncertain whether the PageRank-style propagation this substrate uses behaves enough like linear path-integration for the analogy to pay off; flagged, not yet designed as a standalone cell.
- Sensorimotor-contingency-as-internal-active-query (treating an internal parameter sweep / counterfactual composition as the substrate's "action" in an active-inference loop, extending grounding to concepts only knowable via derived/relational effects on measured quantities rather than a direct attribute) — most speculative finding this drill surfaces; no cheap test proposed yet, needs its own design pass before it is dispatch-ready.
- Direct on-substrate replication of the Naumann abstract-co-occurrence measurement on the existing symbol graph — cheap, not yet run, complementary to but distinct from the already-designed Phase-0 percolation audit.
