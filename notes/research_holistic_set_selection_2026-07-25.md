## 1. HEADLINE

The brain-side literature converges on THREE mechanisms that together (not any one alone) match "assemble a set where no single member is diagnostic, decision is a population/ensemble property": (a) **population-vector-style readout** (Georgopoulos) — this is what the existing bundle combiner already IS, biology-validated, unchanged; (b) **Kintsch Construction-Integration (CI)** — over-generate candidate propositions, then settle a network onto a coherent surviving subset, an iterative CONSTRUCTIVE process, confirmed from primary source with a concrete algorithm (spreading activation over a signed connection matrix, converge to stability, epsilon=.001); (c) **multi-alternative competitive accumulator dynamics** (Usher-McClelland LCA, Wang/Wong attractor, Albantakis-Deco N-alternative extension) — the mechanism for tracking SEVERAL competing hypotheses simultaneously rather than greedily committing to one, which is the literature's answer to the confirmation-trap question. The concrete build: replace the failed per-fact scoring function with a GREEDY SET-CONSTRUCTION loop that adds facts to the (existing, unchanged) bundle one at a time, scored not by any intrinsic per-fact property but by how much each candidate addition increases the CROSS-ANSWER-CHOICE discrimination margin of the bundle — a metric that only exists at the set level, directly reusing the mechanism already proven at 0.687. One clean literature gap found: neither CI nor ECHO describes running separate parallel networks per hypothesis and comparing final scores — competition in the primary literature happens via inhibitory links WITHIN one network. The design below treats the existing per-answer-choice cosine scores as those inhibitory-linked hypothesis nodes, so this maps onto established mechanics rather than requiring an invented one.

## 2. Biology-grounded synthesis (citations vs. speculative — flagged throughout)

### 2a. Global Workspace / Global Neuronal Workspace — coalition formation (BIOLOGY-GROUNDED, STRONG fit)
Baars 1988 (*A Cognitive Theory of Consciousness*); Dehaene, Kerszberg & Changeux, *PNAS* 1998; Dehaene & Changeux, *Neuron* 2011; Dehaene et al. 2020 review. Distributed neural assemblies compete; a subset crossing a nonlinear threshold undergoes "ignition" (sudden, self-sustained, recurrently-amplified firing, largely fronto-parietal), then gets globally broadcast. Coalition membership is explicitly described as an emergent, ensemble-level property, not decided by any single neuron. STRONG fit to "no individually decisive member; decision is a population property," but this is a framework for the *outcome* (a winning coalition gets broadcast), not a specific *algorithm* for constructing the coalition step by step — that detail is thinner in this literature than in CI/ECHO.

### 2b. Population vector coding (BIOLOGY-GROUNDED, STRONG fit — this is what the existing bundle combiner already instantiates)
Georgopoulos, Schwartz & Kettner, *Science* 1986; Georgopoulos, Kettner & Schwartz, *J. Neurosci.* 1988. Individual M1 neurons are broadly tuned (non-diagnostic alone); movement direction is recovered as the vector sum of each neuron's preferred-direction vector weighted by firing rate — the population vector. Direct structural analog to a superposition/bundle readout. Caveat (flagged in the literature itself): Scott 2000 questions whether the vector sum is the brain's actual computation or a descriptive artifact of the decoding method — noted as an open methodological debate, not a refutation of the coding principle. Implication for us: the bundle-combiner's *shape* (weighted vector sum, no per-item diagnosticity required) has a genuine biological precedent — this justifies NOT touching the readout mechanism, only the set-CONSTRUCTION mechanism feeding it.

### 2c. Multi-alternative competitive/attractor decision models (BIOLOGY-GROUNDED, PARTIAL fit — supplies the confirmation-trap answer)
Usher & McClelland, *Psych. Review* 2001 (leaky competing accumulator); Wang, *Neuron* 2002 and Wong & Wang, *J. Neurosci.* 2006 (reduced attractor decision model); Albantakis & Deco, *PNAS* 2009 (extends to N simultaneously-active discrete alternatives, not just binary). Multiple pools race under mutual inhibition + leak until one crosses threshold. PARTIAL fit: each alternative is a single scalar accumulator, not a composite set/coalition — but the N-alternative mutual-inhibition dynamic is exactly the mechanism that prevents one interpretation from being evaluated in isolation while the others are ignored. This is the direct biological answer to "how do you avoid greedily committing to one wrong interpretation": you keep ALL live candidates racing simultaneously and read off the MARGIN between the current leader and its closest rival, not the leader's absolute score alone.

McClelland & Rumelhart's Interactive Activation model (1981/82; McClelland 2014 review) is flagged as the CLOSEST fit found for "multiple composite hypotheses, at multiple representational levels, co-active and mutually constraining via excitation/inhibition, settling together" — closer in spirit to a coalition-of-coalitions than the scalar accumulator models, though it targets word/letter perception, not evidence-based QA, so this is an ANALOGY, flagged as such, not a direct precedent.

No literature was found describing discrete, labeled COALITIONS (as opposed to scalar alternatives or continuous hypothesis distributions) explicitly tracked and compared as competing SETS. This is reported as a genuine gap between existing frameworks and the exact substrate need — SPECULATIVE/INFERRED design territory from here, built on top of the above real precedents, not itself citable.

### 2d. Kintsch Construction-Integration — the concrete settling algorithm (BIOLOGY/COGNITIVE-SCIENCE-GROUNDED, primary-source detail, STRONGEST algorithmic match)
Kintsch 1988, *Psych. Review*; Kintsch 1998 book. CONSTRUCTION: over-generate — build a loosely-constrained, context-insensitive candidate set including irrelevant/contradictory elaborations and even wrong word senses; a buffer carries a subset of prior-cycle propositions forward. INTEGRATION: activation spreads iteratively over a connection-strength matrix W (positive=coherent pairs, negative=contradictory, computed from argument-overlap/coreference); nodes well-connected to the emerging network gain activation, poorly-connected/conflicting nodes lose it; iterate to a stable state (Kintsch's own worked example converges to keeping exactly the top-4 propositions between cycles — corroborated already in the 07-24 note as an independent Cowan's-4 anchor). This is cyclic/incremental across a discourse, not a single upfront settle. Directly gives an ALGORITHM SHAPE: over-generate, then iteratively suppress via a settling process, converge, read off survivors as the accepted subset.

### 2e. Thagard's ECHO — explicit connectionist update rule (BIOLOGY/COGNITIVE-SCIENCE-GROUNDED, primary-source detail)
Thagard 1989, *Behavioral and Brain Sciences*; Thagard & Verbeurgt 1998 ("Coherence as Constraint Satisfaction," compares exhaustive/incremental/connectionist/greedy/semidefinite-programming algorithms for coherence maximization — confirms "greedy" is itself a recognized member of this literature's own solution family, not an ad hoc substitute). Propositions are units; explanatory/coherence relations are excitatory links, contradictions are inhibitory links; a clamped "data-priority" unit privileges evidence nodes. Update rule is the standard Grossberg/interactive-activation-and-competition equation (net input = weighted sum of neighbor activations; asymmetric update depending on sign of net input; asymptote declared when max activation change < 0.001). Units with positive final activation are "accepted." Mutually exclusive hypotheses are linked by inhibitory edges INSIDE THE SAME network — competition is resolved by settling one network with rival-hypothesis nodes present, not by running separate networks per hypothesis and comparing.

### 2f. Hippocampal pattern completion / CA3 attractor dynamics (BIOLOGY-GROUNDED, supports the "full set from partial cue" framing)
Marr 1971; O'Reilly & McClelland 1994 (*Hippocampus* — CA3 recurrent collaterals do pattern completion, DG does pattern separation); Rolls & Treves 1992/1998/2010/2013 (CA3 as Hopfield-style autoassociative attractor network; retrieval as literal "competition between CA3 engrams," ambiguous/morphed cues resolved categorically to one attractor basin, not left blended — evidencing basin-geometry-driven winner-take-all). Kumaran & McClelland's REMERGE model is flagged as the clearest precedent for the READOUT-GUIDED ITERATIVE LOOP question (part 2 of the task): inference proceeds via cycles of retrieval and re-querying — a partial/emerging interpretation triggers further retrieval that refines it, i.e. retrieval and interpretation are INTERLEAVED, not one-shot. Flagged explicitly: REMERGE is a computational-modeling proposal, not settled empirical consensus. Wilson & McNaughton 1994 and Pfeiffer & Foster 2013 confirm hippocampal recall/replay is decoded at the population/ensemble level (joint multi-cell activity state), not single-neuron — reinforcing the population-coding framing of 2b at a different structure.

### 2g. Direct answer to task question 2 (readout-guided iterative construction, confirmation-trap avoidance)
Set assembly is BEST MODELED as iterative/constructive (Kintsch CI's cyclic settling, REMERGE's retrieval-reinterpretation loop) — biology-grounded on both counts, though from two different literatures that were not found cross-cited to each other (this bridging is OUR synthesis, flagged as inferred). Confirmation-trap avoidance is answered by the competitive multi-alternative literature (2c): the brain's resolution is NOT "grow one interpretation and check it's still coherent" but "keep multiple candidate interpretations racing simultaneously under mutual inhibition, and read the decision off the MARGIN between the leader and its nearest rival" — this is a real, repeatedly-cited mechanism class (Usher-McClelland, Wang, Albantakis-Deco), directly usable as a design principle even though it was developed for scalar choices rather than composite sets.

## 3. Concrete build design for the substrate

### 3.1 What stays UNCHANGED (do not redesign)
- The wide retrieval pool (recall@100=0.69, gold_in_pool=0.91) — unchanged.
- The bundle combiner / set-level readout: superpose (HD-bundle) whatever fact set is currently assembled, score each answer choice by cosine similarity to the bundle. This is the population-vector-style readout (2b), already proven at 0.687 given the true gold set. UNCHANGED — this is the one piece of the pipeline that is NOT broken.

### 3.2 The ONE variable that changes: per-fact top-K -> greedy holistic (set-level) construction
Old (FAILED, atom 29548 lineage): score each retrieved fact in isolation with some per-fact function f(fact, question) (relevance / coherence / structure / content / answer-conditioned single-fact scoring), take top-K by that score, feed to the combiner. Precision ~0.15 vs oracle 0.97; the per-fact differential signal is near the dimensionality noise floor (~1/sqrt(2048)).

New (this design): construct the fact set GREEDILY and HOLISTICALLY, one fact at a time, where each candidate's score is NOT an intrinsic property of the fact but its MARGINAL EFFECT on the bundle's cross-answer-choice discrimination — i.e. the same quantity already proven informative at the set level, evaluated incrementally.

**Algorithm (Kintsch-CI-style over-generate-then-settle, read out via the existing combiner, competition resolved Usher-McClelland/Albantakis-Deco-style across ALL answer choices simultaneously):**

1. Retrieve the wide pool P (unchanged, top-100 by existing retrieval).
2. Initialize bundle B_0 = empty (or seeded with the single highest-embedding-similarity-to-question fact, to bootstrap — an ablation variable, not load-bearing).
3. Maintain, at every step t, the FULL vector of cosine similarities of B_t to ALL answer choices (A, B, C, D) simultaneously — these are the "rival hypothesis nodes," directly analogous to Usher-McClelland/Wang/Albantakis-Deco's N racing accumulators, or ECHO's mutually-inhibitory hypothesis units (2c, 2e). Define margin(B_t) = sim(top choice) − sim(second-best choice).
4. At each step, for every unused candidate fact f in P, compute margin(B_t ∪ {f}) (cheap: bundle addition is a vector sum, cosine recompute is O(4) per candidate against 4 answer choices — no retraining, no new model). Add the f that MAXIMIZES margin(B_t ∪ {f}). This is the greedy coherence-maximization algorithm explicitly named as a recognized member of Thagard & Verbeurgt's (1998) own coherence-maximization algorithm family (2e) — not an invented shortcut.
5. Stop when margin plateaus/decreases for 2 consecutive steps (CI's stability/asymptote criterion, 2d/2e — Kintsch epsilon=.001, ECHO epsilon=.001) OR a max set size K_max=10 is reached (a soft ceiling above the known gold-set size of 4-8, giving headroom without unconstrained growth).
6. Light backtracking pass (confirmation-trap guard, see 3.4): after reaching a stable set, re-evaluate whether REMOVING any single early-added fact increases the final margin given the now-larger context (facts added early under partial information may look less useful in hindsight) — analogous to REMERGE's retrieval-reinterpretation loop (2f/2g) and to CI's cyclic re-settling across multiple passes (2d) rather than pure monotonic greedy growth.

### 3.3 Objective/metric: discrimination margin (competitive/contrastive), justified against both biology and mechanism
Of the four candidate metrics (mutual coherence / discrimination margin / explanatory coverage / MDL-parsimony):

- **Chosen PRIMARY: discrimination margin** (top-choice similarity minus runner-up similarity). Justification: (a) biology — the multi-alternative competitive-accumulator literature (2c) converges on margin-between-racing-alternatives as the decision variable, not an intrinsic property of any evidence item; (b) mechanism-fit — margin is computable DIRECTLY from the existing, unmodified bundle combiner (no new scoring function to invent or re-validate), and it is a genuinely SET-level quantity (a single fact cannot have a "margin," only a set-plus-a-candidate can) — this satisfies the mandate that the new metric must be holistic, not a resurrected per-fact score in disguise. This directly avoids repeating the fixed/learned per-fact coherence approaches that already failed (atoms 29544/29545/29546).
- Mutual coherence (ECHO-style pairwise) is EXPLICITLY NOT chosen as primary: it would require a pairwise fact-fact similarity/relatedness metric, which is exactly the same family of thin-meaning per-item signal already shown to underperform (coherence WORSE than random per 29544 lineage) — reusing it here would silently reintroduce the failed mechanism under a new name.
- MDL/parsimony is folded in as the STOPPING rule (smallest set at/near max margin), not the primary driver — this prevents runaway inclusion of the whole pool and gives a principled reason to stop rather than an arbitrary K.
- Explanatory coverage (touching all question sub-parts) is noted as attractive for the CONSTRUCTION/over-generation phase in principle, but the substrate has no sub-part decomposition machinery yet — flagged as a stretch/future extension, NOT part of this build.

### 3.4 Confirmation-trap guard
Because margin is computed against ALL answer choices simultaneously at every step (not "does this fact support my current best guess"), the greedy step itself is already contrastive, not confirmatory, by construction — a fact that would help the CURRENT leader but help a rival MORE would not be added, because it's scored by margin(B_t ∪ {f}), which can go negative or flip the leader. This is the direct operationalization of the Usher-McClelland/Albantakis-Deco N-way competitive dynamic (2c). The backtracking pass (3.2 step 6) is the additional guard against a LOCAL trap: an early fact that looked good in isolation (before enough context existed to reveal a better-supported rival) gets re-evaluated once the rest of the set is in place, and dropped if its marginal contribution has gone negative — modeled on REMERGE's interleaved retrieval-reinterpretation loop (2f/2g), flagged as inferred/constructive rather than a literal literature-established procedure (the primary literature resolves competition within ONE settling network with rival nodes present, not via an explicit remove-and-recheck pass — this addition is OUR synthesis bridging CI's cyclic re-settling with REMERGE's iterative retrieval, not a directly-cited combined mechanism).

### 3.5 MUST-FAIL control (falsifiability)
Random-subset-of-same-size control: for every question, after the greedy algorithm settles on a final set of size |B*|, draw a RANDOM subset of size |B*| from the same retrieval pool P and feed it through the SAME unchanged combiner. If random-same-size subsets achieve comparable answer-choice discrimination margin/accuracy to the greedy-constructed set, the design is falsified — it would mean "more facts in general" (not the greedy construction) is doing the work, which the existing single-fact noise-floor finding (1/sqrt(2048)) already makes implausible but must be checked at the SET level explicitly, since the wall being probed here is specifically about whether SELECTION (not just volume) matters once you're past the single-fact-scoring dead end.

### 3.6 Discriminator: what counts as this working
Baseline anchors already established: chance = 0.25; current per-fact top-K precision ~0.15 (end-to-end Challenge accuracy likely in the low-to-mid 0.30s given that low precision); oracle given-TRUE-gold-set = 0.687 (bundle combiner ceiling with perfect fact selection); oracle given-perfect-selection = 0.97 (upper reference, a different oracle quantity — perfect PRECISION on the selection task itself, not directly the same axis as end-to-end accuracy, kept here only as the ceiling reference the note it comes from used).

- **HARD-PASS:** greedy holistic selection reaches end-to-end ARC-Challenge accuracy >= 0.45, AND beats the random-same-size-subset control by >= 0.15 accuracy points, AND closes at least ~40% of the gap between current-best (~0.30) and the given-gold-set oracle (0.687). This would be strong evidence that holistic/set-level construction recovers a meaningful fraction of the signal the per-fact approaches structurally could not reach.
- **HARD-FAIL:** greedy accuracy is statistically indistinguishable from the random-same-size-subset control (within +/-0.05), OR greedy accuracy does not exceed the current per-fact top-K baseline. Either result means the greedy-margin construction added nothing beyond volume, and the wall is NOT (or not only) about selection algorithm shape — it would push back toward suspecting the retrieval pool's fact GRANULARITY/coverage rather than the selection mechanism.
- Middle band (greedy beats top-K and beats random by a smaller margin, e.g. 0.05-0.15 points, without reaching 0.45): partial credit — real signal, but insufficient set-construction quality; next iteration would tune the stopping rule / backtracking pass rather than abandon the approach.

### 3.7 Shape / place / metric gap — explicit, per the project's deep-brain-analysis method
- **PLACE: unchanged.** Selection still happens at the same pipeline stage — after wide retrieval, before the answer-scoring readout. This build does not move where selection happens.
- **SHAPE: this is what changes, and it is the primary fix.** Old shape = a per-item scalar scoring function f(fact, question) -> real number, independently applied to each candidate, then thresholded (top-K). New shape = a set-valued combinatorial construction procedure — a function of a CANDIDATE SET plus the answer-choice space, built incrementally with each step's decision conditioned on the set built so far. This directly matches the CI/ECHO architecture (over-generate then settle a network) and the multi-alternative competitive-dynamics architecture (track several live hypotheses, decide via margin) rather than the bag-of-independently-scored-items shape that every prior attempt shared and that the noise-floor analysis (1/sqrt(2048)) shows is structurally incapable of carrying the signal.
- **METRIC: this also changes, and it is the second load-bearing fix.** Old metric = an intrinsic property of a single fact (relevance / coherence / structure / content / answer-conditioned score), evaluated in isolation. New metric = a fact's MARGINAL CONTRIBUTION to a GROUP property (cross-answer-choice discrimination margin), which by construction cannot be computed for a fact in isolation — it is only ever meaningful with respect to a set. This is the same quantity (bundle-to-answer cosine) already empirically shown to carry the signal at 0.687; the fix is exposing it to the SELECTION stage incrementally rather than only using it at the final combine-and-answer stage.
- Both SHAPE and METRIC change; PLACE does not. This is an honest, non-hand-wavy characterization: the prior failures were not "wrong place in the pipeline," they were "wrong kind of function, scored against the wrong kind of target."

## 5. Cheap decisive test (before full build)
Before building the full greedy loop + backtracking pass, run the CHEAPEST possible check: on a held-out sample of ~30-50 ARC-Challenge questions where the gold fact set is known, compute margin(B_t) for t=0..|gold| by adding gold facts in RANDOM order (not the true greedy search) and separately compute margin for the same-size RANDOM non-gold subsets. If margin rises monotonically-ish as gold facts accumulate (even in random insertion order) while random-subset margins stay flat/near-chance, that's strong pre-registered evidence the greedy search (which only needs to prefer higher-margin candidates from the pool, not literally find gold) will work, at low cost, before committing to the full pool-wide greedy implementation. This test uses ONLY the existing (unchanged) combiner and existing gold-fact labels — no new code beyond a small loop.

## 6. Falsifiable predictions

- **HARD-PASS thresholds:** see 3.6 (>=0.45 end-to-end accuracy, >=0.15pt over random-same-size control, >=40% of the gap to 0.687 closed).
- **HARD-FAIL thresholds:** see 3.6 (indistinguishable from random-same-size control within +/-0.05, or no improvement over current top-K baseline).
- Additional falsifiable sub-prediction: the cheap decisive test (section 5) should show gold-facts-in-random-order margin visibly separating from random-subset margin by the 3rd-4th fact added (matching Cowan's-4-scale convergence already independently observed in Kintsch's own worked example, cross-referenced in the 07-24 note). If gold-order margin does NOT separate from random-subset margin even with gold facts known, that would falsify the entire premise that FACT IDENTITY (as opposed to some other latent property of the true gold answer) drives the set-level signal — this would be a much bigger and more surprising negative than a simple selection-algorithm failure, and should immediately halt the build and escalate back to Director.

## 7. Substrate-product implications

If HARD-PASS: this closes the primary open item in the ARC-Challenge selection wall (29548) — moves selection from "structurally incapable" (per-fact, noise-floor-bound) to "same-family-as-the-already-proven-readout" (set-level, margin-driven), which is a genuine capability upgrade for any downstream product surface that needs to justify an answer with a SMALL, auditable evidence set (a directly demonstrable feature: "here are the N facts that jointly and verifiably support this answer, and removing any weakens the margin measurably" — an interpretability/trust story a per-fact black-box score could never honestly offer). If HARD-FAIL: the negative result is still valuable — it would mean the wall is not "wrong selection algorithm shape" but something upstream (retrieval GRANULARITY: maybe the pool's facts aren't at the right resolution to ever support a clean margin, independent of how they're combined), redirecting the next research cycle toward retrieval-granularity rather than a 4th per-fact-scoring variant.
