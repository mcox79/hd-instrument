## WHY THIS DRILL
The Scene-Coherence Verifier (SCV) built the USER's picture-verifier idea: type each candidate parse's filler (WordNet noun-supersense) against the verb's expected argument type (VerbNet), emit a BINARY coherent/incoherent bit, use the CONTRAST across candidates as parse-selector AND self-supervised trainer. It HARD_FAILED (selector -0.015; trainer -0.137 all seeds), and the skunkworks VET proved the trainer is null even with a GOLD-PERFECT bit (delta 0.000). Proximate mechanism: the binary bit wrongly rejected 24% of TRUE gold patients — light/support-verb + abstract objects ("spend TIME", "hold HANDS", "forget GRIEF", "take WAY"). The USER directive: DRILL whether we are doing WHAT THE BRAIN ACTUALLY DOES, to tell whether the failure is a fixable design divergence or the approach is wrong.

## VERDICT (deflated): the picture-verifier is BRAIN-FAITHFUL IN PRINCIPLE but MIS-IMPLEMENTED on three axes.

### 1. GRADED vs BINARY — ESTABLISHED: human thematic-fit is GRADED, not a hard type-gate.
- McRae, Ferretti & Amyote (1997, *Lang & Cog Proc*, "Thematic Roles as Verb-specific Concepts") + Ferretti, McRae & Hatherell (2001, instrument/location norms): thematic roles are verb-specific feature-based concepts measured by CONTINUOUS typicality ratings; verbs prime typical fillers gradiently (stir->spoon > shovel > anomalous).
- N400 (Kutas & Hillyard 1980; Federmeier & Kutas 1999; Kutas & Federmeier 2011 review): amplitude scales CONTINUOUSLY and inversely with cloze probability — the most-replicated ERP-language finding — and is reduced by mere semantic relatedness even among equally-low-cloze words. It indexes graded goodness-of-fit, NOT a discrete violation bit.
- Light-verb / abstract-object / idiom constructions pattern as NON-anomalous: figurative and literal processing share the same time course; CONVENTIONAL metaphors' N400 converges with literal-plausible, only NOVEL metaphors pattern with anomalies -> conventionality/FREQUENCY drives the plausibility signal, not literal type-match. (Caveat flagged speculative: no paper directly reports an N400 contrast for LVC nominal complements e.g. "spend TIME" vs "spend MONEY"; the specific absence is reasonable inference, not a directly-tested finding.)
- Synthesis (capped <=0.50, no paper frames the exact "binary supersense gate vs graded score" dichotomy): a hard binary type-gate is predicted to misclassify EXACTLY the high-frequency light-verb/abstract-object fillers the SCV rejected. **Our binary bit is a genuine design flaw.**

### 2. INTEGRATED-INCREMENTAL vs POST-HOC-FILTER — dominant modern consensus favors INTEGRATED; but the coarse re-read MONITOR half is brain-faithful.
- Constraint-based lexicalist models (MacDonald, Pearlmutter & Seidenberg 1994; Trueswell, Tanenhaus & Garnsey 1994): multiple probabilistic constraints incl. thematic/plausibility fit are activated IN PARALLEL with syntactic alternatives and compete continuously — plausibility is one of the constraints DOING the parsing, not a filter over its output. Thematic fit eliminates garden-path cost immediately, word-by-word.
- Visual-world eye-tracking (Tanenhaus et al. 1995; Altmann & Kamide 1999 "the boy will eat the cake" -> anticipatory looks to *cake* BEFORE the noun; Kamide et al. 2003 multi-constraint): plausibility/selectional restriction constrains interpretation PREDICTIVELY, ahead of the argument — the opposite of a post-hoc filter over completed candidates. (The inference that this rules out post-hoc architectures specifically is synthesis-level, ~0.50; a minority Frazier-style modular garden-path tradition is structurally closer to two-stage and is not extinct.)
- Situation-model / monitoring granularity (Zwaan event-indexing; Kintsch CI; Zwaan-Radvansky 1998; O'Brien/van den Broek standards-of-coherence; Ferreira good-enough; Moses illusion Erickson & Mattson 1981; Warren & McConnell plausibility timecourse): comprehension-monitoring is NOT uniform-depth — it is event/situation-level, frequently SHALLOW and permissive of real implausibility, escalating to effortful "does this make sense" reanalysis (visible in regressions/re-reading) only when local heuristics fail or task goals demand. So the USER's "imagine it; if ridiculous, re-read" IS a real, coarse, occasionally-triggered mechanism — the MONITOR half is brain-faithful; it's the SELECTION/parsing half that should be integrated-graded, not post-hoc.

### 3. VERB-EXPERIENTIAL vs CURATED-TYPE-ONTOLOGY — MIXED / cue-integration; distributional verb-specific stats fit humans >= flat ontology.
- Verb-specific/item-specific learning is load-bearing in humans: structural-priming lexical boost (Pickering & Branigan 1998; Bock 1986 for the abstract component); verb-bias in ambiguity resolution (Garnsey et al. 1997; Trueswell 1996 — reduced-relative garden-pathing tracks THAT verb's own past-participle frequency, a direct analog to build-huts vs build-by-stream).
- Selectional-preference acquisition head-to-head: Resnik (1996) = WordNet-class + info-theoretic (the closest analog to our curated ontology); Erk (2007), Erk/Pado/Pado (2010), Pado/Pado/Erk (2007) = distributional/vector-space, no taxonomy. Modeling human plausibility ratings (McRae datasets), DISTRIBUTIONAL/corpus-derived models match human judgments AT LEAST AS WELL AS, often better than, WordNet-class models — because raw stats capture fine verb-specific typicality a flat class smooths away. (Fits model-to-human-ratings; the inference to "the brain computes distributionally" is speculative.)
- Pronoun case (they=men not flowers): cue-integration (Arnold 2010 accessibility/expectancy) — animacy/type does categorical filtering when a hard mismatch exists (flowers can't do agentive actions), verb-specific implicit-causality (Garvey & Caramazza 1974) + grammatical-role parallelism resolve the harder animate-vs-animate residual. Neither ontology-alone nor distributional-alone is sufficient.
- Best-supported picture: a HYBRID — coarse type/animacy for cheap categorical work + verb-specific LEARNED distributional statistics for fine-grained resolution. Our flat curated-ontology scene is the wrong KIND of knowledge for the fine cases (~0.45).

## IMPLICATION FOR THE BUILD (the redesign the drill points to)
Replace the binary WordNet/VerbNet coherence bit with a GRADED, VERB-SPECIFIC, distributionally-learned thematic-fit score (Erk/Baroni-Lenci/McRae-style), INTEGRATED into the parser's incremental patient decision rather than bolted on as a post-hoc contrastive filter. This is the roadmap's named "weak-supervision selectional-preference middle-path." 

CRITICAL CAVEAT (why this is NOT a free win, and what the VET already bounds): the SCV VET proved the POST-HOC CONTRASTIVE architecture is a null trainer even gold-perfect. The integrated-graded version is a DIFFERENT, LARGER build (per-step credit assignment, not a global-scalar contrast). AND graded distributional fit must be LEARNED from corpus — Erk/Pado-style models are validated on millions-to-billions of words; our eval slice is 163 gold sentences and every cheap signal this session hit a corpus-sparsity wall. The forward drill (`..._integrated_graded_experiential_reader_viability_corpus_precondition_...`) resolves the split: learning NEW distributional-fit VALUES is corpus-gated (fork-c investment); learning only the low-dim cue-integration WEIGHTS atop the existing scaffold is buildable on the 99k we have — GATED on a mandatory firing-rate diagnostic that rules out the same sparse-event trap that may hide inside the SCV's 0.000 result.

## MUST-FAIL CONTROL that distinguishes "graded fixes it" from "approach is wrong"
Degrade/scramble the graded thematic-fit cue -> the learned weight on it must DROP toward zero (genuine learning, not hand-coding). If a fixed-perfect scene STILL gives no held-out gain (as the SCV's gold-oracle test showed for the contrastive trainer), the scene-coherence signal is not the brain's trainer for this residual and we accept the bound.

## HONEST FRAME
Established biology: graded thematic-fit, N400 gradedness, incremental integration, verb-specific learning are all well-replicated. The step from these to "our specific binary/post-hoc/ontology SCV is the divergence" is a defensible synthesis but deflated (~0.45-0.50). The picture-verifier is not refuted as an IDEA; the binary post-hoc curated-ontology IMPLEMENTATION is. The brain-faithful version is corpus-gated for its hardest (new-fit-value) part.
