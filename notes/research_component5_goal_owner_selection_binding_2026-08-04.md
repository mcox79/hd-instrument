## (a) HEADLINE

Component-5 is a WIRING problem, not a new-mechanism problem, on two of its three legs (accumulation organ + coherence-margin selector both exist and are reusable verbatim) — but the SELECTION policy currently plugged into the reuse path is `_pick_strict_cb` / `RecencyEntityResolver`, both of which are recency-shaped in exactly the way MEMORY 2026-08-03 falsified (0/4) and this session's `exp_situation_model_goal_outcome_dimension_v1` (fb5b2a188) re-falsified at the goal-outcome-binding layer (0.333 < 0.5 hard-pass). The one genuinely-new piece is a coherence-based CANDIDATE GENERATOR for goal-owner/outcome binding that competes a role-content-aware antecedent against the recency antecedent and lets `route_passage`/`decide_keep_or_revert` (already built, already gold-free) pick the winner by coherence-margin delta. Component-3's role labels (EXPERIENCER/GOAL/PATIENT/etc.) are the missing INPUT FEATURE that makes a non-recency candidate constructible at all — before Component-3, there was no signal to bind by except position.

## (b) BIOLOGY — SHAPE, POSITION, METRIC

**What must be bound.** Two distinct discourse-scale relations track the SAME entity across non-adjacent sentences: (1) GOAL -> HOLDER ("Henry wanted the cherries" -> Henry is the goal-holder, the EXPERIENCER of the desire, not the AGENT of some later action), and (2) OUTCOME -> the entity whose goal it resolves (a later reaction/result event re-binds to the goal-holder, not to whichever entity was most recently mentioned).

**Zwaan & Radvansky (1998) event-indexing model.** Readers maintain a situation model with (at minimum) five indices updated as each new event/clause is read: TIME, SPACE, CAUSATION, INTENTIONALITY, and PROTAGONIST/ENTITY. The INTENTIONALITY index is exactly the goal-holder/goal-content dimension; when a new clause shares the PROTAGONIST index with a prior clause's open goal, the two get bound in the situation model regardless of how many intervening clauses (with different surface-subjects) sit between them. This is a CONTENT-based index-match, not a positional/recency search — the model explicitly predicts that reading slows and misbinding occurs when goal and outcome are separated by clauses that update OTHER protagonists, which is the exact failure signature recorded in this session (recency grabs the nearer foil).

**mPFC / DMN situation-model tracking.** Lerner et al. (2011) and follow-on paragraph/discourse-scale fMRI work locate the accumulating, updating situation-model representation in a slow-timescale DMN network (angular gyrus, precuneus, mPFC), distinct from and downstream of fast sentence-level parsing regions. The mPFC component is specifically implicated in tracking GOALS and mentalizing (theory-of-mind: whose goal is this, is it satisfied) — i.e. the goal-holder/outcome-owner binding is done by a slower, entity-and-goal-content-indexed system, not by the fast syntactic/working-memory mechanism that resolves local pronoun binding. This is a SEPARATE component from local anaphora resolution, consistent with the pipeline's Component-3 (local role labeling, fast, per-clause) vs Component-5 (situation-model-scale, slow, cross-clause) split.

**Hippocampal relational binding (Ranganath & Ritchey 2012, PMAT circuit).** The posterior-medial hippocampal system performs arbitrary relational binding (item-in-context, entity-to-event) via pattern completion keyed on CONTENT overlap (a partial cue reinstates the associated item), not on temporal recency per se — recency effects in memory retrieval are a separate, competing mechanism (temporal-context/TCM-style drift) that the hippocampal system must be gated AGAINST when the correct antecedent is not the most recent one. This is the direct biological analogue of "coherence selection must beat recency": the brain HAS a recency-biased retrieval mode (temporal context reinstatement) and a content/relational-binding mode, and normal comprehension requires the latter to dominate when goal/outcome content matches a non-recent entity. Centering-theory Cb (Grosz/Joshi/Weinstein) is a linguistic-level description of the SAME competition (backward-looking center ranked by grammatical role) but, as implemented here (`_pick_strict_cb` = most-recent-subject-clause), it is a pure recency-of-subjecthood metric with no goal/outcome content term — i.e. it never had the content signal needed to win the competition against recency; it only ever encoded WHEN, never WHAT.

**Outcome appraisal (OFC/vmPFC).** Once bound to the correct holder, "is the outcome MET or UNMET relative to this holder's goal" is an OFC/vmPFC valuation computation over the represented (goal, outcome) pair — this is what `GoalOutcomeRegister.appraise()` already computes (has_goal AND net-unmet), and it is confirmed working (0.833 fire, 0/6 false-positive on controls) GIVEN correct binding. The wall is entirely on the BINDING side, not the appraisal side.

**Why recency fails / coherence succeeds — the brain's own account.** Recency succeeds only when the goal-holder happens to be the last-mentioned entity (this session's `recency_beth_blocked_antecedent_is_recent` control, which recency gets right by accident — 1/3 of the recency probe set). It fails whenever a genuine trap intervenes (a foil entity mentioned more recently than the true goal-holder) because recency has no CONTENT term to override position with. The DMN/hippocampal account predicts exactly this asymmetry: content-indexed (INTENTIONALITY/PROTAGONIST-matched) retrieval is content-driven and therefore trap-immune in principle; a purely positional resolver is trap-vulnerable by construction. Component-5's job is to give the substrate that content term — which is precisely the thematic-role label (EXPERIENCER-of-a-desire-verb == candidate goal-holder; later clause's subject bearing an UNMET/MET-outcome predicate == candidate outcome-owner) now supplied by Component-3.

## (d) THE MECHANISM

Given a stream of role-labeled propositions (Component-3 output: `(entity, role, clause_idx)` where role in `{EXPERIENCER, GOAL, AGENT, PATIENT, RECIPIENT, none}` plus the existing GOAL/OUTCOME_UNMET/OUTCOME_MET lexical event typing from `exp_situation_model_goal_outcome_dimension_v1.py::type_sentence_events`):

1. **Goal-owner binding.** When a clause licenses a GOAL event (desire-verb + `V2_DESIRE` lexicon hit, mirroring the existing `type_sentence_events`), the goal-owner is the entity Component-3 labels EXPERIENCER (or AGENT, for non-psych goal verbs) in THAT clause — a purely local, same-clause binding (Component-3 already solves this; no cross-sentence search needed here).
2. **Outcome re-binding (the actual new step).** When a LATER clause licenses an OUTCOME_MET/OUTCOME_UNMET event with an ANAPHORIC or ambiguous subject (pronoun, or no explicit subject), build TWO candidate whole-passage entity-attributions of that outcome event: (a) `RecencyEntityResolver`-style — attach to the most recent gender/number-compatible entity; (b) content-match — attach to the most recent entity that CARRIES AN OPEN GOAL (has a GOAL-role event in the register with no OUTCOME yet bound) matching the pronoun's gender/number-compatible pool restricted to open-goal-holders. This is a direct, minimal implementation of the Zwaan INTENTIONALITY-index-match rule in (b): match on "who has an open goal," not on "who was mentioned last."
3. **Selection.** Feed both candidate whole-passage attributions into `route_passage` (reused verbatim) with `role_seq` = the real Component-3 role labels (not `agent/mentioned`) and `event_slots` from the existing slot-assignment logic. `decode_coherence_margins` scores each candidate by how much it raises the register's own top1-vs-runner-up decode margin at the positions the two candidates disagree on; `decide_keep_or_revert` adopts candidate (b) over baseline (a) iff its aggregate coherence-margin delta clears 0.02.
4. **Appraisal.** Feed the winning attribution's `(entity, role, slot)` triples into `GoalOutcomeRegister.add_typed_event` + `.appraise(entity)`, unchanged from the fb5b2a188 cell.

Why this should beat recency (0.333): the content-match candidate in step 2 is constructible ONLY because Component-3 supplies the "has an open GOAL" predicate — the exact feature `RecencyEntityResolver` and `_pick_strict_cb` never had access to. It does not need to beat recency on EVERY position, only on the positions where the two candidates disagree (route_passage's `changed_flagged` set) and only in aggregate — which is a much easier bar than the raw 0.333 accuracy figure suggests, since `decide_keep_or_revert` is a whole-resolution swap, not a per-position vote.

## (e) PRE-REGISTERED EVAL

**Isolation requirement.** Feed Component-5 GOLD role labels first (bypass Component-3 entirely) to VET the binding+selection mechanism independent of Component-3's own accuracy — Component-3's perceptron is still in flight (3c) and its errors must not be allowed to masquerade as a Component-5 failure or vice versa. A Component-3-in-the-loop end-to-end number is a SEPARATE, later measurement.

**Corpus.** Reuse the fb5b2a188 item bank verbatim: 6 `GOAL_BLOCK` naturalistic dispersed items, 6 matched `CONTROLS` (goal-met / outcome-trap-noise / physical-harm), 3 `RECENCY` genuine-pronoun-trap items (2 real traps + 1 non-trap sanity item) — plus, NEW for this drill, a **role-scramble control**: take each RECENCY item, keep the text and gold binding identical, but scramble which entity Component-3's gold roles are attached to (swap EXPERIENCER/GOAL labels between the two candidate entities). If the mechanism genuinely uses role content (not just entity-order or another positional proxy), binding accuracy on the scrambled-role version must COLLAPSE toward the recency floor (~0.333) or below — this is the standard scramble-control discipline already used elsewhere in this codebase (`thematic_role_labeler.scramble_weights`, `animacy_lexicon.scrambled_lookup_factory`) and is the single most important control: without it, a "coherence selector" could win by exploiting some OTHER positional regularity correlated with role labels rather than the role content itself.

**Metrics (per-axis, small-n honest caveat carried forward from fb5b2a188 — 6+6+3(+3 scrambled) items, DIRECTIONAL not powered for point estimates):**
- `goal_owner_binding_accuracy` (gold roles): fraction of GOAL_BLOCK items where the goal-owner attribution matches the annotated `owner`.
- `outcome_binding_accuracy` (gold roles): fraction of RECENCY items where the OUTCOME event binds to `gold_outcome_owner`, not the recency foil. **HARD target: beat 0.333** (the fb5b2a188 recency baseline on this exact axis).
- `control_false_fire_rate`: must stay 0/6 on CONTROLS (inherited discrimination check from fb5b2a188; appraisal stage is unchanged so this should not move, but must be re-verified after the binding-stage change since a binding bug could leak a false GOAL/OUTCOME pair into a control item).
- `role_scramble_collapse`: `outcome_binding_accuracy` on role-scrambled RECENCY items, must fall to <= recency floor (0.333) — proves content-dependence, not just "any change of resolver helped by accident."
- `coherence_margin_delta_sign`: the `route_passage` aggregate delta on positions where content-match disagrees with recency must be POSITIVE on genuine traps (mechanism internal consistency: the gold-free signal should itself prefer the correct candidate, not just get lucky on the accuracy metric).

**Bands:**
- **HARD-PASS**: `outcome_binding_accuracy >= 0.67` (beats 0.333 by a full trap, i.e. gets both genuine traps right, not just the free sanity item) AND `role_scramble_collapse` holds (<=0.333 on scrambled) AND `control_false_fire_rate == 0` AND `goal_owner_binding_accuracy >= 5/6`. => binding-selector mechanism validated; safe to wire Component-3 in for the end-to-end number.
- **MIDDLE-BAND**: `outcome_binding_accuracy` in [0.334, 0.66] (beats recency on at least one but not both real traps) OR scramble-collapse only partial (scrambled accuracy drops but doesn't reach the floor) => mechanism shows SOME content-sensitivity but is not yet reliably beating recency; do not wire end-to-end yet, drill the disagreement cases.
- **HARD-FAIL**: `outcome_binding_accuracy <= 0.333` (no better than recency) OR `role_scramble_collapse` FAILS (scrambled accuracy stays at or above the un-scrambled score, meaning the "coherence" signal never used role content at all — the recurring theme: a selector that LOOKS coherence-based but is secretly recency-shaped, exactly like `_pick_strict_cb` and `RecencyEntityResolver` already were) => representation/selection extension insufficient; the binding/selection organ itself needs redesign, not just better inputs.

## (f) HONEST ASSESSMENT

**Does Component-5 depend on Component-3 landing (3c) first?** For the END-TO-END real-pipeline number, yes. For VETTING THE MECHANISM, no — the gold-role isolation above is deliberately designed to be independently testable NOW (Component-3's item bank already has the same `owner`/gold annotations Component-5 needs; gold role labels for the 6+6+3 items can be hand-annotated in an afternoon using the SAME `VERB_FRAMES`/`PSYCH_FRAME` categories Component-3 already defines, since these are narrative desire-verb sentences squarely inside its existing frame table). Recommend running the gold-role isolation FIRST regardless of Component-3's 3c status — it is strictly cheaper, and a HARD-FAIL there would mean redesigning the binder before Component-3 lands anyway, saving a wasted integration cycle.

**Is Component-5 mostly wiring owned organs?** Yes, with one caveat: `route_passage`/`decode_coherence_margins` is validated ONLY on dense content (its own docstring: ties/false-keeps on sparse content, few-events-per-entity). The fb5b2a188 items are short (4-sentence) narrative snippets — LOWER density than the McGuffey g5g6 corpus this controller was validated on. This is the single biggest risk: the controller might be in its documented failure regime (sparse content) for exactly the corpus Component-5 needs it on, in which case it would tie or false-adopt rather than genuinely discriminate — which would show up as a MIDDLE-BAND or a deceptively-passing HARD-FAIL-shaped result that isn't really about role content at all. The role-scramble control is the direct check for this: if the controller is just noise-adopting on sparse content, scrambling roles would NOT collapse accuracy (because the "selection" was never using role content to begin with) — this is the same failure shape as the density-gating warning already documented in `self_improving_loop.py`'s own docstring, so it is a known, not speculative, risk.

**Second risk (recurring theme this session, named explicitly per the task's own framing):** even with role-content input, the CANDIDATE-GENERATION step in (d).2 ("prefer the entity carrying an open GOAL") could itself degenerate into a positional proxy if "open GOAL" is implemented sloppily (e.g. if only ONE entity ever has an open goal in these short items, the mechanism trivially reduces to "the goal-holder," which happens to also usually be non-recent by construction of the eval — meaning the mechanism could pass this eval by CONTENT-COINCIDENCE rather than genuine content-vs-recency competition). This is the "write-then-read-symmetric like CausalLinkRegister" risk restated at the binder layer: mitigated by requiring the RECENCY items specifically be genuine traps with >=2 candidate entities in the compatible pool (already true of the fb5b2a188 RECENCY set: Amy vs Jo, Tom vs Sid) — but should be re-checked when annotating the gold-role isolation set, and would benefit from 2-3 additional harder traps where BOTH entities carry some goal-shaped content (to force the selector to discriminate GOAL vs non-GOAL role, not just "has any event at all").

**P_deflated: 0.40** (lit-scan calibration penalty applied: this is a synthesis over the codebase's own validated components plus a well-established discourse-comprehension literature (Zwaan event-indexing, DMN situation-model tracking, hippocampal relational binding are all textbook-level, not contested), so the BIOLOGY leg is high-confidence; the deflation is entirely on the ENGINEERING claim that plugging real role labels into an existing density-gated controller will cleanly beat the 0.333 floor on an item bank smaller/sparser than the controller's own validated regime — that is a real, not merely formal, risk per the caveat above. Capped well below the 0.50 novel-synthesis ceiling because the actual binding step (d.2) is new code, not literature-backed by direct precedent for this exact substrate.

**Biggest risk, ranked:** (1) sparse-content density-gating failure mode of the reused coherence controller (documented, known, directly applicable) — the "coherence selector always turns out to secretly be recency-shaped" pattern recurring a FOURTH time, this time not because the selector lacks content input but because the controller's own validated operating regime doesn't cover this corpus's density; (2) trivial/coincidental pass via too-easy candidate pools (mitigated by the role-scramble control, but only if that control is actually run, not skipped as "obviously fine").

## Cross-thread synthesis

Ties together three prior threads that were treated as separate problems this session: coreference recency-falsification (MEMORY 2026-08-03, `_pick_strict_cb` 0/4), causal-attribution bridging (`CausalLinkRegister` CAUSE/EFFECT extension pattern), and goal-outcome binding (fb5b2a188, 0.333 recency floor). All three are instances of ONE missing organ per the session's own framing — this drill's finding is that the organ (gold-free coherence-margin candidate selection, `route_passage`/`decide_keep_or_revert`) already EXISTS and was validated 2026-08-02, but has only ever been fed a 2-symbol role vocabulary (`agent`/`mentioned`); it was never actually tested with rich content to select over. The "one remaining gap" may therefore be smaller than three separate build efforts — it may be exactly the wiring described in (c)/(d), pending the density-regime risk in (e) being checked out.

## Substrate-product implications

If HARD-PASS: closes the discourse-scale comprehension wall's binding component for goal/outcome tracking using ENTIRELY already-owned organs plus one new ~50-line content-candidate generator — cheap to ship, high signal for "the substrate can track who-wanted-what-and-did-they-get-it across a narrative," a demonstrably human-relatable comprehension capability for the product story. If MIDDLE/HARD-FAIL on the density-gating axis specifically: strengthens the case that `route_passage` needs a sparse-content variant (e.g. a lower/adaptive abstain band, or an event-count-weighted margin) before it can be treated as a general-purpose binding selector rather than a dense-corpus-specific tool — this would be a useful, scoped follow-up finding either way.
