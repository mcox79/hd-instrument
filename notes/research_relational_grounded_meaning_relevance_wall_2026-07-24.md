## Biology + math synthesis

### 1. WHY distributional/symmetric meaning is thin for relevance (the proof, not just the intuition)

Cosine similarity is **algebraically symmetric by construction**: cos(u,v) = cos(v,u) for all u,v. No scalar function built purely from that quantity can ever distinguish "X causes Y" from "Y causes X," or "A is-a B" from "B is-a A" — the direction information is destroyed before any downstream classifier gets to use it. This is made explicit in recent formal treatments (Kisung You, "Semantics at an Angle," arXiv:2504.16318, 2026) and traces to **Tversky's contrast model** (Tversky, *Psychological Review*, 1977) — the classical demonstration that even HUMAN similarity judgments are asymmetric (people rate "North Korea is similar to China" higher than "China is similar to North Korea"), meaning symmetric cosine is a category mismatch for the target phenomenon, not merely a coarse approximation of it.

The **knowledge-graph embedding literature gives a matched, formal separation result at the model-class level**: RotatE (Sun, Deng, Nie & Tang, ICLR 2019) proves (its own Lemmas 1-3) that TransE (Bordes et al. 2013) cannot represent symmetric relations, DistMult (Yang et al. 2015) cannot represent antisymmetric relations at all (its bilinear form is provably symmetric in head/tail), and ComplEx (Trouillon et al. 2016) cannot represent relation composition — RotatE's rotation-in-complex-space construction is specifically designed to be closed under all of symmetry/antisymmetry/inversion/composition simultaneously, and its empirical gains over the weaker model classes concentrate exactly on the relation types those classes are *provably* unable to represent (FB15k-237 Hits@1: DistMult/ComplEx ~0.16-0.24 vs RotatE 0.241; WN18RR Hits@1: ComplEx 0.41 vs RotatE 0.428). Independent architecture families converge on the same diagnosis: **Order-Embeddings** (Vendrov, Kiros, Fidler & Urtasun, ICLR 2016) replace symmetric cosine with an asymmetric partial-order violation score specifically to encode hypernymy/entailment, with measured gains; **Poincare embeddings** (Nickel & Kiela, NeurIPS 2017) use hyperbolic curvature to jointly encode similarity and hierarchy depth. All of this is a PROVEN structural limitation, not an empirical tendency that more training data could eventually overcome.

### 2. Grounded/embodied cognition: real, but the literature's own honest gap is exactly our task shape

Barsalou's Perceptual Symbol Systems (1999, *BBS*) argues concepts are reactivatable sensorimotor traces, giving them embedded action-outcome dynamics that can in principle be run forward to predict function/affordance — something pure co-occurrence cannot represent even in principle (theoretical-argument-only, well-cited). Lambon Ralph and colleagues' hub-and-spoke model of anterior temporal lobe (Patterson, Nestor & Rogers 2007, *Nat Rev Neurosci*; Lambon Ralph et al. 2017, *Nat Rev Neurosci*) shows integration ACROSS modality-specific spokes (visual, motor, affective) into a transmodal hub is what supports coherent, context-sensitive generalization (evidenced by semantic-dementia patients' graceful, cross-modal degradation) — well-established for the neural-architecture claim, theoretical-argument-only for its relevance-judgment implication. The feature-norm tradition (McRae, Cree, Seidenberg & McNorgan 2005; Cree & McRae 2003) is the clearest EMPIRICAL demonstration that human concepts decompose into TYPED features (perceptual, functional, encyclopedic/causal) that carry different diagnostic weight by concept class and task — a structural argument that a flat, untyped vector cannot natively support the same selective querying.

Direct grounded-vs-distributional comparisons (Andrews, Vigliocco & Vinson 2009, *Psych Review*; Bruni, Tran & Baroni 2011/2014, *JAIR*; Silberer, Ferrari & Lapata 2013/2014, ACL) consistently find real but MODEST, task-narrow gains, concentrated on similarity/categorization/word-association — not on picking the correct relevant fact from a similar-but-wrong one. Two honest counterweights matter here: **Grand, Blank, Pereira & Fedorenko (2022, *Nat Hum Behav*)** show plain text embeddings already contain rich, extractable functional/property information via "semantic projection" — it's present but entangled, not spontaneously separable without an external supervision signal (an important nuance: text vectors are not simply devoid of the needed content, they need the right READOUT). **Jones, Chang, Coulson, Michaelov, Trott & Bergen (2022, CogSci)** directly re-ran Glenberg & Robertson's (2000) classic affordance-sensibility test on modern LLMs — even GPT-3 accounted for only ~1/3 of the human effect size, and this gap did NOT close with scale. **Explicit, confirmed literature gap: no study anywhere in this scan tests whether grounding specifically helps a system pick the correct causally-relevant supporting fact out of a pool of lexically/topically similar distractors** — every grounding-vs-text comparison found tests word-to-word or word-to-concept judgments, not fact-to-fact relevance discrimination. This must not be treated as a settled positive result for grounding on our exact task.

### 3. Concepts-as-theories / causal-model categorization: the sharpest, most convergent, most directly-matching literature

Murphy & Medin (1985, *Psych Review*) founded the "theory theory": concepts cohere via causal/explanatory structure, not similarity metrics. Rehder's causal-model theory of categorization (Rehder & Hastie 2001, *JEP:General*; Rehder 2003, *JEP:LMC* and *Cognitive Science*) and the **causal status effect** (Ahn, Kim, Lassaline & Dennis 2000, *Cognitive Psychology* — corrected venue from the drill prompt's *Cognition*, verified) directly show people weight CAUSALLY CENTRAL features more heavily than causally peripheral ones of equal frequency/typicality — a quantitative, repeatedly-replicated (15+ years) demonstration that human relevance-weighting reads causal TYPE/role, not similarity magnitude. Keil (1989, *Concepts, Kinds, and Cognitive Development*) documents the developmental shift from surface/characteristic-feature categorization toward causal/theory-based categorization (the switched-features paradigm: a raccoon surgically altered to look like a skunk is still a raccoon once the causal/biological essence is understood).

**Gentner's structure-mapping theory (1983, *Cognitive Science*) and Gentner & Toupin (1986, *Cognitive Science*) are the single most directly-matching precedent for our EXACT measured failure mode.** Structure-mapping distinguishes literal similarity (many surface attributes AND many relations shared) from analogy (few surface attributes, many causal/relational structures shared) from mere-appearance match (many attributes, few relations — i.e. a surface-similar lure). Gentner & Toupin's cross-mapping experiments show **younger children default to mapping by surface/object similarity even when it violates the correct causal-relational structure, and only with development do they learn to override surface similarity in favor of the correct relational match under conflict** — this is, mechanism-for-mechanism, our own 29544 finding that the most brain-faithful signal (COH) performs BELOW random, i.e. the substrate's current representation is structurally in the position of Gentner & Toupin's kindergartners, not their older children. Chi, Feltovich & Glaser (1981, *Cognitive Science*) independently show the identical pattern between physics novices (sort by surface features) and experts (sort by causal/relational principle). Markman & Gentner (1993) show adults choose the relational match over the surface/object match ~70% of the time once structural alignment is engaged — a genuine existence proof, computationally instantiated in the Structure-Mapping Engine (Falkenhainer, Forbus & Gentner 1989), that a system representing relation-TYPE explicitly (not just similarity magnitude) can reproduce this override.

**Honest gap in this literature too:** there is no single paper that unifies Murphy & Medin / Rehder / Keil (categorization) with Gentner (analogy) under one explicit "relevance judgment" heading — this is the synthesizing agent's cross-literature integration, not a cited claim. But the convergence across two independently-programmatic literatures onto the same mechanism (explicit causal/relational-type representation + a comparison process that can detect and suppress a surface-match inconsistent with that structure) is about as strong as calibrated cognitive-science synthesis gets.
