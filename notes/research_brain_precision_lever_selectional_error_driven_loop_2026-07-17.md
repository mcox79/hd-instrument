# Research: how does the brain get BOTH breadth and precision in parsing -- what processes are we missing?

**Date:** 2026-07-17. **Filed by:** research (4 parallel Sonnet lit-scan sub-agents + Opus synthesis). **Trigger:** direct Director question following a head-to-head measurement on the glass-box no-LLM reading pipeline: BREADTH (construction coverage) is supplied by a grown construction inventory + schematization/abstraction and this WORKS; PRECISION is supplied by structural disambiguation (a real parse quadrupled a POS-pattern baseline) but frequency/surprisal over surface word/construction identity does NOT disambiguate (statistically indistinguishable from random). Neither alone reaches the classical envelope. Question: what does the brain add/integrate to get both, that the current build lacks?

**Method:** 4 parallel Sonnet lit-scan sub-agents (1: selectional preferences/thematic-fit as the meaning-based precision lever + how it's acquired distributionally; 2: error-driven learning loop -- structural priming, rational adaptation, garden-path reanalysis; 3: consolidation/schematization via sleep + developmental curriculum/bootstrapping; 4: multi-cue constraint-satisfaction integration + ML corroboration of the biggest precision/coverage levers), synthesized here. Calibration penalty per [[feedback-lit-scan-calibration-penalty]] applied throughout (deflate 0.15-0.25, cap novel-synthesis P at 0.50). Sources were WebSearch-snippet-verified in this pass, not full-text-PDF-fetched, unless noted -- flagged per-claim below.

## HEADLINE

**The frequency/surprisal signal that failed in the measurement was almost certainly the wrong KIND of frequency -- surface word/construction-identity frequency, which is meaning-blind. The brain's actual precision lever is a categorically different, also-frequency-based-but-MEANING-CONDITIONED signal: selectional preference / thematic fit (does this argument plausibly fill this role for this predicate), computed over verb-argument-CLASS co-occurrence, not verb-argument-token or POS-pattern co-occurrence.** This is corroborated by both literatures independently:

- **Biology:** McRae, Spivey-Knowlton & Tanenhaus (1998) show thematic-fit ratings (is "cop" a good agent, "criminal" a good patient, of "arrest"?) resolve exactly the ambiguity class (main-verb/reduced-relative garden-paths) that surface frequency cannot -- a documented ~18.5ms RT reduction at the disambiguating region when thematic fit favors the correct reading, on the SAME sentences where verb-frequency bias alone under-predicts. Resnik's selectional-preference-strength formalization (KL-divergence between a verb's argument-class distribution and the class prior) makes this a fully auditable, closed-form computation over class-conditional frequency tables -- **glass-box legal**, and critically, LEARNABLE from raw corpus co-occurrence (distributional, Landauer & Dumais's LSA lineage) with no hand-built ontology required, though a symbolic class hierarchy (WordNet-style) sharpens it.
- **ML:** the field's own largest measured precision levers are (a) reranking with additional lexical/semantic features beyond the base structural parse (Collins 2000: 88.2%->89.75% F1, ~13% relative error reduction) and (b) selectional/plausibility features layered onto syntax-only parsing -- both point at the same place: precision comes from an ADDITIONAL, meaning-conditioned scoring signal layered on top of (not instead of) the structural parse, combined via an explicit weighted rule (MacDonald/Seidenberg/McRae's constraint-based, multi-cue-integration account of human parsing is the direct biological analog of "reranking").

**A second, independent finding closes the loop question:** the brain does not just accumulate this signal passively -- it has a genuine ERROR-DRIVEN update mechanism (Chang/Dell/Bock's delta-rule account of structural priming; Fine/Jaeger/Farmer/Qian's surprisal-scaled "rational adaptation," where the size of the subsequent improvement scales with how surprising the disconfirming evidence was) that is mechanistically distinct from, and does more than, "read more text and recount frequencies" (which the current build already does via entrenchment-grows). This error-driven loop is what's missing alongside selectional semantics, and the two are complementary, not substitutable: selectional-fit scoring is a STATIC precision lever (a table); the error-driven loop is what keeps that table CORRECT and adapts it under distribution shift or when the static score gets a case wrong.

**Third, consolidation adds one more genuinely distinct capability beyond either of the above:** offline batch reprocessing (sleep-replay analog) with interleaved reactivation prevents catastrophic overwriting of the growing table (McClelland/O'Reilly/Norman's Complementary Learning Systems; a 2026 PubMed finding that interleaving novel+familiar replay within the SAME batch, not just repetition, is what prevents interference) and -- most interestingly -- can generate correct judgments on combinations NEVER directly observed, via relational closure over consolidated structure (Ellenbogen et al. 2007's transitive-inference-after-sleep result: A>B, B>C, ..., E>F learned; B>D and B>E only became reliably inferrable after a night of sleep, with the largest gain on the most-inferential-steps-away pairs). This is a genuinely different lever from "grow the table bigger" -- it is "periodically close the table under its own implied relations."

## (a) The brain's improvement processes, what each buys, glass-box legality

| # | Process | What it buys | Mechanism (brief) | Glass-box legal? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Selectional preference / thematic fit** (Resnik KL-divergence; McRae thematic-fit norms) | **PRECISION** specifically on structurally-licensed-but-implausible readings (the exact gap the measurement found) | KL-divergence or simple PPMI over verb x argument-SEMANTIC-CLASS co-occurrence counts (not verb x argument-token, not POS-pattern) | **YES** -- closed-form over named, auditable frequency tables |
| 2 | **Distributional acquisition of plausibility** (LSA: co-occurrence matrix + truncated SVD; count-based/PPMI methods generally) | Lets #1 be LEARNED from raw exposure, no hand-built ontology required | Deterministic linear-algebra decomposition (SVD) or plain co-occurrence counts | **YES** for count/PPMI/SVD-based methods; **NO** for word2vec/neural embeddings (opaque, non-interpretable dims) |
| 3 | **N400 as an online semantic-fit signal** | Confirms plausibility is computed INCREMENTALLY, pre-syntactic-commitment, as a graded (not binary) quantity | Neural measurement, not itself a copyable mechanism; the surprisal/co-occurrence-based MODELING correlate is glass-box | Signal itself N/A to copy; its computational correlate (count-based surprisal) is glass-box |
| 4 | **Multi-cue constraint satisfaction** (MacDonald/Seidenberg/Trueswell; McRae's simulations) | Explains HOW #1 combines with structural/frequency cues to get precision from EITHER alone failing on different construction subtypes -- i.e. breadth-of-applicability across ambiguity TYPES plus sharper precision on each | Weighted-sum / log-linear combination of named cue-values (verb-bias frequency, thematic-fit score, discourse-givenness count), weights tuned by relative cue-validity (frequency-of-being-right) | **YES** -- explicit weighted rule over named numbers |
| 5 | **Structural priming as error-driven learning** (Chang/Dell/Bock 2006 dual-path model) | The IMPROVEMENT LOOP: a delta-rule (weight += lr x error x input) triggered by mismatch between predicted and encountered structure | Prediction, compare to actual, update on mismatch; effect: OR~1.67 (abstract)/~3.26 (w/ lexical overlap) over 73-study meta-analysis; abstract component persists days, lexical component decays within ~1 sentence | **YES at the rule level** if applied to named counts (construction-type / argument-class tables); opaque if applied to distributed connectionist weights |
| 6 | **Rational/surprisal-scaled adaptation** (Fine, Jaeger, Farmer & Qian 2013) | The loop's TUNING law: update size scales with how surprising the correction was, not a flat learning rate | Implicit running Bayesian update of a construction's probability estimate, minutes-scale within a single session (contested: a 2018 replication attempt failed, flag as not fully settled) | **YES** -- explicit probability-update rule |
| 7 | **Garden-path reanalysis as targeted repair** (Frazier & Rayner; van Gompel & Pickering's race model; Christianson et al.'s "lingering misinterpretation") | Single-TRIAL error correction, defined over the specific abandoned structural commitment; empirically NOT a clean overwrite (residual false-belief traces persist) | Down-weight the disconfirmed alternative, up-weight the correct one, but leave a partial residual (do not silently overwrite) | **YES** as an explicit weight-bump rule; the "partial, not clean" finding is a design cue (log confidence decay, don't hard-delete) |
| 8 | **Consolidation / interleaved replay** (McClelland/O'Reilly/Norman CLS; 2026 interleaved-SWS-replay finding) | Prevents catastrophic overwriting as the selectional/construction tables grow; schema-congruent material integrates FAST (Tse et al. 2007: one-trial integration when congruent with existing schema) | Batch reprocessing of stored counts in randomized/INTERLEAVED order (not append-only), with congruence-conditional update rate | **YES** -- explicit batch-reprocessing schedule, fully auditable |
| 9 | **Relational closure beyond raw exemplars** (Ellenbogen et al. 2007, PNAS: transitive inference emerges only after sleep) | Correct judgments on COMBINATIONS NEVER DIRECTLY OBSERVED (e.g., novel verb-argument-class pairs derivable via class-hierarchy closure), a capability distinct from bigger raw counts | Consolidation performs (or enables) an implicit graph-closure step over consolidated relational edges | **YES** -- an explicit transitive/hierarchy-closure computation over a class ontology is fully symbolic |
| 10 | **Developmental curriculum / bootstrapping** (Elman 1993 starting-small; syntactic/semantic bootstrapping cycle, Gleitman/Pinker) | Order-of-acquisition CAN help specific constructions (recursion/center-embedding) but is CONTESTED as a general law (Rohde & Plaut's "Less is Less" directly rebuts generality; effect is corpus/task-dependent) | Explicit complexity-ordering of training/reading data by a named metric (embedding depth, clause count) | **YES**, but LOW PRIORITY -- real effect, not general, smallest expected marginal payoff of the processes reviewed |

## (b) Ranked recommendation

Given the build already has entrenchment-grows (breadth, via construction/lexical frequency) + abstraction/schematization (generalization to unseen forms) + a real structural parse (partial precision), and explicitly LACKS selectional-semantics and an error-driven improvement loop:

**#1 process to add for PRECISION: selectional-preference / thematic-fit scoring as a SECOND weighted cue, integrated with the structural parse via explicit multi-cue combination (processes 1+2+4 above, treated as one build item).**

Concretely: compute a verb x argument-SEMANTIC-CLASS co-occurrence table from the SAME corpus already being read (not a separate resource requirement -- this is "grow," not "install," consistent with the CxG-inventory framing already adopted for constructions), score plausibility via PPMI or Resnik-style KL-divergence over that table, and combine this score with the existing structural-parse score via an explicit weighted-sum rule when >=2 structurally-licensed parses compete. This is the one lever BOTH literatures independently point at as the dominant, best-evidenced precision mechanism that plain frequency-of-surface-form cannot supply (the McRae 18.5ms effect and the Collins 2000 13%-relative-error-reduction reranking result are the two strongest quantitative anchors). **Caution flag (own field pitfall, not a fresh finding):** Resnik's information-theoretic KL formulation is documented to sometimes be BEATEN by a simpler frequency/PPMI-based variant (over-fits to the single modal role, under-covers rarer-but-valid roles) -- if the KL form underperforms on the substrate's own data, the fix is to simplify the SCORING FORMULA (drop to PPMI/raw-joint-frequency), not to abandon selectional semantics as a lever.

**#2, the improvement LOOP: an error-driven, surprisal-scaled update over the SAME named counts (processes 5+6+7).** When a downstream signal (a later grounding check, a contradiction, a hard-rule-abstain case later resolved by some other means) reveals the first-pass disambiguation was wrong, bump the correct alternative's count in the selectional/construction table by an amount SCALED to how surprising the correction was (Fine et al.'s mechanism; Chang/Dell/Bock's delta rule, applied here to named counts rather than distributed weights, keeping it glass-box). This is mechanistically DIFFERENT from entrenchment-grows (passive frequency accumulation from raw exposure): it requires an explicit error/surprise SIGNAL -- something must indicate the first guess was wrong -- to trigger the update, and the update size should be error-proportional, not flat. Corroborated by ML's self-training result (McClosky, Charniak & Johnson 2006: +1.1 F1 absolute, ~12% relative error reduction, from using the parser's own corrected output as additional training signal) as the closest direct engineering analog of "error-driven bootstrapping loop."

**#3 (secondary, lower urgency, do after #1/#2 land): consolidation-style batch reprocessing (interleaved, not append-only) plus explicit relational/hierarchy closure over the argument-class ontology (processes 8+9).** This is the lever that reaches genuinely NOVEL, never-directly-observed verb-argument combinations (the Ellenbogen effect) and prevents order-dependent overwriting as the tables scale -- valuable, but strictly additive on top of #1/#2, not a substitute.

**#4 (lowest priority): developmental curriculum ordering (process 10).** Real but contested-as-general and the smallest expected marginal payoff of the five; worth a cheap side-channel log (does accuracy on deeply-recursive constructions correlate with how much simple-construction exposure preceded it) rather than a dedicated build.

## Falsifiable predictions (HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL)

**Prediction 1 -- selectional/thematic-fit scoring is genuinely independent precision signal beyond surface frequency (not a repackaging of the frequency signal that already failed).**
P estimate: **P=0.40** (deflated; McRae 1998's effect is well-precedented for HUMAN reading times, but translating it to a symbolic engineered scorer on this substrate's own data is untested; capped per novel-synthesis rule).
HARD-PASS: on a test set where structural-frequency score and selectional/thematic-fit score are DECORRELATED by construction (i.e., cases exist where the frequent surface pattern is semantically implausible and vice versa), adding the selectional score improves precision by >=10-15 points over structural-parse-alone, with the gain concentrated specifically on the decorrelated subset (ruling out the confound that selectional score is just re-deriving what frequency already gave).
HARD-FAIL: <3 point gain overall, OR the entire gain comes from cases where selectional score and surface frequency already agree (no independent signal on the decorrelated subset) -- would mean selectional semantics collapses into the same (already-failed) frequency signal for this substrate and a different precision mechanism is needed.

**Prediction 2 -- an error-driven, surprisal-scaled update loop measurably improves precision on repeated occurrences of the same ambiguous construction type within a single corpus pass (distinct from passive entrenchment-grows).**
P estimate: **P=0.35** (deflated; Fine et al.'s human effect is itself contested by a 2018 non-replication, and translating "rational adaptation" to an engineered system with an explicit error-trigger is a novel-synthesis application).
HARD-PASS: precision on LATER occurrences of a given ambiguous construction type rises by >=10 points relative to EARLY occurrences within one corpus pass, and the size of a given update correlates with how surprising (low-prior) the triggering correction was.
HARD-FAIL: no monotonic within-corpus improvement (flat precision across occurrence order), or update size is uncorrelated with surprisal -- would mean this substrate's errors are not usefully modeled as accumulating error-driven experience at this grain, and a STATIC (pre-computed, non-adaptive) selectional table performs equally well, in which case the loop should be deprioritized relative to just building a bigger static table.

**Prediction 3 -- consolidation-style relational closure over the argument-class hierarchy generates correct plausibility judgments on verb-argument combinations never directly observed in raw co-occurrence.**
P estimate: **P=0.35** (deflated; Ellenbogen's effect is well-replicated for episodic transitive inference in humans, but whether an analogous closure over a LINGUISTIC class hierarchy transfers is untested).
HARD-PASS: on held-out verb-argument pairs absent from the raw co-occurrence table but derivable via class-hierarchy closure (verb licenses superclass C, test noun is a member of subclass of C), closure-based scoring beats a no-closure baseline by >=15 points.
HARD-FAIL: <3 point improvement -- would mean the raw distributional counts already capture what's needed for this task and the extra consolidation/closure step is not pulling its weight; defer this lever.

**Prediction 4 -- if the information-theoretic (KL-divergence) selectional-preference formulation underperforms on the substrate's own data, a simpler PPMI/joint-frequency formulation will do better (a known field pitfall, not a fresh claim).**
P estimate: **P=0.45** (moderately confident; directly precedented as a documented finding in the selectional-preference literature, only the substrate-specific transfer is novel).
HARD-PASS: on the SAME test set, if KL-based selectional-association underperforms structural-parse-plus-nothing, a plain PPMI/joint-frequency variant of the same verb x argument-class table matches or beats the KL formulation.
HARD-FAIL: KL-based formulation is clearly and consistently better than the simpler PPMI variant across test cases -- in that case, adopt the KL formulation as specified rather than simplifying.

## Substrate-product implications

For the glass-box, no-LLM read->grow pipeline: do not conclude from the frequency/surprisal-failed-to-disambiguate result that probabilistic/frequency-based methods are categorically the wrong tool for precision -- the ML and biological literatures both show frequency-based methods DO deliver large, real precision gains (Collins 2000's 13% relative error reduction; Bangalore & Joshi's 77.2%->92.2% supertagging jump cited in the adjacent hard-rule-vs-predictive note) when the frequency is computed over the right variable (argument semantic class / thematic role, not surface word sequence or POS pattern). The concrete near-term build target: a verb x argument-class co-occurrence table (learnable from the same reading corpus, no external resource dependency required, though VerbNet/WordNet class hierarchies can seed/sharpen it per the sibling lexicon-richness note), scored via PPMI (fallback) or KL-divergence (primary, per Resnik), combined with the existing structural-parse score via an explicit weighted rule -- ALL of it named, auditable numbers, no learned embeddings, no LLM. Layer the error-driven update on top once the static table is validated (Prediction 1 gates Prediction 2 -- build the static scorer first, only add the adaptive loop if the static one demonstrates independent signal). Consolidation/closure is a genuine additional lever but should wait until the first two are landed and validated, per standard sequencing discipline (do not build the third-order refinement before confirming the first-order lever works).

## VERDICT (one line)

**The missing precision lever is selectional-preference/thematic-fit scoring over MEANING-conditioned (argument-class) co-occurrence -- a different signal than the surface-frequency/surprisal signal that already failed, both glass-box legal and directly corroborated by ML's largest measured precision gains (reranking, self-training) -- and the missing improvement LOOP is an error-driven, surprisal-scaled update over those same named counts (triggered by a downstream disconfirmation signal, not passive re-exposure), with consolidation-style interleaved reprocessing plus relational closure as a validated third-order addition for reaching never-observed combinations.**
