## HEADLINE

The reframe is **half right, half overclaim, and the literature itself supplies the missing half.** Two independent brain literatures were drilled: (1) hippocampal-entorhinal "cognitive map" grid-like codes for non-spatial/abstract content, (2) the symbol-grounding-problem debate over whether relations-among-symbols can ground meaning. The honest synthesis: **relational/map-position grounding is real and neurally dissociable from attribute grounding** (Garvert, Dolan & Behrens 2017, eLife — a hippocampal-entorhinal map built from *purely discrete, attribute-free* stimuli, coded by graph communicability, not coordinate distance) — but **it is not a free-standing alternative mechanism that bypasses exogenous/attribute anchoring.** Every other headline "abstract cognitive map" result (Constantinescu, O'Reilly & Behrens 2016 Science; Park, Constantinescu & Behrens 2021/2024) turns out, on inspection, to be a **2-3 dimensional continuous ATTRIBUTE-COORDINATE code wearing a grid-cell costume** — bird morphology (neck length x leg length), social competence x trust — literally Barsalou attribute channels fused into a joint vector, not a categorically different mechanism. And the philosophy-of-mind literature (Harnad 1990; Taddeo & Floridi's "zero semantic commitment" condition; Bender & Koller 2020's octopus argument) is unambiguous that **pure relational structure with no reachable exogenous anchor is exactly the failure mode, not a solution** — this is the mainstream, still-live critique of distributional semantics and LLM embeddings specifically. So: relational/map grounding is a real, distinct, brain-validated regime, but it is **parasitic on** — not a substitute for — exogenous attribute grounding, and it only earns the name "grounding" (not mere "form") when the graph is built from an experienced interaction/observation history and is composition-reachable back to genuinely-anchored concrete nodes.

## Cover, answered in order

### 1. Is this brain-real? Role-vs-attribute dissociation

**Two lit-scans converged on a clean split, not a single answer.** (Sub-agent A, grid-code literature; Sub-agent B, symbol-grounding literature — both independently returned the same shape of answer.)

- **Constantinescu, O'Reilly & Behrens 2016** (*Science* 352:1464, doi:10.1126/science.aaf0941): the "abstract conceptual space" was bird morphology — neck length x leg length, two **continuous, perceptual, rateable** dimensions. The hexagonal/grid-like fMRI signal is computed from the trajectory angle through this 2D continuous space. This is mathematically a **2-attribute joint coordinate**, not a symbol-free relational structure.
- **Park, Constantinescu, Behrens et al. 2021 (*Nat Neurosci*) / 2024 (eLife)**: social space dimensions were competence and popularity/trustworthiness — again continuous, directly measurable attributes (trust = money returned in a trust game; competence = profit rate), fused into a 2D grid-coded coordinate. Same structure as above.
- **Garvert, Dolan & Behrens 2017** (*eLife* 6:e17086, "A map of abstract relational knowledge in the human hippocampal-entorhinal cortex"): objects were **arbitrary discrete images with explicitly zero continuous attribute content** ("there were no continuous dimensions in our discrete stimulus sets — the dimensions had to be extracted from the associations"). The neural code correlated with **graph communicability** (a pure relational/topological quantity), not coordinate distance. **This is the one clean, direct neural dissociation of role-in-a-map from attribute-value** — and it is real.
- **Whittington et al. 2020 Tolman-Eichenbaum Machine** (*Cell* 183:1249) and **Behrens et al. 2018** (*Neuron* 100:490, "What Is a Cognitive Map?"): formalize exactly this split as an architecture — a **structural/transition code** (entorhinal-like, content-independent, transfers across domains as a scaffold) separate from a **content/sensory code** (hippocampal, binds structure-position to whatever sensory input is locally present). Behrens 2018 explicitly generalizes: map-like coding "may be an instance of general coding mechanisms capable of organizing knowledge of all kinds" — this is the direct brain precedent for "structure reused across domains," i.e., the mechanism a map-builder (Course C) is trying to instantiate computationally.

**Verdict on Q1:** the evidence does NOT support a clean Barsalou-embodied-attributes-vs-relational-role dichotomy at the level the reframe implies. It supports a **dimensionality/regime split within the same neural machinery**: when continuous attribute dimensions exist, the hippocampal-entorhinal system builds a joint-coordinate map over them (still attribute grounding, just multi-dimensional and geometrically organized); when stimuli are genuinely discrete/symbolic with no attribute content, the SAME machinery falls back to pure relational/graph-communicability coding (Garvert) — a real, distinct, and separately useful regime, but a minority case in the literature, not the default or the general answer. Both regimes are brain-real; math/logic operators are closer to Garvert's discrete case than to Constantinescu's/Park's continuous case (an "operation" doesn't have a natural 1-2D magnitude the way bird-neck-length does), which is a genuine point in the reframe's favor for THIS domain specifically.

### 2. Does this reconcile the 3 attribute-negatives, or did the channels just underperform?

Both, in different proportions per negative:

- **Scalar magnitude (MIDDLE_BAND, -0.13, unmoved):** this is exactly the failure mode predicted by Lakoff & Nunez (previously drilled, `research_math_social_abstract_grounding_core_expansion_2026-07-10.md`) — what math's grounding metaphors preserve is **inferential/compositional structure** (ordering, iteration, part-whole), not scalar content. A flat 1D "how much magnitude" value cannot represent "distributes over," "commutes," "is the inverse of." **This is a real reconciliation** — the negative is consistent with both the reframe AND with the earlier note's own prediction that scalar channels underdetermine composition. It does NOT, however, prove attributes are categorically wrong: Constantinescu/Park show a **2-3D joint attribute coordinate** captures real abstract structure the brain actually uses. The scalar channel may simply have been the wrong SHAPE (1D instead of a joint multi-D code), not proof attributes-of-any-kind fail. This is directly testable in parallel (Prediction 3 above) and should not be skipped in favor of a relational-only story.
- **Social power-affiliation (HARD_FAIL_AFFECT_RELABEL):** this is literally the Park/Constantinescu mechanism class (2D competence-affiliation attribute pair, brain-validated) — the substrate's attempted implementation used the RIGHT mechanism class per the literature, but the specific implementation collapsed onto a correlated existing channel (relabeled as affect). This is the exact failure mode the same-day companion note (`research_multi_attribute_grounding_fusion_ATL_hub_2026-07-10.md`, Pitfall #1: "measure pairwise attribute correlation before fusing, or a redundant pair counts almost as one vote") predicted and warned against — **an engineering/independence-verification failure, not evidence against relational/map grounding, and not evidence attributes categorically fail.**
- **Metaphor-structural bridge (HARD_FAIL_FAIRNESS, "any embodied edge inflated it, the SPECIFIC mapping did no work"):** this is the most consequential of the three, and the philosophy-of-mind lit-scan gives it a precise name: **the Bender & Koller (2020) octopus failure mode** — a system whose score depends only on relational/graph FORM (an edge exists) and not on whether the specific mapped CONTENT is correct is, by the field's own dominant definition, exhibiting exactly the non-grounding signature (position in a relational structure with no discriminating connection to what the structure is supposed to represent). Per the memory-locked discipline on discriminator telemetry-sensitivity and saturation-vacuous smoke, this reads as **the fairness gate correctly catching a confound in the TEST's must-fail control** (it apparently never included a same-density-wrong-mapping SCRAMBLED arm) rather than a clean refutation of relational/structural grounding as a mechanism. The Cheap decisive test above is designed specifically to re-run this with the missing control.

**Net: the 3 negatives are reconciled, but the reconciliation splits roughly 1/3 "genuine reframe support" (scalar-channel structural insufficiency for composable operators, matches Lakoff-Nunez + Garvert), 1/3 "engineering failure, mechanism class was right" (social channel), 1/3 "test-design confound, unresolved pending the redo" (metaphor bridge) — not a clean sweep for either the pure-attribute or the pure-relational story.**

### 3. The unifying claim — coherent? non-circular? where does the exogenous referent enter?

**Is "relational grounding" coherent and non-circular, or does the symbol-grounding critique rule it out?** Harnad (1990) does NOT require every symbol be directly sensorimotor-grounded — his own resolution explicitly licenses **inheritance via composition**: "zebra" = "horse & stripes" inherits groundedness from already-grounded "horse" and "stripes" with no direct zebra-experience needed. So pure relational/definitional composition on top of a grounded base is licensed, not rejected, by the founding critique. Taddeo & Floridi's "zero semantic commitment" condition sharpens this into a formal requirement: the relational network must not presuppose ANY meaning not causally earned by the system itself — every claim of grounding must bottom out, via a traceable path, in something non-symbolic. Bender & Koller's 2020 octopus argument is the modern, still-live version of the same critique applied directly to relational/distributional systems (word embeddings, LLMs): **relational position in a co-occurrence/similarity space, however large or well-structured, is form-only and does not constitute grounding** — this is the dominant NLP-community position today, not a fringe view, and it is the exact critique the HARD_FAIL_FAIRNESS result stumbled into.

**So where does the exogenous referent enter?** Precisely where Q3 speculates: **abstract grounding is PARASITIC on concrete grounding via the relational map, not a parallel/independent channel.** The mechanism, stated precisely from the converged literature:
1. A relational/structural map (Garvert-style graph, or Whittington/Behrens TEM structure code) can represent genuinely abstract, non-continuous concepts — this is real and dissociable from attribute coding.
2. That map is only "grounded" (not merely "formally structured") if (a) it is built from an **experienced interaction/observation history** (the graph's edges come from real transitions/associations actually encountered, not asserted/synthetic edges — this is what Taddeo-Floridi's Z-condition and Harnad's causal-coupling requirement both demand), and (b) **every abstract node is composition-reachable, via a finite chain, to at least one node carrying a genuinely exogenous value** (a measured attribute: sensorimotor norm, magnitude, observed affordance/interaction outcome).
3. Relational STRUCTURE (composability, transformation, order-preservation — what Lakoff-Nunez call the preserved content of a grounding metaphor) is what lets abstract concepts INHERIT meaning from concrete anchors without needing their own direct sensorimotor content. This is not circular because the chain terminates in non-relational, non-symbolic values; it IS circular (and IS what the octopus critique correctly rejects) if the chain only ever terminates in other symbols/relations with no exogenous anchor anywhere reachable.

**Is grounding a BYPRODUCT of the reasoning map (grounding + reasoning = same geometry), or separate?** **Separate, and this finding sharpens rather than overturns the same-day convergence-architecture conclusion** (`notes/convergence_architecture_grounding_is_the_verifier_2026-07-10.md`, which already concluded "grounding enters inference through the VERIFIER, not the geometry," based on the loop-closer's negative result that grounding-in-the-geometry didn't help relational inference). This drill's answer is fully consistent: the relational MAP gives you **structure** — composability, transfer across domains, the TEM structure/content split, exactly what the PROPOSE/geometry layer of the generate-and-test architecture needs. It does NOT, by construction, give you grounding-as-verification — a well-structured map built entirely from asserted/synthetic edges with no exogenous reachability is exactly a closed relational island (Bender-Koller's octopus), well-formed and useless for verification. **Building the map correctly (real edges, exogenous-reachable) is a prerequisite that also gets you closer to grounding, but it does not make grounding a free byproduct of reasoning-map construction** — the reachability/anchoring property has to be actively engineered and checked (the reachability-check control in the Cheap decisive test above), not assumed to fall out of building Course C.

## Substrate-product implications

1. **Do not abandon the attribute-channel program in favor of a pure relational/map story** — the literature shows both are real, brain-validated mechanisms operating in different regimes (continuous-dimension concepts use joint attribute coordinates; discrete/symbolic concepts use pure relational codes), and 2 of the 3 negatives are better explained by engineering failures (channel correlation, missing must-fail control) than by "attributes categorically don't work."
2. **The metaphor-bridge mechanism (B) is not refuted — it was undertested.** Re-run with the SCRAMBLED-mapping must-fail control before concluding anything about relational/structural grounding for math/logic concepts.
3. **The single highest-leverage structural fix, if the redo confirms genuine relational signal:** any future relational/geometric-code cell (Course C map-builder, additive/geometric relation codes) must be built from, or explicitly composition-reachable to, genuinely exogenous attribute-anchored nodes — NOT purely from the existing ConceptNet-style symbol graph, which is itself, per the zero-grounded-data finding, a closed relational island. This reframes the 6-instance track record of relational-code negatives: they were tested on a graph with no reachable exogenous anchors, which the philosophy-of-mind literature says is exactly the regime where relational codes cannot ground (though they may still capture real structure for other purposes, e.g. compositional PROPOSE).
4. **Course C (map-builder) gets one new mandatory acceptance criterion**: a reachability audit (fraction of concept-nodes with a finite composition path to an exogenously-anchored node) reported alongside its existing structural-transfer metrics — this is cheap (a graph traversal, no new data) and directly operationalizes the non-circularity requirement this drill establishes as necessary.
5. None of this claims the substrate replicates hippocampal-entorhinal function. It borrows the measured DISTINCTION the literature establishes (attribute-coordinate map vs pure-relational map, and the non-circularity requirement for the latter) as concrete, falsifiable design criteria for the next cell.
