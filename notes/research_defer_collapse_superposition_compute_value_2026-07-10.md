## (a) HEADLINE

**Real, but the win is not "superposition is magic" — it is "premature point-estimation is a provable information loss, and this substrate already has three independent, converging pieces of evidence that it is paying that cost somewhere in its live inference pipeline."** The strongest, most mature, most provable external literature (sum-product vs. max-product equivalence-at-a-limit, Fisher-information loss under quantization, SMC/particle-filter ESS-triggered resampling, BCJR vs. Viterbi in iterative decoding) all converge on one clean, textbook-grade statement: **any hard collapse to a point estimate is a weakly-dominated move whenever a downstream stage could still use the discarded uncertainty, and only becomes STRICTLY the right move once a measured degeneracy criterion (not a fixed schedule) says the distribution has nothing left to give.** This substrate's own resonator work independently rediscovered exactly this principle (soft-decision chaining beats hard-decision chaining across hops, P_deflated=0.35) before this drill ever ran, and the just-banked convergence architecture (`notes/convergence_architecture_grounding_is_the_verifier_2026-07-10.md`) already encodes "geometry PROPOSES in superposition, verifier COLLAPSES" as the working design. **This drill's contribution is not a new mechanism — it is upgrading that design from an empirical pattern-match to a principled one, and naming the concrete missing piece: an adaptive, measured collapse-trigger (an ESS-style degeneracy criterion) rather than a fixed collapse schedule.** Ranked by real-vs-hype, brain-grounded, value-to-substrate: (1) deferred/soft collapse in multi-stage inference — STRONG, load-bearing, already substrate-validated in miniature; (2) sketching/estimation-in-superposition — REAL, has a direct published VSA bridge, currently a genuine on-disk gap (confirmed zero Bloom/Count-Sketch/reservoir primitive exists in `hdlab/`); (3) generative interpolation as a PROPOSE-stage hypothesis generator — real ONLY as an input to a hard verifier, the "interpolation itself is trustworthy" version is actively debunked in the literature; (4) reservoir computing — real, well-established field, but no clear load-bearing connection to THIS substrate's live wall surfaced; (5) quantum-inspired interference/amplitude algorithms — weakest; the literature's own dequantization results argue AGAINST superposition conferring a general classical compute advantage. The single biggest way this whole thesis could be WRONG: this substrate's OWN correlation-hurts-capacity and SNR-floor/order-statistic findings show that deferring collapse does NOT help when the information was already destroyed by crosstalk AT compose/encode time — deferred collapse is a fix for "information present but hard to read," not for "information gone." The crux-v2 HARD-FAIL band already operationalizes exactly this distinction.

## 1. Ranked candidate mechanisms — real vs. hype, brain-grounded

### Rank 1 — Deferred/soft collapse in multi-stage inference (candidate 1) — LOAD-BEARING

**External grounding (mature, provable, not folklore):**
- **Sum-product vs. max-product**: on tree-structured factor graphs both are exact; max-product is literally sum-product at the zero-temperature limit (Kschischang, Frey & Loeliger, "Factor Graphs and the Sum-Product Algorithm," IEEE Trans. Info. Theory 47:498-519, 2001). Carrying full marginals is provably necessary whenever the downstream loss is not itself 0/1 on the full joint MAP.
- **Fisher-information loss under quantization**: any hard-decision (quantizing) map has a nontrivial null space with respect to score-function directions — it strictly reduces recoverable Fisher information except in measure-zero special cases (general information-loss-under-binning results, e.g. arXiv:1304.5075, arXiv:1902.04606). This is the clean, rigorous version of "collapsing early throws information away" — not intuition, a theorem.
- **Particle filtering / SMC resampling timing**: Effective Sample Size `ESS = 1 / sum(w_i^2)` formally measures collapse-risk; Liu & Chen (1998) established that resampling at every step is provably wasteful — resampling always ADDS variance even as it fixes degeneracy, so deferring resampling until ESS crosses a threshold strictly dominates a fixed schedule. This is the single most quantitative, most directly portable result in the whole drill.
- **Viterbi (hard, max-product) vs. BCJR (soft, sum-product, full posterior)**: hard-decision Viterbi discards reliability information at the symbol level; iterative/turbo decoding architectures universally use soft (BCJR-style) message passing between interior stages because hard decisions at ANY interior stage of an iterative loop provably break the iterative gain.

**Cleanest general statement extracted (this is the load-bearing sentence of the whole drill):** *"Any operation that collapses a distribution over hypotheses to a single point estimate is a (possibly strict) information-losing map with respect to the sufficient statistics of the original distribution; therefore in any pipeline with more than one inference stage, deferring that collapse until the final stage (or until a measured degeneracy criterion like ESS is crossed) weakly dominates committing early, with strict dominance whenever downstream stages could exploit the discarded uncertainty."*

**Brain grounding (probabilistic population codes — Ma, Beck, Latham, Pouget, "Bayesian inference with probabilistic population codes," Nature Neuroscience 9:1432-1438, 2006, PubMed 17057707):** Poisson-like neural response variability makes the log-likelihood a LINEAR function of population activity, which is why simple linear combination of two populations implements near-optimal Bayesian cue integration — cortex represents full distributions, not point estimates, as its DEFAULT currency. Collapse to a discrete choice is a property of specific downstream decision/action-selection circuitry (evidence-accumulation regions such as LIP, superior colliculus; drift-diffusion/race-model literature), not something that happens eagerly at every processing stage. This is mainstream, широко-cited computational neuroscience (competing/refining accounts exist — sampling-based codes, distributed distributional codes — but the core "distribution upstream, collapse at the decision bottleneck" architecture is not seriously contested at the level this drill needs it).

**Substrate-internal confirmation (already banked, independent of this drill):**
- `notes/research_resonator_hard_fail_revival_disparate_fields_2026-06-24.md`: the multi-hop resonator HARD_FAIL was diagnosed as hard-decision error-propagation, structurally identical to DFE/turbo-equalization error propagation; soft-decision chaining ranked highest of 5 disparate-field angles (P_deflated=0.35), with CA3 graded (non-winner-take-all) reactivation cited as the brain existence proof — this is the SAME mechanism the external lit-scan just independently re-derived from a completely different angle (SMC/information theory vs. this substrate's own communications-theory drill). Two independent routes landing on the identical prescription is a real corroboration signal, not just agreement-by-coincidence.
- `notes/convergence_architecture_grounding_is_the_verifier_2026-07-10.md`: already prescribes GEOMETRY (propose, superposed/compositional) feeding a GROUNDED VERIFIER (collapse point) as the working architecture — this drill supplies the missing theoretical justification for WHY that shape is right, and the missing operational detail (see Section 3) for HOW to decide when to collapse.
- `notes/research_resonator_decode_capacity_ceiling_crux_v2_2026-07-10.md`: verify+rank stages are HEALTHY conditioned on recall — i.e. correct candidates are not destroyed, just not surfaced by single-shot hard argmax. This is EXACTLY the regime where deferred collapse is expected to help (information present, hard-decision throws it away) as opposed to the regime where it cannot help (information already destroyed — see Section 4, the skeptic's case).

**P_deflated = 0.50 (capped, novel-synthesis rule)** for "an adaptive (ESS-style) deferred-collapse trigger is a real, adoptable general lever for this substrate's multi-stage inference pipeline, beyond what fixed-schedule soft-chaining already gets." The external theory (0.85 raw confidence, genuinely textbook-mature) and the substrate's own converging empirical finding both push this high; the cap applies because the SPECIFIC adaptive-ESS-gating mechanism for VSA hop-chaining has zero direct precedent anywhere (external or internal).

### Rank 2 — Sketching / estimation-in-superposition (candidate 3) — REAL, currently a genuine gap

**Mechanism:** Bloom filters, Count-Min Sketch, Count Sketch, and HyperLogLog all hash items into a FIXED-SIZE accumulator and answer queries from aggregate accumulator statistics alone, never decoding individual members. Count-Min Sketch's error bound (`w = ceil(e/epsilon), d = ceil(ln(1/delta))` gives error `<= epsilon * ||a||_1` w.p. `1-delta`, via Markov's inequality + independence-amplification across hash rows — Cormode & Muthukrishnan) and HyperLogLog's order-statistic concentration (`relative error ~ 1.04/sqrt(m)`, Flajolet et al. 2007) are textbook-tight, decades-mature, not heuristics.

**Direct published bridge to VSA (the load-bearing citation for this substrate specifically):** Clarkson, Ubaru & Yang, "Capacity Analysis of Vector Symbolic Architectures" (arXiv:2301.10352, 2023) formally connects VSA bundling (MAP-I, MAP-B, sparse-binary variants) to sketching/dimensionality-reduction theory and Bloom filters, deriving dimension-vs-accuracy bounds for testing set membership AND estimating set-intersection size directly from a bundled vector without decoding. "HyperCam" (arXiv:2501.10547) is a concrete engineering instantiation already trading a Bloom-filter bundling backend against a Count-Sketch backend in a real deployed system. (Caveat: the sub-agent that surfaced this could not extract the exact theorem constants from the primary PDF — the EXISTENCE of the formal bridge is confirmed via abstract + secondary summary, but precise bound numbers are not independently verified this cycle and should not be quoted as exact without a direct re-read.)

**What this concretely gives, if built:** approximate set-membership, cardinality, and set-overlap estimates read directly off an already-bundled vector this substrate already produces for other reasons — e.g. "roughly how many distinct entities has this composite touched" or "how much do these two multi-hop candidate sets overlap" WITHOUT a decode step, useful as a cheap pre-filter before the expensive verifier runs.

**On-disk gap check (confirmed empty this cycle):** `grep -i "reservoir|echo.state|liquid.state"` across `hdlab/` returned zero hits; `grep -i "bloom|count.min|count.sketch|cardinality"` returned only unrelated string literals (a flower-garden test fixture, a docstring using "cardinality" in the ordinary sense). **This substrate has no sketching-in-superposition primitive today** — this is a real, confirmed, currently-unaddressed capability gap, not a speculative one.

**P_deflated = 0.45** — real established math, real published VSA-specific bridge, but zero on-substrate implementation and the exact bound constants for THIS substrate's bundling scheme are unverified.

### Rank 3 — Generative interpolation / conceptual blending (candidate 4) — real ONLY as a proposal generator, not as a trustworthy inference operation

**The optimistic case is weaker than commonly assumed.** Linzen (2016, ACL RepEval, arXiv:1606.07736) shows the classic word2vec "vector-offset" analogy method (`king - man + woman ~= queen`) largely collapses to ordinary nearest-neighbor retrieval once source words are not artificially excluded from candidates — much of the apparent "linear algebra of meaning" is an artifact of evaluation design, not a genuine geometric property (follow-up: arXiv:2010.03446). Curved/poorly-regularized latent-space geometry means straight-line interpolation is not guaranteed to stay on the data manifold even where local smoothness holds (arXiv:1901.08553).

**Where it IS real and well-supported: as a hypothesis-PROPOSAL feeding a hard verifier, never as the final answer.** Neurosymbolic program synthesis and formal theorem-proving both operationalize exactly this split — neural components propose candidates, a symbolic verifier (Lean/HOL kernel, model checker, DreamCoder's wake-sleep library search, Ellis et al. 2021 PLDI) rejects anything that fails formal checking. This maps EXACTLY onto the already-planned convergence architecture's GENERATE-AND-TEST engine: the geometry's compositional interpolation is the proposer, the grounded verifier is the hard gate — this drill's finding is a direct, independent confirmation that this split (not "trust the interpolation") is the historically correct shape.

**Brain grounding:** hippocampal replay literature (Barron et al., "Imagination as a fundamental function of the hippocampus," PMC9620759) documents population-level generation of novel, structurally-plausible (not arbitrary) trajectories through unvisited state-space during rest — a serious, current hypothesis, not settled dogma, and analogical rather than a proof that biological and vector-space interpolation are the same primitive.

**P_deflated = 0.35** (capped near the novel-synthesis ceiling, per the sub-agent's own honest self-rating of 0.40, further deflated here for consistency with this drill's overall calibration) — real as a proposer, genuinely contested as a standalone inference mechanism.

### Rank 4 — Reservoir computing / liquid-state machines (candidate 2) — real field, unclear load-bearing connection to THIS substrate

**External grounding (confirmed via direct WebSearch this cycle, standard textbook material, high confidence on the core facts):** the echo state property, memory-capacity theorems, and the "edge of chaos" / "edge of echo-state-property" criticality condition (peak information-processing capacity occurs near, but not exactly at, the chaotic transition — Boedecker et al., "Information Processing in Echo State Networks at the Edge of Chaos," 2010/2012, and related edge-of-stability ESN work) are all real, well-established, decades-mature results (Jaeger 2001 echo state networks; Maass, Natschläger, Markram 2002 liquid state machines and the separation-property/approximation-property framework). The mechanism — a fixed, untrained, high-dimensional nonlinear dynamical system performs an implicit kernel-like expansion so a trivial linear readout suffices — is genuinely a different lever from "carry a superposed hypothesis set and collapse later": it is about exploiting nonlinear TEMPORAL dynamics for a fading-memory kernel trick, not about deferring a decision over discrete hypotheses.

**Connection to this substrate: genuinely unclear, not dismissed but not confirmed.** No direct, verified citation surfaced this cycle establishing a load-bearing VSA-reservoir hybrid relevant to this substrate's live inference wall (multi-hop KG chaining, resonator decode). This is flagged honestly as an open question rather than either endorsed or refuted — per [[feedback-dont-dismiss-adjacent-methods]], this deserves exactly one more focused drill (a scope-expansion candidate, not a Tier-1 next step) rather than being treated as settled either way.

**P_deflated = 0.20** — real external field, insufficient evidence this cycle of a load-bearing connection to the substrate's actual bottlenecks; deflated for absence-of-connection, not for weakness of the reservoir literature itself.

### Rank 5 — Quantum-inspired interference / amplitude algorithms, classical (candidate 5) — weakest, honestly ranked

**The literature itself argues against a general advantage.** Tang's dequantization result (STOC 2019, arXiv:1807.04271) showed a "quantum" recommendation algorithm has a classical analog via importance sampling — this is evidence that a claimed superposition-derived exponential advantage does NOT survive classicalization, not evidence that it does. Arrazola, Delgado, Bardhan & Lloyd, "Quantum-inspired algorithms in practice" (Quantum 4:307, 2020, arXiv:1905.10415) found the practical crossover for these methods favors them only under narrow conditions (low rank, low condition number, very large dimension); Chia, Gao & Li (arXiv:2402.15686, 2024) prove lower bounds confirming these are a narrow, bounded technique family. This substrate's own FHRR complex-phasor binding (unit-magnitude phase multiplication for bind/unbind) is a genuine "superposed-hypothesis + linear interference + magnitude-readout-at-the-end" architecture already running on ordinary hardware, and the documented benefit of that specific design choice is NOISE/NORMALIZATION STABILITY (unit magnitude preserved under repeated binding, avoiding blow-up/decay) — a real but narrow numerical-stability argument, not a proven asymptotic compute-complexity advantage.

**P_deflated = 0.15** — real and already partially present (FHRR), but the field's own honest self-assessment (dequantization, lower bounds) argues this is a niche technique, not a general lever. Rank honestly last.

## 2. Cheap decisive test (pre-registered)

**ESS-gated deferred collapse for multi-hop chaining.** Reuses the already-planned soft-chain scaffolding from `research_resonator_hard_fail_revival_disparate_fields_2026-06-24.md` (ARM_SOFT_CHAIN: hop-1 emits a softmax posterior over top-K candidates, hop-2 key is built as a weighted superposition) — this test adds ONE new wiring element: an adaptive collapse trigger instead of a fixed schedule.

**Arms (apples-to-apples, same regime as the parent test: M=1000, K_SET=20, N_DIM=4096, 5 seeds):**
- **ARM_FIXED_SOFT** (existing baseline): always carry the full soft posterior through every hop, collapse only at the final readout (the parent test's ARM_SOFT_CHAIN).
- **ARM_ESS_GATED** (new): at each hop, compute an ESS-analog over the candidate posterior — `ESS = 1 / sum(q_i^2)`. While `ESS > threshold` (e.g. 2), pass the full superposed posterior to the next hop; once `ESS <= threshold` (posterior has concentrated — little left to gain from carrying it), collapse to argmax before the next hop (cheaper, and avoids injecting a now-near-certain candidate's residual noise into downstream crosstalk).
- **ARM_SCRAMBLED_ESS (must-fail control):** identical mechanics to ARM_ESS_GATED, but the collapse-trigger signal is computed from a RANDOMLY SHUFFLED copy of the posterior (destroys the correspondence between the trigger and the actual candidate distribution, while preserving the same collapse/no-collapse duty cycle statistics). If ARM_ESS_GATED's gain is really coming from the ADAPTIVE, information-sensitive nature of the trigger (not merely from occasionally collapsing early for free-diversity/regularization reasons), ARM_SCRAMBLED_ESS must NOT match it.

**HARD-PASS:** `mean(ARM_ESS_GATED) >= mean(ARM_FIXED_SOFT) + 0.03` AND `mean(ARM_ESS_GATED) > mean(ARM_SCRAMBLED_ESS) + 0.05` at p<0.05 paired-seed. This licenses adopting ESS-style adaptive collapse-gating as a general design primitive for the convergence architecture's propose/verify boundary, not just this one cell.

**HARD-FAIL:** `ARM_ESS_GATED` is statistically indistinguishable from EITHER `ARM_FIXED_SOFT` (adaptivity buys nothing beyond always-defer) OR `ARM_SCRAMBLED_ESS` (the trigger's information-content is not doing real work, only the collapse duty-cycle matters). Either failure mode is informative: the first says "defer-forever is already optimal here, don't bother with a criterion"; the second says "the adaptive-trigger idea itself doesn't transfer to this substrate's regime."

**Secondary must-fail control (engaging the skeptic's case directly):** re-run the SAME three arms at a bundling load high enough to be inside this substrate's own confirmed crosstalk/SNR-floor regime (e.g. K_SET raised until `ARM_BASELINE_HARD` per-hop accuracy drops below ~0.6, per the correlated-codebook / order-statistic-crowding findings in `reference_self_margin_taxonomy_splits_by_decode_regime` and `reference_correlation_hurts_associative_store_capacity_decouple_from_retrieval`). **Prediction: at this high-crosstalk setting, ALL THREE arms should converge toward the same (low) ceiling** — because past the SNR floor, the information needed for a correct decision was already destroyed at compose time, and no amount of deferred collapse (adaptive or not) can recover it. If ARM_ESS_GATED instead keeps winning even deep in the SNR-floor regime, that would be a surprising, worth-investigating anomaly (possibly meaning the "SNR floor" is itself partly a hard-decision artifact) rather than a clean confirmation — flagged here as the falsifier for the SKEPTIC's position, not just for the optimist's.

## 4. THE SKEPTIC'S CASE — engaged directly, not waved off

This substrate has its own hard-won, VET-confirmed reasons to distrust "more superposition is better":

- **`reference_correlation_hurts_associative_store_capacity_decouple_from_retrieval_2026-07-08`**: semantically-correlated codes injected into the associative store COLLIDE — SEM_BEAM (BGE-seeded, correlated) HARD-FAILED at Hits@10=0.227 vs. RANDOM_BEAM (near-orthogonal) at 0.502, VET-confirmed HONEST-NEGATIVE. Any "carry more distribution" scheme that increases effective correlation among the things being superposed will pay this same tax.
- **`reference_self_margin_taxonomy_splits_by_decode_regime_2026-07-06`**: order-statistic decode margin `p_correct = E_z[Phi(mu+z)^(m-1)]` degrades as competitor count `m` (or effective rank `PR(V)` under correlation) grows — crowding a superposition with more live hypotheses mechanically shrinks the margin available at eventual readout. The resonator's own K-way factorization HARD-FAILED via a 4th, distinct mechanism (convergence-basin proliferation) that this taxonomy does not even cover — a genuinely open failure mode for "more superposition, more hypotheses held live longer."
- **`research_resonator_decode_capacity_ceiling_crux_v2_2026-07-10`**'s own pre-registered HARD-FAIL band operationalizes exactly the skeptic's case: if residual-gated recall stays below 0.25 even after restarts+dither, that is diagnosed as an SNR-FLOOR problem (compose-time noise variance too large relative to N), NOT a search-space-size problem — meaning no amount of decode-side cleverness (deferred collapse included) can fix it; the fix has to move upstream (bigger N, decorrelated/sparse codebooks, less bundling load at compose time).

**The honest reconciliation (this is the calibrated answer, not a dodge):** deferred collapse is a real, provable win when the failure mode is "correct information present in the distribution but destroyed/discarded by a premature hard decision" (order-statistic/search-space regime — this is what the resonator soft-chain and the crux-v2 verify+rank-are-healthy finding both diagnose). It is NOT a fix, and should not be expected to help, when the failure mode is "the information was already destroyed by crosstalk/correlation at compose time" (SNR-floor/capacity regime — this is what correlation-hurts-capacity and the self-margin taxonomy diagnose). **Deferring collapse does not eliminate crosstalk risk — it relocates WHERE that risk gets paid** (later, at a richer decision point, instead of earlier at a cruder one), and that relocation is only a net win when the later point genuinely has more discriminating power available to it. This is precisely why the cheap decisive test above includes the secondary SNR-floor control: the same experiment that could confirm the thesis is designed to also cleanly show its boundary.
