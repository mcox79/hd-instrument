# Research: Frontier (learned, glass-box) ranker to convert reach into beating frequency — FB15k-237 fair stratum

Date: 2026-07-10. Solo synthesis (Opus) over 3 parallel Sonnet lit-scan sub-agents (external lit only; generic math/CS/neuro terms, no substrate-novel terms sent off-platform). Fresh result this drill designs against: `data/exp_gt_rankfair_fb15k237_lowmid_v1_smoke/metrics.json` (HARD_FAIL — reach=0.424, all 7 hand-crafted confidence rankers lose to POP h@10=0.256, best_fair SYM_BASE h@10=0.159).

## HEADLINE (3-line)

1. All 7 hand-crafted rankers lose to POP not because confidence-as-a-concept is doomed, but because a **single global confidence formula is exactly the wrong shape for a stratum defined by low support-count** — rule support/confidence is a statistic, and on the fair (low+mid degree) stratum each candidate has too few instantiations for that statistic to be reliable, so a fixed-gamma additive confidence just adds noise on top of POP's already-present signal rather than adding orthogonal information. The fix precedented across three independent literatures (KG rule-learning, network-science link prediction, and basal-ganglia/DDM decision neuroscience) is the same shape: **replace the fixed global confidence with a small number of LEARNED, per-relation-type, empirical-Bayes-SMOOTHED scalars, plus an explicit residualization against the popularity/frequency baseline** — not a bigger neural net.
2. The concrete frontier mechanism that has published precedent for converting rule-support into a real ranking gain while staying auditable is **DRUM/AnyBURL-class learned-scalar rule confidence** (low-rank, O(few x |relations|) learned parameters) combined with **Adamic-Adar/Resource-Allocation-style inverse-degree weighting** and an explicit **popularity-residualization** step (RecSys'22 "Regularizing Score Differences" is the closest published analogue) — this stays glass-box. NBFNet and A*Net (the true SOTA reference class) get their edge from **shared opaque MLPs** for message/aggregate/priority functions, which is precisely what glass-box excludes; DRUM/AnyBURL-class methods are the ceiling of what a fully-inspectable ranker can currently borrow from this literature.
3. The brain's prescription is structurally identical and gives the cleanest design principle: multi-alternative drift-diffusion models architecturally SEPARATE the base-rate/frequency term (which only shifts the accumulator's **starting point**) from the evidence/value term (which sets the **drift rate**) — a rare candidate can still win if its drift rate (evidence) is strong enough. The existing 7 arms failed because they never made this separation: they added confidence ON TOP OF an implicit frequency-correlated signal instead of first regressing frequency OUT and ranking by the residual.

Deflated P (a glass-box learned ranker beats POP on the FB15k-237 low+mid FAIR stratum): **P ≈ 0.28** (novel-synthesis cap 0.50 applied, then further deflated — see Calibration).

## 1. Why simple confidence loses (brain-grounded diagnosis first, ML second)

Brain evidence (drift-diffusion / basal ganglia) gives the diagnostic frame, not just an analogy: multi-alternative DDMs generalize the binary accumulate-to-bound race to n options, and the literature explicitly separates **prior/base-rate → starting-point bias** from **evidence/value quality → drift rate** ("Bias in the Brain," *J Neurosci* 2012; PMC12290597). Basal ganglia direct/indirect-pathway action selection is a winner-take-all competition over a **learned value** updated by dopaminergic reward-prediction-error, not over raw action frequency — and this is empirically dissociable: dorsomedial (goal-directed/value) striatum remains sensitive to outcome devaluation even after high repetition, while only dorsolateral (habit) striatum is frequency-driven and devaluation-insensitive (Yin & Knowlton, *Nat Rev Neurosci* 2006). Hippocampal replay is prioritized by **TD-error/value, not visitation tally** — the RL analog, Prioritized Experience Replay (Schaul et al., arXiv:1511.05952), samples transitions proportional to |TD-error|, explicitly not frequency.

The common architecture across all three: **a learned/updated value term that is kept structurally separate from the frequency/base-rate term**, so the two can be combined (or the frequency term explicitly subtracted) rather than conflated into one number. The existing 7 arms (SYM_BASE support, additive-confidence add_g05/g10, max_conf, rule_count, noisyOR) are all variants of a single conflated score — support/confidence is itself a frequency-flavored statistic (count of instantiations), so "add confidence to nothing" just re-exposes frequency with extra sampling noise, which is consistent with the observed result: all 7 arms land at 0.106-0.159, i.e., WORSE than POP's 0.256, not just failing to beat it. AMIE's own PCA-confidence literature independently confirms this failure mode: PCA confidence is known to "break down for high-fan-out relations" and to still correlate with relation functionality/degree structure rather than being genuinely decorrelated from it (Galarraga, "Predicting Completeness in Knowledge Bases").

Cross-referencing the density-floor drill run earlier this session (`research_knowledge_density_information_ceiling_relational_inference_2026-07-10.md`): a support/confidence statistic computed from very few instantiations is not a statistic — and the FAIR stratum here is *defined* as low+mid degree, i.e., precisely the regime where per-candidate support counts are thinnest. This is not a coincidence; it is the same information-floor problem recurring one layer downstream (ranking, not generation).

**Ranking signals that are explicitly orthogonal to (or corrected for) raw frequency, with published precedent:**
- **Adamic-Adar / Resource-Allocation Index**: weight each shared structural evidence unit by 1/log(deg) or 1/deg — explicitly punishes hub-driven (frequency-correlated) evidence (Adamic & Adar 2003; Zhou/Lü/Zhang 2009).
- **Popularity residualization**: regress the raw score against a popularity/frequency baseline and rank by the residual — closest published analogue is "Regularizing Score Differences" (RecSys 2022), which directly penalizes correlation between predicted score and item popularity.
- **Cardinality/functionality constraints**: relation-specific max-cardinality priors (e.g., a person has one birth country) as an explicit re-ranking signal, shown to correct over-prediction for functional relations (Muñoz & Minervini, arXiv:1812.06455; Krompaß et al. type-constrained embeddings, arXiv:1508.02593).
- **Multi-path/multi-rule agreement**: score candidates by how many INDEPENDENT rule/path types converge on them, not any single rule's confidence (Path Ranking Algorithm, Lao & Cohen 2010/2011; MCMH, arXiv:2010.01735) — this is a distinct signal from single-rule confidence and is the most direct analogue of "multi-hop consistency" named in the task.
- **Laplace/empirical-Bayes-smoothed confidence**: AnyBURL's analytic (non-gradient) Laplace-smoothed rule confidence is reported competitive with DistMult on standard benchmarks — smoothing toward a prior is the standard fix for the low-support-count noise problem diagnosed above.

None of the sub-agents found a paper that explicitly evaluates these signals on a DEGREE-STRATIFIED (low/mid vs. high) slice the way this program's harness does — degree-stratified fairness evaluation of KG rankers is itself a gap in the published literature, which is itself informative (see Calibration).

## 2. External SOTA rankers — mechanism and glass-box inspectability

| Method | Scoring mechanism | Learned params | Inspectability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NBFNet** (Zhu et al., NeurIPS 2021, arXiv:2106.06935) | Generalized Bellman-Ford: path rep = generalized product of edge reps, pair rep = generalized sum over paths, via iterative message passing | Small per-relation embedding/operator (`b_r`, TransE/DistMult/RotatE-style) **+ shared opaque MLPs** for message/aggregate/score (6x32-unit + 2x64-unit MLP, ~3M params total) | **Mixed-to-opaque** — per-relation part is small, but the shared nonlinear message/aggregate functions are not per-relation-auditable |
| **A\*Net** (Zhu et al., NeurIPS 2023, arXiv:2206.04798) | NBFNet reasoning + learned priority function pruning to ~10% of nodes/edges per iteration; priority = `σ(f(h(u,x) ⊗ g([h(u,x),q])))`, feed-forward nets shared with the reasoning predictor | Full neural value/heuristic network (weight-shared, no separate priority labels) | **Opaque** — a genuine learned value network, structurally analogous to a basal-ganglia value signal but not inspectable per-relation |
| **DRUM** (Sadeghian et al., NeurIPS 2019, arXiv:1911.00055) | Rule confidence per length-T rule = low-rank tensor decomposition `Σ_j Π_i Σ_k a_{j,i,k} A_k`; final score = weighted sum of relation-adjacency-matrix products | **O(L·T·(|R|+1)) explicit scalars** (low-rank, few per rule-length x relation) | **Most inspectable** — small explicit scalar tensor, auditable per relation |
| **RNNLogic-base** (Qu et al., ICLR 2021, arXiv:2010.04029) | Rule generator (RNN) + reasoning predictor; base variant assigns **one scalar weight per rule** via SLP-style softmax over grounding counts | One scalar per rule | **Inspectable** (base variant only — RNNLogic+ reverts to opaque rule-embedding aggregation) |
| **RulE** (Tang et al., ACL Findings 2024, arXiv:2210.14905) | Each rule gets one k-dim complex embedding (RotatE-style margin scoring vs. relation-rule distance); final score = KGE score + β·MLP-over-soft-confidence | Full per-rule embedding vector (k~500+) | **Less inspectable** than DRUM/RNNLogic-base |
| **Neural LP** (Yang/Yang/Cohen, NeurIPS 2017, arXiv:1702.08367) | Rule confidence emerges from an LSTM controller's per-step softmax attention over operators (TensorLog) | Shared-LSTM-driven attention product, not a standalone scalar | **Opaque relative to DRUM** |

**Ranked most- to least-inspectable**: DRUM > RNNLogic-base (per-rule scalar) > RulE ≈ RNNLogic+ > Neural LP (shared-LSTM) > NBFNet (small per-relation embed + opaque shared MLP) > A*Net (fully opaque value net).

No sub-agent found a controlled ablation isolating "single fixed global confidence constant" vs. "learned per-relation/per-rule confidence" on FB15k-237/WN18RR specifically — the closest evidence is A*Net's ablation (learned priority beats hand-set heuristics: MRR 0.411 vs. 0.374 on FB15k-237) and the general rule-vs-embedding literature gap, which is suggestive but not a direct isolation of the fixed-vs-learned-confidence axis. This is an honest gap, not a confirmed result — treat the "learned beats fixed" claim as plausible-by-analogy, not proven-by-ablation.

**Which mechanism converts reach → rank where hand-crafted confidence fails?** The DRUM/AnyBURL/RNNLogic-base family, because they replace a single hand-set gamma with per-relation-type (or per-rule-length) LEARNED weights — directly addressing the diagnosed failure mode (one global formula cannot correctly weight per-relation confidence whose reliability varies with per-relation support density). NBFNet/A*Net convert reach→rank far more powerfully in the general (unstratified) literature, but they do so by abandoning explicit candidate generation altogether (they score over the WHOLE entity vocabulary via learned path aggregation, not a rule-derived candidate list) AND by using opaque shared nonlinearities — both properties place them outside the glass-box constraint.

## 3. Brain prescription for a glass-box learned ranker

Restated as a build spec (not just analogy):
- Maintain a per-candidate **learned value** (not a raw frequency/support count) — analogous to a striatal value updated by a reward-prediction-error-like signal. Concretely: a per-(relation-type x rule-length) confidence weight, updated via a held-out-accuracy signal (empirical training against a validation split), not hand-set.
- **Smooth toward a prior when support is thin** — the basal-ganglia/DDM literature's "confidence" channel is explicitly a separate, dissociable signal from raw exposure (PFC confidence dissociates from familiarity per intracerebral OFC recordings, *J Neurosci* 2025); computationally this is empirical-Bayes/Laplace shrinkage of the per-relation confidence estimate.
- **Architecturally separate the base-rate term from the evidence term** — DDM's starting-point vs. drift-rate split is the cleanest instruction: compute POP's contribution and the rule-evidence contribution as SEPARATE terms, then either (a) rank by evidence-term alone (residual), or (b) combine them with the base-rate term entering only as an override-able additive offset, never multiplicatively conflated with the evidence term (which is what "additive confidence on top of an implicitly frequency-correlated support count" silently does today).
- **Prioritize by surprise/TD-error, not tally**, when deciding which candidates or rule instantiations to trust most — directly maps to weighting multi-path agreement (independent corroborating rule types) more than raw single-rule instantiation count.

## 4. The glass-box bar — honest gap

**No** — a purely glass-box (per-relation-scalar) ranker is very unlikely to reach NBFNet-class ranking quality. NBFNet's/A*Net's advantage is substantially carried by shared opaque MLP message/aggregate/priority functions, which is exactly the class of mechanism glass-box excludes by construction. DRUM/AnyBURL-class methods are the honest ceiling of what a fully-inspectable ranker can borrow from this literature, and in the general (unstratified) literature rule-based methods typically underperform NBFNet-class neural path-scoring on FB15k-237 specifically (FB15k-237 has historically been considered less strictly rule-compositional than WN18RR, per general KG-completion literature knowledge — consistent with today's earlier density-floor drill noting FB15k-237's higher average degree but not necessarily higher rule-regularity). The honest framing for the product: **the bar to clear here is not "match NBFNet," it is the much lower, more tractable bar "beat POP on this specific fair stratum"** — and DRUM/AnyBURL-class glass-box methods have a real, if unproven-on-this-substrate, shot at that lower bar even though they will not close the full gap to NBFNet.

## Cheap decisive test (new arm on the existing harness, zero new corpus)

Add one new arm, **RESID_CONF**, to `exp_gt_rankfair_fb15k237_lowmid_v1`:
1. Compute a **Laplace/empirical-Bayes-smoothed per-(relation-type, rule-length) confidence weight** in place of the fixed global gamma — `(hits + alpha) / (total + alpha + beta)`, alpha/beta fit or fixed via held-out tuning (AnyBURL-style; small number of hyperparameters, or backed off to a shared global prior when a relation-type has too few instances to fit its own alpha/beta — this shrinkage IS the mechanism, not a bug).
2. Compute a **popularity-residual term**: subtract the expected POP-only score for entities in the same degree bucket, so the ranker's final score is `smoothed_confidence + residual_vs_pop` rather than `confidence` alone (this is the DDM-style base-rate/drift-rate separation from Section 3).
3. Optionally add a **multi-rule-agreement count** (number of independent rule TYPES, not instantiations, supporting the same candidate) as a third additive term — cheap to compute from data already logged (`arm_hashes`/rule dense count in existing `metrics.json` meta block).

**Must-fail controls (non-negotiable, both must fail to count RESID_CONF as real):**
- **Shuffled-relation-label control**: compute the same smoothed confidence but with relation-type labels shuffled before fitting alpha/beta per type — this collapses the per-relation-specific smoothing to a single global prior; it MUST still lose to POP (proves the RELATION-SPECIFICITY of the smoothing is the active ingredient, not smoothing per se — same logic as the existing scramble control).
- **Frequency-leaking control**: set the "learned" weight literally proportional to target-entity frequency — this must NOT count as a pass; if it spuriously beats POP, the harness has a leak and the result must be discarded (this is the task's explicitly named must-fail).

## Falsifiable predictions

**HARD-PASS**: RESID_CONF achieves FAIR low+mid h@10 > 0.256 (beats current POP) AND mrr > 0.131, while (a) the shuffled-relation-label control stays at or below current best_fair (~0.159) and (b) the frequency-leaking control does not exceed POP by construction-consistent margin (i.e., it tracks POP, it doesn't "beat" it via a real orthogonal mechanism). This would be the first demonstrated case this session of a ranking mechanism converting reach into a frequency-beating result.

**HARD-FAIL (ranking still can't beat frequency at this density)**: RESID_CONF fails to clear POP (h@10 <= ~0.16, no material improvement over current SYM_BASE) even with smoothing and residualization — this would indicate the FAIR low+mid stratum's per-candidate evidence is too sparse for ANY confidence-shaped signal (learned or not) to out-perform the base rate, converging with the DDM-shrinkage risk below, and would redirect the program toward corpus density (today's Candidate-A density-floor thread) rather than further ranker iteration.

**HARD-FAIL (leak/confound)**: the frequency-leaking control beats POP, or the shuffled-relation-label control performs statistically indistinguishably from the real RESID_CONF arm — either invalidates the result and the harness needs a leak audit before any claim is made.

## Calibration / honest deflated-P verdict

Three specific risks temper the estimate below the generic novel-synthesis cap:

1. **Ceiling headroom is thin, not generous.** reach=0.424 vs. POP=0.256 leaves only ~0.17 absolute headroom for ANY ranker restricted to the rule-derived candidate set to beat POP by; getting there requires ranking close to the ceiling within that set, a large ask relative to the 0.159 currently achieved.
2. **Empirical-Bayes shrinkage risk — the fix may legitimately converge back to POP.** The FAIR stratum is precisely where support counts are thinnest; the standard Bayesian behavior of a well-calibrated smoothed estimator IS to shrink toward the population base rate as evidence thins — meaning a genuinely optimal learned ranker, in the sparsest sub-regime of the FAIR stratum, may correctly converge close to POP rather than beat it. This is a real, structural risk, not generic hedging.
3. **Corpus/compositionality risk (the task's named worry, independently corroborated)**: FB15k-237 is Freebase-derived and has historically been treated as less strictly rule-compositional than WN18RR in the general KG-completion literature; if the FAIR low+mid stratum's true target relations are dominated by non-compositional idiosyncratic facts (e.g., biographical relations skewing to a few globally common values regardless of local graph structure), no ranker — however good — converts reach into a beat-frequency win, and the corpus itself (not the ranker) is the binding constraint. This is consistent with, and does not contradict, today's earlier density-floor finding that FB15k-237 (avg degree ~37) is the field's "easy" benchmark in aggregate — the FAIR low+mid SUBSET specifically may not inherit that ease.

No sub-agent found a published degree-stratified ("fair" low/mid vs. high) evaluation of ANY of these rankers — this specific evaluation protocol (matching this program's own fairness discipline) appears to be a genuine gap in the public literature, which cuts both ways: it means there is no direct precedent to lean on (deflate), but also means a positive result here would be a genuinely novel, defensible claim (not a replication).

**P ≈ 0.28** (novel-synthesis cap 0.50 applied; further deflated ~0.20 for: no direct degree-stratified precedent + the specific empirical-Bayes-shrinkage-reverts-to-POP risk + the named corpus-compositionality risk). Biggest single risk: risk #3 (corpus is the real lever, not the ranker) — if RESID_CONF HARD-FAILs cleanly (not via leak/confound), the most likely correct read is "move to a more compositional corpus or accept FB15k-237 fair-stratum ranking as bounded by data, not method," not "iterate the ranker again."
