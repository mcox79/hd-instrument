## (a) Brain-first: how does the brain integrate multiple weak role-cues at once?

**Verified, well-established.** The lexicalist constraint-satisfaction / competition-integration tradition is exactly this mechanism. MacDonald, Pearlmutter & Seidenberg (1994, *Psychological Review* 101:676) treat syntactic/role-ambiguity resolution as multiple weakly-constraining probabilistic cues (frequency, thematic fit, prior context) activating competing interpretations IN PARALLEL, with activation proportional to constraint strength — no privileged serial order. McRae, Spivey-Knowlton & Tanenhaus (1998, *JML* 38:283) built an explicit competition/constraint-integration model showing independently-quantified syntactic and thematic-fit constraints combine continuously and simultaneously. Trueswell, Tanenhaus & Garnsey (1994, *JML* 33:285) show thematic-fit constrains parsing from the earliest measurable stage. Spivey/Tanenhaus visual-world eyetracking (2001, *Psychological Science*) shows graded, continuously-updating competition in fixation trajectories — consistent with simultaneous, not staged, cue use.

**Cue weighting is reliability-weighted and learned, cross-linguistically.** MacWhinney & Bates' Competition Model (reviewed MacWhinney 2005, *Int. J. Bilingualism*) formalizes **cue validity = availability × reliability**, with weights learned from experience and differing systematically by language (word order dominant in English; case marking dominant in German and other case-rich languages). Bornkessel-Schlesewsky & Schlesewsky's Argument Dependency Model independently converges on a weighted cue-hierarchy (case > word order/animacy in many languages) with documented override dynamics when cues conflict. **This directly predicts, for English specifically, that word-order/position should dominate the learned weight vector** — which matters for the risk assessment below.

**Not verified:** a canonical paper framing this as *statistically optimal* Bayesian cue combination in the Ernst & Banks (2002) multisensory sense — the tradition is evidence-accumulation/probabilistic in spirit (constraint-satisfaction networks) but not typically cast as an explicit Bayesian posterior. Levy's (2008, *Cognition* 106:1126) surprisal/noisy-channel account is the closest rigorous Bayesian-flavored formalism, but targets structural expectation more than multi-cue role-conflict specifically.

**Calibrated abstain on cue conflict — do NOT pre-assume a clean "hedge" story; the honest answer is mixed and includes real failure.** Kim & Osterhout's (2005) "semantic P600" / semantic-attraction effect ("the hearty meal was devouring the kids") shows that when plausibility conflicts with structure, the parser is sometimes **pulled toward the wrong interpretation** (a genuine misresolution, evidenced by a P600 rather than the N400 a pure-plausibility account predicts) — not a graceful hedge. Ferreira, Bailey & Ferraro (2002, *Current Directions* 11:11) "good-enough representations" show comprehenders frequently retain incomplete/inaccurate role assignments for garden-path and passive sentences even after ostensibly resolving them. **So: cue-conflict does NOT reliably produce calibrated hedging in humans — it sometimes produces confident, uncorrected misassignment.** This is directly relevant to Step 2's design: the substrate's calibrated-abstain layer (conformal/Chow reject option) is a genuine IMPROVEMENT over the raw brain mechanism on cue-conflict cases, not a straight brain-replication — flag this honestly rather than claiming brain-fidelity for the abstain piece. (The USER's own framing — that genuine-ambiguity abstain is "our metacog CG, not a failure" — is the substrate doing BETTER than the uncorrected P600/good-enough baseline, which is a legitimate, flaggable substrate-product claim, not an overclaim, as long as it's stated as an improvement rather than a replication.)

## (b) Glass-box weighted-vote prior art — confirmed, and it IS the brain's mechanism made auditable

**Log-linear/MaxEnt SRL is mathematically the same operation as the brain's constraint integration, made inspectable.** Gildea & Jurafsky (2002, *Computational Linguistics* 28:245) established the classic SRL feature set (path, phrase type, position, voice, predicate, head word, subcategorization); Xue & Palmer (2004, EMNLP) used an explicit MaxEnt (multinomial logistic regression) classifier over this feature family. A log-linear/MaxEnt model IS a weighted linear combination in log-odds space: each named feature gets a learned weight, softmax/logistic normalization performs the same evidence-integration role as the brain's constraint-satisfaction competition — **this is not an analogy of convenience, it is the standard NLP framing, confirmed.** The substrate's existing `train_w7`-style delta-rule logistic (already used in `exp_learned_argstruct_parser_lccp_independent_gold_v1` and `exp_graded_thematic_fit_integrated_reader_gate_v1`) is already this architecture — Step 2 does not need a new mechanism, only a larger, jointly-trained feature vector.

**Thematic-fit / selectional-preference models produce graded, continuous scores usable as one weighted feature — confirmed.** McRae, Ferretti & Amyote (1997, *Language and Cognitive Processes* 12:137) give continuous human typicality ratings per verb/role filler; Erk (2007, ACL, aclanthology.org/P07-1028) gives a distributional similarity-based selectional-preference score. Both are graded, not hard filters, matching the `gfit` cue already built.

**Calibrated abstain — confirmed, standard, non-neural.** Chow (1970, IEEE Trans. Info. Theory) gives the classic error-reject threshold rule; Vovk, Gammerman & Shafer (2005) and Angelopoulos & Bates (2021, arXiv:2107.07511) give distribution-free split-conformal calibration — exactly what `hdlab/conformal.py` already implements and what `exp_metacog_abstain_conformal_transfer_v1` already validated transfers to a disjoint split. No new tool needed.

## (d) Design — the integrated-vote decision cell

**Name (proposed, not yet authored):** `exp_integrated_vote_role_decision_oracle_gold_v1`

**Candidate substrate (parser-independent):** ORACLE gold-extracted (verb, candidate-argument) pairs from `data/gold_mcguffey_lccp_argstruct_v1.json` (the same 29375-line-family independent gold already used by LCCP/graded-thematic-fit/explicit-grammar) — i.e. run the decision layer directly on gold verb-argument PAIRS, not on reader-extracted candidates, so the cell isolates the DECISION problem from extraction noise. This makes it directly comparable to, and reusable regardless of, whichever extraction/parser cell lands from the parallel parser-wiring track.

**Feature vector (single, jointly-trained, per candidate):**
1. `f_adj`, `f_postv`, `f_prep`, `f_func`, `f_clause` — the 5 existing LCCP structural cues (word-order family), reused as-is.
2. `f_passive_flip` — CONTINUOUS-ized version of the explicit-grammar cell's passive detector: not a hard override, but a feature = 1.0 if `detect_passive(...) and governing_prep == "by"` else 0.0 (the flip itself still swaps the candidate's role label at the CANDIDATE-CONSTRUCTION stage, same as today — the vote decides CONFIDENCE in that candidate, not whether the flip happens).
3. `f_prep_sense_*` — one-hot or ordinal encoding of `PREP_SENSE` classes (agent_or_location / instrument_or_comitative / goal / source / partitive / location / topic / unmapped), replacing the crude reader's single binary `f_prep` flag with the graded sense distinction the explicit-grammar cell already computes.
4. `f_alternation` — feature = 1.0 if the candidate resolves via the locative-alternation frame table (`ALTERNATING_VERBS` + with/onto/into governing prep) with the holistic/theme frame directionality already computed by the explicit-grammar cell.
5. `f_gfit` — the existing graded thematic-fit/type-fact cue, unchanged (VerbNet-supersense-class-smoothed typicality).
6. `f_plaus` — the existing GloVe-based selectional-coherence score already used as the LCCP self-supervised teacher, promoted from "teacher signal" to an explicit VOTE FEATURE (this is new: today it's used only to generate training targets, never itself weighted into the decision vector — worth testing since the McRae/Erk literature above treats it as exactly this kind of graded feature).

**Learned weights:** same delta-rule / logistic pattern as `train_w7`, one joint 9-11-dim vector (exact dim = 5 structural + up to 4 prep-sense one-hot or 1 ordinal + alternation + gfit + plaus), trained against the SAME self-supervised semantic teacher + DEFER-band resolution pattern already validated in `exp_graded_thematic_fit_integrated_reader_gate_v1` (reuse, do not reinvent).

**Calibrated abstain:** `hdlab.conformal.calibrate_quantile()` on the vote's margin/nonconformity score (`1 - sigmoid(w·x)` for the kept candidate, or the gap between top-2 candidates for genuinely reversible pairs), split-conformal calibrated on a CAL split, applied to a disjoint TEST split (reuse the exact transfer-verification pattern from `exp_metacog_abstain_conformal_transfer_v1` — do not re-derive; report abstain rate and confident-wrong reduction using the SAME machinery already landed for atom 29367).

### Design gate (pre-registered; verify at smoke before full)

1. **REAL baseline, two-part (not one):**
   - (i) **Best-single-cue-alone**: report EACH cue's standalone precision on the oracle pairs (each cue thresholded independently, no learning) — this recovers the "one-at-a-time ~0.557 wall" honestly, expected to be dominated by `f_postv`/`f_adj` (word-order) per the brain-check finding that word-order has the highest cue-validity in English.
   - (ii) **Existing crude multi-cue reader**, re-derived LIVE via `L.run_arms` on the SAME oracle pairs (not a strawman, not a remembered number — the LCCP arm-B/C precision must be reproduced fresh on this exact eval protocol, since the oracle-pairs setup differs from the reader's own extraction-then-decide protocol and the number will not be identical to any previously-reported figure).
2. **CAN-FAIL (real, not vacuous):** integration might NOT beat best-single-cue or even the existing crude reader — cues may be REDUNDANT (word-order already predicts voice/prep-sense on this corpus) rather than complementary. This is a live, principled risk (see below), not a formality.
3. **DIFFICULTY-ON, reported as separate slices:**
   - CUE-CONFLICT subset: items where at least 2 cues disagree on which candidate is patient (e.g. word-order says candidate X, prep-sense says candidate Y) — construct by cross-tabulating each cue's independent vote per instance.
   - GENUINE-AMBIGUITY subset: items where the calibrated-abstain layer would fire (near-tied vote margin) — report abstain precision (precision among NON-abstained items) vs forced-choice precision on this subset specifically.
   - GRAMMAR-RESOLVABLE subset (reuse the explicit-grammar cell's own `coverage_audit`): by-agent-passive / preposition-governed-gold-patient / alternation-double-frame items.
4. **ONE VARIABLE:** best-single-cue-vote vs full-integrated-vote, same oracle pairs, same weight-learning protocol, same seeds. (A second, secondary one-variable comparison: full-vote WITH calibrated abstain vs WITHOUT, at matched forced-choice coverage — reuses the 29367/transfer-cell pattern exactly.)

### Verdict bands (pre-registered)

- **HARD-PASS (integration beats best-single-cue, the central Step-2 hypothesis):** mean precision of the full integrated vote exceeds the best standalone single cue by >= 0.05 absolute AND exceeds the existing crude multi-cue reader (freshly reproduced) by >= 0.02 absolute, on the SAME oracle pairs, with min-over-seeds > 0 (consistent sign). This is the "complementary not redundant" result.
- **HARD-FAIL (cues are redundant, the 29375-family wall reasserts even on clean oracle pairs):** mean integrated-vote precision <= best-single-cue precision, OR the CUE-CONFLICT subset shows the integrated vote performing NO BETTER than a coin-flip among the conflicting candidates (i.e. the vote doesn't actually resolve conflict, it just inherits whichever cue has the largest learned weight). This is the single most likely negative outcome given the brain-check finding that English word-order cue-validity is so dominant that other cues may carry near-zero MARGINAL information once word-order is already in the vote — flag this explicitly as the pre-registered most-likely-outcome, not a surprise if it lands.
- **MIDDLE-BAND:** integration beats best-single-cue by a positive-but-sub-threshold margin (0 < gain < 0.05), or gain concentrates entirely in the grammar-resolvable subset (which the sibling explicit-grammar cell's coverage audit may show is a small minority of this corpus) with no lift on the residual — i.e. Step 2 "works" but only reproduces what the explicit-grammar cell (D_grammar) already showed as a separate arm, adding nothing beyond what running D_grammar as a hard prefilter would give.
- **Calibrated-abstain sub-verdict (secondary, reuses 29367 bands):** HARD-PASS if abstain raises forced-choice precision by >= 0.02 at a realized abstain rate <= 0.35 on the cue-conflict subset specifically (where it should matter most), transfer-verified CAL->TEST per the existing conformal-transfer cell's protocol.

### Learning-curve measurement (the flexible/improving property, per task instruction)

Vary the number of oracle-pair training examples used to fit the joint weight vector (e.g. 10%/25%/50%/75%/100% of the mining/training pool, same held-out oracle eval throughout) and plot integrated-vote precision vs n. Two diagnostic outcomes: (i) still rising at 100% -> more oracle-labeled examples would help, corpus-size-gated not architecture-gated; (ii) flat/saturated well before 100% -> the wall is the CUE SET itself (redundancy), not data volume — directly discriminates between "need more data" and "need more/different cues," which the one-shot 29375 finding could not distinguish on its own.

## Cross-thread synthesis

This closes the loop the grammar-construction-resources note (Section 5) explicitly left open: "the 0.557 bound was measured WITHOUT first extracting and exploiting this structural layer... this is the single most actionable implication and should be checked against the actual reader pipeline." Step 2 IS that check, generalized to all cue families at once rather than just the structural/grammar one. It also directly operationalizes the brain-check finding (MacWhinney & Bates cue-validity: word-order dominant in English) as a FALSIFIABLE, pre-registered prediction rather than an assumption — if word-order alone already captures most of what the other cues would add, that is not a substrate failure, it is the substrate correctly reproducing a real property of the English cue-hierarchy, and the honest verdict is "additional cues are backup/tie-breakers for a genuine minority of residual cases," which is still worth having (the calibrated-abstain layer's whole value proposition lives exactly in that residual).

## Substrate-product implications

- If HARD-PASS: a single, jointly-trained, glass-box weighted-vote layer (auditable per-cue weights, no runtime LLM) that measurably beats any single heuristic — a legitimate "the substrate integrates evidence like a human reader does, and can point to which cue decided" product claim, with the calibrated-abstain layer as a further product differentiator (transparent "I'm not sure, but I lean X" output, unlike black-box models that force a forced choice).
- If HARD-FAIL/MIDDLE-BAND (the more likely outcome per the brain-check): still valuable — it converts an assumption ("more cues must help") into a measured, falsified prediction, and LOCALIZES where the true residual lives (the cue-conflict subset), which is exactly the population the calibrated-abstain layer should be scoped to rather than applied globally. Either way this is a clean, buildable, LOW-COST cell (all sub-components already exist; only the joint feature vector + joint training loop + oracle-pairs harness are new code) — worth building regardless of which verdict lands.
- Either outcome leaves the semantic-axis closures (29375's per-instance text-derivation null) untouched; this cell operates on grammar+type+plausibility features jointly, not a new signal source.

## Biggest risk (deflated, stated plainly)

**Most likely outcome: cues are REDUNDANT, not complementary, and integration buys little beyond what word-order alone already captures — the 29375-family wall reasserts even on clean oracle pairs.** Three independent, same-day pieces of evidence point this way: (1) the brain-check literature (MacWhinney & Bates) confirms English has an unusually dominant word-order cue-validity, meaning other cues carry limited MARGINAL information once position is in the vote; (2) the grammar-construction-resources note flags that VerbNet's alternation/preposition-sense resources apply only to a narrow, closed class of verbs/prepositions — the grammar-resolvable subset may be a small minority of any given corpus (the explicit-grammar cell's own coverage_audit, in flight, will give the exact number); (3) the affectedness-decomposition note independently found the per-instance residual (where type-facts/grammar would need to do real work) stays genuinely hard even with a correct type-level lookup. If this risk lands, the correct interpretation is NOT "integration doesn't work" — it is "word-order was already doing more of the job than the one-at-a-time framing implied," which is itself useful, falsifiable, brain-consistent information, not a wasted cell.
