## HEADLINE

Cognitive-science and NLP-benchmark-design literatures converge, independently, on the SAME three-part pattern for a memorization-proof comprehension test: (1) generalize to a NOVEL instance/relation never seen at learning time (tests intension, not an exemplar lookup table), (2) build an ablated "identity/hypothesis-only" baseline that MUST score near chance for the test to be valid, and (3) use a tunable, hard-distractor forced-choice/ranked pool (not a pairwise AUC) for dynamic range. This is a well-trodden, citable design pattern in both fields, not something requiring novel synthesis.

## PART 1 (BROAD): neuro/psycholinguistic assessment of "knowing" a concept

1. **Property/category verification, typicality, feature listing** — Rogers & McClelland, *Semantic Cognition: A Parallel Distributed Processing Approach* (MIT Press, 2004). VERIFIED-LIVE. Diagnostic because typicality gradients and coherent covariation of property errors only emerge if the system represents shared feature structure, not an arbitrary label pointer. Follow-up RT verification-task literature exists (VERIFIED-LIVE existence; exact authorship RECALLED-UNVERIFIED).
2. **Category-based induction to novel instances** — Osherson, Smith, Wilkie, Lopez, Shafir, "Category-based induction," *Psychological Review* 97:185-200 (1990). VERIFIED-LIVE. Similarity-Coverage Model: generalizing a property to a novel conclusion category (never paired with that property at learning time) is diagnostic of learned INTENSION vs. an extensional exemplar list — this is the closest human-literature analog to "memorization-proof."
3. **Relational/structural vs. surface similarity** — Gentner & Markman, "Structure Mapping in Analogy and Similarity"; Markman & Gentner (1990) cross-mapping studies; Gick & Holyoak, "Analogical Problem Solving," *Cognitive Psychology* (1980, Duncker radiation-problem paradigm). VERIFIED-LIVE. Cross-mapped stimuli pit surface-featural matches against relational/structural matches; choosing the structural match under cross-mapping is the signature of true relational comprehension, not pattern-matching.
4. **What identity/familiarity alone can solve** — no single named "shortcut-proof" paradigm surfaced; closest verified analogs are items 2-3 plus the Remember/Know familiarity-vs-recollection distinction (Tulving 1985, RECALLED-UNVERIFIED exact cite).

## PART 2 (NARROW): shortcut-learning traps and memorization-resistant NLP eval design

1. **Entity/name-only shortcuts in relation extraction** — Peng, Gao, Han, Lin, Sun et al., "Learning from Context or Names? An Empirical Study on Neural Relation Extraction," EMNLP 2020 (arXiv:2010.01923). VERIFIED-LIVE (method/finding); popular RE benchmarks leak shortcuts from entity mention/type to gold relation, letting models skip context-reading; exact leak-solvable percentage RECALLED-UNVERIFIED (not retrievable from abstract).
2. **LAMA / knowledge-probing critiques** — Petroni et al., "Language Models as Knowledge Bases?" (EMNLP-IJCNLP 2019, base paradigm). VERIFIED-LIVE. Critiques: Poerner, Waltinger & Schütze, "E-BERT" (introduces LAMA-UHN, filters triples answerable as a substring of the subject name); Cao, Lin, Han, Sun et al., "Knowledgeable or Educated Guess? Revisiting Language Models as Knowledge Bases" (ACL 2021, answer-frequency/distribution-leakage artifacts). Both VERIFIED-LIVE.
3. **Hypothesis-only baselines (NLI)** — Poliak, Naradowsky, Haldar, Rudinger, Van Durme, "Hypothesis Only Baselines in Natural Language Inference" (*SEM 2018): **69.0% SNLI hypothesis-only vs. 34.28% majority-class**. Gururangan, Swayamdipta, Levy, Schwartz, Bowman, Smith, "Annotation Artifacts in Natural Language Inference Data" (NAACL 2018): hypothesis-only gets **~67% SNLI / ~53% MultiNLI**. Both VERIFIED-LIVE with numbers. Method: strip content that SHOULD be logically necessary; if accuracy stays well above chance, the benchmark is contaminated — this is the field's standard pass/fail gate.
4. **Contrast sets / counterfactually-augmented data** — Gardner et al., "Evaluating Models' Local Decision Boundaries via Contrast Sets" (Findings of EMNLP 2020, arXiv:2004.02709): models score **up to 25pp lower** on hand-perturbed, label-flipping contrast instances than original test items. Kaushik, Hovy, Lipton, "Learning the Difference that Makes a Difference with Counterfactually-Augmented Data" (ICLR 2020, arXiv:1909.12434): minimal human edits flip gold label; headline drop numbers RECALLED-UNVERIFIED (secondary-source only). Both VERIFIED-LIVE for method.
5. **Tunable-distractor forced-choice / ranked-retrieval design** — KB-completion Hits@k/MRR full-pool ranking (Bordes et al. 2013 TransE; Sun et al. 2019 RotatE; arXiv:1810.07180 eval-methodology survey), VERIFIED-LIVE for metric design, RECALLED-UNVERIFIED for specific SOTA numbers. Adversarial-filtering hard-distractor curricula — Zellers et al., "HellaSwag" (ACL 2019): AF-selected distractors drop contemporaneous SOTA to **<48%** while humans stay **>95%**, i.e. hard distractors compress model score toward chance without touching human ceiling. VERIFIED-LIVE. mAFC signal-detection-theory literature supports k-way forced-choice giving more efficient/higher-power discrimination than pairwise same/different. VERIFIED-LIVE (general finding, not one quoted "more distractors = more range" statement). WinoGrande/HellaSwag: chance ~50%, human ceiling ~94-96%, contemporaneous models well below — VERIFIED-LIVE concrete chance/ceiling exemplar.

## Cheap decisive test (as reported by the literature, not designed here)

The field's own standard FIRST cheap check, directly transferable: before trusting any new comprehension probe, run the ablated "identity-only" baseline (Poliak/Gururangan method) — feed the probe only the concept/entity identity, withhold the relational/property content that should be necessary — and score it. This costs nothing beyond the eval harness already needed for the real probe.

## Falsifiable predictions (as established by the cited field thresholds — HARD-PASS / HARD-FAIL)

- HARD-FAIL (probe invalid, per Poliak/Gururangan pattern): identity-only baseline scores well above chance (e.g., >2x chance rate for the given distractor-pool size) on any planned probe item type.
- HARD-PASS (probe valid): identity-only baseline is near chance (within noise of 1/pool-size), AND held-out novel-instance/novel-relation generalization (Osherson-style) is reliably above chance, AND relational/cross-mapped items separate structural-match from surface-match choices (Gentner-style).
- Dynamic-range benchmark: per HellaSwag/WinoGrande precedent, a well-constructed hard-distractor forced-choice design should separate chance (~1/k) from a genuinely-comprehending system by a wide margin (50+ points in the cited benchmarks); a design achieving <10pp separation from chance should be treated as under-powered/uninformative regardless of pass/fail on any single item.

## Substrate-product implications

A comprehension-eval battery built on this grounding gives the substrate product a reusable, standards-grounded acceptance gate for any future learned-representation claim (not just this loop): every "the substrate learned X" claim can be run through (a) an identity-only baseline that must fail, (b) novel-instance/relation generalization that must succeed, (c) a cross-mapped structural-vs-surface item, and (d) a tunable hard-distractor forced-choice score with reported chance/ceiling. This converts "comprehension" from an informal judgment call into a repeatable, citable, memorization-resistant test — directly reusable across every future concept-learning or reading-comprehension experiment, not a one-off.
