This is the exact audit this session's recurring lesson demands: "HARD_PASS" without checking the substrate is not evidence.

## Honest bottom line

Of the 15 shortlisted, 10 ran on REAL narrative text (McGuffey / Anne of Green Gables / LitBank / Baum), 3 on SEMI-REAL data (real English sentences or real lexical resources, but hand-curated small item sets rather than corpus-scale), and 2 on pure SYNTHETIC FHRR vectors. This is a materially better-attested set than a single arc's worth of cells -- it is nine to ten INDEPENDENT real-text extraction attempts, built by different cell-authors at different times, targeting different sub-problems (causal attribution, coherence selection, valence/appraisal, unstated-goal/schema recovery, coreference-driven entity tracking, situated goal structure).

They all converge on the same finding, not nine different findings: every one of them shows a large, consistent gap between an ORACLE/gold-given-structure arm (0.93-1.00) and a SELF-EXTRACTED arm on real prose (0.25-0.68, several near or below the chance/floor band). The mechanisms downstream of extraction -- FHRR bind/bundle, the coreference resolver, the situation-model accumulate/multibank register, schema-trigger matching, selectional-fit coherence scoring -- are REAL and validated: they work correctly whenever they are fed correct input (oracle arms near-ceiling every time; the multibank capacity fix and the pun coherence-alarm both HARD_PASS on their own narrow, well-posed terms). What is NOT solved, in any of these 15 cells, is turning free-running real prose into the structured input those mechanisms need. `exp_supplied_schema_ceiling` is the sharpest single data point: even with a hand-authored schema library and REAL gold narrative, the honest held-out ceiling is 0.25, a 2x collapse from the in-sample 0.52. `exp_coherence_selector_text_transfer` and `exp_grounded_valence_read_from_text` are the starkest: an approach that is clearly non-trivial on its own terms (0.84 sim accuracy, a genuine grounded read) reduces to chance or WORSE than a frozen baseline once the input is real text instead of gold/oracle structure. `exp_situation_reader_multisent_demo`, the most complete already-assembled pipeline in the registry, self-reports that its composed accuracy is "modest" and that causation is "REDUCIBLE to connective-else-recency" rather than genuine plausibility inference.

So this island audit does not surface a hidden reuse-what-exists shortcut. It CONFIRMS the reckoning. The extraction wall identified on DesireDB/MCScript2.0/WIQA is not an artifact of those three benchmarks or of one arc's construction choices -- it shows up independently, at similar magnitude, across nine-plus real-text cells spanning coreference, causal attribution, coherence, valence, and schema/goal inference, all built by prior cell-authors who did not know about each other's results. A composition of these organs would not plausibly cross the wall: they fail at the SAME upstream step (parsing/extracting structured meaning from prose) rather than at different downstream steps that could be chained around. The one genuinely new, mildly encouraging data point is #10 (exp_intent_valence_via_mentalizing, a REAL-text win on Baum's actual prose) and the honest partial credit in #5/#7 (narrow fixes that measurably move accuracy without closing the gap) -- these say extraction is hard but not flatly impossible, consistent with "45-year field problem, multi-month build," not with "already solved and sitting unused." Recommendation to the Director/USER: this audit does not change the strategic direction set on 2026-08-09 (build the grounded concept/script/world-knowledge foundation); it substantiates it with nine independent replications of the same wall, and gives concrete sub-targets (schema-trigger overfit at N=25 items is a data-scale problem; the sim-to-text representation gap in #3/#5 names the encoding-bridge sub-problem precisely) rather than suggesting a shortcut around it.
