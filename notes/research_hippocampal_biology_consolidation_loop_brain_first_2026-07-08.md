## PART 1 -- THE BIOLOGY, LED (not the ML analogs)

### 1.1 The two-stage architecture (Buzsaki): WHY there are two phases at all

During waking behavior (theta state), entorhinal cortex drives NOVEL information into hippocampus; hippocampal synapses reorganize transiently (fast, one-shot, via LTP at CA3 recurrent collaterals and CA3->CA1 Schaffer collaterals). During subsequent immobility/slow-wave sleep, hippocampus enters the sharp-wave-ripple (SPW-R) state and TRANSFERS the modified content to neocortex. This is not "the same write happening twice at different speeds" -- it is a genuinely two-phase circuit with different input sources at each phase (hippocampus receives the RAW event at write time; cortex receives a REPLAYED, hippocampally-COMPLETED signal at consolidation time, never the raw event directly). **This is the exact mechanism `research_drill_cls_2substrate_rescue_2x_2026-06-11.md` diagnosed as missing from the substrate's original two-substrate HARD_FAIL** (parallel writes gave cortex the same signal as hippocampus, at the same moment) -- the physiology confirms that diagnosis was structurally correct, and gives the missing detail: cortex should receive a hippocampally-COMPLETED (attractor-cleaned) signal, not the raw item, and only during a separate offline phase.

### 1.2 DG -> CA3 -> CA1: the actual circuit doing pattern separation, storage, and comparison

- **Dentate gyrus (DG):** ~10x expansion + strong sparsification (few-percent active granule cells) of entorhinal input. Orthogonalizes overlapping inputs so similar experiences get dissimilar codes -- pattern SEPARATION. DG projects to proximal CA3 via mossy fibers (sparse but powerful "detonator" synapses).
- **CA3:** dense recurrent collaterals make it a genuine AUTOASSOCIATIVE attractor network. In storage mode it writes input patterns into recurrent synapses; in recall mode, a PARTIAL/noisy cue propagates through the recurrent web and reinstates the complete stored pattern -- pattern COMPLETION. Distal CA3 (away from the DG mossy-fiber input) is where this attractor dynamic is strongest; proximal CA3 (near DG) still does separation. CA3 recurrent dynamics are also the proposed GENERATOR of the SPW-R event itself (self-organizing population burst from CA3 recurrent excitation + sparse inhibition).
- **CA1:** receives BOTH the completed CA3 output (indirect path) AND a direct, un-completed entorhinal input (direct path) -- structurally positioned as a COMPARATOR/mismatch detector between "what CA3 says should be there" and "what is actually arriving." CA1 activity during behavior shows a bias toward pattern completion (agrees with CA3); it is the gateway back out to entorhinal cortex and onward to neocortex.

**Substrate mapping (derived, not analogical filler):** the substrate's native decorrelated store code IS the DG/CA3-separation-side representation (already built, already CG-certified as the capacity-correct choice); the substrate's existing attractor-cleanup / iterative-cleanup primitive (already used in the resonator, generation-cleanup, and composed value-gate cells) IS the CA3 pattern-completion operator -- no new machinery needed here, this is a direct reuse of certified substrate primitives, which matters for the "modulating layer over a solid core" build-order prior. What is MISSING is the CA1-style comparator/gateway step that decides whether a completed pattern is confident enough to be handed onward, and the actual cortical-side (rich/correlated) integration step that receives it.

### 1.3 Theta-gamma cross-frequency coupling: HOW the same circuit multiplexes write vs read (NOT a scalar gate)

The earlier self-manager drill (`research_neuromodulatory_self_manager_controller_2026-07-08.md`) modeled the encode/retrieve switch as a single ACh-driven scalar gate on recurrent-connection strength. The deeper hippocampal physiology gives a MORE precise and MORE mechanistic answer that should supersede that framing for consolidation specifically: hippocampal theta (4-9 Hz) nests TWO DISTINCT gamma sub-bands, each sourced from a different afferent pathway and each associated with a different cognitive operation --
- **theta + FAST/high gamma** (sourced from entorhinal cortex layer III, direct EC->CA1 path): associated with ENCODING of new input.
- **theta + SLOW gamma** (sourced from CA3 Schaffer collaterals): associated with RETRIEVAL / pattern-completion, and its coupling strength to theta phase INCREASES specifically after successful memory retrieval.

This is not "one knob turned up or down" -- it is **two structurally different afferent pathways, active preferentially at different phases of the same slower theta cycle**, i.e. a genuine phase-division-multiplexed routing scheme, not a continuous scalar. **This directly refines the self-manager's ACh channel**: encode-vs-retrieve is better modeled on the substrate as a **discrete PATHWAY SELECT** (which afferent route is active this cycle: direct/new-input vs recurrent/completion) rather than a continuous gain knob on one fixed recurrent pathway. This distinction matters for the design below (Section 2.2/2.3): consolidation should route through the CA3-completion pathway explicitly, structurally separate from the direct-ingest pathway, not merely "the same pathway with the gain turned down."

### 1.4 SWR replay: content, timing, and what actually gets selected

- **Physiology:** SWRs are ~50-150ms population events, ~140-220Hz ripple riding a slower sharp-wave, generated by CA3 recurrent burst + sparse inhibition. Firing rate ~5-10/sec during slow-wave sleep and quiet wakefulness ONLY -- essentially absent during active theta exploration. Within a ripple, place-cell sequences from waking replay IN THE SAME ORDER, compressed ~20x (Wilson & McNaughton 1994; Lee & Wilson 2002), and the compressed sequence itself exhibits its OWN phase precession within the ripple band -- i.e. the brain reuses the SAME phase-coding scheme (theta phase precession) at a faster timescale for replay, rather than inventing a new code for offline reactivation.
- **Content is NOT uniform sampling.** SWR content is biased toward NOVEL, REWARDED, and SURPRISING experience (Michon 2025; Ambrose/Pfeiffer/Foster 2016); reverse replay (backward through a just-completed trajectory) scales specifically with REWARD magnitude and supports temporal credit assignment (Foster & Wilson 2006); forward replay scales with novelty and upcoming-choice uncertainty. Two functionally dissociated sweep directions, not one generic "resample" operation.
- **Interleaving of old and new is a REAL, recently-confirmed circuit-level phenomenon, not an ML inference imported after the fact:** within individual slow oscillation events during slow-wave sleep, novel and familiar memory traces are reactivated INTERLEAVED (2025 finding) -- this happens at the granularity of single slow-wave events, not as a separate "sometimes-new sometimes-old" schedule. This is the one point where the biology and the ML replay literature happen to agree in SHAPE (interleaving reduces interference) -- cited here only because independent circuit-level evidence supports it, not because the ML analog is the reason to build it.

### 1.5 The SO-spindle-ripple triple coupling: consolidation is a GATED, DISCRETE PHASE, not a free-running background process

Three nested oscillations orchestrate the transfer window: cortical slow oscillations (SO, <1 Hz) create alternating UP (excitable, plasticity-permissive) and DOWN (silent) states; SO up-states drive the thalamus to generate spindles (12-16 Hz, ~1s bursts, ~10-20 cycles each); hippocampal SWRs are phase-locked to nest inside spindle peaks, which themselves nest inside SO up-states. The whole cascade (SO up-state -> spindle -> SWR, ~10 SWRs per spindle per up-state) is the actual physical transfer window; cortical synaptic plasticity is gated specifically to the up-state, and this coupling strength CORRELATES with how well the memory consolidates (disrupted coupling = worse consolidation, seen in aging). **The finite nightly SWR budget (~10k-30k events per 8hr sleep) means the brain has a hard consolidation budget per offline epoch, not unlimited replay.**

**Substrate implication:** consolidation cannot be a low-priority background thread that runs "whenever." It is architecturally a DISCRETE PHASE with its own internal sub-structure and a FINITE per-cycle budget -- closer to a scheduled batch mode (serve-mode paused or de-prioritized) than to an always-on trickle process.

### 1.6 Synaptic Homeostasis (SHY): the physiology is SELECTIVE, not a uniform rescale

Wake learning nets synaptic potentiation (more/bigger AMPA receptors at active synapses). Slow-wave sleep, driven specifically by the <1Hz SO (not REM, not circadian phase alone), drives net AMPA-receptor INTERNALIZATION, renormalizing synaptic strength back toward baseline. Critically, the downscaling during cortical UP-states is **NOT a blanket global rescale**: up-states bias the network toward synaptic DEPRESSION for inputs that were merely presynaptically active, but synapses that were CAUSALLY responsible for postsynaptic spiking (i.e., synapses that actually did useful work) are PROTECTED from this weakening. This is a Hebbian-selective survival rule -- "if you drove real output, you are spared; if you only ever contributed noise, you are weakened" -- computed locally, using information already available from the SAME up-state event that is also driving replay, not a separate global-norm-clipping pass computed afterward with an arbitrarily-chosen target norm.

### 1.7 A distinct third lever the SHY literature does not cover: adult DG neurogenesis as ACTUAL forgetting (not renormalization)

Continuous integration of new granule cells into the DG circuit throughout adulthood remodels existing dentate connectivity, incrementally reducing the odds that an old retrieval cue reinvokes an old sparse hippocampal code. This is a genuine, ongoing DECAY/forgetting process, mechanistically DISTINCT from SHY (which renormalizes weight magnitude without discarding structure): neurogenesis actively frees hippocampal encoding capacity by degrading old sparse traces over time, trading fidelity of very old episodic detail for continued high-capacity new encoding. It targets the FAST/sparse hippocampal-analog buffer specifically, not the slow cortical store (which, per CLS, has ALREADY extracted the durable statistical structure by the time the hippocampal trace degrades) -- so it is a safe, complementary lever, not a competing one.

## PART 2 -- RANKED BUILDABLE LIST (derived from the biology above, not from the ML analogs)

Ordering rule: reuse of already-certified substrate primitives first (evolutionary-order build discipline, matches the self-manager drill's own conclusion: modulate an existing solid core, do not build bespoke machinery), cheapest test first, and account for prior-art failures/successes documented in Part 0.

### RANK 1 -- CA3-completion + slow cortical integration consolidation pass (the CORE loop; fixes "integrate new KB knowledge without forgetting")

**Biological derivation:** Section 1.1 + 1.2. Hippocampus receives the raw new item; during an offline pass, CA3-style attractor completion cleans it; the CLEANED (not raw) pattern is what gets written, slowly and in small steps (not a single big write), into the cortical/correlated code -- matching how CLS says cortex extracts shared structure across MANY replayed exposures, never a one-shot copy.

**Substrate design:** reuse the existing attractor/iterative-cleanup primitive (already certified; used in resonator + generation-cleanup + composed value-gate) as the CA3-analog completion step over the native/decorrelated store code (already the DG/CA3-analog per the correlation-hurts-capacity law). Feed the CLEANED pattern into the correlated/cortical-analog retrieval code via a LOW learning-rate accumulation (many small updates across many consolidation cycles), never a direct raw copy. This is the fix RESCUE-4 from `research_drill_cls_2substrate_rescue_2x_2026-06-11.md` (temporal segregation of writes) PLUS the missing detail from the physiology (cortex receives a CLEANED signal, not the raw one) that the original CLS cell never had.

**Cheap first test:** reuse the composition/encoder harness that already produces both codes (native decorrelated + correlated/BGE-anchored). Buffer N new synthetic KB atoms into the sparse/hippocampal-analog side only (no direct cortical write). Run ONE offline consolidation pass: CA3-attractor-complete each buffered item, then apply a small-step update into the correlated code. Measure (a) retrieval fidelity on the NEWLY consolidated items in the cortical code vs. a direct-raw-copy control (no completion step), and (b) retrieval fidelity on an UNRELATED old probe set before vs. after the pass (interference check -- this is the "without forgetting" half). HARD-PASS: completed-then-slow-integrated arm beats direct-copy arm on new-item fidelity AND old-probe fidelity delta is ~0 (no interference); HARD-FAIL: either the completion step adds nothing over direct copy, or old-probe fidelity drops after consolidating new items (would mean the two codes are not actually decoupled the way the encoder cert claims).
**P_deflated: 0.42** (high confidence in the completion-step mechanism itself, since it reuses an already-certified primitive; deflated for the untested "does slow-step integration actually avoid interference at scale" claim).

### RANK 2 -- Discrete offline PHASE-GATE with structural pathway-select (fixes "consolidation must not run concurrently with ingest")

**Biological derivation:** Section 1.3 + 1.5. Encode and retrieve/consolidate route through structurally different afferent pathways gated to different phases of a slower rhythm (not a shared pathway with a turned-down gain); the whole cortical transfer window is furthermore GATED to a discrete, finite-budget phase (SO up-state), not a free-running background trickle.

**Substrate design:** a scheduler state with two exclusive modes -- INGEST (new items appended to the sparse/hippocampal-analog buffer only; the correlated/cortical code and the compositional store are NOT written) and CONSOLIDATE (offline; buffer drained through the CA3-completion step from Rank 1; a per-cycle FIXED BUDGET of items processed, mirroring the brain's finite nightly SWR count -- not "until the buffer is empty"). This directly operationalizes RESCUE-4's "temporal segregation" fix, refined with the physiology's insistence that this is a discrete phase-switch with an internal budget, not a continuous scalar blend (course-correcting the earlier self-manager note's ACh-as-scalar-gain framing for this specific channel).

**Cheap first test:** rebuild the original `two_substrate_fastslow_cls` cell (cycle-228 HARD_FAIL) with writes to the cortical/slow store GATED exclusively to a separate CONSOLIDATE phase (never concurrent with ingest), reusing the SAME corpus/harness that produced the original HARD_FAIL so it is a direct, paired re-test. HARD-PASS: recent-item recall AND old-item-consolidated recall both clear the original cell's thresholds (recent >= 0.80 AND old_consolidated >= 0.80), closing the cycle-228 negative. HARD-FAIL: gating alone does not rescue old_consolidated -- would mean the blend-dilution diagnosis (H2 in the 06-11 drill) was incomplete and capacity saturation (H1) is the dominant driver instead.
**P_deflated: 0.45** (RESCUE-4 already carried P=0.48 pre-physiology; physiology refines but does not change the core mechanism, so held near that estimate with standard deflation).

### RANK 3 -- SHY-selective renormalization keyed to replay-time causal margin (fixes the CG-certified SATURATION failure)

**Biological derivation:** Section 1.6. Downscaling is Hebbian-selective (protect synapses that drove real output; weaken synapses that only ever contributed noise), computed from information already available at consolidation time -- NOT a uniform global-norm rescale toward an arbitrarily chosen target (which is what the existing `Path C: HOMEOSTATIC-NORM-RENORMALIZATION` note from `research_drill_additive_beyond_200_2x_2026-06-10.md` proposed, at P=0.40, flagged there as "insufficient alone -- requires choosing B and W_target_norm; wrong choice degrades live recall").

**Substrate design:** piggyback on the Rank-1 consolidation pass's attractor-completion step, which ALREADY computes a per-component margin (how strongly each stored component contributed to a correct completion) as a byproduct. Add a renormalization readout that uses that SAME signal: components with high positive replay-time margin are protected/reinforced; components that contribute near-zero or negative margin are down-weighted/pruned. This removes the free-parameter arbitrariness (no separately-chosen target norm) that made the earlier Path-C note only P=0.40 -- the biology's answer is "use the signal replay is already producing," not "pick a rescale target."

**Cheap first test:** take the already CG-certified saturation cell/regime (the provisioned-N compositional-store saturation result referenced in the 2026-07-08 encoder-fidelity pivot). Run one consolidation+renormalization cycle keyed to replay-margin (as above) vs. a uniform-global-rescale control (the naive Path-C version) vs. a no-renormalization control. HARD-PASS: margin-keyed renormalization recovers measurable capacity headroom (composition/capacity metric improves) relative to BOTH controls, with cv within the house band across seeds. HARD-FAIL: no arm beats no-renormalization, or margin-keyed does not beat uniform-rescale (would mean the selectivity itself isn't the load-bearing part).
**P_deflated: 0.35** (genuinely novel-synthesis-adjacent -- reusing the replay-margin signal for renormalization has direct physiological support but no substrate precedent yet; capped conservatively).

### RANK 4 -- Priority/salience-biased consolidation order under a fixed per-cycle budget (fixes "what gets consolidated first when ingest outpaces the budget")

**Biological derivation:** Section 1.4. SWR content is not uniformly sampled; it is biased toward novelty/reward/surprise, and the brain has a hard finite nightly replay budget. Directly composes with Rank 2's fixed-budget CONSOLIDATE phase: when the buffer exceeds the per-cycle budget, ORDER matters.

**Substrate design:** this is the already-designed-but-never-built `c4_prioritized_replay_v1` from the 2026-06-22 5x drill (deferred pending c1's landing), re-purposed here for the ingest-densification use case rather than the forgetting-rescue use case it was originally scoped for. Priority = NEED (how likely the item is to be queried soon -- recency/frequency proxy) x GAIN (how uncertain/low-margin the item's completion was at write time -- the substrate's own refuse-gate-margin signal is already computed and directly usable here, no new machinery).

**Cheap first test:** exactly the `c4_prioritized_replay_v1` design (Section 3.2/L4 of the 06-22 drill) -- PRIORITIZED vs UNIFORM vs FIFO consolidation order under a fixed per-cycle budget smaller than the buffer size; measure aggregate retrieval fidelity across many ingest-then-consolidate cycles. HARD-PASS: PRIORITIZED beats UNIFORM by >=0.10 at matched budget, or matches UNIFORM's fidelity at <=50% of the budget (replay-budget efficiency win, per Mattar-Daw/Schaul-PER precedent already noted in the 06-22 drill). HARD-FAIL: no ordering scheme beats FIFO -- budget, not order, is the bottleneck.
**P_deflated: 0.40** (inherits the 06-22 drill's own estimate, held with standard deflation; already-designed, cheapest of the four to actually author since the spec exists).

### RANK 5 (exploratory, lowest priority) -- Neurogenesis-style hippocampal-buffer turnover (a genuine forgetting lever, DISTINCT from SHY)

**Biological derivation:** Section 1.7. Complementary to Ranks 1-4: once an item is fully consolidated into the cortical/correlated code (Rank 1) and has gone unconsulted for N consolidation cycles, its sparse-buffer slot can be safely reallocated -- the durable structure already lives in the cortical code; only the fast hippocampal-analog trace is discarded. This targets the fast buffer's OWN capacity ceiling, a distinct resource pool from the compositional store Rank 3 renormalizes.

**Cheap first test:** after Ranks 1+2 land, run an extended ingest simulation (many consolidation cycles) with vs. without buffer-slot recycling for fully-consolidated, long-unconsulted items; measure whether the hippocampal-analog buffer's own capacity ceiling is deferred without any loss of cortical-code fidelity on the recycled items (should be exactly zero loss, since by construction recycling only touches items already safely consolidated).
**P_deflated: 0.30** (lowest confidence -- depends on Ranks 1-2 landing first to even define "fully consolidated"; flagged as exploratory/deferred, not a near-term dispatch).
